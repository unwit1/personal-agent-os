# Research Stop Rule

Use during research-heavy tasks.

Before each additional search/read, ask whether it is needed to:
- resolve a material contradiction;
- obtain a higher-authority source;
- fill a required coverage gap;
- verify a time-sensitive fact;
- satisfy an explicit breadth/depth requirement;
- support validation of the deliverable.

Stop when:
- the central claims are adequately supported;
- new sources are redundant;
- remaining uncertainty is non-material or can be stated;
- the requested coverage has been met.

Do not keep collecting sources merely because more are available.

When stopping, record:
- what is established;
- material uncertainty that remains;
- why more research is unlikely to change the current result.

This skill optimizes research cost without lowering evidentiary standards.
