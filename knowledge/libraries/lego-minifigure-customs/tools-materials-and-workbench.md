# Tools, Materials, and Workbench

## Starter
Digital calipers; self-healing mat; precision knife/blades; fine scissors; tweezers; soft brushes/swabs; dishes; lint-free cloth; appropriate gloves; neutral task lighting; magnification; organized bins; metal ruler; fixed-scale printer; matching waterslide media; compatible clear sealer; sacrificial test parts.

## Artwork
Vector editor; raster editor; consistent display; scanner/camera copy stand; color/reference chart; version-controlled source files.

## Advanced decals
Quality photo printer or color laser matched to media; spare consumables; controlled drying area; tested decal solutions; controlled clear-coat/airbrush setup; cutting plotter for repeatable white-film outlines.

## Painting
Fine brushes; palette; paint system; compatible thinner; primers; clear finishes; holders/jigs; ventilated spray/airbrush arrangement. Keep manufacturer safety information with chemicals.

## Direct print
Prototype through a service before buying machinery. UV can require printer, white-ink maintenance, RIP, custom jigs, pretreatment, extraction/ventilation and ongoing maintenance. Pad printing needs machine, plates/cliches, silicone pads, inks/thinners, fixtures and registration.

## Jigs
Version jigs like software: CAD/source, dimensions, printer origin, compatible mold, orientation, capacity, material, photos. Repeatable fixturing can matter more than nominal resolution.

Separate dirty sanding/painting from clean print/decal work; control dust; label chemicals; document successful setups.
