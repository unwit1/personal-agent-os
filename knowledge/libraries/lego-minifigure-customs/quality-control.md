# Quality Control

Approve a physical golden sample for each design/process combination. Photograph it under standardized lighting and store it with the batch spec.

## Inspect
Correct artwork revision/orientation; physical scale; color; registration; opacity; dust/bubbles/silvering/scratches; decal edges; finish; joint geometry; durability; correct parts/accessories.

## Calibration sheet
Include a known ruler, fine-line ladder, small text, grayscale, color patches, target LEGO-color patches, white-underbase trap/choke tests, registration targets and solid fills. Run it after changes to printer, media, ink, coating or profile.

## Defect codes
MISREG, COLOR, SCALE, OPACITY, EDGE, SILVER, DUST, COAT, SCRATCH, ADH, PART, ART, OTHER.

## Experiment records
hypothesis, changed variable, constants, sample count, exact materials, environment, procedure, photos, measurements, result, conclusion. Promote only repeatable findings.

## Acceptance levels
Prototype: design review.
Display: clean at normal viewing distance.
Collector/premium: clean under close inspection.
Production master: repeatable and documented.

Every finished unit/batch should trace back to art revision, donor inventory, process and QC result.
