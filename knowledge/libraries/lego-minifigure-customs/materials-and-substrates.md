# Materials and Substrates

## LEGO elements are not one plastic
LEGO's current materials information lists multiple material families across elements, including ABS, HIPS, PA, arMABS for transparent elements, MTPO, PC, bio-PE, POM, PP, TPU, SEBS and others. Therefore “LEGO plastic” is not an adequate UV-print substrate field.

## Production consequence
Every part record should contain a material field when known or a confidence-tagged inference when not. UV adhesion, primer need, surface energy, solvent sensitivity and mechanical behavior can differ by material.

## Substrate classes
At minimum distinguish:
- molded ABS-like rigid opaque elements,
- transparent rigid elements,
- flexible/rubbery elements,
- cloth/fabric,
- painted/coated donor parts,
- third-party compatible plastic,
- photopolymer resin,
- primed resin,
- painted resin.

## Surface preparation experiment
For every new substrate family test:
- as received,
- cleaned using manufacturer-compatible method,
- primer/no primer,
- white/no white,
- representative CMYK,
- varnish/no varnish.

Record visual quality and adhesion/durability separately.

## Safety
Do not improvise aggressive solvent treatment on unknown plastics. Use printer/ink/primer and material manufacturer guidance and SDS information.
