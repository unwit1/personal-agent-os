# Source Reliability and Provenance

## Source classes
A: official manufacturer/process documentation
B: ratified technical/community standard such as LDraw official specifications
C: established structured catalog with documented identifiers
D: specialist vendor technical documentation
E: experienced community findings with reproducible evidence
F: anecdotal/community claim
G: AI-generated hypothesis

## Rules
- Store source URL, title, publisher, accessed date and applicable version.
- Separate quoted/source fact from Agent OS inference.
- Never promote G directly to manufacturing truth.
- A community dimension becomes high confidence only after corroboration or physical validation.
- Time-sensitive catalog/process facts carry last_verified.
- Contradictions are records, not overwritten mistakes.

## Licensing
Store source license and permitted-use notes for geometry, textures, images and datasets. “Publicly viewable” does not mean reusable for training, redistribution or commercial artwork.

## Generation provenance
Every AI artifact should record:
model/provider
model/version
prompt/spec
input asset IDs
control maps
reference images
seed where exposed
generation settings
post-processing
human edits
rights/provenance status
production approval state.
