# Printing and Production

## Process ladder
Hand paint: best for one-offs, sculpted parts and corrections; low equipment barrier, high skill/time.
Waterslide: excellent prototypes/small runs; limited by opacity, edges, curves and wear.
Adhesive film: fast and robust but visibly thicker.
UV direct print: digital multi-color, useful for detailed short runs; can support white ink and varnish; needs jigs, adhesion testing and calibration and may have raised texture.
Pad print: color-separated artwork transferred with etched plates/cliches and silicone pads; excellent repeatability/curves and factory-like aesthetics, but setup, plates, registration and equipment favor larger runs.
Screen print: useful for some flat/cloth repeated graphics.
Laser engraving: permanent linework/markings, changes surface instead of adding full-color art.
Custom sculpt/3D print: for new hair/helmets/armor/weapons; requires its own tolerancing, finishing and material/safety workflow.

## Selection
One-off concept: decal/paint.
Small premium run: outsourced UV/specialist printer.
Larger repeat run with restrained colors: evaluate pad printing.
New geometry: sculpt/3D print plus finishing.
Premium mixed media: donor parts + direct print + cloth/custom accessories.

## Outsourcing checklist
Accepted files; genuine-part policy; minimum line size; white underbase; spot colors; color matching; print areas; wrap/arm/leg support; jig/setup fees; MOQ; proof/sample; defect policy; durability; turnaround; ownership of plates/files; IP restrictions.

## Batch record
art revision, donor lot/source, operator/vendor, process, machine/profile, jig, ink/media lot, pretreatment, cure, coating, started quantity, accepted quantity, rejects by reason, representative photos.
