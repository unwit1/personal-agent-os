# Kentucky Transportation Cabinet approval of RTEC EEO/Affirmative Action Plan — December 30, 2020

Source SHA-256: `3b60dd0744ed6da399c3a0a6722e22b4a4608329aa28ceb76eba5235ce06bb11`

The Office of Transportation Delivery letter is dated **December 30, 2020** and is addressed to RTEC's Executive Director.

Subject: Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative Action Plan; Equal Employment Opportunity Guidelines for Grant Recipients (EEO); FTA Circular 4704.1.

The letter states that RTEC's Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative Action Plan was reviewed and found satisfactory.

It identifies the following plan objectives:

- Statement of Policy
- Internal and external dissemination plan
- Designation of personnel responsible for the EEO Program, including an EEO Officer
- Utilization analysis
- Goals and timetables to correct identified underutilization or concentration
- Assessment of employment practices
- Plan for monitoring and reporting on the EEO Program

The letter states that RTEC's next Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative Action Plan submission would be due by **January 30, 2024**.

Historical approval evidence only; the letter does not establish the status of a plan submitted after that due date.
