# SFY 2017 Section 5310 application — checklist and structure

Source: `rtec-grant-fy2017-section-5310`

## Program

Kentucky Transportation Cabinet, Office of Transportation Delivery  
Section 5310 (CFDA #20.513) application guidelines  
Enhanced Mobility of Seniors and Individuals with Disabilities  
SFY 2017 application checklist, due April 1, 2016.

## Main checklist structure

The application checklist identifies the following required areas:

### 1. Type of project
- Traditional Capital Project — Replacement Vehicles
- Project(s) Exceed Requirements of ADA / Improve Access to Fixed Route Services and/or Alternatives that Assist Seniors and Individuals with Disabilities (formerly 5317 New Freedom)

### 2. Summary page
- All agencies contacted/dates listed
- Needs ranked
- Coordination meeting(s) listed, if applicable

### 3. Notification
- Copy(s) of letter and/or list of agencies
- Private sector notified
- Groups serving minorities notified
- Copies of certified receipts
- Coordination meeting(s) summarized, if applicable

### 4. Requirements
- Fiscal/managerial capability of lead agency/applicants
- Title VI: minorities served estimated by category
- Assurance that service is nondiscriminatory
- Operating certificates for applicable applicants
- Preventive maintenance program and forms
- Articles of incorporation for all applicants
- Legal name form
- Operating funds and local match
  - letters from agency providing operating funds
  - vehicles from agency(s) providing required local match
- Incidental services, such as meal delivery, and cost recovery
- Status of open 5310 and 5317 projects

### 5. Assurances
- 504 certification
- Procurement Information Certification
- Standard Assurances
- Local Civil Rights Assurance
- School Bus Operations Certification
- Federally Required and Model Contract Clauses
- Incidental Services Certification
- Authorizing Resolution
- Certification of Equivalent Service
- Charter Certification
- Disabled Assurance
- Lobbying Certification
- Milestones Schedule
- Project Financing and Commitment of Local Share
- Use of Project Facilities

### 6. Assessments
- Each county's need addressed
- One complete form per vehicle

### 7. Training
- RTAP needs/budget(s)
- Training conducted during current and past fiscal years

### 8. Specifications
- Vehicle or equipment specifications and bid package/quote specifications with ICE for each

### 9. Coordinated Plan
- Coordinated plan attached
- Coordinated plan checklist attached, fully completed and signed

The checklist states that all elements must be checked or marked N/A by the project manager for an application to receive state and federal approval.

## Additional early-page evidence

A dated county-needs sheet identifies needs addressed for Cumberland River Comprehensive Care Centers in Laurel and Whitley Counties, Harlan County Committee on Aging in Harlan County, and RTEC in Bell and Laurel Counties. It separately lists multiple agencies not requesting a vehicle that year, including agencies with RTEC contracts.

The preliminary assessment requires agency identification; geography served; service type; requested vehicle/equipment; whether service is replacement, expansion, new, or beyond ADA space; existing vehicle VIN/make/model/year/mileage/capacity/lift/ramp; service days/hours; trip estimates by client category; project description; fleet inventory by grant program; coordination with other providers/programs; and local-match sources.

A procurement certification states that vehicles purchased under the program are procured through competitive bidding in accordance with established state and federal procedures and requires certification that the organization has the fiscal and managerial capability to carry out the project.

A separate local-match assurance requires identifiable local-match sources and states that federal funding or revenues derived from a federally sponsored program will not be used as the local match for equipment granted under Sections 5339/5310.
