# FY2017 Section 5310 — maintenance/operating-authority extract

Source: `rtec-grant-fy2017-section-5310`  
Scanned PDF pages reviewed: 90–96.

## Operating authority

The binder states that applicant agencies other than RTEC requesting Section 5310 vehicles must have the appropriate operating authority/license for the service. It includes RTEC's Kentucky certificate of public convenience and necessity for nonprofit bus and disabled-persons carrier operations.

The certificate shown is No. 5311-16, effective July 1, 2015. It describes noncommercial passenger transportation authority and identifies demand-response, inter-city, fixed-route and semi-fixed-route service categories in the historical authority.

## Preventive maintenance

The grant narrative says attached forms are required for drivers of both applicant agencies and RTEC vehicles to complete on a daily basis for regular/daily inspections and mileage recording. It states that preventive maintenance for RTEC vehicles is entered into a mobile-data computer that sends report data to RTEC computers.

The included **Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. Vehicle Maintenance Program — Operator's Manual** is marked revised March 2000, 2005, 2008, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016.

Visible table-of-contents items include:

The introduction describes a preventive-maintenance program and good record keeping as important to a successful transportation system, with the goal of ensuring safe operating condition and extending useful vehicle life.

The visible Vehicle Inspections section states that the driver is required to conduct a pre-trip inspection before vehicle operation and document it using pre-trip procedures. It also states that the driver must check all emergency exits before boarding passengers and that inspections are generally conducted as pre-trip inspections.

This is historical source material and does not establish current inspection intervals/forms without newer evidence.
