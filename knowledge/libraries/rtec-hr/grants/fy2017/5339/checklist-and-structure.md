## Project description and justification

The application describes RTEC as a nonprofit Section 5311 transit provider serving rural Southeast Kentucky and as the Region 12 Human Services Transportation Delivery broker.

Service descriptions include community/public transportation to shopping, employment, education/job training, and non-emergency medical appointments; rural-to-urban/intercity connections; feeder/connector transportation originating in multiple counties; elderly and disabled service; and coordination with other transportation resources.

The Section 5339 capital request prioritizes:

1. **Vehicle funding** — replacement of older/high-mileage vehicles, with a preference for more economical-to-operate vehicles such as minivans.
2. **Miscellaneous support equipment** — mobile radios compliant with new FTA/FCC narrow-banding rules; the source says approximately $475 per radio including additional funding for replacement base stations.

The project justification states that RTEC continued to add service as requests increased, that many fleet vehicles were above 200,000 miles, and that vehicles proposed for replacement had exceeded 100,000 miles. It also states that narrow-banding mobile radios were being requested and were of great need.
