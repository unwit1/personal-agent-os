# FY2017 Section 5339 — project-facility and environmental controls

Source: `rtec-grant-fy2017-section-5339`  
Scanned PDF pages reviewed: 70–76.

## STIP / TIP

The application states that MPO Transportation Improvement Program inclusion was not applicable to RTEC because it served rural Southeast Kentucky, and states that RTEC was included in the STIP.

## Use of project facilities/equipment

For Section 5339/5310 capital purchases, the source states that assets comprising the project would be owned by RTEC and used in providing mass-transportation services in its service area.

It states that the grant agreement would require continued use of project facilities through their useful life and that sale/diversion to another use during useful life would require refund of a proportionate Federal grant share based on fair market value/proceeds.

The page is signed by the Executive Director and dated March 19, 2016.

## Labor

A March 19, 2016 letter to the Kentucky Transportation Cabinet states that, absent a Department of Labor waiver, the labor-protection terms/conditions of Special Section 49 U.C.C. 5333(b) would apply and were accepted by RTEC for the Section 5339 project.

## No displacement

RTEC certified that no persons, families, or businesses would be displaced by the proposed project.

## NEPA / environmental review

The application marks anticipation of NEPA requirements as not applicable to this project, while noting that RTEC understood FTA must consider environmental impacts under NEPA and would provide environmental analysis/review documentation for any project involving construction.

The Protection of the Environment statement characterizes the project as a Class 2 action with no significant environmental impact and introduces RTEC's environmental policy/goals.

Visible policy text says environmental management is integrated into business activities; RTEC and staff will comply with applicable federal/state/local environmental regulations; RTEC focuses on reducing negative environmental impact of operations; environmental policy implementation is everyone's responsibility.

It describes an **Environmental Protection Committee** convening quarterly to discuss environmental issues/concerns. Membership described includes the Operations Manager, Fleet Manager, Safety & Security Coordinator, committee recommendations, and Executive Director approval.

The policy describes continuing review/evaluation of environmental impacts, cooperation with community organizations, reducing environmental impacts from equipment use/disposal, energy-efficient equipment, anti-idling/environmental driving practices, recycling, safe disposal, and pollution prevention.
