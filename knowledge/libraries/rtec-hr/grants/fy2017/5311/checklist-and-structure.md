## Early operational description captured from the binder

The application describes RTEC as a nonprofit Section 5311 transit provider serving a multi-county Southeast Kentucky area, providing community transportation for shopping, employment, education, job training, and non-emergency medical appointments.

It describes a demand-response model rather than fixed-route service. The application states that reservations are taken during office hours and that customers call in advance for requested service.

The binder describes coordination of capital and operating resources through Section 5309/5310/5311 and the Region 12 HSTD brokerage, plus coordination with local senior organizations and other agencies.

The application also contains an intercity-bus assurance and detailed-description section. It reports a total intercity budget of $508,720, with a federal share of $254,360 and local share of $254,360, and describes demand-response intercity links between the rural service area and urban centers including Lexington, London, Mount Vernon, Bowling Green, and Somerset, with future-route coordination contemplated for Louisville, Nashville, and Knoxville.
