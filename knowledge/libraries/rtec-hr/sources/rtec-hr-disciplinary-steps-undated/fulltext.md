# Disciplinary steps - undated legacy matrix

Source ID: `rtec-hr-disciplinary-steps-undated`

## Workbook structure

The workbook contains a matrix headed:

- TYPE OF VIOLATION
- 1ST OFFENSE POINTS ASSESSED
- DATE
- 2ND OFFENSE POINTS ASSESSED
- DATE
- 3RD OFFENSE POINTS ASSESSED
- DATE

The point-assessment and date cells are blank in the supplied workbook; no numeric offense values are embedded in those columns.

## Interpretation boundary

This is an undated blank discipline matrix/template. Its listed violations are evidence of historical operational concerns, but the workbook itself does not provide numeric point values or establish current policy.
