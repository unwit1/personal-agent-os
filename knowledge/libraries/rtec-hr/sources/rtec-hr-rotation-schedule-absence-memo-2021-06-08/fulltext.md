# Rotation Schedule memorandum

Source ID: `rtec-hr-rotation-schedule-absence-memo-2021-06-08`

**To:** Area Manager, CSRs  
**Date:** June 8, 2021  
**Subject:** Rotation Schedule

The memorandum states that if an employee is unable to work scheduled rotation days in the office, the missed day will be treated as an absence and is subject to the normal point system. It explains that RTEC was transitioning back to normal operations and did not intend to continually switch office days to accommodate sickness, appointments, and similar conflicts.

It further states that if an employee is too sick to come into the office, management does not believe the employee will be productive on the phones, and emphasizes customer-service quality as the top priority.

Signed by Mike Dixon, Operations Manager.

## Interpretation boundary

This is a dated 2021 return-to-normal/rotation-schedule instruction. It should not be generalized beyond the affected roles/time period without later corroboration.
