## Instructions

Please answer the following questions. Select the best answer and write the corresponding LETTER in the BLANK provided. There are 25 questions and each are worth 4 points each. ## Questions and keyed answers

1. TURN YOUR FRONT WHEELS TOWARD THE CURB WHEN YOU ARE PARKED:
   - A. Facing uphill.
   - B. On a level road.
   - **C. Facing downhill.**
   - Key note: When parked facing down, turn wheels to curb. When parked facing uphill with a curb, turn wheels from the curb. When parked facing uphill without a curb, turn wheels to right.

2. THE ONLY WAY A PERSON WHO HAS BEEN DRINKING CAN SOBER UP TO DRIVE SAFELY IS:
   - A. To drink lots of hot, black coffee.
   - B. Take a very cold shower.
   - C. Get lots of fresh air and try exercising.
   - **D. Wait several hours before driving (at least 1.5 hours per drink consumed).**

3. WHEN A CAR WITH BRIGHT HEADLIGHTS COMES TOWARD YOU AT NIGHT, YOU SHOULD:
   - A. Move toward the right edge of your lane.
   - B. Look above the oncoming headlights.
   - C. Look below the oncoming headlights.
   - **D. Look toward the right edge of your lane.**

4. IF YOUR VEHICLE STARTS TO HYDROPLANE, YOU SHOULD:
   - A. Drive at a constant speed to gain better traction.
   - B. Apply the brakes firmly to prevent your vehicle from sliding.
   - **C. Slow down gradually by easing off the gas and not applying the brakes.**

5. WHICH OF THE FOLLOWING ARE DANGEROUS TO DO WHILE DRIVING?
   - A. Reading a road map.
   - B. Eating and drinking.
   - C. Talking on the cell phone.
   - D. Talking on a cell phone using only hands-free device.
   - **E. All of the above.**

6. WHEN THE VERY FIRST EFFECT OF EVEN A SMALL AMOUNT OF ALCOHOL ON A PERSON'S SAFE DRIVING ABILITY IS TO REDUCE HIS/HER:
   - A. Steering ability.
   - **B. Good judgment.**
   - C. Muscle control.
   - D. Eye coordination.

7. WHEN YOU SEE THE ACCESSIBLE-PARKING SIGN IT MEANS THE SPACE PROVIDED IS:
   - A. Parking only to be used in emergency situations.
   - B. Parking for important official political persons only.
   - **C. Parking only for vehicles displaying an official permit and transporting a person with disabilities.**
   - D. Parking only for persons in wheelchairs only.

8. IN ORDER TO SEE WELL AT NIGHT YOU SHOULD:
   - A. Blink your headlights periodically.
   - B. Leave your lights on bright all the time.
   - **C. Use your high beams whenever there are no oncoming vehicles, except when adverse weather conditions would make low beams more appropriate.**
   - D. Wear dark sunglasses.

9. IT IS BEST TO WEAR YOUR SEAT BELT IN A VEHICLE:
   - **A. At all times.**
   - B. Only if you will be driving over 35 MPH.
   - C. Only if your bus holds more than 15 people.
   - D. Only if you suspect a police officer is in the area.

10. A FLASHING RED LIGHT AT AN INTERSECTION MEANS:
   - A. Yield.
   - B. Slow down and continue through intersection slowly.
   - **C. Come to a full stop and proceed only when the way is clear.**
   - D. The light is malfunctioning, proceed as though there were no lights.

11. IF THERE ARE THREE OR MORE LANES MOVING IN ONE DIRECTION, THE MIDDLE LANE OR LANES ARE FOR:
   - A. Slower traffic.
   - **B. Through traffic, unless otherwise marked.**
   - C. Traffic turning left.
   - D. Traffic turning right.

12. YOU SHOULD ALWAYS AVOID BACKING WHENEVER POSSIBLE. BUT WHEN YOU HAVE TO BACK YOUR VEHICLE, YOU SHOULD USE EXTRA CAUTION AND:
   - A. Use the rearview mirror only.
   - **B. Look over your shoulder and back slowly taking all precautions. If unsure, get out and look.**
   - C. Take a chance and hope nothing is behind you.
   - D. Back at the fastest speed possible to get it over with quickly.

13. TO CHECK FOR TRAFFIC IN YOUR "BLIND SPOTS" (USUALLY IN THE LEFT OR RIGHT REAR CORNERS OF YOUR VEHICLE) YOU SHOULD:
   - **A. Turn and glance over your shoulder and then lean forward in seat slightly and check all appropriate mirrors.**
   - B. Check your left side view mirror only.
   - C. Check your rear view mirror only.
   - D. Check both your left and right side view mirror without leaning forward in seat.

14. WHEN APPROACHING A RAILROAD CROSSING, YOU SHOULD:
   - **A. Check for trains and proceed with caution; buses and vans transporting passengers should stop and open doors or windows and then look and listen for trains before proceeding safely across adhering to rules of all signage and crossing lights.**
   - B. Stop and check for trains only if the signal is flashing.
   - C. Speed up and continue across quickly.
   - D. Stop only if there are two separate tracks and visibility is poor.

15. WHEN YOU COME TO A SCHOOL OR CHURCH BUS THAT IS STOPPED ON ANY ROADWAY TO LOAD OR UNLOAD PASSENGERS, YOU MUST:
   - A. Slow down and proceed cautiously in case of children.
   - **B. Stop and remain stopped until all people are clear of the roadway and the bus is in motion.**
   - C. Blow your horn to alert passengers you are passing.
   - D. Flash your headlights before passing and stop only if there is an approaching vehicle.

16. IF YOU ARE DRIVING DURING DAWN, DUSK, ON CLOUDY DAYS, OR DURING INCLEMENT WEATHER YOU SHOULD:
   - A. Use your high-beam lights only if they will help you see.
   - **B. Turn the low-beam lights on to help others to see you and increase visibility.**
   - C. Turn your parking lights on to help others see you but not headlights.
   - D. Flash your turn signals when other cars are approaching.

17. WHEN TWO VEHICLES REACH AN UNCONTROLLED INTERSECTION AT THE SAME TIME:
   - A. The vehicle on the left has the right-of-way.
   - **B. The vehicle on the right has the right-of-way.**
   - C. The biggest vehicle has the right-of-way.
   - D. You should never yield and give up your power.

18. THE SPEED AT WHICH YOU DRIVE SHOULD BE BASED ON:
   - A. The condition of the road only.
   - B. The posted speed limit only.
   - **C. The posted speed limit, the condition of the road, weather and traffic conditions.**
   - D. How many blue lights you see flashing in your rear view mirror.

19. THE MOST IMPORTANT VEHICLE CONTROL TO USE DURING A SKID IS:
   - A. The brakes.
   - **B. The steering wheel.**
   - C. The emergency brake.
   - D. Your cell phone.

20. ALMOST ANY DRUG CAN AFFECT YOUR DRIVING ABILITIES. THIS IS TRUE OF:
   - A. Illegal drugs only.
   - B. Illegal and prescription drugs, but not over-the-counter medications.
   - **C. All drugs including those you can buy with or without a prescription.**

21. IF YOU REFUSE TO TAKE A CHEMICAL TEST AFTER BEING ARRESTED FOR DRIVING WHILE UNDER THE INFLUENCE OF INTOXICATING BEVERAGES OR OTHER SUBSTANCES WHICH MAY IMPAIR ONE'S ABILITY (DUI) YOUR LICENSE MAY BE REVOKED BY LAW:
   - **A. Because you refuse to take the chemical test even though you are subject to the "implied consent" law.**
   - B. Because it is your first DUI offense.
   - C. Only if they can prove you were drunk without the test.
   - D. Only if they find an open alcoholic beverage bottle hidden in your pockets during the search.
   - Source note: the document includes a parenthetical explanation of Kentucky implied-consent law.

22. IF YOU COME TO A CROSSWALK WITH PEDESTRIANS YOU MUST:
   - A. Stop only if the pedestrians are on the street and in your path of travel.
   - **B. Yield to the pedestrians and allow them to cross before proceeding.**
   - C. Blow your horn and proceed when they get out of the way.
   - D. Don't make eye contact and proceed quickly before they have a chance to cross.

23. IF YOU ARE DRIVING AND COME UPON SOMEONE IN YOUR LANE RIDING A BICYCLE, YOU SHOULD:
   - A. Blow your horn and yell at them to get out of the way.
   - B. Flash your lights and signal to them you want to pass.
   - **C. Share the road and treat the bicyclist as you would treat any other vehicle.**
   - D. Report the bicyclist to local law enforcement.

24. WHEN FOLLOWING ANOTHER VEHICLE IN RAINY CONDITIONS YOU SHOULD:
   - A. Follow closely to improve your visibility of the other vehicle's signals.
   - **B. Increase your space cushion to allow more distance between you and the other vehicle.**
   - C. Put your high-beam lights on to let them know you are there.

25. WHEN FOLLOWING LARGE VEHICLES THAT BLOCK YOUR VIEW AHEAD, YOU NEED:
   - **A. A greater following distance.**
   - B. A shorter following distance.
   - C. Bigger side view mirrors.
