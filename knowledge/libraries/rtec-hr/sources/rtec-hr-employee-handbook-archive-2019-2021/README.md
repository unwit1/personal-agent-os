# RTEC Employee Handbook Archive: 2019 and 2021 Copies

This folder preserves four position-level handbook artifacts recovered from historical work-mail attachments: two distinct copies of a handbook printed July 15, 2019, and a document printed July 19, 2021 whose correspondence calls it revised. All four originals are unchanged. Their complete searchable text mirrors are in `fulltext/`; the 2019 pair has a paragraph-level comparison.

## Review findings

- The two 2019 DOCX copies have distinct hashes. Both have five Word sections, no tracked insertions/deletions, and no comments part. Their paragraph-level differences include more than pagination: the Jan 3 copy changes funeral-leave wording; adds work-related accident as an excused-absence category; changes cafeteria food-handling wording and adds a food-handler-card requirement; adds an off-duty-conduct work rule; corrects vehicle/property recording notice wording; and moves the employee-notes page. The Jan 3 email says ?Please post on website,? which establishes a posting request but not completion.
- The 2021 DOCX has five Word sections and no comments part. It contains 66 tracked insertions and 303 tracked deletions, including duplicate/obsolete table-of-contents material and substantive edits in personal/sick leave and attendance. Its searchable mirror marks insertions and deletions inline. The Oct 2021 correspondence says it should replace the website copy, but the document's tracked edits remain unresolved.
- A separate 48-page 2021 PDF was emailed in September 2022 with the instruction that it was the correct handbook for the website. It has extractable text on all pages; all-page contact sheets and pages 15, 24, 25, and 48 were visually reviewed. Its PDF creation date is July 22, 2021, before the DOCX tracked edits from Sep/Oct 2021. It retains the two-day physician-note wording and no-fault attendance wording, while showing struck older point values 10/90 and displayed values 6/60 on page 25. Other wording remains mixed. The 2022 website-copy email does not explain the mismatch, so the PDF and DOCX are linked as conflicting historical evidence.
- The 2021 DOCX redline includes proposed wording for a third-day physician statement, attendance point threshold 10 to 6, point removal after 90 to 60 consecutive days, a partial-day threshold change, and three unpaid days with no points. These are revision evidence, not validated adopted policy.
- The handbooks cover employment terms, work schedules/pay, benefits, leave, attendance, complaints, equal opportunity, harassment, dress and role-specific guidance, safety, confidentiality, discipline, communications, nepotism/fraternization, surveillance, and a certificate of receipt. Consult the complete sources for details.

## Temporal and authority cautions

These are pre-April-2026 company records, not evidence of the user's personal work history. No effective date is established for these copies. Later complete handbook archives from March 2023 and December 2025 are present in the corpus; later is not by itself proof of currentness for every section. Treat these copies as historical evidence and verify the applicable policy version before operational use.

Full text has been extracted for all four artifacts, and structural/version-difference review is complete. Clause-by-clause review of every provision remains in progress. No raw email bodies or employee/applicant case records are included. Position-level source documents are preserved under the full-source rule in the ingestion contract.
