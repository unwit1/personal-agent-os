# Customer Service Representative memo - do not direct clients to call State

Source ID: `rtec-hr-csr-do-not-call-state-memo-2015-06-02`

**Date:** June 2, 2015  
**To:** Customer Service Representatives  
**From:** Human Resources

The memorandum states that some CSRs were still telling clients to call "State," despite this having been addressed previously in a staff meeting. It instructs that there is no reason clients should be told to call State and that CSRs should make every effort to assist clients as much as possible.

Signed by Sheila Stallsworth, Human Resource Department.

## Interpretation boundary

The memo does not define precisely what entity "State" refers to or document escalation exceptions. It is preserved as historical customer-service workflow evidence.
