## Two kinds of Medicare Supplement plans

### Medigap

- higher premiums but lower deductibles and copays
- No networks, you can see any provider in the US who accepts Medicare (98% of doctors in US)
- Does not include Part D (Prescription) coverage.
- Requires Health Underwriting (you can be denied coverage due to preexisting conditions)

### Medicare Advantage

- Low/zero premiums but higher OOP costs up to OOP max
- Usually uses HMO or PPO networks
- Usually includes Part D coverage (Prescription)
