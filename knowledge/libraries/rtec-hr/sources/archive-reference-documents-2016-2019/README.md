# RTEC Driver Handbooks and Historical Leave Excerpt (2016?2019)

This source group preserves two complete RTEC driver-handbook DOCX copies and one one-page handbook excerpt. Original files are unchanged; searchable mirrors are under `fulltext/`.

## Contents and provenance

- `Employee-Driver-Handbook-2017-Revised-Through-2016.docx`: cover is marked 2017 and revision history runs through 2016; archive attachment filename is dated December 30, 2016.
- `RTEC-Driver-Handbook-2019-Draft1.docx`: cover is marked 2019, revision history runs through 2019, and the archive filename identifies it as draft1. No approval or effective date is established.
- `RTEC-Personal-Sick-Leave-Handbook-Excerpt-Printed-Page-13.pdf`: one scanned page, visually read and transcribed. It has an ambiguous `10/11` header, printed page 13, and only the beginning of the annual-leave section. It is not a complete handbook or standalone policy.

## Review findings

The driver manuals cover service and rider context, driver duties, dispatch coordination, training/testing, vehicle use, safety, confidentiality, conduct, and a transit-driver job description. Both contain a blank confirmation-of-receipt section with unfilled name, signature, and date lines. Their wording differs; the 2019 document remains a draft and no form identity equivalence is asserted.

The scanned excerpt states a 90-day employment threshold for Personal/Sick Leave and includes an incomplete annual-leave paragraph. The later 2019 handbook says 30 days; the 2025 handbook also says 30 days. The old page is retained as historical evidence. Its ambiguous date and one-page scope do not establish a current or effective rule.

A contemporaneous 2016 position-level email records that the leave-policy page was shared during time-and-attendance module discussions. The correspondence is summarized without retaining its raw body or contact details.

## Scope boundary

These are pre-April-2026 company sources and are not evidence of the user's personal work history. External-sample handbooks and person-level hiring or employee files are not included in this RTEC source group. Full text is extracted, while clause-by-clause review of the manuals remains in progress.
