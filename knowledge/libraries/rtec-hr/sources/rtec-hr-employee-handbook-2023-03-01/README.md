The handbook covers employment status and complaints; equal opportunity; pay and time; benefits; leave; attendance; harassment; role-specific dress and safety rules; confidentiality; progressive discipline; communications systems; nepotism/fraternization; and audio/video surveillance. Its last page is a handbook receipt certificate.
