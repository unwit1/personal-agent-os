DRUG AND ALCOHOL FREE WORKPLACE ACT Cooperative Agreement

Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated; Inc: (d/b/a RTEC), in order to maintain its transportation system in safe and efficient manner for the public and to provide a safe work environment for RTEC employees, hereby adopts this cooperative agreement between the Corporation and its emplovees. This agreement applies to all RTEC emplovees

Just as RTEC has a responsibility to its employees to provide a safe work environment; each employee has an individual responsibility to the public - to deliver services in a safe and conscientious manner In order to achieve as safe a job performance as possible, employees must be able to work in drug-free environment and themselves be free from the effects of alcohol and other job-impairing substances_

RTEC, because of its contracts with Federal agencies, is subject to the requirements of the Drug-Free Workplace Act of 1988.   Emplovees of RTEC who perform services pursuant to these contracts are also subject to the ACT. You are one of the emplovees who perform these services   Under the Federal Drug- Free Workplace Act, in order for RTEC to be considered a "responsible source" for the award of Federal contracts, the following policy is established:

All employees are absolutely prohibited from unlawfully manufacturing, distributing, dispensing, possessing, or using controlled substances or alcohol in the workplace, while on RTEC property, or while performing duties in the scope of their employment with RTEC Any RTEC employee determined to have violated this policy is guilty of misconduct and will be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including the immediate termination of employment In orderto comply with Federal law, RTEC requires that an employee notify the Executive Director ofany criminal drug statute conviction or alcohol related law violation whether occurring during working or non-working hours no later than five (S) days after such conviction: Failure to report such a conviction will subject the employee to disciplinary action, up to and including immediate termination_
