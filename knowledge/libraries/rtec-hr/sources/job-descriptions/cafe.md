Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.

                               Job Description
                             Cafeteria Assistant

Job Details  Job Type:   Full Time


Under the  general  supervision  of  the  Cafeteria  Manager,  restocks  and
replenishes food service supplies, cleans and assists Manager in all  facets
of Cafeteria operation.  Ability to attend  work  as  needed  on  a  regular
basis and work extra hours if needed.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES/RESPONSIBILITIES

Clears all used tableware, napkins and refuse from tables  to  tubs  in  bus
station and wipes tables, table legs, chairs, chair legs and  clears  debris
and crumbs from floor areas surrounding tables.

Maintains a clean, well-stocked and orderly cafeteria at all times in  order
to improve efficiency and productivity.

Retrieves  cleaned  dishes,  glassware  and  utensils  from  dish  room  and
distributes to service station in order to keep  a  ready  supply  available
for cafeteria use.

Sweep floors, cleans counters & equipment throughout  shift  to  maintain  a
clean, presentable and attractive establishment.  Mop floors at the  end  of
each shift.  Follow all Health Department standards.

Retrieves  requisitioned  items  from  stockroom,  ensuring  requisition  is
complete and correct, and  stocking  supplies  in  proper  places,  rotating
stock at all times.

Assist with orders, serving and cash register as required.

Check all restrooms periodically to be sure they are clean and supplies  are
adequate.

Provide assistance to other employees and departments to contribute  to  the
best overall performance of the food service operation.

Job Requirements:  Must have  previous  food  service  experience  including
cooking and preparing food and  possess  a  food  handler’s  license.   Must
understand proper food service,  health  department  rules  and  regulations
provided by food handler’s class. Minimum:  High School diploma or GED.

Physical Requirements:  Stretching, walking, bending and stooping  to  reach
supplies, lifting and carrying up to 50 pounds.

Hours:   7:00 AM - 3:30 PM (Subject to change)

Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.

Job Description:        Food Service Manager
                 FLSA Classification:   NON-EXEMPT

Characteristics of the Class; Under the general supervision of
Administrative Support.

Requirements and Qualifications:

Minimum - High  School  Education  with  supervisory  background  and  good
computer skills. Knowledge of contemporary office practices and procedures.
Ability to maintain good working relationship with  the  public  and  other
employees.  Must  have  good   telephone   and   communication   etiquette.
Cooperative and have initiative to be a self-starter. Be a team player  and
show courtesy to other employees. Ability  to  resolve  problems  and  make
decisions safely and quickly. Ability to work flexible hours and as needed.
Must be able to  supervise  employees  while  maintaining  a  good  working
relationship with them.

Physical requirements  for  this  position  includes  walking,  stretching,
bending and stooping; lifting and carrying up  to  40  pounds.  Ability  to
attend work as needed and on a regular basis.

Duties and Responsibilities:

    • Set up and maintain Food Service Operation
    • Establish varied Menu with emphasis on fast service and quality
      product.
    • Assign work details as needed to shift employees
    • Train personnel on an ongoing basis for operating procedures and food
      handling.
    • Evaluate employees on work performance
    • Track daily absenteeism
    • Schedule and monitor lunch and ten (10) minute breaks of employees.
    • Maintain standards as required by the Health Department.
    • Assist and coordinate, as needed, with meal periods.
    • Establish Inventory of Food supplies and re-order as required.
    • Responsible for operating within budget guidelines


      Any other assignment provided by the  Administrative  Support  Manager
      and/or Executive Director

      Salary based upon experience
