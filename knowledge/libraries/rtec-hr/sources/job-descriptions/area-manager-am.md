Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.

Job Description: Area Manager

FLSA Classification: Non-Exempt Full Time

Characieristics of the Class:

Under the general supervision of the Operations Manager, Area Managers are safety sensitive employees
that will control the movement of revenue service vehicles.

Positions Supervised:

Drivers in the assigned area.

Requirements and Qualifications:

Minimum requirements include a High School Education having professional supervisory experience.
Must have good working knowledge of computers and vehicle operations. Must have valid Kentucky
Driver’s License. Must have upon employment and maintain a clear criminal background check and good
driving record. Must have knowledge of contemporary office practices and procedures. Good telephone
etiquette and communications skills in the English language required. Cooperative, have initiative, able
to resolve problems and make decisions safely and appropriately. Comply with RTEC, Federal, State and
local regulations and Drug and Alcohol Program policies.

Skills and Abilities:

Essential requirements for this position is good visual and hearing (with or without corrective devises),
the ability to sit for long periods of time with high concentration levels while scheduling transportation
services, working with time records, and various transit statistics (i.e., vehicle costs, gallons of fuel, costs,
rides, hours). Physical requirements include walking, stretching, bending and stooping to reach files;
lifting and carrying up to 30 pounds as the standard rule for copying, filing and storing records in an
office environment. Travel required using Company vehicles. Ability to attend work as needed and on a
regular basis.

Duties and Responsibilities:

The duties and responsibilities of the Area Manager include, but are not limited to:

1. Responsible for implementing and maintaining RTEC'’s transit operation in the assigned area,

2. Report incident and accident reports, including testing guidelines and facilities, record retention,
3. Assure RTEC's compliance with state and federal regulations of transportation and contracts,

4, Participate in interviews, recommend for hire, supervise, retain, monitor and evaluate drivers for

the area supervised. Communicate problems to the Operations Manager,
5. Train, supervise and evaluate Drivers. Daily scheduling. Route information to other departments
as needed. Finalize monthly reports on the Sth of each month,

6. Coordinate with Fleet Manager maintenance problems, breakdowns and services,

a. Responsible for coordination and efficiency of transportation services,

8. Document/evaluate/follow-up and report in writing all complaints involving the area supervised,
9. Any other assignment provided by the Executive Director.

Probation: Ninety (90) days to be proficient in duties and responsibilities.

Salary - $12.84 per hour
