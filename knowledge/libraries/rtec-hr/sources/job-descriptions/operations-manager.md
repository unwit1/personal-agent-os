Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.
Job Description:   Operations Manager
FLSA Classification:  Exempt – Full Time
Characteristics of the Class:
Under the general supervision of the Executive Director, the Operations Manager is a safety sensitive employee that will control the movement of revenue service vehicles.
Positions Supervised:
Area Managers, Dispatchers and CSR’s
Requirements and Qualifications:
The Operations Manager must have a minimum of a high school diploma supplemented by five years’ experience in public transportation and in a supervisory role; or a Bachelor’s Degree in Business Administration.  Must have good working knowledge of computers, particularly using Excel.  Knowledgeable in vehicle operations and preventive maintenance program.  Must have valid Kentucky Driver’s License.  Must have upon employment and maintain a clear criminal background check and good driving record.  Must have knowledge of contemporary office practices and procedures.  Good telephone etiquette and communication skills in the English language required.  Pleasant personality and the ability to get along well with the public. Cooperative, have initiative, able to resolve problems and make decisions safely and appropriately.  Comply with RTEC, Federal, State and local regulations, Drug and Alcohol Program and other company policies.  Must have a home telephone.
Skills and Abilities:
Essential requirements for this position are good visual and hearing (with or without corrective devises), the ability to sit for long periods of time with high concentration levels while working with reports.  Physical requirements include walking, stretching, bending and stooping to reach files; lifting and carrying up to 30 pounds as the standard rule for copying, filing and storing records in an office environment.  Must have the ability to supervise workers in the Operations Department.  Must maintain a valid Kentucky Driver’s License, Clear police background check and participate in Drug and Alcohol Program.  Must have ability to use communications systems appropriately.  Must be adaptable to change and manage conflicts in a calm and business-like manner.  Travel required using Company vehicles.  Ability to attend work as needed on a regular basis.
Duties and Responsibilities:
The Duties of the Operations Manager include but are not limited to:
Responsible for day-to-day operation of the transit program.
Supervise workers in the Operations Department, including Area Managers, Dispatchers and CSR’s.
Responsible for interviewing, recommending for hiring of all positions supervised, following up with performance evaluations and discipline measures with full documentation and the Executive Director’s approval.
Responsible for monitoring and analysis of drivers, routes and schedules, including all necessary documentation.
Monitor vehicle maintenance of all RTEC vehicles.
Investigate all accidents and incidents to ensure program compliance.
Collect and compile monthly reports by the 5th of the following month.
Assure that adequate records are being maintained on the Department’s Personnel, vehicle, and operations.
Remain knowledgeable of company policies and procedures and delegate duties, as appropriate.
Attend conferences, training sessions and meetings as required by the Executive Director.
Responsible for petty cash.
Any other duties assigned by the Executive Director.
These duties have been explained in detail.
Signature _____________________________________   Date: ______________ 
Probation:  Ninety (90) days to be proficient in duties and responsibilities.
Salary commensurate with education and experience.

Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.


                              JOB DESCRIPTIONS

Position:   Operations Manager

Education:  The Operations Manager must have a minimum of a high school
           diploma supplemented by five years’ experience in public
           transportation; or a high school diploma supplemented by three
           years’ experience in a professional management position; or
           Bachelor’s Degree in Business Administration.

Qualifications:  The Operations Manager must have good oral and written
           communication skills, the ability to get along well with the
           public, having a pleasant personality, and the ability to
           supervise workers in the Operations Department.  Working
           knowledge of computers, particularly using Excel spreadsheets
           for reports.

Requirements:    Must maintain a valid Kentucky Driver’s License,
           Clear police background check
           Participate in Drug and Alcohol Program
           Must have ability to use communications systems appropriately,
           Must be adaptable to change,
           Must have ability to delegate,
           Must be able to manage conflicts in a calm and business-like
           manner,
           Must be knowledgeable in vehicle operations and preventive
           maintenance program.

Supervisor: The Operations Manager is directly responsible to the Executive
Director.
Duties:          The Duties of the Operations Manager include but are not
limited to:

1. Supervise workers in the Operations Department, including Area Managers,
   Dispatchers & CSRs.
2. Assure that the drivers receive appropriate training for the safe
   operation of public transportation.
3. Supervise the Operations Department and oversee all transit operating
   activities.
4. Collect and compile monthly reports by the 5th of the following month.
5. Assure that adequate records are being maintained on the Department’s
   Personnel, vehicle, and operation.
6. Remain knowledgeable of company policies and procedures and delegate
   duties, as appropriate.
7. Responsible for petty cash.
8. Attend conferences, training sessions and meetings as required by the
   Executive Director.
9. Any other duties assigned by the Executive Director.


Signature: __________________________________Date: ________________
