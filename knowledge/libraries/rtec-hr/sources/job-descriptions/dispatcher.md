Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.
            02/18


                              JOB DESCRIPTIONS

Position Title:  Dispatcher (Transportation Technician) – Relief Operator

Qualifications:  A high school diploma, or equivalency
           Minimum of two (2) years’ transit (or related field) experience,
           Good computer skills,
           Ability to get along well with the public,
           Good communication skills both oral and written,
           Familiar with RTEC service area

Requirements:    Willing to work flexible hours and as needed basis,
            Maintain a valid Kentucky Driver’s license,
            Proper telephone and communications etiquette a must,
            Ability to be trained in transit procedures.
           Safety sensitive positions controlling the movement of revenue
           rolling stock; must participate in the pre-employment and
           ongoing substance abuse testing and drug and alcohol free
           workplace.

Supervisor: Operations Manager

Duties:          Shall include, but are not limited to:

1. Assist the Operations Manager as directed,
2. Schedule and dispatch transportation services in a safe and efficient
   manner,
3. Maintain daily transportation and passenger records,
4. Document driver daily hours,
5. Knowledge of proper paperwork procedures,
6. Provide daily instructions to drivers,
7. Scheduling for service area,
8. Any other assignment provided by the Operations Manager, or by the
   Executive Director.
