At RTEC, we provide assistance in transportation to the needs of our passengers. There are various services that we offer for passengers to support their needs. These services include: cash trips for shopping, training, appointments, and employment; medical trips for Medicaid recipients and Veterans. 
Rural Transit Enterprise Coordinated, Inc. (RTEC) is hiring Full-Time Transit Drivers, starting pay $16.00 per hour and $16.50 per hour for CDL’s, which are not required. Dispatchers starting pay $16.50 per hour, and Customer Service Representatives (CSR) starting pay $16.00 per hour, must have a High School Diploma or equivalency (GED) and data entry experience. Bilingual or Spanish a plus. RTEC matches up to 9% percent on 401K and gives frequent raises. RTEC has great Health, Vision and Dental benefits. After 1 year of employment you will receive 5 days of vacation, after 2 years you will receive 10 days of vacation, after 10 years you will receive 15 days of vacation and after 15 years you will receive 20 days of vacation. RTEC operates a drug and alcohol free workplace and is an equal opportunity employer.
​
RTEC has immediate Customer Service Representative (CSR) and Dispatcher openings in the Mt. Vernon office. Immediate openings for Transit Drivers in the following counties: Adair, Clinton, Cumberland, Laurel, McCreary, Monroe, Pulaski, Russell, Taylor, Wayne and Whitley.

Rural Transit Enterprise Coordinated, Inc.
Job Description: Receptionist
FLSA Classification: Non-exempt
Characteristics of the Class: Under the general supervision of Executive Director or Designee 
Requirements and Qualifications:
Greet vendors, customers, job applicants and other visitors. Direct visitors by maintaining employee and department directories. May operate a switchboard to route incoming calls and place outgoing calls. Maintain security by following procedures. Monitoring logbooks.
Desirable Qualifications: 
Training and Experience: High School diploma/equivalency (GED), or minimum of 2 years’ formal experience in similar position or related field.
Special Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities: Knowledge of contemporary office practices, procedures, and equipment. Knowledge of business English, spelling, punctuation, and arithmetic. Knowledge of the functions, organization, procedures, rules, and regulations of the agencies involved. Ability to maintain records and documents to prepare accurate reports. Ability to establish and maintain effective working relationships with officials, employees, customers and the general public. Good telephone etiquette a must. A good typing speed a must. Ability to timely enter data into the computer. Ability to be bonded. Tact, patience, thoroughness, accuracy, honesty, orderliness, and sound judgement. Physical requirements include stretching, bending and stooping to reach files, lifting and carrying up to 30 pounds as the standard rule for copying, filing and storing records in an office environment.
Duties and Responsibilities:
Greet clients as soon as they arrive and connect them with the appropriate party
Answer the phone in a timely manner and direct calls to the correct offices
Take and pass on messages to office staff
Answer telephone calls and take messages or forward calls
Check visitors in and direct or escort them to specific destinations
Inform other employees of visitors’ arrivals or cancellations
Sorts and logs the mail/makes copies of checks received in the mail and deposits in bank
Login faxes and distributes to appropriate departments
Makes copies and hands out job applications
