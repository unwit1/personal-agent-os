Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.


Job Description:   Assistant Information Systems Technician FLSA
Classification: Exempt

Characteristics of the Class: Under general supervision of the Executive
Director or her designee and does related work as required.

DESIRABLE QUALIFICATIONS:
Associate Degree in computer science or two years’ professional experience.
Experienced in software skills (i.e. database, spreadsheet, word
processors, etc.).  Experienced in multiple-vendor hardware/software.
Proficient in writing technical documentation clearly and concisely. Strong
knowledge of, Microsoft Windows/SQL Server, and  SQL Scripting.

KNOWLEDGES, SKILLS, AND ABILITIES:
Knowledge of contemporary office practices and procedures, ability to get
along well with and communicate with employees and computer contractors, be
cooperative, have good communication skills and be a team player. A
thorough understanding of RTEC's policies and procedures.
   1. Knowledge of basic information processing and terminology.
   2. Knowledge of basic data processing principles.
   3. Knowledge of process control statements and ability to read and
      understand process control statements.
   4. Knowledge of proper lifting and bending techniques and ability to lift
      50lbs.
   5. Ability to diagnose hardware and software problems.
   6. Ability to read and interpret policies, manuals and instructions.
   7. Ability to gather and analyze data and prepare reports.
   8. Ability to organize and prioritize work assignments.
   9. Ability to communicate effectively with coworkers and clients.
  10. Ability to withstand noise from computer equipment, glare from
      terminal screens and cool temperatures.


EXAMPLE OF DUTIES:
To install, maintain, and troubleshoot standard PC hardware and software;
resolve the majority of user and system problems; perform related work.
Responsible for providing technical support, including, maintaining
computer hardware and software, telephone system, surveillance equipment
buses and in facilities, AVL/MDT, IVR, voicemail, and miscellaneous agency
information infrastructure needs. Keep computers operative and networking,
maintain company's Dictaphones, closed circuit tv, computers, radios,
transmitters, etc. and department inventory. Provide monthly and quarterly
activity reports to the Executive Director. These duties do not necessarily
include all essential functions of this job.  Attendance is required.

It is understood that all software purchased by RTEC and computer programs
created under this project remains the property of RTEC and shall not be
distributed or copied or used wholly or in part outside this project
without the prior written consent of the Executive Director.
