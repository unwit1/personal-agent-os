Rural Transit Enterprise Coordinated, Inc.
Job Description: Receptionist
FLSA Classification: Non-exempt
Characteristics of the Class: Under the general supervision of Executive Director or Designee 
Requirements and Qualifications:
Greet vendors, customers, job applicants and other visitors. Direct visitors by maintaining employee and department directories. May operate a switchboard to route incoming calls and place outgoing calls. Maintain security by following procedures. Monitoring logbooks.
Desirable Qualifications: 
Training and Experience: High School diploma/equivalency (GED), or minimum of 2 years’ formal experience in similar position or related field.
Special Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities: Knowledge of contemporary office practices, procedures, and equipment. Knowledge of business English, spelling, punctuation, and arithmetic. Knowledge of the functions, organization, procedures, rules, and regulations of the agencies involved. Ability to maintain records and documents to prepare accurate reports. Ability to establish and maintain effective working relationships with officials, employees, customers and the general public. Good telephone etiquette a must. A good typing speed a must. Ability to timely enter data into the computer. Ability to be bonded. Tact, patience, thoroughness, accuracy, honesty, orderliness, and sound judgement. Physical requirements include stretching, bending and stooping to reach files, lifting and carrying up to 30 pounds as the standard rule for copying, filing and storing records in an office environment.
Duties and Responsibilities:
Greet clients as soon as they arrive and connect them with the appropriate party
Answer the phone in a timely manner and direct calls to the correct offices
Take and pass on messages to office staff
Answer telephone calls and take messages or forward calls
Check visitors in and direct or escort them to specific destinations
Inform other employees of visitors’ arrivals or cancellations
Sorts and logs the mail
Login faxes and distributes to appropriate departments
Makes copies and hands out job applications

Rural Transit Enterprise Coordinated, Inc.
POSITION DESCRIPTION
CLASS TITLE: Fiscal Clerk/Telephone Receptionist
CHARACTERISTICS OF THE CLASS: Under general direction of the Executive Director and does related work as required.
EXAMPLE OF DUTIES: Provides a variety of clerical duties, responsible for transferring information from driver logs and other records to claim forms, including passenger trips and mileage for billing purposes, timely and accurately. Collects and records the funds of RTEC for passenger fares and other accounts. Answers telephone calls and greets visitors, ascertains nature of business and directs caller or visitor to the appropriate party, makes and cancels appointments by telephone including adequate description of directions to passenger’s home or appointment. Does monthly report of cash/check deposits for the office of the executive director. These duties do not necessarily include all essential functions of this job. 
DESIRABLE QUALIFICATIONS
Training and Experience: High School diploma/equivalency (GED), or minimum of 2 years’ formal experience in similar position or related field.
Special knowledge, Skills, and Abilities: Knowledge of contemporary office practices, procedures, and equipment. Knowledge of business English, spelling, punctuation, and arithmetic. Knowledge of the functions, organization, procedures, rules, and regulations of the agencies involved.  Ability to maintain records and documents to prepare accurate reports. Ability to communicate effectively, orally and in writing. Ability to establish and maintain effective working relationships with officials, employees, customers and the general public. Good telephone etiquette a must. Ability to be bonded. Tact, patience, thoroughness, accuracy, honesty, orderliness, and sound judgement.

Responsibilities for Receptionist
Answer and direct phone calls in a polite and friendly manner
Welcome visitors in a warm and friendly manner, and answer any questions visitors have
Maintain reception area and all common areas in a clean and tidy manner at all times
Operate standard office equipment on a regular basis, including a fax machine, a copy machine, and a computer
Keep detailed and accurate records of visitor requests and of calls received
Receive deliveries; sort and distribute incoming mail
Take inventory of supplies and restock as needed
Maintain the general office filing system
Qualifications for Receptionist
High school diploma or general education degree (GED) required
2-3 years of relevant experience in an office environment
Proficient in Microsoft Office
Able to type 35 wpm minimum
Strong phone skills
Demonstrated ability to read, write, and speak English
Comfortable multi-tasking and prioritizing tasks without guidance
Excellent interpersonal skills
Punctual with strong attendance history
