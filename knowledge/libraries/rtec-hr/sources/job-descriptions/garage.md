Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.
Position Description, Class Title: Automotive Mechanic
Characteristics of the Class:
Responsible for mechanical work in the maintenance and repair of RTEC’s automotive
equipment, Work involves responsibility for the performance of skilled tasks in repairing
automobiles, vans, maintenance equipment and the records of work performed. Must
diagnose, disassemble, reassemble, repair and install to manufactures specifications: All
Mechanical, Air, Hydraulics, Electronic, and Electrical Equipment.
Requires cooperation with fellow workers, coordinating activities with the dispatcher of
the RTEC fleet, and having a sincere concern for the safety of persons and property in
maintenance of the vehicles.
Examples of Duties:
Ability to maintain fleet vehicles. Does general overhaul and repair work of all RTEC
owned automotive equipment, including automobiles, vans, and specialized equipment
with gasoline or diesel engines. Job will include welding. Maintain shop area and tools
in a clean and orderly condition. Make road tests to locate defects in equipment
operation, and perform a check on repair work before the return of equipment to active
service by testing vehicles through test equipment and knowledge of vehicle operation.
Prepare records of work performed on equipment. Perform related work as required.
Other duties as assigned by the Executive Director.
Qualifications:

Training and Experience: Thorough training in the repair and maintenance of
automotive equipment; High School Diploma or equivalent, 2 years formal experience
in mechanics. ASE certifications preferred.

Special Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities: Thorough knowledge of the standard
practices and equipment used in the automotive mechanic trade; Knowledge of the
principles and practices of records maintenance; Thorough knowledge of the
occupational hazards and safety precautions of the trade; Thorough knowledge of the
principles of operations of gasoline and diesel engines, and of mechanical repair methods
applicable to automobiles and vans; Skill in the use and care of tools and equipment 7
employed in all phases of automotive repair and maintenance; Ability to keep accurate
records and prepare reports. Experience in vehicle security cameras and MDC/AVL
(Mobile Data Computers and Automatic vehicle locators) a plus.

The Fleet Mechanic will receive daily instructions from the Fleet Manager.
The base rate of pay for this job will commensurate with experience and certifications.

Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.

Job Description:  Fleet Manager and Superintendent of Facilities
FLSA Classifications:  Exempt – Full Time

Qualifications:  High School Diploma or Equivalent.  Minimum 2 yrs. Vehicle
                 and building maintenance/supervisory experience.

Requirements:    Valid KY Driver’s license.  This is a safety sensitive
                 position requiring participation in pre-employment and
                 ongoing substance abuse testing and drug and alcohol free
                 workplace.  Must pass the DOT physical examination.  Must
                 have a good working knowledge of computers. Willing to work
                 flexible hours. Physical requirements include bending,
                 stooping; lifting and carrying up to 50 pounds.

Supervisor: Executive Director or designee

Duties:     The duties of the Fleet Manager include, but are not limited
                 to:

    1. Supervise workers in the Maintenance Department, including the
       mechanics.
    2. Collect and compile monthly operating expenses and travel reports
       for each vehicle and submit report to the Executive Director by the
       5th of the following month.
    3. Implement and maintain a computerized vehicle maintenance program
       for the RTEC fleet.
    4. Assure that the vehicles receive appropriate maintenance and
       preventive maintenance to ensure their safe operation.
    5. Oversee renovation and regular maintenance of facilities, including
       office, garage, etc.
    6. Light building/vehicle maintenance when deemed appropriate.
    7. Purchase products and services in an efficient manner through a
       State Pricing Contract, or by soliciting bids when necessary with
       purchase orders and an approved budget, under direction of the
       Executive Director.
    8. Maintain inventory of equipment and supplies for the operations
       department.
    9. Assure back-up vehicles are in place (Garage & Counties) to minimize
       downtime.
   10. Provide cost analysis on parts and repairs (in house or locally).
       Knowledge of procurement, price estimating, requisite and purchase
       orders a plus.
   11. Compile and maintain Vehicle specs and submit to Purchasing
   12. Any other assignment provided by the Executive Director.
   13. Must be a team player, cooperate with all co-workers in a
       professional manner and be cost effective.


Salary based upon qualifications
90 days to be proficient in duties and responsibilities.

Job Description

RTEC Garage Attendant

Requires a fully proficient garage attendant capable of performing minor
routine service work on motor vehicles, equipment, grounds and facilities,
including but not limited to the following:



        a. Routine general shop clean-up as required.
        b. Pick-up and delivery of parts and motor vehicles as required.
        c. Replacing head-lamps, light bulbs, wiper blades, enter changes
           into computer.
        d. Monitor the storage of hazardous garage waste receptacles,
           notifying Company when receptacles are full and require removal.
        e. Apply and removes stripes and decals from motor vehicles.
        f. Routine cleaning and washing company vehicles.
        g. General workshop duties, including assisting other personnel as
           required.
        h. Light carpentry/construction facility repairs.
        i. Mowing, weed-eating and landscaping.
        j. Ability to work independently.
        k. Other duties assigned by Fleet manager or Executive director.

Job requirements

The garage attendant/driver must have  a  valid  Kentucky  driver’s  license
with classification as deemed necessary by local  requirements  and  a  five
year driving history.  Must be able to push, pull, stoop, bend, and be  able
to lift up to fifty (50) pounds.  High School Diploma or  GED  is  required.
This is a safety sensitive position and participation in the Drug &  Alcohol
free program is required.  The garage attendant/driver will be  required  to
demonstrate acceptable performance during a 90-day probationary period.

Starting rate of pay is $17.00 per hour.
