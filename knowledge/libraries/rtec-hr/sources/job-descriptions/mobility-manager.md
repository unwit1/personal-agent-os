Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.

Job Description: Mobility Manager
FLSA Classification: Exempt

Scope of work:

 The Mobility Manager in a transportation organization serves the general
public through conceptualization, planning, developing and operating
programs that respond to and influence the demands of the market.  These
actions and supportive strategies are performed directly or in
collaboration with others in order to provide a full range of travel
options that are more effective in meeting needs and more efficient through
reasonable pricing.

This position is responsible to improve business and community support for
the transportation organization.  It will require the development and
distribution of information that explains how to utilize the available
resources in meeting the diverse travel needs of the market it serves.

Characteristics of the Class:

The Mobility Manager is a member of the administrative office, under the
general direction of the Executive Director/CEO (or her designee) and
performs such duties as required for assisting in carrying out Public
Transit, Intercity, and other project grant requirements in compliance with
federal, state and local laws.  As an ambassador of RTEC, the Mobility
Manager coordinates activities toward good working relations for the best
interest of the company, its customers and employees.

Requirements and Qualifications:

A Bachelor’s Degree; or Associate Degree, with experience in a formal
office setting and having held a responsible position in Management for a
minimum of 5 years in service planning, accessible services, human services
transportation coordination or a related field.  Must have a geographic
knowledge of RTEC’s service area.  Must have good oral and written
communication skills; good leadership skills, the ability to read, write,
type and comprehend the English language; good telephone etiquette; and
have an excellent working knowledge of computers and contemporary office
practices. An essential requirement for this position is the ability to sit
for long periods of time with high concentration levels while working with
various productions and efficiency.   Physical requirements include
stretching, bending and stooping to reach files and inspection of vehicles;
lifting and carrying up to 50 pounds as the standard rule for copying,
filing and storing records in an office environment.  Must be flexible and
acceptable to change, since it is impossible to list each project and all
of the requirements that may be associated with the programs of RTEC that
may vary during each grant/fiscal year.  Attendance and travel is required.

Essential job functions:  Below is a list of actions that are required in
the fulfillment of the duties:

   Develops and directs the design, production and distribution of specific
   marketing materials directed at employers, employees, human service
   agencies and other entities;
   Serves as the liaison/salesperson to community leaders in an effort to
   demonstrate how transportation enhances economic development;
   Provides direct outreach to area employers and employment agencies to
   gain support for employer and employee transit programs;
   Researches, develops and writes grant application for future funding;
   Develops potential for future expansion of transit options across
   municipal boundaries;
   Plans and coordinates special promotional events and activities related
   to general public transportation;
   Makes public presentations on the benefits of coordinating Transit
   Projects for the community;
   Builds supportive community networks;
   Leads in the design of operational functions that are nontraditional in
   service delivery;
   Is familiar with technological advances that increase travel options
   and/or convenience;
   Is knowledgeable about techniques that foster transit ridership through
   links with land development.

Job Summary:

Responsible for management of Public Transit, Intercity, and other projects
assuring all aspects of contracts are monitored.  Manages project to assure
quality in service.  This includes periodic visits to monitor
transportation to assure understanding of transportation issues and solve
problems that may arise. Maintain, check and enforce compliance of trips,
including provider compliance with all contractual agreed items such as
timeliness, vehicle cleanliness, record keeping, etc.  Adhere to the Drug
and Alcohol policy.  Assure training requirements and all trips in program
software system.  Prepare invoices and monthly reports and submit to
Executive Director for payment approval.  Receive and assist in resolution
of complaints.  Maintain records of all complaints received using
appropriate software.  Assure that Providers receive all assigned trips and
that they are performed in a safe and timely manner.  Responsible for
marketing; route design and implementation of projects.
