Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.

Job Description: Accounts Receivable Clerk


FLSA Classification:    Non-exempt


Characteristics of the Class: Under the general supervision of Executive
Director or Designee

Requirements and Qualifications:

High School Diploma with bookkeeping experience, good computer skills with
data entry experience, business office, billing and general accounting
experience; Knowledge of contemporary office practices and procedures;
Knowledge of business English, good spelling, punctuation and arithmetic;
Ability to work well with people; Must be proficient in the use of office
equipment including copier, fax machine, telephone, postage meter and
calculator; Ability to maintain accurate records and files; Must be detail-
oriented; Good communication skills; Ability to establish and maintain
effective working relationship with employees and businesses; to enter and
research data in computer accurately and timely; Good telephone etiquette;
Trainable in functions, organizational procedures, rules and regulations of
the agencies involved; Self-starter, team player and ability to work
independently; Must possess tact, patience, thoroughness, accuracy,
honesty, orderliness and sound judgment.

Duties and Responsibilities:

These duties include but not limited to verifying accuracy of cash fares
and charge accounts.  Data collection includes mileage, rates and payments.
 Reconcile collections and payments.  Generate invoices for charge
customers, evaluate contract compliance, payment collection, mailing,
filing, faxing, and other general clerical duties assigned.  Record and
verify daily deposits.  Record billing and deposits on Excel software.
Issue and fax outing confirmation numbers to agencies.  Filter and sort
Transview data for billing inconsistencies. Check client eligibility to
insure payment method is correct.   Compare and match deposit slips with
computer data to insure correct payment and correct mileage.  Maintenance
and accountability of the Electronic Payment Gateway (EPG) system for
charge fares, veterans, charter and reports.

Responsible for accurate cash fare collection, charge accounts
receivables/balances and reporting to the Executive Director at the end of
each month.

These duties do not necessarily include all essential functions of this
job.   Performs other duties as assigned by the Executive Director.

Ninety (90) days to be proficient in duties and responsibilities.

Administrative Associate


Job Characteristics – Quality Assurance



Purpose

Performs administrative support, file maintenance duties and other work as
required, all within the context of RTEC’s Mission of providing responsive,
cost effective, and innovative transportation services through a
collaborative effort with the community to the highest quality.

Position is typically located in offices having administrative and program
responsibilities with considerable diversity. The work is sensitive and
confidential in nature, involves conveying information and requires
considerable knowledge of RTEC’s organization and operations.




Equipment, Methods & Guidelines

Typically uses Microsoft Office, word processing and other software
(Transview & Radios), computer terminal, telephone, typewriter, calculator,
copy and facsimile machines, and automobile; Reference materials include
policies and procedures, administrative directives, departmental
instructions, and user manuals.


Working Conditions

Work is generally performed in an office setting, but may include travel,
which extend hours of work beyond the normal workday. Work includes
pressure generated by frequent interruptions, deadlines, complaints, and
peak workloads.


Physical Demands

Work may include prolonged sitting, as well as moderate lifting, reaching,
stooping, pulling, pushing and carrying up to 30 lbs; clear speech; and
visual and hearing acuity.


Examples of Duties


 1. Receives inquiries and complaints; coordinates with others as necessary
    to obtain the required information; may compose appropriate written
    responses.

 2. Searches files, gathers facts and compiles a variety of data required
    for preparation of reports; acts as liaison to expedite the flow of
    information.

 3. Schedules and coordinates transportation when assigned to operations
    department (Dispatcher).

4.    Coordinates activities to ensure timely submission of documents or
    information that may be required of several staff personnel to complete
    a major project.

5.    May participate in departmental monitoring activities.

6.    Prepares reports where the information may be gathered from several
    sources and compile; searches records and provides factual data from
    information on file. For relief purposes may be assigned clerical or
    activities of other personnel.

7.    Performs other work as assigned by executive Director or Designee.


Desirable Qualifications



Knowledge and Abilities

Advanced-level knowledge of modern computer word processing. Considerable
knowledge of modern office procedures and practices of correct punctuation,
spelling, and grammatical usage, together with an excellent vocabulary; of
filing, indexing, and cross-referencing methods; of RTEC’s organization and
vehicle operations.   Ability to organize, prepare and maintain accurate
records and reports; to research, compile and interpret data; to write
effectively, to analyze situations and adapt effective courses of action
when dealing with various inquiries, complaints, and problems; to
coordinate and perform multiple activities efficiently; to work effectively
under pressure with frequent interruptions; to understand and carry out
oral and written instructions; and to establish and maintain cooperative
and effective relationships with those contacted in the course of work.

Education: Any combination equivalent to four-year business degree or
related field or five years experience in quality control and education
that would likely provide the required knowledge and abilities would be
qualifying.  An education minimum is satisfactory completion of high school
or the equivalent, supplemented by completion of specialized coursework.



Other Requirements

Selected positions require a Kentucky Driver's License and satisfactory
driving record as a condition of initial and continued employment. This is
a safety-sensitive position.
