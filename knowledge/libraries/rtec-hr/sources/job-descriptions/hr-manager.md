﻿Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.

Job Description:		Manager of Human Resources Department 
FLSA Classification:		Non-Exempt 
Reports to:			Executive Director/CEO
 
Education Requirements: Bachelor's Degree in Human Resources or High School Diploma with 10 years 
formal experience in Human Resources management of 30 or more employees, supplemented with course 
training in related field. The ability to develop policies and procedures.
Position Summary 
Responsible for managing the human resources function company-wide, with specific accountability in: 
staffing and recruitment, company policies and procedures, compensation, performance management, employee relations, training and development, and safety. Working knowledge of Workers Compensation, Unemployment Hearings, and other employee benefits. Assist with affirmative action, legal compliance, benefits, and time and attendance. Primarily responsible for the development and implementation of human resources programs that support corporate goals and objectives, operating within established time lines. Accountable for Human Resources Department operations, administration, policy updates and employee notices under the direction of the Executive Director. 
Essential Functions 
    1. Actively participates in strategic planning, recommending human resources programs that meet company needs and support company goals. 
    2. Develops new human resources programs to meet legal requirements and changing organizational 
needs. Manages special needs programs, e.g., relocation, reduction-in-force. 
    3. Implements new programs, including providing appropriate training. Regularly monitors and reviews 
effectiveness of existing programs. 
    4. Evaluates the cost-effectiveness and efficiency of existing human resources programs, recommending 
improvements and revisions to maximize organizational performance. 
    5. Establishes and maintains effective reporting systems for the human resources function. 
    6. Monitors, interprets and reports results on all human resources related programs. Recommends 
appropriate actions that should be taken to ensure optimal company-wide performance. 
    7. Maintains required employee records. 
    8. Participates in employee recruitment and interviews. 
    9. Develops and maintains staffing plans designed to anticipate future staffing needs. Conducts work force planning review to ensure proper staffing at all times. 
    10. Represents the company in all legal proceedings, working with appropriate legal counsel. 
    11. Provides consultation to management as needed. 
    12. Oversees the daily operations of the human resources department, ensuring accurate, legally compliant, 
and timely human resources administration. 
    13. Performs other related duties as assigned by the Executive Director/CEO. 

Must have excellent record keeping skills, a thorough understanding and good leadership of RTEC's 
policies and regulations and other Federal/State/Local Regulations as appropriate in the transit industry. 


An essential requirement for this position is the ability to sit for long periods of time with high 
concentration levels while working with various production numbers and record keeping. Physical 
requirements include stretching, bending and stooping to reach files, lifting and carrying up to 30 pounds 
as the standard rule for copying, filing and storing records in an office environment. Must be flexible and 
acceptable to change, since it is impossible to list each project and all of the requirements that may be 
associated with the programs of RTEC that may vary during each grant/fiscal year. Travel required.
