## RTEC'S Interview Form

**Position:** Fleet Manager and Superintendent of Facilities

### Do you have the following? (minimum requirements)

- High School Diploma or GED?
- Commercial Driver's License (CDL) with a passenger endorsement, or ability to obtain one within 30 days of employment?
- Minimum of 2 years' experience in vehicle and building maintenance; Supervisory experience? (references)
- Are you willing to work flexible hours to meet the needs of the transit system; during vehicle breakdowns, etc.?
- (Plus) Describe computer experience, if any; and type of software you are familiar with.
- Discuss supervisory experience, particularly related to vehicle and building maintenance.
- Discuss your background or working knowledge of vehicle mechanics that you will be able to identify work/repairs on a vehicle done by our mechanic, in order to adequately supervise the maintenance staff and ascertain good performance of our mechanics and work performed by outside contracts.
- Will you be able to maintain adequate reports on the vehicles and be able to compile these? Any history of preparing reports?

## RTEC's Interview Form continued

- Do you understand the importance of a good preventive maintenance program to help assure that RTEC's vehicles will operate in the safest condition possible; including keeping the vehicles in good, clean and safe operating condition to extend the useful life of the vehicles and reduce the number of breakdowns? Describe experience in this field.
- In addition to overseeing the performance of the mechanic and some shop attendants, can you perform some minor maintenance to vehicles and facilities? This includes tire and oil changes in the absence of the mechanic and changing light bulbs or other minor building maintenance? Occasionally, this position has done such small chores as hanging flags, blinds, etc.
- Are you familiar with purchase orders and maintaining inventory of vehicles, supplies and other equipment?
- Are you willing to drive for passenger service if needed?
- Do you have a safe driving record and is your police background check clear?
- Since it is impossible for us to define all the duties of this position, we are looking for an individual who is cooperative, a team player and have the potential of being a good member of our team, while representing RTEC well. This includes good working relations with vehicle operators, dispatchers and third party contractors.
