# RTEC Equal Employment Opportunity Policy Statement

Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. has a strong commitment to the community we serve and our employees. As an equal opportunity employer we strive to have a workforce that reflects the community we serve. No person is unlawfully excluded from employment opportunities based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex (including gender identity, sexual orientation, and pregnancy), age, genetic information, disability, veteran status or other protected class.

RTEC's Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) policy applies to all employment actions, including but not limited to, recruitment, hiring, selection for training, promotion, transfer, demotion, layoff, termination, rates of pay or other forms of compensation.

All applicants and employees have the right to file complaints alleging discrimination. Retaliation against an individual who files a charge or complaint of discrimination, participates in an employment discrimination proceeding (such as an investigation or lawsuit), or otherwise engages in protected activity is strictly prohibited and will not be tolerated.

RTEC is committed to providing reasonable accommodations to applicants and employees who need them because of a disability or to practice or observe their religion, absent undue hardship.

As RTEC's Executive Director, I maintain overall responsibility and accountability for RTEC's compliance with its EEO Policy and Program. To ensure day-to-day management, including program preparation, monitoring, and complaint investigation, I have appointed Jacob Salsman, EEO Manager, 1-800-321-7832 Ext. 223, as RTEC's EEO Officer. Jacob Salsman will report directly to me and acts with my authority with all levels of management and employees.

All RTEC's executives, management, and supervisory personnel, however, share in the responsibility for implementing and monitoring RTEC's EEO Policy and Program within their respective areas and will be assigned specific tasks to ensure compliance is achieved. RTEC will evaluate its managers' and supervisors' performance on their successful implementation of RTEC's policies and procedures, in the same way RTEC assesses their performance regarding other agency's goals.

RTEC is committed to undertaking and developing a written nondiscrimination program that sets forth the policies, practice and procedures, with goals and timetables, to which the agency is committed and make the EEO Program available for inspection by an employee or an applicant for employment upon request.

I am personally committed to a workplace that acts upon its daily responsibility to treat all applicants and employees with dignity and respect, as well as equitably under the guidelines of our EEO Policy and Program.
