## Full searchable transcription

Examples - within 12 month period

| Points | Action | Alternate points | Alternate action |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 2 | verbal | 5 | verbal |
| 4 | written warning | 7 | WW1 |
| 6 | final warning | 10 | WW2 |
| 8 | suspension | 15 | final warning |
| 10 | discharge | 18-20 | termination |

Attendance examples:
- 1/2 point - tardiness
- 1 point - unexcused absences
- Extra 1/2 point for failing to give notice

Warning statement:
- Under certain circumstances the infraction may justify discharge for a first offense.
- Progressive discipline is discretionary and RTEC retains the right to discharge employees immediately.
- While the company will generally take disciplinary action in a progressive manner, it reserves the right, in its sole discretion, to decide whether and what disciplinary action will be taken in a given situation.
- If an employee maintains a satisfactory record for a period of one year, any new violations may be treated as first occurrences. However, the Company may still consider all past disciplinary actions in evaluating the employee.

## Interpretation boundary

The source does not identify what distinguishes the two side-by-side point ladders. It is preserved as historical evidence and must not be treated as current policy without corroboration.
