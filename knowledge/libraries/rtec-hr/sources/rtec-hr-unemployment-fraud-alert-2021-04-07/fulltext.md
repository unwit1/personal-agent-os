# Fraud Alert memorandum - April 7, 2021

Source ID: `rtec-hr-unemployment-fraud-alert-2021-04-07`

**To:** RTEC Employees  
**From:** Human Resources  
**Date:** April 7, 2021  
**Subject:** FRAUD ALERT!

The memorandum addresses employees who had not applied for Kentucky unemployment benefits but received correspondence from the Kentucky Office of Unemployment Insurance indicating that they had.

It directs affected employees to take several actions:

1. Report suspected fraud/identity theft to the U.S. Department of Justice National Center for Disaster Fraud, using the then-current online form or telephone number, and categorize the report as COVID-19-related identity theft/insurance fraud.
2. Submit the then-current Kentucky unemployment-insurance fraud report. If a hard-copy check was received, mark it VOID and return it to the OUI Fraud Unit. If an incorrect 1099-G was received, contact the Kentucky Department of Criminal Investigations using the address listed in the memo.
3. Consider contacting Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion to place initial fraud alerts and ask that reports display only the last four digits of the Social Security number.
4. Employees with an existing Kentucky unemployment account were instructed to review banking information and verify account information.

The memo also offers Human Resources assistance.

The signed PDF identifies Sheila Stallsworth as Human Resource Manager and includes a note that its information sources included the London Police Department Facebook page and the Kentucky Career Center Fraud Alert page.
