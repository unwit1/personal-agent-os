Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc.

                            Position Description

Class Title: Customer Service Representative (CSR)

Characteristics of the Class: Under the general supervision of the
Operations Manager and does related work as well.

Example of Duties: Answers the telephone, greets visitors, and directs  the
caller  or  visitor  to  the  appropriate  party,  checks   Medicaid/Waiver
eligibility, updates passenger profiles, geocodes landmarks and residences,
scans physicians’ statements, referrals, urgent  care  statements,  vehicle
statements, makes and  cancels  transportation  appointments  received  via
telephone and 500 form requests,   including  adequate  directions  to  the
passenger's home and appointment information, data entry  of  appointments,
pick-up and drop-off times, cash fares collected and provides a variety  of
clerical duties.

Desirable Qualifications

Training and Experience: High School Diploma or equivalency (GED).  Good
computer skills and data entry experience preferred, Spanish or bilingual,
a plus.

 Special Knowledge Skills, and Abilities: Experience in contemporary  office
 practices,  procedures,  and  equipment.  Knowledge  of  business  English,
 spelling,  punctuation,  and  arithmetic.  Knowledge  of   the   functions,
 organization, procedures, rules, and regulations of the agencies  involved.
 Ability to maintain records and  documents  to  prepare  accurate  reports.
 Ability to communicate effectively, orally,  and  in  writing.  Ability  to
 establish and maintain  effective  working  relationships  with  officials,
 employees, customers and the general public. Good telephone  etiquette  and
 good typing speed a must. Ability to timely enter data into  the  computer.
 Ability  to  read  maps  and  determine  the  most   efficient   route   of
 transportation.   Tact,   patience,   thoroughness,   accuracy,    honesty,
 orderliness, and sound judgement a must.  An essential requirement for this
 position is the  ability  to  sit  for  long  periods  of  time  with  high
 concentration levels while scheduling  transportation  services.   Physical
 requirements include stretching,  bending  and  stooping  to  reach  files,
 lifting and carrying up to 30 pounds as  the  standard  rule  for  copying,
 filing and storing records in an office environment.

 CSR is not considered a safety sensitive position but is subject to a clear
 background record check and must remain drug and alcohol free  and  may  be
 subject to drug and alcohol testing.

 These duties do not necessarily include all  essential  functions  of  this
 job.
