RTEC'S EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY POLICY STATEMENT

Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. has a strong commitment  to  the
community we serve and our employees.  As an equal opportunity employer,  we
strive to have a workforce that reflects the community we serve.  No  person
is unlawfully excluded from employment opportunities based on  race,  color,
religion,  national  origin,  sex   (including   gender   identity,   sexual
orientation, and pregnancy), age, genetic information,  disability,  veteran
status, or other protected class.

RTEC's Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) policy applies to  all  employment
actions, including but not limited to, recruitment,  hiring,  selection  for
training, promotion, transfer, demotion, layoff, termination, rates  of  pay
or other forms of compensation.

All applicants and employees have the  right  to  file  complaints  alleging
discrimination.  Retaliation against an individual who  files  a  charge  or
complaint of discrimination, participates in  an  employment  discrimination
proceeding (such as an investigation or lawsuit), or  otherwise  engages  in
protected activity is strictly prohibited and will not be tolerated.

RTEC is committed to proving reasonable  accommodations  to  applicants  and
employees who need them because of a disability or to  practice  or  observe
their religion, absent undue hardship.

As  RTEC's  Executive  Director,  I  maintain  overall  responsibility   and
accountability for RTEC's compliance with its EEO Policy  and  Program.   To
ensure day-to-day management,  including  program  preparation,  monitoring,
and complaint  investigation,  I  have  appointed  Sheila  Stallsworth,  EEO
Manager, 1-800-321-7832, as RTEC's EEO  Officer.   Sheila  Stallsworth  will
report directly to me  and  acts  with  my  authority  with  all  levels  of
management and employees.

All RTEC's  executives,  management,  and  supervisory  personnel,  however,
share in the responsibility  for  implementing  and  monitoring  RTEC's  EEO
Policy and Program within  their  respective  areas  and  will  be  assigned
specific tasks to ensure compliance is achieved.   RTEC  will  evaluate  its
managers' and supervisors' performance on  their  successful  implementation
of RTEC's policies and procedures, in  the  same  way  RTEC  assesses  their
performance regarding other agency's goals.

RTEC is committed to undertaking and developing a written  nondiscrimination
program that sets forth the policies, practices and procedures,  with  goals
and timetables, to which the agency is committed and make  the  EEO  program
available for inspection by an employee or  applicant  for  employment  upon
request.

I  am  personally  committed  to  a  workplace  that  acts  upon  its  daily
responsibility to treat  all  applicants  and  employees  with  dignity  and
respect, as well as equitably under the guidelines of  our  EEO  Policy  and
Program.

ASSURANCES

RTEC has adopted the Affirmative Action Plan for Region  12  Brokerages  and
public transportation and shall use it to  serve  as  a  guide  designed  to
initiate  and  maximize  motivation   and   employment   opportunities   for
minorities, women, disabled individuals, veterans and the poor. This  policy
shall further serve to provide for Equal Opportunity/Affirmative  Action  in
all phases of daily action, inclusive of any and all employees  or  affected
individuals, participants, or beneficiaries.  RTEC  pledges  assistance  and
guidance to positively affect  Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative  Action  needs
within the community.

RTEC abides by all  applicable  Equal  Opportunity  laws,  regulations,  and
policies as set forth to insure nondiscrimination as  an  employer,  service
provider, and administering agency for delegated programs,  projects  and/or
activities. Specific laws and regulations shall govern all actions  of  this
Company. As RTEC has established its  Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative  Action
Policy, Plan and Program  in  accordance  with  the  intent  of  the  rules,
regulations, and guidelines of the various federal, state,  and  local  laws
and agencies having administrative authority in the Equal Opportunity  area,
RTEC provides assurance of compliance with either human rights  policies  or
directives as may be created and implemented  during  future  periods.  This
Company establishes and implements an Affirmative Action  Plan  and  Program
to insure that positive action steps  are  taken  to  assist  the  Company's
internal operational system as well as any entities outside the Company.


RESPONSIBILITY FOR AFFIRMATIVE ACTION PLAN ADMINISTRATION

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors shall be  responsible  for  the  approval  of  RTEC's
policies, plans and priorities. The Board shall also  approve  policies  and
procedures for the Company subject to grant guidelines.

The  Board  of  Directors  will  be  involved  and  advised  of  the   Equal
Opportunity/Affirmative  Action  Policy,  Plan   and   Program   to   insure
compliance with the applicable laws, regulations, and grant requirements.

The   Board   of   Directors   shall   approve   goals   for    the    Equal
Opportunity/Affirmative  Action  Plan  and  shall  approve   the   planning,
implementation, conduct and evaluation of the Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative
Action Policy, Plan, and Program performance.


The Board of Directors shall have the  authority  to  insure  and  guarantee
implementation in every  phase  of  employment  and  service.  The  policies
invoked by the Board of  Directors  shall  be  binding  for  all  staff  and
applicants for employment.

Executive Director

The Executive Director shall have the responsibility for insuring the  Equal
Opportunity/Affirmative Action Policy  as  outlined  has  been  followed  to
assure compliance with all regulations.

The Executive Director shall appoint  the  EEO  Administrative  Manager  and
shall establish appropriate duties, responsibilities and accountability.

The Executive Director and  the  Equal  Opportunity  Administrative  Manager
shall strive to insure that every form of prejudice or discrimination  based
on race, color, religion, creed, gender, national origin,  age,  disability,
or veteran status,  genetic  information  or  any  other  legally  protected
status is eradicated.


 EEO Administrative Manager -  Sheila Stallsworth


The EEO Administrative Manager implements activities relative to  the  Equal
Opportunity/Affirmative Action Policy, Plan and Program,  as  determined  by
the Executive Director and  the  Board  of  Directors.  Duties  of  the  EEO
Administrative Manager shall include, but not limited to, the following:

Provides assurance that the Equal Opportunity  Policy,  Personnel  Policies,
and  Affirmative  Action  Plan  and  Program  are  implemented  per  current
regulations.

Maintains   materials   and   working    knowledge    on    current    Equal
Opportunity/Affirmative  Action   laws,   implementation   procedures,   and
compliance.

Provides assistance and guidance in human  rights  areas  to  the  Board  of
Directors, Executive Director and other management.

Establishes and maintains a system for analyzing, evaluation, and  reporting
data as it relates to equal opportunity employment, and as mandated by law.

Collects and submits information and reports to the Board of  Directors  and
Executive Director as requested and or as needed.

Receives and assists in resolving complaints of discrimination and  provides
aggrieved persons with information and advice on grievance procedure.

Participates in  all  phases  of  the  personnel  process  to  insure  equal
treatment of all applicants and employees.

Analyzes skills and abilities of all staff, determines  training  needs  and
provides counseling to employees as needed.

DUTIES/RESPONSIBILITIES: (ESSENTIAL)


Conducts self in an appropriate manner as a representative of RTEC, working
effectively in a diverse work environment.

Develops  the  RTEC's  Affirmative  Action  Plan  and  other  informational
material relative to administering and implementing the  Plan.  Interprets,
implements, and oversees compliance to the plan.

Manages, reviews, and evaluates the  equal  employment  opportunity  program
designed to provide equal access and opportunity throughout  RTEC's  Service
Area.

Develops and implements appropriate training programs to further  the  goals
of the equal employment opportunity program throughout  the  area.  Monitors
other training programs; reviews and provides direction  to  ensure  content
addresses the diversity of the RTEC's workforce.

Conducts research to identify barriers  to  equal  employment  opportunity,
makes recommendations and takes approved  action  to  assist  the  RTEC  in
overcoming those barriers.

Takes and investigates internal equal employment opportunity  and  customer
service discrimination complaints, analyzes complex information,  comes  to
conclusions, recommends appropriate action  and  ensures  that  appropriate
corrective action is taken according to legal standards.

Conciliates and/or mediates between employees  to  resolve  EEO  employment
related  issues.   Investigates   and   officially   responds   to   formal
discrimination  charges  filed  with  state  and   federal   administrative
agencies.

Consults with and provides expert advice to all departments and  levels  of
management  regarding  personnel  procedures  and   practices   to   ensure
compliance with employment laws.

Advises the RTEC Executive Director and senior managers of the  most  recent
regulations and guidelines from federal, state, and local agencies  as  they
affect affirmative action, equal employment opportunity and  diversity  when
appropriate.

Advises the RTEC Executive Director and senior managers about pertinent EEO-
related issues affecting the region/area when appropriate.

Represents the RTEC and serves as a liaison with the Department  of  Labor,
EEOC, and FTA.

Counsels employees/managers on work related problems, to determine  if  EEO
related. Refers employees  to  other  sources  as  appropriate,  to  ensure
consistent and proper application of progressive discipline procedures.

Monitors the consistent application  of  RTEC's  Policy  by  reviewing  for
concurrence employment requisitions, requests  for  new  hires,  transfers,
demotions,  change  of  status  forms,  job  analyses,  job   descriptions,
selection tools, the recruitment process for new hires and  promotions  and
other practices which affect employee terms and conditions  of  employment.
Evaluates inconsistencies and addresses them relative to EEO law.

Monitors  and  evaluates  personnel  actions,  oversees  and  reviews   the
development of RTEC's policy relative to employment practices for adherence
to policies and employment laws.

Works with Department Heads, reviews and concurs  with  all  reorganization
plans. Establishes and maintains databases  for  monitoring  and  reporting
employment actions.

On a regular basis, collects and  compiles  applicable  data  for  the  EEO
related reports, submits reports to the Executive Director for approval and
files reports with the appropriate Federal and State offices.

Monitors contractors' and subcontractors' EEO and Affirmative Action  plans
to ensure compliance with federal requirements for affirmative  action  and
equal employment opportunity. Supports  recruitment  efforts  by  reviewing
recruitment plans and making recommendations.
