# Historical AAP — Employment Practices and Driver Hiring

This section preserves the supplied historical AAP's employment-practice and hiring statements. It is historical evidence and is **not** assumed to be current hiring policy.

## Program Guidelines and Employment Practices

RTEC states that special effort is required to prevent and eliminate discrimination and that recruitment, selection, advancement, compensation, transfer and other terms or conditions of employment are to be conducted without discrimination based on protected status.

The source states that training opportunities and career counseling are to be available to all employees without regard to minority status and that the company will establish goals to address under-utilization.

The source refers to employment-practice addenda including the driver-orientation synopsis, details of driver orientation, and resources used for RTEC criminal-background and driving-record searches.

## Review of Personnel Processes

The source says RTEC reviews its personnel processes annually to determine whether procedures assure careful, thorough and systematic consideration of known qualified individuals with disabilities and qualified protected veterans.

It states that this review covers procedures related to filling job vacancies by hire or promotion and training opportunities.

It also states that physical and mental job qualifications are reviewed annually so that qualifications that tend to screen out qualified individuals with disabilities or qualified disabled veterans are consistent with business necessity and safe job performance.

## Dissemination — hiring/recruitment-relevant provisions

The source states that all job advertisements and applications used for employment shall bear the masthead **"An Equal Employment Opportunity Employer."**

It assigns the EEO Administrative Manager responsibility for maintaining information needed to communicate the AAP/EEO program to minority groups, organizations and the community.

It states that recruitment sources, including state employment agencies, had been informed of RTEC's commitment to EEO and Affirmative Action.

## Historical hiring process printed in the AAP

The source lists the following hiring sequence:

1. Approval is obtained from the CEO to advertise and interview.
2. An advertisement is placed in the local newspaper, with EEO language and a note encouraging minority applicants.
3. A copy of the advertisement and application is sent to the Office of Employment & Training (OET) for that location. For driver applicants, the source additionally lists:
   - Driving Record Release authorization
   - Authorization for criminal background check
   - Information release form
   - Copy of RTEC's Drug and Alcohol Policy
   - Job Description
   - Voluntary Affirmative Action Data Record
4. OET also advertises the position and selects/forwards candidates described by the source as most qualified.
5. Applicant is scheduled for interview. For drivers, the source lists the following items as considered:
   - Experience in driving, handicap, wheelchair, CDL
   - GED minimum education
   - Turnover rate for previous employment
   - Pay rate requested
   - Ability to perform the job description with or without reasonable accommodation
   - Complete and signed application
   - Score on pre-employment applicant driver test

The source states that the interview begins with background on the company, position, hours, pay rate, some policy and benefits.

It then states that selected candidates' applications are sent to the Safety/Training/Accident/Drug/Alcohol Program Manager for background checks.

## Historical pre-employment screening listed for safety-sensitive positions

- Driving Record Check
- Background Record Check
- D.O.T. Physical (source cites USDOT 49 CFR 391.41)
- Pre-employment drug test (source cites USDOT 49 CFR Part 40 as amended)

## Historical "Criteria for Hiring Drivers" printed in the source

The source states:

1. Criminal background check must show no prior convictions for violent or sexual crimes.
2. Must have a clear five-year background check with no felonies or drug/alcohol-related offenses.
3. Must have a clear three-year driving-record background check with no more than two moving violations.

It further states that, to be retained as a driver, a driver must not have more than two moving violations during three years, that driving-law violations may be grounds for suspension or termination, and that a ten-year criminal/driving record indicating a habitual offender was stated as grounds not to hire an individual as a professional driver.

The source says the final hiring responsibility/decision rests with the Chief Executive Director.

## Compensation / Benefits

The source states that RTEC reviews benefits and conditions of employment annually to ensure equal availability and describes one complete benefit package for all full-time eligible employees with the same waiting period and company/employee contribution structure.

## Discipline / Termination

The source describes an at-will framework combined with a philosophy of progressive discipline. It says progressive steps may include verbal warnings, written warnings, unpaid administrative leave and termination, but that RTEC may skip steps and termination may occur without prior progressive steps.
