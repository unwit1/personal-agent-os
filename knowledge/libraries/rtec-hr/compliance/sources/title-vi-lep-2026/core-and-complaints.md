## Information dissemination

The source requires Title VI information posters to be publicly displayed in the RTEC lobby, on the RTEC website, in public meeting rooms, in the lobbies of the London and Somerset transit facilities, and on all RTEC buses.

It states that Title VI coordinator contact information and the complaint form are available on the RTEC website.

The source says Title VI information is disseminated to employees to remind them of RTEC's policy and daily responsibilities, and that new employees are informed of Title VI provisions and expectations during New Employee Orientation.

## Subcontracts and vendors

The source states that subcontractors and vendors receiving RTEC payments originating from federal assistance are subject to Title VI and that RTEC is responsible for ensuring compliance.

Written contracts are to contain nondiscrimination language either directly or through the bid-specification package incorporated into the contract.

## Record keeping

## Title VI complaint procedure

A person who believes they or a class of persons were subjected to discrimination based on race, color or national origin may file through a representative or personally by completing and submitting RTEC's Title VI Complaint Form.

The source states:

- RTEC investigates complaints received no more than **180 days** after the alleged discrimination, unless FTA extends the filing time.
- RTEC processes complete complaints.
- RTEC reviews the complaint for jurisdiction.
- The complainant is sent an acknowledgement letter within **10 days** stating whether RTEC will investigate.
- RTEC has **90 days** to investigate.
- If more information is needed, the complainant has **30 business days** from the request letter to provide it.
- RTEC may administratively close a case if the investigator is not contacted or the additional information is not received within 30 business days.
- A case may also be administratively closed if the complainant no longer wishes to pursue it.
- After review, the investigator issues a closure letter or Letter of Finding.
- The source gives the complainant **90 days** after the closure/LOF letter date to appeal.
- A person may also file directly with the Federal Transit Administration Office of Civil Rights.
