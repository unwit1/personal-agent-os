## Complete substantive text of supplied one-page letter

The letter is addressed to Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. with subject **Certification**.

It states that RTEC had been certified to do business with the Commonwealth of Kentucky pursuant to Kentucky's Equal Employment Opportunity Act, KRS 45.550 to 45.640.

The letter states that the **one-year certification expires September 30, 2017**.

It further states that RTEC would receive a recertification notice at least ten business days before the expiration date and provides the Office of EEO and Contract Compliance contact number for questions.

The letter is signed by Yvette M. Smith, Executive Director.

## Historical-use note

This document establishes a dated certification period only. It does not establish current EEO certification status.
