TO:         All Finance and Administration Cabinet Employees


FROM:       Jamie Link, Executive Director


DATE:       November 19, 2004

SUBJECT:    Educational Achievement Awards


Many Finance and Administration Cabinet employees have recently completed,
or are currently enrolled, in various programs that may make them eligible
for an Educational Achievement Award.  These programs include GED programs,
the Kentucky Certified Public Manager program, and post-secondary education
or training.  The Cabinet appreciates and values your willingness to pursue
additional education in order to enhance your skills and future career
opportunities.

Effective this date, the decision has been made, due to current fiscal year
budgetary issues and concerns, to defer all pending Educational Achievement
Award requests.  However, a program has been established to perform an
