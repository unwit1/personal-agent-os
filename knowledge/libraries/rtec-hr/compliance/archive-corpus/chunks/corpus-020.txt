ESTABLISHING AFFIRMATIVE ACTION GOALS


The long-range goal of RTEC’s Affirmative Action Program  is  to  achieve  a
RTEC workforce at all job levels in which minorities,  women,  veterans  and
individuals  with  disabilities  are  represented  in  proportion  to  their
representation in  the  “applicable  labor  market”.   RTEC  identifies  its
“applicable  labor  market”  as  those  areas  which  include:  Bell,  Knox,
Whitley,   Clinton,   Cumberland,   Monroe,   Russell,   McCreary,   Laurel,
Rockcastle, Pulaski, and Wayne counties.

To address this long-range goal, RTEC will continue to advocate  the  hiring
and utilization of minorities and women at rates reasonably proportional  to
their corresponding rates of availability in the “applicable labor market”.

RTEC will also make every good faith effort to:

   1. Promote equal opportunity and achieve a  workplace  that  is  free  of
      discrimination.


   2.  Ensure  opportunities  for  all   qualified   applicants,   including
      underutilized groups.

   3. Hire the most qualified applicant using a fair  and  nondiscriminatory
      process.

   4. Make career development and  promotional  opportunities  available  to
      interested and qualified employees, including minorities and women.

   5. To ensure fairness and nondiscrimination in the termination process.




                            EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES


With each job opening, the request is forwarded to Human Resources to  start
the hiring process:

   1. Approval is obtained from the CEO to advertise and interview.
   2. Ad is placed in the local newspaper; with special attention  given  to
      EEO, Minority encouraged to apply noted on Ad.
   3. Copy of the ad is sent to Office of Employment &  Training  (OET)  for
      that location, along with the application. Drivers  applications  also
      require:


         o Driving Record Release authorization
         o Authorization for criminal background check
         o Information release form
         o Copy of RTEC’s Drug and Alcohol Policy
         o Job Description
         o Voluntary Affirmative Action Data Record


   4. OET also advertises the position and selects candidates most qualified
      and forwards to RTEC.
   5. Applicant is scheduled for interview.  Significant items considered on
      Application: ex. Drivers:


         o Experience in driving, handicap, wheelchair, CDL
         o GED Minimum Education
         o Turnover rate for previous employment
         o Pay rate requested
         o Ability to perform the job as listed on the job description with
           or without reasonable accommodation
         o Application is complete and is signed
         o Score on pre-employment applicant driver test


To begin the interview, we provide  some  background  on  the  Company,  the
position, hours, pay rate,  some  policy,  and  review  of  Benefits.   Upon
completion of the interview, selected candidates application is sent to  our
Safety/Training/Accident/Drug/Alcohol   Program   Manager   for   background
checks.

RTEC currently has the following  pre-employment  screenings  in  place  for
safety sensitive positions:

         o Driving Record Check
         o Background Record Check
         o D.O.T. Physical (USDOT 49CFR391.41)
         o Pre-employment drug test (USDOT 49CFR part 40 as amended)

             Invitation to Self-Identify for Protected Veterans

     1.  RTEC  is  subject  to  the  Vietnam  Era  Veterans’   Readjustment
        Assistance  Act  of  1974,  as  amended,  (VEVRAA)  which  requires
        employers to take affirmative action to employ and advance veterans
        in  employment.   VEVRAA  prohibits  discrimination  and   requires
        affirmative action in all personnel practices  regarding  protected
        veterans.  The  statute  covers  disabled  veterans,  Armed  Forces
        service medal veterans,  recently  separated  veterans,  and  other
        veterans who served during a war, or in a  campaign  or  expedition
        for which a campaign badge has been authorized.


     2. An invitation to veterans: If you are a recently separated veteran,
        other protected veteran, or Armed Forces service medal veteran,  we
        would like to include you under our affirmative action program.  If
        you would like to be included under the affirmative action program,
        please tell us.  The term “recently separated veterans”  refers  to
        any veteran during the three-year period beginning on the  date  of
        such veteran’s discharge or release from  active  duty.   The  term
        “other protected veteran” refers to a person who served  on  active
        duty during a war or in  a  campaign  or  expedition  for  which  a
        campaign badge has been authorized, under law  as  administered  by
        the Department of Defense.  The term “Armed  Forces  service  medal
        veteran” refers to any person who, while serving on active duty  in
        the  Armed  Forces,  participated  in  a  United  States   military
        operation for which an  Armed  Forces  service  medal  was  awarded
        pursuant to Executive Order 12985 (62 FR 1209).


        An invitation to disabled veterans only:  If  you  are  a  disabled
        veteran, we would like to include you  in  our  affirmative  action
        program.  If you would like to be included  under  the  affirmative

                   DRIVER TRAINING & ORIENTATION SYNOPSIS
                            RTEC Driver Training




RTEC Driver Training:

Once an application for a posted job opening is received, it is reviewed  by
RTEC Administration. Administration checks the  application  for  completion
and mistakes. Prior experience and education are  taken  into  consideration
before  the  application  is  processed.   A  Driving  Record  Check  and  a
Background Record Checks are performed.  References are also  contacted  and
checked.  Once an application is approved for further review,  an  interview
with  the  applicant  is  scheduled.   At   least   two   members   of   the
Administration or Management Team conduct the interview.  If  the  interview
goes well, then the applicant is called and made a pre-offer  of  employment
pending completion of a  Department  of  Transportation  Physical  and  Pre-
Employment Drug Test (see below for further details). Once an  applicant  is
chosen, the following must also be completed in a timely fashion.

Completion of Paperwork (Prior to Beginning Employment Work):

    ✓  Application:   Must   include   “Applicant   Letter”   agreement   to
      participate in the Drug & Alcohol Program with ongoing testing  (Check
      for prior experience and completion; check   references; interview)
    ✓ Employment Eligibility Verification
    ✓ Agreement  to  Assure  Confidentiality;  Agreement  with  Cabinet  for
      Health & Human Services
    ✓ Agreements with Employer
    ✓ Driving Record Release Form
    ✓ Background Record Release Authorization Form
    ✓ Drug-Free Agreement & Agreement  to  Participate  in  Drug  &  Alcohol
      Program
    ✓ Tax Forms
    ✓ Job Description and Requirements Reviewed
    ✓ Verification of Receipt of Policies/Procedures
    ✓ New Driver Orientation Routing Sheet
    ✓ Previous Employer Drug & Alcohol Testing Release  Form  completed  and
      Previous Employer contacted regarding testing records  information  as
      required by DOT/FTA

Medical:  Employee must have a Department of Transportation Physical &  Pre-
Employment DOT Drug Collection/Testing; RTEC must have  negative  drug  test
results back before  operation  of  a  vehicle  or  performance  of  safety-
sensitive function.  The applicant completes the D.O.T.  Physical  and  must
be defined as qualified by meeting standards in USDOT  49  CFR  391.41.   An
applicant/candidate must produce a verified negative drug test result  prior
to being hired to first perform a safety-sensitive duty  as  required  under
USDOT 49 CFR Part 40 as amended. Once results from the D.O.T.  Physical  are
received  and  a  verified  negative  drug  test  result  is  received,  any
remaining Pre-Employment Paperwork is finished and an  official  start  date
for the applicant/candidate is assigned.    D.O.T.  Physicals  may  also  be
reviewed by National Toxicology Specialists, and RTEC  will  adhere  to  the
recommendation of the M.R.O.


Background Record:  Application references and  criminal  background  record
search and driver record search must be completed prior  to  performance  of
safety-sensitive  function.   Additional  searches  must  be  performed   by
searching the “Sex Offender Registry” and the “Office of  Inspector  General
Search for Excluded Individuals” websites respectively.  Findings must  meet
Office of Transportation Delivery and RTEC qualification standards.

On-The-Job Training:  Drivers must  train  on-the-job  with  an  experienced
driver for a minimum  of  30  hours  after  Orientation  and  Driver  Basics
Training is completed.  This on-the-job training is necessary  for  the  new
hire  to  gain  experience  in  dealing  with  passengers,   agencies,   and
performance of required  job  duties.   This  on-the-job  training  includes
experience in a wheelchair/mobility  device  accessible  vehicle.   Training
time may exceed 30 hours or may be reduced to  less  depending  on  the  new
driver’s  experience  and  proficiency  of   job   requirements.    Training
documentation is  to  be  reviewed  by  the  immediate  supervisor  and  the
Operations  Manager  to  see  if   the   employee   performance   level   is
satisfactory.  If training level is not satisfactory,  then  the  Operations
Manager will implement a corrective plan of action to  Human  Resources  and
the Training Coordinator in a  timely  manner.   Training  documentation  of
successful completion of on-the-job training must be  forwarded  on  to  the
Training Coordinator prior to the employee  being  assigned  a  vehicle  and
placed on the job.  Additional on-the-job  training hours may be  necessary.


Reading and understanding  important  literature:   During  the  orientation
process, company handbooks, policies, and procedures are distributed to  the
Driver.  This literature  may  include  (but  not  limited  to):   Personnel
Policy Handbook, Driver Handbook, Vehicle  Maintenance  Program,  and  other
company policies/procedure manuals.  Verification of  receipt  is  collected
for each.  The employee receives a great deal of information  pertaining  to
their job and the requirements thereof. RTEC understands this is  a  lot  of
information to receive all at once.  Therefore, it is  understood  that  the
Driver will be given two (2) weeks to read  and  understand  this  important
literature.  Administration is available to  the  Driver  if  any  questions
arise during this time regarding company policy or procedure.

Other Training (Offered Within 3 Months)

    ➢ Nonviolent Crisis Intervention (NCI) Training Program.  The course  is
      offered by The Crisis Prevention Institute, Inc. and the International
      Association of Nonviolent Crisis Intervention  Certified  Instructors.
      Crisis intervention is a small segment of time in which staff  members
      must intervene with  another  person  to  address  behavior  that  may
      escalate into disruptive or even violent incidents. The  overall  goal
      of the program is to  provide  the  best  care,  welfare,  safety  and
      security at any given moment to the individuals in  our  charge.   The
      program covers the basic elements of violent behavior  and  identifies
      how a crisis can escalate and conversely, de-escalate.  It  identifies
      strategies that have proven successful and builds confidence that help
      staff maintain control of situations Drivers may encounter on the RTEC
      bus.  The course does have a Unit on  Personal  Safety.   The  primary
      focus of CPI’s Personal Safety Techniques  is  to  protect  staff  and
      acting out individuals from injury if behavior escalates to a physical
      level.
    ➢ Managing Threatening Confrontations Training DVD program (1 Hour)  may
      be offered in lieu of NCI  or  until  NCI  can  be  provided  (Usually
      completed during Initial 3-days of Training Scheduled).

Training Completion

After RTEC Orientation, Driver  Basics,  On-The-Job  Training,  and  initial
training  certifications  are  completed,  the   driver’s   performance   is
evaluated  by  a   responsible   member   of   Management.    All   training
documentation shall be retained in the employee’s Training File.

Periodic refresher training and re-certifications may be required  and  must
be completed satisfactorily.  RTEC will also offer corrective training  when
deemed appropriate.  For example, if a Driver is  involved  in  a  vehicular
accident then the Driver may  be  required  to  complete  Defensive  Driving
Training again. Other training may be scheduled as deemed necessary by  RTEC
Administration.  In order to qualify to be  an  RTEC  Driver,  all  required
training must be completed satisfactorily in addition  to  satisfactory  job
performance.

The Driver is given three (3) months to develop the skills and complete  the
training necessary to perform his/her job.  At the end  of  the  three-month
introductory period, the employee is  evaluated  by  the  Supervisor  and/or
Human  Resource  Manager  to  determine  if  he/she  is  performing   at   a
satisfactory level.
