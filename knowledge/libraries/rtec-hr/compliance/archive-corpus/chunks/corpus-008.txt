Although the EEO Officer is primarily responsible  for  implementing  RTEC’s
EEO Program, all officials, managers, and supervisors  are  responsible  for
ensuring EEO and must not discriminate based  on  a  protected  class.   All
managers-from the supervisor of the smallest unit to  the  Board  Chair  and
Executive Director-bear responsibility for ensuring that RTEC’s EEO  Program
policies and programs are carried out.

EEO responsibilities for RTEC officials, supervisors and managers include:

    • Participating actively in periodic audits of all aspects of employment
      to  identify  and  remove  barriers  obstructing  the  achievement  of
      specified goals and objectives
    •  Holding  regular  discussions  with  other   managers,   supervisors,
      employees, and affinity groups to ensure RTEC policies and  procedures
      are being followed
    • Cooperating  with  the  EEO  Officer  in  review  of  information  and
      investigation of complaints
    • Encouraging employee participation to support the advancement  of  the
      EEO  Program  (e.g.,  professional  development  and   career   growth
      opportunities,   posting   promotional    opportunities,    shadowing,
      mentoring)


Employees who fail to adhere to  the  Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative  Action
Policy, Plan and Program are subject to immediate disciplinary action.


PROGRAM GUIDELINES AND EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES

RTEC believes that special effort is required to prevent discrimination  and
to eliminate it within the Company and the community at  large.  RTEC  shall
continually  conduct  recruitment,  selection,  advancement,   compensation,
transfer and other terms or conditions of employment without  discrimination
as  to  race,  color,  religion,  creed,  gender,  national   origin,   age,
disability, or veteran status, genetic  information  or  any  other  legally
protected status, or other factors which cannot lawfully be used as a  basis
for an employment decision.

Training opportunities and career counseling will be made available  to  all
employees without regard to minority  status.  The  Company  will  establish
goals  to  alleviate  any  under-utilization  of  minorities  and  establish
efforts to fulfill its goals.

*See Employment Practices, orientation  of  driver’s  synopsis,  details  of
driver orientation and resources used to conduct  RTEC  criminal  background
record  searches  and  driving  record  searches  addendums  for  additional
information.


REVIEW OF PERSONNEL PROCESSES

RTEC reviews annually its  personnel  processes  to  determine  whether  its
present procedures assure careful, thorough and systematic consideration  of
the qualifications of known  qualified  individuals  with  disabilities  and
qualified protected veterans.  This review covers all procedures related  to
the filling of job vacancies either by hire or by promotion, as well as  all
training opportunities offered or made available to employees.

The physical  and  mental  job  qualifications  of  all  jobs  are  reviewed
annually to ensure that, to the extent of such  qualifications  requirements
tend to screen out qualified individuals  with  disabilities  and  qualified
disabled  veterans,  job  qualifications  are   consistent   with   business
necessity and the safe performance of the job.

DISSEMINATION OF POLICY, PLAN, AND PROGRAM

Internally

Directors, managers and supervisors are fully informed of RTEC’s policy  and
their role in the implementation of the EEO/AA Plan.   This  information  is
conveyed in the following manner:

   1. By written communication from RTEC’s Chief Executive Officer.


   2. By the inclusion  of  the  Chief  Executive  Officer’s  EEO/AA  Policy
      Statement in  Personnel  Policy  Handbook  and  training  manuals,  as
      appropriate and as applicable.

   3. Through periodic meetings to review RTEC’s Plan, as well as the goals,
      problems, obstacles, and/or concerns encountered in the implementation
      of the Plan.

   4. By informing non-supervisory staff of RTEC’s EEO/AA Policy through the
      following means:

        a.  Posting  Federal  and  State  EEO  notices  and  RTEC’s  policy
           statement on bulletin boards.   These  postings  are  placed  in
           conspicuous, accessible, and  heavily  trafficked  areas,  where
           they can be easily seen and read.


        b. Mentioning RTEC’s EEO/AA Policy  in  publications  prepared  and
           published for employees.  These publications  include,  but  are
           not limited to, employee handbooks and other official  documents
           issued to RTEC’s non-supervisory workforce.

        c. Soliciting from employees’  recommendations  to  improve  RTEC’s
           EEO/AA Program and  requesting  feedback  on  existing  program,
           efforts,  and  actions.   Accountable  for  Action:  RTEC’s  EEO
           Officer.

        d. Explaining the EEO/AA philosophy at all new employee orientation
           sessions and, when appropriate, in RTEC’s training sessions.



Externally


All advertisements for job openings and  applications  used  for  employment
shall  bear  the  masthead  "An  Equal  Opportunity  Employer".    The   EEO
Administrative Manager will be responsible for  maintaining  information  as
needed to minority groups, other organizations, and the community  at  large
to disseminate information for the Affirmative Action Plan and Program.  All
maintenance of records and reports for  the  Equal  Opportunity  Policy  and
Affirmative Action Plan and Program  shall  be  kept  on  file  in  the  EEO
Office.

Equal Employment Opportunity Clauses shall be incorporated in  all  purchase
orders, leases, contracts, etc., where practical.

Written notification of our EEO policy will be sent to  all  subcontractors,
vendors and suppliers, and  they  will  be  requested  to  take  appropriate
action.

All recruitment sources, including  State  employment  agencies,  have  been
informed of RTEC’s commitment to EEO and Affirmative Action.


COMMENT

The performance standards of RTEC employees will continue to  be  maintained
at a high level, consistent with job requirements.

Any employee or applicant for employment who believes that he/she  has  been
discriminated against because  of  race,  color,  religion,  creed,  gender,
national origin, age, disability, or veteran status, genetic information  or
any other legally protected status should report his/her complaint to  their
immediate supervisor, Human Resource Manager, EEO Administrative Manager  or
Executive Director.

In connection with the execution of any resulting contract,  RTEC  will  not
discriminate against any employee or applicant  for  employment  because  of
race, color, religion, creed, gender, national origin, age,  disability,  or
veteran status, genetic information or any other legally  protected  status.
RTEC will take affirmative action to insure that  applicants  are  employed,
and that employees are treated  equally  during  their  employment,  without
regard to their race, color, religion, creed, gender, national origin,  age,
disability, or veteran status, genetic  information  or  any  other  legally
protected status.  Such actions will include, but  not  be  limited  to  the
following: employment, upgrading,  demotion,  or  transfer;  recruitment  or
recruitment advertising; layoff or  termination;  rates  of  pay,  or  other
forms  of  compensation;  and  selection   of   training.    The   foregoing
requirements of this paragraph will be incorporated in all subcontracts  for
services covered by the contract.  RTEC  will  post  in  accessible  places,
available to employees and applicants for employment, notices setting  forth
the provision of this nondiscrimination clause.

According to RTEC policy, no employee or applicant for  employment  will  be
subject to harassment or discrimination because of  race,  color,  religion,
creed, gender, national origin, age, disability, or veteran status,  genetic
information or any other legally protected status.

This policy applies to all personnel actions, including but not limited  to,
recruiting,  hiring,  classification/compensation,   benefits,   promotions,
transfers, layoffs, reinstatement,  and  educational  programs.   We  expect
full cooperation  and  assistance  from  everyone  associated  with  hiring,
developing, and promoting  personnel  to  achieve  a  diverse  workforce  of
minority,  women,  and  disabled  employees  at  all  levels.   Any  program
evaluation shall include an assessment of equal opportunity performance.

The Board of Directors has adopted the Affirmative  Action  Plan  for  Rural
Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. d/b/a RTEC.   The  Executive  Director
Shirley L. Cummins  is  responsible  for  RTEC’s  compliance  with  the  EEO
policies.  Implementing the Affirmative Action Plan  is  the  responsibility
of the Equal Employment Opportunity Department  for  RTEC.   For  assistance
concerning our EEO, policies and efforts call,  EEO  Administrative  Manager
at the RTEC Office 606-256-9835 Extension 223 or toll-free 1-800-321-7832.

We expect the full cooperation and affirmation of the Action Plan  and  from
all section heads, managers, supervisors, and employees.

RTEC will annually assess its work force to determine any  under-utilization
of affected groups and will establish goals and  timetables  to  remedy  any
deficiencies.  Managers of  RTEC  will  be  evaluated  on  Equal  Employment
Opportunity goal attainment just as they are evaluated  on  other  goals  of
the company.

RTEC has agreed to assert leadership  within  its  communities  and  to  put
forth maximum effort to achieve full employment and to utilize  and  develop
the capabilities and productivity of all citizens.

RTEC further recognizes that the effective application of a policy of  merit
employment  involves  more  than  a  policy  statement  and  will  therefore
implement this Affirmative Action  Program  in  a  positive  and  aggressive
manner.  RTEC will make known its commitment to provide equal  opportunities
within the company on the basis of individual merit.

The  Board  of  Directors  solicits  and  encourages  all  persons  to  seek
opportunities with RTEC and to take advantage of advancement possibilities.
