                   DRIVER TRAINING & ORIENTATION SYNOPSIS
                            RTEC Driver Training




RTEC Driver Training:

Once an application for a posted job opening is received, it is reviewed  by
RTEC Administration. Administration checks the  application  for  completion
and mistakes. Prior experience and education are  taken  into  consideration
before  the  application  is  processed.   A  Driving  Record  Check  and  a
Background Record Checks are performed.  References are also  contacted  and
checked.  Once an application is approved for further review,  an  interview
with  the  applicant  is  scheduled.   At   least   two   members   of   the
Administration or Management Team conduct the interview.  If  the  interview
goes well, then the applicant is called and made a pre-offer  of  employment
pending completion of a  Department  of  Transportation  Physical  and  Pre-
Employment Drug Test (see below for further details). Once an  applicant  is
chosen, the following must also be completed in a timely fashion.

Completion of Paperwork (Prior to Beginning Employment Work):

    ✓  Application:   Must   include   “Applicant   Letter”   agreement   to
      participate in the Drug & Alcohol Program with ongoing testing  (Check
      for prior experience and completion; check   references; interview)
    ✓ Employment Eligibility Verification
    ✓ Agreement  to  Assure  Confidentiality;  Agreement  with  Cabinet  for
      Health & Human Services
    ✓ Agreements with Employer
    ✓ Driving Record Release Form
    ✓ Background Record Release Authorization Form
    ✓ Drug-Free Agreement & Agreement  to  Participate  in  Drug  &  Alcohol
      Program
    ✓ Tax Forms
    ✓ Job Description and Requirements Reviewed
    ✓ Verification of Receipt of Policies/Procedures
    ✓ New Driver Orientation Routing Sheet
    ✓ Previous Employer Drug & Alcohol Testing Release  Form  completed  and
      Previous Employer contacted regarding testing records  information  as
      required by DOT/FTA

Medical:  Employee must have a Department of Transportation Physical &  Pre-
Employment DOT Drug Collection/Testing; RTEC must have  negative  drug  test
results back before  operation  of  a  vehicle  or  performance  of  safety-
sensitive function.  The applicant completes the D.O.T.  Physical  and  must
be defined as qualified by meeting standards in USDOT  49  CFR  391.41.   An
applicant/candidate must produce a verified negative drug test result  prior
to being hired to first perform a safety-sensitive duty  as  required  under
USDOT 49 CFR Part 40 as amended. Once results from the D.O.T.  Physical  are
received  and  a  verified  negative  drug  test  result  is  received,  any
remaining Pre-Employment Paperwork is finished and an  official  start  date
for the applicant/candidate is assigned.    D.O.T.  Physicals  may  also  be
reviewed by National Toxicology Specialists, and RTEC  will  adhere  to  the
recommendation of the M.R.O.


Background Record:  Application references and  criminal  background  record
search and driver record search must be completed prior  to  performance  of
safety-sensitive  function.   Additional  searches  must  be  performed   by
searching the “Sex Offender Registry” and the “Office of  Inspector  General
Search for Excluded Individuals” websites respectively.  Findings must  meet
Office of Transportation Delivery and RTEC qualification standards.

On-The-Job Training:  Drivers must  train  on-the-job  with  an  experienced
driver for a minimum  of  30  hours  after  Orientation  and  Driver  Basics
Training is completed.  This on-the-job training is necessary  for  the  new
hire  to  gain  experience  in  dealing  with  passengers,   agencies,   and
performance of required  job  duties.   This  on-the-job  training  includes
experience in a wheelchair/mobility  device  accessible  vehicle.   Training
time may exceed 30 hours or may be reduced to  less  depending  on  the  new
driver’s  experience  and  proficiency  of   job   requirements.    Training
documentation is  to  be  reviewed  by  the  immediate  supervisor  and  the
Operations  Manager  to  see  if   the   employee   performance   level   is
satisfactory.  If training level is not satisfactory,  then  the  Operations
Manager will implement a corrective plan of action to  Human  Resources  and
the Training Coordinator in a  timely  manner.   Training  documentation  of
successful completion of on-the-job training must be  forwarded  on  to  the
Training Coordinator prior to the employee  being  assigned  a  vehicle  and
placed on the job.  Additional on-the-job  training hours may be  necessary.


Reading and understanding  important  literature:   During  the  orientation
process, company handbooks, policies, and procedures are distributed to  the
Driver.  This literature  may  include  (but  not  limited  to):   Personnel
Policy Handbook, Driver Handbook, Vehicle  Maintenance  Program,  and  other
company policies/procedure manuals.  Verification of  receipt  is  collected
for each.  The employee receives a great deal of information  pertaining  to
their job and the requirements thereof. RTEC understands this is  a  lot  of
information to receive all at once.  Therefore, it is  understood  that  the
Driver will be given two (2) weeks to read  and  understand  this  important
literature.  Administration is available to  the  Driver  if  any  questions
arise during this time regarding company policy or procedure.



Driver Training Plan (Times are approximate and may vary)

Day 1

    ➢ Orientation
    ➢ Drug & Alcohol Awareness & Policy Training (2 Hours Minimum):  Lecture
      using workbooks from National Toxicology Specialists  and/or  Training
      Videos; Quiz on Drug & Alcohol Awareness.
    ➢ Bus Evacuation Training (30 Minutes Minimum): Training  Video  viewed;
      hands-on practice with  seatbelt  cutter;  discussion/lecture;  review
      emergency exits and practice evacuation drill to be  completed  during
      Passenger Assistance Certification Training.
    ➢ Blood borne Pathogens Awareness & Prevention of  Disease  Transmission
      (1 Hour): this training may also be completed and/or supplemented when
      employee completes CPR & First Aid certification.
    ➢ System Safety  and  Security  Training):  National  Transit  Institute
      “Warning Signs” Training Video (30 Minutes); RTAP  Video  on  “Problem
      Passengers & Challenging Situations” (30 Minutes and includes  segment
      on Seizure Awareness).
    ➢ Defensive Driving  (1  Hour  Minimum;  typically  1-3  Hours  Credit):
      Coaching the Van Driver Video  Series;  Lecture  &  Tests  Follow;  In
      Vehicle Ride & Evaluation on Driving Skills; if time does  not  permit
      in vehicle ride/evaluation, then on-the-job  training  w/  experienced
      driver and evaluation by trainer will substitute (Good driving license

Other Training (Offered Within 3 Months)

    ➢ Nonviolent Crisis Intervention (NCI) Training Program.  The course  is
      offered by The Crisis Prevention Institute, Inc. and the International
      Association of Nonviolent Crisis Intervention  Certified  Instructors.
      Crisis intervention is a small segment of time in which staff  members
      must intervene with  another  person  to  address  behavior  that  may
      escalate into disruptive or even violent incidents. The  overall  goal
      of the program is to  provide  the  best  care,  welfare,  safety  and
      security at any given moment to the individuals in  our  charge.   The
      program covers the basic elements of violent behavior  and  identifies
      how a crisis can escalate and conversely, de-escalate.  It  identifies
      strategies that have proven successful and builds confidence that help
      staff maintain control of situations Drivers may encounter on the RTEC
      bus.  The course does have a Unit on  Personal  Safety.   The  primary
      focus of CPI’s Personal Safety Techniques  is  to  protect  staff  and
      acting out individuals from injury if behavior escalates to a physical
      level.
    ➢ Managing Threatening Confrontations Training DVD program (1 Hour)  may
      be offered in lieu of NCI  or  until  NCI  can  be  provided  (Usually
      completed during Initial 3-days of Training Scheduled).

Training Completion

After RTEC Orientation, Driver  Basics,  On-The-Job  Training,  and  initial
training  certifications  are  completed,  the   driver’s   performance   is
evaluated  by  a   responsible   member   of   Management.    All   training
documentation shall be retained in the employee’s Training File.

Periodic refresher training and re-certifications may be required  and  must
be completed satisfactorily.  RTEC will also offer corrective training  when
deemed appropriate.  For example, if a Driver is  involved  in  a  vehicular
accident then the Driver may  be  required  to  complete  Defensive  Driving
Training again. Other training may be scheduled as deemed necessary by  RTEC
Administration.  In order to qualify to be  an  RTEC  Driver,  all  required
training must be completed satisfactorily in addition  to  satisfactory  job
performance.

The Driver is given three (3) months to develop the skills and complete  the
training necessary to perform his/her job.  At the end  of  the  three-month
introductory period, the employee is  evaluated  by  the  Supervisor  and/or
Human  Resource  Manager  to  determine  if  he/she  is  performing   at   a
satisfactory level.

            RTEC'S EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY POLICY STATEMENT





Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. has a strong commitment  to  the
community we serve and our employees.  As an equal opportunity employer,  we
strive to have a workforce that reflects the community we serve.  No  person
is unlawfully excluded from employment opportunities based on  race,  color,
religion,  national  origin,  sex   (including   gender   identity,   sexual
orientation, and pregnancy), age, genetic information,  disability,  veteran
status, or other protected class.

RTEC's Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) policy applies to  all  employment
actions, including but not limited to, recruitment,  hiring,  selection  for
training, promotion, transfer, demotion, layoff, termination, rates  of  pay
or other forms of compensation.

All applicants and employees have the  right  to  file  complaints  alleging
discrimination.  Retaliation against an individual who  files  a  charge  or
complaint of discrimination, participates in  an  employment  discrimination
proceeding (such as an investigation or lawsuit), or  otherwise  engages  in
protected activity is strictly prohibited and will not be tolerated.

RTEC is committed to proving reasonable  accommodations  to  applicants  and
employees who need them because of a disability or to  practice  or  observe
their religion, absent undue hardship.

As  RTEC's  Executive  Director,  I  maintain  overall  responsibility   and
accountability for RTEC's compliance with its EEO Policy  and  Program.   To
ensure day-to-day management,  including  program  preparation,  monitoring,
and complaint  investigation,  I  have  appointed  Sheila  Stallsworth,  EEO
Manager, 1-800-321-7832, as RTEC's EEO  Officer.   Sheila  Stallsworth  will
report directly to me  and  acts  with  my  authority  with  all  levels  of
management and employees.

All RTEC's  executives,  management,  and  supervisory  personnel,  however,
share in the responsibility  for  implementing  and  monitoring  RTEC's  EEO
Policy and Program within  their  respective  areas  and  will  be  assigned
specific tasks to ensure compliance is achieved.   RTEC  will  evaluate  its
managers' and supervisors' performance on  their  successful  implementation
of RTEC's policies and procedures, in  the  same  way  RTEC  assesses  their
performance regarding other agency's goals.

RTEC is committed to undertaking and developing a written  nondiscrimination
program that sets forth the policies, practices and procedures,  with  goals
and timetables, to which the agency is committed and make  the  EEO  program
available for inspection by an employee or  applicant  for  employment  upon
request.

I  am  personally  committed  to  a  workplace  that  acts  upon  its  daily
responsibility to treat  all  applicants  and  employees  with  dignity  and
respect, as well as equitably under the guidelines of  our  EEO  Policy  and
Program.

RTEC EEO Responsibilities

Although the EEO Officer is primarily responsible  for  implementing  RTEC’s
EEO Program, all officials, managers, and supervisors  are  responsible  for
ensuring EEO and must not discriminate based  on  a  protected  class.   All
managers-from the supervisor of the smallest unit to  the  Board  Chair  and
Executive Director-bear responsibility for ensuring that RTEC’s EEO  Program
policies and programs are carried out.

EEO responsibilities for RTEC officials, supervisors and managers include:

    • Participating actively in periodic audits of all aspects of employment
      to  identify  and  remove  barriers  obstructing  the  achievement  of
      specified goals and objectives
    •  Holding  regular  discussions  with  other   managers,   supervisors,
      employees, and affinity groups to ensure RTEC policies and  procedures
      are being followed
    • Cooperating  with  the  EEO  Officer  in  review  of  information  and
      investigation of complaints
    • Encouraging employee participation to support the advancement  of  the
      EEO  Program  (e.g.,  professional  development  and   career   growth
      opportunities,   posting   promotional    opportunities,    shadowing,
      mentoring)


Employees who fail to adhere to  the  Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative  Action
Policy, Plan and Program are subject to immediate disciplinary action.


PROGRAM GUIDELINES AND EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES

RTEC believes that special effort is required to prevent discrimination  and
to eliminate it within the Company and the community at  large.  RTEC  shall
continually  conduct  recruitment,  selection,  advancement,   compensation,
transfer and other terms or conditions of employment without  discrimination
as  to  race,  color,  religion,  creed,  gender,  national   origin,   age,
disability, or veteran status, genetic  information  or  any  other  legally
protected status, or other factors which cannot lawfully be used as a  basis
for an employment decision.

Training opportunities and career counseling will be made available  to  all
employees without regard to minority  status.  The  Company  will  establish
goals  to  alleviate  any  under-utilization  of  minorities  and  establish
efforts to fulfill its goals.

*See Employment Practices, orientation  of  driver’s  synopsis,  details  of
driver orientation and resources used to conduct  RTEC  criminal  background
record  searches  and  driving  record  searches  addendums  for  additional
information.


REVIEW OF PERSONNEL PROCESSES

RTEC reviews annually its  personnel  processes  to  determine  whether  its
present procedures assure careful, thorough and systematic consideration  of
the qualifications of known  qualified  individuals  with  disabilities  and
qualified protected veterans.  This review covers all procedures related  to
the filling of job vacancies either by hire or by promotion, as well as  all
training opportunities offered or made available to employees.

The physical  and  mental  job  qualifications  of  all  jobs  are  reviewed
annually to ensure that, to the extent of such  qualifications  requirements
tend to screen out qualified individuals  with  disabilities  and  qualified
disabled  veterans,  job  qualifications  are   consistent   with   business
necessity and the safe performance of the job.

DISSEMINATION OF POLICY, PLAN, AND PROGRAM

Internally

Directors, managers and supervisors are fully informed of RTEC’s policy  and
their role in the implementation of the EEO/AA Plan.   This  information  is
conveyed in the following manner:

   1. By written communication from RTEC’s Chief Executive Officer.


   2. By the inclusion  of  the  Chief  Executive  Officer’s  EEO/AA  Policy
      Statement in  Personnel  Policy  Handbook  and  training  manuals,  as
      appropriate and as applicable.

   3. Through periodic meetings to review RTEC’s Plan, as well as the goals,
      problems, obstacles, and/or concerns encountered in the implementation
      of the Plan.

   4. By informing non-supervisory staff of RTEC’s EEO/AA Policy through the
      following means:

        a.  Posting  Federal  and  State  EEO  notices  and  RTEC’s  policy
           statement on bulletin boards.   These  postings  are  placed  in
           conspicuous, accessible, and  heavily  trafficked  areas,  where
           they can be easily seen and read.


        b. Mentioning RTEC’s EEO/AA Policy  in  publications  prepared  and
           published for employees.  These publications  include,  but  are
           not limited to, employee handbooks and other official  documents
           issued to RTEC’s non-supervisory workforce.

        c. Soliciting from employees’  recommendations  to  improve  RTEC’s
           EEO/AA Program and  requesting  feedback  on  existing  program,
           efforts,  and  actions.   Accountable  for  Action:  RTEC’s  EEO
           Officer.

        d. Explaining the EEO/AA philosophy at all new employee orientation
           sessions and, when appropriate, in RTEC’s training sessions.

any other legally protected status should report his/her complaint to  their
immediate supervisor, Human Resource Manager, EEO Administrative Manager  or

regard to their race, color, religion, creed, gender, national origin,  age,
disability, or veteran status, genetic  information  or  any  other  legally
protected status.  Such actions will include, but  not  be  limited  to  the

forms  of  compensation;  and  selection   of   training.    The   foregoing
requirements of this paragraph will be incorporated in all subcontracts  for
services covered by the contract.  RTEC  will  post  in  accessible  places,
available to employees and applicants for employment, notices setting  forth
the provision of this nondiscrimination clause.

subject to harassment or discrimination because of  race,  color,  religion,

information or any other legally protected status.
