            RTEC'S EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY POLICY STATEMENT


Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. has a strong commitment  to  the
community we serve and our employees.  As an equal opportunity employer,  we
strive to have a workforce that reflects the community we serve.  No  person
is unlawfully excluded from employment opportunities based on  race,  color,
religion,  national  origin,  sex   (including   gender   identity,   sexual
orientation, and pregnancy), age, genetic information,  disability,  veteran
status, or other protected class.

RTEC's Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO) policy applies to  all  employment
actions, including but not limited to, recruitment,  hiring,  selection  for
training, promotion, transfer, demotion, layoff, termination, rates  of  pay
or other forms of compensation.

All applicants and employees have the  right  to  file  complaints  alleging
discrimination.  Retaliation against an individual who  files  a  charge  or
complaint of discrimination, participates in  an  employment  discrimination
proceeding (such as an investigation or lawsuit), or  otherwise  engages  in
protected activity is strictly prohibited and will not be tolerated.

RTEC is committed to proving reasonable  accommodations  to  applicants  and
employees who need them because of a disability or to  practice  or  observe
their religion, absent undue hardship.

As  RTEC's  Executive  Director,  I  maintain  overall  responsibility   and
accountability for RTEC's compliance with its EEO Policy  and  Program.   To
ensure day-to-day management,  including  program  preparation,  monitoring,
and complaint  investigation,  I  have  appointed  Sheila  Stallsworth,  EEO
Manager, 1-800-321-7832, as RTEC's EEO  Officer.   Sheila  Stallsworth  will
report directly to me  and  acts  with  my  authority  with  all  levels  of
management and employees.

All RTEC's  executives,  management,  and  supervisory  personnel,  however,
share in the responsibility  for  implementing  and  monitoring  RTEC's  EEO
Policy and Program within  their  respective  areas  and  will  be  assigned
specific tasks to ensure compliance is achieved.   RTEC  will  evaluate  its
managers' and supervisors' performance on  their  successful  implementation
of RTEC's policies and procedures, in  the  same  way  RTEC  assesses  their
performance regarding other agency's goals.

RTEC is committed to undertaking and developing a written  nondiscrimination
program that sets forth the policies, practices and procedures,  with  goals
and timetables, to which the agency is committed and make  the  EEO  program
available for inspection by an employee or  applicant  for  employment  upon
request.

I  am  personally  committed  to  a  workplace  that  acts  upon  its  daily
responsibility to treat  all  applicants  and  employees  with  dignity  and
respect, as well as equitably under the guidelines of  our  EEO  Policy  and
Program.

ASSURANCES

RTEC has adopted the Affirmative Action Plan for Region  12  Brokerages  and
public transportation and shall use it to  serve  as  a  guide  designed  to
initiate  and  maximize  motivation   and   employment   opportunities   for
minorities, women, disabled individuals, veterans and the poor. This  policy
shall further serve to provide for Equal Opportunity/Affirmative  Action  in
all phases of daily action, inclusive of any and all employees  or  affected
individuals, participants, or beneficiaries.  RTEC  pledges  assistance  and
guidance to positively affect  Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative  Action  needs
within the community.

RTEC abides by all  applicable  Equal  Opportunity  laws,  regulations,  and
policies as set forth to insure nondiscrimination as  an  employer,  service
provider, and administering agency for delegated programs,  projects  and/or
activities. Specific laws and regulations shall govern all actions  of  this
Company. As RTEC has established its  Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative  Action
Policy, Plan and Program  in  accordance  with  the  intent  of  the  rules,
regulations, and guidelines of the various federal, state,  and  local  laws
an agencies having administrative authority in the Equal  Opportunity  area,
RTEC provides assurance of compliance with either human rights  policies  or
directives as may be created and implemented  during  future  periods.  This
Company establishes and implements an Affirmative Action  Plan  and  Program
to insure that positive action steps  are  taken  to  assist  the  Company's
internal operational system as well as any entities outside the Company.


RESPONSIBILITY FOR AFFIRMATIVE ACTION PLAN ADMINISTRATION

Board of Directors

The Board of Directors shall be  responsible  for  the  approval  of  RTEC's
policies, plans and priorities. The Board shall also  approve  policies  and
procedures for the Company subject to grant guidelines.

The  Board  of  Directors  will  be  involved  and  advised  of  the   Equal
Opportunity/Affirmative  Action  Policy,  Plan   and   Program   to   insure
compliance with the applicable laws, regulations, and grant requirements.

The   Board   of   Directors   shall   approve   goals   for    the    Equal
Opportunity/Affirmative  Action  Plan  and  shall  approve   the   planning,
implementation, conduct and evaluation of the Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative
Action Policy, Plan, and Program performance.


The Board of Directors shall have the  authority  to  insure  and  guarantee
implementation in every  phase  of  employment  and  service.  The  policies
invoked by the Board of  Directors  shall  be  binding  for  all  staff  and
applicants for employment.

Executive Director

The Executive Director shall have the responsibility for insuring the  Equal
Opportunity/Affirmative Action Policy  as  outlined  has  been  followed  to
assure compliance with all regulations.

The Executive Director shall appoint  the  EEO  Administrative  Manager  and
shall establish appropriate duties, responsibilities and accountability.

shall strive to insure that every form of prejudice or discrimination  based
on race, color, religion, creed, gender, national origin,  age,  disability,
or veteran status,  genetic  information  or  any  other  legally  protected
status is eradicated.

RTEC EEO Responsibilities

Although the EEO Officer is primarily responsible  for  implementing  RTEC’s
EEO Program, all officials, managers, and supervisors  are  responsible  for
ensuring EEO and must not discriminate based  on  a  protected  class.   All
managers-from the supervisor of the smallest unit to  the  Board  Chair  and
Executive Director-bear responsibility for ensuring that RTEC’s EEO  Program
policies and programs are carried out.

EEO responsibilities for RTEC officials, supervisors and managers include:

    • Participating actively in periodic audits of all aspects of employment
      to  identify  and  remove  barriers  obstructing  the  achievement  of
      specified goals and objectives
    •  Holding  regular  discussions  with  other   managers,   supervisors,
      employees, and affinity groups to ensure RTEC policies and  procedures
      are being followed
    • Cooperating  with  the  EEO  Officer  in  review  of  information  and
      investigation of complaints
    • Encouraging employee participation to support the advancement  of  the
      EEO  Program  (e.g.,  professional  development  and   career   growth
      opportunities,   posting   promotional    opportunities,    shadowing,
      mentoring)


Employees who fail to adhere to  the  Equal  Opportunity/Affirmative  Action
Policy, Plan and Program are subject to immediate disciplinary action.


PROGRAM GUIDELINES AND EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES

RTEC believes that special effort is required to prevent discrimination  and
to eliminate it within the Company and the community at  large.  RTEC  shall
continually  conduct  recruitment,  selection,  advancement,   compensation,
transfer and other terms or conditions of employment without  discrimination
as  to  race,  color,  religion,  creed,  gender,  national   origin,   age,
disability, or veteran status, genetic  information  or  any  other  legally
protected status, or other factors which cannot lawfully be used as a  basis
for an employment decision.

Training opportunities and career counseling will be made available  to  all
employees without regard to minority  status.  The  Company  will  establish
goals  to  alleviate  any  under-utilization  of  minorities  and  establish
efforts to fulfill its goals.

*See Employment Practices, orientation  of  driver’s  synopsis,  details  of
driver orientation and resources used to conduct  RTEC  criminal  background
record  searches  and  driving  record  searches  addendums  for  additional
information.


REVIEW OF PERSONNEL PROCESSES

RTEC reviews annually its  personnel  processes  to  determine  whether  its
present procedures assure careful, thorough and systematic consideration  of
the qualifications of known  qualified  individuals  with  disabilities  and
qualified protected veterans.  This review covers all procedures related  to
the filling of job vacancies either by hire or by promotion, as well as  all
training opportunities offered or made available to employees.

The physical  and  mental  job  qualifications  of  all  jobs  are  reviewed
annually to ensure that, to the extent of such  qualifications  requirements
tend to screen out qualified individuals  with  disabilities  and  qualified
disabled  veterans,  job  qualifications  are   consistent   with   business
necessity and the safe performance of the job.

      problems, obstacles, and/or concerns encountered in the implementation
      of the Plan.

   4. By informing non-supervisory staff of RTEC’s EEO/AA Policy through the
      following means:

        a.  Posting  Federal  and  State  EEO  notices  and  RTEC’s  policy
           statement on bulletin boards.   These  postings  are  placed  in
           conspicuous, accessible, and  heavily  trafficked  areas,  where

        b. Mentioning RTEC’s EEO/AA Policy  in  publications  prepared  and

RTEC PROCEDURES FOR TRACKING AND INVESTIGATING TITLE VII COMPLAINTS

Title  VII  of  the  Civil  Rights  Act  of  1964,  as  amended,   prohibits
discrimination on the basis of race, color, national origin,  sex,  age,  or
disability in programs and services funded, in whole or part,  by  financial
assistance from the United States Government.

All services and  programs  operated  or  sponsored  by  the  Rural  Transit
Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. (RTEC)  located  at  100  Main  Street,  Mount
Vernon, KY 40456 are subject to the requirements and  obligations  of  Title
VII.  It is the intention of RTEC to fully comply with Title VII.

Persons who believe that they have  experienced  or  witnessed  any  act  or
inaction, intentional or otherwise, in any  program,  service,  or  activity
operated by or sponsored by RTEC that results in or may result in  disparate
treatment or impact, or perpetuates the effects of prior  discrimination  of
the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability may  file
a written complaint with RTEC or the U.S. Federal Transit Administration.

Complaints filed  directly  with  Federal  Transit  Administration  must  be
mailed within 180 days of any alleged discrimination. Complaints  should  be
mailed to:

            Federal Transit Administration Office of Civil Rights
            Attention Title VI Program Coordinator
            East Building, 5th. Floor – TCR
            1200 New Jersey Ave., SE
            Washington, D. C, 20590

It shall be the responsibility of the Executive Director, or  her  designee,
to track, investigate and  document  Title  VII  complaints.  If  complaints
cannot be resolved by RTEC, the Kentucky Office of  Transportation  Delivery
will be notified for assistance in reaching a resolution.

RTEC  has  NO  active  investigations/complaints  open  for  this  reporting
period.


HOW TO FILE A TITLE VII COMPLAINT TO RTEC

A person with a Title VII or other complaint may also submit  the  complaint
to RTEC using the following procedures:

1.  A complaint may be submitted in writing and must  include  the  person’s
name and contact information, the date of the incidence,  and  the  identity
of  the  person  or  department  or  service  that  caused  the   complaint.
Complaints may be sent  via  mail,  email,  fax,  or  hand  delivered.   See
attached complaint form.

2.  A complaint may be taken verbally and must  include  the  person’s  name
and contact information, the date of the incidence, and the identity of  the
person, department or service that caused the complaint.

3.  Persons with a complaint may request a neutral third  party  to  hear  a
verbal complaint or assist with a written complaint.   The selection of  the
neutral third party shall be made cooperatively between RTEC and the  person
filing the complaint.

4.  All complaints shall  be  addressed  to  the  RTEC  Executive  Director,
Shirley Cummins.

RTEC COMPLAINT PROCEDURE


1.  The person filing a complaint on the basis of  discrimination  based  on
race, color, national origin, sex, age, or disability will be informed  that
the complaint may be either filed directly with the FTA or with  RTEC.   The
contact information for the FTA will be provided should the  person  opt  to
file directly with FTA.   RTEC’s Executive  Director  shall  be  responsible
for follow up and monitoring FTA action.


2.  If the person opts to file the complaint with RTEC, the  complaint  will
be directed by the Executive Director to the appropriate department  manager
for a fact-finding review. The manager will prepare a  written  response  to
the complaint and submit it to the RTEC Executive Director.

3.  If the  complaint  is  valid  and  supported  by  facts,  the  Executive
Director will order corrective action be taken.

4. The person who filed the complaint will be consulted as to  the  adequacy
of the proposed remedy.   If acceptable, the matter is concluded.

5.  If the proposed remedy is not  acceptable,  the  person  who  filed  the
complaint may request a  hearing  with  the  RTEC  Personnel  Committee  for
purposes of stating their complaint and identifying an appropriate remedy.


6.  The Finance and Personnel Committee will issue a response and  recommend
a remedy within ten days of the hearing.

7.  If acceptable, the matter is concluded.  If not, the person  is  advised
of the appropriate steps to  file  the  complaint  with  the  Department  of
Transportation Delivery and FTA.
