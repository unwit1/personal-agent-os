SUBJECT:  Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative Action Plan 
Equal Employment Opportunity Guidelines for Grant Recipients (EEO) 
  FTA Circular 4704.1 
 
Dear Mrs. Cummins:  
 
Rural Transit Enterprises Coordinated, Inc. Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative Action Plan, 
which includes the implementation of laws and regulations that prohibit discrimination on the basis 
of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, disability, and age in the provision of services to the 
public, has been reviewed and found satisfactory. Your Plan reflects the following objectives for 
public transportation:  
 
a) Statement of Policy 
b) Plan for dissemination both internally and externally 
c) Designation of appropriate personnel responsible for carrying out the EEO Program, 
including the designation of an EEO Officer 
d) Utilization analysis 
e) Goals and timetables to correct identified areas of underutilization or concentration 
f) Assessment of an agency’s employment practices 
g) Plan for monitoring and reporting on the EEO Program. 
 
Your next Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative Action Plan submission will be due by January 
30, 2024. We appreciate your commitment to providing equal opportunities for all persons to 
participate in the valuable services you provide. If you have any questions or need further assistance 
with your Equal Employment Opportunity/Affirmative Action Plan, please contact Project Manager, 
Charles Jackson, or Branch Manager, Tabitha Martin, at (502) 564-7433.  
 
Sincerely, 
 
 
 
Vickie Bourne 
Executive Director 
Office of Transportation Delivery
