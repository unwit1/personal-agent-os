## Privacy handling

This archive mixes organization-level compliance source material with restricted person-level records.

Examples of restricted categories include:
- applicant logs;
- employee/EEO rosters;
- completed AAP self-identification forms;
- EEOC charges/case correspondence;
- individual reasonable-accommodation cases;
- other completed case forms.

Those records must not be copied row-for-row or by PII-bearing filename into the generalized GitHub knowledge corpus. They may contribute privacy-safe aggregate/process evidence and source hashes under `restricted-evidence/`.

Safe organization-level sources—plans, policies, blank forms, instructions, training materials, approval/certification letters, notices, public participation material and similar references—should be preserved in full searchable form.
