# Black Books

- Type: artifact family / liminal knowledge objects
- Status: developing
- Tags: Hermaeus Mora; Apocrypha; knowledge; portals; Dragonborn

## Canon boundary
This page deliberately separates official-game Black Book lore from claims introduced by Apocryphal Library. A mod-original explanation can be useful worldbuilding without becoming evidence for Bethesda/ZeniMax continuity.

## Apocryphal Library continuity evidence

### On Stealing from Apocrypha
- Source: [full text](../../sources/apocryphal-library/books/040000DE-on-stealing-from-apocrypha.txt)
- Compilation FormID: `040000DE`
- Provenance: `verified_mod_original_text`
- Perspective: anti-Mora practical/religious guide.
- Claims in this continuity: Black Books can provide ingress to Apocrypha; the reader is described as remaining partly anchored in the mortal world while fully experiencing Apocrypha; the guide contrasts this with constructing a direct portal and claims Mora may more readily notice the reader.
- Reliability: these mechanics are assertions by the mod-original narrator and require independent official evidence before use in strict canon answers.

### Mananaut's Guide to Apocrypha
- Source: [full text](../../sources/apocryphal-library/books/04000A1D-mananauts-guide-to-apocrypha.txt)
- Compilation FormID: `04000A1D`
- Provenance: `verified_mod_original_text`
- Perspective: exploratory/manual-style account.
- Claims in this continuity: some Black Books function as conduits to Apocrypha; the guide distinguishes them from the Oghma Infinium, proposes an origin involving exceptionally insightful or Mora-inspired mortal writings, describes the reader as physically remaining in Mundus, and claims objects may be carried back from Apocrypha.
- Named examples in this source include *The Sallow Regent* alongside mod-expanded or otherwise continuity-specific titles.
- Reliability: origin theory and travel mechanics are attributed to this source, not normalized as objective official lore.
