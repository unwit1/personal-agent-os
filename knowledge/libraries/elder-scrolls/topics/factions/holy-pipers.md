# Holy Pipers

- Type: Stendarr/Vigilant-associated organization
- Relevance tier: supporting-major within `tes.mod.vicn`
- Status: developing
- Continuity: VICN mod continuity; not base Elder Scrolls canon
- Tags: VIGILANT; GLENMORIL; DAc0da; Sacred Anatomancer; Stendarr; Radiance; Vigilants

## Cross-VICN identity

The **Holy Pipers** recur directly across VIGILANT, GLENMORIL, and DAc0da.

Parent synthesis:
- `../../mods/continuities/vicn/relationships/holy-pipers-thread.md`

## VIGILANT state

VIGILANT directly implements the **Society of the Holy Pipers** as a Sacred-Anatomancer-led splinter from the Vigilants of Stendarr.

Its records associate the organization with:
- kidnapping and torture;
- anatomancy/flesh practices;
- Radiance and Song-of-Life doctrine;
- Blue-Star imagery.

## GLENMORIL state

GLENMORIL expands the organization through:
- excommunication/extremist descriptions;
- Piper's Hill;
- Falkreath-area victims;
- perception/information-hazard claims;
- Sacred Anatomancer leadership traditions.

## DAc0da state

DAc0da's **Vigilant Athanasius** calls himself Keeper of Stendarr of a Holy-Pipers branch and describes duties of:
- teaching Stendarr's doctrine;
- traveling;
- helping the less fortunate;
- rescuing people in danger.

This creates a real continuity tension: DAc0da preserves a benevolent Holy-Piper branch/state while VIGILANT and GLENMORIL preserve apostate/extremist states.

## Current model

Treat the Holy Pipers as a **shared VICN institution with source-dependent historical or branch states**.

Do not assume Athanasius served the Sacred Anatomancer or participated in the later abuses unless a source says so.
