## Core official-lore profile
Miraak is presented in *The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim — Dragonborn* as an ancient Dragonborn and former Dragon Priest whose history is bound to Solstheim, dragons, Hermaeus Mora, and Apocrypha. This dossier treats quest dialogue, environmental records, books, and later retellings as separately attributable evidence rather than collapsing them into a single omniscient biography.

## Relationships
- [Hermaeus Mora](./hermaeus-mora.md) — patron/adversarial master relationship central to Miraak's later history; claims about motives should remain attributed to the relevant speaker or source.
- [Apocrypha](../locations/apocrypha.md) — principal realm associated with Miraak's later existence and the Dragonborn storyline.
- Black Books — artifacts linking mortal readers with Apocrypha and Mora's knowledge.
- Dragons / Dragon Cult — Miraak's Dragon Priest and Dragonborn identities connect him to the dragon-dominated ancient Nordic period.
- Skaal — Solstheim tradition preserves important historical context around Miraak; oral and religious traditions should remain provenance-distinct.

## Source and reliability rules
- Quest events directly witnessed by the player can support event claims, while dialogue about ancient history remains testimony by the speaker.
- Hermaeus Mora's statements are Daedric testimony and should not automatically be treated as neutral narration.
- Skaal traditions should be preserved as culturally situated oral/religious history.
- Later scholarly accounts can corroborate or conflict with those traditions without erasing the distinction.
- Apocryphal Library mod-original material may expand Miraak/Apocrypha themes but must not silently support official-canon claims.
