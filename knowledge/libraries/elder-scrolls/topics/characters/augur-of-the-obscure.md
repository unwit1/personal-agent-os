## Recurring identity

GLENMORIL and DAc0da independently contain the exact unique title **Augur of the Obscure**.

The overlap includes:
- absurd/comic speech masking useful clues;
- non-linear temporal awareness;
- Numidium/Jill/possibility material;
- a relationship to Rolls-On-Roads.

The parent VICN reconciliation therefore treats them as a **high-confidence recurring entity identity**.

## DAc0da-specific evidence

- he is a traveler from an **Adjacent Place**;
- his visible skull-like body is a perceptual/light effect;
- Grabbers inhabit/threaten adjacent realities;
- he experiences meetings and player deaths non-linearly.

These claims should not automatically be copied into GLENMORIL scenes.

## GLENMORIL-specific evidence
