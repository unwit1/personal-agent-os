# Radiance

- Type: recurring VICN metaphysical term
- Relevance tier: major within VICN synthesis
- Status: developing / definitions conflict
- Continuity: VICN mod continuity with external-source overlap
- Tags: VIGILANT; GLENMORIL; UNSLAAD; DAc0da; Mnemo-Li; Blue Star; Holy Pipers; memory; many paths

## Central warning

**Radiance does not currently have one safe universal VICN definition.**

Different works and speakers use the same term in materially different ways.

Parent synthesis:
- `../../mods/continuities/vicn/relationships/radiance-mnemo-li-memory-thread.md`

## VIGILANT

Holy-Piper/Sacred-Anatomancer doctrine invokes **Radiance** alongside:
- Song of Life;
- Blue-Star imagery;
- doctrinal purification/extermination.

This is direct Piper doctrine, not a neutral cosmological definition.

## GLENMORIL

Ja'zel describes a **dark Radiance** dwelling in Flesh and attributes it to **Xero-Lyg**, the Black Star beside the Serpent.

This is Ja'zel's source-local cosmology.

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD gives multiple incompatible/partial Radiance descriptions.

Examples include:
- the Sacred Anatomancer wanting to see a name's Radiance;
- the Hermit describing Radiance as an echo of a past never experienced and a coffin rather than a guiding light;
- an **End of Radiance** location/state.

## DAc0da

Athanasius says mnemolichite showed him a spark of Radiance at the end of a sea of possibilities.

He says attaining Radiance reveals many paths and pluralizes identity (“we”).

DAc0da also contains:
- a direct Altano/VIGILANT path-to-Radiance letter;
- Kagrenac describing Blue-Star **radiance** spilling from the future into the past.

## Current synthesis

High confidence:
- Radiance is a deliberate recurring VICN term.
- It repeatedly appears near identity, memory, transformation, suffering, star imagery, or unusual temporality.

Not established:
- all Radiance references are one energy/substance;
- Radiance = Mnemo-Li;
- Radiance = Blue Star;
- DAc0da Athanasius and VIGILANT Holy-Piper doctrine use the same mechanism;
- GLENMORIL dark Radiance is identical to DAc0da's Sea of Radiance.

Store as a **shared but disputed ontology**.
