## GLENMORIL account

Ja'zel describes Xero-Lyg as:
- a **Black Star** beside the Serpent constellation;
- associated with a **dark Radiance** dwelling in Flesh;
- mythically resembling/coinciding with Namira.

These remain Ja'zel's source-local cosmological claims.

## DAc0da account

Crabbimarco says:
- Xero-Lyg and her son participated in a slave revolt in Lyg;
- the revolt ruined his attempt to seize power;
- the world was later liberated/ascended;
- he became unstable and expects attachment to the present kalpa.

This is a separate Lyg/kalpic narrative.

## External-source caution

Xero-Lyg also exists in broader Elder Scrolls apocryphal/developer-text traditions.

- deliberate VICN continuity reuse;
