## DAc0da mechanism

Mecha-Kanra says Brain-Billies are spirits that:
- sting memories;
- make forgotten things return;
- can be eaten to help prevent forgetting.

DAc0da also implements Brain-Billies mechanically and connects repeated use to **Mnemolic Sign**.

## GLENMORIL context

GLENMORIL uses Brain-Billies in the **Brain-Billy's Sting** dialogue network around:
- Rolls-On-Roads;
- the Augur of the Obscure;
- Romion;
- memory/identity questions.

The exact DAc0da mechanical explanation is not yet independently established there.
