Vanus offers several explicitly speculative theories, including:
- Mnemo-Li protecting memories;
- artificial/manufactured divinity;
- a relation between Mnemo-Li, contradictory causality, and mnemolichite.

The Sea of Radiance later links Mnemo-Li imagery to:
- a sea of possibilities;
- multiple selves;
- Radiance;
- a blood/tower image.

These theories conflict and should remain attributed.
