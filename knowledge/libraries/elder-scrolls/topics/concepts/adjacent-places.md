## VICN recurrence

The phrase **Adjacent Place** recurs independently in DAc0da and UNSLAAD.

Parent comparison:
- `../../mods/continuities/vicn/relationships/lyg-thread.md`

## DAc0da

The Augur of the Obscure says he is a traveler from an Adjacent Place.

He describes Adjacent Places through deliberately strange metaphors and says entities there may fight with weapons of pure mathematics.

DAc0da also uses Adjacent-Place language for:
- old-kalpa material entering the Sea of Causality;
- Numidium isolating Apocryphal contamination.

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD explicitly describes **Lyg** as an adjacent place reachable through a special mirror.

Its witness says:
- fate can be changed there;
- worlds should not be mixed carelessly;
- remaining too long risks attracting Grabbers.

## Current synthesis

Safe:
- VICN repeatedly uses Adjacent Place for realities/spaces that are not ordinary Nirn locations.
- Lyg is explicitly one adjacent place in UNSLAAD.
- Grabbers are a recurring hazard associated with adjacent-reality travel.

Unresolved:
- whether all adjacent places share one cosmology;
- whether every old-kalpa intrusion is travel through Lyg;
- whether DAc0da's Apocrypha compartment and UNSLAAD's mirror route use the same mechanism.
