## Recurrence

Parent comparison:

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD warns that prolonged presence in Lyg/an adjacent place can attract Grabbers.

This directly establishes:
- Grabbers as an adjacent-reality hazard;
- a connection to Lyg travel.

UNSLAAD does not currently provide the same explicit stolen-name mechanic recovered from DAc0da.

## DAc0da

DAc0da's Augur says Grabbers can “suck out all sorts of things.”

Crabbimarco says Grabbers stole the **name** of the King of Dreugh from his memory.

The quest **In Grabbers' Hands** supplies mechanical corroboration:
- boss script `DcdNymicStealerAliasScript`;
- a quest property named `NymicObj`;
- a tangible nymic/name object;
- the **Book of the Prisoner**;
- an objective to **restore your name**.

The exact Papyrus runtime logic is unavailable from the ESM, but the name/nymic relation is structurally strong.
