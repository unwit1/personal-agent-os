## GLENMORIL

GLENMORIL associates Blue Star with:
- Mnemo-Li / Blue-Orphan-adjacent language;
- memory preservation;
- sanity/remembering;
- transformation;
- freedom / reaching beyond;
- Yelem, Jhunal, Ja'zel, and test-subject material.

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD includes:
- a Hermit dream in which the Blue Star survives destructive twilight;
- transformation through contact with it;
- Orlando saying he has met the player at the Blue Star;
- related Mnemo-Li / Blue-Orphan vocabulary elsewhere.

## DAc0da

DAc0da gives two Dwemer-facing Blue-Star nodes:
- Kagrenac: radiance spills from the **future into the past** and dominates non-temporal space;
- Dumac: asks about a **hidden Blue Star** during the Red-Moment-adjacent dispute.

The two are not explicitly proven identical.

## VIGILANT

Holy-Piper/Sacred-Anatomancer liturgy invokes the **Blue Star** alongside:
- redshift;
- Radiance;
- repeated threes;
- Song-of-Life doctrine.

## Identity boundaries

Do not automatically equate:
- Blue Star and Mnemo-Li;
- Blue Star and Blue Orphan;
- Blue Star and Xero-Lyg;
- Blue Star and Radiance;
- every Blue-Star appearance with one event/state.
