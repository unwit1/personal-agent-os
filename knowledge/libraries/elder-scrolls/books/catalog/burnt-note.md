## Research summary
A conspiratorial letter to Valano describing a false-flag plan against Queen Ayrenn: agents are to ambush First Auridon Marines, use their uniforms and weapons, and attack Silsailen so blame falls on Ayrenn. The letter invokes the Veiled Queen and promises Valano political advancement.
