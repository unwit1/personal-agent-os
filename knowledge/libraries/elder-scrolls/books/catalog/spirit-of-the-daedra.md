## Research summary
An in-universe explanation of Daedric existence and social organization. It distinguishes destruction of a Daedra's body from destruction of its animus, discusses the Darkness and return after bodily destruction, and describes fear, clan bonds, oath bonds, hierarchy, and service among Daedra.
