## Research summary
A Baandari story preserved by Eilirsu-dro about an invading Dark Elf mage, the “Fiery One,” who mistakes an ordinary gem for a sacred source of power. The tale emphasizes Baandari wit, survival, storytelling, and the contextual rather than intrinsic value of the Eye of Baan Dar.
