## Research summary
One of the Nerevarine prophetic texts. It enumerates seven trials associated with the Incarnate, including resistance to disease/age, recognition through Moon-and-Star, becoming Hortator, recognition by the Velothi tribes, confronting inherited sin, and dealing with the Tribunal/Dagoth Ur crisis.
