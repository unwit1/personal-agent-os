## Extracted claims
1. Favis identifies himself as the Bell Tower watchman and describes Prnell as the Harbor Tower counterpart in the ship-arrival signaling system.
2. Favis says Restless League members had previously come looking for Iszara; he also gives his own negative/admiring characterization of the League.
3. Favis reports that sailors from the Garamar said they saw A'tor aboard his flagship immediately before a dragon burned the ship, with no survivors from the flagship. Epistemic mode: hearsay reported by Favis from claimed eyewitness sailors.
4. Favis says he had heard no foul-play story about Thassad's death and describes Thassad as hostile to the League. Epistemic mode: personal knowledge / reported political characterization.
5. Favis attributes the Crowns' defeat primarily to Imperial military power and describes the Old Quarter as their final stand after A'tor's fleet was destroyed. Epistemic mode: historical testimony/opinion.
6. Favis says the League previously destroyed Richton's flagship and that Richton subsequently rounded up people associated with League members. Epistemic mode: local historical testimony.
7. Favis describes occupied Stros M'Kai as depopulated and economically diminished compared with its past. Epistemic mode: local first-person assessment.
8. Favis explicitly says he has not personally seen the dragon and is repeating survivor reports that Richton keeps it in the Catacombs and feeds it prisoners. Epistemic mode: explicit hearsay.
9. Favis characterizes Richton as a capable soldier but cruel ruler. Epistemic mode: evaluative opinion.
10. The bell interaction shows Favis concluding Cyrus repaired the bell even after Cyrus says nothing was wrong with it. Epistemic significance: demonstrated fallibility in interpreting events.
