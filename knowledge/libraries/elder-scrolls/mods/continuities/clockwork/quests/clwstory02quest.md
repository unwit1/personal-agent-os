# Steam-Powered

Quest FormID: `052908C1`  
Editor ID: `CLWStory02Quest`

## Journal stages

### Stage 10

A strange metal man at the gate introduced himself as "Lahar" and informed me that I have found Clockwork Castle. He's asked me to follow him into the castle to meet someone named "Lamashtu".

### Stage 20

Lahar has led me to the keep rooftop, where a metal woman waits for me. This "Lamashtu" wants to speak to me, apparently...

### Stage 30

Lamashtu told me that I can leave Clockwork Castle and return to Skyrim by using the "Travel Machine" beneath the keep. She directed me back to Lahar, who can lead me to the machine.

### Stage 40

Lamashtu told me that I can leave Clockwork Castle and return to Skyrim by using the "Travel Machine" beneath the keep. She directed me back to Lahar, who has warned me that the machine isn't working, but is leading me to it anyway.

### Stage 50

Lamashtu told me that I can leave Clockwork Castle and return to Skyrim by using the "Travel Machine" beneath the keep. Lahar has warned me that the machine isn't working, and suggested that I enter it to see for myself.

### Stage 60

Lamashtu told me that I can leave Clockwork Castle and return to Skyrim by using the "Travel Machine" beneath the keep. Lahar warned me that the machine isn't working however, and sure enough, nothing happened when I entered it. I should see what he has to say about it.

### Stage 70

I am trapped in Clockwork Castle unless I can restore steam-power to the machines here. Lahar tells me I can do this by going down into "Nurndural" beneath the castle, and replacing the breached sections of the steam pipeline there. Lamashtu has the key to the mausoleum, which is apparently where I can find the passage to Nurndural.

### Stage 80

I am trapped in Clockwork Castle unless I can restore steam-power to the machines here. Lahar tells me I can do this by going down into "Nurndural" beneath the castle, and replacing the breached sections of the steam pipeline there. Lamashtu has given me the key to the mausoleum, which is apparently where I can find the passage to Nurndural.

### Stage 90

I have found the entrance to Nurndural, beneath Clockwork Castle - along with the main steam pipeline. Lahar tells me I can follow the pipeline from the entrance to find the ten breaches that need to be repaired. Each ruptured point must be replaced with a new pipe section. These replacement pipes can either be found throughout Nurndural or - with some knowledge of working Dwemer metal - cast at a forge myself.

### Stage 100

I have repaired the main steam pipeline in Nurndural. I should find Lahar and see if the machines in Clockwork Castle have indeed been restored to working order, allowing me to return to Skyrim.

### Stage 105

I have repaired the main steam pipeline in Nurndural. I should find Lahar and see if the machines in Clockwork Castle have indeed been restored to working order, allowing me to return to Skyrim.

### Stage 110

I have repaired the main steam pipeline in Nurndural. I should find Lahar and see if the machines in Clockwork Castle have indeed been restored to working order, allowing me to return to Skyrim.

### Stage 120

I have repaired the steam pipeline and restored the machines in Clockwork Castle to working order. According to Lahar I should now be able to use the Travel Machine to return to Skyrim.

### Stage 130

When I entered the Travel Machine in order to return to Skyrim, something stopped me. I simply turned and walked right back out again. I... can't leave. Perhaps Lahar or Lamashtu will know what is happening to me.

### Stage 140

I asked Lamashtu if she knew what keeps me from using the Travel Machine to leave Clockwork Castle. She said she would tell me, but first revealed that she and the other Gilded were all once living Dwemer people. Before she could explain herself fully, she lapsed into some kind of nonsense speak, and I can't get anything else out of her.
