# Castle Mausoleum

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

The Chlodovech family mausoleum is both a burial structure and the castle-side access route to Nurndural.

Ludwig's journals say mausoleum expansion in 4E 18 breached ancient Dwemer structures beneath the castle, beginning direct contact with the Gilded. In the player's story, Lamashtu provides the mausoleum key so the player can descend and repair the main steam pipeline.

## Evidence

- `BOOK 05332EA2`, `05332EA7` — expansion and discovery beneath the castle.
- `QUST 052908C1 stages 70-90` — mausoleum key and access to Nurndural.
- Lahar dialogue also refers to placing Ludwig's body in the mausoleum once access is available.
