# Velothi Mountain Tunnels

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

The Velothi Mountain Tunnels form the player-facing approach to Clockwork Castle and were once part of the Chlodovech family's mountain trade route. A landslide newly exposes ruins near Cragwallow Slope, drawing Camilla and later the player inside.

Camilla's trail notes document an unnamed female presence following her, her growing inability to leave, and eventual death. Later Clockwork story material identifies the tunnel-stalking entity as Shadow.

For the player, collapses close both the entrance and the route back after emerging into Clockwork Valley, turning exploration into forced forward movement.
