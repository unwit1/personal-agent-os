# Velothi Mountain Tunnels

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
LCTN: `05063E39` / `CLWVelothiTunnelsIntLocation`

The approach is split into three named cells:

- `CELL 05063DB9` — **Velothi Mountain Tunnels**
- `CELL 05079DDF` — **Velothi Tunnels - Bone Hollow**
- `CELL 050B78E0` — **Velothi Tunnels - Glass Lake**

A landslide exposes the route near Cragwallow Slope, drawing Camilla and later the player inside. Camilla's trail notes document an unnamed female presence, increasing inability to leave, and eventual death. Later story evidence identifies the presence as Shadow.

The technical Shadow-management packages sharpen that route: internal stages 10–30 target markers in the first tunnel cell, while stages 40–50 target Bone Hollow. The Shadow management VMAD also references the dedicated `Shadow Under The Mountain` slow-time spell whose description echoes Camilla's “Time stood still” note.

For the player, collapses close both the entry and the route back after emerging into Clockwork Valley.
