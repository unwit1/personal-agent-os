# Clockwork Valley

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

Clockwork Castle stands in an isolated high valley beyond the Velothi Mountain Tunnels. The player reaches the valley after the tunnel route collapses behind them.

The valley's isolation is both physical and narrative: by the mod's events, ordinary overland access has failed, the castle's historical trade route is gone, and the Travel Machine is the meaningful remaining route to the outside world.
