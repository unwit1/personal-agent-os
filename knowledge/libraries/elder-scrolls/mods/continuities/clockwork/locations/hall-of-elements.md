# Hall of Elements

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

The Hall of Elements is a named Nurndural area encountered while searching for Amalgam. At *Crystalline Heart* stage 40, the player follows unexplained music there as the next lead in the search.

Current evidence supports its role as a search location but does not justify a more specific historical function.
