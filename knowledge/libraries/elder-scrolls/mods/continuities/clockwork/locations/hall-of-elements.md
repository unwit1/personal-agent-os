# Hall of Elements

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
CELL: `0537A08F` / `CLWNurndural01` / **Nurndural - Hall of Elements**  
LCTN: `0537A210` / `CLWNurnduralInt01Location`

The Hall of Elements is a distinct Nurndural interior. During *Crystalline Heart* stage 40 the player follows unexplained music here while searching for Amalgam.

It is also the exact location of the scripted three-Gilded introduction in `SCEN 05384489`: the Gilded01, Gilded02, and Gilded03 scene packages each target a marker placed in this CELL.

The record structure therefore identifies the Hall as a recurring encounter/story space, while still not establishing a more specific ancient institutional function.
