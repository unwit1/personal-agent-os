# Master Bedroom and hidden chamber

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

The Master Bedroom is one of the locations Lahar suggests searching for Ludwig's journals. A hidden chamber behind its walls contains Ludwig's desiccated corpse and the final portion of his written record.

The room therefore serves as the endpoint of *A Bed of Dust* and materially confirms Ludwig's extreme final isolation.
