# Glass Garden

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

The Glass Garden is a heated castle garden dependent on the steam system. When steam is lost, Lahar says the castle is too cold for it to grow. After the pipeline is restored he replants it, and later reports that regrowth is progressing well and may be ready for harvest.

Ludwig's dated journals record the older installation becoming functional in 4E 21.
