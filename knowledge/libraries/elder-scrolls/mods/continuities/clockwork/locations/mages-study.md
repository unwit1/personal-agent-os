# Mage's Study

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

The Mage's Study is a castle work/study area associated with books and magical crafting. It is one of the suggested locations for Ludwig's journals.

After later repairs, Lahar reports restoring the Alchemy and Enchanting tables there. The player can also provide a Heart Stone and three empty Greater Soul Gems so Lahar can construct a Staff Enchanter in the Study.
