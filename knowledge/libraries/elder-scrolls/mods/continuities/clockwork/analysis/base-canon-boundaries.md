# Base-TES continuity boundaries and crosslinks

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

Clockwork deliberately attaches its story to major Elder Scrolls concepts, but Lorekeeper must distinguish **reference** from **promotion into base canon**.

The current base-TES dossiers for several relevant concepts are still priority stubs. These links therefore function as routing/provenance pointers, not as detailed canonical fact checks.

## Kagrenac

Clockwork reference:
- Lamashtu says her death coincided with Kagrenac striking the Heart of Lorkhan.
- She describes Kagrenac as the Chief Tonal Architect.
- She says the Nurndural immortality project was distinct from his but influenced by his Brass God.

Base-library anchors:
- `knowledge/libraries/elder-scrolls/topics/characters/kagrenac.md`
- `knowledge/libraries/elder-scrolls/topics/characters/kagrenac-chief-tonal.md`

Both are currently marked **priority stub — source expansion pending**.

**Boundary:** Clockwork's Lamashtu-specific accident and its consequences remain `tes.mod.clockwork` claims.

## Heart of Lorkhan

Clockwork uses the Heart as the event against which Lamashtu dates her own death.

Base-library anchor:
- `knowledge/libraries/elder-scrolls/topics/characters/lorkhan.md` — currently a priority stub.

**Boundary:** the temporal association in Clockwork does not establish that the Heart caused the Gilding accident in base continuity.

## Numidium / Brass God

Lamashtu says Nurndural's Gilded project used a different immortality method but was influenced by Kagrenac's Brass God, calling Gilded bodies **“Numidium in miniature.”**

The current library has Numidium-related tags and character pointers but not yet a mature standalone sourced Numidium dossier.

**Boundary:** “Numidium in miniature” is Lamashtu's analogy inside Clockwork. It is not a base-TES classification of Gilded or Dwemer automata.

## Red Mountain and the Dwemer disappearance

Clockwork places Lamashtu's death at the same moment as the Red Mountain event she learned about later from Ludwig's library.

Crucially, Lamashtu also says:
- she did not know Kagrenac personally;
- Red Mountain was far from Nurndural;
- she learned the historical timing later;
- she has **no answer** for the disappearance of the Dwemer.

**Boundary:** Clockwork intentionally leaves the larger disappearance unresolved. Its story supplies a local anomaly, not a universal answer.

## Soul gems

Clockwork builds a very specific model around crystalline hearts:
- the soul resides in a soul-gem heart;
- the soul drives the machine;
- the machine body supplies consciousness back to the soul;
- a compatible empty heart can receive a transferred Gilded soul.

The current base library does not yet contain a sufficiently developed source-backed soul-gem topic for a rigorous comparison.

**Boundary:** generic TES soul-gem lore must not be used to silently validate Clockwork's specific soul/body feedback theory.

## Retrieval rule

When a user asks “is this Elder Scrolls canon?” Lorekeeper should answer at the claim level:

1. identify what Clockwork says;
2. mark it as `tes.mod.clockwork`;
3. link any relevant base dossier;
4. report the base dossier's evidence maturity;
5. never convert absence of contradiction into canonical confirmation.
