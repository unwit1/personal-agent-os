# Contradictions, revisions, and apparent tensions

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

This file distinguishes true contradictions from later reinterpretations or claims that can coexist.

## Lamashtu: "my soul split in two" vs "we were both copies"

### Earlier model

After her replacement heart is installed, Lamashtu says that when she died, only half her soul entered the crystalline heart and the other half became Shadow.

Evidence: `INFO 05679F5A`, *Crystalline Heart* stage 160.

### Later model

After Shadow is drawn into her, Lamashtu says reunion felt like looking at her reflection. She concludes that both were copies of the living Lamashtu rather than two incomplete halves.

Evidence: `INFO 05788344`.

### Lorekeeper treatment

This is an **explicit revision**, not two equally current facts. Spoiler-aware retrieval should show the split model before the final quest and the copy model afterward.

## Lahar: creator of the Gilded vs "just a caretaker"

Lamashtu identifies Lahar as the father of the Gilded and says he devised the soul-transfer process.

Evidence: `INFO 0566AC24`, `0566AC27`.

Lahar later rejects the claim and says he is only a caretaker.

Evidence: `INFO 0569D6F3`.

### Lorekeeper treatment

The strongest synthesis is not that one speaker must be lying. The mod repeatedly establishes Lahar's autobiographical degradation, so his denial is itself evidence of identity loss. Still, the creator claim remains attributed to Lamashtu.

## "The Gilded were Dwemer" vs "the Gilded are copies"

These statements are compatible if "were Dwemer" refers to origin and "copies" refers to continuity of identity.

Lamashtu says dying Dwemer underwent the Gilding process, but later emphasizes that the surviving machine persons are not the long-lost original Dwemer in a simple recovery sense.

Evidence: `INFO 0566AC27`, `056934C6`, `05788344`.

### Lorekeeper treatment

Do not flatten this into either "they are literally the original Dwemer unchanged" or "they have nothing to do with those Dwemer." The mod is specifically interested in the ambiguity between origin and personal continuity.

## "Lamashtu cannot die" vs heart failure can kill her

Lamashtu and related dialogue sometimes frame Gilded bodies as effectively deathless, while Lahar says a degrading or shattered heart can result in permanent death.

### Lorekeeper treatment

This is best treated as **conditional immortality**: ordinary bodily damage is not equivalent to mortal death, but destruction/failure of the soul-gem heart remains existentially dangerous.

Evidence: Lahar *Crystalline Heart* dialogue; heart-replacement sequence.

## Amalgam is "destroyed" vs Lamashtu doubts destruction is final

The quest can frame the encounter as defeating/destroying Amalgam, but Lamashtu responds that this would not be his first "destruction."

Evidence: `INFO 0566AC22`.

### Lorekeeper treatment

Physical defeat of an Amalgam body should not automatically be indexed as confirmed final death.

## Shadow as hostile captor vs lonely dependent

Before reunion, Shadow is experienced as a threatening entity who traps the player.

After reunion, Lamashtu interprets the same behavior as loneliness and an inability to communicate.

Evidence: quest events; `INFO 0578D460`.

### Lorekeeper treatment

These are two perspectives on the same behavior, not mutually exclusive facts. The later explanation adds motive without erasing the harm or coercion of the earlier actions.

## Kagrenac timing vs Dwemer disappearance explanation

Lamashtu says her death coincided with Kagrenac striking the Heart and associates that moment with something going wrong.

She also explicitly says she has no answer for the disappearance of the Dwemer.

### Lorekeeper treatment

Clockwork supports a **local temporal/possibly causal connection to Lamashtu's accident**, not a complete solution to the canonical Dwemer disappearance.

Evidence: `INFO 05679F5A`, `0568E3AD`.
