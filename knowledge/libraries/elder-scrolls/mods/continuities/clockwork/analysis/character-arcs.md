# Character arcs

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

## Lamashtu

**Initial state:** controlled, distant, ancient, and apparently the most intact Gilded.

**Crisis:** her heart begins to fail just as she attempts to explain the Gilded and the player's entrapment.

**Recovery:** the player and Lahar move her soul into a replacement heart.

**Interpretive shift:** she first understands Shadow as her missing half, then after reunion understands both herself and Shadow as copies of the dead living Lamashtu.

**End state:** more self-aware but not "restored" to an original whole. She accepts continued existence despite recognizing herself as a ghost/copy and gives the player freedom to come and go.

## Lahar

**Ancient role:** according to Lamashtu, architect/father of the Gilded project.

**Present role:** caretaker, repairer, gardener, guide, trader, and protector of the castle.

**Core tragedy:** he retains extraordinary procedural competence but has lost enough autobiographical continuity to deny or fail to recognize his own authorship.

**Arc shape:** unlike Lamashtu, he does not receive a dramatic restoration. His character is the persistence of care after identity has eroded.

## Shadow

**Initial presentation:** silent supernatural pursuer and source of dread.

**Middle function:** force preventing the player from leaving Clockwork Castle.

**Lamashtu's first interpretation:** missing half of her soul.

**Final interpretation:** another copy of the original Lamashtu, deprived of body and voice.

**Motivating emotion after reunion:** loneliness.

**Arc shape:** monster → missing self → parallel self.

## Amalgam

**Origin:** one of the early Gilded.

**Response to degradation:** radical self-modification and accumulation of hearts.

**Driving fear:** irreversible nonexistence if a single heart breaks.

**Paradox:** modifications intended to preserve him make his body increasingly destructive to the hearts he depends on, intensifying the need to take more.

**Arc shape:** survival strategy becomes self-perpetuating pathology.

## Ludwig Chlodovech

**Initial state:** inheritor of a castle and commercial/family legacy.

**Middle period:** fascinated collaborator who integrates Dwemer/Gilded technology into daily life.

**Decline:** staff and Gilded social contacts disappear while the castle becomes more automated.

**Late state:** intensely isolated, barely speaking, dependent on systems that remove the need for contact, ultimately hidden and forgotten within his own home.

**Arc shape:** mastery of environment becomes withdrawal from humanity.

## Camilla

**Initial state:** aggressive, opportunistic treasure hunter.

**Middle:** fascinated by the newly exposed ruins and potential wealth.

**Decline:** begins perceiving the unseen follower, becomes unable to leave, and abandons practical plans.

**End:** waits to die in the tunnels.

**Narrative role:** a miniature prefiguration of the player's later entrapment by Shadow.

## Player

**Beginning:** investigator following a violent encounter and treasure-hunter trail.

**First loss of agency:** trapped physically by tunnel collapses.

**Second:** trapped mechanically by failed steam infrastructure.

**Third:** trapped psychologically/supernaturally by Shadow even after the machine works.

**End:** gains voluntary mobility and may choose Clockwork as a home.

**Arc shape:** involuntary arrival → understanding → restored agency.
