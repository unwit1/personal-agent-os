# Source reliability matrix

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

Reliability here means **usefulness for a particular class of claim**, not an overall truth score.

## Lamashtu

**Strongest for**
- direct experience of the Gilding project,
- her own body and heart,
- the Gilded generation model,
- memories recovered after reunion with Shadow.

**Limitations**
- mortal-life memories are explicitly distant/vague;
- she did not know Kagrenac personally;
- she learned historical Red Mountain timing later from Ludwig's library;
- her initial "split soul" interpretation is later revised.

**Retrieval rule**
Treat first-person Gilded-process testimony as high-value evidence, but mark Red Mountain causation and Shadow metaphysics as testimony/revision-sensitive.

## Lahar

**Strongest for**
- present-day castle operation,
- repair procedures,
- Nurndural access,
- machinery,
- maintenance routines.

**Limitations**
- autobiographical identity is degraded;
- he rejects or cannot remember Lamashtu's claim that he created the Gilded;
- he describes himself as only a caretaker.

**Retrieval rule**
Use him confidently for current technical operation, cautiously for his own ancient biography.

## Ludwig Chlodovech

**Strongest for**
- dated human history of Clockwork Castle,
- construction and modernization,
- his own actions and mental/social withdrawal,
- first contact with Lahar and Lamashtu.

**Limitations**
- journals are first-person and subjective;
- conclusions about Gilded motives are observations, not omniscient facts;
- later entries reflect severe isolation.

**Retrieval rule**
Excellent chronological source when describing what Ludwig records, with motive claims kept attributed.

## Camilla

**Strongest for**
- immediate tunnel conditions,
- the presence following her,
- her own increasing inability to leave.

**Limitations**
- she lacks later context and never identifies Shadow by name;
- her final notes are written under severe distress.

**Retrieval rule**
Use as primary evidence for the pre-player haunting; identify the presence as Shadow only through later cross-source reconstruction.

## Amalgam

**Strongest for**
- his own need for multiple hearts,
- his fear of permanent destruction,
- effects of his enlarged body on heart survival.

**Limitations**
- identity is heavily degraded;
- dialogue is distributed across a primary NPC form, an eight-form encounter pool, and remote speaker-horn scenes.

**Retrieval rule**
Treat repeated self-statements across technical forms as semantically Amalgam, but preserve form-level attribution distinctions.

## Shadow

**Strongest for**
- direct observed behavior: stalking, compulsion, confrontation.

**Limitations**
- almost no ordinary direct testimony;
- motives and subjective experience are primarily interpreted by post-reunion Lamashtu.

**Retrieval rule**
Separate observed Shadow behavior from Lamashtu's later explanation of that behavior.

## Player quest journal

**Strongest for**
- event order,
- objectives,
- what the player currently believes/knows.

**Limitations**
- journal entries often preserve provisional interpretations current at that stage.

**Retrieval rule**
Excellent for chronology and spoiler gating; do not automatically treat every stage summary as the story's final metaphysical answer.

## Generic Gilded dialogue

**Strongest for**
- collective ambient/combat behavior and vocabulary.

**Limitations**
- 519 records resolve to a broad actor pool rather than one named individual.

**Retrieval rule**
Never quote these as Lahar, Lamashtu, or another specific Gilded without stronger scene/condition evidence.
