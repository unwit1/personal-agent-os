# Kagrenac and Red Mountain claims

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: continuity-bound claim cluster

Clockwork connects Lamashtu's death to the same moment Kagrenac struck the Heart of Lorkhan at Red Mountain. She says that coincidence accompanied something going wrong with her gilding and Shadow's emergence.

The mod does **not** make Lamashtu an authority who solves the canonical Dwemer disappearance. She says she did not know Kagrenac personally, learned the historical timing much later from Ludwig's library, and explicitly says she has no answer for where the Dwemer went.

Nurndural's immortality project is presented as separate from Kagrenac's, though Kagrenac's Brass God influenced its machine-body design.

## Core evidence

- `INFO 05679F5A` — death coincides with Kagrenac striking the Heart; associated with split.
- `INFO 0568E3A7` — different immortality method, Brass God influence.
- `INFO 0568E3A9`, `0568E3AB` — limits of personal knowledge.
- `INFO 0568E3AD` — explicitly denies knowing the Dwemer disappearance's answer.
- `INFO 056934C6` — Gilded are copies, not recovered missing Dwemer.

## Continuity policy

These are Clockwork-continuity claims, not automatic evidence for Bethesda continuity.
