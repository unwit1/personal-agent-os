# Kagrenac and Red Mountain claims

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: continuity-bound claim cluster

Clockwork connects Lamashtu's death to the same moment she says Kagrenac struck the Heart of Lorkhan at Red Mountain. She associates that coincidence with something going wrong during her gilding and Shadow's emergence.

The mod does **not** make Lamashtu an authority who solves the canonical Dwemer disappearance. She says she did not know Kagrenac personally, that Red Mountain was far from Nurndural, that she learned the historical timing only later from Ludwig's library, and that she has no answer for where the Dwemer went.

Nurndural's immortality project is presented as separate from Kagrenac's, though Lamashtu says his Brass God influenced its machine-body design and calls the Gilded “Numidium in miniature.”

## Core evidence

- `INFO 05679F5A` — death coincides with Kagrenac striking the Heart; Lamashtu associates this with the split.
- `INFO 0568E3A7` — distinct immortality method; Brass God influence; “Numidium in miniature.”
- `INFO 0568E3A9`, `0568E3AB` — limits of Lamashtu's personal knowledge.
- `INFO 0568E3AD` — explicitly says she has no answer for the Dwemer disappearance.
- `INFO 056934C6` — Gilded are copies, not recovered missing Dwemer.
