# Gilding process

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: technology / ritual / soul transfer

Nurndural's Gilding process sought to preserve dying Dwemer in constructed bodies. Lamashtu describes soul-trap magic, molten metal, the subject's death, a crystalline soul-gem heart, and—by her generation—the subject's own gilded bones incorporated into the replacement body.

Earlier bodies without the subject's bones were psychologically traumatic. Lamashtu says those subjects did not survive the transition. The process was dangerous and accompanied by a warning alarm; she says the alarm was the last sound she heard before death, later reproduced by Shadow's wail.
