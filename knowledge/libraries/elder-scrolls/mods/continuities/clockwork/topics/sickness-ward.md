# Sickness Ward

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: location / institution

The Sickness Ward is Nurndural's hospital area. Lamashtu says she and the other future Gilded were terminally ill Dwemer sent there because they were dying; the desire to avoid death motivated the Gilded experiment.

During *Crystalline Heart*, the player follows Amalgam into the Ward and encounters remains/machinery associated with the old project.
