# The Gilded

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: people / immortality experiment

## Summary

The Gilded are Dwemer-derived, machine-bodied persons created in Nurndural as an attempt to survive terminal disease. Lamashtu says Lahar's project captured a dying subject's soul in a crystalline soul-gem heart and placed it in a metal body. The soul drives the machine while the machine supplies consciousness back to the soul.

Lamashtu describes three developmental generations: earliest **first children** whose identities washed away; **second children** such as Lahar whose identities survived but distorted; and herself as the **third child**, designed for greater stability.

Clockwork explicitly limits the wider implication: Lamashtu says the remaining Gilded are **copies**, not recovered long-lost Dwemer.
