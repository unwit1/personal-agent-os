# Crystalline hearts

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: technology / soul magic

A Gilded's crystalline heart is a soul gem installed in the machine body. Lamashtu says the soul resides in the gem and drives the machine while the machine body supplies consciousness back to the soul.

The mod directly depicts heart replacement when Lamashtu's original heart fails: Lahar uses a soul-transference machine to move her soul into an empty darkened heart recovered from Amalgam.

Amalgam treats multiple hearts as redundancy against permanent death, but says his enlarged body breaks them one after another.
