# Castle services and restoration states

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`

Clockwork Castle's player-home functions unlock in stages rather than all at once.

## Steam restoration

After the main steam system is restored, Lahar says he can use the Travel Machine for supply runs and trading, the Work Room becomes safe because its exhaust fans function again, food becomes available, and the Glass Garden can be replanted.

This is the castle's main **infrastructure-restoration** milestone, but it occurs before Shadow's hold on the player is resolved.

## Later repairs

After Lamashtu's heart crisis, Lahar repairs the Alchemy and Enchanting stations in the Mage's Study and reports that the Glass Garden has matured further.

After *I Against I*, he begins using the Armoury to display mementos of the player's achievements.

## Optional Staff Enchanter

The player may separately fund a Staff Enchanter upgrade by supplying a Heart Stone and three empty Greater Soul Gems. That upgrade also enables Lahar's blank-staff trade path.

The important narrative distinction is that the castle progressively becomes a **home chosen and customized by the player** only after its earlier role as an involuntary enclosure has been dismantled.
