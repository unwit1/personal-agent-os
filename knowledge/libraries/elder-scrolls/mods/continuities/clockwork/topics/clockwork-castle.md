# Clockwork Castle

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: location / Chlodovech estate

Clockwork Castle began as **Chlodovech Castle**, built by Maximilian Chlodovech in a mountain valley on a proposed land trade route between Skyrim and Morrowind. The Red Year destroyed the Morrowind-side route but Maximilian completed the castle as the family home.

After Ludwig met the Gilded, the estate became increasingly mechanized: hot/cold running water, steam heating, pneumatic tubes, the restored Travel Machine, and other labor-saving systems. Ludwig records that outsiders eventually called it **Clockwork Castle**.

Those conveniences also made the household increasingly self-sufficient; as staff left and most Gilded withdrew, Ludwig could live with almost no human contact.
