# Identity and memory degradation

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: theme / metaphysics of the Gilded

Clockwork separates bodily survival from preservation of identity.

Lamashtu's three-children analogy models three durability levels: earliest Gilded lose themselves rapidly; second-generation Gilded such as Lahar retain more continuity but distort; Lamashtu is the final third-generation subject intended for greater stability.

Lahar demonstrates selective degradation: he retains complex technical ability but rejects or cannot remember Lamashtu's claim that he invented the Gilded process; his self-concept has contracted to "caretaker."

After reuniting with Shadow, Lamashtu does not conclude the original woman is restored; she concludes that both machine-bound Lamashtu and Shadow were copies of the dead mortal woman.

Ludwig supplies a human parallel through progressive isolation, repetitive routine, loss of speech, and fear of outside judgment.
