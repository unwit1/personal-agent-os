# Lamashtu's voice and speech mechanism

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: Gilded physiology / machine-body interface

Lamashtu explicitly says she has no mouth, lips, tongue, or throat. Her speech is mechanical: her crystalline heart shivers in her chest and words are emitted through a speaker horn.

This is useful evidence for how a Gilded body mediates an otherwise disembodied soul-pattern. It should not be generalized to every Gilded without additional evidence, because the line is specifically Lamashtu describing her own body.

## Core evidence

- `INFO 057BFF32` — Lamashtu describes the lack of ordinary speech organs, heart vibration, and speaker-horn output.
- `INFO 05506BB4` — establishes the crystalline heart as her soul gem and central body component.
