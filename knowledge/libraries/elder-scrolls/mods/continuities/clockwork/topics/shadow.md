# Shadow

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Topic type: entity / soul-copy phenomenon

Shadow stalks the player through the Velothi tunnels and prevents departure through the Travel Machine.

Lamashtu initially says her soul split when her death coincided with Kagrenac striking the Heart of Lorkhan: one half entered her crystalline heart and the other became Shadow. After reunion, she revises this: the encounter felt like looking at her reflection, and both she and Shadow were copies of the living Lamashtu rather than complementary halves.

Post-reunion memory frames Shadow's motive primarily as loneliness. She called to the player and kept the player close because she could not tolerate being alone or communicate normally.
