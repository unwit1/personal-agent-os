# Crystalline hearts

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Object type: soul gem / Gilded core

A crystalline heart is the soul-gem core installed in a Gilded machine body. Lamashtu says her soul resides in the heart; the soul drives the body and the body in turn supplies consciousness to the soul.

The player handles at least two important states of heart during *Crystalline Heart*:

- Lamashtu's failing, glowing heart.
- An empty/darkened heart recovered from Amalgam and used as the replacement vessel.

Lahar builds a soul-transference machine and guides the player through moving Lamashtu's soul into the replacement heart.

Amalgam's dialogue shows a different use: multiple hearts as redundancy against destruction, although his enlarged body repeatedly breaks them.
