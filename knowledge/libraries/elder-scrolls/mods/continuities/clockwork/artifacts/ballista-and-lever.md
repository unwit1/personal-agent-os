# Sickness Ward ballista and lever

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Object type: siege mechanism / quest tool

During *Crystalline Heart*, Amalgam retreats behind a portcullis in the Sickness Ward. A nearby ballista can breach the barrier, but its control lever is missing its handle.

An unfinished note says a compatible handle can be found in a house at the far end of the Mortal Layer. The player retrieves the handle, repairs the control lever, and uses the ballista to breach the gate.
