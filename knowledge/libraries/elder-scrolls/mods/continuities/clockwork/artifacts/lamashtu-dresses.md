# Lamashtu's dresses

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Object type: clothing / memory artifact

A note from Lahar says he made dresses for Lamashtu "some time ago." One resembles what she liked to wear long ago; he does not remember why he made the pale one. Lamashtu did not want them, so Lahar leaves them for the player.

The ESP contains the actual clothing records:

- `ARMO 0579768C` / `CLWClothesRedDress` — **Red Dress of Angles**
- `ARMO 05792579` / `CLWClothesPaleDress` — **Pale Dress of Angles**

The dresses are useful character evidence because they connect Lahar's caretaker behavior with fragmentary memory of Lamashtu's earlier identity. His inability to explain the pale dress is another small example of preserved action/skill alongside damaged autobiographical context.
