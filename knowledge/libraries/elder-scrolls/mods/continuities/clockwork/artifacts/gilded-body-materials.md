# Gilded bodies and material records

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Object type: body/armour implementation evidence

The ESP contains multiple Gilded body/armour variants rather than one generic shell, including:

- `ARMO 05000D66` — Gilded Armour
- `ARMO 05000D67` — Gilded Boots
- `ARMO 05000D68` — Gilded Gauntlets
- `ARMO 05000D69` — Gilded Helmet 01
- `ARMO 05220ECC`, `05220ED2`–`05220ED6` — additional numbered Gilded helmets
- `ARMO 055C2E14` — Gilded Armour **No Heart**
- `ARMO 055C2E1F`, `055C2E20` — helmet variants without eyes
- `ARMO 050013EC`, `050013E9`, `050013EA` — Lamashtu-specific body/helmet records
- `ARMO 05510DF0` — Amalgam Centurion skin
- `ARMO 05067523` — Shadow Dress

The `Gilded Armour No Heart` record is particularly consistent with the story's explicit treatment of the crystalline heart as a separable core. These records are implementation evidence for visual/body construction; they should not be used alone to infer social ranks or ancient Dwemer fashion.
