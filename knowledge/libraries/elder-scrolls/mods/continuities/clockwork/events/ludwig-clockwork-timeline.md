# Ludwig / Clockwork Castle dated timeline

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Primary evidence: Ludwig's four journals (`BOOK 05332EA2`, `05332EA7`, `05332EAA`, `05332EAF`).

This timeline records dated claims from Ludwig's journals. Earlier family-history events such as the Red Year are described retrospectively and are not assigned a new exact date unless Ludwig supplies one.

- **3rd Evening Star, 4E 16** — Maximilian Chlodovech is laid to rest; Ludwig begins the journal.
- **10th Morning Star, 4E 17** — Ludwig records the castle project, Velothi Tunnels trade-route plan, the Red Year's destruction of the Morrowind route, his mother's death, and his role as lord/jeweller.
- **12th Last Seed, 4E 17** — Ludwig orders expansion of the family mausoleum.
- **4th Rain's Hand, 4E 18** — workers breach ancient Dwemer structures beneath the castle and encounter the Gilded.
- **7th Rain's Hand, 4E 18** — first detailed account of Lamashtu; she says "No harm" and begins reading in the Study.
- **12th Rain's Hand, 4E 18** — Lamashtu is named; she rapidly learns Cyrodiilic and identifies the underground complex as Nurndural.
- **1st Second Seed, 4E 18** — Ludwig explores Nurndural; Lahar follows him back and takes an interest in caretaker duties.
- **22nd Second Seed, 4E 18** — Lamashtu permits salvage of unusable Dwemer metal.
- **25th Midyear, 4E 18** — Gilded workers move salvaged metal into the Work Room.
- **1st Sun's Height, 4E 18** — the human castle staff leave, frightened by the increasing Gilded presence.
- **3rd Heartfire, 4E 20** — Ludwig has pressurized-water plumbing, including heated water, using Dwemer machinery and steam.
- **23rd First Seed, 4E 21** — the Chlodovech Trading Company ceases to exist.
- **6th Frostfall, 4E 21** — steam heat makes Maximilian's glass garden functional.
- **14th Sun's Dawn, 4E 23** — workers uncover the Dwemer Travel Machine; Lamashtu identifies its long-distance travel function.
- **30th Heartfire, 4E 23** — Ludwig commits to restoring the Travel Machine.
- **22nd Second Seed, 4E 24** — Ludwig describes his pneumatic tube network; outsiders have begun calling the estate "Clockwork Castle."
- **11th Rain's Hand, 4E 30** — Ludwig works on a relief-map control interface for the Travel Machine.
- **4th Frostfall, 4E 32** — a Gilded worker attacks Ludwig; he reports increasing fear of the Gilded.
- **20th Sun's Dusk, 4E 33** — the Travel Machine successfully opens portals; Lahar scouts Terminus destinations.
- **9th Evening Star, 4E 33** — Ludwig briefly visits the area outside Markarth, his first time outside in more than a decade.
- **18th Midyear, 4E 35** — all Gilded except Lamashtu and Lahar withdraw to Nurndural. Ludwig writes, "There is just me. Only me."
- **5th Sun's Height, 4E 37** — Ludwig worries that if he died, increasingly few people would remember or discover him.
- **13th Evening Star, 4E 44** — Ludwig reflects on dependence on the castle's machines.
- **10th Frostfall, 4E 53** — Ludwig cannot remember the last time he spoke aloud and says every day has become mechanically identical.
- **29th First Seed, 4E 54** — Ludwig contrasts his own warmth with Lamashtu, Lahar, and "the lady in the walls."
- **1st Heartfire, 4E 60** — Ludwig wonders whether soul-gem-driven clocks are aware.
- **7th Midyear, 4E 68** — Ludwig asks whether a mind requires other minds to prevent its thoughts from endlessly reflecting back on themselves.
- **7th Sun's Dusk, 4E 83** — Ludwig stays in a hidden chamber, fears others' judgment, and believes his remaining time is short.

## Reliability note

This is Ludwig's first-person record. Observations outside his direct experience, especially conclusions about other people's motives, remain Ludwig's testimony rather than omniscient fact.
