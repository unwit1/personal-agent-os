# Lahar

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Entity type: Gilded / Dwemer-derived machine-bound person  
Primary NPC: `052908BF` (`CLWLahar`)

## Continuity summary

Lahar is one of the surviving Gilded and presents himself primarily as the caretaker of Clockwork Castle. Lamashtu identifies him as a **second child** in the Gilded development sequence: his identity endured far better than the earliest Gilded, but it has distorted over time.

Lamashtu identifies Lahar as the originator of the Gilded process and calls him the **father of the Gilded**. She says he devised the plan to capture dying Dwemer souls in soul gems and place them into metal bodies. Lahar does not remember this role; when confronted with it, he says Lamashtu must be mistaken and repeats that he is only a caretaker.

This makes Lahar a clear example of uneven memory survival: autobiographical identity has degraded while substantial technical and procedural ability remains.

## Relationships

- **Lamashtu:** surviving fellow Gilded; she remembers more of Lahar's original role than he does. Lahar acts as her caretaker and can repair/restore her.
- **Other Gilded:** Lahar calls them brothers and sisters. He says they have grown wild and that he and Lamashtu are no longer welcome in Nurndural.
- **Amalgam:** Lahar knows the story of Amalgam's deterioration and heart-taking, although he says he has never personally seen him.
- **Ludwig Chlodovech:** Ludwig's journals record Lahar learning castle routines and taking on caretaker work soon after first contact with the Gilded.
