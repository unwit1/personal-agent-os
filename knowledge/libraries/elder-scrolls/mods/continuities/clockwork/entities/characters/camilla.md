# Camilla

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Entity type: explorer / adventurer  
NPC: `0507ECA8` (`CLWCamilla`)

## Continuity summary

Camilla is the explorer whose expedition into the newly exposed ruins precedes the player's. Her surviving written trail provides the player's earliest evidence that something in the tunnels is stalking travelers and interfering with their ability or willingness to leave.

Camilla begins excited by the prospect of untouched ruins, then becomes increasingly frightened by moving shadows, a disappearing woman, footsteps, paralysis, and an inability to face returning to the outside world. Her final note says she will remain with the bones until death.
