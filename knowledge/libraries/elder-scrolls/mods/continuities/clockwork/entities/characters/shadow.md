# Shadow

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Entity type: spectral/copy manifestation associated with Lamashtu  
Representative NPC forms include `05065463` (`CLWShadow01`) and encounter variants.

## Continuity summary

Shadow is the spectral figure that stalks the player through the Velothi tunnels and later prevents the Travel Machine from releasing the player from Clockwork Castle. Lamashtu initially interprets Shadow as the missing half of her soul, created when her death and gilding coincided with Kagrenac striking the Heart of Lorkhan.

After their reunion, Lamashtu revises that theory: she and Shadow appear to have been two copies of the same living Lamashtu rather than incomplete halves. Lamashtu remembers Shadow's state primarily as loneliness and an inability to communicate.

## Dialogue note

No ordinary INFO records currently resolve uniquely to Shadow as an independent speaker. Her interior perspective appears after the reunion inside Lamashtu-attributed response sequences, so Lorekeeper keeps that testimony linked to those records without fabricating a separate speaker attribution.
