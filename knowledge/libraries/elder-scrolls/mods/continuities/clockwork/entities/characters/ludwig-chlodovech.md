# Ludwig Chlodovech

Continuity: `tes.mod.clockwork`  
Entity type: human noble, jeweller, inventor, lord of Clockwork/Chlodovech Castle

## Continuity summary

Ludwig Chlodovech is the principal human chronicler of Clockwork Castle. His four journals span decades and document the castle's history, first contact with Nurndural and the Gilded, his collaboration with Lamashtu and Lahar, the restoration of Dwemer-derived machinery, and his progressive social isolation.

As the castle becomes increasingly self-sufficient, Ludwig loses reasons to interact with other people. By 4E 53 he cannot remember the last time he spoke aloud; by 4E 83 he has withdrawn into a hidden chamber and expects to die there.

Lamashtu later says Shadow likely tried to keep Ludwig close as she did the player, but adds that with Ludwig she does not think it was necessary. This leaves Ludwig's withdrawal primarily grounded in his own isolation and dependence on the castle's systems rather than reducing it to supernatural compulsion.
