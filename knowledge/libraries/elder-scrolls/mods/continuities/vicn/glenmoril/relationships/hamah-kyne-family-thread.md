- Hamah is described as a **Daughter of Kyne**.
- He met her in **Vahdin Holt**, described as a boundary forest following Kyne's teachings.
- They fell in love despite priestly opposition.
- He was banished and Hamah was imprisoned.
- Hamah later found him while injured, bloody and pregnant, and told him the child was his.
- He chose to believe her.
- Their child was a girl who grew to resemble Hamah.
- He says Kyne's priestesses could not bear their own children in order to connect people with Kyne, while simultaneously recounting Hamah's pregnancy.
