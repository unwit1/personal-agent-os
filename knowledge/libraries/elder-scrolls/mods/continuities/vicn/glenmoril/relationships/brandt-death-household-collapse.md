# Brandt death / Lalanoah household collapse

Primary source: **Little Red**.

## Brandt

Brandt is found mortally wounded.

He tells the player:
- he cut an arm from the attacking monster;
- Lalanoah must be cared for;
- he is going on to Sovngarde.

His death line asks the player to:
> “Take care of the others...”

This directly closes Brandt's guardian arc with his death.

## Other household losses

The player asks Ja'zel:
> “Where are the bodies of the rest of the family?”

Ja'zel says:
- the bodies were contaminated;
- he burned them;
- their ashes were scattered in the flower garden.

This establishes a broader household/family-loss event.

## Lalanoah

Lalanoah survives but is severely physically deteriorated.

Cerebline becomes the immediate active caretaker.

## Identity guardrail

The “rest of the family” line does not enumerate individual names.

Do not automatically mark Groll, Skoll, Kanra, Kalka, or every household-associated actor dead without their own direct state evidence.
