# Clavicus Vile / unnamed bookworm

Primary source:
**Clavicus Vile**

Vile says:
- every desire in this land is covered by the handiwork of an unnamed male;
- the player's wish is included;
- Vile wants no part in helping him.

Vile calls the figure:
> a **typical bookworm**.

Asked who he means, Vile refuses the name and says:
- the player already knows it;
- Vile does not want the figure to appear;
- if the player says the name, the figure would gladly show up.
