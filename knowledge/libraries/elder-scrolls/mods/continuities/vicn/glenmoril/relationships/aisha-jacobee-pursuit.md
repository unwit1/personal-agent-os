# Aisha / Ja'cobee pursuit thread

Primary sources:
- **Snow Cat**
- **Strange Fruit**

## Snow Cat

Aisha searches for a Khajiit who is:
- low-born;
- miserly;
- involved in the Chick-Trader mission.

She repeatedly calls the target:
**Prince**

and describes him as a **bastard prince**.

She says:
- he should have abandoned the impossible mission;
- he refused to go with her back to her hometown;
- she had been ordered to kill him;
- she has now rejected that order.

## Strange Fruit

Prince Ra'hajir independently says:
- Ja'cobee is his **half-brother**;
- Ja'cobee was born to a low-status concubine;
- Ja'cobee was pushed into Chick-Trader work;
- Ja'cobee should have run away with **Aisha**.

## Assessment

The evidence strongly converges on:
**Aisha's missing bastard prince = Ja'cobee**.

Confidence: **high cross-quest identity**.

It is not an exact structural identity because Snow Cat's target is not filled as a named quest alias.
