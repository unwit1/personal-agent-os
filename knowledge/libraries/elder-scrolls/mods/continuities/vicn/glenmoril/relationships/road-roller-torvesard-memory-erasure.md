# Rolls-On-Roads — Torvesard / erased-Prince analysis

Primary source: **Brain-Billy's Sting**

## Historical Torvesard

Rolls says his later research identifies Torvesard as:
- a Dremora involved in Second-Era events in the Telvanni Peninsula and West Weald;
- an infiltrator of Apocrypha;
- someone whose actions contributed to an existential crisis extending to Fargrave;
- someone eventually defeated and sent into the Void.

Because of that, Rolls initially concludes the player cannot have met the original Torvesard.

## Maligraphy

When the player reports that Torvesard called himself a **maligraphic replica**, Rolls connects this to a living-ink replication incident in the Infinite Archive.

He treats the explanation as plausible:
- book characters/relics were replicated;
- some living-ink creations may have survived.

This supports keeping the **Torvesard maligraphy** distinct from the original Torvesard.

## Large-scale memory alteration

Rolls says:
- a large-scale memory alteration occurred shortly after Torvesard's disappearance;
- Torvesard's Prince can no longer be properly perceived;
- something vanished from Mundus and its memory was concealed from history;
- **Hermaeus Mora** is a leading suspect in that concealment.

## Augur clue

The Augur of the Obscure says:
- Torvesard's Prince's word/name is in principle perceivable;
- linking that word to its **truth** causes the perception to become garbled;
- a wordplay/riddle may let someone recognize the name without attaching the forbidden truth.

## Provenance rule

The evidence strongly converges on a deliberately erased Prince, but preserve the channels separately:
1. Torvesard's own testimony;
2. Rolls's Psijic research;
3. Augur riddle;
4. Ithelia quest metadata.

Do not rewrite any one source as if it independently states the entire synthesis.
