# Oneiromancer protection / Chick Trader nursery thread

Primary source: **Or the Gospel** topic dialogue.

Ozwald explicitly reverses the explanation originally given for the hunters' post-Oneiromancer illness.

## Public/apparent explanation

After the Oneiromancer was defeated:
- more hunters developed the disease;
- Ja'zel implied she released/spread a curse as she died.

## Ozwald's recovered-memory explanation

Ozwald says his Quagmire memories show:
- **the Oneiromancer was shielding and protecting them**;
- the Chick Trader was instead using the hunters as a **nursery for the curse**.

This claim motivates Ozwald's desire to betray/remove Ja'zel.

## Consequence

The temporal sequence can therefore be read in two competing ways:

1. Oneiromancer death → she curses hunters.
2. Oneiromancer death → her protection disappears → the Chick Trader's existing curse process becomes visible/active.

Ozwald explicitly endorses the second interpretation.

## Guardrail

This is powerful direct dialogue evidence for Ozwald's understanding, but it remains **Ozwald's reconstructed interpretation** of his Quagmire memories until separately corroborated.
