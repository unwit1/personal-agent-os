# Groll / Lalanoah guard thread

Primary source: **The Way is Blocked**.

Groll says:
- he was ordered to guard something;
- the protected target is “a small green thing called Lala”;
- he receives “glowing stones” as a reward.

The dialogue context and quest alias structure strongly resolve **Lala = Lalanoah**.

Groll initially frightens Lalanoah but, after speaking with her, explicitly becomes her friend.

Lalanoah later says:
> “I have a new friend now! Her name is Groll.”

## Unresolved controller

Groll cannot or will not clearly identify who issued the guard order.

Do not attribute the order to:
- Brandt;
- Laelnoah;
- Gerhard;
- Jhunal;
- the Chick Traders;
- or another actor

without direct evidence.

## Identity note

When asked whether Groll is female, Groll says:
> “Not female. Maiden.”

Preserve the source wording rather than normalizing it to a modern gender label.
