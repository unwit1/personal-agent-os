# Ja'zel / real Chick final thread

Primary source: **Lice and Maggots**.

Ja'zel tells the player:
> “You are Ja'zel's little Chick... precious and beloved...”

Near the final confrontation he says:
- he loved the player from the moment they met;
- he thought he had finally found **the real Chick**;
- he gave the player what he had to help them grow;
- he wanted to keep feeding them so they would become bigger;
- it is now time to leave the nest and fly;
- he is empty/hollow after giving everything away.

## Relationship to earlier Chick language

Earlier:
- Lalanoah is literally the paid sixth Chick in Act 1;
- Ja'zel later offers to exchange her for Maria's tainted baby;
- Ja'cobee says Chick Trader language deliberately substitutes words/metaphors.

Lice and Maggots shows Ja'zel eventually applying the **real Chick** identity to the player.

## Guardrail

Do not flatten:
- Lalanoah as sixth Chick;
- Maria's baby as special replacement;
- player as “real Chick”;
- later six-EGG/CHICK language

into one literal identity model.

The source supports changing/ritualized uses of Chick terminology.
