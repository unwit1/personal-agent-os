# Sacred Anatomancer — rewriting biography

Primary source: **The Pipers**.

## Direct phenomenon

Romion says his documents about the **Sacred Anatomancer** change while he studies them.

He reports:
- the Anatomancer was first a Nord, then a Breton, then an Imperial;
- other records make him Khajiit, Dunmer, Bosmer, Redguard, and more;
- some records say he founded the Holy Pipers;
- other versions say the Pipers summoned him;
- biographies contradict one another more with every reading.

Romion explicitly says:
> the contents keep changing

and eventually:
> “The more I read this, the fewer similarities there are...”

He then says he feels as though he is losing his mind.

## Dossier variants

The raw INFO records intentionally provide incompatible stories including:
- former Vigil Keeper;
- Khajiit traveler;
- Dunmer peddler;
- Breton Dawnguard member;
- Bosmer hunter;
- young Nord;
- Redguard bandit;
- even a version claiming he was the Dragonborn.

These should **not** be reconciled into one composite biography.

## Related source behavior

The Sacred-Anatomancer manifestations themselves speak in unstable identity grammar:
- “Love, I and We”
- repeated/multiple selves
- fake/remnant language
- return/birth/womb metaphors.

## Evidence model

Promote as direct fact:
**the Anatomancer's historical record is unstable/rewrite-prone.**

Do not promote any single dossier as the true biography unless later source evidence stabilizes it.
