# Maria / tainted Baby thread

## The Cat and the Chick

Ja'zel seeks a **special tainted baby** in Markarth.

He says:
- mother: Maria;
- father: Marcus Bruiant;
- the baby was born years ago but **never grows**;
- the Chick Trader of the time failed to collect it.

Orlando says:
- the child inherited Maria's curse;
- guards took it from Maria;
- they threw it into a river.

Maria's doll contains the baby's umbilical cord.

Ja'zel takes the cord and says:
> “That baby will be easily found now.”

## Tainted Orphan

and asks how he could have known a **cursed child** would be born.

The mission then searches for the anomalous “Baby” in the Cradle Woods and Ja'zel personally recovers it.
