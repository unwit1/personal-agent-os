# Tainted Orphan — Baby / parasite / blood-curse thread

Tainted Orphan provides the first direct biological mechanism for the Baby's spreading curse.

## Ghoul evidence

Romion dissects a ghoul and finds a **string-like worm** attached to altered internal organs.

He transplants the worm to a Skeever and observes ghouling within hours.

Romion concludes that the worms likely:
- carry the Baby's curse;
- disseminate it between hosts;
- reshape host bodies.

## Exposure diagnostics

After the mission Romion checks Ozwald and the player for:
- parasites;
- physical mutation;
- memory impairment associated with the **blood curse**.

At that point both pass.

## Cross-quest links

Later material says:
- the Baby is neither alive nor dead and is a “curse given flesh” in Ja'zel's terminology;
- Ja'zel intends the Baby to absorb tuning-box side effects;
- the Lucid Dream plan uses Anem and the river to carry the Baby's curse downstream;
- Jhunal the Black calls the Orphan “empty” while noticing it still cries for its mother.

## Ontology discipline

The worm is direct biological evidence for curse transmission.

It does **not** by itself prove whether the Baby, curse, worm, blood curse, and later Quagmire taint are one single mechanism. Maintain linked but separable mechanisms until the source explicitly unifies them.
