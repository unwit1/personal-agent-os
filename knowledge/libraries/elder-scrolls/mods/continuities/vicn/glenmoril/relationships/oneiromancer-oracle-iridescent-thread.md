# Oneiromancer / Oracle Iridescent thread

Direct source: `zzzGHMq05ScBrWitch`.

Romion reports that old records describe **Zuzu the Oneiromancer** as:
- the daughter of a **Dumb Old Giant** who served as Ysgramor's counselor;
- someone who begged the **Oracle Iridescent** for knowledge;
- someone who consequently gained the power to come and go in dreams.

Romion says almost all other records about her are absent.

Ozwald hypothesizes that she intentionally removed information about herself before departing Nirn. Romion names the **Green Tablet Steps of Jhunal** as one possible location where information might remain.
