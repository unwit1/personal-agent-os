# Laelnoah / inscription-family descendant

Primary source: **The Pipers**.

Ozwald says the woman kidnapped by the Holy Pipers is:
> “a descendant of the family mentioned in the inscription.”

Romion then gives her identifying biography:
- healer in Falkreath;
- husband: Gerhard;
- daughter: Lalanoah.

Therefore the descendant is directly resolved as:
**Laelnoah**.

## Consequence

This creates a source-backed bridge:
**Red-Drop thief/family inscription → Laelnoah's family line → Lalanoah**

It does **not** by itself prove:
- which ancestor committed the theft;
- whether Laelnoah or Lalanoah is the Red Drop;
- whether “walking around in another form” refers to either woman.

Keep those as separate hypotheses.

## Family significance

Romion and Ozwald immediately recognize the convergence as suspicious because Lalanoah is already personally connected to the player/Brandt and the wider Witch-Hunt events.

The family line should now be indexed as a high-priority Red-Drop genealogy thread.
