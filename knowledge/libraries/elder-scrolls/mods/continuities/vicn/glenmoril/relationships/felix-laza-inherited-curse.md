# Felix / Laza / inherited curse

Primary source:
**Replay Gen**

Felix explicitly says:
> “I am Laza... and so are you.”

He then says:
- his memories are disappearing;
- this is a **blood curse of fading memories**;
- he failed;
- the player must **inherit the curse**;
- the dream must not end;
- he still seeks the crying girl / family promise.

## Related evidence

Lucid Dream separately frames historical Laza as:
- trapped in the past;
- anomalously surviving events that should have killed everyone;
- central to a curse involving longing for something that can no longer exist.

The player elsewhere inherits Felix's curse.

## Working model

The evidence supports a repeated identity/curse lineage:

**historical Laza → Felix memory/identity state → player inheritance**

but not a simple reincarnation conclusion.

Possible mechanisms include:
- inherited memory;
- dream identity;
- curse role;
- copied persona;
- temporal recurrence;
- another metaphysical structure.

Keep the mechanism unresolved.
