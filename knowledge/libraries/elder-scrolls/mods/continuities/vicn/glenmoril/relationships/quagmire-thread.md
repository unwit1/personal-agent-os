# Quagmire evidence thread

Romion identifies **Quagmire** as Vaermina's realm / a nightmare world and describes research intended to recover memories from it. He attributes waking access to research associated with **Divayth Fyr** and **Morian Zenas**, and says Dwemer technology can create pseudo-gates. The quest structure subsequently includes physical interaction with a machine-opened gate and Quagmire-space targets.

Related retrieval nodes are Romion, Arkved, the historical Oneiromancer, Zuzu the Oneiromancer, and Vaermina.

These are GLENMORIL/Vicn claims and interpretations. They must remain separate from official-continuity records for Vaermina, Fyr, Zenas and Arkved.
