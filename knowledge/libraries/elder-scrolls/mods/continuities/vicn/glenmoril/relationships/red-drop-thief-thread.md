# Red Drop / thief / family thread

Direct Or the Gospel scene evidence establishes the following attributed chain:

1. Ozwald expects the Red Drop at the attacked temple.
2. Ja'zel says it was removed long ago.
3. Ja'zel says the temple contains an inscription naming the thief and the thief's family.
4. Ja'zel says Hela guarded this secret.
5. Ja'zel says the Oneiromancer hid the path leading to it.
6. Ja'zel describes the treasure as walking around in a different form.

## Unresolved

- identity of the thief;
- identity of the family;
- meaning of “different form”;
- whether “treasure” refers exactly to the Red Drop or to a related underlying entity;
- why Hela and the Oneiromancer protect the thief/family.

Do not resolve these from thematic similarity alone.
