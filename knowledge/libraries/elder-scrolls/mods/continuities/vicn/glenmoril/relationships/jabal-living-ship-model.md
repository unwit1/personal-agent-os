# Ja'bal — living ship / mental-entity model

Primary sources:
- Professor Y, Vol. 5
- Secret Tale Vol. 2 — The Seed
- Secret Tale Vol. 3 — The Tones

## Remains / head

Professor Y says Ja'bal's head cannot be penetrated by instruments and interprets this as the head generating localized universes that avoid unwanted futures.

This is Professor Y's interpretation of an observed resistance to examination.

## Living ship

Secret Tale Vol. 2 describes a giant sphere as:
- not the Egg;
- a **living ship**;
- anatomically Argonian-like;
- associated with Ja'bal's remains as a terminal/interface.

Professor Y says the researchers became integrated into the ship network after consuming the terminal's flesh/blood.

## Mental entity

Professor Y explicitly rejects a simple one-body model:
> Ja'bal is not a specific person with merely one body.

He calls Ja'bal a **mental entity** and says only part of it remains with the researchers while most was trapped in Oblivion.

## Hist / Dunmer / worm layers

Secret Tale Vol. 3 says:
- the ship's original Argonian consciousness was erased;
- a Dunmer virtual personality was transcribed into the brain-organ;
- a white root/worm-like Argonian variant was attached near the brain stem;
- two mental entities occupied the ship.

Professor Y says either, neither, or the combined event could underlie what Khajiit later called Ja'bal.

## Interpretation guardrail

The books themselves warn against over-literal identity assignment.

Professor Y says:
- Great Cat;
- Rolly-Polly;
- Ja'bal

may be interpretive names imposed by Anequina's observers.

Therefore do not flatten:
**Ja'bal = Hist ship = Dunmer mind = worm**
into one proven identity.

Store them as competing/overlapping components in the Professor Y model.
