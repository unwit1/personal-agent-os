# Yelem ↔ Chick Trader vocabulary bridge

Individual source normalization has uncovered a particularly important direct textual bridge.

## Strong evidence

`Glenmoril.esm:006448` (Yelem's Lizard-series note LiB) explicitly places **Great Cat** and **Rolly-Polly** in the same statement: Yelem says the first Great Cat was a Rolly-Polly.

This matters because Rolly-Polly is also central to Ja'cobee's deliberately coded Chick Trader testimony. The overlap is therefore no longer merely thematic similarity inferred by analysis: the Yelem corpus itself uses Chick-Trader-associated vocabulary.

## Still not established

This does **not** yet prove:
- that Ja'cobee and Yelem mean exactly the same thing by Rolly-Polly;
- that every Great Cat is a Rolly-Polly;
- that Rolly-Polly is a specific known TES deity;
- that Ja'cobee's EGG is identical to Yelem's Egg/cocoon.

Keep those as hypotheses pending additional records.
