# Holy Pipers — perception and Falkreath victims

Primary sources:
- The Cat and the Chick
- Shop of Curiosities
- Brain-Billy's Sting

## Identity

Brandt identifies the Holy Pipers as a faction of **Vigilants of Stendarr** that had been excommunicated.

Rolls-On-Roads calls them an **extremist branch of the Vigil of Stendarr**.

## Falkreath victims

Rolls says the three recent missing women were:
- **Yalanwe** — Altmer dissident refugee, later found burned to death;
- **Weeva** — Argonian apothecary, taken by Holy Pipers;
- **Laelnoah** — Nord healer / Lalanoah's mother, taken by Holy Pipers.

Brandt independently confirms Laelnoah's abduction.

## Location

Rolls places the Holy Pipers somewhere in the **Jerall Mountains** near the Falkreath/Bruma border.

Later he says a cleared space-time distortion reveals **Piper's Hill** and that the Red Drop is in Holy-Piper hands.

## Perception hazard

Rolls says:
- merely knowing about the Holy Pipers is dangerous;
- they spread/influence through **perception**;
- listening to them is like opening one's heart to them;
- the Psijic Order deliberately withholds information about them.

The Augur gives an analogous warning in comic language: knowing them lets them “enter the meat-brain.”

## Later operational state

Rolls says:
- Holy Pipers are moving north toward Falkreath;
- their leader is the **Sacred Anatomancer**;
- defeating that leader may slow their proliferation;
- Psijics themselves cannot approach Piper's Hill.

## Evidence discipline

These later details are Rolls-On-Roads's Psijic intelligence and should remain attributed unless corroborated by direct Holy-Piper records.
