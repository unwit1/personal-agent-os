# Laelnoah / Jhunal the White relationship

Primary source: **The Blue Bird**.

Lalanoah explicitly says:
- **Jhunal is her mother's pet owl**;
- Jhunal is very wise;
- he often brings back things that are **lost or forgotten**;
- her mother frequently spoke to him;
- Jhunal normally responded only by nodding.

Brandt independently calls the white owl **Laelnoah's pet**.

Because Laelnoah is directly established elsewhere as Lalanoah's mother, the relationship is:

**Laelnoah ↔ Jhunal the White**

with an explicit functional association:
**Jhunal → retrieves lost/forgotten things**.

## Later tension

Jhunal later becomes the center of a selective-forgetting event in which Lalanoah and Brandt lose memories of him.

That makes his early association with retrieving “lost or forgotten” things especially relevant, but the source does not explicitly state that these are the same mechanism.
