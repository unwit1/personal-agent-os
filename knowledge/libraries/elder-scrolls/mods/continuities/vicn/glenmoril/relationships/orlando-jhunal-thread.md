# Orlando / Jhunal thread

Multiple resolved scenes now provide direct but non-identical links between Orlando and Jhunal.

## Night of Tears

The Owl actor explicitly introduces himself as **Orlando**, a mage from Atmora. He says that in his homeland he was once thought to be an **incarnation of Jhunal**.

## The Owls

Jhunal the Black converses with a Gray Owl actor from the Orlando actor family. The Gray Owl identifies **Atmora** as his homeplace and discusses inhabiting “this body,” alternatives, and possible separation from a lord.

## St. Bazura

Orlando distinguishes the **White Owl** from himself when the player asks about the White Owl. The quest separately resolves its White-Owl actor as **Jhunal the White**.

## Ontology decision

Do not merge Orlando, Jhunal the White, or Jhunal the Black into one character.

The evidence supports a deliberately entangled relationship involving incarnation claims, Owl bodies/representations, Atmora, and distinct Jhunal manifestations/actors.
