# Ja'zel / Ja'zeri / Orlando thread

The Dream Within a Dream scene sequence resolves several previously ambiguous identities.

## Structural identities

- `JazelChild` → NPC **Ja'zeri** (`04012DBB`)
- `JazelBoy` → NPC **Ja'zel** (`04012DBD`)
- `JazelMad` → NPC **Ja'zel** (`04012DBE`)
- `Owl` → NPC `zzzGHM05OwlOrland01`, who explicitly identifies himself in dialogue as **Orlando the Knowledgeable**

## Narrative sequence supported directly

Ja'zeri speaks of himself and Ja'zel growing up together. In the Boy scene, Ja'zel repeatedly denies Ja'zeri's existence. Orlando tells Ja'zel that Ja'zeri died by decapitation and that Ja'zel is now alone. Orlando then introduces Ja'zel to a story about the Chick Traders of ancient Anequina.

The later Mad scene has Ja'zel speaking directly about Rolly-Polly, Ja'bal the Cat, chicks, and his treasured EGG.

## Still unresolved

This evidence distinguishes Ja'zeri and Ja'zel as source actors, but does not yet explain the metaphysical relationship between them, the neck/head imagery, or how this sequence relates to Ja'cobee's later accusation that Ja'zel is an impostor.
