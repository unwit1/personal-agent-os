# Ja'bal — Chick / EGG / key doctrine

Primary source:
**Generic Revenant**

Ja'bal the Great Cat says:

## Chick / EGG

Asked what Chick and EGG mean, Ja'bal says:
- “it is what we are and what you are”;
- they are **the key**;
- the purpose is to open **the door to the outside**;
- flesh is remade by nightmares to fit the lock.

He says:
> “We are a failure, but you will fulfill the Owl's expectations.”

## Player as harvest

Ja'bal calls the player:
- harvest;
- fruit of freedom;
- a seed/fruit entrusted with future betrayal.

## Owl

Asked the Owl's secret, Ja'bal says:
- the **real one** can no longer be found;
- it was forgotten long ago;
- a **golden-eyed one wears its shell**;
- invoking the name of a lurking creature can invite it.

## Agency

Ja'bal says “we” cannot force the player because of limitations enforced by **Alkosh**.

They can only:
- suggest;
- show the way.

He compares this to the Owl's earlier conspiracy.

## Guardrail

Ja'bal's language is overtly metaphysical and factional.

Do not globally decode every Chick/EGG reference from these statements alone.
