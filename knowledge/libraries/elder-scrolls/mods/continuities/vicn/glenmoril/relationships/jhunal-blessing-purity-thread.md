# Jhunal blessing / purity thread

## The Blessing

After Lalanoah heals the player and the player sleeps, Jhunal the White says:
> “You are blessed. But you must remain pure.”

He repeats that otherwise:
> “the blood curse will crawl towards you.”

## Ta Ta!

Ja'zel later repeats:
> “If you are blessed... remain pure. The Owl said so.”

Kalka sees the player and says:
> “Ah, you're blessed. I envy you.”

## Significance

Direct chain:
**Jhunal blessing → purity requirement → blood-curse avoidance/resistance.**

## Open questions

The source does not yet establish:
- what the blessing materially changes;
- whether Lalanoah's healing caused or enabled it;
- whether purity is moral, ritual, bodily, or metaphysical;
- whether the blessing is the same force Romion later sees restraining/healing the player's curse.
