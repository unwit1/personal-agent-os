# Eyes of Hinnom

Primary direct naming source: **The Pipers**.

Ozwald says:
- Ja'zel will likely turn on the hunters once they acquire the Red Drop;
- Ja'zel will send his partner after them;
- that partner is **the Eyes of Hinnom**.

## Earlier descriptions

Earlier quests describe Ja'zel's partner as:
- silent;
- uncanny;
- wall/ceiling-crawling;
- slimy;
- moving inside/through walls at extreme speed;
- difficult for Ozwald and FL-4N to perceive precisely.

## Identity upgrade

The Pipers provides the first direct name binding:
**Ja'zel's partner → Eyes of Hinnom**.

This should update retrieval for the previous anonymous “Chick Trader partner” thread.
