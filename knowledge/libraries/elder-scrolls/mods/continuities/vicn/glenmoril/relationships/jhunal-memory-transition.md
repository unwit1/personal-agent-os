# Jhunal memory transition — search to forgetting

The Broken Egg and St. Bazura dialogue together establish a direct before/after memory transition around **Jhunal**.

## Before — Broken Egg

Lalanoah:
- knows Jhunal by name;
- is distressed that he vanished;
- worries she caused his disappearance;
- searches for him with Brandt for at least a week;
- insists the group should all remain together.

Brandt:
- also remembers Jhunal/the owl;
- says Jhunal disappeared about a week earlier;
- searches Falkreath and a final feeding area with Lalanoah.

## After — St. Bazura

Lalanoah says she does not know anyone named Jhunal.

Brandt likewise says he does not know Jhunal and specifically replaces the earlier owl-search memory with a **treasure-hunt** explanation.

Kalka later says this forgetting is **what Jhunal wants**.

## Conclusion

The source therefore depicts active **alteration/removal of an existing memory**, not merely characters failing to know Jhunal in the first place.

This strengthens the Jhunal-forgetting thread while still leaving the exact mechanism unresolved.
