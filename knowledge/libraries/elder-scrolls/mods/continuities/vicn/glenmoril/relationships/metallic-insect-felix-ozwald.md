# Metallic insect — Felix / Ozwald connection

Primary source: **Falling Sky**.

FL-4N detects:
> **a small metallic insect**

on Ozwald's corpse.

He immediately says:
> when Felix disappeared, he found **the same thing in Felix's bed**.

## Direct relationship

The source therefore establishes a repeated object/device/creature association:

**Felix disappearance → metallic insect**

**Ozwald death/transformation → same metallic insect**

## Significance

This is one of the strongest direct mechanical links between Felix's disappearance and Ozwald's final transformation.

## Open questions

The source does not yet establish:
- who made the insect;
- whether it is Dwemer, Eton Nir, Chick Trader, Holy Piper, or another technology;
- whether it causes, tracks, records, infects, or merely observes;
- whether one individual insect moved between victims or multiple identical devices exist.

Do not assign origin or function until another record does so.
