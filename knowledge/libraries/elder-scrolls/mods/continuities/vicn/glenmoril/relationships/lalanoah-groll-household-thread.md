# Lalanoah / Groll household thread

The early Act-1 quests show a rapid progression:

## The Way is Blocked
Groll is initially an intimidating guard figure but becomes Lalanoah's friend.

## The Chick Trader
Lalanoah and Groll collect apples together.

## A Curious Animal
Groll respects Lalanoah's wish not to eat Kanra and stops when Lalanoah becomes angry.

## Ah, Apple Pie!
Groll and Lalanoah cook together.

Groll affectionately calls Lalanoah:
> “little miss gluttony”

and promises to make sweet rolls with her later.

Lalanoah:
- is comfortable cooking with Groll;
- gives the player pie they made together;
- saves a portion for Brandt;
- wants Brandt to join them next time.

## Significance

By Mq05, Groll is no longer merely a guard/friend. The dialogue presents her as part of Lalanoah's emerging **substitute household/family network** alongside Brandt, Skoll, Kanra, and the player.

This remains a social/relationship inference from repeated direct interactions, not a biological kinship claim.
