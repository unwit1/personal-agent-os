# Ozwald / Felix / player curse thread

Primary source: `zzzGHMq04ScCedric02`.

Ozwald tells Cedric that the new recruit/player **inherited Felix's curse**.

Ozwald says this makes him feel as though Felix is still somehow alive in the recruit and explains why he could not kill them and instead kept them close.

Cedric explicitly rejects an identity collapse:
- the recruit is **not Felix**;
- Felix is dead.

Ozwald agrees that he knows Felix is dead.

## Ontology guardrail

Current direct evidence supports:

`Felix --curse inherited by--> player/recruit`

and

`Ozwald --perceives emotional continuity with--> Felix through player`

It does not establish:

`player == Felix`

or reincarnation, possession, soul transfer, or resurrection.
