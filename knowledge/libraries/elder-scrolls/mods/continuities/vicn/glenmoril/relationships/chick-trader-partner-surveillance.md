# Chick Trader partner / surveillance thread

Several quests describe a second figure associated with Ja'zel.

## Blue Bird — FL-4N

FL-4N describes the pair as:
- Ja'zel, who talks constantly;
- a second figure who is silent and uncanny;
- a “big head” that crawls across walls and ceilings and leaves slimy traces.

## Or the Gospel — Ozwald

Ozwald says Ja'zel's **partner is nearby** and is still checking on the hunters, although Ozwald cannot see him clearly.

## Or the Gospel — FL-4N

FL-4N says:
- the partner appears to be **inside the wall**;
- he is moving so quickly FL-4N cannot determine the exact location;
- the hunters should not attack Ja'zel while the partner is present.

## Synthesis

The dialogue strongly supports a persistent **Ja'zel-partner surveillance role** across the Witch Hunt.

Do not assign the partner a specific identity merely from movement/body imagery. Candidate links can be tested later against actor records and Chick-Trader lore.
