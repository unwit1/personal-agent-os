# Anem / baby / river causal chain

Source: **Lucid Dream**, scene 01.

Ja'zel describes a planned causal sequence:

1. the baby is put into the river;
2. the river carries it through the Cradle Woods toward/under Falkreath;
3. **Anem** encounters it;
4. her kindness compels her to save it even at the cost of her life;
5. Anem washes the baby;
6. the river carries the baby's curse downstream;
7. the curse defiles an old **Nedic god statue beneath Falkreath**.

## Significance

This directly links the Baby/curse material to Anem, Cradle Woods, Falkreath, and a Nedic religious site.

## Evidence boundary

This is Ja'zel's plan/forecast. Later records should determine which steps actually occur. Do not promote planned causality into completed history without corroboration.
