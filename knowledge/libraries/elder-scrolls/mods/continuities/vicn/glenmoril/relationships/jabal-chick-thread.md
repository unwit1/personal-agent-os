# Ja'bal / Chick thread

Source: **Lucid Dream**, scene 05.

Ja'zel deliberately intends to arrive late after learning that Falkreath has six chicks. He says this delay will allow **another to appear and replace one chick**, producing the **real Chick sought by the Chick Trader**.

Ulrik connects this to the Egg → Chick → Chicken story.

Ja'zel says that story was brought/spun by fish associated with **Sheggorath** from another river or from downstream, and says the story both shows people what they want and destroys them.

When Ulrik asks whether **Ja'bal of Anequina** entered this world with a mission comparable to Pelinal, Ja'zel rejects that idea. He says Ja'bal wants only to **return to where he came from**.

## Guardrail

Do not collapse Ja'bal, the replacement Chick, the Chick Trader, Ja'zel, or the unnamed interference into one entity. The dialogue links them narratively but preserves distinct roles.
