# Hircine / Anequina / Blue-Star contract

Primary source:
**Gen Hungry Cat**

Hircine says the ancient king of Anequina asked him to:
> **paint the Blue Star red**.

He says:
- he placed a bloody tent around the king to hide the star's blue;
- Ja'bal later took the king prisoner.

## Contract

Hircine says the king contracted him to:
- transform those led astray by Ja'bal into beasts;
- let the king's followers hunt them;
- receive their pelts as tribute.

## Escapees

Some victims escaped both:
- the hunters;
- Hircine's curse.

Hircine identifies two of these as:
- **Yelem**
- **Yelem's assistant**

and says they devoured Ja'bal's flesh and blood.

## Chick Hunt

Hircine says he later helped with the Chick Hunt.

## Significance

This creates a direct Hircine-source chain:

**Anequina king → Blue Star hidden/red → Ja'bal → Hircine beast curse → Yelem + assistant escape → Chick Hunt**

## Guardrail

This is Hircine's account of ancient/revenant events.

It should be compared with Ra'hajir, Ja'bal, Clerk Ja'i, Ja'cobee and Aisha accounts rather than treated as the sole authoritative version.
