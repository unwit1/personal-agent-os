# Ruddy Man / deep-sea memory / echo

Primary source:
**Memory of the Deep Sea**

The Ruddy Man says:
- a red shell transformed him into a Dreugh-like form;
- he remembers nothing before the shell;
- memories of an old kalpa remain at the bottom of the deep sea;
- the shell whispers of an old Dreugh kingdom and a king pursuing Love.

He later recognizes:
> he is “just an echo.”

## Vision

The shell gives visions involving:
- Vivec;
- a future child;
- harmony/marriage;
- Lorkhan's empty heart being filled;
- the Dragon no longer devouring its tail;
- release from Mundus.

## Retrieval significance

This should be cross-retrieved with:
- Dreugh / old-kalpa material;
- Love;
- Vivec;
- echo/revenant identity;
- deep-sea memory.

## Guardrail

The quest presents these as shell-mediated memories/visions.

Do not promote them as objective prophecy or settled old-kalpa history.
