# Ja'zel antidote / poison contradiction

## Little Red

Ja'zel prepares a Red-Drop medicine and says:
- the player's curse has been lifted;
- they no longer need to fear becoming a monster.

## Lice and Maggots

The player asks Jhunal the Black:
> whether Ja'zel's medicine is really an antidote.

Jhunal answers:
> “It is only poison.”

He says:
- death removes the curse;
- therefore calling it an antidote is technically “not a complete lie”;
- it would no longer work on the player because their curse is too advanced.

## Consequence

This creates an explicit source contradiction:

**Ja'zel:** cure/antidote  
**Jhunal the Black:** lethal poison mislabeled as cure

Do not silently choose one interpretation.

The contradiction itself is canonical source evidence and should be surfaced whenever the Red-Drop cure is analyzed.
