# Ozwald final Red-Drop / memory sequence

Primary source: **Little Red**.

## Persistence after death

Previous quests establish:
- Ozwald is shot/dies;
- his corpse rapidly transforms.

Little Red presents another Ozwald actor/form.

Ozwald:
- calls the player **Felix**;
- says he found the Red Drop;
- gives the Drop to the player;
- says he cannot feel his legs or move;
- asks the player to end his suffering.

## Memory sequence

Ozwald recounts first-person images:
- crying because a sister was lost even though “she wasn't supposed to be there”;
- standing at a forest edge with anger;
- gouging out his eyes and giving them to an Owl;
- learning to walk in darkness;
- smelling decay and being redirected by an Owl;
- finding the Spring;
- washing something in it;
- hearing women's screams;
- sinking into a fathomless sea;
- experiencing a lost maternal warmth.

## Relationship to Felix memory anomaly

Felix previously remembered a sister Ozwald insisted never existed.

Now Ozwald:
- calls the player Felix;
- reports a sister-loss image with the same kind of impossibility marker.

This strongly links the curse/memory problem across:
**Felix ↔ player ↔ Ozwald**.

## Guardrail

Do not rewrite these memories as Ozwald's verified biography.

The source supports an unstable/inherited/contaminated memory state, but the original owner of each image is unresolved.
