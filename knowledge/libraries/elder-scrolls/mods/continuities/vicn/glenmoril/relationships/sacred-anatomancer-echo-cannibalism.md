# Sacred Anatomancer — echo / self-cannibalism model

Primary source:
**Baby of Nightmares**, Athanasius.

Athanasius says of the Sacred Anatomancer:
> “It's Yelem, and you, and me... an echo of us.”

He says:
- Yelem devoured himself but failed to reach the Blue Star;
- failed beings who knew Love nested together and **stitched each other up**;
- the resulting monster continues cannibalizing its other echoes.

## Yelem

Athanasius says Yelem:
- devoured his own future;
- reached the end of his dream;
- departed this land.

To follow him, the player must:
- cannibalize themself;
- grind away possible futures.

## Baby / names

Athanasius says:
- let the baby suck the player's blood;
- it will call the player by many names;
- kill those names until only the player's own name remains.

## Relation to rewriting records

Earlier Piper's Hill records show the Sacred Anatomancer's biography continuously rewriting itself.

The Revenant explanation provides a possible source-local model:
**multiple failed echoes + stitching/cannibalization → unstable identity/history**.

This is a useful explanatory relation, but remains Athanasius's testimony rather than a proven universal mechanism.
