# Hamah — Snow Elf / Anem rebirth

## Direct Revenant testimony

Primary direct source:
**The Lost Storyteller**

Hamah says:
> “Before Anem rebirthed me, I was once a Snow Elf girl.”

She says her parents:
- were storytellers;
- served Jephre;
- raised her on fairy tales.

Direct chain:
**Snow Elf girl → Anem rebirth → Hamah**

## Professor Y corroboration

`The Secret Tale of Professor Y: Vol. 7 — Hamah` supplies an earlier historical account.

Professor Y says:
- Hamah's outward body appears Nord;
- her soul belonged to one of the Snow-Elf subjects from his Arkngthamz experiment;
- those Snow Elves were supposed to be destroyed but were dumped into the sewers;
- survivors were protected by **Anem the Rebirther**;
- Anem rebirthed Hamah outwardly as a Nord;
- Hamah later traveled Skyrim and was welcomed into **Vahdin Holt**;
- Hamah stole a stone there and used it in a ritual.

The assistant independently remembers the Snow-Elf experiment and the disposal order, which gives partial internal corroboration to Professor Y's account.

## Evidence hierarchy

Strongest:
- Hamah's own Revenant-layer first-person statement that Anem rebirthed her from a Snow Elf girl.

Supporting historical source:
- Professor Y's Secret Tale account.

Still unresolved:
- whether all later Hamah manifestations descend from this same individual;
- how continuity of soul/memory works through rebirth;
- the identity and later fate of the stolen Vahdin Holt stone.
