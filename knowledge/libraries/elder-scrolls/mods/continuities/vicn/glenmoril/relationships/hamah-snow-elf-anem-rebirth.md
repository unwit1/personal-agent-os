# Hamah — Snow Elf / Anem rebirth

Primary source:
**The Lost Storyteller**

Hamah directly says:
> “Before Anem rebirthed me, I was once a Snow Elf girl.”

She says her parents:
- were storytellers;
- served Jephre;
- raised her on fairy tales.

## Direct chain

**Snow Elf girl → Anem rebirth → Hamah**

This is direct first-person testimony from a Revenant-layer Hamah manifestation.

## Related evidence

Earlier material connects Anem to:
- Nedic/Reach mythic material;
- the Cradle Woods / Baby chain;
- ancient curse structures.

The Lost Storyteller makes Anem personally responsible for Hamah's rebirth.

## Guardrail

The source does not define:
- when the rebirth happened;
- whether Hamah retained continuous identity;
- whether the reborn Hamah is the historical Daughter of Kyne from the Hanged Man account;
- whether every Hamah manifestation derives from this same Snow Elf girl.

Keep those questions open.
