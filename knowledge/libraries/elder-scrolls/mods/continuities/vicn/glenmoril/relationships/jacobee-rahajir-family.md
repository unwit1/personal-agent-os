# Ja'cobee / Ra'hajir family thread

Primary source:
**Strange Fruit**

Prince **Ra'hajir** directly says:
> Ja'cobee is his **half-brother**.

He remembers Ja'cobee following him around as a cub.

## Exile

Ra'hajir says:
- Ja'cobee struggled socially;
- Clerk Ja'i pushed for his exile;
- Ja'cobee was forced into the dirty work of the Chick Traders.

Clerk Ja'i describes Ja'cobee as:
- a child of a lowly concubine;
- still carrying out the royal command involving the Chicks.

## Aisha

When told Ja'cobee completed the mission, Ra'hajir says:
> Ja'cobee should have run away with **Aisha**.

This directly links Aisha to Ja'cobee's imagined alternative life.

## Conflict in interpretation

Ra'hajir treats Ja'cobee with familial concern.

Clerk Ja'i treats the Chick-Trader assignment as useful distraction/royal business.

Preserve both perspectives.
