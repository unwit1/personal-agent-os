# Aushahlaas / Laza / dragon curse

Primary source:
**Farewell Gift**

Aushahlaas says:
- a Nede shepherd broke an oath and trespassed into Kyne's forest;
- this brought disaster;
- many dragons were swallowed by his curse;
- even Bormahu's grace became tainted.

He names the shepherd:
**Laza**.

## Player parallel

Aushahlaas says the decay/stench in the player resembles the one in Laza.

This gives an independent draconic source linking:
**Laza ↔ player's curse/decay**.

## Alduin / broken oath

Aushahlaas also says:
- the black sea is tied to Alduin's fall from his role;
- he and other dragons abandoned Alduin in favor of Bormahu;
- this was still a betrayal;
- their own broken oath became a curse they are repaying.

## Briiviingrein

Aushahlaas asks the player to end the suffering of his cursed friend Briiviingrein.

## Evidence boundary

Aushahlaas's account is a distinct provenance channel for Laza.

Do not collapse it into Ja'zel's Laza chronology or Felix's inherited-Laza identity without preserving the differences.
