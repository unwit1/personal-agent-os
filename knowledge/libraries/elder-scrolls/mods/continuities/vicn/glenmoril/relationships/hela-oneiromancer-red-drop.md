# Hela / Oneiromancer / Red Drop protection thread

Source anchor: `zzzGHMq06ScEnd` in **Or the Gospel**.

Ja'zel's briefing after the temple assault creates a direct three-part protection chain:

1. an inscription identifies the thief who took the Red Drop and the thief's family;
2. **Hela guarded the secret**;
3. **the Oneiromancer hid the path leading to it**.

This is stronger than a general thematic association: Ja'zel explicitly assigns complementary protective roles to Hela and the Oneiromancer.
