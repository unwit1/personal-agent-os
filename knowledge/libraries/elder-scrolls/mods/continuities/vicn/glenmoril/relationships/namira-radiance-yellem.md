# Namira / Radiance / Yelem thread

Primary sources:
- **Gen Namira Cat**
- **To Fishing Village**

## Radiance

The Mouth of Namiira describes the Radiance as:
- older than music;
- leaking through a hole of Sithis;
- darker than Namira's own place/domain;
- refusing to rot.

This strongly distinguishes the Radiance from ordinary Namiran decay.

## Player

The Mouth says the player's accumulated curse/filth eventually makes them capable of:
> devouring the Radiance.

## Yelem

The Mouth says:
- Yelem abandoned his physical body;
- his soul is elsewhere;
- a residual cord/connection remains.

It instructs the player to:
- follow the cord;
- devour Yelem;
- breathe him back to life inside the player's body.

## Route

After further corruption, the Mouth sends the player west toward:
- a decayed harbor;
- a hill;
- a daughter of Ehlnofey who refused to return to the soil.

## Guardrail

Namira discusses the Radiance as something distinct.

Do not identify:
**Radiance = Namira**
without other evidence.
