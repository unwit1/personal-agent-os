# Anem / Na clan / Cradle Woods thread

Primary source: **Tainted Orphan**.

## Romion's briefing

Romion says:
- the **Na clan** is a secluded Reachfolk group living in the Cradle Woods;
- the clan avoided the Forsworn uprising;
- after the ghoul outbreak there was no further word from them;
- every few years the clan sacrifices a Baby to calm the forest ghosts;
- **Anem** leads that ritual;
- Anem has traveled the Reach performing fertility and childbirth rites.

## Anem's direct dialogue

Anem addresses the player as:
**“You who are cursed by blood.”**

She warns them to leave and later says she will pray for them if they continue.

## Later bridge

Lucid Dream later has Ja'zel predict that:
- the Baby will travel through the river;
- Anem will rescue/wash it;
- the Baby's curse will be carried downstream.

This makes Anem a recurring node connecting childbirth/fertility, the Baby, Cradle Woods, and curse transmission.

## Guardrail

Romion's account of Na-clan ritual history is attributed research/briefing. Do not convert it into neutral anthropological fact outside the GLENMORIL continuity.
