# Ozwald death / corpse mutation thread

Primary sources:
- **In Plain Sight**
- **Falling Sky**

## Death

Near the end of In Plain Sight:
- Ragnvald acts as the sniper/attacker;
- FL-4N says Ozwald has been hit;
- a player/topic state explicitly says **Ozwald is already dead**.

Falling Sky opens with the player telling Romion:
> “Ozwald is dead.”

Romion accepts this as the current reality.

## Corpse transformation

FL-4N says:
- tentacles began growing from Ozwald's corpse;
- the corpse rapidly changed shape into another creature.

The party then follows the resulting monster.

The quest alias table separately contains:
`zzzLaMM02OzwaldMutated` — display **Ozwald**.

## Earlier prognosis

This follows Ozwald's long deterioration:
- Quagmire exposure;
- curse progression;
- Romion's three-month-at-best prognosis;
- increasingly severe illness.

## Guardrail

The dialogue directly establishes:
**Ozwald dies → corpse transforms.**

It does not yet establish:
- whether Ozwald's consciousness survives;
- what the transformed creature fundamentally is;
- whether the change is caused solely by the curse, the metallic insect, Eyes of Hinnom, Ja'zel, or another mechanism.
