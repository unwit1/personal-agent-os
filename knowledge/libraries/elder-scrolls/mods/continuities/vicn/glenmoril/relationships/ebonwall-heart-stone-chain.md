# Ebonwall Heart Stone chain

The Heart Stone now has a direct multi-quest chronology.

## Ebonwall

- Tabiah is found in a broken/obsessive state around the stone.
- Ozwald says the stone's influence drove her mad.
- The hunters remove it rather than kill Tabiah.
- Romion later confirms the stone was sent to **Ja'zel**, who specifically requested it because of its enormous magic.

## Or the Gospel

Ja'zel later says:
- the Heart Stone came from **Ebonwall**;
- it contains the souls/grudges of Ebonwall's people;
- he arranged for Ebonwall to be exposed to the Thalmor so the stone would become available;
- the stone is used to break the target barrier and is ultimately fired during the assault.

## Evidence distinction

Ebonwall directly establishes possession/recovery and transfer to Ja'zel.

The claims about souls/grudges and Ja'zel's orchestration are later **Ja'zel/Ozwald dialogue claims** and should remain attributed accordingly.
