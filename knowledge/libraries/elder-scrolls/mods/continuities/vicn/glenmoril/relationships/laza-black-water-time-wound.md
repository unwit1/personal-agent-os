# Laza / black water / time-wound model

Primary Professor Y source:
**The Secret Tale, Vol. 6 — Black Waters**

## Directly described phenomena

The book presents:
- black water in a forest identified by the characters with the Garden of Kyne;
- a dragon affected by active black water;
- Laza's altered body/state;
- a great hole at Hinnom.

Professor Y says the water:
- consumes souls;
- consumes their time;
- is normally inert;
- becomes catastrophic in an active state.

## Professor Y hypothesis

Professor Y proposes:
- Laza served as a medium for time-related magic;
- a deep wound in space/time opened;
- the black water flowed through it;
- the water may be **Alduin's or Alkhan's blood**, or one of his forms;
- some future actor may wound/defeat Alduin deeply enough to produce the event;
- that future event can echo backward through story/myth.

These are explicitly speculative.
