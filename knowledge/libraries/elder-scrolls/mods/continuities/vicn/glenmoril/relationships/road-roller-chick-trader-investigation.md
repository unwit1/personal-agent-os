# Rolls-On-Roads / Chick Trader investigation

Primary source: **Brain-Billy's Sting**

## Legality / public institution

Rolls says:
- Chick Traders operate legally in Skyrim as **adoption brokers, ostensibly**;
- Falkreath's jarl invited them because the hold could not support all its war orphans.

He later states more directly:
> the Chick Traders are really **slave traders**.

## Missing orphans

Rolls says:
- many children taken by Chick Traders disappear;
- the magical countermeasures concealing them are sophisticated enough to defeat his tracking;
- the concealment resembles **ancient Reach magic**;
- only a few children can be accounted for as mine laborers in Markarth;
- the numbers otherwise do not add up.

## Ja'zel

Rolls says:
- Ja'zel noticed his Psijic surveillance net;
- Ja'zel deliberately remained at its edge as if provoking him;
- if Ja'zel is not himself a mage, his perception is extraordinary;
- Rolls prefers surveillance over direct confrontation until Ja'zel reveals more.

After Ja'zel's hidden-glen attack, Rolls says Ja'zel delayed him during the blood-curse incident and outplayed him.

## Institutional conflict

Rolls says the Psijic Order generally will not intervene simply to save the orphans unless a child has exceptional magical importance.

This is a direct characterization of Rolls's/Psijic institutional priorities, not a neutral moral judgment by the library.
