# Ja'zel — Or the Gospel role

Resolved Gospel scenes substantially expand Ja'zel beyond his earlier Chick Trader / childhood-memory material.

## Directly demonstrated role

Ja'zel:
- supplies intelligence on enemy composition;
- tracks the temple barrier and cockatrice;
- coordinates an assault involving Eton Nir Tech automatons;
- briefs Ozwald on Hela and the expected Hircine-avatar summoning;
- claims the Oneiromancer anticipated their movement;
- knowingly directs Ozwald toward a temple from which the Red Drop was already removed;
- reveals that the true immediate objective is an inscription identifying the thief and family;
- intends to decipher that inscription.

## Character implication

The source supports Ja'zel as an active planner and information gatekeeper, not merely a symbolic Chick Trader figure. His withholding of the Red Drop's absence from Ozwald is directly demonstrated behavior.

Avoid inferring motive until later dialogue establishes why he withholds information.
