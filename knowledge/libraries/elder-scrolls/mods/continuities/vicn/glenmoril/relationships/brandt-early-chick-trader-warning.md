# Brandt — early Chick Trader warning

Primary source: **The Way is Blocked**.

Before Ja'zel is introduced as the active Chick Trader in the next quest, Brandt already knows that **the Chick Traders are coming soon**.

Brandt:
- wants Lalanoah kept away from Falkreath;
- calls Chick Traders disgusting people;
- reacts as though their arrival is an established local danger rather than a new discovery.

## Significance

This directly establishes that the Chick Trader threat predates the player's formal involvement and that Brandt has prior knowledge of it.

It should be retrieved with:
- The Chick Trader (`zzzLrhMq02`);
- Ja'zel;
- Lalanoah/Chick terminology;
- later Ja'cobee claims that Ja'zel is an impostor.

## Unresolved

The source does not yet explain how Brandt knows the Chick Traders, what he thinks they will do in Falkreath, or whether he knows Ja'zel specifically at this point.
