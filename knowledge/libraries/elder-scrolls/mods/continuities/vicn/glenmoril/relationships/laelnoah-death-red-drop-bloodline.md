# Laelnoah death / Red Drop / daughter-blood thread

Primary source: **In Plain Sight**.

## Laelnoah's death

Ozwald finds the woman targeted for rescue dead after torture.

The preceding quest directly identifies that woman as **Laelnoah**.

Ozwald immediately worries about how to tell **Lalanoah**.

Therefore:
**Laelnoah is dead by this quest.**

## Gray Drop

A gray, powerless object resembling the Red Drop is found at the body.

Ozwald describes it as:
- stone-like;
- gray;
- apparently empty of power;
- possibly the “remnant” described by the Sacred Anatomancer.

## Daughter blood

Eyes of Hinnom says:
- “The Drop prefers blood.”
- specifically **the dead woman's daughter's blood**;
- the daughter should be killed/sacrificed;
- “Give us the daughter.”

The same voice calls the target:
- a Chick;
- a blue bird;
- “our redemption.”

## Identity assessment

Immediate context strongly resolves:
**dead mother = Laelnoah**
and
**daughter = Lalanoah**.

This establishes a direct bloodline relation between Lalanoah and the Red-Drop ritual logic.

## Guardrail

Do not promote the Eyes' claim that Lalanoah's blood is truly required as objective magical law.

What is direct:
**Eyes of Hinnom intends/wants to use Lalanoah's blood/flesh to fill/activate the Drop.**
