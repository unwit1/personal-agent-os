# Jhunal forgetting thread

St. Bazura provides direct evidence that Jhunal's disappearance is accompanied by selective forgetting.

## Direct evidence

- Lalanoah: does not remember anyone named Jhunal.
- Brandt: does not remember Jhunal and replaces the earlier owl-search memory with a treasure-hunt memory.
- Goroll: does not remember Jhunal.
- Orlando: says nearly everyone has forgotten the White Owl and only his family, the player, and Orlando's “us” can still remember.
- Kalka: remembers Jhunal and explicitly says the others' forgetting is **what Jhunal wants**.

Kalka further says Jhunal has gone into hiding for hundreds of years and that the blessing on the land will soon disappear.

## Significance

This is stronger than a simple disappearance: the quest depicts a **memory alteration/erasure pattern** around Jhunal, with exceptions.

Do not yet equate this mechanism with Ellison/Dream Eater-style memory behavior or with every other GLENMORIL forgetting event; keep it source-local until the mod itself links mechanisms.
