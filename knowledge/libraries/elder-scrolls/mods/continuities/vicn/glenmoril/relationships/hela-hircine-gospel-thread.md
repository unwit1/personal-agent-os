# Hela / Hircine / Or the Gospel thread

Quest family: **Or the Gospel**

Existing quest/dialogue evidence connects this portion of the story to **Hela**, the **Goji clan**, a battle, and an **avatar of Hircine**.

## Provenance rule

Keep the following distinct:
1. Hela as the character already resolved in the Oneiromancer scene.
2. Hircine as the Elder Scrolls deity/concept.
3. Any actor or representation described by the mod as an **avatar of Hircine**.

Do not collapse an avatar actor into the Prince's full ontology merely because the source labels it Hircine-related.

## Cross-quest significance

Hela previously:
- knew Zuzu;
- mourned Zuzu's loss;
- invoked Jhunal as God of Runes.

The Gospel material therefore intersects the Hela/Zuzu/Jhunal memory thread with Hircine/hunt material.
