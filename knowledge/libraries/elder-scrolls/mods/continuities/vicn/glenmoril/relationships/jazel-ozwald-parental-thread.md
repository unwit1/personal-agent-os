# Ja'zel / Ozwald parental thread

In the Or the Gospel Orc briefing, Ja'zel describes himself as a **caring parental figure** to Ozwald.

Ozwald directly rejects the claim: he says he never thought of Ja'zel as a parent and never will.

Ja'zel responds that Ozwald has not changed from **the little boy he was**, and says he has learned to love Ozwald's rebellions.

## Interpretation

The dialogue directly supports a longstanding relationship reaching back to Ozwald's childhood and a parental framing asserted by Ja'zel. It equally directly establishes Ozwald's rejection of that framing.

Do not normalize the relationship simply as “parent/child”; preserve both perspectives.
