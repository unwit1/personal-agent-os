# Jhunal the White / Zuzu / curse-door thread

Primary source: non-SCEN Shop of Curiosities topic dialogue.

The quest alias `Owl` directly resolves to **Jhunal the White**.

Jhunal tells the player:
- they have met **Zuzu**;
- Zuzu despises those **tainted by blood**;
- Zuzu **rejected** the player.

Jhunal then gives the player a **key** to another door at the bottom of the well.

He frames entering that door as a test of concern for **Lalanoah** and says the player should:
- sleep in the “starving darkness”;
- wake after **someone else breaks their curse**.

Jhunal calls this the best remaining choice.
