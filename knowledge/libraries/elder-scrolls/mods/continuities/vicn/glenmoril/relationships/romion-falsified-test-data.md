# Romion / falsified test data

Primary source:
`Glenmoril.esm:12D779 zzzLamNoteRomion` — **Romion's Last Note**

## Admission

Romion says:
- Ozwald's Blood Curse of Laza was already far advanced when the player joined the hunters;
- Ozwald sometimes thought the player was Felix;
- Romion feared Ozwald would dispose of the player;
- he therefore **falsified the test data**;
- Ja'zel knew;
- Ja'zel instructed him to emphasize the seriousness of mutation and the curse to the player.

## Consequences

Early medical dialogue cannot be treated as a completely neutral clinical record.

The Last Note creates a reliability distinction:

- **physical observations Romion makes in the moment** may still be useful;
- **reported test conclusions used to influence the player's behavior** may have been manipulated;
- Ja'zel was consciously involved in the deception.

## Relation to Ja'zel's plan

Romion retrospectively says this should have made him suspicious of Ja'zel's plan.

He does not claim he knew the entire plan at the time.

## Guardrail

Do not assume every single Romion test/result in the mod was falsified.

The note proves intentional falsification occurred, but does not identify every altered measurement by quest/stage.
