# Five Chicks — Revenant forms

Primary sources:
- **The Chick Trader**
- **Legless Friends**

## Act 1

The five Chick aliases are:
- Cilla
- Asmo
- Sanna
- Heikki
- Urmas

Ja'zel counts these five and expects a sixth.

## Revenant

Legless Friends directly reuses the same five names as quest actors.

Cilla says:
> “We used to have two legs like you, but now we don't.”

The group is now represented by legless/slippery forms.

## Lalanoah

Cilla says Lalanoah is absent because:
> she missed the Chick Trader's carriage.

This directly distinguishes Lalanoah's path from the five transported Chicks in this Revenant branch.

## Interpretation

Direct:
**Cilla/Asmo/Sanna/Heikki/Urmas → transported Chick group → Revenant altered forms**

Unresolved:
- what process changed their bodies;
- where the carriage originally took them;
- whether the Revenant forms are physical survivors, dream/revenant echoes, or recreated story forms.
