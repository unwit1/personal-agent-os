# Rolls-On-Roads / Romion — Psijic thread

Primary source: **Brain-Billy's Sting**

When the player first mentions Romion, Rolls asks whether the Dwemer researcher is an **Altmer**.

After the player describes Romion as an Altmer with glasses, Rolls says he had an old friend from his Psijic-apprentice days who:
- was an eccentric;
- left to study the Dwemer;
- later wrote that he had found employment with the **Thalmor**;
- then disappeared from Rolls's life.

Rolls expresses surprise that someone with those skills would now work with the Witch Hunters.

## Cross-evidence

Romion independently says elsewhere that:
- he belonged to the Eton Nir Research Institute;
- the institute became increasingly militarized under the Thalmor;
- he later left and eventually became associated with Ozwald's Witch Hunters.

## Assessment

The overlap is strong:
- Altmer;
- Dwemer researcher;
- Thalmor research employment;
- later Witch-Hunter association;
- approximate chronology compatible with Romion's history.

Within GLENMORIL alone, Rolls never says the direct sentence:
> “Romion is my old Psijic friend.”

However, DAc0da later has Rolls-On-Roads explicitly name **Romion** as his old friend/former colleague and describes the same Altmer Dwemer-scholar/apprentice relationship.

Therefore:

- **GLENMORIL-only reading:** high-confidence hypothesis from description;
- **parent VICN synthesis with DAc0da:** high-confidence shared-character relationship.
