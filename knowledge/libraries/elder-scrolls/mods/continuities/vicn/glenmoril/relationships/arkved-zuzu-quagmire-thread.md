# Arkved / Zuzu / Quagmire bridge

The Bronze Key supplies the direct transition into the Oneiromancer quest.

## Arkved

Romion says the “Bronze Key” is **Arkved's body**, recovered by a Thalmor spy and used by Eton Nir for Vaermina-related research.

At recovery, only Arkved's brain/spinal-cord material is visibly preserved in the labeled biometric container.

Ja'zel later says Arkved's body remains **connected to Quagmire**.

## Jhunal the Black / guide role

In **The Black Owl 04**, Jhunal the Black speaks to someone the player cannot see and explicitly identifies him as **Arkved**.

Jhunal says:
- Arkved is a **guide in Quagmire**;
- the player cannot reach the intended destination there without a guide;
- dreams constantly change, so one must “fly” from dream to dream.

Jhunal closes by saying he will wait for the player's **true awakening**.

This independently corroborates Arkved's guide function from a separate Black-Owl source.

## Zuzu

In the same Eton Nir facility, the hunters find a giant female body.

Ja'zel identifies it as **Zuzu**, the Oneiromancer / giant maiden of Vaermina, and says:
- the body is merely a shell;
- Zuzu's soul is in Quagmire;
- she sends dreams from there to warn witches about threats.

## Dream Engine Room

The Thalmor Radio orders guards to keep intruders away from the **Dream Engine Room** and says their target is probably **“her.”**

Given the quest's direct Zuzu/Arkved chamber sequence, this is a strong facility-level link to the Oneiromancer operation, but retain “her” as contextually resolved rather than rewriting the radio line itself.

## Red Drop

Ozwald concludes that Zuzu's warning network explains why the hunters cannot locate elder witches and their Red Drop.

## Bridge

**Eton Nir research facility → Arkved connection → Zuzu shell → Quagmire → Oneiromancer hunt**

This bridge should be used when reconstructing late Act-2 chronology.
