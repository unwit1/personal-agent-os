# Torvesard / Guide / memory-loop thread

Primary source: **Ghost in the Mirror**.

Torvesard describes a deliberately constructed causal loop:

1. the player repeatedly reaches Torvesard through Quagmire;
2. Hermaeus Mora rewrites the player's memories;
3. deep instinct survives those rewrites and returns the player to the same contact point;
4. Torvesard has prepared a **Guide in Falkreath** whose fate is hidden;
5. the Guide is intentionally kept ignorant;
6. instinct will cause the Guide to find the player;
7. if the player leaves the planned path, the Guide reveals the forgotten Prince's name;
8. Mora then rewinds the observation point and the player must begin again;
9. at a later stage, the Guide's father's soul creates access to the Guide's dream;
10. the player must kill the Guide in that dream and offer his head to summon Torvesard.

## Significance

This is one of GLENMORIL's clearest explicit statements of:
- memory erasure;
- retained instinct beneath erased memory;
- fate/path engineering;
- observation-point resets;
- an intentionally ignorant guide.

## Guardrail

Do not automatically merge this mechanism with:
- Jhunal's forgetting;
- the player's curse-memory inheritance;
- Ja'zel's free-will loops;
- Dream Eater-style memetic effects;
- Dragon Break mechanics.

They are retrieval-linked themes but require source-local proof for mechanism identity.
