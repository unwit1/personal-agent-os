# Tuning box — organic interior

Primary source: Romion's non-SCEN Oneiromancer topic dialogue.

When asked what is inside the tuning boxes, Romion says:
- he asked the Chick Trader, who laughed and refused to explain;
- Romion nevertheless saw **something organic** inside;
- it looked fleshy, “like a pig organ or something like that”;
- he did not believe it was human or mer.

## Cross-evidence

Other resolved dialogue establishes:
- Ja'zel calls tuning boxes **dreaming machines** used to stabilize form in Quagmire.
- Ja'zel later says there are **exactly five chicks in the box** and that the chicks dream.
- Romion opposes the technology because it suppresses physical transformation without preventing mental effects.

## Ontology boundary

The combined evidence makes the tuning boxes both technological and organic/chick-bearing, but does not yet establish:
- what species/ontology the organic matter has;
- whether each “chick” is literally a creature, mind, soul, dreamer, or another substituted term;
- whether Romion saw a chick or a separate component.

Keep those questions open.
