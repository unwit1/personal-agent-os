# Rolls-On-Roads — Jhunal / Oneiromancer model

Primary source: **Brain-Billy's Sting**

This page preserves Rolls-On-Roads's evolving interpretation rather than treating it as objective truth.

## Early White-Owl possibilities

Rolls initially considers the talking owl could be:
- a mage's familiar or remote proxy;
- a servant/avatar associated with Jhunal;
- an impostor.

He identifies the owl-holding ancient statue as **Jhunal / Julianos** and connects the hidden glen to an old perception-blocking ward.

## Hidden glen ward

Rolls says:
- the hidden glen uses a perception-blocking ward;
- unauthorized observers can fail to perceive or enter it properly;
- the ward can persist for at least another decade;
- the **altar of Jhunal** serves as the base of the sealing magic.

This independently supports the concealment/protection function already associated with the Jhunal rune site.

## Later White-Owl interpretation

After the Oneiromancer events, Rolls says the player probably did **not** defeat Jhunal himself.

He proposes that the opponent was:
> “Jhunal's forgotten divinity and the spirit who wore it.”

Rolls further says:
- this spirit appeared as the **White Owl**;
- it was following an ancient oath;
- its protective purpose went out of control;
- spirits cannot simply break such vows themselves.

## Oneiromancer / perception protection

Rolls says Zuzu's perception barrier was a form of **mental protection**.

After Zuzu's defeat:
- the protection disappears;
- more people become vulnerable to taboo knowledge;
- mental collapse/madness increases;
- Zuzu appears to have accepted this because maintaining the status quo could not solve the underlying problem.

This broadly aligns with Ozwald's later claim that the Oneiromancer had been protecting the hunters.

## Reliability boundary

Rolls repeatedly frames these points as analysis, deduction, or changing hypothesis.

Store:
- direct observations as high-confidence evidence;
- his ontology of Jhunal/White Owl and Zuzu's intentions as **attributed analytical claims**.
