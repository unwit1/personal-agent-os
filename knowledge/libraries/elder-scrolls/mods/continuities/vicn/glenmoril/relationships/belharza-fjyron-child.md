# Belharza / fjyron child thread

Primary source:
**Song of Fjyron**

Sister Gaga says Emperor **Belharza** entrusted her with:
- a secret gift;
- intended for an old friend.

After recovering the stolen gift/cocoon, Sister Gaga:
- gathers **fjyrons**, which she calls soul echoes / accumulated experience;
- spins their songs into thread;
- knits a swaddling cloth;
- wraps a child in those songs.

She says:
- the child will become a good advisor;
- the child's **words** will guide Belharza's old friend;
- those words will push the friend onward like Kyne's breeze.

## Open identities

The source does not directly name:
- Belharza's old friend;
- the child/advisor;
- the eventual historical recipient of the child's words.

Do not identify them from external myth parallels alone.
