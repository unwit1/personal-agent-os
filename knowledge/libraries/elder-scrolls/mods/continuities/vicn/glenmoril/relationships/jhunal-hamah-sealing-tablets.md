# Jhunal / Hamah sealing tablets

Primary MESG records:
- `Glenmoril.esm:11E010 zzzLaMMq04MsgTablet01`
- `Glenmoril.esm:11E011 zzzLaMMq04MsgTablet02`

## Tablet 1

The first tablet says, in the name of **Jhunal**, those who committed abominations are sealed in the land so no one will be enchanted by their curse.

## Tablet 2

The second says:
- **Sinful Hamah** and her abominations are hidden away;
- the concealment remains until people forget their **names**;
- and forget their **forms**.

## Direct chain

**Jhunal invocation → sealing/concealment → Hamah → curse prevention → forgetting names/forms**

## Cross-links

Retrieve with:
- Jhunal concealment magic;
- Hamah succession;
- Hamah's Snow-Elf/Anem rebirth;
- selective forgetting;
- Name/identity themes;
- hidden glen/perception wards.

## Guardrail

The tablets do not prove Jhunal personally performed the sealing.

They prove the sealing was **performed/invoked in Jhunal's name**.
