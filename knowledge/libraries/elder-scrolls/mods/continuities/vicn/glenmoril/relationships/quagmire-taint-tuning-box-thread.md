# Quagmire taint / tuning-box thread

The main Oneiromancer briefing directly establishes the intended logic for entering Quagmire.

## Romion's research briefing

According to material he says was recovered from the Thalmor, physical entry into Quagmire can cause:
- bodily change;
- mental harm;
- accelerated beast possession.

## Ja'zel's countermeasure

Ja'zel describes tuning boxes as **dreaming machines** that can:
- show the dream;
- stabilize the entrant's shape.

He then proposes using the Cradle-Woods baby to take care of the boxes' side effects.

## Later corroboration links

This should be retrieved with:
- the resolved Tuning Box scene, where Ja'zel says each box contains five chicks and that the chicks dream;
- the Medic scene, where Romion says Ozwald's condition worsened rapidly after Quagmire;
- the Baby scene, where Ja'zel describes the child as a curse given flesh.

The briefing therefore supplies an explicit causal bridge between Quagmire exposure, tuning-box stabilization, the baby, and later taint progression.
