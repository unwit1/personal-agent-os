# Apka-Lul — nymic surgery

Primary source:
**Nymic Surgery**

Apka-Lul presents:
- a replica of **Mehrunes' Razor**;
- a blade whose power he says still derives from Dagon.

He describes it as:
> **the word of fire itself**

and says that through:
- destruction;
- rebirth;

it can carve:
> **new words into blood and bone**.

He asks the player which words should be added.

## Relation to Words of Worlding

Words of Worlding says:
- nymics define existence;
- Daedra are constrained by their defining words;
- mortals can grow beyond names.

Nymic Surgery turns that theory into an explicit **rewriting procedure** applied through a Dagon/Razor-derived blade.

## Evidence boundary

This is Apka-Lul's ritual model.

Do not generalize it to every nymic alteration mechanism in Elder Scrolls lore.
