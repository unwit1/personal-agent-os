# Player curse — suppression, decay, memory transfer, and healing

## Name

**Romion's Last Note** directly names the condition:
**Blood Curse of Laza**.

Romion describes Laza as a Nedic nomad, while admitting very little reliable information about him survives.

## The Blessing

Jhunal the White says the player is **blessed** and must remain pure or the blood curse will crawl toward them.

## The Blue Bird

Romion says:
- mutation should normally have begun;
- the condition is unusually stable;
- **something is restraining the curse**.

## Brain-Billy's Sting — Rolls-On-Roads model

Rolls-On-Roads describes the blood curse as:
- a type of **parasitic mind magic**;
- capable of transferring **personality and memories** into the subject;
- relatively harmless while the victim retains a strong sense of self;
- worsened by mental instability/stress.

He says:
- the player's physical wounds regenerated at an abnormally fast rate;
- **something acts as a protective barrier** holding back the curse's erosion.

This independently overlaps Ja'zel's later claim that memories can pass through the curse, but remains Rolls-On-Roads's technical interpretation.

## Romion's Last Note — direct mechanism

Romion says the Blood Curse of Laza causes:
- hallucinations;
- clouding of consciousness from the **overflow of Laza's memories**;
- mistaking nearby people for family and loved ones;
- progressively improved healing and regeneration.

Crucially, Romion says the curse **by itself should not mutate the infected into a monster**.

He says terminal abnormal mutations must be caused artificially by **outside influences** and suspects the Chick Trader may have tampered with the process.

## Falsified testing

Romion admits:
- Ozwald's curse was already advanced when the player first joined them;
- Ozwald sometimes mistook the player for **Felix**;
- Romion **falsified the player's test data** because he did not want Ozwald to get rid of the player;
- Ja'zel knew about the falsification;
- Ja'zel told Romion to emphasize how dangerous the mutation/curse was to the player.

This materially changes how the early medical briefings should be read.

## Tainted Orphan

Romion later tests:
- parasites;
- physical mutation;
- memory integrity.

At that point:
- no parasites are detected;
- mutation is absent;
- memory remains clear.

Those results remain direct dialogue evidence, but the Last Note proves Romion had already falsified at least some earlier test data, so diagnostic claims should be read with chronology/source caution.

## Or the Gospel

Romion later says:
- the player's body is undergoing **constant decay and mutation**;
- something simultaneously **heals the damage too quickly for the mutation to take hold**.

## Current model

The strongest combined source model is:

1. **Blood Curse of Laza** transfers/overflows Laza-associated memories and destabilizes identity.
2. The base curse increases healing/regeneration.
3. Monstrous terminal mutation is **not intrinsic to the curse**, according to Romion's final written assessment.
4. One or more outside agents/processes artificially drive mutation.
5. A separate protective/healing force also resists or reverses part of the damage.

Do not yet identify either the mutation driver or the protective force with a single actor without direct evidence.
