# Player curse — suppression, decay, and healing

Resolved Act-2 dialogue gives a chronological progression for the player's curse.

## The Blue Bird

Romion examines the newly cursed player and says:
- mutation should normally have begun;
- instead the condition is unusually stable;
- **something is restraining the curse**.

## Tainted Orphan

After further exposure, Romion tests:
- parasites;
- physical mutation;
- memory integrity.

At that point:
- no parasites are detected;
- mutation is absent;
- memory remains clear.

## Or the Gospel

Romion later says:
- the player's body is undergoing **constant decay and mutation**;
- something is simultaneously **healing the damage too quickly for the mutation to take hold**.

## Related evidence

Ozwald says the player inherited **Felix's curse**.

Ja'zel says memories can pass through a curse and warns the player to preserve their sense of self.
