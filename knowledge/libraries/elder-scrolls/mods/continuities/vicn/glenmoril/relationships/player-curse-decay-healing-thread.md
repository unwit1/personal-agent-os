# Player curse — suppression, decay, memory transfer, and healing

## The Blessing

Jhunal the White says the player is **blessed** and must remain pure or the blood curse will crawl toward them.

## The Blue Bird

Romion says:
- mutation should normally have begun;
- the condition is unusually stable;
- **something is restraining the curse**.

## Brain-Billy's Sting — Rolls-On-Roads model

Rolls-On-Roads describes the blood curse as:
- a type of **parasitic mind magic**;
- capable of transferring **personality and memories** into the subject;
- relatively harmless while the victim retains a strong sense of self;
- worsened by mental instability/stress.

He says:
- the player's physical wounds regenerated at an abnormally fast rate;
- **something acts as a protective barrier** holding back the curse's erosion.

This independently overlaps Ja'zel's later claim that memories can pass through the curse, but remains Rolls-On-Roads's technical interpretation.

## Tainted Orphan

Romion checks:
- parasites;
- physical mutation;
- memory integrity.

At that point:
- no parasites are detected;
- mutation is absent;
- memory remains clear.

## Or the Gospel

Romion later says:
- the player's body is undergoing **constant decay and mutation**;
- something simultaneously **heals the damage too quickly for the mutation to take hold**.

## Related evidence

Ozwald says the player inherited **Felix's curse**.

Ja'zel says memories can pass through a curse and warns the player to preserve their sense of self.

## Current model

The source strongly supports:
1. a memory/personality-transfer curse process;
2. active resistance/suppression/healing that prevents or delays its full effect.
