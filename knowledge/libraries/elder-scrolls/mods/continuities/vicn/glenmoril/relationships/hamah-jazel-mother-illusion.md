# Hamah on Ja'zel — mother illusion

Primary source:
**Generic Dialogue Hamah**

Asked directly about Ja'zel, Hamah says he is:
> “an illusion of a mother, created by a bastard child who never knew one.”

She says the illusion:
- takes on a body;
- produces a child;
- eventually tries to devour that child;
- desires undivided love;
- cycles between devouring and being devoured.

## Significance

This is one of the strongest direct source statements interpreting Ja'zel through:
**mother / child / imitation / consumption / love** imagery.

It should be retrieved alongside:
- Ja'zeri's orphaned/enslaved history;
- Mar-Dro and Tahana;
- Ja'zel's “real Chick” attachment to the player;
- Hamah's own created-daughter/imitation model.

## Guardrail

This remains **Hamah's interpretation**.

Do not turn “mother illusion” into a literal biological identity claim without additional evidence.
