# Hamah / Lalanoah blood-curse construction

Primary source:
**The Lost Storyteller**

The quest structurally includes:
**Blood Curse of Lalanoah**.

Hamah asks the player to leave the remains of her daughter with her.

She then says:
> from flesh and blood, she spun the memories of her daughters and wove them into a single blood curse.

## Acceptance

Hamah asks whether the player has the resolve to:
> devour the curse and make it their own.

She says accepting it will:
- bind the player to Hamah more deeply;
- cause Hamah's voice to resonate more strongly.

She also says the player can refuse.

## Identity significance

This source directly links:
- Lalanoah's remains;
- Hamah's created daughters/duplicates;
- preserved memories;
- a transmissible blood curse;
- deeper Hamah/player integration.

## Purity reversal

Hamah says:
> what is required is not purity, but a soul that will not be crushed by corruption.

This stands in deliberate tension with Jhunal the White's earlier:
**blessing → remain pure → resist blood curse**
language.

Do not collapse the two doctrines into one mechanism without further evidence.
