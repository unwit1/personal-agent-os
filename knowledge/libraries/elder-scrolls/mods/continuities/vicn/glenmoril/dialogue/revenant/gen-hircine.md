# Gen Hungry Cat — Hircine dialogue

Quest:
`Glenmoril.esm:33DEF1 zzzRevGenHircine`

**21 exact responses**

Speaker:
- Hircine: 21

## Vahdin Holt

Hircine remembers the player's earlier encounter in Vahdin Holt and says the hunt entertained him.

## Ancient Anequina contract

Hircine says:
- the king of ancient Anequina asked him to **paint the Blue Star red**;
- Hircine hid the king beneath a bloody tent so the king would not see the star's blue;
- Ja'bal later took the king prisoner.

## Beast curse

Hircine says he made a contract with the king:
- everyone led astray by Ja'bal would be turned into beasts;
- the king's followers would hunt them;
- pelts would be paid as tribute.

He says some victims escaped:
- both the hunters;
- and Hircine's curse.

Those escapees then devoured **Ja'bal's flesh and blood**.

When asked who they were, Hircine answers:
> **Yelem and his assistant.**

## Chick Hunt

Hircine says:
- he helped with the Chick Hunt;
- he considers hunting parents who protect their children especially entertaining.

This is direct Hircine characterization, not a moral or historical endorsement.

## Ja'bal

Hircine says:
- he does not know Ja'bal's true origin;
- Ja'bal may be from another kalpa, a future/past one, or an adjacent place;
- Ja'bal is something that “cannot be” yet became real;
- Ja'bal now **lurks within the player**.

He warns:
> never let the cage break.

## Chick / Y'ffre

Asked what a Chick is, Hircine says:
- it is something named/sung about by **Y'ffre**;
- Y'ffre once made even the stars dance;
- Hircine dismisses the rest as nonsense.

## Evidence boundary

**Anequina king → Blue Star → beast curse → Chick Hunt → Yelem/Boltzmer escape → Ja'bal**.

Keep it attributed to Hircine.
