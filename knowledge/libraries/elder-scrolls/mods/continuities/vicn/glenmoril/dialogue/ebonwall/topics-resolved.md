## Player transmitter / curse

FL-4N reveals that the hunters implanted a **transmitter in the player's body** with effective range across Tamriel.

Romion says Ja'zel only refrained from executing the player on condition that the transmitter be attached.

Ozwald says it is temporary and should remain only until the player's curse fades.

## Ja'zel / memory inheritance

Ja'zel warns the player:
- not to ask too much about certain hidden matters;
- an owl can be black or white, but one should avoid involvement with the **gray one**;
- memories can be **passed down through the curse**;
- the player must remember who they are and what it feels like to be themselves or risk becoming a monster.

This is direct evidence that at least one curse mechanism can transmit **memory/identity content**.

## Ebonwall / Ashlanders

Romion provides in-world historical and cultural background on:
- Ashlanders;
- Ashkhan/Gulakhan/Wise Woman roles;
- Wulfharth's Ebonwall;
- the urban Ashlander community formed by displaced people after the Argonian invasion;
- the escalating Nord–Ashlander conflict.

These are Romion's source-attributed explanations.

## Eton Nir

Ozwald identifies Eton Nir as an Altmeri Dwemer-technology institute and Romion's former organization.

He suspects its agents want to collect the Glenmoril witch's curse.

FL-4N describes an Eton Nir communication device with roughly hold-scale range and soul-gem power.

## Branching aftermath

Romion has conditional aftermath lines:
- in one state, the Nord army retreats and some Ashlanders survive and settle in Windhelm;
- in another, all Ashlanders are executed and Ebonwall becomes abandoned.

Romion confirms the recovered **Heart Stone** was sent to the Chick Trader because Ja'zel wanted it and it contained enormous magic.
