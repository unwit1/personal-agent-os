# St. Bazura — Goroll, Kalka, and FL-4N dialogue bundle

All rows resolve through INFO `GetIsAliasRef` quest-alias conditions.

## Goroll — alias 3

Goroll prepares food and oils the St. Bazura effigy. After watching it burn, he says his bad shoulder feels much better, as though he were possessed.

He does not remember Jhunal.

Goroll's St. Bazura folklore identifies Bazura as a **Wood Elf who died and rose again after three days**.

He says Lalanoah is eating more since beginning fighting/hunting practice and considers that part of her growth.

## Kalka — alias 6

Kalka says the burning statue makes her mind clearer each time it burns. She dislikes St. Bazura because **her people were burned because of his mistake**.

Unlike the others, Kalka remembers Jhunal. She says:

- Jhunal has gone into hiding;
- it will take hundreds of years for him to return to this land;
- the blessing on the land will soon disappear;
- the player should protect **Hamah** when that happens;
- the others' failure to remember Jhunal is **what he wants**.

Kalka says Lalanoah is growing well and will soon become a **suitable vessel for succession**, adding that “we” are delighted with her.

Asked what happens after Lalanoah can care for herself, Kalka says they will wait **until the next Hamah, a much later Hamah**.

## FL-4N — alias 7 `DweGear`

FL-4N enjoys the feast and says he entered through a door he had never noticed before.

Later he says the inscription has been deciphered, the ship is anchored nearby, and the upcoming mission is the last one.
