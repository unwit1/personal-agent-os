## Pailune

Boltzmer says:
- Yelem built **Pailune** in an oasis of Anequina;
- after Yelem succumbed to madness, much of it sank into the cursed land;
- residents became trapped in nightmares/madness.

Mayor Ra'scon says:
- he is mayor only in name;
- Yelem and Boltzmer are the real leaders.

Wilmer describes the crisis as:
- plague;
- Alessian intervention;
- sudden darkness;
- blue moon/star;
- eastern forest;
- southern ruins;
- beast transformations.

## Daughter of Kyne

Multiple speakers say a captured priestess/Daughter of Kyne:
- was brought from Skyrim;
- was exhibited in a freak show;
- later ended up in Yelem's research facility.

This points toward the Hamah/Daughter-of-Kyne Revenant thread while remaining speaker-attributed.

## Seed of Mythos

Boltzmer describes the **Seed of Mythos** as:
- a star-seed left by Y'ffre;
- capable of bringing stories back toward the past;
- capable of mending broken memories;
- able to sprout only in a human body.

He tells the player they may become its **nursery**.

## Baby

Boltzmer calls the baby:
- the unwanted child of men;
- a child wanted by the unwanted.

He says the player should hold it to their chest and sleep in darkness when the time comes.

## Ja'bal

Ja'bal says:

- it remains unreachable;
- the player is the awaited harvest.

Asked the Owl's secret, he says:

Asked about Chick/EGG terminology, he says:

- the player is also a key;
- flesh is remade by nightmares to fit the lock;
- “we” failed but the player will fulfill the Owl's expectations.

- this may be why he cannot perceive them;
- the player's own “stink” may make Laza able to perceive the player.
