## Feast

Lalanoah welcomes the player enthusiastically, repeats the Feast motto “Bazura, Bazura, Bazura,” talks about the food, wonders what Bazura actually means, comments that Bazura burns well, and wishes the fun could continue forever.

Asked about Bazura, she gives a family folklore version: a man tried to bait and defeat a Falkreath monster while shouting “Bazura.” Her father said the word meant having a good time; her mother said it meant shouting for help.

She says the Feast is held when there is no particular celebration but people still want to party, and this is her third St. Bazura's day.

## Letter

## Family and fear

Lalanoah says she has recently become afraid at bedtime that she will wake to find everyone gone and be alone again. She does not want to leave for the Temple of Kynareth and wants to stay with everyone.

She fears never seeing the player again “like my dad.” She says Uncle Brandt became drunk and tearfully told her about her father.

Asked about hope for her mother, Lalanoah says she probably will never see her again because **the search party is gone too**.

She nevertheless resolves to become stronger and ultimately says the player should come back home soon.
