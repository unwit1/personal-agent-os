## Star-child of the Hist

Azurah says:
- long ago a **child of the Hist descended as a star**;
- it twisted the souls and memories of her followers;
- it remade them into monsters.

She says:
- with Hircine's help, its body was destroyed;
- its spirit was sealed;
- nevertheless it took root in dreams and continues attracting future victims.

## House Sul / unknown child

Azurah says:
- House Sul had been loyal to her;
- aboard/among them she sensed a presence similar to House Sul but unknown to her;
- the being must have been born without her knowledge;
- the Hist observed him and made an **imperfect replica**.

## Yelem

> is now trapped in the soul of the Dwemer **Yelem**.

She asks the player to:
- defeat Yelem and his disciples;
- release the trapped being.

She says the released heart, however broken, can fill the hole in the player's missing heart.
