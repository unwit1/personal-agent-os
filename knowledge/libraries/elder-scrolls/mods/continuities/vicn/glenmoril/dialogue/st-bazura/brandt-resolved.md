## Jhunal / altered memory

## St. Bazura folklore

Brandt says the St. Bazura story is famous in Falkreath. His version says that two hundred years ago the Hero of Cyrodiil defeated the monster of Falkreath and Bazura was the Hero's follower.

Brandt says Bazura set himself on fire as a decoy to draw the monster's attention and was then resurrected three days after being burned to death, continuing to follow the Hero.

When the player suggests “Bazura” is a corruption of **“By Azura,”** Brandt says some scholars argue exactly that, although people dislike the theory. He speculates the word may mean something closer to “over my dead body.”

Brandt says the Feast is held when people want noise and excitement. He organized this one to cheer Lalanoah up. The ignition ceremony consists of burning the St. Bazura statue/effigy.

## Lalanoah family

Brandt says a relative from the Temple of Kynareth recently visited: **Cerebline**, an Altmer who claims to be Lalanoah's grandmother's sister. Brandt says he did not know Lalanoah had elven ancestry.

The search for Lalanoah's mother is going badly. Contact with the search party has been lost, and the road to **Piper's Hill** is blocked until the snow melts.

Brandt says Lalanoah cries for her parents less often and is getting stronger. He expects Lalanoah eventually to become an apprentice at the Temple of Kynareth.

Brandt also expresses a desire for a quiet, mundane life and a family of his own after his guardianship role ends.
