## Vaermina / Quagmire

Prompt: **Tell me more about Vaermina and her realm.**

1. Romion identifies Vaermina as the Daedric Prince ruling dreams and nightmares and Quagmire as her realm.
2. He reports a theory that she collects sleeping mortals' memories and leaves nightmares of fear and despair.
3. He proposes recovering those memories from Quagmire to obtain otherwise missing knowledge.

## Waking entry

Prompt: **Tell me more about breaking into the dream world.**

1. Romion cites research by **Divayth Fyr and Morian Zenas** showing that special magic can enter Quagmire while awake.
2. He says Dwemer technology can now generate pseudo-gates based on that research.

## Arkved

Prompt: **I'd like to know more about Arkved.**

1. Romion says Arkved stole a treasure from Vaermina in the Third Era and was imprisoned in nightmares as punishment.
2. Romion says he contacted Arkved “in a special way”; Arkved is not mentally sound but agreed to help. Romion warns not to trust him.

## Baby / tuning boxes

Prompt: **What can you tell me about that baby and the tuning boxes?**

1. Romion says he opposes their use: they may suppress physical change but do nothing to prevent mental effects.
2. He calls the technology uncertain and dangerous and wishes he had more time to test it.

Prompt: **Do you know what's inside the tuning boxes?**

1. Romion says the Chick Trader laughed and refused to answer, but Romion believes he saw **something organic inside**.
2. He describes it as fleshy, like an organ, and says it did not look human or mer.

## Oneiromancer

Prompt: **I'd like to know more about the Oneiromancer.**

1. Romion says she is a witch dating to the **Dawn Era**.
2. He says she wiped out most information about herself when she left for Quagmire and warns that ancient magic may have been much stronger than modern magic.

## Entering Quagmire physically

Prompt: **Is there anything special I need to be aware of in Quagmire?**

1. Romion stresses that the group is entering **in the flesh** and warns against interfering with things or inhabitants there.
2. Otherwise, he tells the player to treat it like any other dangerous mission.
