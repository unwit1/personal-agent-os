# Chaplain's Song 1 — resolved dialogue

Quest: `Glenmoril.esm:009B83 zzzGHMq01SideA`
Speaker: **Chaplain's Louse**
Rows: **10 exact SCEN responses**

Five paired warnings are delivered over five scenes.

The Chaplain repeatedly calls the player a **little louse** and urges them to turn back.

The warning sequence includes:
- “Why are you heading towards the blood curse? Chicks should remain pure.”
- “It is foolish to smear yourself with mud.”
- “You still have your eyes... Ignorance is good.”
- “It is foolish to give yourself over to the Owl.”
- “You still have a song to sing... Myrrh smells bitter.”
- “It is foolish to stretch yourself on the grave.”
- “You still have the blessing... Kyne must be mad.”
- “It is foolish to taint the Spring.”
- “You are past all prayers... Mortals are truly nothing but lice.”
- “It is foolish to submit to the maggots.”

## Cross-links

This establishes very early use of:
- blood curse;
- Chick purity;
- Owl danger;
- Kyne;
- Spring;
- louse imagery.

Later Chaplain dialogue reuses the same symbolic/religious vocabulary, culminating in the recurring **Kyne's Feast of Love** language.
