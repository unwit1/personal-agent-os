## Lalanoah

Lalanoah says:
- she is waiting because she cannot go home;
- Uncle Brandt told her not to enter town;
- she believes Brandt's behavior may somehow be her own fault;
- when her father was still there, he was kind and played with her;

- her mother taught her to run away rather than overextend herself in danger;
- after meeting Groll, she considers Groll a new friend;
- she wants the player to become her friend too and visit again.

## Brandt

Brandt says:
- Falkreath's orphans lost their parents in the civil war;
- temples and orphanages are already full;
- he does not want outsiders interfering with Lalanoah;
- he will not say where her parents are;
- he cannot simply take responsibility for Lalanoah because his “position” does not allow it;
- he wants her kept away from town because **the Chick Traders are coming soon**;
- Chick Traders are, in his words, disgusting people.

His outward hostility is therefore already paired with active concern about keeping Lalanoah away from a known threat.

## Groll

Groll says:
- he is guarding the area because **“they” ordered him to**;
- what he guards is “a small green thing called Lala,” strongly resolving Lalanoah as the protected target;
- he receives **glowing stones** as reward;
- he will not/cannot clearly identify who issued the order;
- he tries to stop frightening Lalanoah;
- he accepts the friendship with Lalanoah;
- when the player calls him female, he replies: **“Not female. Maiden.”**

## Unresolved

- why the jarl forbids Brandt from helping/bringing Lalanoah into town;
- why Brandt says his position prevents him from simply caring for her;
- what exactly Brandt already knows about the coming Chick Traders.
