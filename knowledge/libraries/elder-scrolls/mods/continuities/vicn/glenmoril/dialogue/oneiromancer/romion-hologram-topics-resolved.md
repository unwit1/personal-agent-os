1. Romion says the Dwemer actively explored Oblivion, but most attempts failed because they could not withstand its harsh environments.
2. He says some empty Dwemer ships may still roam after the Dwemer disappearance and mentions rumors that **Psijics recovered some**.

2. Romion says he will handle the memory extraction and recovery process.

This bundle strengthens the interpretation of the Quagmire machine as an old Dwemer Oblivion-exploration vessel rather than an ordinary stationary laboratory.
