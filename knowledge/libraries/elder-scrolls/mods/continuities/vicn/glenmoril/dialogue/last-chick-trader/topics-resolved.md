## Ja'cobee

Ja'cobee directly says:

- he is the **last** Chick Trader and all the others died;
- the Chick Traders' duty is effectively complete, but he cannot quit because the **EGG has not been completely broken**;
- neither he nor the player needs to know exactly what the EGG is; **Ja'i** said they would recognize it when they saw it;
- Chick Trader rules are deliberately vague and the duty is performed without knowing the truth;
- he does not recognize **Ja'zel** as a Chick Trader and calls him an impostor;
- the original Chick Traders were selected from the **prince of Anequina and his royal guards**, whose names/faces Ja'cobee remembers;
- the current dream began when the EGG cracked and will end when it breaks completely;
- when Ja'cobee finishes his duty he will finally be able to die;
- the girl in the dream will merely wake and forget this place, while Ja'cobee will forget her.

### Origin story

Ja'cobee says:
- their beginning was prophesied;
- **Sheggorath** spoke through a severed head and told the clerk **Ja'i three stories**;
- from these they learned that Rolly-Polly prefers kittens;
- the resulting “baby hunt” killed kittens in Anequina.

Ja'cobee explicitly says the Chick Trader story is made misleading by **replacing words and adding metaphors** so listeners do not understand it.

He describes the mission as:
- tie down the Chickens;
- crush the Chicks;
- warm the Eggs;
- avoid Rolly-Polly.

### EGG → CHICK → CHICKEN

Ja'cobee says:
- once he realized what was inside the EGG, it ceased to be an EGG and became a CHICK;
- a CHICK grows into a CHICKEN;
- when the CHICKEN cackles, **dawn comes and cats lose their sleep**;
- he crushed it before that could happen.

He says he escaped into the dream after breaking the EGG but before Rolly-Polly caught him. When the dream ends, Rolly-Polly will catch and “melt” him, and **everyone will forget Ja'cobee**.

### Owl

Asked what is inside the EGG, Ja'cobee refuses to say but says the secret is **about the Owl**.

He says:
- learning the secret of the eggs let him foresee what the player could not, including Rolly-Polly;
- **the Owl had been observing them all along, from the very beginning**;
- the player should be wary of the Owl.

### Sacrifice

Ja'cobee says that in exchange for **the life of a kitten**, Chick Traders bring peace to the world, but concludes that the arrangement is “nothing but a sacrifice.”

He says the player will also have to pay Rolly-Polly and calls this fate.

## Ulliss

Ulliss says:
- she was walking with **Aisha** when a booming noise and snow suddenly transported her here;
- Aisha is “a cat, a very cute cat”;
- she has become attached to Ja'cobee because he is lonely;
- Ja'cobee tells her that a tail makes someone practically Khajiit;
- she helps him collect **yellow round things**;
- Ja'cobee told her not to touch the EGG;
- she believes a chick is “a yellow and round thing.”

Her cheerful literal interpretation of Chick/Khajiit language should not be used to decode Ja'cobee's substituted vocabulary.
