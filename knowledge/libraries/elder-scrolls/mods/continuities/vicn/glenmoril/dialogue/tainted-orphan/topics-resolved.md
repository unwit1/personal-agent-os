## Ja'zel / Chick Trader mission

FL-4N tells the player that Ja'zel specifically requested them.

Ja'zel says:
- the Baby's location has been found;
- he considers the mission a rare opportunity for the player;
- he personally chose/entrusted the player with it;
- the “Slacker” in the Markarth jail is a former Chick Trader who broke a rule and must be killed;
- the future mission target is the “rotten stillborn baby” in Cradle Woods;
- this sort of killing/work is simply part of their daily life and will now be part of the player's life too.

The Slacker denies understanding what rule he broke and blames events involving a woman named **Maria** and the birth of a cursed child.

## Cradle Woods / Na clan

Romion says:
- Cradle Woods is an ancient western-Reach forest tied to Glenmoril;
- the **Na clan** lived there until the ghoul outbreak and then vanished;
- the clan was highly secluded and did not join the Forsworn uprising;
- Reachfolk society has close connections to Glenmoril/Hagraven matriarchs.

The cultural generalizations remain Romion's in-world descriptions.

## Cuhlecain / ghosts

Romion calls Cuhlecain “Emperor Zero,” says the details of his death remain uncertain, and references the Arcturian Heresy as interesting reading.

Artur:
- says a blood-smelling man passed through before the player;
- reports black-clad elves in strange masks;
- continues to insist Hjalti will return and did not betray them;
- blames Botvid's suspicion after the king's death for their executions.

## Baby manifestations

Artur says a Baby sometimes appears in the area, stops moving, and disappears again.

After mission completion Romion says removing the source will not restore those already transformed into ghouls.

He also reports that Ja'zel was attacked several times by the Thalmor while transporting the Baby but successfully reached the hunters' base.

## Markarth children

Ja'zel says children work in mines because they fit where adults cannot, comments coldly on injured/sick children, and warns that Markarth's stones conceal many dark secrets.
