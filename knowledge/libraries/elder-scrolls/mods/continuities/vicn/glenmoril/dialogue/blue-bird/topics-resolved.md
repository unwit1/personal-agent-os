## Lalanoah / Laelnoah / Jhunal the White

Lalanoah says:
- her mother's owl has returned;
- **Jhunal is her mother's pet owl**;
- he is wise and often brings back things that are lost or forgotten;
- Laelnoah often spoke to Jhunal, although he only nodded;
- she hopes her mother will return and wants to surprise her by learning restoration magic.

This directly links:
**Laelnoah → Jhunal the White → lost/forgotten things**.

## Brandt / Gerhard

Brandt says Lalanoah has been crying at night because she misses both parents.

He asks the player to locate **Gerhard**, beginning with Quartermaster Agrippa.

When Gerhard's death is proven with his sword, Brandt asks the player not to tell Lalanoah and later says Gerhard is probably in Sovngarde.

Brandt also confirms he has been looking for **Laelnoah** but has no leads at this point.

## Agrippa / missing Imperial units

Agrippa says:
- Gerhard's unit is officially treated as deserters;
- they were dispatched to Fort Sungard and then Bilegulch Mine;
- several other units around Fort Sungard also “disappeared/deserted”;
- **Ja'zel** previously asked him similar questions about those disappearances.

This places Ja'zel's investigation ahead of the player's.

## Gerhard / Blue Bird

Gerhard says:
- his unit was attacked by a **giant blue bird**;
- it said it was hosting dinner and wanted to eat them;
- he has a wife and daughter in Falkreath;
- he asks that his sword be returned;
- he bought Lalanoah a hair ornament and asks that it be given to her if found.

## Ozwald / Witch Hunt

Ozwald says:
- the player is cursed and will eventually mutate unless something changes;
- the choice is join the witch hunt and try to break the curse or die;
- Ja'zel is already watching the player;
- elder witches of Glenmoril created the curse affecting Ozwald and the player;
- even **Hinnom** is affected;
- elder witches possess **Red Drops**;
- Hinnom's wet nurses say Red Drops are catalysts needed to break the curse.

Ozwald says Hinnom is a place for children abandoned by their parents and calls it his home.

## Felix contradiction

Ozwald identifies the monster the player defeated as **Felix**.

Asked whether Felix had a sister, Ozwald says:
> “No, he didn't. I'm sure of it.”

He refuses to engage with Felix's remembered “child who smiles like the sun and likes white flowers.”

## Jhunal the White

Jhunal the White:
- tells the player not to search for Lalanoah's father;
- says Lalanoah already has the current household and will forget the shadow of her parents after leaving the nest;
- predicts her parents will already be dead by the time they are found;
- refuses to explain the curse;
- says breaking it requires finding the **untainted daughter of Kyne**;
- says the player must remain pure to meet her;
- warns that **the Black Owl has found the player**.

## Ja'zel

Ja'zel says he is waiting near the ghoul cave for help because a **kitten** entered.

When challenged about chick/kitten terminology, he explicitly says:
- kittens and chicks are different;
- cats do not lay eggs.

When ordered to stay away from Lalanoah, Ja'zel calls her **“just one chick”** and jokes that the player could obtain replacements.

This demonstrates deliberate context-dependent use of Chick terminology without making kitten/chick synonymous.

## Romion / Yelem School

Romion says:
- he is reproducing technology from the **Yelem School**;
- Yelem of Arkngthamz and his blacksmiths founded it;
- other Dwemer considered them heretics;
- their technology differed from eastern-Skyrim/Morrowind Dwemer work;
- in simplified terms, they preferred **explosions over steam**.

Romion identifies Felix as Ozwald's missing partner.
