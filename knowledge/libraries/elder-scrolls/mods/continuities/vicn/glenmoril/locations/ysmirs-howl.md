# Ysmir's Howl

Quest anchor: **The Bronze Key**

Romion describes Ysmir's Howl as a valley southwest of Solitude that once belonged to Solitude and later came partly under Thalmor control.

According to local legend as reported by Romion:
- Talos cut the cliff with his Voice during the Second Era;
- the sound of wind through the terrain gave the place the name **Ysmir's Howl**.

The area contains the fortified **Temple City of Talos** and an Eton Nir Research Institute facility central to The Bronze Key.

## Late state

The quest presents the city as depopulated after a purported epidemic, with Romion and Ozwald suspecting Thalmor/Eton Nir involvement.

After recovering Arkved and equipment, Ozwald says the hunters blew up the laboratory and city to obstruct pursuit.

All of these historical/geographic claims are GLENMORIL-continuity source claims.
