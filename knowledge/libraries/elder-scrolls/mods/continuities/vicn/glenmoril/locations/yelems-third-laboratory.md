# Yelem's Third Laboratory

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

**Yelem's Third Laboratory** is a named GLENMORIL location.

The ESM also contains multiple BOOK records titled **Yelem's Note**, while earlier Act 2 dialogue lets the player ask Romion about the **Yelem School**. These are three independent record-level anchors—school, laboratory, and notes—supporting Yelem as a recurring knowledge/research thread.

The identity of Yelem, the laboratory's purpose, and its connection to Romion, Zuzu, Dwemer studies, or oneiromancy remain unresolved pending textual and quest evidence.
