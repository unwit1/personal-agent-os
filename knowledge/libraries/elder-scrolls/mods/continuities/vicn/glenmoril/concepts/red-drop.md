# Red Drop

The **Red Drop** is a major Or the Gospel objective/reference.

## Direct Gospel evidence

Ozwald expects it at the attacked temple. Ja'zel reveals that it was taken away long ago and that he knew this before the assault.

Ja'zel says the temple instead contains an inscription giving the name of the thief and the thief's family. He says Hela guarded the secret and the Oneiromancer hid the route to it.

Ja'zel further describes the treasure as **walking around in a different form**.

## Unresolved ontology

Do not yet equate the Red Drop with:
- a particular person;
- a bloodline;
- the EGG;
- the Baby/curse entity;
- Lalanoah;
- any other transformed object/entity.

Those may be hypotheses for later testing, but the current direct evidence supports only theft, family information, protection, and a claimed alternate/mobile form.
