# Blue Star

The **Blue Star** recurs across several high-value late-Act-2 sources.

## Direct GLENMORIL evidence

### Louse
Yelem says he is waiting for **“the one who stands on Mnemo-Li, the Blue Orphan”** because “we did not qualify for it.”

### Confined
Jhunal the Black says his interference must be minimal while forcing a no-choice situation, otherwise **“we will not be able to see the Blue Star.”**

### Lucid Dream
Ja'zel eventually says the **Blue Star is visible now**, but that seeing/following it is not his role.

### Subject Dialogue
- Test Subject No.32 says seeing a Blue Star through the clouds was followed by severe bodily itching/transformation symptoms.
- Test Subject No.43 tells someone to **aim for the Blue Star**, saying freedom lies beyond it.

## Interpretation boundary

The source tightly clusters Blue Star, Mnemo-Li/Blue Orphan, freedom, transformation, engineered events, and Ja'zel/Jhunal/Yelem material.

Do not silently collapse **Blue Star**, **Mnemo-Li**, and **Blue Orphan** into one ontological entity merely because Yelem's line places the terms together. Store the equivalence/relationship question explicitly for later corroboration.
