# The Great Cat

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary evidence:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

The **Great Cat** recurs in Yelem's notes and also appears in the named location **Rift of the Great Cat**.

Yelem's writings connect the Great Cat with a red rift/trail, Shezarr, repeated lives, endless falling, and existence beyond ordinary moral categories. One note states that “the cat lives a million lives” and calls this “the essence of Shezarr.”

These statements are Yelem's metaphysical claims. The library therefore records a strong **Yelem → Great Cat ↔ Shezarr** conceptual association without treating the equivalence as official Elder Scrolls cosmology.
