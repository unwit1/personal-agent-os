# Oneiromancy

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

Oneiromancy is directly represented in the plugin through:

- the Act 2 quest **Oneiromancer**;
- the named NPC **Zuzu the Oneiromancer**;
- dream-associated location records such as **Dream Surface**;
- additional dream-related records to be resolved in the same quest cluster.

This page is intentionally source-specific. It does not yet equate GLENMORIL's oneiromancy with Vaermina's Dreamstride, standard Elder Scrolls dream magic, or any particular Vicn-wide metaphysical mechanism until the relevant dialogue/books explicitly support those connections.
