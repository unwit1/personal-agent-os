# Beast-Possessed Soul

## Cleaning Up Falkreath

Brandt says:
- the creature is a **beast of Hircine**;
- unlike a werewolf, it cannot return to human form;
- the condition is therefore worse than ordinary lycanthropy.

The slain creature carries a broken Imperial Legion amulet. Brandt interprets it as a prisoner transformed by Reachmen.

## The Blessing

Ja'zel says:
- werewolves can become men again;
- beast-possessed souls cannot;
- when a man becomes a beast, **“his soul turns white”**;
- he calls it a “sweet curse.”

## Bronze Key

Later dialogue says Eton Nir is experimenting on and mass-producing beast-possessed souls, possibly for sabotage.

## Guardrail

The source supports multiple actors/processes associated with beast possession. Do not assume every beast-possessed soul was created by the same faction or method.
