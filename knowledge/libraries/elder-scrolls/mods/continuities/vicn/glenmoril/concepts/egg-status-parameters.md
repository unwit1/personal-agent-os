# Egg / Star status parameters

Primary source:
`Glenmoril.esm:09CFA8 zzzRevMsgMenuEgg`

The MESG status display explicitly exposes two parameter groups.

## Star color

“The Star's color depends on the beholder's multiples of three”:

- **Humanity**
- **Beasthood**
- **Insight**

## Egg rotation / direction

“The Egg's rotation and direction depends on”:

- **Scarab**
- **Enantiomorph**

## Significance

This is unusually direct system-facing evidence that:
- the Star's perceived color is parameterized by observer state;
- Egg orientation/rotation is parameterized separately;
- Scarab and Enantiomorph are not merely decorative words in this interface.

## Guardrail

The MESG does not itself define:
- numerical ranges;
- what actions alter each parameter;
- whether these values are literal metaphysical quantities or gameplay abstractions;
- the exact cosmological meaning of Scarab/Enantiomorph.

Store as **mechanic-facing ontology evidence**.
