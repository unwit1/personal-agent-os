# Witch Hunt

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

**Witch Hunt** is the explicit title of Act 2 in GLENMORIL's internal **Black Book: Debug**.

## ESM-supported structure

The act contains, in the debug book's order:

1. The Blue Bird
2. Ebonwall
3. Embers
4. Percussive Maintenance
5. Tainted Orphan
6. A Thousand Daggers
7. The Bronze Key
8. Shop of Curiosities
9. Oneiromancer
10. Broken Egg
11. Or the Gospel
12. St. Bazura

The Blue Bird contains direct dialogue allowing the player to agree to join a witch hunt, and Ja'zel's threatening letter attempts to coerce the player into participating.

Subsequent QUST records establish a recurring operational pattern involving FL-4N, Ozwald, airship briefings, mission documents, field targets, and post-mission conversations.

This page describes the act/operation as represented by the ESM; it does not equate GLENMORIL's use of “witch” with any single official Elder Scrolls faction or category.
