# Shivering Mushroom

Source actor:
`Glenmoril.esm:0085B8 zzzLrhFamShiveringMush`

Display: **Shivering Mushroom**

## Direct dialogue

The mushroom says:
- “We can share a strawberry torte?”
- “Strawberry torte! Ta ta!”

Kanra bites it, triggering an explosive transformation that enlarges Kanra.

The player also undergoes a temporary edible/sweet-roll-like bodily transformation in which movement causes the body to crumble.

Kalka's potion reverses the effects.
