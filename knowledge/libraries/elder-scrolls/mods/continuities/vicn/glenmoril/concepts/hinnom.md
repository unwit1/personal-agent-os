# Hinnom

Hinnom is introduced directly in **The Blue Bird**.

Ozwald describes it as:
> a place for children abandoned by their parents

and calls it his **home**.

## Curse role

Ozwald says:
- the Glenmoril witches' curse affects both him and the player;
- **even Hinnom is affected**;
- the wet nurses of Hinnom say **Red Drops** are necessary catalysts for breaking the curse.

## Felix

During his mutated encounter, Felix asks Ozwald whether he **still believes in Hinnom**.

This gives Hinnom both:
- an institutional/home meaning for Ozwald;
- a belief/ideological meaning in Felix's challenge.

## Unresolved

The current evidence does not yet establish:
- Hinnom's physical location;
- whether “wet nurses” is literal institutional staffing or specialized terminology;
- what it means for Hinnom itself to be affected by the curse;
- the exact relationship between Hinnom, Felix, Ozwald, and later Witch Hunter organization.
