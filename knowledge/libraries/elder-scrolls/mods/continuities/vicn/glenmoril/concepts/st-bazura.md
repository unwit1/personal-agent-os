# St. Bazura

St. Bazura is a named feast/saint/effigy complex in late Act-2 GLENMORIL material.

## Direct feast evidence

- Lalanoah invites the player to a Feast of St. Bazura.
- The celebration includes food, drinking, a motto of “Bazura,” and an **ignition ceremony** in which an oil-soaked St. Bazura effigy/statue is burned.
- The resolved SCEN dialogue explicitly blesses St. Bazura's repeated **death and rebirth**.

## Folklore variants

The participants do not give one stable history:

- Brandt says Bazura followed the Hero of Cyrodiil roughly two hundred years ago, set himself on fire as a decoy against the monster of Falkreath, died, and returned three days later.
- Goroll has heard a version in which Bazura was a Wood Elf who died and rose after three days.
- Lalanoah says her father connected “Bazura” with having a good time, while her mother said it was a cry for help.
- Kalka dislikes Bazura and says her people were burned because of his mistake.

Treat these as **in-world folklore variants**, not automatically reconcilable historical facts.

## “By Azura” theory

The player can explicitly ask whether “Bazura” is a corruption of **“By Azura.”** Brandt replies that **some scholars argue this**, although ordinary people dislike the theory.

This upgrades the etymology from an analyst-only hypothesis to an **explicit in-world scholarly theory**, but it still is not established as the true derivation.

Do not normalize St. Bazura to Azura in entity IDs.

## Possible effects of the burning

After the statue burns:
- Goroll says an old shoulder problem feels much better.
- Kalka says her mind gets clearer each time the statue burns.

Record these as reported effects/associations, not proven supernatural causation.
