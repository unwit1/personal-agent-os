# St. Bazura

St. Bazura is a named celebration/event concept in the late Act-2 GLENMORIL material.

## Direct associations

- Lalanoah sends an invitation connected to a St. Bazura resurrection celebration.
- The associated subquest reconnects Lalanoah, Jhunal, Orlando, and White/Black Owl material.
- ignition/resurrection language is part of the event cluster.

## Etymology

A possible “Bazura” ↔ “By Azura” wordplay/corruption has been proposed during analysis, but remains an **interpretive hypothesis**, not direct canon.

Do not normalize the term to Azura in indexes or entity IDs.
