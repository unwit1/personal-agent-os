# Seed of Mythos

Primary source:
**Generic Revenant**, Chaplain Master Boltzmer.

Boltzmer describes the Seed of Mythos as:
- a **seed of the star** left by Y'ffre;
- something whose miracle can bring other people's stories back toward the past;
- something that can **mend broken memories**;
- something that can grow only in a **human body**.

He tells the player:
> “You will become its nursery when the time is right.”

## Guardrail

This is Boltzmer's description.

The source does not yet independently establish:
- which star supplied the seed;
- whether “bring stories back to the past” means literal time travel, restoration, memory reconstruction, narrative recreation, or another mechanism;
- how it relates to the Blue Star, White Star, Seed imagery elsewhere, or the player's curse.
