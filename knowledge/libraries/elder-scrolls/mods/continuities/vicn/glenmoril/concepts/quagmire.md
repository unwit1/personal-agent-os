# Quagmire

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

In the Oneiromancer quest, Romion has dialogue topics explicitly concerning **Vaermina and her realm**, breaking into the dream world, and precautions in **Quagmire**.

The following quest, **Or the Gospel**, contains retrospective dialogue asking what happened in Quagmire and discussing information recovered from the Oneiromancer's memory.

This provides direct ESM evidence that GLENMORIL uses Quagmire as a Vaermina-associated dream-world destination entered during the Oneiromancer sequence.

The exact relationship between Quagmire, **Dream Surface**, Zuzu's memories, and other dream spaces still requires CELL/scene mapping.
