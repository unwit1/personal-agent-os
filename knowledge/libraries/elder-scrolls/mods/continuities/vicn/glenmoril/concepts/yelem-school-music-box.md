# Yelem School music box

In **Percussive Maintenance**, Romion directly identifies Lalanoah's music box as a rare artifact made by the **Yelem School**.

## Condition

Romion finds:
- missing screws;
- a bent frame caused by impact;
- nearly dried-out lubricating oil.

He repairs it quickly with ordinary workshop materials.

## Significance

This demonstrates that Yelem School production included **small domestic/entertainment devices**, not only the larger experimental machinery associated with Yelem's laboratories.

## Organizational guardrail

The Yelem School is not automatically identical to **Eton Nir**.

Romion explicitly treats the music box as a Yelem-School artifact while separately describing his own former membership in Eton Nir.
