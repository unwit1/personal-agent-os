# Monocle of Intelligence

Source item:
`Glenmoril.esm:007EF8 zzzLrhMonocleG01`

Display name: **Monocle of Intelligence**
Description: **“Fortifies Intelligence.”**

## Arrival

In **Ah, Apple Pie!**, the monocle falls onto Lalanoah's roof after a shimmering **flying ship** passes overhead.

The quest does not identify the ship.

## Lalanoah effect

Lalanoah wears the monocle and quickly experiences:
- headache;
- a sense that something feels wrong.

She removes it.

## Groll effect

Groll wears the monocle and immediately reports:
- clearer thoughts;
- smoother speech.

Her dialogue changes from broken speech to fluent, grammatically complete sentences.

She also experiences a strong desire to cook.

## Retrieval links

Groll; Lalanoah; flying ship; Dwemer-derived technology; Romion; Yelem School; Ozwald airship.

## Guardrail

The item should be cross-retrieved with later Dwemer/airship technology, but **no direct source currently identifies the flying ship or monocle as Romion, Ozwald, Yelem School, or Eton Nir technology**.
