# Avatar of Hircine

GLENMORIL's Or the Gospel material includes a Hircine-related battle/avatar representation.

## Ontology

Store the avatar as a **representation/manifestation claim** within the GLENMORIL continuity. Do not automatically merge its source actor with the canonical Hircine entity.

Recommended graph relation:

`[source actor] --represented_as/avatar_of--> Hircine`

rather than:

`[source actor] == Hircine`

This permits later dialogue, scripts, transformations, or quest aliases to refine the relationship without losing provenance.
