# Chick

**Chick** is a deliberately overloaded GLENMORIL term and should be represented as an evidence-backed concept rather than translated into one fixed referent.

## Direct evidence

### Dream Within a Dream
Child Felix says he would rather remain a **chick forever** than undergo baptism, grow up, and become what he fears as a monster. Child Ozwald connects baptism rules to Ja'zel's teaching.

This directly links chick-language to childhood, baptism, and growing up in this scene.

### Tuning Box
Ja'zel says there are **exactly five chicks** in a tuning box and says those chicks have happy dreams and nightmares.

This directly links chicks to dreaming and to a machine/object context.

### Ja'zel
Ja'zel's later speech places chicks alongside Rolly-Polly, Ja'bal the Cat, and EGG.

### Ja'cobee
The Last Chick Trader uses CHICK/CHICKEN/EGG vocabulary while discussing the Chick Traders, repeated cycles, dawn, sleep/dream, sacrifice/payment, and the Owl.

## Ontology rule

Do **not** globally rewrite Chick as child, soul, dreamer, sacrifice, or any other single concept. The library should retrieve all of these evidence clusters and let higher-level interpretation remain explicitly qualified.
