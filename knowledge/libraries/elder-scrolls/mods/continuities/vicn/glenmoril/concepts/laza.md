# Laza

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL

Laza is a recurring Nedic/Reach-linked figure at the center of the Blood Curse, the Spring, Owl guidance, and time-distortion material.

## Primary text anchors

### Laza
The short book `Laza` says:
- take on the name of Laza and become an immortal hunter;
- chase the Stone;
- hunt souls burned by the Stone;
- crush the Stone and bring peace to captive souls and the Old Forest.

### The People of Grasslands
This book says:
- the grassland nomads served Kyne;
- a dark voice destroyed them, leaving only Laza;
- Laza blinded himself;
- he crawled on hunting the Stone;
- peace existed at the bottom of the Spring;
- Laza never slept again.

### Laza and the Owls
White, Gray, and Black Owl guidance leads Laza through:
- abandoning shepherdhood;
- becoming wolf/wind;
- blinding both eyes;
- avoiding blood;
- reaching Kyne's Spring;
- washing Nedic maiden blood from a dagger;
- losing the staff and gaining fangs.

## Professor Y observations

`Professor Y, Vol. 4 — The Endless Fall` says:
- Professor Y was led to Laza's remains by a black-clad figure calling himself Jhunal;
- Laza is not exactly dead;
- Professor Y describes him as still active at extreme speed while appearing suspended;
- Laza is dreaming.

`The Secret Tale, Vol. 6 — Black Waters` adds Professor Y's interpretation that:
- Laza became a medium for time-related magic;
- a wound in space/time opened around Hinnom;
- black water consumes souls and time;
- Professor Y speculates that this water may be Alduin/Alkhan blood or one of his forms.

## Blood Curse

Romion's Last Note explicitly names the player's/Ozwald's condition:
**Blood Curse of Laza**.

It causes:
- memory overflow;
- hallucinations;
- identity confusion;
- abnormal healing/regeneration.

## Guardrail

Directly supported:
- Laza is a named Nedic-linked figure;
- blindness, Spring, Stone, Owl, immortality/hunting, and blood-curse themes recur around him;
- Professor Y physically studies a Laza-associated body/remain.

Attributed/speculative:
- Alduin/Alkhan origin of the black water;
- exact mechanics of Laza's “lightning-speed” state;
- whether Laza is person, role, curse-carrier, mythic identity, or layered combination.
