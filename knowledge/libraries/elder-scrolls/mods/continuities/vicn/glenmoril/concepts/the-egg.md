# The Egg

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary evidence:** Yelem's Note BOOK records in the supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

“Egg” is a recurring GLENMORIL concept, not merely the title imagery of **Broken Egg**.

Yelem's notes associate an egg with unrealized possibility, death, a body without an image, a “body without organs,” larvae/worms beneath organized form, and—in the Laza series—a possible alternative description of something first called a moon: “Not a moon. A cocoon or an Egg.”

These are Yelem's in-world formulations. Their relationship to the quest **Broken Egg**, the Tainted Orphan, dream mechanics, or later Vicn-continuity metaphysics remains a hypothesis until explicit links are extracted.
