# The Owl

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

The Owl is one of GLENMORIL's most persistent symbolic/entity terms.

## Direct evidence clusters

- Yelem's notes repeatedly invoke an Owl.
- Later dialogue says Yelem once **chased an owl and awakened a nightmare sleeping in the depths**.
- Ja'cobee says the secret of the Chick Traders' eggs concerns **the Owl**.
- Act 2 separately distinguishes **White Owl** and **Black Owl** topics.
- **St. Bazura** contains explicit questions about the locations of both the White Owl and Black Owl.

## Evidence boundary

The generic Owl, White Owl and Black Owl are indexed as related terms but not automatically treated as one entity. Likewise, the Owl is not yet identified with Rolly-Polly, Vaermina, Hircine, or another established TES power without explicit supporting dialogue.
