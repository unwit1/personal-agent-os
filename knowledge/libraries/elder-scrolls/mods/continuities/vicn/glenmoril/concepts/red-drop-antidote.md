# Red-Drop antidote

Primary sources:
- **Little Red**
- **Lice and Maggots**

## Ja'zel's recipe

Ja'zel gives an explicit recipe:
- a little blood from each race;
- Nirnroot;
- the **Red Drop** soaked in the mixture.

He converts this into two pills.

Intended use:
- one for the player;
- one for Ozwald.

After Ozwald's loss, Ja'zel suggests giving the spare to **Lalanoah**.

## Ja'zel's claim

After treatment Ja'zel says:
- the curse has been lifted;
- the player no longer needs to fear becoming a monster.

## Jhunal the Black's contradiction

In **Lice and Maggots**, the player directly asks Jhunal the Black whether the medicine Ja'zel provided is truly an antidote.

Jhunal answers:
> “It is only poison.”

He explains:
- death removes the curse, so calling it an antidote is “not a complete lie”;
- it would no longer work on the player because **the player's curse is too advanced**.

This directly contradicts the straightforward reading of Ja'zel's earlier cure claim.

## Evidence model

Directly supported:
- Ja'zel prepared and administered the pills;
- Ja'zel called them a cure;
- Jhunal the Black later calls the same medicine poison;
- Jhunal says its “cure” mechanism is death;
- Jhunal says it is already ineffective for the player because of advanced curse progression.

Do not resolve the contradiction in favor of either speaker without additional evidence.

Possible interpretations such as deception, partial cure, conditional toxicity, false diagnosis, or multiple curse states remain hypotheses.
