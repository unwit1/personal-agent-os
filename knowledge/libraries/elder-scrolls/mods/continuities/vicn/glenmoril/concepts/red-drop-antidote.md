# Red-Drop antidote

Primary source: **Little Red**.

Ja'zel gives an explicit recipe:
- a little blood from each race;
- Nirnroot;
- the **Red Drop** soaked in the mixture.

He converts this into two pills.

Intended use:
- one for the player;
- one for Ozwald.

After Ozwald's loss, Ja'zel suggests giving the spare to **Lalanoah**.

## Claimed effect

After treatment Ja'zel says:
- the curse has been lifted;
- the player no longer needs to fear becoming a monster.

## Evidence boundary

Direct:
- ingredients;
- preparation;
- two pills;
- Ja'zel's claimed cure.

Not yet independently established:
- exact biochemical/magical mechanism;
- whether every blood curse variant is cured;
- whether the cure is permanent;
- what role the Red Drop plays beyond being required in the mixture;
- whether Lalanoah's condition is the same curse as the player's.
