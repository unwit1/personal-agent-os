# Rolly-Polly

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary evidence:** Ja'cobee/Chick-Trader INFO responses in supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

Rolly-Polly is a central term in the Chick Trader's deliberately encoded mythology.

Ja'cobee says the Chick Traders' origin stories revealed that Rolly-Polly targets “kittens.” He deliberately replaced terms and added metaphors because, in his account, understanding the story causes Rolly-Polly to pursue the knower.

He says breaking the EGG delayed Rolly-Polly but did not escape him, and expects to be caught when the dream ends. Another response frames the lives sacrificed by Chick Traders as payment to Rolly-Polly.

Other quest dialogue associates Rolly-Polly with repeated birth/death, a million rotations, the Blue Star, the EGG/CHICK cycle, and a Wild Hunt.

## Interpretation boundary

The source itself warns that these terms are substitutions. “Rolly-Polly” should therefore be indexed as a coded referent rather than prematurely identified with a known TES deity/entity.
