# Dream gates

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary evidence:** Romion dialogue, Oneiromancer quest

Romion says special magic can allow a waking person to enter **Quagmire**, citing research associated with **Divayth Fyr** and **Morian Zenas**. He says his group can use **Dwemer technology** to generate pseudo-gates from that research.

Before the mission he tells the player that the gate will be opened by a machine. He also warns that the player will enter Quagmire **in the flesh**, rather than merely dreaming.

This establishes GLENMORIL's operational dream-entry mechanism as a hybrid of magical research and Dwemer technology.
