# Chick Traders

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

Chick Traders are a recurring GLENMORIL narrative institution/title.

## Oneiromancer-era evidence

Ja'cobee's dialogue branch includes direct questions about:
- what being a Chick Trader means;
- what Chick Traders did in **Anequina**;
- the EGG;
- why Ja'cobee broke the EGG;
- Ja'zel and whether he is an impostor;
- the “last” Chick Trader.

Ulliss can likewise be asked about Chick Traders and the EGG.

## Later fracture

By **Or the Gospel**, Ozwald dialogue explicitly discusses betraying and later getting rid of “the Chick Trader.” This establishes growing conflict with the institution/representative that recruited or coerced the player into the Witch Hunt.

The exact origin, rules, payment language, and identity succession of Chick Traders require the associated INFO responses before being promoted as lore facts.
