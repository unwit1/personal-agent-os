# Curse given flesh

In the Oneiromancer Baby scene, Ja'zel describes the recovered child/entity from the Cradle Woods placenta material as neither alive nor dead.

His metaphors include:
- like a stone or wind-chime;
- both a star and its rotation;
- power itself;
- pure curse;
- a curse given flesh.

Ozwald reduces this to the idea that it simply exists, like a roadside rock, and Ja'zel agrees.

## Evidence status

These are **Ja'zel's attributed metaphysical descriptions**, not a neutral taxonomy established by the plugin. Index them as descriptions of the Baby/Cradle Woods entity without promoting “curse” to proven ontology.
