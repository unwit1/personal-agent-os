The Yelem School is a branch of Dwemer smithing philosophy that was started by Yelem of Arkngthamz at the beginning of the First Era. Yelem developed and manufactured weapons that could cause explosions by using special chemicals, but the Council of Arkngthamz considered his ideas dangerous and banished him. After his exile, he moved his base to Pailune in Anequina, where he used his technology to help develop the city. Most of the materials used for the tools developed by the Yelem School are now lost, as they were destroyed when the Marukhati Selectives of the Alessian Order burned Pailune to the ground during the Thrassian Plague epidemic.
