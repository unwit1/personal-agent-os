There are no organs to excrete waste, even when they are full of rot and filth.

<p align="left">
Alive, but already dead and rotting without internal organs. This is the demesne of Radiance. This is the sum of multiples of threes. Behold the noose strangling the three fools who became the wedges; that is Love bound by the observer's strings. Behold the gods with three wedges driven into them; it is I who is seeping into the walls of jealousy. Behold the maggots of the three worlds rising from the wedges; it is We who are smeared with erasing bread. These are the multiples of three. Time breaks down and goes insane, and veers out of tune that the dreaming God gave it. You will be free of the old Wheel, and your relationship with stars will be overturned. For you are rediscovered as a void and a pure symbol. This is the repetition of the multiples of three.
</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
Let us now expose his schemes with the multiples of three. For though much blood may be spilled and many names may be lost, all shall be redeemed under the Blue Star.

Boneless limbs heaving, delving pincers cut the tainted branches. Bared rows of blades gnashing, prying orbs gaze at the fish swimming in the river.
</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="center">
Love is violence, hate is peace.
The multiples of three keep on repeating.
AE HERMA MORA ALTADOON PADHOME LKHAN AE AI
