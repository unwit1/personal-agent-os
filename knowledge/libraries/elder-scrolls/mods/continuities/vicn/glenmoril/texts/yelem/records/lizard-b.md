# Yelem's Note — LiB

**Observed Form ID:** `04006448`  
**Source ID:** `Glenmoril.esm:006448`  
**Editor ID:** `zzzRevYelemNoteLizard02`

Yelem states that the first **Great Cat** was a **Rolly-Polly** and says that “the lizard failed.” This is a high-value direct bridge between vocabulary otherwise found in the Yelem and Chick Trader corpora.

It supports a relationship between Great Cat and Rolly-Polly terminology, but does not by itself establish that every use of either term denotes the same entity.
