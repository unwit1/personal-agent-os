About the curse:
What was eating away at you and Ozwald is called the Blood Curse of Laza. I believe he was a Nedic nomad. Very little is known of the Nedes, and next to nothing is known about Laza himself. The symptoms of the blood curse include hallucinations, clouding of consciousness due to the overflow of Laza's memories, and mistaking the people around the infected for their family and loved ones. As these symptoms progress, the infected's body becomes capable of improved healing and regeneration, but not to the point of mutating into a monster. The only thing I know for sure is that the abnormal mutations supposedly occurring in terminally infected must be caused artificially by outside influences. Perhaps the Chick Trader tampered with this...
