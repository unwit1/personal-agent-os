Three Owls live in the northern forest. They give counsel to lost travelers and guide their way.

The White Owl shows travelers a pure path, one that leads whence they came.
But this path has no end and leads only to the moonless night.

The Black Owl shows travelers a foul path, one that leads whither they wish to go.
This path is muddy and difficult, but moonlight will illuminate the way.

The Gray Owl shows a road that must not be taken.
This is not a road for travelers, but a road for the Owls. It is a road drenched in suffering, and it leads nowhere but the wilderness.
