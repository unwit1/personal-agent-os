Vol. 5: Rolly-Polly

Assistant: "These are the remains of Ja'bal we found in the rift of Anequina..." 

Professor Y: "Body parts are missing. I hoped to get these remains in a perfect condition." 

Assistant: "It's the head that's the problem. We can't pierce it with the instruments, so we can't examine the brain."

Professor Y: "No need. As long as Ja'bal doesn't want us to, we'll never be able to look inside his head." 

Assistant: "What is that supposed to mean? Are you saying his head is avoiding our instruments?" 

Professor Y: "Indeed. His head produces an infinite number of localized universes around it. He simply avoids any unwanted future."

Assistant: "So, is the fact that his head's now lying before us also something he wanted?" 

Professor Y: "It's an honor. The moment we recognized it as Ja'bal, we became a part of his universe. Temporarily, of course, but still."

Assistant: "Is it possible to exit this temporary universe?"

Professor Y: "We shall consume his universe. Along with his spinal cord."

Assistant: "This is just wrong... but it feels strangely appetizing. Let's devour it."

Professor Y: "This is what Ja'bal wants for his shriveled Egg. Now let's ascend to the higher plane!"
