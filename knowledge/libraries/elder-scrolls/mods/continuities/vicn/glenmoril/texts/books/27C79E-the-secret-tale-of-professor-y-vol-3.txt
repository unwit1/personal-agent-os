The Secret Tale, Vol. 3: The Tones

Professor Y: Strange. I can still sense the distinctive tones of a Dunmer inside the ship.

Assistant: Dunmer? Was one of them on the ship?

Professor Y: There are traces of virtual personality being transcribed to the brain organ of the ship. The Argonian's consciousness, which was the original body and mind of the ship, was deliberately erased and overwritten.

Assistant: So you're saying that the origin of Ja'bal is not the Hist?

Professor Y: No, it was most likely the Hist who built the ship and gave it its original personality. However, this transcribed personality did not originate from the Hist. But why? I don't understand the Hist's intentions.

Assistant: Professor, there's a white worm-like substance sticking to what may be the brain stem ... What is this?

Professor Y: This looks like another variant of Argonian. Its limbs look like roots ... only the tones of the Hist remain inside. Was it a parasite? Did it try to take over the ship?

Assistant: If this is true and this worm was trying to take over the ship's control, then what's the purpose of the Dunmer transferred to its brain? The Hist is acting erratically, isn't it? I really don't understand.

Professor Y: As a result of all this, two minds were living on this ship. It's as if they were rushed into this ... Was the Hist impatient with something? Just like you when the deadline for your research report approaches.

Assistant: Oh, don't make fun of me, please. This has to be a joke - why would a tree care about time? Well, perhaps. Maybe the Hist who built the ship was faced with some kind of imminent threat.

Professor Y: I suppose. What we know so far is that the ship had two occupants, one of Hist and the other of the Dunmer race, and both of them were mental entities. Also, both of them have left the ship since the crash.

Assistant: Which of the two mental entities is Ja'bal?

Professor Y: Discussing that may be pointless. It could be either of them, or it could be neither. The Great Cat, Rolly-Polly, Ja'bal ... all of these are just different names for the same entity, and they are all just a result of how the cats of Anequina interpreted this series of events at the time. As are our interpretations. All we can do is apply our own knowledge and concepts to these outlandish events. The truth may never be found, not even by the forces of Apocrypha.

Assistant: Be that as it may, I would at least like to know which of the mental entities was in the body we ate.

Professor Y: They say that the heartbroken King of Anequina often dreamt of the great trees. But we dream of a brass giant towering over the world that became nothing but a wasteland. Perhaps it was the Dunmer.

Assistant: Ah, no wonder sujamma tastes so good.
