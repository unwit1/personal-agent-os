(Rolly-Polly Ja'bal was beheaded, and they displayed his head in front of the temple of Anequina until the moons turned three times. When the clerk named Ja'i stepped before Ja'bal's head, Ja'bal spoke with a mysterious look on his face. This is the story he told during the second lunar cycle.)

"There are male and female chickens.
Two types of chickens: ones that lay eggs and ones that do not.
If you must decide which chicken to strangle, you usually strangle the male one.

There are male and female chicks, too.
Two types of chicks: ones that can lay eggs and ones that cannot.
If you must decide which chick to crush, you usually crush the male one.

There are even male and female eggs.
Two kinds of eggs: ones that will become female and ones that will become male.
But they are both round and roll around, so you cannot tell the difference.

Strangling chickens is cruel, crushing chicks is only slightly cruel, and breaking eggs is as nothing. We cannot change this anymore. That is why the cats no longer strangle the chickens but crush chicks instead. Truthfully, the cats want to break the eggs and yet they cannot, for the chicks break their own eggshells. And so we have no choice, absolutely no other choice but to crush the chicks."

Clerk Ja'i said to Ja'bal: "What is that supposed to mean? There is a limit to everything..."

Hearing this, Ja'bal laughed and laughed.

"So chickens, chicks, and eggs have a limit, too. Perhaps it is like the blue sky, too low for the cat to leap up to, and like a bottomless pit, too shallow for the cat to dive into."
