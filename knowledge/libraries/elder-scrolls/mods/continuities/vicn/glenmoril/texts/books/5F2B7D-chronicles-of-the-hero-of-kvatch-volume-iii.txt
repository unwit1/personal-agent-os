Preface
In short, the times simply did not allow it.

The two met in the year 433 of the Third Era, when the world was in the midst of the Oblivion Crisis. Mazoga, a bandit, changed her ways after the death of her best friend and began to walk the path of a knight. Since then, she has torn apart many bandits and thrown them aside like refuse to fertilize the forests of Leyawiin. There was no way she could lose against the Hero of Kvatch. Of course, she had the spirit to rub the Hero of Kvatch's banana. This book focuses on the "why" part and is a documentary presented by the testimony of those involved, collected by me, an Adoring Fan, who was the official attendant of the Hero of Kvatch at the time. Through interviews with Count Marius Caro, Weebam-Na, the Knight of Thorns Farwil, and others, we reconstruct a vivid picture of those days. The details will be spilled of the late-night wrestling tournaments at the White Stallion Lodge, where Mazoga made full use of grappling techniques that were said to be sensual, and the heated debates that took place in the Black Horse Courier. These accounts convey the author's affection for the Hero of Kvatch and his expectations for the heroic tale of Kvatch that is about to blossom. This is a book that the author has put all his effort into writing, not about "why they didn't kill each other," but about "why fate saw fit to let these two souls walk the world at the same time.
