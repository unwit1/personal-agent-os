Words of Jhunal

</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">Soul to body, heart to words, myth to wheel. The power of storytelling connects everything that can be told. These are the words, the logic, that spin the world.

It is not the branches and leaves of a tree, nor is it the roots. It is neither the One nor the Many. Not a fixed point but the speed at which a line is drawn. There are no correct thoughts, merely one short thought. That is what makes a map.

Love, like the wasp and the orchid, like Khajiit and Imga, is the word that lets the pink panther run wild around the world.</p>
