(Rolly-Polly Ja'bal was beheaded, and they displayed his head in front of the temple of Anequina until the moons turned three times. When the clerk named Ja'i stepped before Ja'bal's head, Ja'bal spoke with a mysterious look on his face. This is the story he told during the first lunar cycle.)

"Once upon a time, when Ja'bal was still an old kitten,
Strangling chickens, crushing chicks, and breaking eggs meant nothing to him.
It was all as nothing.

Many years later, when Ja'bal became a young cat,
Strangling chickens became slightly cruel.
Crushing chicks and breaking eggs was still as nothing to him.

It was yesterday when Ja'bal was beheaded,
And strangling chickens became cruel, and crushing chicks turned slightly cruel.
But breaking eggs remained as nothing to him.
 
Today, the birds will feast on Ja'bal's corpse,
And there will be no more chickens, no more chicks, and no more eggs.
Nothing is needed anymore, not chickens, nor chicks nor eggs.

Then the old kitten will be born again,
And all that he gained will be lost and gone.
All will be as nothing once more."

Clerk Ja'i turned pale when he heard this. He quickly chased away the birds flocking to Ja'bal's corpse and ordered the guards to throw the corpse into the deepest, darkest rift of Anequina. Then he turned to Ja'bal and said: "Now your conscience will not starve to death and neither will it wither to nothing."

Hearing this, Ja'bal laughed and laughed.
 
"It all ended the moment Ja'bal was beheaded. His conscience no longer bears fruit and there is no one left to eat the fruit of freedom."
