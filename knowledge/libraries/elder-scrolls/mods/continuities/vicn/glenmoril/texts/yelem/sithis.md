# Yelem's Notes — Sithis series

**Record family:** `zzzRevYelemNoteSithis*`  
**Source:** user-supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

This family contains some of Yelem's densest metaphysical writing.

## Indexed subjects

Recurring subjects include:
- cosmic orphanhood;
- the Void;
- black and white souls;
- the Spring;
- identity and sensation;
- harmony;
- gods and the conditions under which divinity is perceived or constructed.

## Interpretation boundary

The family is evidence for **Yelem's model** of Sithis, souls, identity and the Spring. It is not treated as an authoritative explanation of official TES cosmology.
