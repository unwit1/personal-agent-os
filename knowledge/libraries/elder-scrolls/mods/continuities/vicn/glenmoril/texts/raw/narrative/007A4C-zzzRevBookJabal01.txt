This is one of the nursery rhymes of Anequina, Elsweyr. 
 
Rolly-Polly, Ja'bal the Cat 
Kitten born of the red moon 
He is old since birth 
Rolly-Polly, he laughs and laughs 
 
Rolly-Polly, Ja'bal the Cat 
The red Stone in the rift of Anequina 
He is rejuvenated with every moonlit night 
Rolly-Polly, he laughs and laughs 
 
Rolly-Polly, Ja'bal the Cat 
The more he jumps, the more he is chased 
Even when the king of Anequina beheads him 
Rolly-Polly, he laughs and laughs
