<p align="center">

It matters not how much the God may dream of a sleeping heart, this heart will never sleep.

</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
The sleepless heart is me. I, torn by the beating heart, am We. Love is the outcome of such thoughts and those who preceded them all. The sleepless heart returns with repeating fate. It is a false awakening, and yet it is also a dream without sleep. And each time the sleepless mind is divided by chance. This mysterious gamble promises victory to the Prisoner, but only if it is affirmed by the ceaseless blinking of the eye.
</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
Let the place you're heading to now be marked by Love, I and We. For though much blood may be spilled and many names may be lost, all shall be redeemed under the Blue Star.

May the sleepless mind think, may the unreturned heart keep beating, may the nameless heart love and feel peace. O Blue Star, undo all the wedges with your radiance.
</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="center">
Love is violence, hate is peace.
Love, I and We, as the sleepless heart does.
AE HERMA MORA ALTADOON PADHOME LKHAN AE AI
</p>
