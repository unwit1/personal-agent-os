The Secret Tale, Vol. 7: Hamah

Assistant: Professor, can I ask you about the Nord woman you captured in Skyrim? Hamah?

Professor Y: Did she look like a Nord to you? I saw traces of the now-extinct Snow Elves in her.

Assistant: Are you saying she's part Nord, part Snow Elf? That's ... horrible.

Professor Y: Her body may be that of a Nord, but her soul belongs to one of the subjects of an experiment I once conducted in Arkngthamz. She told me she hated me the moment she saw my face.

Assistant: Was that the experiment to investigate the cause of the Snow Elves' blindness? I remember it was stopped by the higher-ups and we were supposed to dispose of all the Snow Elves used in it.

Professor Y: Yes. But I heard that the Snow Elves were not incinerated ... instead, they were dumped into the sewers.

Assistant: Those apprentices would do anything to save some money, wouldn't they?

Professor Y: Be that as it may, the surviving Snow Elves were protected by Anem, a Nede sorcerer.

Assistant: Anem the Rebirther? I've only heard rumors of her, but they say she's skilled in the magic of body, mind, and soul.

Professor Y: Anem rebirthed Hamah as a Nord on the outside. She then traveled across Skyrim and was welcomed into Vahdin Holt at the end of her journey.

Assistant: Isn't that the forest where priestesses who worship Kyne and swear themselves to purity gather? I heard they worshipped some kind of strange stone there.

Professor Y: Hamah says she stole the stone and used it in some kind of ritual.

Assistant: She says she hates you and then she's so surprisingly honest with you. No matter. Where is the stone now?

Professor Y: No one lies when faced with pain. But she won't tell me where the stone is.

Assistant: So what are you going to do with her, then?

Professor Y: That stone doesn't interest me that much, and I've already heard all I needed to from her. Still, she is a rare thing ... I'll keep her as a specimen of ancient Nede magic.

Assistant: ... I don't understand your taste, professor.
