Pailune was an oasis city in the desert in the northeastern part of Anequina. Its only mention in the Chronicles of Anequina describes it as the birthplace of Ja'bal the contemptuous cat and the place where he was finally defeated. Although it was once a lonely frontier town, it developed rapidly and grew to be an enormous city after the establishment of the Yelem School, second only to the Imperial capital. However, during the Thrassian Plague epidemic, it was burned to the ground by the Alessians. It is said that Pailune was ruined within a single night.
