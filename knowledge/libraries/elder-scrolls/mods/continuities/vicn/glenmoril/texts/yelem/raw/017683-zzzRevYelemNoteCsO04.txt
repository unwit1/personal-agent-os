No mouth. No tongue. No teeth. No larynx. No esophagus. No belly. No anus. The automata stop dead and set free the unorganized mass they once served to articulate. The full body without organs is the unproductive, the sterile, the unengendered, the unconsumable. The death instinct: that is the name of the egg.
