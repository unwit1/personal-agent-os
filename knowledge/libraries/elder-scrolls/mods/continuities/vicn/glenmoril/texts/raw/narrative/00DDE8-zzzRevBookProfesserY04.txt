Vol. 4: The Endless Fall 

Assistant: "That man in black who asked us to investigate the great hole, the one calling himself Jhunal... Who in Oblivion is he?"

Professor Y: "Neither man nor mer. He's not even a mortal." 

Assistant: "There's a god with the same name in the Nord myths... Could he be an incarnation of that god?" 

Professor Y: "No, no. More likely he's just a lesser Daedra fooling around with disguises." 

Assistant: "Then why did you accept his request? Even if he's just a lesser Daedra, it's still not a good idea to tangle with them."

Professor Y: "Out of curiosity. I wanted to see what he was trying to show me. And it turned out to be far more than I expected." 

Assistant: "The remains of the creature the Reach natives call Laza... Yes, that was very interesting." 

Professor Y: "Did I say it was a corpse? That thing is not exactly dead. The problem is that we can only see it suspended in motion."

Assistant: "Does that mean it's acting very slowly, then?" 

Professor Y: "No, quite the opposite. He's still active, but at lightning speed... And he's dreaming." 

Assistant: "How is that even possible?" 

Professor Y: "It's possible. That's the most interesting part."
