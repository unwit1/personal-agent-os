The Secret Tale, Vol. 1: The Gray Fruit 

Professor Y: "What do you think of the dragon-man's corpse we excavated in Atmora?" 

Assistant: "All I can think of is that it's a spliced-up corpse."

Professor Y: "You are correct about the splicing. But if I told you that this was done before death and not after?"

Assistant: "How could anyone do such... such a horrible thing?"

Professor Y: "But I think it's wonderful. It means that long ago, there were people with similar goals to ours. We can learn a lot from their mistakes. The secrets of the dragons should be an excellent guide for us to explore the depths of the world." 

Assistant: "But who were the blasphemers who did this?"

Professor Y: "In Atmora there used to be a magical society called the Owls. That dragon-man's corpse could be the work of one of those Owls, Orlando the Gray to be more precise. The corpse fits what he was studying at the time." 

Assistant: "Orlando... Do you mean Orlando the Knowledgeable?"

Professor Y: "Well, it seems you already met him. When did that happen?"

Assistant: "When I was still a student. I was in a tavern, wondering which laboratory to join. He recommended me yours..." 

Professor Y: "I see. So, this is all going according to their plan. But don't be afraid. There will always be an opportunity to outsmart them."

Assistant: "When will that be?" 

Professor Y: "When the fruit ripens, the ones standing on the Blue Star will receive true freedom."
