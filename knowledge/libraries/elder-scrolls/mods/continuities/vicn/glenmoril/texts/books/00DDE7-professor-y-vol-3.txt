Vol. 3: The Great Cat 

Assistant: "Professor, what do you think about the Brass Giant theory proposed by the Kagrenac School? If we could realize that idea, we'd be eternal, wouldn't we?"

Professor Y: "Yes, it's possible. But our bodies could not bear the strain. What we should aim for instead is inner transformation. And that would bring physical change along with the mystical."

Assistant: "Do you mean... like with the Falmer?"

Professor Y: "Yes, indeed. Though it's not a miserable change like that. It will be a more sublime transformation through deeper insight and contemplation of the higher planes. In the end, the Dwemer as we are now will be extinct and a new race will be born. We are the nursery for that race to come." 

Assistant: "This idea is horrifying for me. I'd like to remain as I am now." 

Professor Y: "I quite understand your feelings. You can stay the same as long as you want to. But beyond that is nothing but darkness and stagnation."

Assistant: "You have changed, Professor. It's like you are a completely different person after investigating that great hole..."

Professor Y: "Correct. The transformation is already taking place."
