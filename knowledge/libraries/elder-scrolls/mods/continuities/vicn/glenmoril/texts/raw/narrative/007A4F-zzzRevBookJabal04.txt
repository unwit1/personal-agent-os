(Rolly-Polly Ja'bal was beheaded, and they displayed his head in front of the temple of Anequina until the moons turned three times. When the clerk named Ja'i stepped before Ja'bal's head, Ja'bal spoke with a mysterious look on his face. This is the story he told during the third lunar cycle.)

"There are male and female eggs, but no one knows which will become which. The only thing known about them is that they can become either. That is why the cat warms the eggs until they become chicks. When the chicks hatch, the cat sorts them based on whether they are female or not. But no matter how careful the cat, some males will inevitably get mixed with the females, and such chicks become female-like male chickens. Yet they cannot go against their own sex and one day, they all crow out all at once. Then the cat can no longer sleep and forgets how to even land. And the cat's soul starts seeking the ground in an endless fall."

Clerk Ja'i turned around and ordered the guards to kill all the kittens born in the three lunar cycles. Ja'i then said to Ja'bal: "Now the cat's soul can dream in the winds of the endless fall."

Hearing this, Ja'bal laughed and laughed.

"All is well with the world, all is well with the world, all is well with the world. A female-like male chicken is already crowing and loudly disturbing the sleep of all cats."
