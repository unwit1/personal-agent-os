<p align="center">

There is no beating heart, but there is a time marked by heartbeat.

</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
The heartbeat marks the time. It now passes and is shared by all life like a giant cone. It's not mine, it's not ours, but it's Love's. All life repeats itself there in all its dimensions. This is fate. You will know this there. You will know what your tone is. But still the song of life echoing in the darkness remains the same, echoing in equal measure.
</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
Let the song of life now show you what he's up to. For though much blood may be spilled and many names may be lost, all shall be redeemed under the Blue Star.

Time and time again, sound and sound, word and word, there was one who always hummed between the repetitions. No one will remember that, but it will shake the wedges that pierce the gods.
</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="center">
Love is violence, hate is peace.
The song of life keeps on echoing.
AE HERMA MORA ALTADOON PADHOME LKHAN AE AI
</p>
