The Secret Tale, Vol. 6: Black Waters

Assistant: That black water that sprang up in the forest ... Is this the plague of Laza that got passed onto the northern nomads?

Professor Y: It must be. If the myths are true, then this forest is the Garden of Kyne.

Assistant: But how could have Laza, just an ordinary shepherd, done all this?

Professor Y: He made it possible. The Owls, or the forces of Apocrypha, are forbidden from doing anything themselves, but it's not difficult to tamper with mortals. Laza must have been a medium for time-related magic.

Assistant: If we base this knowledge on myths, does this black water come from vampire blood, then?

Professor Y: That water eats away at the souls of any who touch it, and devours their time. Even if one were to use the curse of Molag Bal, it would be impossible to recreate this.

Assistant: The mystery deepens. Where did it come from, then? And can we simply leave it like that?

Professor Y: This black water is inert. As long as it takes longer to consume materia than it would have usually, it does no significant harm. But we can see what happens if one comes in contact with it when it's in an active state - just observe that dragon. In my opinion, this water is Alduin's blood ... or Alkhan's. One of his forms.

Assistant: Are you saying Alduin shared his blood with Laza?

Professor Y: No, of course not. Laza must have simply opened the way to Alduin. Judging by the state of Laza's body and the great hole in Hinnom, a deep wound was made in space and time. Alduin's blood must have flowed through it. However, Alduin himself was not able to come through. Whatever the time and place, he must have been wounded so deeply that he could no longer respond.

Assistant: I don't think anything could have possibly wounded Alduin that deeply.

Professor Y: This is beyond our knowledge. However, there may be someone so powerful in the future that they'll be able to defeat even Alduin. And as such, this event will be sung about and repeated in stories.

Assistant: If so ... this is the confluence the forces of Apocrypha are aiming for.
