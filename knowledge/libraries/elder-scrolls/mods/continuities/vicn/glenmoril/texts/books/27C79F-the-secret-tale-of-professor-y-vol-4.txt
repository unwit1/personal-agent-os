The Secret Tale, Vol. 4: The Statue of Azura

Assistant: That's a big statue of Azura. Chimer made, or is it Dunmer now? Why would they put so much effort into something so useless?

Professor Y: There's no need to waste our time laughing at them. We simply went our different paths.

Assistant: That is true, I suppose. That said, can we conclude this place was pulled from Resdayn?

Professor Y: No, the soil around the statue was from Skyrim, not Resdayn. There was a pale corpse nearby. What did you find out?

Assistant: It was a Dunmer woman. She had a diary, but it was too weathered and nearly unreadable. I can only make out the date and even that barely.

Professor Y: The Fourth Era ... this is a good sign. It's almost here now.

Assistant: Fishing for leftovers doesn't sound right, especially only on the basis of the myth about Alduin the World Eater. Can we truly expect that to happen?

Professor Y: Time here is not linear. Sometimes we get closer, sometimes we move away again. Nothing is certain.

Assistant: Professor, I'm worried. Can we really leave this place?

Professor Y: Be strong. There is no fixed fate in this place. This is why that Daedra gambles on it. We must do so, as well.

Assistant: What are you staking your bet on?

Professor Y: Lorkhan's shattered dream. The whole reason for mortality. A mortal becoming Godhead of their own.
