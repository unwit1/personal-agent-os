Vol. 2: A Dream Without a Dreamer 

Professor Y: "What was it that the Falmer race lost?"

Assistant: "Their sight and intelligence?"

Professor Y: "Correct. But they lost something more important still. Do you know what that was?"

Assistant: "Was it the color of their souls?"

Professor Y: "Correct. And therefore they turned into beasts. But that is still not the essence of what they lost." 

Assistant: "Was it that Tower advocated by the Kagrenac School? But isn't the Tower just a myth?" 

Professor Y: "That is correct. It is a myth. Therefore, their souls changing color was a mythical change, and they lost their eyes as a physical change."

Assistant: "What do you mean?"

Professor Y: "Perception defines reality. They lost the most primitive way of perceiving the world, the myth." 

Assistant: "I don't understand why the mutation would occur just because they couldn't perceive reality..." 

Professor Y: "If it cannot be perceived, reality does not exist. It only exists as long as one can perceive it." 

Assistant: "Are you saying that everything is a hallucination constricted by perception?" 

Professor Y: "I am, indeed. I like to call this a dream."

Assistant: "If this is true, then it's a nightmare, Professor."
