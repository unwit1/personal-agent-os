# Quest objective pass 03 — Ebonwall and Tainted Orphan

This pass upgrades the Act 2 graph using the actual `NNAM` objective text embedded in the supplied ESM's QUST records.

## Ebonwall

`zzzGHMq02` directly establishes an airship-based mission with Ozwald and FL-4N, a watchtower phase, a Gulakhan encounter, an optional assassination of volunteer-army commander Magni, a Thalmor commander target, a graveyard phase, an Ashkhan encounter, and a return to the airship. Tabiah has an optional kill objective and remains referenced in aftermath dialogue. A recovered heart stone is also referenced after the mission.

## Tainted Orphan

`zzzGHMq03` directly establishes a mission beginning with FL-4N and Ja'zel, followed by an airship briefing, travel to a village and the Cradle Woods, repeated Botvid encounters, entry into a cave, discovery of the Tainted Orphan, and battles involving Botvid, Artur, and a witch before reporting completion to Ozwald.

## Graph consequences

The Act 2 backbone can now be represented as:

**The Blue Bird** → **Ebonwall** → **Tainted Orphan**

with optional/subquest material branching around the latter missions as defined by the debug-book act index. This ordering is supported jointly by the internal debug book and direct QUST records.
