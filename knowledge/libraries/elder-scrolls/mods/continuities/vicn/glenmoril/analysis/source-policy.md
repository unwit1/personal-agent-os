# GLENMORIL source policy

## Evidence tiers

1. **Primary plugin evidence** — records parsed directly from the supplied `Glenmoril.esm`. This is authoritative for what this imported build contains.
2. **Vicn first-party material** — useful for version/build context and author-provided framing.
3. **Translation/community documentation** — useful for navigation, translation context, and hypotheses, but must not silently replace plugin evidence.
4. **Interpretation** — thematic or continuity synthesis; always label as interpretation and retain supporting evidence.

## Cross-continuity policy

GLENMORIL belongs to the parent `tes.mod.vicn` continuity alongside VIGILANT, UNSLAAD, and DAc0da.

A cross-mod relationship should record:

- originating work;
- source record or textual evidence where possible;
- whether the relationship is explicit, strongly implied, or interpretive;
- whether an apparently shared entity is literally the same entity or merely a reused name/archetype.

Official Elder Scrolls material remains a separate evidence layer. Vicn's reinterpretations do not overwrite Bethesda/ZeniMax canon in the base library.
