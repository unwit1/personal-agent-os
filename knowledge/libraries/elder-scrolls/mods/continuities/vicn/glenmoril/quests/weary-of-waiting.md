## Plot

Brandt is waiting for a peddler named **Radcliffe**, who is overdue.

He recruits the player to look for him.

The search finds:
- a damaged carriage;
- dried meat barrels scratched by claws;
- a crate full of low-quality iron daggers;
- a badly slashed horse;
- large blood pools;
- drag marks;
- Radcliffe dead.

## Cause assessment

Brandt concludes the scene is unlikely to be ordinary banditry because:
- the gold was left untouched;
- the horse was torn open;
- claw marks are present.

He considers a beast or **ghoul** more likely.

Asked what a ghoul is, Brandt says a priest described them as some form of **Namira worshippers**, though he admits he does not remember the details.

## Radcliffe

Brandt knew Radcliffe personally enough to:
- complain that he repeatedly warned the peddler not to skimp on escorts;
- react with frustration/sadness at arriving too late;
- say he now has to clean the site, complete paperwork, and write a letter to Radcliffe's family.

## Iron-dagger precursor

Brandt finds a crate full of poor-quality **iron daggers** and says Radcliffe has once again bought a haul of trash.

This predates the later **A Thousand Daggers / Operation Iron Dagger** material and is retained as a possible early distribution clue, not a proven direct link.
