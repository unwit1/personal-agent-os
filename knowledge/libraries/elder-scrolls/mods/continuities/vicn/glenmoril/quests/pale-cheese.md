## Double Sheogorath / Skooma Cat

The opening deliberately presents Sheogorath and Skooma Cat as parallel versions of the same identity.

They repeatedly call one another another “me,” while joking about which self originated the current scheme.

This is direct source identity-play, not enough by itself to establish a single metaphysical ontology for every Skooma-Cat appearance.

## The old squire

Sheogorath says that before becoming his current self he had:
- a follower/squire;
- an onion-like appearance;
- a plan that ended with the follower sealed inside a cheese barrel.

He says roughly four hundred years passed before he remembered the follower.

The quest's “cheese” target is structurally associated with **St. Bazura**.

## Name and shape

Sheogorath says:
- forgetting one's name can make a mortal lose their shape;
- unnamed things can grow horns/tails;
- before Y'ffre gave things names, everything was a formless “Ooze.”

He jokes that if Y'ffre had named everything cheese, Tamriel would belong to “Cheesemer.”

## Quantum cheese

Sheogorath calls the matured result a cheese whose:
- potential has matured;
- secret ingredient is “the essence of what I was before I became me”;
- recipe includes a little blessing from Arkay;
- possibilities ferment through repeated reincarnation.

He calls the result **quantum cheese**.

## Ending branches

The player may:
- eat the cheese;
- or release/return it to the sea.

Sheogorath and Skooma Cat treat both outcomes as comic experiments.

## Evidence discipline

The quest intentionally mixes metaphysical language with absurd Sheogorath comedy.

The name/shape statements are direct dialogue and retrieval-significant, but should not automatically be generalized into universal Elder Scrolls metaphysics without corroboration.
