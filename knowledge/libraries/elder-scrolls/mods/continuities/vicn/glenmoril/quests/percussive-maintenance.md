**Title:** **Percussive Maintenance**

This side quest centers on Lalanoah's broken **music box**.

## Direct plot

Brandt tries to fix Lalanoah's treasured music box by hitting it and breaks it.

Lalanoah locks herself in her room, refuses food, and later runs away after writing a note.

The player takes the music box to Romion.

Romion identifies it as a rare product of the **Yelem School**, repairs its bent frame, missing screws, and dried lubricant, and returns it.

Kalka finds/listens to Lalanoah while she is away. Lalanoah regrets what she said and fears she will be alone again.

Kalka repeatedly addresses Lalanoah as **“Ms. Hamah.”**

Lalanoah ultimately returns home and reconciles with the household.

## Romion background

The quest also contains Romion's direct personal history with the Eton Nir Research Institute and Ozwald:
- Eton Nir member >50 years earlier;
- departure after Thalmor militarization;
- later Thalmor imprisonment in Hammerfell;
- rescue by Ozwald;
- subsequent Witch Hunter work.
