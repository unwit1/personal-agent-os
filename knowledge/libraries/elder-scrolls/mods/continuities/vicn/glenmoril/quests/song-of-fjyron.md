## Sister Gaga

The moth-form character is structurally named **Sister Gaga**.

She says:
- she spent her earlier life in a monastery;
- she was guided through the **Abyss of Marukh** by a sphinx moth given by Emperor Belharza;
- she then found herself in this land in the form of an **ancestor moth**.

She does not understand exactly how the transformation occurred.

## Belharza's secret gift

Sister Gaga says:
- **Emperor Belharza** entrusted her with a gift for an old friend;
- the gift was meant to remain secret;
- a strange creature stole it;
- she later recovers it.

The source does not name Belharza's intended old friend.

## Fjyrons

Sister Gaga describes a **fjyron** as:
- an echo of the soul;
- sometimes called the “will to peace”;
- a place/state where experiences across time and space accumulate.

In ancestor-moth form, she says she can hear fjyrons clearly as songs.

## The child / woven song

She gathers fjyrons and:
- spins their songs into thread;
- knits a swaddling cloth;
- wraps a “child” in the songs of spirits.

She predicts:
- the child will grow into a good advisor;
- the child's **words** will guide Emperor Belharza's friend;
- those words will push the friend forward like Kyne's breeze.

She asks the player to deliver/send the child from an altar in the northern mountains.

## Daughter of Kyne lead

When asked about a maiden of Kyne, Sister Gaga consults the surrounding spirits and says a matching woman is being held in the north tower.

## Apostle of Landfall

The Apostle of Landfall:
- demands the cocoon;
- recognizes the smell of ancestor moths;
- attacks rather than surrender the pursuit.

## Evidence boundary
