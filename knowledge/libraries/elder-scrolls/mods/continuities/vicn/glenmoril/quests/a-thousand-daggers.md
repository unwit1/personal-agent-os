## Direct plot

Brandt takes a bounty on a mysterious smith responsible for flooding the market with poor-quality **iron daggers**.

The investigation reaches a hidden workshop in a cave Brandt knew as a child.

A “Mysterious Voice” is overheard reporting to a superior that:
- “it” is mixed into shipments of iron daggers;
- tired guards do not inspect the shipments closely;
- the blacksmiths' bounty is becoming a problem;
- the caller closes with **“Glory to the Thalmor!”**

The bounty target resolves as **Kodaavkiin**.

After defeat, Kodaavkiin detonates/destroys the workshop rather than surrender. Brandt and the player survive, but the bounty's head/prize is lost.

## Lalanoah / Brandt

Brandt wants the bounty money for Lalanoah's education.

He considers sending her to a good magic school in the Imperial City because he believes she has talent for healing.

Lalanoah instead says she can eventually go to the **Temple of Kynareth**, which is closer to Falkreath and everyone she loves.

A scene also directly recalls Brandt playing in this cave as a child with **Gerhard and Laelnoah**, strengthening their shared childhood/friend-group chronology.
