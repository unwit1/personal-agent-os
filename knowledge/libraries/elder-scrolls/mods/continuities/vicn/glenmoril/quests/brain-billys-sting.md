- he frequently marks ideas as guesses, theories, or incomplete;
- later reports revise his earlier assumptions;
- some claims are direct Psijic observations, while others are speculation.

The Augur of the Obscure is even less suitable as an objective narrator:
- Rolls explicitly tells the player not to take everything the Augur says at face value;
- Augur dialogue intentionally mixes useful higher-perspective statements with absurd jokes.

Therefore every high-value claim should retain speaker attribution and confidence.
