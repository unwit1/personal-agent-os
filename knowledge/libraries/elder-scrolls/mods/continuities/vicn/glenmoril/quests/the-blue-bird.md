## Direct family investigation

Lalanoah says **Jhunal the White is her mother's pet owl**. She says:
- Jhunal is very wise;
- he often brings back lost or forgotten things;
- Laelnoah used to speak to him, although Jhunal only nodded.

Brandt independently says the owl is **Laelnoah's pet** and initially hopes its return carries a message from her.

Brandt asks the player to find Lalanoah's father, **Gerhard**. Quartermaster Agrippa says Gerhard's unit was sent from Fort Sungard to Bilegulch Mine and officially classified as deserters.

Gerhard is found mortally wounded after an attack by a **giant blue bird**. He asks that his sword and a purchased hair ornament be taken back to Falkreath for his wife/daughter. The player can later give the ornament to Lalanoah and either conceal or disclose Gerhard's death to Brandt.

## Felix

The mutated Felix recognizes Ozwald and says he has remembered a sister:
> “A child who smiles like the sun and loves white flowers... Or am I remembering it wrong?”

Ozwald later explicitly says **Felix had no sister**.

Store this as an unresolved memory anomaly rather than inventing a literal sister.

## Curse / player recruitment

After Felix is defeated, Romion says the player's condition is unusually stable:
- no mutation yet;
- something appears to be **restraining the curse**.

Ozwald offers the player a coercive choice:
- join the witch hunt and try to break the curse;
- or die.

He says the elder witches of Glenmoril created the curse affecting both him and the player and that even **Hinnom** is affected.

Ozwald says elder witches can be identified by a **Red Drop**, which Hinnom's wet nurses describe as a catalyst needed to break the curse.

## Black / White Owl warnings

Jhunal the White:
- tells the player not to search for Lalanoah's father;
- predicts her parents will be dead by the time they are found;
- says breaking the curse requires finding the **untainted daughter of Kyne**;
- warns that “the louse is singing and the Black Owl found you.”

Jhunal the Black appears around the sacrament/chalice sequence and tells the player that what they have been searching for is inside the chalice.

Five Black-Owl actors also deliver the numbered “blind your eye” / wonderful-night sequence.

## Chick Trader

Ja'zel:
- appears near the Gerhard/ghoul area;
- says he is looking for a **kitten**, explicitly distinguishing kittens from chicks;
- treats Lalanoah as “just one chick” when the player orders him to stay away from her.

Agrippa says Ja'zel previously investigated the same missing Imperial units around Fort Sungard.

## Romion / Yelem School

Romion says he studies surviving **Yelem School** technology and reproduces it.

He describes the school as founded by **Yelem of Arkngthamz and his blacksmiths**, considered heretical even by other Dwemer, and summarizes their engineering preference as favoring **explosions over steam**.
