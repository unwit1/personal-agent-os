## Directly supported narrative evidence

The quest's DIAL records contain branches explicitly named for Lalanoah, Brandt, the Owl, Agrippa, Ja'zel, Gerhard, Romion, and Ozwald.

The player can investigate a white owl with Lalanoah and Brandt. The quest then contains explicit dialogue topics concerning Lalanoah's parents and a search for **Gerhard's unit**.

The NPC inventory identifies `04009BB3` / `zzzGHLalanoahFather` as **Gerhard**, directly supporting Gerhard as Lalanoah's father.

Dialogue with Legion Quartermaster Agrippa includes inquiries about Gerhard's unit, where it was dispatched, its disappearance, and whether similar disappearances occurred before.

Later player topics explicitly allow the player to report to Brandt that **Gerhard is dead** and that **ghouls killed him**, including an option to hand over Gerhard's sword. A Lalanoah branch allows the player to give her a hair ornament described as a gift from Gerhard; alternate topics allow the player to lie to her about Gerhard's condition.

## Witch-hunt transition

Ja'zel's branch contains player responses warning him away from Lalanoah. A quest-specific book, `0406EDB1` / `zzzGHMq01Blackmail`, is titled **Threatening Letter from Ja'zel**. Its text says Ja'zel wants the player to participate in a witch hunt and threatens consequences against named captives if the player refuses.

Ozwald's dialogue includes an explicit player acceptance: “Fine, I'll join the witch hunt.” His branch also asks about witch-hunt equipment, witches, **Red Drop**, and **Hinnom**.

This makes **The Blue Bird** a directly evidenced bridge between the Lalanoah/Gerhard family investigation and the Act 2 witch-hunt framework.
