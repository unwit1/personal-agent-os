- she became separated from her friends;
- she came all this way to escape;
- she does not want to be alone;
- she wants to play with the others again.

She says:
> “We used to have two legs like you, but now we don't.”

She also says:
> Lalanoah is not here because she **missed the Chick Trader's carriage**, so she probably never arrived in this place.

## The others
