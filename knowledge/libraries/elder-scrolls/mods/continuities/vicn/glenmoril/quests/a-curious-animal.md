## Plot

Lalanoah finds an injured animal in the forest while walking with Skoll.

She brings it home and wants to heal and care for it.

Groll initially treats it as meat; Lalanoah refuses and insists they help it.

The creature is named **Kanra** in the quest structure.

## Lalanoah / healing

Lalanoah says:
- her mother taught her healing magic;
- she only knows a simple spell so far;
- when she grows up she wants to study at the **Temple of Kynareth** and become a healer.

This is the earliest direct source for Lalanoah's healing vocation and Laelnoah's role in teaching it.

## Kanra

The player can argue over whether Kanra is:
- a raccoon;
- a fox;
- simply “Kanra.”

Lalanoah found Kanra lying injured in the forest and decides to take responsibility for him if he has no home.
