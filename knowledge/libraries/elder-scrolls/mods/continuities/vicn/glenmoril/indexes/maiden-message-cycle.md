# Maiden message cycle

Source: GLENMORIL MESG records.

The plugin contains two parallel five-member sets centered on:
**Anem, Zuzu, Hela, Aradia, Hamah**.

## Early invocation set

### Anem — daughter of mercy
Asked to heal the lowly on mountain paths so they are not deceived by the beast.

### Zuzu — daughter of silence
Asked to hide secrets so people do not find the beast.

### Hela — daughter of the spinner
Entrusted with a **drop taken from the beast**, to hide it in the forest.

### Aradia — daughter of the sleepless
Asked to watch over a dragon's fire sealing a hole.

### Hamah — daughter of song
Asked to drown out the beast's whispers so it cannot speak through dreams.

## Later revision dossiers

### Anem
Described as offering flesh, heart, and soul:
- soul carried by wind;
- heart sinking into deep sea;
- warm flesh/blood remaining.

### Aradia
Linked to:
- a golden dragon sleeping in Atmora;
- Hinnom;
- Namira;
- hiding the Radiance;
- the sleepless-heart formulation.

### Hela
Linked to:
- hearing the Beast;
- moonlit song/dance;
- the Tree Spirit;
- loss of sleep;
- tranquility at Kyne's Spring.

### Zuzu
Described as:
- Witch of Wire and Strings;
- traveling backward in time;
- giving eyes to the giant's daughter;
- becoming Vaermina-guided through nightmare cliffs;
- whispering from the dream world.

### Hamah
Described as:
- inhabiting a body Anem created;
- resisting rot;
- carrying unusually heavy blood;
- possessing corrupted soul but untainted flesh/blood.

## Structural significance

The early set frames the five as **functional guardians/daughters** around one “beast” problem:
- heal;
- conceal;
- hide the Drop;
- seal the hole;
- suppress dream-whispers.

The later set rewrites each figure into a denser revision-layer biography.

## Guardrail

Do not assume:
- one narrator authored both sets;
- “the beast” is identical in every line;
- the early titles “daughter of …” indicate biological descent;
- every later dossier is neutral history rather than symbolic/revision data.

Preserve the two sets as parallel source layers.
