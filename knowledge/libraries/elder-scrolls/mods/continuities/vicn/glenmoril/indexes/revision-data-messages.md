## Use

These are compact revision-layer data statements.

They are valuable for cross-retrieval, but should not be silently promoted over:
- character dialogue;
- books;
- scene chronology;
- contradictory revision records.

Use them as **high-density ontology clues with explicit MESG provenance**.
