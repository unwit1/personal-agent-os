## Major thematic progression

### Observation / reality
Vols. 1–2 establish Professor Y's working model:
- superposition and branching outcomes;
- perception defines experienced reality;
- myth functions as a primitive reality-defining perception layer.

### Transformation
Vol. 3 argues for internal/mythic transformation producing physical transformation.

The assistant notices Professor Y has changed after investigating the great hole.

### Jhunal / Laza
Vol. 4 says:
- a black-clad being calling himself Jhunal directed Professor Y to Laza;
- Professor Y rejects a straightforward divine-Jhunal identification;
- Laza appears suspended but is claimed to be active at extreme speed and dreaming.

### Ja'bal
Vol. 5 and Secret Tales 2–3 propose:
- Ja'bal's head evades unwanted futures by producing localized universes;
- a giant sphere is a **living ship**;
- Ja'bal is better modeled as a mental entity than one body;
- the ship has Argonian/Hist-like anatomy;
- an Argonian/Hist mind, a transcribed Dunmer personality, and a white root/worm entity appear entangled in its history.

Professor Y explicitly warns that “Ja'bal,” “Great Cat,” and “Rolly-Polly” may simply be interpretive names applied to incomprehensible events.

### Orlando / Owls
Secret Tale 1 says:
- an Atmoran dragon-man corpse was spliced before death;
- Professor Y attributes it to the magical society of Owls;
- he names **Orlando the Gray / Orlando the Knowledgeable** as a possible responsible researcher;
- the assistant says Orlando directed them toward Professor Y's laboratory.

### Time / Fourth Era / mortality
Secret Tale 4 describes:
- non-linear time;
- Fourth-Era remains appearing in their environment;
- Professor Y gambling on **Lorkhan's shattered dream** and the possibility of a mortal becoming Godhead.

### Apocrypha interference / transference
Secret Tale 5 says:
- an intended transfer to Moonshadow goes wrong;
- black water floods the destination;
- Professor Y hears Apocrypha-associated tones;
- he describes millennia of repeated minimal interference creating a larger current that can no longer be corrected by small changes.

### Laza black water
Secret Tale 6 connects:
- Garden of Kyne;
- Laza;
- a space-time wound around Hinnom;
- black water that eats souls/time.

Professor Y speculates this may be Alduin/Alkhan blood. Preserve as hypothesis.

### Hamah
Secret Tale 7 gives the Professor Y account of:
**Snow Elf experiment → Anem protection/rebirth → outwardly Nord Hamah → Vahdin Holt → stolen ritual stone.**

Later Hamah testimony independently confirms the Snow-Elf/Anem-rebirth core.

### C0DA
The final overview says:
- a C0DA document was extracted from Apocrypha's “Mythos” with help from the First Servant;
- the author's visible name is perceptually concealed by mind-altering magic;
- the text is attributed to `[NUMINIT]`;
- the document describes Fifth-Era House Sul material;
- Yelem-Boltzmer claims it contains advanced theories shared with Dwemer/Psijic secret knowledge.

## Reliability model

Treat the family as a **research notebook/dialogue corpus**, not omniscient scripture.

Evidence classes:

**A — direct observation**
- physical corpse/ship/statue/soil/diary/remains actually encountered.

**B — internally corroborated account**
- assistant remembers prior experiments/events that Professor Y also references.

**C — Professor Y interpretation**
- Hist authorship, Alduin-blood theory, cosmological mechanisms.

**D — intentionally speculative/metafictional**
- future Godhead bets, C0DA-author identity, deep Apocrypha purpose.

Retrieval should surface the claim together with its evidence class.
