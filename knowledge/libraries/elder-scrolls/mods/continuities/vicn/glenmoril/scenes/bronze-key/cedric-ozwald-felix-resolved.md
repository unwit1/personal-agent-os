# The Bronze Key — Cedric, Ozwald, Felix, and the rookie

Scene: `Glenmoril.esm:0147FE zzzGHMq04ScCedric02`
Quest: `zzzGHMq04 The Bronze Key`
Rows: **11 exact**

Cedric confronts Ozwald about why he did not kill the new recruit/player.

Ozwald first says the recruit could be useful as a hunter. Cedric rejects that as the public explanation and asks for the real reason.

Ozwald then says:

- the recruit **inherited the curse from Felix**;
- Ozwald feels as though **Felix is still somehow alive in them**;
- because of that feeling, Ozwald could not kill the recruit;
- he wanted to keep the recruit nearby.

Cedric calls this insane and says Ozwald's decision attracted the Chick Trader's attention, resulting in unreasonable missions.

Cedric explicitly reminds Ozwald that **the rookie is not Felix and Felix is already dead**.

Ozwald answers that he knows.

## Significance

This is direct evidence for an important distinction:

- Felix is dead.
- The player/recruit is **not** Felix.
- Ozwald nevertheless perceives continuity because the recruit inherited Felix's curse.

Do not convert the relationship into reincarnation, possession, or literal identity without additional evidence.
