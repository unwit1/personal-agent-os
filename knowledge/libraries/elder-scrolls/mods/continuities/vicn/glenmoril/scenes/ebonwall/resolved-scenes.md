# Ebonwall — resolved scenes

Quest: `Glenmoril.esm:00E3EC zzzGHMq02`
SCEN response count: **60**

## Mission order

Ja'zel orders the group to Ebonwall, where Ashlanders have revolted under a Glenmoril witch.

Ozwald summarizes the objective: kill the witch and take her **Red Drop**.

## Battle plan

Romion says Nord retaliation is inevitable after Ebonwall citizens were killed.

The hunters plan to bypass the main Nord assault, land in the hills, and reach the witch before the volunteer army advances too far.

## Targets

### Gadaratto
Romion identifies Ashkhan Gadaratto as the Ashlander leader and dismisses rumors that he is a child of the Nerevarine as politically useful legend.

### Magni the Iron Fist
Romion identifies Magni as the Nord volunteer commander, reputed to have killed a giant and to take no prisoners.

### Tabiah
Romion identifies **Wise Woman Tabiah** as:
- the mission's Glenmoril-witch target;
- originally from Mournhold;
- once a lady of Almalexia;
- later a traveler who came into contact with Glenmoril.

### Mudspawn
Romion describes Mudspawn as similar to Solstheim ash spawn and says their bodies consist of mixed solids/ash/snow held together unnaturally.

## Thalmor / Eton Nir

FL-4N locates an Eton Nir communicator while Ozwald urges focus on the witch.

## Tabiah ending

FL-4N locates Tabiah beneath the graveyard/Boethiah-statue area.

Tabiah repeats:
> “No one will take these children away...”

Ozwald attributes her state to the **Heart Stone**, refuses to kill her, and removes the stone.
