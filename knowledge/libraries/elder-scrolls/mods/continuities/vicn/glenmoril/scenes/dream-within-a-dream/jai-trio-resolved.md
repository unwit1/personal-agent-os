# Dream Within a Dream — Ja'i trio

Quest: `Glenmoril.esm:00C946 zzzGHMq05Sub01`

Three one-line SCEN records use aliases `JaiTA01`, `JaiTA02`, and `JaiTA03`.

Those aliases share a `VTCK`/presentation anchor to `0400C9AF zzzGHM05Jai` — **Ja'i**, while each uses a different talking-actor location-ref type.

## `00C9AC zzzGHMq05ScJai01`

Ja'i says there was no choice and that he did not wish to do what he did.

## `00C9AD zzzGHMq05ScJai02`

Ja'i says that after everything he still has nothing, and that he only stacks corpses in the name of righteousness while calling it a good deed.

## `00C9AE zzzGHMq05ScJai03`

Ja'i insists it was the right thing to do, repeats that there was never a choice, invokes the gods in Aetherius, and says all is wonderful in the world.

## Resolution note

Unlike ordinary forced-reference aliases, these three aliases are presentation/talking-actor aliases. Their shared Ja'i anchor makes the intended speaker strong/high-confidence, while the exact placed talking activator differs by scene.
