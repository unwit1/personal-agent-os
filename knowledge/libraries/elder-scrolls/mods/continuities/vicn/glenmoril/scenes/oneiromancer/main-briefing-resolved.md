# Oneiromancer — main briefing

Quest: `Glenmoril.esm:01719C zzzGHMq05`

24 exact rows are resolved across two previously unnormalized scenes.

## `0171C6 zzzGHMq05Sc01` — 16 rows

Ozwald asks how they will enter **Quagmire**, which he understands as a nightmare plane.

Romion says they must pry open a gate from the base point connecting **Arkved** to Quagmire and physically enter through a transitional gate.

Romion says the gate itself is stable, but Quagmire's latent effect on intruders is dangerous. Citing material recovered from the **Thalmor**, he reports:
- physical changes;
- negative mental effects;
- use of Quagmire's effects to accelerate **beast possession**.

Ja'zel proposes using **tuning boxes**, which he calls dreaming machines, to see the dreams and stabilize their forms.

When Ozwald asks about side effects, Ja'zel proposes letting the **baby recovered in Cradle Woods** absorb/deal with them. Ozwald calls this miserable but necessary. Ja'zel says the child was born dead and argues that they must use every available resource to have a future.

## `017215 zzzGHMq05ScBrWitch` — 8 rows

Ozwald asks what is known about **Zuzu the Oneiromancer**.

Romion says old records describe her as:
- a very old witch;
- a **daughter of a Dumb Old Giant who was Ysgramor's counselor**;
- someone who begged the **Oracle Iridescent** for knowledge;
- someone who gained the power to enter and leave dreams.

Romion says almost no other descriptions or stories remain.

Ozwald proposes that the Oneiromancer deliberately purged information about herself before leaving Nirn, perhaps even from Apocrypha.

Romion suggests the **Green Tablet Steps of Jhunal** as another possible surviving source, though he says reaching and leaving such places would be impractical.

They decide it is more practical to find her directly in Quagmire.
