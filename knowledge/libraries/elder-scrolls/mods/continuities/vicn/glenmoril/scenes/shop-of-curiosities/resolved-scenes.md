## St. Jiub sword — `014C94 ScSword` — 10 rows

Treat the artifact provenance as Orlando's shop claim, not independently verified history.

## Laelnoah's staff — `014CCF ScRod` — 11 rows

Lalanoah recognizes the staff as one her **mother had**.

Brandt immediately says:
> “This is Laelnoah's staff.”

Orlando says he bought it for 10 gold from men in strange helmets near Helgen.

After Brandt pays for their destination, Orlando says:
- **Piper's Hill**;
- south of Bloodlet Throne;
- past the Jerall Mountains.

Brandt tells Orlando the staff belonged to **Lalanoah's mother**.

Orlando gives it back for free.

This scene directly establishes **Laelnoah = Lalanoah's mother**.
