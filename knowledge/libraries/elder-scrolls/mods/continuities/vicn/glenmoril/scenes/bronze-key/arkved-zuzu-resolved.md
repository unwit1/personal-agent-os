# The Bronze Key — Arkved and Zuzu

Quest: `Glenmoril.esm:0147BD zzzGHMq04`

## “Key” identification — `01484F zzzGHMq04ScTrgKey` — 6 rows

Romion explains that **“Bronze Key” is Eton Nir / Thalmor jargon for a person, not a literal key**.

He identifies the Bronze Key as **Arkved**, an Altmer mage. According to Romion:
- a Thalmor spy recovered Arkved's body from Cyrodiil in the Third Era;
- Eton Nir is using the body for research connected to **Vaermina**.

## Recovery chamber — `01498D zzzGHMq04ScArkved` — 10 rows

FL-4N identifies the preserved material in the container as Arkved because the container bears his name and matching biometric data.

Ozwald describes the visible remains as a **brain and spinal cord**.

The chamber also contains a giant female body.

Ja'zel identifies that body as **Zuzu, the giant maiden of Vaermina — the Oneiromancer**.

When Ozwald says she appears dead, Ja'zel says:
- the body is only a **shell**;
- her **soul resides in Quagmire**;
- from Quagmire she sends dreams that warn witches of threats;
- this explains why the witch hunters cannot locate the elder witches and their **Red Drop**.

Ja'zel orders the group to hunt the Oneiromancer and then bring him Arkved.

## Topic corroboration

Ja'zel later says the Bronze Key's body is **still connected to Quagmire** and that he wants it for future plans.

After the recovery, Ozwald says the **Oneiromancer attempted to seize the player's mind**. He believes a normal person's mind would have been destroyed and regards the player's survival as extraordinary; he also suspects Ja'zel knows why the player survived.

## Causal bridge

This quest directly creates the transition:

**Arkved's preserved body / Bronze Key → Quagmire connection → Zuzu shell discovered → Oneiromancer identified → next Quagmire operation.**
