# Or the Gospel — Heart Stone

Scene: `Glenmoril.esm:017818 zzzGHMq06ScpHeartStone`

Seven exact rows resolve between **Ozwald** and **Ja'zel**.

Ja'zel says the Heart Stone from **Ebonwall** contains the souls and grudges of Ebonwall's people and will break the barrier and rattle the witch. When Ozwald questions the convenience, Ja'zel says he arranged to have the stone by **letting the Thalmor know about Ebonwall**.

Ozwald explicitly interprets this as Ja'zel orchestrating the Ebonwall massacre and confronts him over uninvolved deaths. Ja'zel does not deny the information leak and responds that fewer involved people are better.

## Evidence boundary

The leak to the Thalmor is Ja'zel's direct admission. “Orchestrated the massacre” is Ozwald's accusation/interpretation and should remain attributed as such.
