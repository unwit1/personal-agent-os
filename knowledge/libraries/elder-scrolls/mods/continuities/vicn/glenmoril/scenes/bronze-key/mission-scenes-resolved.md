# The Bronze Key — resolved mission scenes

Quest: `Glenmoril.esm:0147BD zzzGHMq04` — **The Bronze Key**

The quest contains 71 exact SCEN dialogue responses across nine dialogue-bearing scenes.

## Briefing — `01481A zzzGHMq04ScBriefing` — 10 rows

Romion and Ozwald discuss **Ysmir's Howl**, formerly Solitude territory and later under Thalmor control.

Romion says:
- the Thalmor originally intended to convert the local Talos worshippers gradually under Justiciar Varano;
- Varano died during an epidemic two years earlier;
- **Eton Nir Research Institute** then took control of the Temple City;
- Eton Nir's head researcher reported that the entire population died in the epidemic;
- Solitude repeatedly sent scouts, all of whom disappeared;
- something similar had happened when non-elven settlements disappeared in Alinor.

Ozwald explicitly links the simultaneous epidemic, missing scouts, and rising **beast possession** as suspicious.

## Alfilion — `01484E zzzGHMq04ScTrgThalmor` — 8 rows

Romion identifies **Chief Justiciar Alfilion** as chief of Eton Nir's Skyrim branch.

His attributed dossier says Alfilion:
- came through Alinor Youth and an assassination unit;
- participated in the siege of Cloud Ruler Temple;
- retired after being badly wounded by one of the last Blades;
- regained his former fighting ability through Dwemer technology;
- makes masks from the skulls of victims.

Romion warns that Alfilion remains a dangerous master assassin.

## First-Council automatons — `014850 zzzGHMq04ScTrgDwe` — 7 rows

Romion identifies unusual automatons as likely dating to the **Resdayn Union / First Council** period and showing Chimeri cultural influence.

He says they were powerful, highly durable, difficult to maintain, extensively used in the War of the First Council, and prestigious enough that owning one became a status symbol.

## Skeeven — `014851 zzzGHMq04ScTrgRat` — 8 rows

Romion identifies the bipedal rat-creatures as **Skeeven**:
- originally Rieklings from Solstheim;
- mutated by the Thalmor using Skeever blood;
- an early beast-possession experiment;
- intelligent enough to use weapons but not communicate;
- uncontrollable and discarded in the sewer;
- highly fertile.

## Chaplain — `0148D0 zzzGHMq04ScChaplain` — 2 rows

The Chaplain's Louse again addresses the player as a **tainted soul**, speaks of a dreamer and a wingless bird, calls everyone **suffering lice**, and invokes **Kyne's Feast of Love**.

## Cedric mission handoff — `0147D3 zzzGHMq04ScCedric01` — 9 rows

Cedric says he killed the **Beast of Haafingar**, which had been murdering Talos worshippers, and discovered Thalmor involvement in deploying monsters.

He then says he came on behalf of the **Chick Trader**, who orders Ozwald's group to infiltrate a Thalmor facility at Ysmir's Howl and steal a “key.”
