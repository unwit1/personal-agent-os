# Torvesard — maligraphy instance

Quest: **Ghost in the Mirror**
Source actor: `04474EC3 zzzRevQTorvesardDream`

## Self-description

This Torvesard says:
- the original Torvesard has been scattered into the Void;
- he is a **maligraphy**, a replica made of living ink;
- he was recreated from a book's description;
- the recreation includes appearance, powers, and thought-patterns.

## Lost Prince

He repeatedly refers to:
- **his Prince**;
- the Prince as female;
- the Prince having left Mundus / being forgotten;
- a sacred duty she gave him.

He says only the prepared Guide can currently recognize the Prince's name.

## Hermaeus Mora conflict

Torvesard positions himself against Hermaeus Mora and claims Mora is repeatedly rewriting the player's memory and perception.

## Ontology rule

Keep:
**original Torvesard**
and
**Torvesard maligraphy**
as linked but distinct ontology nodes.

The maligraphy explicitly denies being the original person.
