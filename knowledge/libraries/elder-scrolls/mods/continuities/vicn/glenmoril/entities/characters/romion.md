# Romion

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** `Glenmoril.esm`

NPC `04008826` / `zzzGHRomion` is named **Romion**.

In **The Blue Bird** (`zzzGHMq01`), the player can ask Romion about:

- what happened to the player;
- Romion's Dwemer studies;
- the **Yelem School**;
- Ozwald;
- Felix.

The plugin also contains later Romion-associated records, including a hologram and infected form, plus records named **FL-4N** associated with Romion/Dwemer gear namespaces. These later forms are logged for future quest-level reconciliation and are not yet synthesized into a biography.
