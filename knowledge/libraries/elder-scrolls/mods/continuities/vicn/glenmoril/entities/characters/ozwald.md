# Ozwald

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** `Glenmoril.esm`

## Record evidence

NPC `040083AD` / `zzzGHHunterOzwald` is named **Ozwald**. Additional later Ozwald records exist in other quest namespaces and should be reconciled as the narrative progresses.

## The Blue Bird

Ozwald has an extensive `zzzGHMq01` dialogue branch. Player topics include:

- requesting a fuller explanation of the situation;
- refusing/leaving;
- explicitly agreeing to join the witch hunt;
- asking whether the witch hunt has started;
- asking about Felix and whether Felix had a sister;
- saying the player has seen the Black Owl;
- asking where to obtain witch-hunt equipment;
- asking about witches;
- asking about **Red Drop** and **Hinnom**.

The quest-specific threatening letter from Ja'zel also says Ozwald is present and will try to kill the player if the demand is refused. That statement is Ja'zel's claim in the letter and is retained as attributed testimony rather than independently established motive.
