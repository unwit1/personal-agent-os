# Augur of the Obscure

Source:
`Glenmoril.esm:61451E zzzLrhPsijicAugur`
Display: **Augur of the Obscure**

Primary GLENMORIL quest:
**Brain-Billy's Sting**

## Role

Rolls-On-Roads calls the Augur:
> a convenient tool capable of answering questions from a higher perspective

but immediately warns:
> **do not take anything he says at face value.**

## Dialogue style

The Augur speaks in deliberately absurd, non-linear explanations about:
- brain-billies;
- chocolate milk;
- beavers;
- non-linear time;
- Numidium;
- parallel realities;
- dreams;
- Daedric Princes;
- Torvesard's forgotten Prince.

Some lines appear to contain useful higher-order clues, but they are mixed with obvious jokes and nonsense.

## Notable source claims

The Augur says:
- a prior/future Numidium incident has been “censored by the Jills”;
- dreams can sometimes move beings between realities;
- Torvesard's Prince's name is perceivable only when disconnected from its truth;
- the name can be expressed through a wordplay/riddle;
- there are “18” Princes if counted one way and “16” if counted another.

These are **Augur claims**, not objective cosmology.
