# Yelem

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

Yelem is a major recurring GLENMORIL figure whose record footprint extends far beyond the early Witch Hunt material.

## Direct ESM evidence

Dialogue calls Yelem a **Dwemer** and repeatedly addresses him as **Lord Yelem** or **Professor Yelem**. One speaker identifies **Boltzmer** as Yelem's former assistant. Another says Yelem and a disciple had worked together in **Arkngthamz** before parting ways.

Yelem is associated with a school/workshop, laboratories, a library/collection, Dwemer machines, experiments, mutagens, a blood curse, and numerous handwritten notes.

Later dialogue states that Yelem's soul has travelled across time and space. A quest item/quest named **Yelem's Cord** is said to be connected to his soul and usable to track him.

One dialogue response says Yelem “went to meet the Nerevarine” and directs the player to seek **Akulakhan**. This is recorded as in-mod dialogue, not as a claim about official Elder Scrolls canon.

## Nightmare / Owl evidence

A response explicitly says that “in the old days, Yelem chased an owl and awoke the nightmare sleeping in the depths.” Another says Yelem's journal records many futures seen in his nightmares. These statements make the Owl/nightmare relationship a major evidence target.

## Evidence discipline

Yelem's notes contain philosophical and metaphysical assertions. They are Yelem-authored in-world claims, not automatically objective cosmology.
