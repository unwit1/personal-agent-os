# Ozwald evidence bundle

Ozwald is a major operational and interpretive node in the Witch Hunt arc.

## Directly supported topics

- discusses joining the witch hunt;
- discusses equipment and witches;
- references **Red Drop** and **Hinnom**;
- is associated with workshop/airship material in Ebonwall;
- in **Or the Gospel**, explicitly discusses betraying or getting rid of the Chick Trader;
- says there is no longer a need to hunt witches.

## Narrative significance

Ozwald's late dialogue marks a shift away from the stated Witch Hunt mission and directly reorients attention toward the Chick Trader problem.
