# Boltzmer

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

A direct INFO response states:

> “I am Boltzmer, the former assistant of Lord Yelem.”

The same speaker describes himself as a preacher living in seclusion in a church.

Another response concerning Pailune says that Yelem and his assistant Boltzmer are its true leaders despite a Khajiit holding the title of mayor.

This establishes Boltzmer as a former Yelem assistant and a significant Pailune/Yelem-network character. The timing between his assistant role, leadership role, and later religious seclusion remains to be ordered from quests/scenes.
