# Professor Y

Primary source family:
`zzzRevBookProfesserY01–13`

Professor Y is a Dwemer researcher presented through a series of recorded dialogues with an unnamed assistant.

## Research interests

His corpus covers:
- perception and reality;
- Falmer transformation;
- Dwemer transcendence;
- Laza;
- Ja'bal / Rolly-Polly;
- Orlando and the Owls;
- living ships;
- Hist / Argonian anatomy;
- non-linear time;
- Azura and transference;
- Apocrypha interference;
- Hamah and Anem;
- C0DA.

## Characterization

Professor Y is:
- highly curious;
- ethically reckless;
- willing to experiment on living beings;
- comfortable consuming anomalous remains;
- dismissive of suffering when it advances research;
- openly speculative.

The assistant repeatedly acts as a moral/reality check.

## Reliability

Professor Y is useful but not omniscient.

He often distinguishes observation from theory, and several books explicitly use phrases such as:
- “in my opinion”;
- “it must be”;
- “perhaps”;
- “could be.”

Therefore his metaphysical conclusions should be stored as attributed research claims unless independently corroborated.

## Important corroboration

His Hamah account is partially confirmed later by Hamah's own first-person Revenant testimony:
**Snow Elf girl → Anem rebirth → Hamah**.
