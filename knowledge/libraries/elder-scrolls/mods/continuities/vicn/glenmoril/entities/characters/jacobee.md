# Ja'cobee

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

Ja'cobee identifies himself as the **last Chick Trader**. He says the other Chick Traders died and their duty is effectively finished, but he cannot stop because the **EGG** has not yet been completely broken.

## Chick Trader testimony

Ja'cobee says:
- the Chick Traders were selected from the prince of Anequina and his royal guards;
- he remembers those Chick Traders and therefore regards **Ja'zel** as an impostor;
- their beginning was prophesied through stories delivered by Sheogorath through a severed head to a clerk named **Ja'i**;
- the Chick Traders deliberately obscure their lore with substituted words and metaphors;
- their work involves Chickens, Chicks and Eggs while evading **Rolly-Polly**;
- the system ultimately amounts to sacrifice.

## The EGG

Ja'cobee says he broke an EGG after realizing what was inside. In his coded explanation an EGG becomes a CHICK, then a CHICKEN; the completed process would bring “dawn” and cost cats their sleep. He also says the secret of the eggs concerns **the Owl**.

He says the dream he inhabits began when the EGG cracked and will end when it breaks completely. He expects Rolly-Polly to catch him afterward and says everyone will forget him.

Because Ja'cobee explicitly admits that his terminology is intentionally misleading, these statements must remain attributed testimony until the code is independently resolved.
