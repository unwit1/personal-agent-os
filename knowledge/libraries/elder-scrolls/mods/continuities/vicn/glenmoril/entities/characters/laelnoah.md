# Laelnoah

Laelnoah is **Lalanoah's mother**.

## Direct identity evidence

In Shop of Curiosities:
- Lalanoah recognizes a recovered staff as the staff **her mother had**.
- Brandt identifies it as **Laelnoah's staff**.
- Brandt then explicitly tells Orlando that it belonged to **Lalanoah's mother**.

This directly establishes:
**Laelnoah = Lalanoah's mother**.

## Family

- daughter: Lalanoah
- partner/spouse: Gerhard, by the established Lalanoah parentage
- maternal aunt: Cerebline, who calls Laelnoah her niece
- mother: the anomalously long-lived human-born woman raised by Cerebline's elven family

## Staff

Lalanoah says:
- Laelnoah intended to give her the staff once she became a healer;
- Laelnoah used it while fighting bandits in the hold;
- Laelnoah once struck a drunken Brandt with it.

The recovered staff is missing a gem.

## Disappearance

Brandt says the **Holy Pipers** kidnapped women during the civil war for being perceived as witches, including Laelnoah.

Orlando says he bought her staff from strange-helmeted men near Helgen and directs Brandt toward **Piper's Hill**.

A search party is later sent there, but by St. Bazura contact with it has been lost.

## Status

Missing / unresolved. Do not mark Laelnoah dead without direct evidence.
