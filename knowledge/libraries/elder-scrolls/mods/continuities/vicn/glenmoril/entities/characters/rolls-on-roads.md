# Rolls-On-Roads

Source:
`Glenmoril.esm:61451C zzzLrhPsijicRoadRoller`
Display: **Rolls-On-Roads**

Primary quest:
**Brain-Billy's Sting**

## Role

Rolls-On-Roads says he is a **temporary advisor dispatched by the Psijic Order** to investigate anomalies around Falkreath.

He:
- flies/uses levitation;
- deploys a surveillance network;
- works with the Augur of the Obscure;
- studies space-time breaches;
- provides a teleportation link;
- receives running reports from the player;
- eventually designs a Psijic protection/extraction plan.

## Personality

His dialogue combines:
- serious investigative reasoning;
- scholarly exposition;
- institutional caution;
- jokes about chocolate milk, iron daggers, flying, food, and other oddities.

He is not omniscient.

His own investigation changes over time and he explicitly revises hypotheses.

## Important relationships

- **Augur of the Obscure** — investigative tool/companion.
- **Romion** — Rolls says he knew an eccentric Altmer friend in the Psijic Order who left to study the Dwemer and later worked for the Thalmor; when the player describes Romion, Rolls strongly suspects it may be this old friend.
- **Ja'zel** — Rolls detects Ja'zel deliberately skirting his surveillance network and treats him as highly dangerous.
- **Ritemaster / Psijic Order** — Rolls negotiates a protection plan for the player and associates.
- **Torvesard** — subject of Rolls's later historical/maligraphy investigation.

## Evidence boundary

Rolls is a source of **attributed analysis**.

His claims should not automatically override direct event records, especially when he uses words such as “suspect,” “probably,” “maybe,” or explicitly says evidence is incomplete.
