# Radcliffe

Quest anchor: **Weary of Waiting**

Radcliffe is a peddler known to Brandt.

## Direct evidence

Brandt:
- expects Radcliffe to arrive in Falkreath;
- says Radcliffe repeatedly economized on escort protection;
- identifies his body after the attacked carriage is investigated;
- must later notify Radcliffe's family.

## Cargo

Radcliffe's carriage contains:
- dried meat;
- gold;
- low-quality iron daggers.

The gold being left behind helps Brandt reject ordinary robbery as the attack motive.

## Fate

Dead by the end of the quest.

Brandt attributes the attack tentatively to a beast or ghoul based on clawing, the horse's injuries, and the untouched valuables. The exact attacker is not proven by Brandt's dialogue alone.
