# Zuzu the Oneiromancer

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

The NPC inventory contains **Zuzu the Oneiromancer**.

“Oneiromancer” is retained as the explicit in-game epithet/title. Because Act 2 also contains a quest titled **Oneiromancer**, Zuzu is a high-priority alias/dialogue-resolution target.

## Evidence boundary

The title establishes an explicit association with oneiromancy/dream-working terminology. It does not, by itself, establish Zuzu's allegiance, metaphysical nature, relationship to Yelem, or role in the quest. Those claims require quest aliases, scenes, or dialogue.
