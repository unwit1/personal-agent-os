# FL-4N

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

FL-4N is a recurring Act 2 operational node.

## Direct quest evidence

- **Ebonwall** begins with an objective to talk to FL-4N and later contains an optional communicator/contact objective involving FL-4N.
- **Tainted Orphan** likewise begins with an objective to talk to FL-4N.
- The plugin also contains Romion/Dwemer-associated records using the FL-4N name.

## Current interpretation boundary

The repeated placement makes FL-4N part of the mission-routing/communication structure of the Witch Hunt act. The precise nature of FL-4N, its construction/origin, and its relationship to Romion remain queued for NPC, scene, and dialogue resolution rather than inferred from editor IDs.
