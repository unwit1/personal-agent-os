# Ja'zel

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** `Glenmoril.esm`

## Record evidence

The plugin contains several Ja'zel records across different narrative contexts. The early record `04006F0B` is **Ja'zel the Chick Trader** (`zzzLrhChickTrader`). Later records reuse Ja'zel's name, including forms associated with later GLENMORIL namespaces.

These records should not yet be collapsed into a single physical state without quest/scene evidence, but the repeated naming is a strong continuity-resolution target.

## The Blue Bird

The `zzzGHMq01` Ja'zel dialogue branch includes player responses telling him not to approach Lalanoah again.

BOOK `0406EDB1`, **Threatening Letter from Ja'zel**, explicitly demands that the player participate in a witch hunt. The letter frames this as repayment for having saved the player's life and threatens three quest aliases/captives if the player refuses.

This provides direct ESM evidence that Ja'zel is involved in coercing the player into the Act 2 witch hunt.
