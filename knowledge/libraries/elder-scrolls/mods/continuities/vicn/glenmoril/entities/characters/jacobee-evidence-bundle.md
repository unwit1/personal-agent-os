# Ja'cobee evidence bundle

Ja'cobee is a central interpretive node for the Chick Trader material.

## Direct dialogue claims

- identifies himself as the **last Chick Trader**;
- says the other Chick Traders died;
- says the Chick Trader duty is effectively finished, but the **EGG** is not completely broken;
- says the original Chick Traders were selected from the prince of Anequina and royal guards;
- remembers the prior Chick Traders and calls Ja'zel an impostor;
- says their origin stories were delivered by Sheogorath through a severed head to the clerk Ja'i;
- says he broke an EGG after discovering what was inside;
- explains an EGG → CHICK → CHICKEN progression;
- says completing the process would bring “dawn” and cost cats sleep;
- says the secret of eggs concerns the **Owl**;
- says the current dream began when the EGG cracked and ends when it breaks fully;
- expects a **Rolly-Polly** to catch him and everyone to forget him.

## Vocabulary warning

Ja'cobee deliberately uses substitutions/metaphors involving EGG, CHICK, CHICKEN, cats/kittens, Rolly-Polly, Owl, dawn, sleep/dream, sacrifice/payment, birth/death and Wild Hunt.

Preserve these terms literally. Do not decode them into external entities unless the plugin supplies an explicit mapping.
