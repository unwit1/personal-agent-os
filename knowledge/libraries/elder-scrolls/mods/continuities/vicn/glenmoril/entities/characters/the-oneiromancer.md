# The Oneiromancer

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

Romion describes the Oneiromancer as a **female witch** whose existence reaches back to the **Dawn Era**. According to him, she removed most information about herself when she left for **Quagmire**, leaving few reliable details.

The Oneiromancer quest contains a dedicated Oneiromancer interaction target and a **Zuzu's Memories** target. The precise identity relationship between the title-holder and **Zuzu the Oneiromancer** should be resolved through aliases/scenes before the records are merged into one entity.

This page therefore represents the dialogue-defined figure/title while `zuzu.md` remains the NPC-record entity.
