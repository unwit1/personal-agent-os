# Gerhard

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** `Glenmoril.esm`

## Identity

NPC `04009BB3` has editor ID `zzzGHLalanoahFather` and display name **Gerhard**. The editor ID therefore directly identifies Gerhard as Lalanoah's father within the plugin data.

## The Blue Bird

`zzzGHMq01` contains multiple investigation topics concerning Gerhard and his unit. The player can ask Legion Quartermaster Agrippa where Gerhard's unit was dispatched and about its disappearance.

Later dialogue options allow the player to tell Brandt:

- Gerhard is dead and ghouls killed him; or
- Gerhard is dead while handing over his sword.

A Lalanoah branch lets the player give her a **hair ornament** as a gift from Gerhard. The same quest contains lie options in which the player tells Lalanoah that Gerhard is fine or says they forgot to ask him something but that he appeared fine.

These records establish Gerhard's paternal relationship and death within this quest's supported outcomes/dialogue state. The precise encounter sequence and circumstances of his death require stage/scene resolution.
