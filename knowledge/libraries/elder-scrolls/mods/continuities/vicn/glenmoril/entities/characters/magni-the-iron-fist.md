# Magni the Iron Fist

**Observed Form ID:** `0400D5DF`  
**Source ID:** `Glenmoril.esm:00D5DF`

Magni the Iron Fist is explicitly identified by the ESM as a commander figure in the Ebonwall sequence.

An optional **Ebonwall** objective instructs the player to assassinate Magni, described there as commander of the volunteer army.

This is direct quest-objective evidence for his military role. Broader motives/allegiances should not be inferred from the objective alone.
