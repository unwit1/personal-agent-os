# Lalanoah

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary evidence:** `Glenmoril.esm`

## Early-game evidence

The opening quest/dialogue cluster `zzzLrhMq01` repeatedly names Lalanoah in topic editor IDs and dialogue text.

Direct response text establishes that:

- Lalanoah is outside Falkreath rather than inside the town.
- She says she cannot go back home.
- She asks to remain where she is for a while.
- Brandt's dialogue describes Falkreath's orphans as children whose parents were lost in the civil war and says the available temples and orphanages are full.
- The player can obtain bread and give it to Lalanoah; her responses include surprise and thanks.
