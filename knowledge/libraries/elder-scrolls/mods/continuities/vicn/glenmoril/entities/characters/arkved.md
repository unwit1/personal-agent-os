# Arkved

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL overlay  
**Primary source:** supplied `Glenmoril.esm`

Arkved is directly discussed during the Oneiromancer mission.

Romion describes him as a Third-Era figure who stole a treasure from Vaermina and was imprisoned in nightmares as punishment. Romion says he contacted Arkved through an unusual method; Arkved offered to help the player, but Romion warns that he is no longer mentally reliable.

This is GLENMORIL's use/interpretation of an established Elder Scrolls character and must remain provenance-separated from the official-canon Arkved entity.
