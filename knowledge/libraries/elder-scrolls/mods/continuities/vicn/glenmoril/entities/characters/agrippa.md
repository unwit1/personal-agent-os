# Agrippa

**Continuity:** Vicn / GLENMORIL  
**Primary source:** `Glenmoril.esm`

NPC `04009B5A` / `zzzGHQuarterMaster` is **Legion Quartermaster Agrippa**.

In **The Blue Bird**, Agrippa is an information source concerning Gerhard's unit. The player's dialogue options include direct questioning, intimidation, persuasion, and a 100-gold payment. Follow-up topics ask:

- the current treatment/status of Gerhard's unit;
- where the unit was dispatched;
- about the unit's disappearance;
- whether anything similar had happened previously;
- what the Chick Trader wanted.

This establishes Agrippa's role as a Legion quartermaster with information relevant to the disappearance investigation. The content of each response still needs INFO-to-topic ordering for a fuller account.
