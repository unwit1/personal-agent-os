# Draco-Nord summon and encounter family

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

Three Dragon Bone records teach summon spells for named Draco-Nord forms:

- Draco-Nord Fonaar
- Draco-Nord Sahqon
- Draco-Nord Veddu

## Summoned forms

All three summoned actors use:
- **Dragonord Gargoyle Race**
- no 500 Companion Faction

## Hostile/encounter forms

### Fonaar
At least one hostile Fonaar form uses the same Dragonord Gargoyle Race and belongs to:
- 500 Companion Faction

### Sahqon
Multiple hostile Sahqon variants use the same race and belong to:
- 500 Companion Faction

### Veddu
Multiple hostile Veddu variants use the same race and belong to:
- 500 Companion Faction

## Broader Draco-Nord forms

UNSLAAD also contains actors simply named **Draco-Nord** that use a humanoid/Nord race implementation rather than the Gargoyle race.

This demonstrates that "Draco-Nord" is a broader authored category with multiple body/form implementations.

## Interpretation

The Dragon Bone summons reproduce named gargoyle-form Draco-Nord archetypes.

They should not automatically be treated as summoning the exact hostile individuals encountered in the trials.
