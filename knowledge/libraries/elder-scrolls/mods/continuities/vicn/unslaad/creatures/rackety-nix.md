# Rackety-Nix

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD implements both hostile and summoned forms of **Rackety-Nix**.

## Summon chain

BOOK:
- Dragon Bone: Rackety-Nix

teaches:
- Summon Rackety-Nix

which resolves to a summoned Rackety-Nix NPC form.

## Shared Voice package

Both the hostile/sub-boss form and the summoned form carry:

### Chaotic Fire Ball
Custom SHOU 03460DA0
- invokes Nix Fire Ball

### Nix Shout
Custom SHOU 03463623
- invokes Nix Shout

This is stronger form-continuity evidence than the shared display name alone: the summoned proxy reproduces the hostile archetype's custom Voice package.

## Recipe relationship

**Staff of Orkey** becomes craftable after Rackety-Nix's death count condition is satisfied.

The same staff is also associated with Kjhelt of the Cult of Orkey in Frozen Abyss.

## Boundary

The Rackety-Nix -> Staff of Orkey recipe gate is direct gameplay progression.

It does not establish that Rackety-Nix created, owned, or historically served Orkey.
