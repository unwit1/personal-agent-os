## Hostile form

NPC 033F2776:
- **Dagonic Watcher**
- same Watcher Race
- Dagon Faction
- custom Watcher death list

A stronger named Watcher-family actor also exists:
- **Red Instigator**
- same Watcher Race
- Dagon Faction

Mankar Camoran's Eye likewise uses Watcher Race and Dagon Faction.

## Significance

Watcher Race is therefore a broader body/archetype shared by:
- Dagonic Watchers;
- Mankar Camoran's Eye;
- Red Instigator.

The summon spell reproduces a Dagonic-Watcher body without importing the hostile actor's Dagon-Faction affiliation.

## Guardrail

Race continuity is not individual identity.

Do not treat a summoned Dagonic Watcher as Mankar Camoran's Eye or Red Instigator merely because they share the Watcher Race.
