# Dreugh / Hor summon family

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

Two BOOK records are physically styled as **Dreugh Eggs**:

- Dreugh Egg: Hor Priest
- Dreugh Egg: Hor Warrior

Each teaches a summon spell.

## Hor Priest

Summoned form:
- NPC 033EAE51
- Dreugh Druid Race
- no Lyg Crab Faction

Hostile form:
- NPC 033CDD56
- same Dreugh Druid Race
- Lyg Crab Faction
- custom Dreugh-Druid death list

## Hor Warrior

Summoned form:
- NPC 033EAE50
- Werecrab Race
- no Lyg Crab Faction

Hostile form:
- NPC 033CDD62
- same Werecrab Race
- Lyg Crab Faction
- Dreugh death list

## Significance

The egg items provide a direct gameplay mechanism for reproducing Hor/Dreugh creature forms.

Combined with Khev, Molag, and Lyg Crab Faction, this gives UNSLAAD a coherent Dreugh creature/spell/reward network.

## Boundary

The phrase "Dreugh Egg" is direct item text.

It does not establish the exact biological reproductive mechanism of every Lyg/Dreugh actor, and the summoned actors should not be assumed to be the same individuals as the hostile Hor Priest/Warrior.
