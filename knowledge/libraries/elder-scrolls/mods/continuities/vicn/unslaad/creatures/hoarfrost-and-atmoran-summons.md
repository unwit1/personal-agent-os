# Hoarfrost and Atmoran beast summons

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

## Hoarfrost Guardian

Book:
- Spell Tome: Conjure Hoarfrost Guardian

Summoned actor:
- Hoarfrost Guardian
- Frost Atronach custom race
- no Frost Power Excursion Faction

Hostile actor:
- same Hoarfrost Guardian display identity
- same Frost Atronach race
- Frost Power Excursion Faction

## Giant Hoarfrost Guardian

Book:
- Spell Tome: Conjure Giant Hoarfrost Guardian

Summoned actor:
- Giant Hoarfrost Guardian
- Frost Lord Race

The summon form itself retains Frost Power Excursion Faction in this plugin, unlike the ordinary Guardian summon.

Hostile giant form uses the same race and faction plus a death item.

## Freezing Enbarr

Book:
- Spell Tome: Conjure Freezing Enbarr

Summoned actor:
- Freezing Enbarr
- horse race
- no Frost Power Excursion Faction

Hostile form:
- same display identity/race
- Frost Power Excursion Faction

## Warrior of Tsun

Book form:
- Beast Bone: Warrior of Tsun

Summoned actor:
- Warrior of Tsun
- Atmoran Bear Race
- no custom faction

Trial boss:
- same display identity and race
- 500 Companion Faction
- Frost Power Excursion Faction
- custom boss death list

## Storm Atronach Bear

Book form:
- Beast Bone: Storm Atronach Bear

Summoned actor:
- Storm Atronach Bear
- Storm Atronach Bear Race
- no custom faction

Trial boss:
- same race/identity
- 500 Companion Faction
- Frost Power Excursion Faction

## Interpretation

UNSLAAD repeatedly allows the player to reproduce the **form/archetype** of creatures encountered in the Atmoran/Frost trial layer.

The summon should not automatically inherit the historical identity or factional role of the boss form.
