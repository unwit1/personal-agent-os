# Long Winter — future-Unslaad time structure

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary evidence: QUST 03117B3E / zzzCrbMq04 and its dialogue.

## Direct temporal evidence

The quest objective explicitly instructs the player to travel to Unslaad's future.

When the player returns, Fluffy says it has been roughly four or five hundred years, after first considering two or three hundred. His uncertainty matters: this is an approximate in-world estimate, not an exact chronology.

Jhunal the Gray says he had heard the Dragonborn died a long time ago. The Blades have meanwhile reached Unslaad, fought its inhabitants, and left the place ruined.

This establishes a large forward displacement from the earlier quest state, with characters such as Fluffy and Aisha persisting across centuries in altered circumstances.

## Blades incursion

Fluffy says the Blades invaded and attacked Ulliss despite her not harming anyone. Jhunal claims the Blades reached the land by following the Dragonborn's trail and attributes their deaths to greed and ambition.

These are character accounts. The fact of the Blades incursion is corroborated by the quest structure and named Blades Grandmaster boss; Jhunal's causal/moral interpretation remains his testimony.

## Ulliss revival

Jhunal instructs the player to pour dragon souls into Ulliss and says her "twisted life" was born that way. This aligns with the Twinkling Star/ring resurrection message discovered in the preceding quest layer.

After the revival sequence, Fluffy describes something warm filling Ulliss, "like a life growing inside her." The precise metaphysical meaning remains unresolved.

## Owls

Jhunal explicitly refers to "my other friends, the Owls." This is direct evidence that Gray Owl is not merely a solitary nickname but is presented by Jhunal as belonging to or associated with a plural Owl group.

## Time-transition closure

The Long Winter time jump is structurally resolved at the level needed for retrieval:

- the quest explicitly sends the player to future Unslaad;
- Fluffy gives an approximate several-century elapsed interval;
- Mq05 later exposes `LizzTimeWarpMarker` plus present/Lost-Unslaad door routing;
- persistent characters and the Blades aftermath corroborate a major forward displacement.

Do not convert Fluffy's rough estimate into an exact date.

The warm/life-growing language after Ulliss's revival is narratively important and later aligns with Lizz's lineage, but its exact biological/metaphysical mechanism remains deliberately unstated. That uncertainty is now classified rather than pending extraction.
