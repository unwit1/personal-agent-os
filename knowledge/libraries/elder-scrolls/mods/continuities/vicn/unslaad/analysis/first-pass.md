# UNSLAAD first record-derived lore pass

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

## What the ESM establishes immediately

UNSLAAD is structurally much larger than the old “short half-dragon quest” description retained in the voiced-addon readme. Version 3.0.6 contains ten numbered main quests, dozens of side/radiant/boss quests, 1,015 embedded dialogue response strings, 50 books, 59 scenes, and multiple dedicated worldspaces.

Named worldspaces include **Unslaad**, **Lost Unslaad**, **Northern Sanctuary**, and **Dragon's Peak**. Named cells include **Chapel of Bormahu**, **Hermit's Tower**, **Hidden Hot Springs**, and many later-story spaces still awaiting indexing.

The quest structure makes time displacement a direct gameplay/narrative element: **Long Winter** explicitly instructs travel to “Unslaad's future,” while **Lingering Snow** instructs travel to a “distant future.” That observation is direct quest text; the mechanism and cosmological meaning remain to be established from dialogue/scenes/scripts.

The later main chain explicitly introduces a **Mural of Jhunal**, a **Black Owl**, **Dragon's Cradle**, a Cleverman, a colossus/frozen tomb, a ship pointed toward **Snow Throat**, an offering to the **Shadow of Shor**, the **Loveletter to the Fifth Era**, and an **Elder Tree**. These are high-value retrieval nodes for the next passes.

## Strong early character nodes

The plugin directly defines named NPC forms for **Ulliss**, **Aisha**, **Austella**, **Lizz**, **Ja'cobee**, **Jhunal the Gray**, **Black Owl**, **Arkved the Dreamer**, **Herkel the Fool**, **Wulf the Veteran**, **Mankar Camoran's Eye**, and others. Identity pages should be created only after dialogue/alias resolution distinguishes ordinary actors, ghosts, transformed forms, summons, and boss variants.

## Provenance cautions

- Reused official books can appear inside the ESM; presence is not proof Vicn authored their prose.
- NPC names based on established TES figures do not by themselves establish literal identity.
- Quest/objective wording is high-confidence evidence for structure, but weaker evidence for metaphysical truth.
- The English ESM is a translation layer; unusual terminology should preserve the translated wording and, where later needed, be checked against Japanese/original-context material rather than silently normalized.
