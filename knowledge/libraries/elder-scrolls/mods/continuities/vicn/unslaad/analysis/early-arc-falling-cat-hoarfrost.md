# Early UNSLAAD arc — Falling Cat and Hoarfrost

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary evidence: zzzCrbMq01 and zzzCrbMq02 dialogue, quest aliases, and SCEN records in the translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6.

## Directly established story facts

Aisha is the first named guide encountered in the extracted dialogue. She tells the player to jump and explicitly invokes Kyne and a favorable wind. She also says another girl previously jumped and was able to return home. This makes wind/Kyne imagery part of the actual traversal dialogue rather than merely environmental symbolism.

Aisha says the player is the second visitor to arrive. The prior visitor is described as a man from Alcaire whom Aisha disliked.

Ulliss independently describes that earlier visitor as a passionate man who was initially called Hjalti and who used different names on different visits. This is a high-value Talos/Tiber retrieval hook because Hjalti is a known name in Elder Scrolls material, but UNSLAAD's dialogue should be preserved as its own testimony rather than silently collapsing the visitor into every external Hjalti tradition.

Ulliss is directly described as a dragon/half-dragon. Her mother is Austella. Ulliss says she has never met her father; her mother told her that an armored courtyard statue represents him. Aisha adds that the father can appear in human, dragon, or cat form and calls him a liar and trickster.

Ulliss once left Unslaad while young to look for her father. Aisha says the trip took three hundred years before Ulliss returned, and both Aisha/Ulliss associate the outside period with conflict between men and dragons. Aisha says returning depended on the right wind.

Austella's scene dialogue makes her rejection of Ulliss explicit: she frames Ulliss as ambiguously human/dragon, sees darkness in her future, and says she could not love her as she is. Ulliss says Austella hates her tail and horns. The doll branch supplies a contrasting maternal-remembrance object, and Ulliss reacts to it as proof that her mother had been thinking of her.

Fluffy is structurally quest alias Senpai and NPC 03004906. His dialogue identifies him as Khajiit despite his snow-spirit-like form and incomplete memory. Aisha calls him a snow spirit; Ulliss is unsure what snow spirits truly are and repeats her mother's claim that people who die in snow can become them. These statements should remain character beliefs, not universal ontology.

## Interpretation boundaries

- Hjalti + Alcaire + changing names is a deliberate-looking historical/mythic hook, but this pass records the hook rather than declaring a fully resolved identity.
- Aisha's statement that Ulliss' father can appear as a cat, dragon, or human is Aisha's testimony.
- The three-hundred-year duration is direct dialogue, but the exact chronology and temporal mechanism require later quest/scene evidence.
- Snow-spirit origin is explicitly uncertain even to Ulliss.
