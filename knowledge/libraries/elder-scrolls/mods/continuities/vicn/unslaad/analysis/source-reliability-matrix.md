# UNSLAAD source reliability / contradiction matrix

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

This matrix is designed for Lorekeeper retrieval and synthesis.

It does **not** rank characters as globally truthful or deceptive.

Instead, each source is classified by:
- what kind of source it is;
- what kinds of claims it is strongest for;
- what known distortions/contradictions affect it;
- how Lorekeeper should phrase conclusions drawn from it.

---

## Jhunal the Gray / Gray Owl

### Source type
- participant confession;
- experimenter testimony;
- self-mythologizing identity narrative;
- quest antagonist/guide;
- implementation-supported multi-form identity.

### Strongest evidence for
Use as high-value participant testimony for:
- his own dream of becoming a dragon;
- his use of the name **Jhunal** for thousands of years;
- his admission that people no longer distinguish him from the god Jhunal;
- his own experimental history involving Laza, Ulliss, dragon souls, vessels, and Saarthal;
- his membership/association with the Owls;
- claims about what **he says he did** to create or shape Ulliss.

### Reliability hazards
- openly manipulative;
- repeatedly uses assumed/appropriated identity;
- participates in memory erosion and dream/repetition systems;
- multiple bodies/representations exist;
- motives are hidden;
- late dialogue frames several experiments retrospectively after failures.

### Important contradiction
The Hermit says Austella was influenced by someone who **pretended to be Jhunal**.

Gray Owl later effectively confirms the core identity problem by saying he used the name Jhunal until nobody remembered the difference between him and the god.

### Retrieval phrasing
Prefer:
- "Jhunal the Gray says..."
- "The Gray Owl claims..."
- "The participant account attributes..."

Avoid:
- "Jhunal objectively was..."
- "The god Jhunal did..."

unless independently corroborated.

## Hermit

### Source type
- former advisor;
- philosopher;
- prophetic/dream witness;
- interpretive narrator.

### Strongest evidence for
Use as informed testimony for:
- Austella having an advisor before the Gray-Owl influence;
- the advisor's perception that the Jhunal claimant was a pretender;
- Hermit's own philosophy of love, fire, Radiance, flowers, divinity;
- existence/content of his dream visions.

### Reliability hazards
- much of his evidence is symbolic dream material;
- philosophical statements are normative/metaphysical teachings, not neutral mechanics;
- does not claim direct omniscience;
- dream figures may be archetypal, predictive, distorted, or retrospective.

### Key disagreement
**Radiance**

Hermit:
- Radiance is an echo of a past never experienced;
- a coffin rather than a guiding light.

DAc0da's Athanasius:
- Radiance reveals many paths and pluralized identity.

Preserve the disagreement.

### Retrieval phrasing
Prefer:
- "The Hermit interprets..."
- "In the Hermit's dream..."
- "As Austella's former advisor, he says..."

Do not phrase his dreams as direct event transcripts.

## Herkel the Fool

### Source type
- Clever Man;
- guide;
- historical/mythic storyteller;
- memory-compromised witness.

### Strongest evidence for
Use as high-value but fallible testimony for:
- practical Lyg mirror instructions;
- names of places/figures he recognizes;
- Atmoran seals and routes;
- Woodland-Man / Herma-Mora associations;
- the idea that heroes can deviate from fate;
- warnings about Dragonborn hunger/devouring.

The Lyg-mirror claims receive unusually strong support because quest VMAD directly contains:
- DoorLyg;
- IceMirror markers;
- LygMid/LygEnd markers.

### Reliability hazards
- explicitly forgetful;
- memories can be influenced by Apocrypha/Herma-Mora;
- some recollections are songs, tales, rumors, or sudden remembered fragments;
- often mixes practical instructions with mythic explanation.

### Retrieval phrasing
Split his claims into:
1. **mechanically corroborated route information** — high confidence;
2. **historical recollection** — informed/fallible;
3. **cosmological interpretation** — attributed testimony.

### Source type
- doctrinal speaker;

### Strongest evidence for
Use as direct evidence for:

- identity is self-fashioned around a mirror/inversion;

### Major contradiction
**Nu-Mantia**

Loveletter:

## Insight messages

### Source type
- collectible lore fragments;
- myths;
- historical fragments;
- damaged logs;
- hostile doctrine;
- reused external excerpts.

### Strongest evidence for
Use as direct evidence for:
- exact vocabulary deliberately included by UNSLAAD;
- names of places/figures;
- local myth traditions;
- implementation-level thematic clusters;
- damaged technical/log material where wording is explicit.

### Reliability hazards
Different Insight messages have different source voices.

They include:
- hostile Crab-Arkay teachings;
- mythic Atmoran prose;
- damaged ship logs;
- KINMUNE excerpts copied from external text;
- GC9 implementation-tagged communication logs;
- Gral/Owl-Forest story cycles.

Do **not** assign one narrator or one reliability level to all Insights.

### Retrieval rule
Always preserve:
- FormID / EditorID;
- local placement;
- whether source is myth/log/hostile/reused/damaged.

## Reused official / developer / apocryphal texts

### Source type
External source embedded by UNSLAAD.

### Provenance classes

**Official game text**
- Sermon 37 — ESO: Morrowind.

**Unofficial developer/apocryphal**
- Loveletter From the Fifth Era;
- Sermon Zero;
- KINMUNE fragments / ANON, ANU, ANUI-EL.

**External provenance unresolved**
- Vivec and Molag Bal parody.

### Strongest evidence for
Two separate questions must be tracked:

1. **What does the external text say?**
2. **Why/how does UNSLAAD use it?**

The first belongs to the external provenance layer.
The second belongs to Vicn-continuity analysis.

### Reliability hazard
Embedding a text does not mean:
- Vicn authored it;
- UNSLAAD endorses it as objective truth;
- the text becomes official canon;
- every external claim becomes literal in the mod.

## Mechanical / implementation evidence

### Source type
- quest aliases;
- GetIsID;
- factions;
- races;
- outfits;
- placed references;
- VMAD properties;
- recipe conditions;
- BOOK -> SPEL -> MGEF -> NPC chains;
- death lists;
- direct inventory.

### Strongest evidence for
Very high confidence for:
- "this actor is wired to this alias";
- "this item is carried by this NPC";
- "this recipe unlocks after this quest";
- "this object is placed in this cell";
- "this form uses this race/faction/outfit";
- "this spell summons this actor form."

### Reliability hazard
Implementation evidence can be **gameplay-semantic rather than cosmological**.

Examples:
- faction membership may control hostility rather than social membership;
- race may be a body/model container rather than species;
- recipe unlock does not establish historical creation;
- outfit does not necessarily cause identity;
- shared combat spell does not prove same person.

# Retrieval policy

When Lorekeeper answers a lore question, prefer this order:

1. **Implementation fact** if the question is structural.
2. **Direct participant confession/testimony** for what a character says/did.
3. **Independent corroborating source** if available.
4. **Myth/dream/Insight** as attributed evidence.
5. **External reused text** with provenance label.
6. **Synthesis** only after the source layers are visible.

For contested claims, answer with:
- claim;
- speaker/source;
- corroboration;
- contradiction;
- current confidence boundary.

This matrix is intended to prevent confident-sounding flattening of VICN's deliberately unstable lore.

## Confidence shorthand

Lorekeeper may use the following retrieval shorthand internally:

- **structural-direct** — explicit implementation relation (alias, placed ref, VMAD property, inventory, recipe condition, faction/race assignment);
- **participant-direct** — a named participant describes their own actions, motives, identity, or witnessed event;
- **independent-corroboration** — two source-local witnesses or one witness plus implementation evidence converge;
- **mythic-attributed** — dream, Insight, sermon, legend, prayer, damaged memory, or symbolic testimony;
- **external-reuse** — embedded source whose original authorship/provenance lies outside UNSLAAD;
- **synthesis-only** — an analytical relationship inferred by comparing sources and not directly stated by one record.

These are evidence classes, not numerical truth scores. A mechanically direct fact can still have limited cosmological meaning, while a mythic claim can be narratively important without being literal.

## Speaker-scoped contradiction rule

When two sources conflict, preserve both with attribution before attempting synthesis. In particular:

- Khev versus the Loveletter on Nu-Mantia;
- Hermit versus later Radiance framings;
- Hermit versus Gray Owl/Jhunal self-identification;
- competing frozen-Atmora causal explanations;
- external KINMUNE identity tradition versus source-local staging.

Do not let a later or more mechanically elaborate source silently overwrite an earlier contradictory testimony.
