## Ulliss and Lizz

Lizz repeatedly calls Ulliss **mom**, while Ulliss openly says she is happy to have Lizz.

Their scenes include:
- making and throwing snowballs;
- Lizz making snowmen representing the people around her;
- Ulliss correcting Lizz's language;
- discussions of Austella and family history.

Ulliss says becoming a mother herself helps her understand Austella somewhat better.

## Austella / Kyne

When Lizz asks where her grandmother is, Ulliss says Austella is **with Kyne**, far away.

This is Ulliss's religious/familial framing of Austella's death or passage.

## Family snowmen

Lizz makes figures representing:
- Lizz;
- Ulliss;
- Fluffy/Ja'cobee;
- Aisha;
- the Dovahkiin, represented by a helmet because the player is difficult for her to model.

This is strong direct evidence for Lizz's post-story conception of her family/social circle.

## "Ja'cobee of this side"

Lizz asks Fluffy whether he is:

**"Ja'cobee of this side"**

Fluffy initially resists the boring name, then accepts being called Ja'cobee after Lizz insists.

This is stronger than a simple same-name recurrence. The epilogue presents:
- continuity between Fluffy and Ja'cobee;
- a distinction between "this side" and the other Ja'cobee previously said to be "on the other side";
- incomplete/self-inconsistent recognition by the local Fluffy form.

The exact metaphysical mechanism remains unresolved.

## Gold and reaching upward

Fluffy says he gathers gold for satisfaction, but also wonders whether he is trying to **reach something at the top of the pile**.

This echoes Fluffy's Secret, where gold, guilt/forgiveness, memory, and a desire to pile gold toward Aetherius are already linked.

## Aisha

Aisha tells Lizz:
- she is both cat and Khajiit;
- she prefers not to become a dragon;
- Lizz should be herself rather than merely imitate Ulliss;
- she came from a white city by the sea aboard a white ship.

Aisha also says she may have seen an adult Lizz before, but cannot remember when.

That last line is a significant time/memory anomaly and should be cross-indexed with UNSLAAD's future travel, Arkved's memory ontology, and the two-Ja'cobee evidence without assigning a single mechanism prematurely.

## Handling

The epilogue is low-conflict domestic material but high-value relationship evidence. Lorekeeper should use it to resolve family/identity edges while avoiding turning playful dialogue into unsupported cosmology.
