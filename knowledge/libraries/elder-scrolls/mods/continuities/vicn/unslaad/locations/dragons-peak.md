# Dragon's Peak — inspection cluster

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Worldspace: WRLD 0332F628 / zzzCrbWyrmWorld
Display name: **Dragon's Peak**

Dragon's Peak contains a deliberately arranged late-story inspection cluster.

## Sacrifice group — CELL 03331E9B

An Etched Tablet says a daughter is offered to a dragon "with three words" so its awakening may end winter.

A **Sacrificial Maiden** corpse:
- reminds the observer of Austella;
- appears calm/almost smiling in death.

A nearby **Dragonborn** corpse may have been related to the sacrifice and still protects her in death.

A **Dragon Priest** is described as having prepared the failed sacrifice to end endless winter.

Two Amaranth inspection objects are also placed in this cell.

## Jhunal / Gray Owl bodies

### Jhunal the Gray — CELL 03331EA9
ACTI 0333F0ED.

Inspection says:
- the man was struck in the left side of the head by magic;
- **no one knows his real name**.

### Gray Owl — CELL 03331EB3
ACTI 0333F0EC.

Inspection says:
- he pursued the dragon until the end;
- his body is broken and shattered with ambition;
- he will never reach it.

These are separate physical representations.

Elsewhere, dialogue directly has Jhunal the Gray identify himself as the Gray Owl. The best evidence model is therefore linked identity with multiple bodies/representations, not a forced one-body merge or an unrelated-person split.

## Ja'cobee and Aisha — CELL 03331EB3

ACTI 0333F0EF identifies **Ja'cobee and Aisha**.

Their corpses are described as still warm, as if they died moments ago.

## Timeless threshold

A teleport trigger in CELL 03331E9B says the player needs **something timeless** to enter the dreams of sleeping Atmorans in a land where time no longer flows.

In quest context, this is tied to the Loveletter requirement.

## Significance

Dragon's Peak physically clusters sacrifice/endless-winter history, Austella-like imagery, Dragonborn protection, multi-body Jhunal/Gray-Owl evidence, Ja'cobee/Aisha temporal anomalies, Amaranth, and timeless dream access.
