# Mnemo-Li / Grabbers / books and words

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary evidence: Crab-Arkay Insight sequence and late Owl/Atmora material

UNSLAAD contains a concentrated Insight sequence that treats **books, words, stories, Mnemo-Li, and the Grabbers** as one dangerous information ecology.

## Books as prisons

Several Insight messages repeat the phrase **books are prisons for words**.

The sequence associates:
- written words with motionlessness/freezing;
- curiosity with chains;
- knowledge with predation;
- bodies with prisons for souls;
- forgotten voices with freezing.

These are stylized teachings, not necessarily a single literal mechanism.

## Mnemo-Li

MESG 033E033A calls Mnemo-Li a sister of Merid-Nunda and describes her as consuming words from blood and stories from bone marrow.

It warns that when she is sated, a terrible world is born.

This is openly hostile testimony and should not be used as neutral biography.

## Grabbers

MESG 03425E7D warns of invisible hands of the **Grabbers**.

It says they:
- steal stories from books;
- dedicate those stories to Mnemo-Li;
- exclude Nu-Mantia from the resulting "worlding of the words."

UNSLAAD elsewhere warns that staying too long in Lyg can attract the Grabbers.
