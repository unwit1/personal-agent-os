# Snow Whales and sky ecology

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary speakers: Cawk the Dirtbird, Ninawa, Anaku, Atta

## Snow Whales

Cawk treats **Snow Whales** as ordinary migratory creatures that swim/fly through the sky. He says he hopes to locate his flock by following where the Snow Whales travel and says they can be seen in the skies above Atmora.

Ninawa is presented as a whale-like speaker who says:
- if separated from the pod, flying again should eventually lead back to it;
- the snow in the region is comfortable and gentle;
- when hungry, simply opening the mouth while flying somehow results in becoming full.

## Atta

Atta says that at the end of life, their blubber will be made into **paint**, and as paint they will take on many different forms.

Atta also gives a local tour of Uncle Bug's tower and paintings, the blacksmith, Kanra, Naghu, hot springs, Pot Baba, Cave of Radiance, a rat diner, and Dragon's Throat.

## Anaku

Anaku discusses deliberately opaque concepts named **THIS**, **THAT**, and **IT**.

One direct line says IT sometimes talks, while THAT will disappear if nobody remembers it anymore.

Because the referents are never resolved in this dialogue cluster, preserve the wording as memory-persistence evidence without inventing identities for THIS/THAT/IT.

## Handling

Snow Whales are direct diegetic creatures in UNSLAAD's local ecology. Their existence in this continuity should not be used to assert an official Elder Scrolls zoological fact outside the mod.
