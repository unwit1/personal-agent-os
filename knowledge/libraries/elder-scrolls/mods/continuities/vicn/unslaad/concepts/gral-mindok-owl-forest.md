# Gral / Mindok / Owl Forest cycle

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary evidence: Insight messages 033C8809–033C880B and 0348BF7B

## Gral's origin

The giants of Jhunal find an orphan in the Owl Forest and name him **Gral**.

He masters the magics of Jhunal, becomes **Gral the Knowledgeable**, and is later selected as a dragon priest, receiving the name **Mindok**.

## Withered Owl Forest

After the Owl Forest withers and Woodland Men begin moving among the dead trees, Gral increasingly listens to a **heavy-eyed hare** rather than the dragons.

The hare teaches that things do not precede words and that words separate things from Sithis, then tempts Gral with a world before that separation.

This is doctrinal testimony within an Insight message, not a neutral definition of language or Sithis.

## Oath-breaking and transformation

Gral later:
- breaks an oath;
- consumes owl flesh and blood;
- is called **Gral the Shameless**;
- is sentenced to death by blood eagle.

At execution, his body is described as no longer human:
- blood → ink;
- flesh → writing quills;
- bones → parchment bundles.

People fear the Woodland Man / Herma-Mora is responsible and discard Gral's empty skin in the withered forest.

## BBBux

A fourth Insight says **BBBux** wraps himself in Gral's skin to feel warmth.

He repeatedly skins others as each body grows cold, becomes increasingly mad, abandons his Lord, and leaves seeking a new body to wear.

## Significance

This cycle tightly joins:
- Jhunal;
- Owl Forest;
- dragon-priest naming;
- words/writing;
- Herma-Mora/Woodland Men;
- bodily identity as skin/form;
- memory/forgetting;
- the recurring VICN interest in bodies as replaceable or writable containers.

Do not merge Gral, Mindok, BBBux, the Gray Owl, or Orlando merely because all intersect Owl/Jhunal imagery.
