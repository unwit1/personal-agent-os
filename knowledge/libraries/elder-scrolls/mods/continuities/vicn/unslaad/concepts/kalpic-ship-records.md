# Kalpic ship records

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary evidence: MESG 03491193–03491195

Three damaged Insight messages form a short sequence of ship records.

## Record 01 — 03491193

The damaged text names:
- Snow-Throat;
- the **World-Eater's-Waking**;
- a Boat-Thane identified only as a deleted **Northerly Dragon**;
- first-clutch sons;
- a cycle/ending represented by broken text.

The ship or fleet reaches sight of Snow-Throat after the **Morag** is broken and sent into eastern slush.

## Record 02 — 03491194

Ysgramor lands and shouts.

A deleted subject shatters and sheds a body **like scales**.

As dawn arrives and sailors emerge from Joy-Snow elation, the creaking of wood returns to **Ylgermet**.

## Record 03 — 03491195

The World-Eater's-Waking reaches the shore before the Northerly Dragon can cry out and sinks into snow.

The first person to board is identified as the dragon's destroyer:

**Ysgramor the Returned.**
