# The Owls — UNSLAAD evidence

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary evidence: Jhunal the Gray dialogue in zzzCrbMq03, zzzCrbMq04, and zzzCrbMq05.

Jhunal the Gray explicitly self-identifies as the Gray Owl in the Bell Tower scene.

In Long Winter, when challenged about his motives, he calls himself kind "just like my other friends, the Owls." This is direct text supporting an Owl collective/association in UNSLAAD.

Lingering Snow materially expands the concept:

- when asked where the other Owls are, Jhunal says he last saw them in **Saarthal during the Night of Tears**;
- he calls the barrier protecting Lizz one of the **Arcanas of the Owls**, left to the priestesses of Kyne;
- when dying, he says he will **serve the Owls one last time** under the pledge of his mask.

The corpus therefore supports the Owls as more than a nickname. UNSLAAD presents them as a historical group/association with shared arcana, a Saarthal/Night-of-Tears past, and some kind of continuing service or pledge.
