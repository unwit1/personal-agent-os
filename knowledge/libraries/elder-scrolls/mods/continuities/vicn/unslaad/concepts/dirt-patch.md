# Dirt Patch

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary speaker: Cawk the Dirtbird

Cawk describes the **Dirt Patch** as:
- a magical land where it never snows;
- the only soil suitable for Cawk's nest;
- a place whose soil is unusually soft and unlike ordinary ground;
- mobile, moving from place to place.

Most strikingly, Cawk says the Dirt Patch **popped up while Alduin was absent** and has moved around ever since.

## Evidence boundary

The dialogue establishes Cawk's belief and the authored local phenomenon. It does not explain what period "Alduin was absent" refers to, who or what created the Dirt Patch, why it moves, or whether its snowlessness is temporal, magical, or ecological.

Treat Alduin's absence as a retrieval hook rather than an automatic chronology anchor.
