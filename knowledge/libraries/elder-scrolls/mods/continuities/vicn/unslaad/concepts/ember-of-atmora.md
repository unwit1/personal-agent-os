# Ember of Atmora

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary source: repeatable quest zzzCrbRadiantEmber / Ember of Atmora  
Primary witness: Mifa Xanadu

Mifa says the **Ember of Atmora** is:
- fire brought from Atmora by the Dragon Cult;
- said to be a piece of fire created by a dragon as large as a mountain;
- something that may have been lit in **Saarthal**;
- lost after the **Night of Tears**;
- possibly still recoverable from ruined temples across Skyrim.

## Evidence boundary

Every historical detail is delivered as Mifa's account, including uncertainty about Saarthal.

The Ember should be cross-indexed with:
- Atmora;
- Dragon Cult;
- Saarthal;
- Night of Tears;
- the wider VICN Jhunal/Owl/Saarthal network.

Do **not** automatically identify it with:
- Jhunal's Arcana of Magnus;
- Marukh's Torch;
- any other eternal/flame artifact

without a direct record establishing that equivalence.
