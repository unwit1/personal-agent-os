# Name and Radiance — UNSLAAD evidence seed

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD repeatedly uses **name** and **Radiance** as more than ordinary labels, but the exact ontology is not fully resolved.

## Sacred Anatomancer

NPC 0331B06B / Sacred Anatomancer repeatedly asks the player to give it their name.

When asked why, it says:

"I want to see its Radiance."

This directly links a person's name with something the Anatomancer calls Radiance.

## Hermit on Radiance

The Hermit says Radiance:
- behaves like a splinter of the present but is not one;
- was once "there" but no longer is;
- is an echo of a past that was never experienced;
- does not guide people to dawn;
- is instead a coffin for the cowardly.

This is philosophical testimony rather than a technical definition.

## Hermit dream: nameless child

In one painting/dream:
- a second child appears beside a lonely child;
- trees say the second child has **no name**;
- the earth says the child has **no heartbeat**;
- a Black Owl watches the children.

Do not infer that "no name" automatically means the same mechanism as the Anatomancer's requested Name. Store them as linked evidence.

## Environmental node

CELL 033A1E1B is named **End of Radiance**.

The name strengthens Radiance as an authored recurring concept but does not explain the cell's metaphysics by itself.

## Cross-VICN policy

Search other VICN works for exact Name/Radiance language before promoting any universal theory. Similar memory, Blue Star, body/vessel, or Owl material is not sufficient by itself to equate the concepts.
