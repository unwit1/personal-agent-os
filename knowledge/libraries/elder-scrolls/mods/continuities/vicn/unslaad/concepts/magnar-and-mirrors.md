# Magnar and the mirror-replacement tradition

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary evidence: MESG 033DD7CD, Harakk Warrior's Final Note, and The Final Journey

## Headless Idol Insight

The Insight associated with the Headless Idol says:
- Kyne took the head of **Magnar**;
- Magnar had betrayed the spear-lines and fled;
- Shor says the figure being discussed is not truly Magnar;
- Magnar fell at sunrise and **became replaced by mirrors**.

This is a mythic statement and should retain that status.

## Harakk's note

Harakk Warrior's Final Note asks Kyne to:
- cut off Magnar's head again;
- raise it into the sky;
- trap the speaker among mirrors at sunset;
- grant spring/light to a frozen body.

The note independently clusters Magnar, decapitation, mirrors, sunset, seasonal warmth, and frozen death.

## Lyg mirror

In The Final Journey, Herkel says the mirror under the ice is:
- an **imitation of Magnar**;
- made from a polished piece of Merid-Nunda's essence;
- a route to Lyg.

This gives the mirror-replacement myth a concrete quest function.

## Current model

UNSLAAD deliberately connects:
- Magnar;
- sunrise/sunset;
- decapitation;
- mirrors;
- Merid-Nunda;
- transitions to Lyg;
- warmth/thaw versus frozen Atmora.

Do not yet conclude that every mirror is a literal fragment/replacement of Magnar or that Herkel's Lyg mirror is the exact object described in Harakk's note.

## Evidence and promotion boundary

This dossier currently combines three different evidence classes that should remain distinguishable:

- **Insight testimony:** the Headless Idol myth about Magnar, sunrise, decapitation, and replacement by mirrors.
- **Textual/prayer evidence:** Harakk Warrior's Final Note independently repeats Magnar + decapitation + mirrors + sunset/frozen-body imagery.
- **Quest-object testimony:** Herkel identifies the under-ice Lyg mirror as an imitation of Magnar made from polished Merid-Nunda essence.

The convergence is strong **inside UNSLAAD**, but the current indexed VICN corpus does not yet contain an independent non-UNSLAAD Magnar occurrence strong enough to justify a dedicated parent Magnar identity thread.
