# Ice of Atmora

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary placement: CELL 03322AB6 — **Frozen Cave**
Primary records: MESG 03322B41–03322B44

Four Atmora Study messages are placed as Etched Tablets in Frozen Cave.

## Study sequence

1. Two refugee ships from Atmora are investigated; both are laden with corpses.
2. Soft ice is extracted from the recovered corpse of a **pregnant woman** and tentatively named **Ice of Atmora**.
3. Luminescent larvae are observed inside the Ice of Atmora; extraction fails.
4. An **Atmoran ice seal** is documented; tentacles of ice creep from beyond it and require frequent thinning.

## Related UNSLAAD evidence

Cross-retrieve with:
- frozen-time Atmora;
- Naghu's ice trees;
- Jokul's deeply rooted Atmoran ice;
- Ulliss's ice/dragon-soul construction;
- Dragon's Cradle ice-doll research.

## Boundary

Do not automatically assert that:
- the pregnant corpse is Austella;
- the larvae are Dragon Souls;
- Ice of Atmora is exactly Ulliss's construction material;
- the ice tentacles and ice trees are one organism.
