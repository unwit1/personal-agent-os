# Ysmaalithax

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary evidence: Oohoo'Laf dialogue in The Final Journey

Oohoo'Laf the Woodland Man reveals **Ysmaalithax** as a dragon name that the player is meant to inherit.

He claims:
- it is the name Ysgramor shed along with his scales;
- it was later inherited by a young man named Miraak;
- Miraak was meant to keep the name safe;
- the player is next in this inheritance;
- the name was intentionally hidden by Herma-Mora;
- historical remembrance of the name is dangerous to its bearer.

## Evidence status

The name and inheritance chain are direct UNSLAAD dialogue.

The historical/metaphysical claims are spoken by a Woodland Man who openly serves Herma-Mora and bargains with the player, so they should not be treated as neutral official-TES history.
