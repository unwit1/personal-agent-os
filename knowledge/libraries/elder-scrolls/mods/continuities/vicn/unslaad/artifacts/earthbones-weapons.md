# Earthbones weapon family

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains playable and encounter-form **Earthbones** swords.

## Playable forms

### Earthbones Sword
WEAP 033BE451

Description:
- attacks ignore **99% of armor**.

Physical acquisition:
- reward chest in **Dragon's Throat**;
- another placement in Frozen Abyss / a Jhunal-city cell.

### Earthbones Greatsword
WEAP 033BE452

Description:
- attacks ignore **99% of armor**.

A custom death list used by **Hgmieil** in Dragon's Throat yields the playable Greatsword.

## Encounter forms

Separate non-player/encounter Earthbones weapon records are carried by:
- Draco-Nord variants;
- Pseudo-Man / Dragoman variants.

These actors belong to the broad Atmoran/500-Companion encounter layer rather than one single named historical owner.

## Hgmieil split

One Hgmieil form:
- is a wolf-form boss in Dragon's Throat;
- drops the Earthbones Greatsword.

A separate **Hgmieil the Wolf Warrior** form:
- appears in Wolf's Hearth;
- carries the Wolf King's Sword.

This is evidence for multiple forms/states around the Hgmieil identity, not one static loadout.

## Interpretation boundary

The direct item mechanic is extreme armor penetration.

The ESM does not explain whether "Earthbones" here refers literally to:
- Aedric Earth Bones;
- laws of nature;
- material made from an Earthbone;
- a symbolic weapon tradition.

Cross-index with official/apocryphal Earthbones material, but do not import an external cosmology into the weapon name without direct UNSLAAD evidence.

## Recipe progression

Both playable Earthbones weapon recipes are gated by the Hgmieil encounter family:
- **Earthbones Sword** — GetDeadCount on NPC 033BE44B / Hgmieil.
- **Earthbones Greatsword** — same Hgmieil death-count gate.

This is a progression relationship, not proof that Hgmieil historically forged or owned both weapons.
