# Dragon / Jill / Orkey staff reconstruction family

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains a late staff family whose lore relationship is expressed through names, item descriptions, placement, and recipe gating rather than enchantment records.

## Staff of the World-Eater

WEAP 034AF6DE / zzzCrbHm2StaffDragon

Description:
- Shouts are 50% more powerful.

Recipe unlock:
- The Final Journey completed.

Placed reward:
- chest in Frozen Abyss.

## Staff of Bormahu

WEAP 034AF6DF / zzzCrbHm2StaffDragonNest_Gold

Description:
- time between shouts reduced by 20%.

Recipe unlock:
- Arkved completed.

No direct source in this pass explains why the Arkved quest gates this reconstruction.

## Staff of the Jills

WEAP 034AF6E0 / zzzCrbHm2StaffDragonNest_Silver

Description:
- time between shouts reduced by 20%.

Recipe unlock:

Placed reward:

The exact Jill cosmology is not explained by this item alone, but the name strengthens Jill terminology as an intentional UNSLAAD node in addition to the Jill trial bosses.

Placed reward:

## Structural observation

The staff family uses direct item descriptions rather than EITM enchantments for its primary player-facing mechanics.

Lorekeeper should therefore preserve:
- name;
- description;
- placement;
- recipe gate

even when the WEAP has no enchantment FormID.

## Guardrail

The shared model/family and similar mechanics do not prove:
- Bormahu and the Jills are the same category of entity;
- Arkved is the source of either staff;
- Staff of the Jills implements DAc0da's temporal-censorship mechanics.

These remain retrieval links only.
