# Khemkel's Carving Knife

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

WEAP 033AEC33 / zzzCrbBjrnDagger

## Direct ownership

**Idolmaker Khemkel** directly carries the weapon.

Khemkel is encountered in:
- **Jhunal-Men's Deception**

and belongs to:
- Frost Power Excursion Faction;
- 500 Companion Faction.

## Item description

The knife is:
- **especially effective against Daedra**.

## Lore relationship

The Khemkel Insight material separately says the Idolmaker carved companions from Amora trees and used those forms to deceive Herma-Mora.

The direct-carried carving knife therefore reinforces Khemkel's authored role as an **Idolmaker/carver**, while its anti-Daedra description gives the tool a specific combat property.

## Boundary

The knife does not by itself prove:
- it was used to create every Khemkel idol;
- its anti-Daedra property enabled the deception of Herma-Mora;
- every carved body in UNSLAAD derives from this exact weapon.
