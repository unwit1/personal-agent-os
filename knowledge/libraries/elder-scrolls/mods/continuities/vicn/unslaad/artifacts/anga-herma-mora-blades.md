# Anga-Mora and Herma-Mora blade effects

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary acquisition: stronger Sinak/Toor death lists

## Anga-Mora Blade family

- Anga-Mora Blade
- Anga-Mora Greatblade

Enchant:
- **Absorb Magicka**

Custom effects:
- Drain Magicka
- Frost Damage

The stronger Rein-Sinak and Pogaan-Sinak branches yield Anga-Mora weapons.

## Herma-Mora Blade family

- Herma-Mora Blade
- Herma-Mora Greatblade

Enchant:
- **Absorb Stamina**

Custom effects:
- Absorb Stamina
- Poison Damage

Ru-Sinak Toor yields a Herma-Mora Blade.

## Significance

The equipment layer makes a deliberate mechanical distinction:

**Anga-Mora**
- Magicka drain
- frost

**Herma-Mora**
- Stamina drain
- poison

Both remain embedded in the Sinak/Owl/Woodland-Man network.

## Guardrail

Do not derive a full theological distinction between Anga-Mora and Herma-Mora solely from these damage types.

The effects are implementation evidence and a useful comparison signal, not a complete etymology or cosmology.
