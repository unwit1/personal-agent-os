# Elder Wood relic family

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

"Elder Wood" appears in both dialogue and a broad equipment family.

## Dialogue anchor

Orlando the Knowledgeable tells the player that he and the Black Owl come from:

**the far-off icy north, from the depths of a forgotten Elder Wood.**

The Hermit independently describes crossing frozen mountains in search of an **elder wood**, where priests prayed for the end of endless winter and a pilgrim's Shout revealed sunlight and a reflected golden dragon.

These are separate witnesses and should remain separately attributable.

## Snow Guardian Jokul

Snow Guardian Jokul directly carries the NPC form of:

**Elder Wood Greatsword**

in Frozen Cave.

## Elder Wood sword family

### Elder Wood Sword / Greatsword
Description:
- bashing places a **frost rune** on a nearby surface.

Reward placements include:
- Ysgrim's Seal;
- Frozen Abyss for a Sword placement.

### Elder Wood Axe / Greataxe
Description:
- especially effective against **undead**.

Reward containers appear across:
- present Unslaad;
- Northern Sanctuary.

## Shields

### Elder Wood Shield
Enchant family:
- Fortify Health Regeneration.

### Elder Wood Greatshield
Enchant family:
- Fortify Health Regeneration.

Both have reward placements spanning Unslaad and Northern Sanctuary.

## Elder Wood Wolf armor

The Elder Wood Wolf armor set is:
- stored in a Northern Sanctuary reward chest;
- also yielded by Hgmieil the Wolf Warrior's death-list family.

## Significance

The equipment family physically bridges:
- Jokul / Snow Guardians;
- Northern Sanctuary;
- Ysgrim's Seal / Atmoran trials;
- Hgmieil / Wolf material;
- Orlando's forgotten-Elder-Wood testimony.

## Guardrail

The shared "Elder Wood" name is meaningful retrieval evidence.

It does not prove that every item was literally made from one named tree or that every Elder-Wood reference describes the same exact location unless another record states so.

## Recipe progression

The Elder-Wood family is split across several progression gates:

- **Elder Wood Axe / Greataxe** — craftable after **Lingering Snow**.
- **Elder Wood Shield / Greatshield** — craftable after **Lingering Snow**.
- **Elder Wood Knight armor** — craftable after **Snow Guardian**.
- **Elder Wood Wolf armor** — craftable after **Hgmieil the Wolf Warrior** is dead.

This confirms Elder Wood as a broad recurring material/artifact tradition rather than one uniform reward set.
