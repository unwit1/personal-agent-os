# Merid / Magnar Lyg mirror

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary quest: The Final Journey

The late Atmoran quest uses a mirror beneath an ice seal as a functional route to **Lyg**.

## Herkel's description

Herkel says:
- the mirror is placed at the foot of the structure beneath the ice;
- it is an **imitation of Magnar**;
- it was made from a polished piece of **Merid-Nunda's essence**;
- even a dragon's Voice cannot break the mirror itself;
- the player should break the ice covering it;
- the mirror leads to the adjacent place called **Lyg**;
- another mirror connected to the original world can be used to return.

The quest objectives explicitly include:
- break the ice covering the mirror;
- bypass the seal of ice via the mirror.

## Merid's water mirror Insight

MESG 03416914 says:
- people shed dragon skins at the dawn of departure;
- those scales originate from an old kalpa;
- **Merid's water mirror reflects the dawn and serves as a reminder of the past**.

This is direct Insight text, but it does not explicitly say the Insight describes the exact quest mirror.

## Magnar mirror tradition

A separate Insight says Magnar fell at sunrise and became **replaced by mirrors**.

Harakk's note also invokes Magnar, decapitation, mirrors at sunset, and frozen-body warmth.

Together these sources create a deliberate Magnar/Merid/mirror cluster.

## Current classification

High confidence:
- the quest mirror is an imitation of Magnar;
- Herkel says it is made from Merid-Nunda's essence;
- it functions as a Lyg transit device.

Unresolved:
- whether it is literally "Merid's water mirror";
- how many such mirrors exist;
- whether mirrors are fragments, replacements, portals, memories, or several different categories;
- how the mirror mechanism relates to Radiance or Mnemo-Li.

## VMAD corroboration

The Final Journey's quest VMAD includes properties named **DoorLyg**, **Alias_IceMirrorMarker**, **Alias_LygMidMarker**, and **Alias_LygEndMarker**, alongside the ice-seal properties.

This is direct implementation evidence that the Lyg door and ice-mirror route are one deliberate quest mechanism.

## Identity closure

The quest mirror has three directly supported properties:

1. Herkel calls it an **imitation of Magnar**.
2. Herkel says it is made from polished **Merid-Nunda essence**.
3. VMAD and quest objectives establish it as the operational **Lyg transit mirror** beneath the ice seal.

The separate **Merid's water mirror** Insight is therefore a related mirror tradition, not a proven second name for this exact object.

For retrieval, merge the concepts but not the object identities:
- `quest Lyg mirror` = resolved implementation object/route;
- `Merid's water mirror` = separate Insight claim;
- `Magnar replaced by mirrors` = mythic tradition.

No additional object-identity claim should be made without a direct record link.
