# Dragonbone Smasher weapons

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains one-handed and two-handed **Dragonbone Smasher** weapons.

## Dragonbone Smasher

WEAP 0326574E

Description:
- **especially effective against dragons**.

Placed reward containers exist in:
- **Cave of Radiance**;
- **Lost Unslaad**.

Crafting unlock:
- **Long Winter** completed.

## Great Dragonbone Smasher

WEAP 03262F0B

Description:
- **especially effective against dragons**.

Placed reward containers exist in:
- **Mifa Xanadu's Forge**;
- **Lost Unslaad**.

Crafting unlock:
- **Long Winter** completed.

## Significance

The weapons are firmly attached to the future/Lost-Unslaad arc through:
- duplicated Lost-Unslaad placements;
- the Long Winter reconstruction gate.

The additional present-side locations give the family a wider temporal distribution.

## Boundary

Do not infer that Mifa Xanadu forged the entire weapon family simply because one Great Dragonbone Smasher chest is in her forge.

The direct evidence is placement plus quest-gated reconstruction.
