# Snake School Akaviri weapons

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD's future-Blades layer contains a three-weapon **Snake School** family.

## Snake School Tachi

Description:
- 5% chance to avoid all damage from a melee attack.

Direct carrier:
- **Akaviri Spymaster** in the future Training Room.

Reward placements:
- Pot House;
- Lost Unslaad / Blades Camp.

## Snake School Kodachi

Description:
- 3% chance to avoid all damage from a melee attack.

Direct carrier:
- **Akaviri Spymaster**.

Reward placements:
- Soul Gem Mine;
- Lost Unslaad / Blades Camp.

## Snake School Odachi

Description:
- 8% chance to avoid all damage from a melee attack.

Direct carrier:
- **Akaviri Dragonguard** in the future Training Room.

## Relationship to future Blades

Both named carriers belong to the future Akaviri/Blades combat layer indexed under **Blades Dragonslayer Faction**.

The weapons therefore provide a direct equipment tradition for that group rather than merely using Akaviri-looking models.

## Boundary

The ESM does not explain:
- the history of the Snake School;
- whether it is a formal Akaviri school outside this continuity;
- why avoidance percentages differ by weapon.

Treat it as an UNSLAAD-specific named combat tradition unless another source independently corroborates it.
