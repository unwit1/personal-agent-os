# Woodland Man weapon arts

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

Four Woodland-Man Knight weapons carry the direct VMAD-linked weapon art **Woodland Man's Whisper**.

## One-handed
- Woodland Man's Knight Sword
- Woodland Man's Knight War Axe
- SPEL 033B6711

## Two-handed
- Woodland Man's Knight Greatsword
- Woodland Man's Knight Battleaxe
- SPEL 033B6712

## Shared effect
Both arts use MGEF 033B906C / **Fortify Health Regeneration**.

This gives the Woodland-Man weapon family an explicit regenerative mechanical identity.

Do not automatically equate "Whisper" with Khenarthi's winds, Ja'cobee's passage, or word-walking without direct source evidence.
