# Spear of the Fox

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

Primary weapon:
- WEAP 0345653C / zzzCrbFoxSpear

UNSLAAD also contains armor/body-display records named **Spear of the Fox**, showing the spear as part of a visual/impalement state as well as a usable weapon.

## Item description

The weapon is:
- especially deadly to **Daedra and Hares**.

## Recipe gate

The playable Spear of the Fox becomes craftable after:

**Loveletter to the Fifth Era** / zzzCrbMq09

is completed.

## Related actor

UNSLAAD also contains:
- **Pelinaalilargus the Fox**

who has a custom Pelinal-prefixed Voice package and belongs to the late Atmoran trial complex.

The shared Fox naming is an important retrieval edge, but the ESM evidence in this pass does not directly state that Pelinaalilargus carried or created the spear.

## Interpretation boundary

Do not infer why completing Loveletter to the Fifth Era unlocks the spear unless a dialogue/script source explains that relationship.

The hard evidence is:
- Fox-named artifact;
- anti-Daedra/anti-Hare effect;
- post-Mq09 reconstruction gate;
- Fox/Pelinal actor elsewhere in the same late mythic layer.
