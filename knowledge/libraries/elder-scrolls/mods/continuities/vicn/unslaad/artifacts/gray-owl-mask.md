The direct enchantment effects resolve to Skyrim-master MGEFs in the supplied ESM; this pass does not guess their names.

## Worn by Jhunal the Gray

The mask is included in the default **Gray Owl outfit** used by multiple Jhunal-the-Gray forms:

- Bell-Tower ghost / Mq03
- Long Winter / Mq04
- Lingering Snow / Mq05

The wounded Mq05 Jhunal form uses a separate wounded Owl outfit that still contains the mask.

This is direct implementation evidence that the Jhunal-the-Gray presentation is visually encoded as Gray Owl.

## Crafting

A player reconstruction recipe exists for Gray Owl's Mask.

Its recipe unlock belongs to the late Gray-Owl progression rather than proving ownership of one historical physical mask.

## Identity boundary

Use the mask as evidence for:
- Jhunal-the-Gray <-> Gray-Owl representation linkage.

Do not use it alone to prove:
- one continuous physical body across all Owl forms;
- every wearer is literally the same person;
- the mask itself causes the identity.
