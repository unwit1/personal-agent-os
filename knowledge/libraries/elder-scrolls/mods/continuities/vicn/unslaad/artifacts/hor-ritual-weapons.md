# Hor Ritual weapons

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains two Hor Ritual weapons:

- WEAP 033EACEA — **Hor Ritual Sword**
- WEAP 033EACEB — **Hor Ritual Greatsword**

Both have the same direct item mechanic:

**weapon damage increases by 10% of the wielder's Restoration skill.**

They are not ordinary enchanted weapons in the EITM layer.

## Hor Ritual Sword

The Sword is yielded by the custom death list of:

**Kire the Heteromorph**
- Dragonord Gargoyle Race
- 500 Companion Faction
- placed in **Ysgrim's Seal**

Kire's death list also includes a **Draco-Nord Veddu** summon bone.

## Hor Ritual Greatsword

The Greatsword is yielded by:

**Eamal the Priestess of Arkayn**
- HH Dragon Race
- **Lyg Crab Faction**
- placed in **Coldsalt Ruin**

## Significance

The pair deliberately crosses two different encounter contexts:
- Kire / Draco-Nord / Ysgrim trial layer;
- Eamal / Arkayn / Lyg-Dreugh layer.

The shared Restoration-scaling mechanic and shared **Hor Ritual** title are stronger evidence of a common ritual-weapon family than either owner alone.

## Guardrail

The ESM does not directly define:
- "Hor";
- the ritual itself;
- why Restoration skill strengthens the weapons;
- whether Kire and Eamal belong to one religious order.

Treat **Hor Ritual** as a linked artifact tradition with unresolved doctrine.
