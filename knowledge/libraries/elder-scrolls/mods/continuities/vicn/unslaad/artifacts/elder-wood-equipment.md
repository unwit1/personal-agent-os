# Elder Wood equipment family

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains several distinct **Elder Wood** equipment lines rather than one single set.

## Elder Wood Axe / Greataxe

Descriptions:
- especially effective against undead.

Reward placements occur in both:
- present Unslaad;
- Northern Sanctuary.

Crafting unlock:
- **Lingering Snow** completed.

## Elder Wood Shield / Greatshield

Both use a named **Fortify Health Regeneration** enchantment family.

Reward placements again span present Unslaad and Northern Sanctuary.

Crafting unlock:
- **Lingering Snow** completed.

## Elder Wood Knight Sword / Greatsword

Descriptions:
- bashing places a **frost rune** on a nearby surface.

Enchant:
- Frost Damage family.

Reward placements:
- **Ysgrim's Seal**
- one Sword placement also exists in Frozen Abyss.

## Elder Wood Knight armor

Boots / Armor / Gauntlets / Helmet become craftable after:
- **Snow Guardian** quest completed.

## Snow Guardian Jokul

Jokul directly carries the NPC combat form of:
- **Elder Wood Greatsword**

This gives the Elder-Wood Knight weapon line direct ownership evidence inside the Snow-Guardian story layer.

## Elder Wood Wolf set

The Wolf armor family becomes craftable after:
- **Hgmieil the Wolf Warrior** is dead.

A Northern Sanctuary chest contains the set.

## Interpretation

"Elder Wood" is a broad equipment/material tradition spanning:
- anti-undead axes;
- health-regeneration shields;
- frost-rune Knight swords;
- Snow Guardian/Jokul equipment;
- Wolf-themed armor.

Do not collapse all Elder-Wood items into one historical owner or one exact enchantment mechanism.
