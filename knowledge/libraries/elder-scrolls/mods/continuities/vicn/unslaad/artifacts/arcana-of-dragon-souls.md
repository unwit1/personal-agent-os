# Arcana of Dragon Souls

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary record: ACTI 0311CC23 / zzzCrbTrgPourDragonSouls  
Display name: **Arcana of Dragon Souls**

## Direct implementation

The activator's use verb is **Pour**.

MESG 0311CC24 / zzzCrbMsgRequireDragonSouls requires **10 Dragon Souls**.

Long Winter includes the explicit objective:

**Pour 10 Dragon Souls into Ulliss**

## Narrative explanation

Jhunal the Gray tells the player:
- gather Dragon Souls to save Ulliss;
- pour them into her;
- her twisted life was originally born in that way.

Lingering Snow later gives his fuller account that Austella sealed dragon souls in ice and Jhunal shaped that ice into Ulliss.

## Relationship to other systems

This object belongs beside:
- Ulliss's early Dragon Souls conversion quest;
- the Ancient Dragon Ring / Twinkling Star revival system, which spends a Dragon Soul;
- Lizz's dragon-soul lineage;
- Jhunal's vessel experiments.

## Boundary

The ESM directly establishes the Arcana as a Dragon-Soul transfer mechanism.

It does not by itself establish:
- who created the Arcana;
- whether it is one of the Arcanas of the Owls;
- whether it is related to the Arcana of Magnus;
- whether Dragon Souls are being stored, transformed, or merely transferred.

Keep those as separate questions.

## Placed-reference context

REFR 0311CC26 places the Arcana of Dragon Souls in exterior CELL 03074401 of **Lost Unslaad** (WRLD 03064266).

This confirms that the soul-pouring mechanism is physically present in the future/Lost-Unslaad world layer used by Long Winter.
