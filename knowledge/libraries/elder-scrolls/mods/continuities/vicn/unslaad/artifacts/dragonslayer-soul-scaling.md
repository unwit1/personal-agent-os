# Dragonslayer armor and Dragon-Soul scaling

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Associated boss: Dov-Ah-Kiin

UNSLAAD's craftable Dragonslayer equipment converts the player's accumulated **Dragon Souls** into persistent equipment bonuses.

## Armor

**Dragonslayer Armor**
- enchantment: Dragon Bone
- effect: Dragon Bones
- +1% physical resistance per Dragon Soul
- maximum 50%

## Helmet

**Dragonslayer Helmet**
- enchantment/effect: Dragon Horn
- +1% magic resistance per Dragon Soul
- maximum 50%

## Boots

**Dragonslayer Boots**
- enchantment/effect: Dragon Feet
- +1% stagger resistance per Dragon Soul
- maximum 50%

## Ring

**Dragonslayer Ring**
- enchantment/effect: Dragon Eye
- +1% critical-hit chance per Dragon Soul
- maximum 50%

## Gauntlets

**Dragonslayer Gauntlets**
- enchantment: Dragon Scale
- effect: Reflective Ward
- creates a ward protecting against spells while blocking

Unlike the other set effects, the direct description does not state Dragon-Soul scaling for the gauntlets.

## Greatsword

**Dragonslayer Greatsword**
- enchantment/effect: Dragonfire

The effect has no explanatory player-facing DNAM text in the supplied ESM.

Dov-Ah-Kiin directly carries the unplayable combat form and drops the playable form.

## Fang of the Old Dragon

Dov-Ah-Kiin's death list also yields **Fang of the Old Dragon**.

It uses:
- enchantment: Shout of Paarthurnax
- custom MGEF: zzzCrbMgeVoiceParrthurnax
- a voice-push effect script

## Narrative significance

Dragon Souls now have several independently implemented roles in UNSLAAD:

1. Ulliss converts Dragon Souls into player attributes/perk knowledge.
2. Ancient Dragon Ring + Twinkling Star spends a Dragon Soul to revive Ulliss.
3. Arcana of Dragon Souls consumes Dragon Souls for Ulliss.
4. Lizz's lineage is described through the player's dragon soul.
5. Dov-Ah-Kiin contains a colossal quantity of Dragon Souls.
6. Dragonslayer equipment becomes stronger according to the player's Dragon-Soul count.

This makes Dragon Souls a recurring material/resource substrate for body, power, identity, and inheritance rather than only Skyrim's normal Shout currency.

## Boundary

The equipment effects are direct mechanics.

They do not prove a universal metaphysical rule that every resistance/skill improvement in the setting is literally caused by soul assimilation.

## Recipe progression

The core Dragonslayer armor, boots, gauntlets, helmet, ring, and greatsword recipes are gated behind completion of **Lingering Snow**. The Dragonslayer Shield/Greatshield instead unlock after **Long Winter**.

This confirms that the craftable set is a staged reconstruction reward across the future/Dov-Ah-Kiin arc rather than one undifferentiated loot drop.
