# Staff weapon arts

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

## Staff of Bormahu
Direct VMAD spell: **Weapon Art: Circle of Bormahu**.
The custom effect radiates sunlight, creates a healing circle, and restores Health to caster and allies.

## Staff of the Jills
Direct VMAD spell: **Weapon Art: Circle of the Jills**.
The custom effect radiates **blue sunlight**, creates a healing circle, and restores Magicka to caster and allies.

This is the strongest direct mechanic currently attached to the Staff of the Jills. It does not establish that all Jills universally use this power.

## Staff of the World-Eater
Direct VMAD spell: **Weapon Art: Flames of Alduin**.
The custom effect explicitly describes an explosion of **Alduin's flames**.

## Staff of the Old Knocker
Direct VMAD spell: **Weapon Art: Blizzard**.
The custom effect summons a blizzard around the caster.

## Structural conclusion
The associations are real item-specific authored mechanics:
- Bormahu -> sunlight / Health
- Jills -> blue sunlight / Magicka
- World-Eater -> Alduin flame
- Old Knocker -> blizzard

They are not universal definitions of the named beings/groups.
