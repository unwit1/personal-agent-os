# GHARTOK weapons / GC9 placement

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains two named GHARTOK weapons:

- WEAP 034AF6DA — **GHARTOK Sword**
- WEAP 034AF6DB — **GHARTOK Greatsword**

## Item descriptions

GHARTOK Sword:
- power attacks against elves and dragons do 25% more damage.

GHARTOK Greatsword:
- power attacks against elves and dragons do 30% more damage.

Neither record carries an ordinary EITM enchantment; the anti-elf/anti-dragon behavior is expressed through the item's direct description/mechanics.

## Old City of Chains placement

The one-handed GHARTOK Sword is directly placed in **Old City of Chains**.

The GHARTOK Greatsword is stored inside the container:

**GC9: John Satisfaction**

also placed in Old City of Chains.

That same GC9 object is already linked to the Insight message describing:
- MEHRUNES activation;
- memory evaporation in the LYG sector;
- current-timeline disposal;
- contaminated-memory purging.

## Significance

This gives GHARTOK a hard physical relationship to the **GC9 / LYG / Old City of Chains** cluster.

The ESM does not provide ordinary explanatory dialogue defining the term GHARTOK or explaining why these weapons target elves and dragons.

## Guardrail

Do not infer from placement alone that:
- Ghost Choir 9 created GHARTOK;
- GHARTOK is a GC9 technology;
- the anti-elf/anti-dragon effect explains the LYG erasure event.

Current evidence is:
**GHARTOK weapons are deliberately staged inside the GC9/Old-City-of-Chains environment.**
