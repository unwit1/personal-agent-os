# Atmoran trial relics — direct carriers and reward forms

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

## Warrior of Stuhn — Dragon's Throat

Direct inventory:
- **Shield of Stuhn**
- **Atmoran Champion Sword**

Shield of Stuhn is enchanted with a Fortify-Stamina-Regeneration package.

## Remnant of Yngol — Ghostfire Forge

Direct inventory:
- **Yngol's Shield**
- **Yngol's War Axe**

Separate chest in Ysgrim's Seal:
- **Yngol's Battleaxe**

## Idol of Magnar — Frozen Tomb

Direct inventory:
- **Magnar's Warrior Spear**

Death list:
- recoverable Magnar-spear form;
- **Magnar's Warrior Helmet**;
- base-game items.

Dragon's Peak reward containers separately hold:
- Magnar's Warrior armor set;
- Magnar's Warrior Shield;
- Magnar's Warrior Spear.

## Last Giant of Jhunal — Ysgrim's Seal

Direct inventory:
- **Giant of Jhunal Club**

Death list:
- **Priest of Jhunal Mask**
- base-game materials/items.

The mask is enchanted with the named enchantment **Priest of Jhunal**.

## Hoary King Ysgrim / Ysgramorsbelt — Ysgrim's Seal

Hoary King Ysgrim directly carries:
- **Saarthal's Sorrow**

Its description says it is especially deadly to Daedra.

Ysgramorsbelt directly carries:
- two **Fists of Ysgramor**

Its death list yields:
- two **Storm Fists**
- recoverable **Saarthal's Sorrow**
- base-game items.

## Reading rule

The trial structure uses both direct actor gear and later recoverable/reconstructed reward forms.

For historical-lore retrieval, record:
- what the boss physically carries;
- what its death list yields;
- what nearby reward chests contain

as separate evidence types.
