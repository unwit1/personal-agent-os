# Firekeeper and Cannibal weapon families

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

Two distinctive weapon families are tied to late prison/Radiance encounters.

## Firekeeper weapons

### Firekeeper Sword / Greatsword

Description:
- bashing places a **fire rune** on a nearby surface.

Both use the same Fire-Rune weapon enchantment family.

Direct carrier:
- **Forgotten Prisoner** carries the NPC Firekeeper Sword in **Frozen Prison**.

Reward:
- Firekeeper Greatsword appears in a chest in **Frozen Tomb**.

## Cannibal weapons

### Cannibal Sword / Greatsword

Description:
- attacks cause extra **bleeding damage**.

Direct carrier:
- **Scab the Executioner** carries the NPC Cannibal Greatsword in **End of Radiance**.

## Significance

These families give direct equipment context to:
- Forgotten Prisoner / Frozen Prison;
- Scab the Executioner / End of Radiance.

The Cannibal weapon's placement beside End-of-Radiance / Scarab-Frame material makes it a valid contextual retrieval edge, but not a definition of Radiance.

## Boundary

Names such as Firekeeper and Cannibal are direct item labels.

Do not infer a formal cult/order or metaphysical category unless dialogue, faction, or other records establish one.
