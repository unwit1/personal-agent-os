# GHARTOK weapons

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains two named GHARTOK weapons.

## GHARTOK Sword

WEAP 034AF6DA / zzzCrbElinalSword1H

Description:
- power attacks against elves and dragons deal 25% more damage.

Placement:
- directly placed in **Old City of Chains**.

Crafting:
- becomes craftable after **VS Dreugh King** is completed.

## GHARTOK Greatsword

WEAP 034AF6DB / zzzCrbElinalSword2H

Description:
- power attacks against elves and dragons deal 30% more damage.

Acquisition:
- stored in the **GC9: John Satisfaction** container in Old City of Chains.

Crafting:
- becomes craftable after **VS Dreugh King** is completed.

## Direct magic-layer note

Neither GHARTOK record carries an ordinary EITM enchantment in the direct weapon layer. The anti-elf/anti-dragon behavior is expressed through the item's description/mechanics rather than a named enchantment FormID.

The Greatsword's GC9 container is the same implementation object tied to the LYG-sector memory-erasure Insight, strengthening the physical staging relationship without proving authorship or causality.

## Significance

The GHARTOK family therefore sits at the intersection of:
- Old City of Chains;
- the implementation-tagged GC9 object;
- the Khev/Dreugh King encounter;
- anti-elf / anti-dragon item effects.

This is a deliberate local relationship graph, not merely a borrowed weapon name.

## Boundary

The ESM does not explain the historical identity or etymology of GHARTOK in ordinary dialogue.

Do not infer:
- a specific external owner;
- direct equivalence to similarly named external texts/artifacts;
- that GC9 created the weapons;
- that Khev historically wielded them.

The hard evidence is placement plus post-Dreugh-King reconstruction gating.
