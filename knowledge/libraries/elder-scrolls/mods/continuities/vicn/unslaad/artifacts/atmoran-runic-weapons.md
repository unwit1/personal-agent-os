# Atmoran Runic weapon family

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains several **Atmoran Runic** weapons spread across its late trial actors.

## Axe branch

### Atmoran Runic War Axe
Description:
- critical-hit chance +15%.

Direct carrier:
- **Idolmaker Khemkel** in Jhunal-Men's Deception.

### Atmoran Runic Greataxe
Description:
- critical-hit chance +25%.

No unique direct historical owner is established in the current placement graph.

## Sword branch

### Atmoran Runic Sword
Description:
- critical-hit chance +15%.

Direct carrier:
- **Oracle-Oocyte Nem-yet** in Ysgrim's Oracle.

### Atmoran Runic Greatsword
Description:
- critical-hit chance +25%.

The current direct ownership pass does not identify one unique named carrier for the playable form.

## Significance

The Runic family physically links:
- Khemkel / Jhunal-Men's Deception;
- Ysgrim's Oracle;
- the broader 500-Companion/Atmoran trial layer.

Its repeated critical-hit mechanics distinguish one-handed and two-handed forms but do not define what makes the weapons "Runic."

## Guardrail

Do not assume that every Atmoran Runic weapon:
- uses Jhunal's secret runes;
- was forged by Khemkel;
- belongs to one historical arsenal.

Those would require direct source confirmation.
