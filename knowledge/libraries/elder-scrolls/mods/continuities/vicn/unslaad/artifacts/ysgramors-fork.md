# Ysgramor's Fork

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

WEAP 034286DF / zzzCrbYsgrimFork
Display name: **Ysgramor's Fork**

## Direct item property

Description:
- **Especially deadly to elves.**

The weapon is unenchanted in the ordinary EITM layer; the anti-elf behavior is represented directly by its item mechanics.

## Acquisition relationship

The custom death list for **Bjorga-mawr the Leopard** includes:
- Ysgramor's Fork;
- the Atmoran Warrior armor set.

Bjorga-mawr belongs to the 500 Companion Faction / late Atmoran trial structure.

## Interpretation

The item deliberately invokes the traditional Ysgramor-versus-elves motif through a blunt gameplay effect.

However, the ESM does not establish that:
- this was a historical utensil/weapon personally carried by official-canon Ysgramor;
- Bjorga-mawr historically inherited it;
- the Fork belongs to the same physical artifact chain as Saarthal's Sorrow.

Treat it as an UNSLAAD trial/reward relic bearing the Ysgramor name.

## Recipe gate

The playable reconstruction recipe unlocks after **VS Leopard** / zzzCrbBqYsLeopard is completed.

The weapon's direct description says it is **especially deadly to elves**.

This gives Ysgramor's Fork a clear post-Leopard-trial progression relationship inside the broader Ysgrim/Atmoran complex, without proving that Bjorga-mawr historically owned or created it.
