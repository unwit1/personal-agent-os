# Jill equipment profile

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary trial location: Ysgrim's Seal

UNSLAAD uses several distinct Jill-named equipment families.

## Void Jill

### Void Jill Battleaxe
Elja the Void-Jill directly carries an NPC combat form.

Playable Battleaxe description:
- **Shock spells and Shouts are 30% stronger.**

### Void Jill Axe
A reward chest in Ysgrim's Seal contains the one-handed Void Jill Axe.

Description:
- **Shock spells and Shouts are 25% stronger.**

### Void Jill armor family
The ESM contains:
- Boots
- Armor
- Gauntlets
- Mask
- Hood
- Cloak

These are post-encounter reconstructable gear forms.

## Thunder-Scale

### Borgasa the Thunder-Shield
Directly carries:
- Thunder-Scale Greatsword

### Frekshild the Thunder-Shield
Directly carries:
- two Thunder-Scale Swords

A Ysgrim's Seal reward chest contains:
- Thunder-Scale Boots
- Thunder-Scale Circlet
- Thunder-Scale Armor
- Thunder-Scale Gauntlets
- Thunder-Scale Spear

Recipe progression is split across the two Jill trials.

## Staff of the Jills

Placed in Dragon's Peak and craftable after Arkved.

Direct item description:
- time between Shouts reduced by 20%.

VMAD weapon art:
- **Circle of the Jills**
- radiates blue sunlight;
- restores Magicka to caster and allies.

## Shared mechanical profile

Across Jill-named equipment, UNSLAAD repeatedly emphasizes:
- Shock
- Shouts / Voice
- blue-sunlight Magicka support

This is a genuine authored mechanical cluster.
