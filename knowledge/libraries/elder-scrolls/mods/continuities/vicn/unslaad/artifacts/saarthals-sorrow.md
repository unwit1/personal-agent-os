# Saarthal's Sorrow

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD contains two forms of **Saarthal's Sorrow**.

## Hoary King Ysgrim

Hoary King Ysgrim directly carries the NPC combat form in **Ysgrim's Seal**.

This is a direct carrier relationship.

## Playable form

The playable weapon's description says:

**Especially deadly to Daedra.**

The direct EITM layer is unenchanted; the special anti-Daedra behavior is represented through item mechanics rather than a standard enchantment FormID.

## Recipe gate

The playable reconstruction recipe is gated by:

**GetQuestCompleted — VS Ysgramor**

This makes Saarthal's Sorrow a post-Ysgramor reconstruction reward.

## Significance

The artifact sits at the intersection of:
- Ysgrim/Ysgramor;
- Saarthal;
- anti-Daedra weaponry;
- the Atmoran trial complex.

UNSLAAD separately contains major Jhunal/Saarthal/Magnus-fire traditions, but this weapon is not directly identified as the same Saarthal artifact involved in the Magnus theft.

## Guardrail

The shared place-name **Saarthal** is a retrieval link, not proof that:
- Saarthal's Sorrow is the Arcana of Magnus;
- it was present during the Night of Tears;
- Hoary King Ysgrim historically forged or owned it outside the trial representation.
