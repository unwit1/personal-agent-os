## Significance

This is the strongest direct item-level connection yet between the active Gray-Owl/Jhunal representation and a **Magnus-named artifact**.

It complements, but does not replace, the dialogue claim that Jhunal stole an Arcana of Magnus from Saarthal.

Do not automatically equate:
- Shield of Magnus;
- Arcana of Magnus;
- fire of Magnus;
- Eye-of-Magnus assets.

They are linked retrieval nodes, not proven identical objects.

## Darkspeaker effect

The Darkspeaker Ring's custom MGEF states that Destruction spells become **50% stronger** while the wearer is **50% weaker to Destruction spells**.

This direct high-risk/high-output effect is part of the Gray-Owl/Jhunal reward set.
