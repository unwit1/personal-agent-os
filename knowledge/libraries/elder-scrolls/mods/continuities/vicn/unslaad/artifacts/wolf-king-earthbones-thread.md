# Wolf King / Earthbones / Hgmieil reconstruction thread

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

Two distinct weapon families are mechanically keyed to the Hgmieil encounter complex.

## Wolf King's Sword

Direct carrier:
- **Hgmieil the Wolf Warrior**
- location: **Wolf's Hearth**

Description:
- reduces damage taken by 10% while accompanied by a follower;
- increases damage taken by 10% when alone.

Crafting:
- gated by GetDeadCount on **Hgmieil the Wolf Warrior**.

Combat/NPC versions of Earthbones weapons are also carried broadly by Draco-Nord and Pseudo-Man forms in Old City of Chains / Coldsalt Ruin.

## Interpretation

The source establishes two separate Hgmieil-linked reconstruction traditions:
- Wolf King weapons;
- Earthbones weapons.

It does **not** say the two weapon families are the same artifact lineage or that Hgmieil is literally the Wolf King.

The safest model is:
- Hgmieil encounter/state unlocks both families;
- Wolf King gear has direct Hgmieil-the-Wolf-Warrior ownership evidence;
- Earthbones gear is additionally embedded in the Draco-Nord/Pseudo-Man combat population.
