# Ancient Dragon Ring and Twinkling Star

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

## Direct records

- MISC 030576E3 / zzzCrbItemDragonRing — Ancient Dragon Ring.
- ARMO 03061A4B / zzzCrbRingAncientDragon — Ancient Dragon Ring. Its DESC states that faint dragon power remains in the ring and that it seems connected with the Twinkling Star.
- ACTI 0311023E / zzzCrbMq03TriggerTwinkleStar — Twinkling Star.
- MESG 0311023F / zzzCrbMsgResurrection — states that the Twinkling Star is attuning to the ring and offers to spend one Dragon Soul to revive Ulliss.
- MESG 03110240 / zzzCrbMsgTimeWarp — states that the Twinkling Star is attuning to the ring and offers to open the portal of Time.
- QUST 03112A83 / zzzCrbMq03 — When You Wish Upon a Star; objectives include getting the Bell Tower key, exploring the Bell Tower, and wishing upon a star.

## Dialogue evidence

Ulliss says the ring has a draconic presence. Fluffy dismisses it as old-fashioned and apparently worthless. Aisha says it smells bad and claims no knowledge of it.

## Claim boundary

The ESM directly establishes ring ↔ Twinkling Star attunement, Dragon Soul-powered revival, and a time-portal function. It does not yet establish who created the ring, why the Star responds to it, or whether the Star is an astronomical body, artifact, mechanism, or localized manifestation. Those questions remain open pending quest fragments, references, later dialogue, and scene evidence.
