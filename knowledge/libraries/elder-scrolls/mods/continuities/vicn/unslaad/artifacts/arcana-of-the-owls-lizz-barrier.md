# Arcana of the Owls — Lizz barrier

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary source: Lingering Snow / INFO 0318C89A

Jhunal the Gray identifies the barrier protecting Lizz as **one of the Arcanas of the Owls**.

He says:
- it was left to the priestesses of Kyne;
- it can be observed but not normally breached;
- destroying its keystone allows entry.

## Significance

This is direct evidence that **Arcana** is not a one-off name for a single relic.

UNSLAAD presents the Owls as having a plural magical/technical tradition whose works can be transmitted to other groups, including priestesses of Kyne.

## Relationship to other Arcana

Separate currently identified nodes:
- Arcana of the Owls — barrier around Lizz;
- Arcana of Dragon Souls — direct activator used to pour souls into Ulliss;
- Arcana of Magnus — Saarthal/Magnus fire Jhunal says he stole.

The ESM does not yet say these are interchangeable components of one system.

## Cross-VICN

VIGILANT and GLENMORIL independently contain Jhunal/Owl Arcana language and related Saarthal/Owl traditions.

Cross-retrieve them, but do not flatten all Arcana into one artifact.
