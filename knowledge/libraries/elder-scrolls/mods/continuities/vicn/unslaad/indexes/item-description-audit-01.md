# Item-description audit 01

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

This pass audits embedded item-description text outside the BOOK/MESG layers.

## Coverage

- ARMO records with DESC: **5**
- WEAP records with DESC: **55**

Most WEAP descriptions are gameplay-effect descriptions. The strongest new narrative lore is concentrated in three Gray-Owl ARMO records.

## WEAP description classification

Of the 55 WEAP descriptions, the overwhelming majority describe combat mechanics:
- effectiveness against dragons/undead/Daedra;
- critical-hit chances;
- rune-on-bash effects;
- armor penetration;
- shout modifiers;
- resistance bonuses.

The one description with clear narrative wording is:

### 03175D0A — Fang of the Old Dragon
> Remember the Voice of the old dragon and push away everything standing in your way.
