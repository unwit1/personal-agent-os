## Pus / World-Eater family

Many pus creatures share a standard death list containing:
- Dovahshit;
- dragon remains;
- Frozen Void leveled food.

Named bosses can add unique gear such as:
- Festering Sword / Greatsword;
- Golden Pus;
- Festering Lesion.

## Method rule

Death lists establish configured loot, not necessarily literal historical possession.

A boss can:
- carry one unplayable combat form;
- drop a different playable form;
- unlock craftable reconstructions;
- share a death list with unrelated training/test actors.

Keep these evidence types separate.
