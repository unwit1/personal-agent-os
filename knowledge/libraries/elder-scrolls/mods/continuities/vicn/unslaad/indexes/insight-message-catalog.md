## Retrieval rule

Insight messages are **direct plugin text**, but many are mythic, fragmentary, hostile, damaged, or stylized.

Lorekeeper should distinguish:
- record existence and exact vocabulary;
- in-world testimony/myth;
- reused apocryphal fragments;
- later synthesis.

No Insight should be promoted to neutral cosmological fact solely because it is delivered through the collectible system.
