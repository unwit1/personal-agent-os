## High-value taxonomy observations

### Half-Dragon lineage/body states
- adult Half-Dragon Race: Ulliss, Austella, Hoarfrost Witch proxy;
- Half-Dragon Child Race: Lizz;
- Ulliss's dragon form instead uses Skyrim DragonRace and a one-member Ulliss Dragon Faction.

This is strong body/form implementation support for the Ulliss-Lizz lineage without making race alone a complete genealogy.

### Owl identity is not race identity
- secret Gray Owl uses Gray Owl Race;
- Black Owl uses Black Owl Race;
- human-looking Jhunal-the-Gray forms use a Skyrim/base humanoid race while joining Owl Faction.

Therefore Owl identity spans multiple race/body implementations.

### Jill transformations reuse Draco-Nord body technology
Eljaalithathisalif, Borgaasaalthoom, and Fraalxshildadoon use the same Dragonord Gargoyle Race as named Draco-Nord forms.

This is a direct shared body implementation, not proof that Jills are Draco-Nords.

### Manakin is technical, not a species
Manakin Race contains narratively unrelated figures including Clone of Ayrenn, Hermit, Jokul, Magnar idol, Mnegmegh, Atmoran Idol, Domhnall, Forgotten Prisoner, and Scab.

It is clearly a model/behavior container rather than one biological species.

### Summoned-versus-hostile forms
Sinak, Hor, Watcher, Draco-Nord, Hoarfrost, Atmoran Bear, and Storm-Bear summons commonly reuse the same custom race as hostile forms while dropping hostile faction membership.

Race continuity therefore often means **same creature archetype/body**, not same individual.

## Retrieval rule

Use custom race as one evidence edge among:
- FormID/EditorID;
- faction;
- quest alias;
- placement;
- dialogue;
- ability package;
- summon chain;
- death list.

Never merge identities from race alone.
