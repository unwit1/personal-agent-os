# Radiant and repeatable quest dialogue — completeness pass

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

This pass audits the remaining dialogue-bearing repeatable quest families so Lorekeeper can distinguish local worldbuilding from unresolved narrative backlog.

## Lost Kanra

Client: Lord of Over Here.

Local rescue quest for missing Kanra. No major metaphysical claims.

## Ice Tree

Client: Naghu the Gardener.

Supports the already-indexed ice-tree ecology and recurring need to remove new growth.

## Ember of Atmora

Client: Mifa Xanadu.

Mifa identifies the Ember as:
- a fire brought from Atmora by the Dragon Cult;
- said to be a piece of fire created by a dragon as large as a mountain;
- possibly once lit in Saarthal;
- lost after the Night of Tears;
- perhaps still present in ruined temples across Skyrim.

This is the highest-value historical claim in the radiant set and has its own concept page.

## Dregs of Alduin

Client: Wulf the Veteran.

The player hunts a black shadow seen flying among the clouds. The quest title calls the target **Dregs of Alduin**; Wulf's ordinary dialogue does not explain its ontology.

## Picture Book

Client: Lizz.

Lizz asks for and treasures a Dovah picture book. Relationship/character flavor; no new cosmological claim.

## Rare Coin

Client: Fluffy.

Fluffy describes the **Twin Dragons coin** as a gold coin with a dragon on both sides, considered lucky and popular with collectors. A coin with an emperor on both sides is said to be unpopular because it is associated with civil war.

Treat this as local cultural/collector testimony.

## Intruder

Client: Aisha.

Aisha says black-sludge-covered intruders appear from nowhere periodically and that she normally removes them herself.

## Amaranth Flower

Client: Ulliss.

Ulliss says Amaranth flowers:
- never fade or wither;
- once bloomed widely in the garden;
- disappeared as the land froze.

When the player returns one, she says her mother would be pleased.

Do not equate the local flower automatically with the metaphysical Amaranth merely from the shared name.

## Infected

Client: Snow Guardian Jokul.

Jokul says a strange worm made from Atmoran ice burrows into Frost Atronachs and drives them mad. He says the underlying cause has already been dealt with and the damage is declining.

## Briarheart Brandy

Client: Mudcrab Baron.

Local item-retrieval dialogue only.

## Rogue Rat

Client: Rat Chef.

Describes a recurring tribe of rats that work briefly and then flee with the gold.

## Memento Collection

Client: Pot Baba.

Pot Baba suggests some mementos may not yet be visible to the player. This may be presentation/gameplay language; do not promote it into memory metaphysics without further evidence.

## Raging Pot

Client: Soul Gem Pot Merchant.

Local repeatable incident; no major lore conclusion.

## Overall classification
