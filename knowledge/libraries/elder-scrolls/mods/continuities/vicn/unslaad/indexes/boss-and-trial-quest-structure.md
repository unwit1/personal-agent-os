## Handling rule

Named bosses are valid UNSLAAD entities and retrieval nodes, but a name/title alone does not establish a biography, divine identity, or historical claim.

Future expansion should prioritize placed references/cells, death items/artifacts, spells/effects, scripts/properties, and explicit links from Herkel/Khev/Jhunal dialogue.
