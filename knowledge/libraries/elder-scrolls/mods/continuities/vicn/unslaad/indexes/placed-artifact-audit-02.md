Khev combat form in Old City of Chains directly carries **Muatra**.

Khev hologram/source forms in Coldsalt Ruin also carry the combat-form Muatra.

This is separate from the **Short Muatra** stored with the Vivec/Molag-Bal material in Hermit's Tower.

## Reading rule

Physical placement answers **where the player encounters a specific implementation of an item**.

Keep it distinct from:
- historical provenance;
- death-list drops;
- recipe unlocks;
- shared display names;
- symbolic association.
