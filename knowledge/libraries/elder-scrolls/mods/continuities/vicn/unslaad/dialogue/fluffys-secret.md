# Fluffy's Secret — Twinkling Star / Egg memory

Quest: zzzCrbSq02 / 0305268B
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

## Speaker

Quest alias Kedama resolves to NPC 03052687 / zzzCrbKedamaSenpai_Mq03, the Fluffy quest variant.

## Twinkling Star

Fluffy says:
- the Twinkling Star is his greatest treasure;
- he found it hidden at the feet of the courtyard statue;
- looking at it feels reassuring and makes pieces of his former life return.

This places the Twinkling Star in Fluffy/Ja'cobee's personal memory network before its later Dragon Ring/time/resurrection use.

## Recovered memories

Looking at the Star makes Fluffy remember:
- snowmen;
- piles of gold;
- an Egg.

He thinks the snowman with ears may represent himself and the other may be a child. He believes he made an important promise to that child but cannot remember it.

Gold makes him feel "as if all is forgiven." He says he wants to keep piling gold until it reaches Aetherius.

## Egg

When asked about the Egg, Fluffy says:
- he is afraid of the round Egg;
- he wonders whether he died because of it;
- he jokes that perhaps he choked on a boiled egg;
- he begins to hear/remember cackling laughter;
- trying to remember who was laughing causes a headache.

The comic boiled-egg explanation is explicitly Fluffy's own attempt to rationalize a frightening, incomplete memory.

## Name / promise

When asked whether the Star can restore his name, Fluffy refuses to pursue it. He says perhaps coming to Unslaad was what he wanted before death and perhaps he was honoring a promise.

## Cross-VICN significance

GLENMORIL's Ja'cobee explicitly identifies himself as the Last Chick Trader and discusses the EGG, breaking it, its contents, dream cycles, sacrifice, and the Owl.

UNSLAAD now independently ties the Fluffy/Ja'cobee presentation to:
- an Egg;
- fear/death surrounding that Egg;
- a forgotten child/promise;
- guilt/forgiveness;
- the Twinkling Star as a memory trigger.

This upgrades Fluffy ↔ Ja'cobee ↔ EGG from a name-based hypothesis to a direct source-text continuity bridge, while still preserving the fact that Fluffy does not consciously remember the full mechanism in this scene.
