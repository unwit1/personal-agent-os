# VS Dreugh King — Khev ending dialogue

Quest: zzzCrbBqDreughKing / 03407436  
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

## Quest aliases

- Boss01 → NPC 03407434 — **Khev the Dreugh King**
- Boss02 → NPC 033D8514 — **Molag the Lord of Lies**

All five dialogue rows resolve structurally to **Khev the Dreugh King**.

## Final doctrine

Khev says:
- he alone can bind the listener;
- he wants to be remembered, but asks that his fate be forgotten;
- his voice is dead "like Lyg" and his syllables no longer echo through the sea;
- **Nu-Mantia** is "the slavery at the end of freedom";
- he is love;
- he will shed the language of love with his carapace;
- he now shelters a **cold flame**;
- the name of that warmth is **Molag**.

## Relationship to Sermon of Khev

The boss dialogue is not a separate doctrine; it sharpens the paradox already present in the holographic sermon:

- Loveletter layer: Nu-Mantia → Liberty / road to Liberty.
- Sermon layer: Nu-Mantia → binding, struggle, heaven, transcendence.
- Boss ending: Nu-Mantia → "slavery at the end of freedom."

This tension should be preserved rather than harmonized away.

## Molag

The quest also contains an alias named **Molag the Lord of Lies**, but the spoken lines are Khev's.

Khev's final phrase associates "cold flame" / warmth with the name Molag. The exact relation between Khev, the Molag alias, Molag Bal, and the theological language remains unresolved unless other records establish it.
