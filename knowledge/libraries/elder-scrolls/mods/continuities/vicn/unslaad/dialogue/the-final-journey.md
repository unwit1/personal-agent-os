# The Final Journey — lore-bearing dialogue pass

Quest: zzzCrbMq08 / 03465EBF
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

- Cleverman → 033F5019 — Herkel the Fool
- Boss → 03460DA1 — Pelinaalilargus the Fox
- Woody → 033F5012 — Oohoo'Laf the Woodland Man
- Arkved → 0333F0B9 — Arkved the Dreamer

## Atmora

Herkel identifies the location as the skies above Atmora and says everything below is frozen.

He repeatedly calls the player **Ysmir** and treats the player's Voice/Thu'um as the means of progressing through the ancient landscape.

## Jhunal seal / Lyg

Herkel says:
- the ice seal was placed by the giants of Jhunal;
- a mirror beneath the ice is an imitation of Magnar;
- the mirror was made from a polished piece of Merid-Nunda's essence and cannot be broken even by a dragon's Voice;
- it leads to an adjacent place called **Lyg**, where fate can be changed;
- the player should return by finding a connection to the original world and using a nearby mirror;
- worlds should not be mixed too freely;
- staying too long risks attention from "the Grabbers."

## Frozen tomb and Woodland Men

Herkel says the tomb was built by the combined Voice of mighty Ysgrim and the wisdom of the giants of Jhunal.

He says Herma-Mora's wicked offshoots, the **Woodland Men**, were sealed beneath the ice after defiling the woods of Atmora.

Later he says impossible memories and histories are spilling from Apocrypha and attributes the effect to Herma-Mora.

## Jhunal fire

Herkel says followers of Jhunal left a fire behind, calling it long-lost magic of Jhunal born from yearning.

He also recalls a tale that Ysmir the Forefather lit four braziers named:
- Past
- Present
- Future
- Eternity

and that when the path is lit and heroes fall, the Way of the Warrior opens.

## Memory concealment

Herkel remembers a strange boy, but his memory of the encounter blurs. He attributes this to Herma-Mora and says the Daedra can conceal memories and historical events that do not suit him.

He says exposure to daylight can make such concealment difficult to maintain.

This remains Herkel's testimony, but the quest repeatedly dramatizes unstable/forced memory.

## Pelinaalilargus / Hans the Fox

Herkel identifies the dragon-boss as Hans the Fox, a hero given the name **Pelinaalilargus** for his strength.

He says the words/name that were once a blessing have become a curse and asks the player to free the dragon.

Herkel also earlier addresses Lord Hans as someone praised as an incarnation of Shor.

## Dragonborn role

Herkel repeatedly challenges the player's relationship to the Dragonborn role:
- some heroes can deviate from fate;
- Daedra covet such heroes;
- many previous warriors/Dragonborn became intoxicated by power;
- the more the player devours, the stronger the player becomes;
- he warns it may ultimately be the player rather than Alduin who devours the world.

These are Herkel's moral/metaphysical interpretations, not objective mechanics.

## Ysgramor projection and end-of-eternity vision

Herkel encounters what he believes is Ysgramor and speaks as if he knew him personally.

He imagines the return of Ysgramor and Sovngarde's heroes and says at the end of eternity it may be people, not the dragon, who devour the world.

When the player calls the apparition a projection, Herkel becomes confused and says Saarthal still has not fallen in his prayers, then warns the player not to pray to Alduin.

## Oohoo'Laf the Woodland Man

Oohoo'Laf identifies himself as:
- one of the Woodland Men;
- born from the void of the Elder Tree;
- granted personality by Herma-Mora;
- permitted to call himself a Demi-Prince.

He says:
- water is memory;
- ice is therefore like a library to residents of Apocrypha;
- Woodland Men can walk through words and let their minds escape seals;
- his offshoots carried out work that enabled the player's arrival;
- one of his tasks is observing and guiding the player's fate.

## Dragon name: Ysmaalithax

Oohoo'Laf says Herma-Mora ordered him to hide the name of a certain dragon that the player is meant to inherit.

If the name is revealed, he identifies it as **Ysmaalithax**.

He says:
- Ysmaalithax is the name Ysgramor shed along with his scales;
- the name was later inherited by a young man called Miraak and was meant to be kept safe with him;
- the player is now inheriting the same name;
- the name should have been forgotten by history lest the bearer be forgotten with it.

These are extremely high-value UNSLAAD continuity claims, but they remain Oohoo'Laf's testimony until corroborated elsewhere in the mod.

## Black Owl relationship

The player directly accuses Oohoo'Laf of being behind the Black Owl.

Oohoo'Laf does not simply identify himself as the Black Owl. Instead he explains how minds/offshoots can pass through seals and says his offshoots have done their work.

Lorekeeper should therefore record **Black Owl ↔ Woodland Man/Oohoo'Laf association** without collapsing every Black Owl manifestation directly into Oohoo'Laf's body.
