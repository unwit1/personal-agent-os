## Black Owl

INFO 031E2667 / 031E2668:
- the Black Owl says the Gray Owl "got his wish at last";
- calls the result a happy ending.

When challenged that the Gray Owl never became a dragon, the Black Owl distinguishes **dream** from **wish**:
- becoming a dragon was his dream;
- he knew it could never happen;
- the player nevertheless helped fulfill his wish.

INFO 031E266C:
- the Black Owl says a key leads "to the truth";
- says the player should entrust that truth to the future, as others did before;
- urges the player to dream with them.

The Black Owl also says they will meet again elsewhere.

## Ja'cobee warning

A Ja'cobee talking-activator warns:
- "he" will take the player in;
- he took in others before and restarted his dream repeatedly;
- the player should end the dream for the final time;
- the Gray Owl should be lured to the fire and burned.

This establishes repeated dream cycles as authored quest content.

## Degraded Gray Owl speech
