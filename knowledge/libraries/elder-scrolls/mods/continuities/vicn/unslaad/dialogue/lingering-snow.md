## Lizz and Ja'cobee

Lizz introduces herself as a little Dovah with horns and a tail. She says her name was the first and last present from her mother.

INFO 0317FE92 — Ja'cobee:
- Lizz was born after the player left; Ulliss later died.

INFO 0317FE95 — Ja'cobee:
- Ja'cobee says Lizz is Ulliss's child and that he personally saw her birth in a shining light.

INFO 0317FE97 — Ja'cobee:
- Lizz initially spoke an unknown language that Ja'cobee suspects may have been dragon language.
- A single word pushed him away.
- Ja'cobee taught her another language and says she now speaks Cyrodiilic.

Lizz says Ja'cobee taught her language, games, and stories about her mother and Aisha.

## Jhunal and the distant future

INFO 0318C886 — Jhunal the Gray:
- says he waited for the Dragonborn for over a thousand years.

INFO 0318C888 — Jhunal the Gray:
- says he deliberately set the Blades on Unslaad and inferred the player would return whenever the land was endangered.

This strengthens the future chronology established in Long Winter and changes the Blades-incursion claim from mere causal speculation: in this later dialogue Jhunal explicitly takes responsibility for setting them on the land.

## Alduin gate / world-poison claim

INFO 0318C88B — Jhunal the Gray:
- calls the northern breach a gate to Alduin the World-Eater;
- calls the material spilling from it Alduin's pus;
- describes it as a poison of the world left behind by Akatosh;
- associates it with chaos, sleep, and old age.

These are Jhunal's metaphysical statements and remain attributed testimony.

## Owls, Saarthal, and the Night of Tears

INFO 0318C88E — Jhunal the Gray:
- when asked where the other Owls are, says he has not seen them since Saarthal during the Night of Tears.

This is direct UNSLAAD evidence placing Jhunal and other Owls together at Saarthal during that event.

## Ulliss's origin

INFO 0318C891 — Jhunal the Gray:
- says Austella sealed dragon souls in ice;
- says he shaped that ice into human form;
- claims Ulliss was neither dragon nor human but ice;
- says Ulliss gradually lost the human form and manifested a dragon form while growing;
- treats the result as a failed experiment;
- says Austella's attempt to resist the change included making the doll.

The construction mechanism is direct dialogue from the character who claims to have performed it. The wording remains Jhunal's account rather than omniscient narration.

## Lizz's lineage and vessel status

INFO 0318C894 — Jhunal the Gray:
- calls Lizz a successor made by Ulliss;
- says some of the player's dragon soul is inside Lizz;
- says that makes Lizz, "in a way," the player's child as well;
- treats her as research material.

INFO 0318C897 — Jhunal the Gray:
- says he wants Lizz as a vessel because she contains part of the player's dragon soul.

These claims connect Lizz directly to the player's earlier Dragon-Soul-powered restoration of Ulliss.

## Owl Arcana / barrier

INFO 0318C89A — Jhunal the Gray:
- describes the barrier protecting Lizz as one of the Arcanas of the Owls;
- says it was left to the priestesses of Kyne;
- says the barrier can be observed but not breached normally;
- says destroying its keystone would allow entry.

This is direct evidence for a named Owl magical tradition and a Kyne-priestess transmission route.

## Arcana of Magnus

If spared, Jhunal offers "the knowledge of Magnus."

INFO 03196A0A:
- Jhunal calls the power an Arcana of Magnus that he stole from Saarthal.

Subsequent scene lines call it the fire of Magnus / ancient fire and describe it as warm but spreading death.

This strongly parallels existing VIGILANT material in which Berich says Jhunal stole a fire rune / Wisdom of Magnus from Saarthal. The two works independently preserve the same core claim inside the VICN continuity.

## Jhunal's ending and Dov-Ah-Kiin

In wounded dialogue, Jhunal says:
- he still cannot become a dragon despite all he has done;
- the world is ending and no one will remember it;
- he will serve the Owls one last time under the pledge of his mask;
- the Dragonborn's blessing is a curse that binds the Dragonborn to fate;
- the true ruin is Dov-Ah-Kiin, which he calls the fate of all dragon slayers.

The quest objective then explicitly requires:
- "See one of the fates of the Dragonslayer"
- "Defeat Dov-Ah-Kiin"

NPC 03161AB6 / zzzCrbBoss03Dovahkiin is named Dov-Ah-Kiin.

No ordinary dialogue is attributed to this boss in this pass, so its exact ontology remains open.

## Returning Lizz

After Jhunal's defeat, Ja'cobee asks the player to take Lizz back to Ulliss. He says his intuition tells him the player is unbound from time.

During the farewell scene, Ja'cobee says there are two Ja'cobees and that the other one is already "on the other side" with Ulliss. Lizz is sent back through the player's route to the earlier time.

Ulliss accepts Lizz readily, saying she feels a bond with the child and will be her mother even if their relationship cannot be fully explained.

The quest completion text states:
"Lizz was finally able to meet her mother. Ja'cobee's promise has been fulfilled."

## Evidence boundary

This quest contains several first-person metaphysical explanations from Jhunal. Lorekeeper should distinguish:
- hard structural facts: quest aliases, named NPCs, objectives, actor presence, return-to-present sequence;
- direct testimony: Jhunal's claims about Alduin, Akatosh, Ulliss's construction, Owl Arcana, Magnus, and Dov-Ah-Kiin;
- interpretation: any attempt to equate Dov-Ah-Kiin with the player, a timeline double, or a universal Dragonborn destiny.
