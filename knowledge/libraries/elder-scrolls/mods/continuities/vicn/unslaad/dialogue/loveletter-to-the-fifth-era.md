# Loveletter to the Fifth Era — lore-bearing dialogue pass

Quest: zzzCrbMq09 / 0347A32B
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

## Quest objectives

- Examine the ship's control unit
- Search the Cave
- Talk to Herkel
- Choose an offering to the Shadow of Shor
- Talk to Herkel
- Return to Skyrim

## Herkel's framing of the player

Herkel addresses the player as:
- Ysmir;
- King of Dragons and Men;
- a king without a throne on Nirn.

INFO 0347A354:
- Herkel says the player has regained the scales of **Ysmaalithax** and "became the dragon in truth."

INFO 0347A361:
- Herkel says only the **Prisoner** who has seen the world from outside can know its true form.

This is direct UNSLAAD dialogue and should be cross-indexed with Elder Scrolls Prisoner terminology, without treating Herkel as an omniscient narrator.

## Shor / creation burden

Herkel repeatedly compares the player's choice to Shor:
- closing the shadow's hole cannot heal Shor's wound;
- imitating Shor means continuing to suffer in the world;
- the player can choose to bear a creation-related burden too large for one mortal;
- Herkel calls this a doomed but loving choice.

## Time, inheritance, and Unslaad

INFO 0347A36F:
- Herkel says nothing changes while time continues to flow;
- at the end of time, someone will inherit the player's greatness;
- the player's act becomes a beacon;
- he calls it a fire of eternity that Ysmir showed "us," **Unslaad**.

INFO 0347A371:
- the current people are fated to be forgotten;
- what they leave behind may survive in another form.

INFO 0347A373:
- possible world endings named by Herkel include Alduin, the player, and Numidium;
- regardless of the ending, he says the player's legacy can guide later people.

## Forgetting

Herkel closes by saying the player's barren journey will last forever because everyone will forget the player, while calling the player a king among kings.

The quest therefore keeps the same memory/forgetting axis already present in the Gray Owl, Woodland Man, and Herma-Mora material.
