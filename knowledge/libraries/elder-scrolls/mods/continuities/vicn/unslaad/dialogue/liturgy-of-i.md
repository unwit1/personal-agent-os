# Liturgy of I — Loveletter / freedom dialogue

Quest: zzzCrbSq05 / 03376B99
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

## Structure

Quest objectives:
1. collect seven letter fragments;
2. return to the Hermit;
3. talk to the Hermit;
4. read the Loveletter;
5. talk to the Hermit;
6. a final hidden/unknown objective ("???").

The seven fragments are BOOK 03376B91 through 03376B97 and together reproduce sections of **Loveletter From the Fifth Era**.

Quest aliases distribute fragments among:
- Naghu the Gardener;
- Fluffy;
- Lord of Over Here;
- Snow Guardian Jokul;
- Rat Chef;
- Lizz;
- plus the Hermit and Black Owl.

Several holders explicitly say the pages arrived by wind. Jokul calls them carried by the winds of Kyne.

## Hermit's restoration

The Hermit says:
- the text is difficult and uses unfamiliar language;
- its style resembles Dunmeri but feels less old-fashioned;
- it is a "loveletter";
- after restoration the player should read it.

When asked who it was addressed to, he says he does not know:
- perhaps it is for the player;
- perhaps for someone the player has not met yet;
- whoever wrote it nevertheless "sent it to you."

This is the Hermit's interpretation of the apocryphal text inside UNSLAAD.

## Player-future responses

The quest gives the player several deliberately divergent answers about what to do after reading it:
- retire and leave the world-role to successors;
- continue travelling;
- become a pastry chef;
- keep seeking struggle;
- chase the Nerevarine;
- hunt Owls;
- do whatever one wants;
- decide that it does not matter.

The Hermit's replies repeatedly reject a fixed external mission.

Notably:
- "There is no mission assigned to you, after all. That is the meaning of freedom."
- "There is no greater meaning out there... it is all up to you."

These are explicit UNSLAAD thematic statements about post-quest player agency, delivered through the Hermit.

## Owls

If the player proposes hunting Owls, the Hermit calls them jealous bystanders and says they do not care about those who do not "gamble with their souls."

The Black Owl then re-enters the quest:
- asks whether the player has made a wish;
- says its Lord does not want the Owl simply to leave;
- asks only for a little of what falls through the player's grasp;
- says its wish lies at the bottom of the abyss;
- invites a later meeting in the land of dreaming.

## Provenance

The underlying Loveletter prose is reused apocryphal/developer-text material and must not be attributed to Vicn. What *is* UNSLAAD-authored evidence is:
- fragment placement;
- the wind/Kyne delivery framing;
- the Hermit's reconstruction and interpretation;
- the player's response choices;
- the Black Owl follow-up.
