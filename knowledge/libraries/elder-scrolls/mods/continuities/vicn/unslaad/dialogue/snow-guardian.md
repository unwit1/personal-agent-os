# Snow Guardian — dialogue and lineage corroboration

Quest: zzzCrbSq04 / 03364E2B
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary speaker: NPC 03364E28 — Snow Guardian Jokul

## Ice-spirit watch

Jokul says he has been assigned to watch a block of ice continuously.

He says:
- the object originally appeared to be ordinary ice;
- an ice spirit now lies there;
- ordinary weapons could not scratch the block;
- someone able to use the Voice might be able to affect it.

After the player uses the relevant Shout, Jokul calls the player a master of the secret art of the Voice.

## Austella

When told Austella has died, Jokul says he is both sad and happy for her because Unslaad had become like a prison and she has finally found peace.

## Lizz / player-parent framing

A player dialogue option says directly:

"Ulliss is doing well. We have a daughter now."

Jokul responds with congratulations and says his part in events has not been in vain.

This is important corroboration for Lizz's lineage. Earlier, Jhunal called Lizz "in a way" the player's child because she contains part of the player's dragon soul. Snow Guardian independently lets the player themselves describe Lizz as **our daughter** with Ulliss.

The exact biological/metaphysical mechanics remain unusual, but the narrative parental relationship is no longer dependent solely on Jhunal's interpretation.

## Ending

Once the ice-related task is resolved, Jokul says he can finally return to ordinary guard duties.
