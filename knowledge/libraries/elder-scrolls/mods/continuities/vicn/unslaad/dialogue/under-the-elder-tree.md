# Under the Elder Tree — dialogue and secret-story endpoint

Quest: zzzCrbMq10 / 0347A38C
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

## Quest objectives

- Talk to Woody
- Read Book
- Talk to Woody

The dynamic Book alias is directly configured with ALCO 0347A359:
**Ysmir the Forefather, Volume IV**.

The Woody alias resolves to NPC 03486E5B / zzzCrbHoholaughGoodEnd, the spared/final Oohoo'Laf variant.

## Woodland Man after release

Oohoo'Laf says:
- the player is cruel because freedom/exposure is a harsher punishment than expected;
- the hidden story must mean something to the player, which is why it was concealed;
- seeing the world again fills him with envy rather than the hatred he expected;
- while imprisoned he convinced himself the sacrifice of the Aedra was meaningless.

## Black Owl offshoots

INFO 0347A39C:
- Oohoo'Laf says his Black Owl offshoots are no longer under his control;
- he expects their plans to fail without him;
- he says Herma-Mora gave him an individual personality but that he was still a puppet;
- he expects the offshoots eventually to realize they are puppets too.

This is the strongest direct explanation so far of the Black Owl ↔ Woodland Man relationship:
the Black Owls are described as **Oohoo'Laf's offshoots**, but they can become operationally independent.

## Remaining Woodland Men

Oohoo'Laf says Ysgramor's warriors hunted down most of his brothers and that few Woodland Men remain; some have not yet "sprouted."

## Creation regret

Oohoo'Laf says that after seeing the world again he wonders whether he could have been part of it if Herma-Mora had participated in creation.

This is his personal reflection, not a neutral statement about Aedric/Daedric cosmology.

## Secret story / rebellion

Oohoo'Laf says he was ordered to keep a story secret but chooses to entrust it to the player as a small rebellion.

The quest then explicitly assigns the player **Ysmir the Forefather, Volume IV**.

After the player reads it, Oohoo'Laf says:
- understanding the story is necessary to "ascend into the sky";
- forgotten stars still shine;
- in time, the player's ladder will hang at the sky's rim.

These statements connect the book's Ysmir-to-Aetherius ascent story directly to the quest's closing symbolism.

## Evidence boundary

The quest gives a clear structural chain:
Oohoo'Laf secret → Ysmir the Forefather, Volume IV → discussion of ascent / sky / forgotten stars.

It does not explicitly identify the mysterious boy in the book, the single stone, or the exact metaphysical mechanism of ascent. Those remain open.
