# Sermon of Khev

Quest: zzzCrbqLessonKhev / 034073F0  
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

## Speaker / hologram structure

The quest uses five hologram aliases.

Implementation links:
- Holo00 and Holo01 use NPC 033D8503 — **Khev the Dreugh King** as their VTCK/source actor.
- Holo02, Holo03, and Holo04 use NPC 033D8508 / zzzCrbDreughVehkHologram02.

The opening hologram explicitly says:
- the Grabbers call him **"a mirror of Vehk"**;
- he therefore calls himself **Khev**;
- he was once a king, but those who called him king are dead.

Treat the sequence as Khev's holographic sermon/recording, while preserving the multiple implementation forms.

## Lost places

Khev names:
- Kuri;
- Djaf;
- Horma-Gile;
- Malbioge, called the old city of chains.

He says the first two were felled by red legions, Horma-Gile was crushed with coldsalt, and Malbioge is sinking into mud.

These names/claims are direct UNSLAAD material and need not be mapped to external geography until corroborated.

## Nu-Mantia

Every major sermon phase returns to the same "magic word":

**Nu-Mantia**

Khev associates it with:
- a final word;
- love and binding;
- hatred and unbinding;
- slavery and resistance;
- agony/spawning/charity;
- immortality beyond the world;
- the beginning of eternity.

## Love as chains

Khev teaches a deliberately paradoxical doctrine:
- love is violence;
- love is a chain that binds;
- those bound to him can reach heaven through him;
- domination and slavery are part of the test;
- one must not simply submit, because an obedient slave forgets the chains;
- the follower must struggle against the one they love/enemy who binds them.

The sermon therefore treats resistance to bondage as necessary rather than praising passive obedience.

## Lyg / Merid-Nunda

Khev says:
- those unbound by chains will "thirst like Lyg";
- his chains will reach **Merid-Nunda** and create a rainbow path;
- beyond that path lies a deep sea no light reaches;
- whoever is born from the bowels of **Lyg** will prove "our immortality."

These are Khev's doctrinal/metaphysical claims.

## Provenance links

UNSLAAD's embedded **Loveletter From the Fifth Era** independently uses **Nu-Mantia** as Liberty / the road to Liberty.

The shared term is a strong retrieval link inside UNSLAAD, but Khev's chain doctrine should not simply be treated as an authoritative explanation of the Loveletter's usage.
