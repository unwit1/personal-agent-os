# Watcher from the Third Era

Quest: zzzCrbSqMankar / 03411777
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary actor: NPC 033F2783 / zzzCrbWatcherMankar — Mankar Camoran's Eye

## Dialogue

Mankar's Eye calls the player an angel sent by Lord Dagon.

When the player asks who is watching, the Eye says:
- Dagon granted Mankar the Mysterium Xarxes;
- through the Xarxes, Mankar looked into this land and found the player;
- Mankar will remember what he sees;
- the player's blood will become his guidance and he will follow it.

He then says he will restore Tamriel to its "rightful place" and gives glory to the player and Dagon.

## Handling

This side quest deliberately creates a Third-Era observation link from Mankar Camoran to events/visions in UNSLAAD.

The lines are Mankar's own religious interpretation. The ESM supports the existence of the Mankar-Eye observer and Mysterium-Xarxes viewing claim inside the mod continuity; it does not turn that interpretation into official Oblivion-era canon.

Cross-index with:
- Mankar Camoran;
- Mehrunes Dagon;
- Mysterium Xarxes;
- temporal observation / future-past visibility;
- blood as guidance;
- later Herma-Mora / memory / history manipulation.
