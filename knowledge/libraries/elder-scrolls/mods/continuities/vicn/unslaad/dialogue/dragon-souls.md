# Dragon Souls — Ulliss conversion system

Quest: zzzCrbsq01 / 03022986  
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary source: translated Unslaad.esm 3.0.6

## Speaker resolution

All ten INFO rows in the quest use **GetIsID 030022F6**, resolving directly to **Ulliss**.

The voiced addon independently uses the FemaleYoungEager voice, but the GetIsID condition is sufficient structural attribution.

## Capability

Ulliss tells the player that she can turn a Dragon Soul into power.

The player can exchange a soul for:
- +10 Health;
- +10 Magicka;
- +10 Stamina;
- +1 Perk Point / "Knowledge."

For successful conversions Ulliss invokes the winds of Kyne.

## Lore significance

This system establishes, very early in UNSLAAD, that Ulliss can directly manipulate or transform **Dragon Soul power**.

Later layers then add:
- the Twinkling Star / Ancient Dragon Ring spending a Dragon Soul to revive Ulliss;
- Jhunal saying Ulliss was originally made from dragon souls sealed in ice;
- Jhunal saying Lizz contains part of the player's dragon soul;
- the player later calling Lizz "our daughter" with Ulliss.

The early conversion quest does not explain those later mechanisms by itself, but it establishes Dragon-Soul manipulation as a persistent property of Ulliss's story rather than a one-off late-game invention.
