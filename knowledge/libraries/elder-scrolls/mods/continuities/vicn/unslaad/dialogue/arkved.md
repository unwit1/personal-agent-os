# Arkved — generic-dialogue resolution

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Side quest: zzzCrbSq03 / 0333F0E0 — Arkved
Primary forms:
- NPC 0333F0B8 — Arkved the Dreamer
- NPC 0333F0B9 — Arkved the Dreamer

The dedicated side quest contains the Arkved actors and two named dragons, Uliizkaan and Maliizkrein, but Arkved's actual conversation is stored under the shared dialogue quest zzzCrbGeneric / 0302012D. The relevant INFO rows use GetIsID conditions against the two Arkved NPC forms, providing direct speaker attribution.

## First Arkved form — 0333F0B8

Arkved speaks of a promise that the player once made to him, or will eventually make.

When asked what fulfilling that promise requires, he says he is tired of constant nightmares and wants a peaceful dream.

The temporal wording deliberately makes the promise non-linear: "once made" / "will eventually make."

## Second Arkved form — 0333F0B9

After the nightmare sequence changes, Arkved says:
- the cold place has become warmer;
- the player will thaw the frozen dreams of the Atmorans;
- the player's breath will end the never-ending winter and bring spring.

When asked what Arkved has done in return, he says he led the player to "the secret."

## Dream / reality / memory

Arkved says it is irrelevant whether the land is dream or reality.

He explains:
- if a place is forgotten, it dissolves like morning fog;
- while it is remembered, its echoes remain;
- the place can now continue without the player because "that child" will laugh and sing it into existence;
- the player should sing their own self into the world rather than merely be sung to and eventually fade.

This is direct UNSLAAD testimony linking memory, song, identity, and persistence of worlds/stories.

## Atmoran abyss

Arkved says the abyss leads to sleeping Atmorans and their dreams:
- their bodies are covered in snow;
- their minds are submerged in a deep sea;
- the player should sing for them.

## Black Owl

Arkved says:
- the Black Owl waits at the bottom of the abyss;
- even the Atmorans could not satisfy its desire;
- the player cannot kill it, but it cannot kill the player either;
- beneath its attempts to twist/shatter the player, it is desperately pleading for help.

He then says:
"the Prisoner holds the reins of fate in their hands."

If the player chooses to hunt the Owl, Arkved answers that the player should "sing" and let three words guide them.

## Identity / shell

When directly asked whether he is really Arkved, he replies:
"Perhaps, as long as you call me that. Either way, this body is merely a shell."

The library should therefore keep the Arkved-name/body relation intentionally unstable rather than assuming the NPC shell is a simple persistent body.

## Oneiromancer

Asked whether he made a deal with the Black Owl, Arkved denies that explanation and says:
- the Black Owl was talking only to empty air;
- it does not have that kind of power;
- he made a deal with **the Oneiromancer**;
- she asked him to show the player around and collect payment.

## Cross-VICN bridge

GLENMORIL independently has a large Oneiromancer/Quagmire sequence in which Arkved is a direct participant:
- Romion says Arkved is a Third-Era figure imprisoned in nightmares by Vaermina;
- the group uses Arkved as a connection/key into Quagmire;
- GLENMORIL contains direct Arkved negotiation scenes and Zuzu/Oneiromancer material.

UNSLAAD's Arkved explicitly naming "the Oneiromancer" as his contracting party is therefore a strong direct continuity link between the two works, not just a reused Elder Scrolls character.
