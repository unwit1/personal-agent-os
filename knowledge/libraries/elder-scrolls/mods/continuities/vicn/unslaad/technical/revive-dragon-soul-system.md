# Revive — Dragon Soul resurrection system

Quest: zzzCrbSqResurrection / 0311023D
Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

## Quest aliases

- Ulliss → NPC 030022F6
- UllissHuman → NPC 0302525C
- UllissDragon → NPC 03025223

The quest itself contains no ordinary INFO dialogue.

## Direct message evidence

MESG 0311023F / zzzCrbMsgResurrection states:

"Twinkling Star is attuning to the ring. Would you like to spend 1 Dragon Soul to revive Ulliss?"

This directly establishes the gameplay/narrative transaction:

Ancient Dragon Ring ↔ Twinkling Star attunement → expenditure of one Dragon Soul → revival of Ulliss.

## Relationship to later lore

This deterministic implementation supports later dialogue:
- Jhunal tells the player to pour Dragon Souls into Ulliss;
- Jhunal later says Lizz contains part of the player's dragon soul;
- Snow Guardian gives the player the explicit option to call Lizz "our daughter" with Ulliss.

Lorekeeper should therefore distinguish:
- **hard implementation fact:** the resurrection system literally spends a Dragon Soul;
- **later interpretation:** how that soul becomes part of Ulliss/Lizz and what kind of parentage that creates.
