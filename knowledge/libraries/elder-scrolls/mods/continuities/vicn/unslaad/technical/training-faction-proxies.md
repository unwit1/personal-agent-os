## Significance

Training Faction is direct evidence that UNSLAAD intentionally creates reusable practice/proxy representations of major combat archetypes.

This is extremely important for Lorekeeper identity hygiene:

**Training proxy != historical/story individual.**

Shared:
- race;
- ability;
- weapon;
- AI

can identify an archetype without identifying a person.

## Rule

When a record belongs to Training Faction, default to:
- proxy;
- simulated/practice form;
- combat archetype

unless independent narrative records explicitly give it a story identity.
