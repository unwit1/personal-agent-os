Chapter 1: "Threats of Intimidation! Molag Bal Strikes Back"
After the battle, the Hortator is heading home on his siltstrider. Unfortunately, perhaps due to exhaustion, he collides with a black siltstrider. Vivec takes all the blame for this to protect the Hortator, and the owner of the black siltstrider, the King of Rape, Molag Bal, offers him the terms of settlement ... 
 
Chapter 2: "Muatra strikes back, Vivec gets CHIMed"
Molag Bal, the King of Rape, stops Vivec on a street corner in Ald Velothi. Although Vivec agrees to be CHIMed, it comes to a disagreement between him, who is still reluctant, and Molag Bal, who is unable to get the pictures that he wants. Molag Bal sneers: "Can't you even polish a spear?" And Vivec's anger becomes uncontrollable ... 
 
Chapter 3: "CHIM! And beyond, to AMARANTH ..."
Molag Bal leaves Vivec alone in the room. Despite being in a friend's room, Vivec indulges in some spear polishing, but the entire affair is captured by a soul gem Molag Bal planted in the room in advance. When Molag Bal returns, 
Vivec falls asleep after a night of drinking. As Molag Bal gazes into Vivec's sleeping face next to his, his imagination enters the world of AMARANTH ... 
 
Chapter 4: "The sleep of CHIM! Molag Bal turns into a beast"
One midsummer evening, Vivec is invited to Coldharbour, the domain of Molag Bal. After some idle conversation, Molag Bal asks Vivec whether he'd like to go to the Waterfront to get a tan. Vivec readily agrees, and together they strip naked and begin to sunbathe there. However, this turns out to be naught but an elaborate plan of Molag Bal to attack Vivec, for whom he secretly harbors feelings.
