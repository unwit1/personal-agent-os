## Narrative relationship

Long Winter / Lost Unslaad already frames the Blades as a future hostile force invading or threatening Unslaad.

This faction gives hard implementation cohesion to that force.

## Guardrail

Do not infer that:
- every member is from the same historical era;
- the Grandmaster is literally Delphine;
- the weapon name proves direct historical possession by Delphine;
- the training-room Akaviri actors are contemporaneous with the main invasion.

The faction establishes authored grouping, while chronology and identity remain source-specific.
