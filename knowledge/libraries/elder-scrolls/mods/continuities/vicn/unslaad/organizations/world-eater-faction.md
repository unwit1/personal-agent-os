# World Eater Faction — implementation membership

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
FACT 03095AAA / zzzCrbWorldEaterFaction  
Display name: **World Eater Faction**

The faction contains a broad set of hostile/custom actors, including:

- multiple Dragon Pus / Brendon / Dilon / Evenaar forms;
- Dregs of Alduin;
- Three-Headed Priest;
- Dilon Aisha;
- Clone of Ayrenn;
- Scab the Executioner;
- Pinwheel;
- Sacred Anatomancer;
- Dark Precursor;
- one active Jhunal-the-Gray form.

## Significance

This faction is a hard implementation relationship among many of UNSLAAD's corrupted, pus-associated, or late-hostile actors.

It is particularly notable that:
- **Dregs of Alduin** belongs to it;
- many pus creatures belong to it;
- the Mq05 Jhunal form belongs simultaneously to **Owl Faction** and **World Eater Faction**;
- Clone of Ayrenn and Sacred Anatomancer also belong to it.

## Guardrail

This is likely at least partly a hostility/combat-control faction.

Do not infer from membership alone that:
- every member knowingly serves Alduin;
- Clone of Ayrenn is cosmologically part of Alduin;
- Sacred Anatomancer worships the World-Eater;
- Jhunal's dual membership means the Owls and Alduin are one organization.

Use it as implementation context requiring narrative corroboration.
