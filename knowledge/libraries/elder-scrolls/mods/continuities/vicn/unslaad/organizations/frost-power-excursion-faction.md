# Frost Power Excursion Faction

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
FACT 03090973 / zzzCrbFrostExcursionFaction  
Display name: **Frost Power Excursion Faction**

This is a broad late-combat faction with more than fifty members.

Representative members include:
- Austella;
- Hoarfrost Knights and Guardians;
- Frost Lurkers / Frost Overlords / Frost Storms;
- Snow Guardian Uradech / Gartnait / Galam;
- Remnant of Yngol;
- Jill trial actors;
- Hoary King Ysgrim;
- Warrior of Stuhn / Tsun;
- Khev;
- Molag;
- Pelinaalilargus;
- Hgmieil;
- Idol of Magnar;
- Last Giant of Jhunal;
- many other Atmoran trial actors.

## Interpretation

The faction is much too heterogeneous to be a literal in-world organization.

Its strongest use is:
- hostile/combat-alliance grouping;
- late Atmoran/frost trial layer;
- encounter orchestration.

## Why it matters

Several characters who also have strong narrative identities belong here only in particular combat forms.

Examples:
- living Austella uses Frost Power Excursion Faction while ghost Austella uses UnslaadFaction;
- Khev/Molag combine it with Lyg Crab Faction;
- many 500-Companion trial actors combine both factions.

This makes faction changes useful **state/encounter evidence**.

## Guardrail

Do not use Frost Power Excursion membership to infer:
- shared religion;
- common ancestry;
- one historical army;
- literal frost-based metaphysics.

It is primarily technical encounter topology.
