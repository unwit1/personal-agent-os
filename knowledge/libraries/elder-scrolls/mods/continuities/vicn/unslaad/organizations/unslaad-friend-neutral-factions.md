# Unslaad Friend / Neutral factions

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

Two small factions organize much of the non-hostile local ecology.

## Unslaad Friend Faction

Members include:
- Mudcrab / Mudcrab Baron;
- Rat Chef;
- Snow Kanra;
- Pot Baba / Pot Brat;
- Ninawa / Anaku / Atta;
- Sina the Snow Mushroom;
- Soul Gem Pot Merchant / Pot Boy;
- Unicorn;
- Lord of Over Here;

This is a direct implementation grouping for friendly/local NPCs and creature-people around settled UNSLAAD.

## Unslaad Neutral Faction

FACT 03327BEA / zzzCrbUnslaadNeutralFaction

Members include:
- infected frost creatures;
- Memory Sprite;
- Rogue Rat;
- Raging Pot Boy;
- Snow Hare.

This is best treated as neutral/ambient encounter handling rather than a social organization.

## Significance

Together these factions help distinguish:
- settlement/friendly local ecology;
- neutral/radiant encounter ecology;
- hostile factions such as World Eater, Owl, Lyg Crab, Dagon, and Frost Power.

## Guardrail

Faction membership here says more about interaction state than lore allegiance.

Do not treat "Friend Faction" as evidence that all members know each other or form one polity.
