## Significance

The faction is hard evidence that the plugin intentionally groups these actors for a common gameplay/trial relationship.

It is particularly useful for retrieving:
- Ysgrim/Ysgramor-associated figures;
- Atmoran champions;
- Jill trials;
- Jhunal giant/priest material;
- Khev/Dreugh trial actors;
- legendary/heroic beast forms.

## Guardrail

The name **500 Companion Faction** should not be read naively as proof that every member was literally one of Ysgramor's historical Five Hundred Companions.

The membership is too heterogeneous and includes clearly symbolic, transformed, mythic, or later figures.

Use it as:
- implementation-level trial/alliance grouping;
- retrieval context for Atmoran mythic actors.

Require independent text before asserting historical Companion membership.

## Apocryphal source-substrate note

The combination of this faction's **500 Companion** naming with UNSLAAD's direct use of **Hans/Pelinaalilargus** and **Yngol/Ylgar/Tstunal** strongly links the late trial layer to the apocryphal *Five Hundred, or Thereabouts, Companions of Ysgramor* tradition discussed in *The Shezarrine*.

This strengthens the interpretation of the faction as a **mythic trial/reconstruction layer**, not a literal historical roster. See `relationships/five-hundred-apocryphal-substrate.md`.
