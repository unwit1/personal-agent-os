# Dagon Faction

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
FACT 033F2777 / zzzCrbFactionDagon
Display name: **Dagon Faction**

This is a small, tightly defined custom faction with three named members:

- Dagonic Watcher
- Mankar Camoran's Eye
- Red Instigator

## Shared body/archetype

All three use the custom **Watcher Race**.

## Dagonic Watcher

Carries:
- Dagonic Agony;
- Drain Life.

A separate summon spell can reproduce a Dagonic-Watcher form without Dagon Faction membership.

## Mankar Camoran's Eye

Uses:
- Watcher Race;
- Dagon Faction.

The name creates a direct Mankar-Camoran retrieval hook, but no ordinary dialogue in this pass defines whether the Eye is literal, symbolic, summoned, or reconstructed.

## Red Instigator

Uses:
- Watcher Race;
- Dagon Faction;
- stronger Dagonic Agony variant;
- Drain Life.

Its death list includes:
- Watcher Eye;
- Conjure Dagonic Watcher;
- Draco-Nord Sahqon summon bone.

## Significance

Dagon Faction is a much stronger narrative grouping signal than broad factions such as Frost Power Excursion.

It directly groups a Watcher/Mankar/Dagon combat family.

## Guardrail

The faction does not by itself prove:
- literal direct service to Mehrunes Dagon;
- Mankar Camoran's personal physical eye;
- one shared individual identity among the three members.

Use it as an authored Dagon/Watcher relationship layer.
