# Naghu the Gardener

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary form: NPC 0331D91B — Naghu the Gardener  
Dialogue owner: zzzCrbGeneric

Naghu describes himself as the caretaker of Lady Austella's garden and forest.

## Ice trees

Naghu says:
- ice trees disguise themselves as ordinary trees and trick soil into allowing them to grow;
- if left unchecked they can make the area uninhabitable;
- simply burning them is dangerous because thawed ice trees can take root deeply and freeze the land;
- the proper method is to shatter them, after which Frost Atronachs handle the remains.

A repeatable quest asks the player to destroy new ice-tree buds.

## Austella's residence

Naghu calls the area **Lady Austella's temporary residence** and says she is not allowed to leave until a certain date.

He remembers Ulliss visiting and playing there as a child.

## Evidence boundary
