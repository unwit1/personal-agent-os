## Prior meetings

Orlando tells the player they have met many times before:
- in a lonely inn;
- in the Warrens;
- when the player stood on the "Blue Star."

He says the player may prefer not to remember him, but insists their acquaintance is real.

## The Egg

When asked about the Egg, Orlando says:
- the Egg is already empty;
- only a shell remains;
- a cat called **Ja'cobee broke the shell**;
- the player **crushed its contents**;
- Ja'cobee used trickery, but the player ruined the plan.

This is one of the strongest UNSLAAD statements directly interpreting the Ja'cobee/EGG thread.

It also independently aligns with GLENMORIL, where Ja'cobee says he broke the EGG after learning what was inside.

## Black Owl

Orlando says he and the Black Owl:
- come from the same place;
- originate in the far-off icy north;
- come from the depths of a forgotten Elder Wood.

He says "we miss our people" and frames their apparent kindness toward the player through that shared origin, though he also says he is tired of it all.

## Cross-VICN identity handling

GLENMORIL contains Orlando as an Owl-linked Atmoran mage and says he was once regarded as an incarnation of Jhunal. It also contains a Gray Owl actor from the Orlando family.

The exact-name Orlando recurrence, Blue Star reference, Black Owl kinship/origin claim, and Elder Wood/Atmoran context make the UNSLAAD Orlando a high-confidence cross-work identity/manifestation node.

Do not nevertheless collapse Orlando, Jhunal the Gray, Jhunal the Black, Jhunal the White, and every Owl form into one physical person. The evidence supports deliberately distributed/incarnated identities.
