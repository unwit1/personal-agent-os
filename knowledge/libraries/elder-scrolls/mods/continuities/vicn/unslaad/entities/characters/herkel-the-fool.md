# Herkel the Fool

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary form: NPC_ 033F5019 / zzzCrbClevermanHerkel
Primary quest: The Final Journey

## Role

Herkel calls himself a Clever Man and serves as guide/advisor to the player, whom he repeatedly calls Ysmir.

He claims familiarity with ancient Atmoran figures and places, including Ysgramor, Saarthal, old warrior traditions, the giants/followers of Jhunal, and High Hrothgar.

## Key claims

Herkel says:
- the journey takes place above frozen Atmora;
- mirrors can lead through Lyg, an adjacent place where fate can change;
- Woodland Men are Herma-Mora offshoots sealed beneath Atmoran ice;
- Jhunal-associated wisdom/magic was involved in the ancient seals;
- memories and impossible histories can spill from Apocrypha;
- Herma-Mora can hide memories and historical events;
- heroes can deviate from fate, which makes them valuable to Daedra;
- Dragonborn can become addicted to power and devouring;
- the player may ultimately become a world-devourer rather than merely opposing Alduin.

## Reliability

Herkel is useful but not omniscient. The quest itself shows his memories becoming unstable and potentially influenced by Apocrypha/Herma-Mora. Some of his recollections are framed as songs, fairy tales, rumors, or things he suddenly remembers.

Store his statements as informed but fallible testimony.

## Evidence tiers

Herkel's material should be split into explicit confidence layers.

### Mechanically corroborated
High confidence for route/interaction structure when his instructions are backed by quest implementation:
- Lyg-door handling;
- ice-mirror markers;
- Lyg transition markers;
- quest-state routing in The Final Journey.

### Participant/historical recollection
Moderate, source-attributed confidence for:
- names he remembers;
- Atmoran seals;
- Woodland-Man and Jhunal associations;
- ancient routes and stories.

### Mythic/cosmological interpretation
Always attribute:
- fate and hero deviation;
- Dragonborn hunger/devouring as destiny;
- metaphysical implications of Lyg or Apocrypha.

This prevents a VMAD-confirmed doorway from being used as blanket validation of every cosmological explanation Herkel gives in the same scene.
