# Aisha

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary form: NPC 0302011D — Aisha  
Major quests: Falling Cat; Hoarfrost; Long Winter; Lingering Snow; Epilogue Scenes

## Role

Aisha is the player's first guide into UNSLAAD. She pushes the player toward the initial jump and repeatedly frames travel/return in terms of favorable wind and Kyne.

She is closely attached to Ulliss and later treats Ulliss's safety as more important than her own.

## Hjalti visitor

Aisha says the visitor before the player:
- was a man she disliked;
- said he was from **Alcaire**.

Ulliss independently says this visitor was first called **Hjalti** and used different names on different visits.

Aisha's testimony is therefore one half of the internal Hjalti/Alcaire thread.

## Ulliss

Aisha has known Ulliss since childhood.

She says:
- Ulliss once left to search for her father;
- returning took centuries and depended on the right wind;
- Ulliss's father could appear in human, dragon, or cat form;
- she regards that father as a liar and trickster.

During Long Winter, after Ulliss falls, Aisha continues fighting and is mortally wounded. Her dialogue focuses on sending the player to help Ulliss rather than herself.

## Ja'cobee / Fluffy

Aisha repeatedly rolls Fluffy around and is part of his half-remembered past.

The epilogue shows Lizz treating Aisha, Fluffy/Ja'cobee, Ulliss, the player, and herself as one familiar social/family group.

## Origin

In an epilogue conversation with Lizz, Aisha says:
- she is both a cat and Khajiit;
- she comes from a **white city by the sea**;
- she arrived in UNSLAAD on a **white ship**;
- the local port later froze so ships can no longer come.

The city and ship are not named in this scene and should remain unresolved.

## Lizz and time

Aisha encourages Lizz not to imitate Ulliss mechanically, but to remain herself.

When Lizz asks whether Aisha has ever seen adult Lizz, Aisha answers that she **perhaps has**, but cannot remember when.

This is direct epilogue evidence for temporally unstable or non-linear acquaintance with Lizz, but it does not by itself identify the mechanism.

## Death / other-side evidence

Stairway to Llesw'er and related inspection text later associate Aisha with Ja'cobee's peaceful death/passage. Ja'cobee says Aisha is waiting for him, and an inspection message identifies their bodies together with contented expressions.

## Cross-VICN significance

GLENMORIL independently places Aisha in the Ulliss/Ja'cobee Last Chick Trader dialogue network. Preserve that as a strong continuity bridge while keeping UNSLAAD-specific biography source-scoped.

## State and endpoint closure

The implementation layer already distinguishes Aisha's ordinary, dead, ghost, wounded/sabre, and corrupted Dilon-named forms. These should remain separate body/state records under one linked narrative identity rather than be flattened.

Mq05 also carries an explicit `Alias_Aisha` inside the same implementation graph as Lizz, Ulliss, the time-warp marker, and present/Lost-Unslaad routing.

The later Stairway-to-Llesw'er endpoint independently provides:
- Ja'cobee's statement that Aisha is waiting for him;
- the inspection record identifying Ja'cobee and Aisha together after death.

What remains intentionally unresolved:
- the name of Aisha's white seaside city;
- the identity of the white ship;
- the mechanism behind her possible prior encounter with adult Lizz.

Those are source-level unknowns, not missing extraction tasks.
