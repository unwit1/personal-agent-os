# Dov-Ah-Kiin

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary boss: NPC 03161AB6 / zzzCrbBoss03Dovahkiin
Inspection activator: ACTI 031A0B51 / zzzCrbMq05TriggerDovahkiin
Placement: Northern Sanctuary, exterior CELL 030CBC88

## Quest framing

Lingering Snow asks the player to:
- see one of the fates of the Dragonslayer;
- defeat **Dov-Ah-Kiin**.

Jhunal the Gray calls Dov-Ah-Kiin the fate/ruin of dragon slayers.

## Inspection evidence

MESG 031A5C14 says:
- Dov-Ah-Kiin is the **Dragonslayer**;
- he appears to have **frozen himself in time and slept there**;
- he killed many dragons;
- a **colossal amount of Dragon Souls** can be sensed inside him.

## Interpretation boundary

This strengthens Dov-Ah-Kiin as a dragonslayer endpoint built around accumulated Dragon Souls and arrested time.

It does not prove that Dov-Ah-Kiin is literally the current player's future self or that every Dragonborn must become him.
