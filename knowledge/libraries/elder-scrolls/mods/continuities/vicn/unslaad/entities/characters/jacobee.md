# Ja'cobee in UNSLAAD

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary form: NPC_ 0313878F / zzzCrbKedamaSenpai_Mq05 — Ja'cobee
Primary quests: Lingering Snow; Stairway to Llesw'er

## Transition from Fluffy

Earlier UNSLAAD quests use the large white Khajiit/snow-spirit figure under the name **Fluffy**. In Lingering Snow, the corresponding later figure remembers the name Ja'cobee but says he remembers only the name.

The generic dialogue after Lizz's return gives unusually direct identity-discontinuity evidence:
- Lizz calls the earlier Fluffy form **Ja'cobee**;
- Fluffy asks why Lizz calls him Ja'cobee and says he wants a richer name;
- Aisha says it feels strange that Lizz calls Fluffy Ja'cobee.

The best current model is therefore **continuity without stable self-recognition**. Preserve Fluffy and Ja'cobee as linked forms/states rather than either treating them as unrelated or flattening them into one perfectly continuous memory state.

## EGG evidence inside UNSLAAD

Fluffy's Secret makes Fluffy remember an **Egg** when looking at the Twinkling Star. The memory frightens him and is associated with possible death, laughter, a child/promise, and guilt/forgiveness.

Orlando the Knowledgeable later states directly:
- the Egg is empty;
- Ja'cobee broke its shell;
- the player crushed its contents.

This gives UNSLAAD its own direct Ja'cobee/EGG evidence rather than relying only on GLENMORIL.

## Guardian of Lizz

Ja'cobee raises Lizz after Ulliss's death. He says he witnessed Lizz's birth and teaches her Cyrodiilic after her initial unknown/dragon-like speech.

He entrusts Lizz to the player and asks that she be taken back to Ulliss.

## Time awareness

Ja'cobee greets the player as someone not seen for a thousand years and later says his intuition tells him the player is unbound from time.

## Two Ja'cobees

During Lizz's departure, Ja'cobee says there are two Ja'cobees and that the other one is already with Ulliss "on the other side."

The statement is direct, but the mechanism is not explained.

## Death / passage

In Stairway to Llesw'er, Ja'cobee says:
- it is his time to leave;
- he feels cleansed and perhaps forgiven;
- Aisha is waiting for him;
- his path is on the whispering winds, Khenarthi's path.

The quest then asks the player to retrieve Ja'cobee's remains.

A separate inspection message identifies Ja'cobee and Aisha's bodies and describes both as dead but content.

## Cross-VICN significance

GLENMORIL independently contains Ja'cobee as the Last Chick Trader and directly places Ulliss and Aisha in his dialogue cluster. GLENMORIL Ja'cobee also says he broke the EGG.

With Orlando's UNSLAAD line and Fluffy's Egg memory, the cross-work EGG link is now direct on both sides.

Source-specific interpretations of what the EGG contains or means must still remain separately attributable.
