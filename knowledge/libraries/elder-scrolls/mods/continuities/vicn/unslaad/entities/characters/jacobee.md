# Ja'cobee in UNSLAAD

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary form: NPC_ 0313878F / zzzCrbKedamaSenpai_Mq05 — Ja'cobee
Primary quests: Lingering Snow; Stairway to Llesw'er

## Transition from Fluffy

Earlier UNSLAAD quests use the large white Khajiit/snow-spirit figure under the name Fluffy. In Lingering Snow, the corresponding later figure explicitly remembers the name Ja'cobee but says he remembers only the name.

The current source supports a continuity of presentation and memory between Fluffy and Ja'cobee, but Lorekeeper should preserve each quest form and not delete the distinction.

## Guardian of Lizz

Ja'cobee raises Lizz after Ulliss's death. He says he witnessed Lizz's birth and teaches her Cyrodiilic after her initial unknown/dragon-like speech.

He entrusts Lizz to the player and asks that she be taken back to Ulliss.

## Time awareness

Ja'cobee greets the player as someone not seen for a thousand years and later says his intuition tells him the player is unbound from time.

## Two Ja'cobees

During Lizz's departure, Ja'cobee says there are two Ja'cobees and that the other one is already with Ulliss "on the other side."

The statement is direct, but the mechanism is not explained.

## Death / passage

In Stairway to Llesw'er, Ja'cobee says:
- it is his time to leave;
- he feels cleansed and perhaps forgiven;
- Aisha is waiting for him;
- his path is on the whispering winds, Khenarthi's path.

The quest then asks the player to retrieve Ja'cobee's remains.

A separate inspection message explicitly identifies Ja'cobee and Aisha's bodies and describes both as dead but content.

## Cross-VICN significance

GLENMORIL independently contains Ja'cobee as the Last Chick Trader and directly places Ulliss and Aisha in his dialogue cluster. That makes the UNSLAAD Ja'cobee/Ulliss/Aisha grouping a direct named continuity bridge, not merely a thematic resemblance.

However, GLENMORIL's EGG/Chick-Trader testimony should not be copied wholesale onto the UNSLAAD character page unless an UNSLAAD source itself invokes it. Cross-work identity is strongly suggested by repeated names and relationships but still kept provenance-aware.
