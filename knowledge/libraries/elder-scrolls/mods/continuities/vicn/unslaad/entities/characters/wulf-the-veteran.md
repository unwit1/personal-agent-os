# Wulf the Veteran

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary form: NPC 0334E324 — Wulf the Veteran  
Dialogue owner: zzzCrbGeneric

Wulf is an old, friendly figure living in the later UNSLAAD environment.

## Dialogue

Wulf:
- explicitly gives **Wulf** as his name;
- reacts with amusement if the player says their mother warned them not to talk to strangers;
- may discuss a lucky coin, including a branch where a strange Khajiit took it;
- declines an invitation to the castle because he is too old to frolic and does not like being scratched by cats;
- can direct the player toward a black shadow flying among the clouds as a repeatable combat target.

## Evidence boundary

The shared name **Wulf** is a retrieval hook to Elder Scrolls figures of the same name, but this dialogue alone does not identify Wulf the Veteran with any official or other-VICN Wulf.
