# Mifa Xanadu

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary form: NPC 0330E581 — Mifa Xanadu
Dialogue owner: zzzCrbGeneric

## Role

Mifa is a long-lived craftsperson/blacksmith figure associated with Lady Austella's residence.

She says:
- the residence belonged to Austella;
- Austella had once been a hostage of elves;
- the place is now forgotten by men and mer;
- Ulliss is a good person and Austella should have treated her more kindly.

## Hjalti commission

Mifa says she is making custom armor.

She estimates it will take a few more centuries to finish and assumes the client is long dead.

Asked who commissioned it, she answers:

"A little boy called Hjalti. He just left me some gold and left."

This is direct UNSLAAD evidence for a Hjalti visiting the same broad setting in the past.

## Crafting continuity

A generic greeting can react to armor and has Mifa say, "So that's how it should be made." A repeatable request asks the player to recover an Ember of Atmora for a stronger fire.

The implementation supports Mifa as an ongoing ancient craftsperson, but does not by itself identify the commissioned armor with any specific historical armor set.
