# Austella

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary form: NPC 030188B1 — Austella  
Ghost form: NPC 03112A8A — Austella  
Major quests: Hoarfrost; When You Wish Upon a Star

## Role

Austella is Ulliss's mother and the reclusive lady associated with Castle Unslaad and the surrounding residence.

Naghu the Gardener calls the area **Lady Austella's temporary residence** and says she is not allowed to leave until a certain date. The authority, reason, and date are not identified in his dialogue.

Ulliss says Austella became very ill, shut herself inside the castle with ice, and refused to see even her own daughter.

## Ulliss

Austella's Hoarfrost scene explicitly frames Ulliss as ambiguously human/dragon and says she sees darkness in her daughter's future.

She also says she could not love Ulliss "as she is."

Ulliss interprets Austella's rejection through her horns and tail.

The doll branch complicates that rejection. When the player brings Ulliss a doll from Austella, Ulliss reads it as proof that her mother had still been thinking about her.

In the epilogue, Ulliss says that becoming a mother herself helps her understand Austella somewhat better and concludes that Austella must have loved her in her own way.

## Jhunal / Gray Owl bargain

In the Bell Tower ghost scene, Austella asks whether the Gray Owl's proposal will connect her with **"that person."**

She asks what he wants in return.

Jhunal the Gray, speaking as the Gray Owl, says he only wants to help and fulfill her deepest desire.

Austella agrees to trust him "for now."

The identity of **that person** is not established by this scene and must remain unresolved.

## Pretended Jhunal

The Hermit says he was once Austella's advisor and lost that role after a man **pretended to be Jhunal**.

He says Austella may not have completely believed the man's identity claim, but was seduced by his words and ignored the Hermit's warnings.

Combined with the later Gray Owl confession that he has used the name Jhunal for thousands of years, this is strong evidence that Austella's bargain involved the long-lived Gray Owl/Jhunal appropriator rather than requiring a direct appearance by the original/deific Jhunal.

## Ulliss's creation — Jhunal's testimony

Jhunal the Gray later claims that:
- Austella sealed dragon souls in ice;
- he shaped that ice into human form;
- the result was Ulliss.

This is high-value testimony from the claimed participant, but it is still Jhunal's account rather than neutral narration.

## Death / Kyne

Austella's final Hoarfrost line describes warmth.

In the later epilogue, Ulliss tells Lizz that Austella is now **with Kyne**.

Treat the Kyne statement as Ulliss's family/religious framing of Austella's post-death state.

## Interpretation boundaries

Do not automatically identify:
- "that person" with Ulliss's father;
- Austella's bargain with every later Owl plan;
- the Hermit's "pretender" with every Jhunal manifestation in the VICN continuity.

The direct connection is specifically between Austella and the Gray Owl/Jhunal representation used in UNSLAAD.

## Bargain-target closure

The **speaker** side of Austella's bargain is sufficiently resolved: the active UNSLAAD scene uses the Gray-Owl/Jhunal representation, and the Hermit's testimony plus the later Gray-Owl confession support the pretender/appropriated-Jhunal reading.

The object of Austella's phrase **"that person"** is not named by the scene, and the current indexed quest/VMAD evidence does not expose a property or alias that resolves it.

Therefore:
- Gray Owl/Jhunal as bargain partner: resolved.
- identity of "that person": intentionally unresolved.

Do not keep this phrase in the active ingestion backlog unless new script, scene, or dialogue evidence directly names the referent.
