# Jill trial forms

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary location: Ysgrim's Seal

## Meaning

UNSLAAD therefore explicitly stages a transformation from Jill-named humanoid warrior forms into dragon/gargoyle-like forms.

The plugin does **not** provide ordinary explanatory dialogue saying what Jills are or why these names/forms exist.
