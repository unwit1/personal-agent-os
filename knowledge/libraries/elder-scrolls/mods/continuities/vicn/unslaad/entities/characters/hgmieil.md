# Hgmieil

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD uses at least two named Hgmieil representations with different bodies, placements, and relic relationships.

## Hgmieil — wolf form

NPC 033BE44B / zzzCrbSubBossWolf

Implementation:
- wolf race/body;
- 500 Companion Faction;
- Frost Power Excursion Faction;
- placed in **Dragon's Throat**.

Death reward:
- **Earthbones Greatsword**.

## Hgmieil the Wolf Warrior

NPC 033A994F / zzzCrbSubBossWolfWarrior

Implementation:
- Manakin Race;
- 500 Companion Faction;
- Frost Power Excursion Faction;
- placed in **Wolf's Hearth**.

Direct equipment:
- **Wolf King's Sword**
- Heavenly Bull Shield

Its custom death list yields the **Elder Wood Wolf** armor set.

## Wolf King's Sword

Description:
- reduces damage taken by 10% while accompanied by a follower;
- increases damage taken by 10% while alone.

## Wolf King's Greatsword

A separate reward chest in **Frozen Tomb** contains the Greatsword.

Description:
- reduces damage taken by 15% while accompanied by a follower;
- increases damage taken by 15% while alone.

## Interpretation

The Wolf King weapon mechanic is explicitly relational: strength/safety increases in company and reverses into vulnerability when isolated.

That is a real authored gameplay theme.

The ESM does not directly state:
- that Hgmieil is literally a king;
- who the Wolf King title historically refers to;
- whether the wolf-form and Wolf-Warrior form are chronological transformations, memories, symbolic incarnations, or trial representations.

Current model: **linked Hgmieil representations with distinct body/loadout states.**

## Reconstruction relationships

Hgmieil's two implementation states gate two separate artifact traditions:

- wolf-form Hgmieil is tied to **Earthbones** reconstruction and drops the Earthbones Greatsword;
- Hgmieil the Wolf Warrior directly carries **Wolf King's Sword**, gates the Wolf King weapon recipes, and yields the Elder Wood Wolf armor family.

This makes Hgmieil a shared progression node across Earthbones, Wolf King, and Elder-Wood-Wolf equipment without proving those traditions share one historical origin.
