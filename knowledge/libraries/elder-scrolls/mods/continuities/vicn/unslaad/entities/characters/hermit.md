# Hermit / former advisor to Austella

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary form: NPC 0336F073 / zzzCrbProphet — Hermit
Dialogue owner: zzzCrbGeneric
Major side quest: Liturgy of I

## Background

The Hermit says he was once Lady Austella's advisor.

He says he lost that role after a man "pretended to be Jhunal." He does not think Austella fully believed the claim, but says she was seduced by the man's words and ignored his warnings.

This testimony introduces an important identity caution around the Jhunal/Owl figures: at least one informed UNSLAAD character explicitly describes Austella's Jhunal as an impostor or pretender.

## Teachings

### Love
The Hermit says love sprouts from free will and dies when forced. He says recognizing the divinity within oneself and shared by all allows one to become one with one's own star.

### Fire
He says fire consumes holy and unholy alike, purifying everything into love, and that one must consume oneself in one's own fire.

### Radiance
He calls Radiance an echo of a past that was never experienced and a coffin rather than a guiding light.

### Flowers
He says Kyne's winds scatter seeds across the world; many flowers divide the Aurbis into infinite truths while their roots remain joined in the soil.

These are the Hermit's philosophical/metaphysical teachings, not neutral ontology.

## Dream paintings

The Hermit describes several dream-images.

### Two children under a tree
He sees:
- one lonely child;
- then a second child;
- the trees say the second has no name;
- the earth says the second has no heartbeat;
- the children play happily;
- a Black Owl watches ominously.

### Six chicks / Blue Star
He sees:
- a twilight world where the stars hide from destruction except the Blue Star;
- six chicks cannibalizing one another;
- one bird becoming especially ugly and vindictive;
- that bird finding love in the hatred;
- the bird resting on the Blue Star and being burned/scorched/broken into ashes and love.

### Pilgrim / golden dragon
He sees:
- freezing mountains and an elder wood;
- priests preparing sacrifice and praying for the end of endless winter;
- a pilgrim whose shout stops snow and clears clouds;
- a golden dragon reflected in the pilgrim's eyes;
- he does not know the dragon's name.

### Egg / dreamer
He wonders whether he has not even been born yet and is merely someone sleeping in an egg, dreaming of someone else.

## Dream-evidence boundary

For retrieval:
- use dream imagery as a bridge to Blue Star, Chick/EGG, Black Owl, Elder Wood, and golden-dragon topics;
- keep the dream source label attached;

His first-person statement that he advised Austella is stronger historical testimony than the symbolic content of the paintings, but it is still witness testimony rather than implementation proof.
