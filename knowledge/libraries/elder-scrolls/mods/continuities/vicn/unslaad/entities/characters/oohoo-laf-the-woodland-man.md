# Oohoo'Laf the Woodland Man

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary form: NPC_ 033F5012 / zzzCrbHoholaugh — Oohoo'Laf the Woodland Man
Good-end form: NPC_ 03486E5B / zzzCrbHoholaughGoodEnd
Primary quests: The Final Journey; Under the Elder Tree

## Self-description

Oohoo'Laf says:
- he is one of the Woodland Men;
- he was born from the void of the Elder Tree;
- Herma-Mora gave him personality;
- Herma-Mora permitted him to call himself a Demi-Prince.

## Function

He says one of his assigned tasks was to observe and guide the player's fate, beginning long before the player's birth.

He also says Herma-Mora ordered him to hide the dragon name Ysmaalithax.

## Offshoots / Black Owls

Oohoo'Laf says his offshoots did work that enabled the player's arrival.

In Under the Elder Tree he explicitly calls them **Black Owl offshoots** and says:
- they are no longer under his control;
- their plans can continue without him;
- they will eventually recognize their puppet-like condition.

Thus the strongest current model is:
**Oohoo'Laf → creates/produces/controls Black Owl offshoots → offshoots can become independent.**

Do not reduce every Black Owl body to Oohoo'Laf himself.

## Memory and words

Oohoo'Laf says:
- water is memory;
- ice is like a library to Apocrypha's residents;
- Woodland Men can "walk through words";
- minds can use that process to escape seals.

This belongs to UNSLAAD's recurring memory/story/book ontology and should be linked to Grabbers, Mnemo-Li, concealed histories, and later Loveletter material.

## Final change

If spared/freed, Oohoo'Laf says seeing the world again changes him. He envies it and questions his former belief that Aedric sacrifice was meaningless.

He then rebels against Herma-Mora by revealing a story he was ordered to conceal: Ysmir the Forefather, Volume IV.
