# Ulliss

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary form: NPC 030022F6 — Ulliss  
Additional forms include human/dragon variants used by quest aliases.  
Major quests: Falling Cat; Hoarfrost; Long Winter; Lingering Snow; Revive; Epilogue Scenes

## Identity

Ulliss is presented as a half-dragon / dragon-like girl with horns and a tail.

Her mother is **Austella**. Ulliss says she never met her father and was told that an armored courtyard statue represented him.

Aisha says the father could appear as human, dragon, or cat and calls him a liar and trickster.

## Childhood and departure

Ulliss says she once left UNSLAAD while young to search for her father.

Both Ulliss and Aisha associate that outside period with conflict between men and dragons. Aisha says the trip lasted roughly three hundred years before the right wind allowed Ulliss to return.

## Austella

Ulliss says Austella rejected her tail and horns and refused to see her.

The doll branch complicates that rejection: after receiving the doll from Austella, Ulliss interprets it as proof that her mother had still been thinking about her.

In the epilogue, after becoming a mother herself, Ulliss says she understands Austella somewhat better and believes Austella must have loved her in her own way.

Ulliss tells Lizz that Austella is now **with Kyne** and cannot be visited normally.

## Constructed origin — Jhunal's testimony

Jhunal the Gray claims:
- Austella sealed dragon souls in ice;
- he shaped that ice into human form;
- Ulliss was therefore neither ordinary human nor ordinary dragon, but ice given form;
- her dragon form manifested as she grew.

This is a direct statement from the character claiming to have performed the work, but remains Jhunal's testimony rather than neutral narration.

## Death, revival, and Dragon Souls

The Revive system directly implements:

Ancient Dragon Ring + Twinkling Star attunement + expenditure of one Dragon Soul → revival of Ulliss.

Jhunal later tells the player to pour Dragon Souls into Ulliss and says this is related to the way her life was made.

## Lizz

Lizz is Ulliss's daughter.

Ja'cobee raises Lizz in the distant future and eventually sends her back through time to Ulliss.

Ulliss accepts Lizz quickly, saying she feels a bond even before fully understanding the mechanism.

The epilogue confirms a stable mother-daughter relationship:
- Lizz repeatedly calls Ulliss "mom";
- Ulliss says she is happy to have Lizz;
- they play, make snowballs/snowmen, and discuss family history.

Snow Guardian additionally lets the player describe Lizz as **our daughter** with Ulliss.

## Epilogue family model

Lizz constructs snow figures representing:
- herself;
- Ulliss;
- Fluffy/Ja'cobee;
- Aisha;
- the Dovahkiin/player.

This gives direct authored evidence for the social/family grouping after the main story.
