# Sacred Anatomancer

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary form: NPC 0331B06B — Sacred Anatomancer  
Dialogue owner: zzzCrbGeneric / Unslaad Generic

## Name request

The Sacred Anatomancer repeatedly demands that the player give it their **name**.

When asked why, it answers:

> I want to see its Radiance.

It continues asking for the name even when refused or dismissed.

## Evidence boundary

The dialogue directly links **name → Radiance** in the Anatomancer's own understanding.

It does not establish what it means to literally give away a name, whether the Anatomancer can take or alter one, whether Radiance is a soul/memory/image/timeline residue, or whether every use of "name" elsewhere in VICN uses this exact mechanism.

Cross-link with the existing Name and Radiance concept page, the Hermit's Radiance testimony, and the cell **End of Radiance**.
