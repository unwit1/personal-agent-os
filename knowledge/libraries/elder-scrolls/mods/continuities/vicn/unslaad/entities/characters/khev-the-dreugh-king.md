# Khev the Dreugh King

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary boss form: NPC 03407434 — Khev the Dreugh King  
Hologram/source forms include NPC 033D8503 and NPC 033D8508  
Major quests: Sermon of Khev; VS Dreugh King

## Self-identification

In the holographic sermon, the speaker says the **Grabbers** call him a **mirror of Vehk**, and that this is why he calls himself **Khev**.

He says he was once a king, but those who called him king are dead.

This establishes Khev as a deliberately mirrored/inverted Vehk identity in his own account. It does not establish literal identity with Vivec.

## Lost places

Khev names:
- Kuri;
- Djaf;
- Horma-Gile;
- Malbioge, the old city of chains.

He says these places were destroyed, crushed, or are sinking.

Their exact mapping to external Elder Scrolls geography remains unresolved.

## Nu-Mantia doctrine

Khev repeatedly returns to **Nu-Mantia**.

His sermon links it to:
- love;
- chains;
- domination;
- resistance;
- transcendence;
- immortality beyond the world.

The later boss dialogue sharpens the paradox by calling Nu-Mantia **"the slavery at the end of freedom."**

This deliberately conflicts with the embedded Loveletter's description of Nu-Mantia as Liberty / the road to Liberty.

Preserve the contradiction.

## Lyg

Khev repeatedly invokes **Lyg**.

He says:
- those not bound by chains will thirst like Lyg;
- his chains will reach Merid-Nunda and create a rainbow path;
- whoever is born from the bowels of Lyg will prove "our immortality";
- in the boss ending, his voice is dead "like Lyg."

These claims make Khev one of UNSLAAD's two independent Lyg witnesses, alongside Herkel.

## Molag

The boss quest also has an alias named **Molag the Lord of Lies**.

Khev's final speech says he shelters a **cold flame** and gives the name of its warmth as **Molag**.

The exact relationship among Khev, the Molag alias, Molag Bal, and the sermon theology is unresolved.

## Reliability

Khev is a doctrinal and self-mythologizing speaker.

Use his statements as direct evidence for:
- what Khev teaches;
- what vocabulary UNSLAAD intentionally associates with him;
- the existence of named concepts such as Nu-Mantia, Lyg, Merid-Nunda, and the Grabbers in his discourse.

Do not automatically promote his theology into objective cosmology.
