# Lizz

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary form: NPC_ 030CE5F5 — Lizz
Primary quest: Lingering Snow / zzzCrbMq05

## Identity

Lizz describes herself as a little Dovah because she has horns and a tail. Her name was given by her mother.

Ja'cobee says:
- Lizz is Ulliss's child;
- he witnessed her appear in a shining light;
- she initially spoke an unknown language, possibly dragon language;
- he raised her and taught her Cyrodiilic.

Lizz identifies Ulliss as her mother even before meeting her directly, based on Ja'cobee's stories.

## Dragon-soul lineage

Jhunal the Gray says Lizz is Ulliss's successor and contains part of the player's dragon soul. He therefore calls her, "in a way," also the player's child.

This statement is consistent with the preceding quest layer in which the player can pour Dragon Souls into Ulliss to restore her, but the exact reproductive/metaphysical mechanism remains Jhunal's interpretation unless later records explain it more directly.

## Jhunal's interest

Jhunal wants Lizz as a vessel because of the dragon soul within her and attempts to use the player to bypass the barrier protecting her.

## Ja'cobee relationship

Ja'cobee is Lizz's long-term guardian in the distant future. He teaches her language, stories, play, and memories of Ulliss/Aisha.

He eventually asks the player to take Lizz back through time to Ulliss.

## Reunion

Ulliss says she feels an immediate bond with Lizz and chooses to accept the role of mother even if the literal mechanism is uncertain.

The quest completion record treats the reunion as fulfilling Ja'cobee's promise.
