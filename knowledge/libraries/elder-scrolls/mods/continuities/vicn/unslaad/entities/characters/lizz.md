# Lizz

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
Primary form: NPC_ 030CE5F5 — Lizz
Primary quest: Lingering Snow / zzzCrbMq05

## Identity

Lizz describes herself as a little Dovah because she has horns and a tail. Her name was given by her mother.

Ja'cobee says:
- Lizz is Ulliss's child;
- he witnessed her appear in a shining light;
- she initially spoke an unknown language, possibly dragon language;
- he raised her and taught her Cyrodiilic.

Lizz identifies Ulliss as her mother even before meeting her directly, based on Ja'cobee's stories.

## Dragon-soul lineage

Jhunal the Gray says Lizz is Ulliss's successor and contains part of the player's dragon soul. He therefore calls her, "in a way," also the player's child.

This statement is consistent with the preceding quest layer in which the player can spend a Dragon Soul through the Twinkling Star / Ancient Dragon Ring system to revive Ulliss.

## Explicit parental framing

The side quest **Snow Guardian** adds stronger narrative corroboration.

A player dialogue option to Jokul states:

"Ulliss is doing well. We have a daughter now."

Jokul congratulates the player.

This means UNSLAAD itself permits the player to identify Lizz straightforwardly as **the player and Ulliss's daughter**, even though the underlying reproductive/metaphysical mechanism remains unusual.

Current classification:
- Ulliss as mother: direct and high confidence.
- Player as parent: direct narrative framing plus dragon-soul mechanism; high confidence as relationship, mechanism still metaphysically unresolved.

## Jhunal's interest

Jhunal wants Lizz as a vessel because of the dragon soul within her and attempts to use the player to bypass the barrier protecting her.

## Ja'cobee relationship

Ja'cobee is Lizz's long-term guardian in the distant future. He teaches her language, stories, play, and memories of Ulliss/Aisha.

He eventually asks the player to take Lizz back through time to Ulliss.

## Reunion

Ulliss says she feels an immediate bond with Lizz and chooses to accept the role of mother even if the literal mechanism is uncertain.

The quest completion record treats the reunion as fulfilling Ja'cobee's promise.

## Implementation corroboration

Mq05 / **Lingering Snow** exposes VMAD properties for:
- `Alias_Lizz`;
- `Alias_Ulliss`;
- `Alias_Aisha`;
- `Alias_Kedama`;
- `LizzTimeWarpMarker`;
- present/Lost-Unslaad door routing.

This is structural evidence that Lizz's reunion/family sequence and the time-warp transition belong to one quest graph. It strengthens the narrative relationship network, but it does **not** define the biological/metaphysical mechanism by which the player's dragon soul contributes to Lizz.

Completion status:
- Ulliss ↔ Lizz mother/daughter relationship: resolved at high confidence.
- player ↔ Lizz parental framing: resolved at high confidence as narrative relationship.
- exact metaphysical reproduction mechanism: intentionally unresolved, not an ingestion gap.
