## Insight tradition

Two UNSLAAD Insight messages independently expand Khemkel's role.

One says:
- Amora-tree dolls possess hearts;
- Khemkel carved figures of Ysgramor's companions;
- those carvings deceived Herma-Mora.

Another says:
- Khemkel's carvings preserve the companions' **shapes against forgetting**;
- the Jhunal-men became harder to deceive.

## Significance

Khemkel is therefore not merely a combat NPC.

The source layers converge on:
- carving;
- artificial bodies/figures;
- preservation of shape;
- resisting forgetting;
- deception of Herma-Mora;
- a personal anti-Daedra carving knife.

This makes Khemkel a strong local node in UNSLAAD's wider body/form/memory ontology.

## Boundary

The Insights are stylized mythic testimony.

Do not automatically conclude that every Khemkel carving literally contains the preserved soul or identity of the person depicted unless a source states that.
