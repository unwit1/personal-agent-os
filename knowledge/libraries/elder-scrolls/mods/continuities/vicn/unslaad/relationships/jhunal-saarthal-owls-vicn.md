# Jhunal / Saarthal / Owl continuity bridge

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

This page records a multi-work VICN correspondence that is now supported by independent source records in UNSLAAD, VIGILANT, and GLENMORIL.

## UNSLAAD

In Lingering Snow, Jhunal the Gray says:
- the other Owls were last together at Saarthal during the Night of Tears;
- a barrier protecting Lizz is one of the Arcanas of the Owls and was left to priestesses of Kyne;
- the Arcana of Magnus he uses was stolen from Saarthal.

Earlier UNSLAAD dialogue explicitly identifies Jhunal the Gray as the Gray Owl.

## VIGILANT

VIGILANT's Jhunal evidence independently includes:
- Jhunal the Owl;
- Arcana of Jhunal / Arcane Fire;
- Berich's testimony that Jhunal stole a fire rune / Wisdom of Magnus from Saarthal;
- environmental support through Burnt Casimir carrying the Arcana.

This is a strong cross-work recurrence of Jhunal + Owl + Saarthal + Magnus-fire material.

## GLENMORIL

GLENMORIL independently contains:
- The Night of Tears subquest;
- The Owls subquest;
- Jhunal the Black and Jhunal the White;
- a Gray Owl actor in the Orlando actor family;
- Orlando describing himself as an Atmoran mage once regarded as an incarnation of Jhunal.

The overlap makes Night of Tears / Owl / Jhunal / Atmora-Saarthal a central VICN continuity node.

## Ontology rule

Do not collapse:
- Jhunal the Gray,
- Jhunal the Black,
- Jhunal the White,
- Jhunal the Owl,
- Orlando,
- or every generic Owl actor

into one body/person.

The corpus instead supports a recurring, deliberately entangled identity network involving multiple Owl titles/forms, Jhunal-associated knowledge, Atmora/Saarthal, and distributed bodies or incarnations.

## Confidence

- Cross-work thematic recurrence: very high.
- Repeated Saarthal/Magnus theft claim between VIGILANT and UNSLAAD: high as VICN-continuity corroboration.
- Exact identity among colored Owls/Jhunal/Orlando: unresolved.
