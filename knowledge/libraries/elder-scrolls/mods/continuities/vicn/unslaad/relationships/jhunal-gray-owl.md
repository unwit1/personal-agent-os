# Jhunal the Gray / Gray Owl — UNSLAAD identity evidence

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad  
Primary evidence: When You Wish Upon a Star; Long Winter; Lingering Snow; Boss Rush

## Direct Gray Owl identity

In the Bell Tower sequence, quest alias 9 OwlGhost resolves to a **Jhunal the Gray** NPC form.

That actor explicitly calls himself the **Gray Owl**.

This is direct source evidence that the active UNSLAAD representation uses both identities/titles.

## Austella bargain

The Gray Owl approaches Austella with an offer he says will connect her with "that person" and frames his role as fulfilling her deepest desire.

The Hermit, who says he once advised Austella, independently describes the influential figure as a man who **pretended to be Jhunal**.

## Millennia-long use of the Jhunal name

Boss Rush adds the clearest identity confession.

The speaking alias OwlTA uses **Jhunal the Gray** as its VTCK/source actor.

The speaker says:
- he has used the name **Jhunal for thousands of years**;
- people can no longer distinguish him from their god;
- nobody remains who remembers the difference.

This establishes that the active Gray Owl figure himself believes there was originally a distinction between:
1. himself;
2. the Jhunal people worshipped/remembered as a god.

The distinction was then eroded by time and forgetting.

## Memory and identity

The same confession says everyone forgot the speaker and that isolation almost drove him mad.

This makes collective memory part of the identity problem itself: people no longer distinguish original Jhunal from the long-lived Gray Owl because the alternative memory has vanished.

## The Owls

Elsewhere, Jhunal the Gray:
- calls other Owls his friends;
- says he last saw the other Owls at Saarthal during the Night of Tears;
- identifies a barrier around Lizz as an Arcana of the Owls;
- says he will serve the Owls one final time under the pledge of his mask.

Thus the Gray Owl is not merely an isolated Jhunal impersonator; he is embedded in a broader Owl network.

## Experiments and Laza

Boss Rush has the speaker list **Bard, Laza, and Ulliss** as failures before reaching "the real thing."

Lingering Snow separately has him describe Ulliss as an artificial form made from dragon souls sealed in ice.

The **Laza** reference is especially important cross-VICN evidence:
- VIGILANT's Jhunal the Owl independently says he used Laza to harvest dragons;
- UNSLAAD's Gray Owl independently places Laza among his failed experimental history.

This is strong continuity evidence around the Jhunal/Laza/dragon-experiment complex, but does not require the VIGILANT and UNSLAAD bodies to be literally the same physical incarnation.

## Dragon dream

The Gray Owl says his final surviving dream is:
- to become a dragon;
- to fly in the sky;
- to feel wind in his wings.

He calls it a childhood dream and the last thing he still possesses.

This aligns with his other experiments involving dragon souls, Ulliss, Lizz, and vessel construction.

## Current ontology model

High confidence:
- Gray Owl is a Jhunal-associated long-lived Owl representation.
- He has deliberately used the name Jhunal for millennia.
- He admits a lost distinction between himself and the god Jhunal.
- Forgetting erased that distinction socially.
- He belongs to or serves a broader Owl network.
- His experiments repeatedly center dragons, dragon souls, vessels, and Laza.

Unresolved:
- whether he is an incarnation, copy, appropriator, fragment, vessel, successor, or some combination;
- how he relates physically/temporally to VIGILANT's Jhunal the Owl;
- how Gray, Black, and White Owl manifestations relate at the body level;
- what the original/deific Jhunal's exact relationship to these actors is.

Lorekeeper should link these identities densely while refusing a premature global merge.

## Post-quest craft-form evidence

After **The Owl Flies at Dusk** is completed, three Gray-Owl craft forms become available.

Their descriptions add direct identity/history statements:
- **Gray OWL** calls him the **second CHICK**, once an Atmoran mage seeking the "dragon";
- **Gray EGG** says an EGG was crushed by a **Chick Trader of Anequina** and lost its "future";
- **Shadow of the Gray OWL** says the secret of Saarthal granted him insight into higher planes.

These post-quest descriptions strongly reinforce the existing Atmora / dragon-dream / Saarthal / Chick-Trader network, while leaving the exact meaning of "second CHICK" unresolved.

## Multiple physical representations at Dragon's Peak

Placed-reference analysis finds separate inspection objects for **Jhunal the Gray** and **Gray Owl** in different Dragon's Peak cells. The Jhunal body has an unknown real name; the Gray-Owl body is described as shattered after pursuing the dragon.

Because dialogue elsewhere directly links Jhunal the Gray to the Gray Owl title, this supports a model with **multiple bodies/representations/states** inside the identity network rather than either a universal one-body merge or a complete identity split.

## Merge boundary

The supported merge is **narrative identity linkage**, not universal deific equivalence.

Safe:
- the active Jhunal-the-Gray representation calls himself the Gray Owl;
- multiple Jhunal-the-Gray forms participate in one narrative identity family;
- the Gray Owl says he used the name Jhunal for millennia;
- he acknowledges an older distinction between himself and the god remembered as Jhunal.

Unsafe without additional source evidence:
- every historical deed of deific/original Jhunal was performed by this Gray Owl;
- every Owl body is one continuous physical body;
- VIGILANT/GLENMORIL/UNSLAAD Jhunal manifestations are automatically the same body or timeline-state.
