## Direct identity link

Quest alias 9 OwlGhost resolves to NPC 03112A8B / zzzCrbGrayOwl_Mq03Ghost — Jhunal the Gray.

In the Bell Tower scene, this actor tells Austella to trust in the wisdom of "this Gray Owl" and says that the aim of the Gray Owl is to fulfill her deepest desires.

This is direct source evidence that, in this scene, the title/identity Gray Owl is attached to Jhunal the Gray. It is stronger than an inference based only on owl imagery.

## Austella bargain seed

Austella asks whether what Jhunal proposes will connect her with "that person" and then asks what he wants in return. Jhunal presents the arrangement as help and desire-fulfillment. The referenced person and the actual cost/mechanism are not identified in these lines and should remain unresolved until later evidence establishes them.
