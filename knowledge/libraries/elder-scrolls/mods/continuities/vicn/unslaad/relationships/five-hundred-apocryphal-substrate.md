# Five Hundred / Atmoran apocryphal substrate

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
External provenance layer: apocryphal **Five Hundred, or Thereabouts, Companions of Ysgramor** tradition as discussed in *The Shezarrine*

UNSLAAD's late Atmoran trial layer appears to deliberately draw from this external apocryphal tradition.

The evidence is cumulative rather than a single copied paragraph.

## External tradition

The source library's **The Shezarrine** discusses an apocryphal work called:

**Five Hundred, or Thereabouts, Companions of Ysgramor**

That tradition includes distinctive material such as:
- Hans the Fox;
- Pelinaalilargus the Pragmatist;
- Yngol and Ylgar;
- alternate names Tsunaltir / Stuhnalmir;
- the fused/returned **Grit-Prince Tstunal**;
- extensive mythic reinterpretation of Ysgramor-era figures.

This is not ordinary released-game canon.

## Interpretation

The combination of:
- 500 Companion Faction;
- Hans/Pelinaalilargus;
- Yngol/Ylgar/Tstunal;
- a mythic Ysgrim/Atmora trial structure

is strong evidence that UNSLAAD intentionally **adapts the Five Hundred apocryphal tradition as a source substrate**.

## What remains Vicn-authored

Even when a name or mythic pattern comes from the external substrate, UNSLAAD independently authors:
- exact boss implementations;
- placements;
- factions;
- transformations;
- equipment;
- Shouts;
- encounter ordering;
- quest mechanics;
- new dialogue and interpretation.

## Guardrail

Do not read **500 Companion Faction** as:

"every member is literally one of Ysgramor's historical Five Hundred Companions."

The faction contains:
- transformed entities;
- mythic figures;
- symbolic bosses;
- characters from other cosmological layers;
- bodies/representations not compatible with one literal historical company.

The safest classification is:

**apocryphal Five-Hundred source substrate + Vicn-authored mythic trial reconstruction.**
