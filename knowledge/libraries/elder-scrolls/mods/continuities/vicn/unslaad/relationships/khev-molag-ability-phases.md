# Khev / Molag combat-phase abilities

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

## Khev the Dreugh King

NPC 03407434

Carries:
- **Khev01 abilities**
- custom **Khev01 Combat AI**

He is placed in Old City of Chains and directly carries Muatra.

## Molag the Lord of Lies

NPC 033D8514

Carries:
- **Khev02 abilities**
- custom combat effect internally labeled as the second Khev phase.

He is also:
- Dreughman Race;
- Lyg Crab Faction;
- 500 Companion Faction;
- placed in Old City of Chains.

He carries Coldfire Swords and drops playable Muatra.

## Significance

The internal ability naming is direct implementation evidence that the Molag encounter is structurally a **Khev phase/state continuation** rather than an entirely unrelated boss encounter.

This complements:
- Khev's final speech naming Molag as the warmth/name of the cold flame;
- their shared race/factions;
- Muatra handoff/drop structure.

## Boundary

This still does not automatically mean:
- Khev and Molag are one literal soul;
- Khev = Vivec;
- Molag = Molag Bal.

The strongest safe statement is:
**UNSLAAD implements Molag as a second Khev-linked boss phase inside the same Lyg/Dreugh narrative complex.**
