# Ulliss / Lizz shared intrinsic ability

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

## Direct implementation evidence

NPC 030022F6 — **Ulliss**

and

NPC 030CE5F5 — **Lizz**

both carry the exact custom spell:

**Ulliss Ability**
EditorID: zzzCrbUlliss

Both also carry the custom perk displayed as Dragonskin, although that perk is broadly used as technical resistance scaffolding elsewhere and is not itself lineage evidence.

## Race relationship

- Ulliss: Half-Dragon Race
- Lizz: Half-Dragon Child Race

This makes the shared Ulliss Ability especially meaningful because it is paired with an explicit parent/child race implementation.

## Narrative corroboration

Dialogue/quest evidence independently establishes:
- Ulliss is Lizz's mother;
- Lizz is sent back through time by Ja'cobee;
- Jhunal says Lizz contains part of the player's dragon soul;
- the player can call Lizz "our daughter" with Ulliss;
- epilogue scenes show a stable mother-daughter relationship.

## Current interpretation

The shared ability is strong implementation corroboration that Lizz is designed as a direct derivative/descendant of Ulliss's body/power archetype.

It does not by itself define which powers are inherited or whether every effect in the hidden base-game portion of the ability is genealogically meaningful.
