# Muatra / Vivec / Khev relationship

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

UNSLAAD places the name **Muatra** in two deliberately different contexts.

## Hermit's Tower

Container:
- **Vivec and Molag Bal**

contains:
- BOOK Vivec and Molag Bal
- **Short Muatra**

The parody text itself repeatedly invokes Muatra/spear imagery.

This gives the Short Muatra item a direct local intertextual relationship with that book.

## Khev / Dreugh layer

Khev the Dreugh King:
- directly carries the combat-form **Muatra**;
- hologram/source forms also carry it.

Molag the Lord of Lies:
- carries Coldfire Swords;
- has a death list yielding the playable two-handed **Muatra**.

Khev describes himself as a **mirror of Vehk**.

## Recipe layer

After VS Dreugh King:
- Short Muatra
- Muatra

become craftable.

## Interpretation

UNSLAAD deliberately distributes Muatra across:
- Vivec/Molag-Bal parody material;
- Khev's mirrored-Vehk identity;
- Molag;
- Dreugh/Lyg boss progression.

This is stronger than an isolated borrowed weapon name.

## Boundary

Do not conclude from this alone that:
- Khev literally is Vivec;
- Molag literally is Molag Bal;
- the Hermit's Tower Short Muatra and Khev's weapon are one physical object;
- UNSLAAD is reproducing one authoritative version of Muatra's official history.

The evidence supports an intentional **Muatra/Vehk/Molag mirror network** inside UNSLAAD.
