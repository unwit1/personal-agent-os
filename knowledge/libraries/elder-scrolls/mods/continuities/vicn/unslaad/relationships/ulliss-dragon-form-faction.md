## Contrast with human forms

Human/ghost Ulliss forms generally use:
- Half-Dragon Race;
- UnslaadFaction.

The dragon form instead uses:
- Skyrim DragonRace;
- **Ulliss Dragon Faction**;
- dragon-combat package.

## Narrative corroboration

Dialogue already establishes:
- Ulliss develops/manifests a dragon form;
- Aisha says Ulliss's father could appear as human, dragon, or cat;
- Jhunal describes Ulliss as constructed from dragon souls sealed in ice.

## Significance

The dedicated one-member faction is strong implementation evidence that the dragon body is meant as a distinct **state/form of Ulliss**, not a generic dragon actor accidentally sharing her display name.

## Boundary

Faction separation does not mean:
- separate soul;
- separate person;
- permanent transformation.

It encodes gameplay state/body handling.
