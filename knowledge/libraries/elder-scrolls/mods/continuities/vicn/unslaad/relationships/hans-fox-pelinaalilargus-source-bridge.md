# Hans the Fox / Pelinaalilargus source bridge

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
External provenance layer: apocryphal-library / **The Shezarrine**

## UNSLAAD dialogue

In **The Final Journey**, Herkel identifies the dragon encountered by the player as:

**Hans the Fox**

and says he was given the name:

**Pelinaalilargus**

for his strength.

Herkel also says the figure was once a man like the player and asks Ysmir to free the dragon.

## Existing apocryphal source

The library's **The Shezarrine** discusses Hans the Fox using the apocryphal *Five Hundred, or Thereabouts, Companions of Ysgramor* tradition.

It says:
- Hans the Fox appears in the first paragraph of that tradition;
- a mirrored final paragraph replaces the characters with names believed to be dragon-names;
- in Hans's corresponding position appears **Pelinaalilargus the Pragmatist**;
- the name is associated by the text with Shor-the-Fox / Shezarrine speculation.

## Continuity significance

The rare pair:

**Hans the Fox ↔ Pelinaalilargus**

appears independently in the existing apocryphal source and in UNSLAAD's authored quest dialogue.

This is strong evidence that UNSLAAD is deliberately **reusing/adapting that apocryphal Nordic tradition**, rather than inventing the name pair in isolation.

## Spear of the Fox

UNSLAAD also contains a craftable **Spear of the Fox**.

Description:
- especially deadly to **Daedra and Hares**.

However:
- Pelinaalilargus the Fox does **not** directly carry the Spear in his NPC inventory;
- the boss is represented instead through a custom Pelinal-prefixed Shout/Voice package.

Therefore the Spear is a related Fox relic, not a directly evidenced carried weapon of Pelinaalilargus.

## Guardrail

The external text itself is apocryphal/unofficial source material.

UNSLAAD's reuse of Hans/Pelinaalilargus is valid VICN continuity evidence, but it does not make the Shezarrine interpretation official Bethesda/ZeniMax canon.

## Provenance closure

The rare **Hans the Fox ↔ Pelinaalilargus** pairing is sufficiently source-resolved for this ingestion pass.

Classification:
- external apocryphal substrate supplies the rare name-pair tradition;
- UNSLAAD supplies its own Herkel dialogue, dragon encounter, Shout/Voice implementation, and related Fox relic staging;
- the Spear of the Fox remains related thematic/item evidence, not a directly carried Pelinaalilargus weapon.

This bridge should now be treated as a resolved source-adaptation relationship rather than an open provenance question.
