# Yngol / Ylgar / Tstunal — The Shezarrine source bridge

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad
External provenance layer: apocryphal-library / **The Shezarrine**

UNSLAAD's Yngol/Tstunal Insight is not an isolated invention.

It reuses or adapts a distinctive apocryphal tradition already present in the source library.

## External source

The apocryphal text **The Shezarrine** discusses an older work that radically renames/reframes Ysgramor-era figures.

Its distinctive claims include:
- **Yngol and Ylgar**;
- alternate names **Tsunaltir** and **Stuhnalmir**;
- the fused/returned figure **Grit-Prince Tstunal**;
- a mirrored set of human and Dragon-name identities;
- Hans the Fox / Pelinaalilargus in the same broader source tradition.

This is external apocryphal material, not official released-game canon.

## UNSLAAD Insight

MESG 033B3ECD / zzzCrbMsgInsightYngolFlame includes the same rare cluster:
- Yngol;
- Ylgar;
- their ashes/fusion;
- **Grit-Prince Tstunal**.

UNSLAAD additionally frames Yngol as:
- strategist;
- blacksmith;
- dreamer of a dragon wearing his armor.

The mod therefore **adapts and extends** the external Tstunal tradition rather than merely repeating a generic Ysgramor legend.

## Related UNSLAAD implementation

UNSLAAD separately contains:
- Remnant of Yngol;
- Yngol's Shield;
- Yngol's War Axe;
- Yngol's Battleaxe;
- Yngol's War Cry weapon art;
- Ysgrim/Ysgramor trial material.

Those are Vicn-authored implementation choices around the reused/adapted mythic substrate.
