# Hjalti / Alcaire / armor thread

Continuity: tes.mod.vicn.unslaad

This page combines three independent UNSLAAD references without forcing an external identity merge.

## Falling Cat — Aisha

Aisha says the visitor before the player was a man she disliked.

When asked who he was, she remembers that he said he was **from Alcaire**.

## Hoarfrost — Ulliss

Ulliss independently describes the earlier visitor:
- he was passionate;
- he was called **Hjalti at first**;
- he had a different name every time he visited;
- he eventually stopped returning after Aisha clawed him.

This directly links the prior visitor with the name Hjalti.

## Generic dialogue — Mifa Xanadu

Mifa says a **little boy called Hjalti** commissioned custom armor, paid her in gold, and left.

She has continued working on the commission for centuries and assumes the client is dead.

## Interpretation boundary

The combined source establishes a recurring UNSLAAD Hjalti associated with:
- Alcaire;
- changing names;
- repeated visits;
- Aisha/Ulliss's past;
- an ancient armor commission.

This strongly invites comparison with Elder Scrolls Hjalti/Tiber traditions, but UNSLAAD's Hjalti should remain source-scoped unless an explicit record completes that identity.

Cross-index with Hjalti Early-Beard, Tiber Septim, Talos, Alcaire, and Ysmir without silently equating them.
