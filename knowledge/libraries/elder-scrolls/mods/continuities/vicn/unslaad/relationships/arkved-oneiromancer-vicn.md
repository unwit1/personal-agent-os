# Arkved / Oneiromancer cross-VICN bridge

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

## GLENMORIL

The existing GLENMORIL corpus establishes Arkved as a direct Oneiromancer/Quagmire participant:
- Romion identifies Arkved as a Third-Era figure imprisoned in nightmares by Vaermina;
- Arkved serves as a connection/key for access toward Quagmire;
- dedicated Arkved negotiation scenes exist inside the Oneiromancer sequence.

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD has a side quest named Arkved and two Arkved the Dreamer forms.

In the shared generic dialogue, Arkved explicitly says:
- he made a deal with **the Oneiromancer**;
- she asked him to guide the player;
- she asked him to collect payment.

He also talks about nightmares, dream/reality equivalence, and frozen Atmoran dreams.
