# Cross-VICN reconciliation 01

Parent continuity: `tes.mod.vicn`

Works in scope:

- VIGILANT
- GLENMORIL
- UNSLAAD
- DAc0da

## Purpose

This pass separates **real continuity bridges** from three things that can otherwise look deceptively similar:

1. reuse of the same external Elder Scrolls obscure/developer text;
2. recurrence of a rare term without proof of identical mechanics;
3. thematic resemblance without direct naming evidence.

The matrix is intended for retrieval and future claim-graph promotion.

## Evidence classes

### A — direct recurring identity / relationship

Use when multiple VICN works independently identify the same named person/entity or one work directly names the relationship established in another.

Safe for parent-level entity/relationship promotion.

### B — direct recurring institution

Use when the exact organization recurs with concrete actors, reports, quests, or explicit affiliation.

Safe for a shared institution node, while historical states remain source-local.

### C — exact rare vocabulary / named concept

Use when multiple works independently use a highly distinctive proper noun or term.

Safe for co-retrieval and a shared concept/entity candidate. Mechanics remain source-specific unless independently matched.

### D — direct cross-work interpretive pointer

Use when one VICN work explicitly points to a named character/event from another and assigns it interpretive meaning.

Safe as a relationship edge; do not retroactively insert the later interpretation into the earlier work.

### E — shared external substrate

Use when multiple works reuse or adapt the same Bethesda/developer/community source.

This proves a shared source vocabulary, **not by itself a VICN-original continuity claim**.

### F — thematic/conceptual comparison only

Use when the resemblance is meaningful for research but no rare proper noun, explicit relationship, or source-level identity establishes continuity.

Do not promote to a shared-identity claim.

# Direct identity / institution conclusions

## Rolls-On-Roads and Romion

GLENMORIL describes Rolls's unnamed old Psijic friend as an eccentric Altmer Dwemer researcher who later worked in a Thalmor research environment.

DAc0da explicitly names **Romion** as Rolls-On-Roads's old friend/former colleague and describes the same Altmer/Dwemer/apprentice profile.

Status:

> **Promotable shared relationship.**

Do not merge every employment detail across works without retaining its source.

## Augur of the Obscure

Both GLENMORIL and DAc0da independently contain the exact unique title **Augur of the Obscure**.

Both connect him to:

- Rolls-On-Roads;
- non-linear time;
- Numidium/Jills;
- possibility/reality instability;
- absurd but potentially informative speech.

DAc0da additionally identifies him as an Adjacent-Place traveler.

Status:

> **Promotable recurring entity identity.**

Individual claims remain work-specific.

## Holy Pipers

VIGILANT directly implements the Society of the Holy Pipers as a Sacred-Anatomancer-led apostate Stendarr splinter organization.

GLENMORIL continues the Holy-Piper institution through excommunication, kidnappings, Piper's Hill, perception hazards, and Psijic intelligence.

DAc0da's Athanasius independently calls himself Keeper of Stendarr of a Holy-Pipers branch and describes benevolent teaching/rescue duties.

Status:

> **Promotable recurring institution with unresolved historical/branch-state differences.**

Do not define Athanasius as an Anatomancer cultist without evidence.

# Shared-source warnings

## Ghost Choir 9

DAc0da embeds Michael Kirkbride's **Ghost Choir 9** text and also implements Ghost Choir 9 as actors/boss material.

UNSLAAD contains a `GC9`-tagged insight record about LYG-sector memory evaporation and timeline disposal.

The recurrence is important, but part of the vocabulary is inherited from the same external Kirkbride substrate.

Status:

> **Cross-work VICN usage is real; source reuse must remain visible.**

The correct graph should contain separate edges:

- `Michael Kirkbride Ghost Choir 9 text -> reused by DAc0da`
- `Ghost Choir 9 entity -> implemented directly by DAc0da`
- `GC9-tagged LYG memory record -> UNSLAAD implementation`

rather than one provenance-free merged claim.

## Mnemo-Li / Blue Star

VICN works repeatedly use Mnemo-Li/Blue-Star vocabulary, but Elder Scrolls obscure/developer sources already provide part of this substrate.

Status:

> **Strong cross-work retrieval cluster, not proof that every implementation derives from one VICN-original event.**

# Current promotion rules

A shared parent claim can be promoted when at least one of these is true:

1. one VICN work explicitly names a relationship involving a character/entity independently established in another;
2. two works independently implement the same highly distinctive named entity and surrounding role strongly matches;
3. a recurring institution has concrete actors/quests/reports in multiple works.

A shared claim should remain comparison-only when:

1. the overlap is primarily inherited from the same external obscure text;
2. only broad themes match;
3. identical terminology is present but the mechanics contradict or remain undefined;
4. the conclusion requires copying one work's explanation into another.
