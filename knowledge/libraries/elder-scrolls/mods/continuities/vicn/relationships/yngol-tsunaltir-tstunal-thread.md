# Yngol / Tsunaltir / Tstunal — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

This is a strong cross-work Atmoran identity thread connecting **DAc0da**, **UNSLAAD**, and a shared external apocryphal substrate.

## External substrate

The apocryphal tradition discussed in **The Shezarrine** references:
- Yngol and Ylgar;
- alternate names **Tsunaltir** and **Stuhnalmir**;
- the fused/returned **Grit-Prince Tstunal**;
- mirrored human / Dragon-name identities in the broader Five-Hundred tradition.

This is not ordinary released-game canon.

Both DAc0da and UNSLAAD appear to adapt different parts of this rare source complex.

---

## DAc0da

DAc0da gives the most explicit personal transformation/identity sequence.

### Yngol

Yngol directly identifies himself as:
- Yngol;
- son of Ysgramor.

He has several quest-state actors.

Later state:
- **Yngol the Tsunaltir**

### Golden dragon

Separate actor:
- **Tsuunalinfaxtir**

Yngol says:
- old Atmoran teachings claim people once had dragon forms in an earlier kalpa;
- people shed their scales;
- Alduin's scales slumber within them;
- he wants to reclaim his dragon name/scales;
- the golden dragon is **the other half of his being**;
- its name is Tsuunalinfaxtir.

Samon separately says the golden dragon came from an **Adjacent Place** and is old-kalpa material intruding through time distortion.

Thus DAc0da explicitly transforms the source tradition into:
- Yngol identity;
- Tsunaltir title/state;
- split golden-dragon other-half;
- old-kalpa / Adjacent-Place / memory-correction narrative.

# Cross-work synthesis

High confidence:

1. **Both works deliberately draw from the same rare Yngol/Tsunaltir/Tstunal source complex.**
2. Both connect Yngol to:
   - alternate identity/name states;
   - dragon imagery;
   - old/kalpic Atmoran history.
3. DAc0da makes the Dragon-name transformation explicit and personal.
4. UNSLAAD uses the Tstunal fusion/return myth plus artifact/trial staging.

## Important difference

DAc0da:
- Yngol -> Tsunaltir state -> Tsuunalinfaxtir as golden-dragon other half.

UNSLAAD:
- Yngol/Ylgar -> Tstunal fusion/return tradition.

These are **related adaptations**, not automatically the same event.
