# Snow Whales — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

**Snow Whales** are a direct rare-vocabulary recurrence in **UNSLAAD** and **DAc0da**.

No exact Snow-Whale occurrence is currently indexed in VIGILANT, GLENMORIL, or the apocryphal source library.

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD treats Snow Whales as fully diegetic creatures rather than a one-line metaphor.

### Custom race and named actors

Custom race:
- **Snow Whale Race**

Named friendly Snow Whales:
- **Ninawa**
- **Anaku**
- **Atta**

### Ecology testimony

Cawk the Dirtbird says:
- Snow Whales migrate through the sky;
- they can be seen above Atmora;
- their migration can be followed to locate other flying creatures/flocks.

Ninawa says:
- a separated whale can fly until it finds its pod;
- the region's snow is comfortable;
- opening the mouth while flying somehow satisfies hunger.

Atta says:
- at the end of life, Snow-Whale blubber becomes **paint**;
- as paint, they take many different forms.

### Harakk Memory

UNSLAAD MESG 033AEC3B says:
- Snow Whales dance in the cold sky;
- Sea Giants throw harpoons at them;
- sailors of Harakk watch the scene before regressing into childhood dreams beneath the freezing sea.

Thus Snow Whales participate in both ordinary local ecology and mythic Atmoran memory.

## DAc0da

In **Drowned Nighthawk**, Yngol remembers:
- an expedition toward Mereth;
- a severe storm and ghosts;
- a voice from the sky he interprets as Stuhn;
- a **school of snow whales in the sky**;
- then a gap in his memory.

This memory belongs to DAc0da's displaced/unstable Atmoran chronology.

The same quest later links Yngol to Harakk, a twin star, providing additional shared sky/Harakk context with UNSLAAD.

## Cross-work synthesis

High confidence:
- Snow Whales are an intentional recurring VICN Atmoran creature/myth node.
- Both works explicitly place them **in the sky**.
- Both connect them to Atmoran voyage/memory material.
- UNSLAAD gives them a detailed living ecology; DAc0da uses them inside Yngol's unstable historical memory.

## Boundary

Do not assert:
- DAc0da's remembered whales are the named UNSLAAD individuals;
- Snow Whales are official base-TES zoology;
- every sky-whale scene occurs in one chronology;
- the creatures are purely symbolic.

UNSLAAD's actor/race records make literal creature existence inside that continuity direct implementation evidence.
