# Gray Owl / Chick Trader / Anequina cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

## UNSLAAD

Post-quest Gray-Owl equipment directly says:

- **Gray EGG**: an EGG was crushed by a **Chick Trader of Anequina** and its "future" is lost.
- **Gray OWL**: the Gray Owl is **the second CHICK**, formerly an Atmoran mage who sought the "dragon."

These records unlock after The Owl Flies at Dusk.

## GLENMORIL

GLENMORIL independently establishes:
- Ja'cobee as the **last Chick Trader**;
- original Chick Traders came from the **prince of Anequina and royal guards**;
- EGG → CHICK → CHICKEN as deliberate substituted vocabulary;
- Ja'cobee says the EGG has not been completely broken;
- a fully broken EGG is associated with the end of the current dream;
- Orlando/Ja'zel scenes use ancient-Anequina Chick-Trader stories.

This makes **Chick Trader + Anequina + EGG** a direct named institutional bridge between UNSLAAD and GLENMORIL.

## "Second CHICK" comparison

GLENMORIL also contains dialogue describing a **second Chick-Trader-associated figure**, but that figure is:
- unnamed;
- silent/uncanny;
- physically described in a way that does not directly identify it as the Gray Owl.

Therefore:

**Cross-work vocabulary link: high confidence.**  
**Gray Owl = GLENMORIL unnamed second figure: unresolved and not merged.**
