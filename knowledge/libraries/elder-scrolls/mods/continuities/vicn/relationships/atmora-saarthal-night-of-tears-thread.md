# Atmora / Saarthal / Night of Tears — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

This thread links the recurring Atmora/Saarthal/Night-of-Tears history across VIGILANT, GLENMORIL, UNSLAAD, and DAc0da.

It is a **contested-history node**, not one harmonized chronology.

---

## VIGILANT

VIGILANT independently preserves a Jhunal/Saarthal tradition in which:
- Jhunal the Owl is tied to Saarthal;
- Berich says Jhunal stole a fire rune / Wisdom of Magnus from Saarthal;
- the stolen flame has destructive consequences;
- Jhunal later becomes entangled with Laza, dragon harvesting, and Arcana/fire material.

This is one of the strongest repeated cross-work claims because UNSLAAD independently preserves a closely matching Magnus-fire theft tradition.

VIGILANT also presents its own fragmented Jhunal biography and bodily relic trail, so even within one work the historical actor is not simple.

---

## GLENMORIL

GLENMORIL contains direct quest/history nodes titled or centered on:
- **The Night of Tears**
- **The Owls**

It directly resolves:
- Jhunal the Black / Black Owl;
- Jhunal the White / White Owl;
- Gray-Owl / Orlando-family material.

GLENMORIL therefore expands the Night-of-Tears event into a broader **Owl/Jhunal identity-history network**.

It also describes Orlando as an Atmoran mage once regarded as an incarnation of Jhunal.

The exact historical body relations remain unresolved.

---

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD makes the connection especially explicit.

Jhunal the Gray says:
- the other Owls were last together at **Saarthal during the Night of Tears**;
- one of his powers is an **Arcana of Magnus** stolen from Saarthal;
- his history includes Laza, Ulliss, dragon-soul experiments, and vessel construction.

Post-quest Gray-Owl item text additionally says:
- the Gray Owl was once an **Atmoran mage**;
- the secret of Saarthal granted him insight into higher planes.

UNSLAAD also contains:
- **Saarthal's Sorrow**;
- Ember-of-Atmora material;
- Frozen Atmora;
- old Atmoran trial actors;
- later time/memory distortions.

### Important distinction

Do not merge:
- Saarthal's Sorrow;
- Arcana of Magnus;
- fire of Magnus;
- Ember of Atmora

into one artifact without a direct source.

They are all Saarthal/Atmora-linked but remain separate nodes.

---

## DAc0da

DAc0da adds the most explicit **contested historical testimony**.

### Yngol's initial position

Yngol:
- supports Ysgramor's war against the Snow Elves;
- explicitly cites **Saarthal and the Night of Tears**;
- frames the Snow Elves as aggressors;
- treats the Atmoran response as justified.

### Contradictory Atmoran testimony

Later Drowned Nighthawk dialogue introduces opposing Atmoran voices that:
- accuse Ysgramor of deception;
- say many civilians wanted peace and migration away from Atmora's civil war;
- condemn purges of elves and mixed-blood people;
- claim Mereth had been peaceful before the Atmoran expedition brought war.

DAc0da therefore deliberately turns the Night-of-Tears/Ysgramor tradition into a **historical dispute inside the continuity**.

### Written records

Additional DAc0da records describe:
- Ysgramor, Yngol, and Ylgar surviving Saarthal and returning to Atmora;
- regrouping for another invasion;
- Wintercaller-unit material tied to the same conflict.

# Where the works differ

## Jhunal role
VIGILANT / UNSLAAD:
- emphasize Jhunal's Saarthal/Magnus-fire theft and dragon-experiment history.

GLENMORIL:
- emphasizes colored Owl/Jhunal identities and Night-of-Tears continuity.

DAc0da:
- focuses more heavily on Ysgramor/Yngol and contested Atmoran/Snow-Elf history.

## Historical reliability
UNSLAAD:
- mythic recollection, Owl testimony, frozen-memory traditions.

VIGILANT:
- fragmented biography, relics, in-world authored testimony.

GLENMORIL:
- Owl-network history and identity records.

DAc0da:
- directly stages mutually hostile witness accounts about the conquest.

No single work supplies a neutral historian.

---

# Important contradiction

A simplistic account would say:

**Night of Tears -> Snow Elves attack Saarthal -> Ysgramor retaliates.**

DAc0da deliberately complicates that narrative with Atmoran witnesses who claim:
- Ysgramor deceived his own people;
- the migration/conquest had prior political violence behind it;
- purges extended beyond battlefield retaliation.

This does not automatically invalidate Yngol's account.

It establishes **competing in-world histories**.

Lorekeeper should preserve both.

---

# Frozen Atmora relationship

UNSLAAD additionally makes Atmora a place where:
- time stops;
- death freezes;
- memory stops flowing;
- old bodies/roads/waters become arrested.

DAc0da also uses time/memory instability elsewhere, but does not directly state that UNSLAAD's frozen-Atmora mechanism caused its Saarthal history.

Do not equate:
- frozen Atmora;
- Dragon Break;
- mnemolichite;
- Lyg memory erasure;
- Blue Star/Radiance

without direct evidence.

# Current synthesis

High confidence:

**VICN intentionally reuses Saarthal / Night of Tears / Atmora as one of its major shared historical knots.**

Also high confidence:

**the continuity does not present one clean, uncontested history of that knot.**

The correct model is:
- repeated named event;
- recurring actors;
- repeated Owl/Jhunal/Magnus material;
- conflicting historical witnesses;
- later memory/time reinterpretation.

Lorekeeper should preserve the conflict rather than select one canonicalized mod-history account.
