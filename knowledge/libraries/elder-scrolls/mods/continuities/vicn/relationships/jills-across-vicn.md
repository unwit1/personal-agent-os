# Jills — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

## DAc0da

DAc0da gives explicit explanatory dialogue about **Jills**.

The Monk describes them as female dragons whose function includes repairing/correcting the flow of time.

Later DAc0da material says they can:
- censor unstable events;
- optimize/reassign participants and histories;
- interact with time breaches and contradictory outcomes.

These are DAc0da-specific statements and remain attributed to its speakers/records.

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD independently contains Jill-named trial bosses:
- Thunder-Scaled Jill;
- Elja the Void-Jill;
- Borgasa the Thunder-Shield;
- Frekshild the Thunder-Shield.

Several have transformed forms using a custom **Dragonord Gargoyle Race**:
- Eljaalithathisalif Hate-Fire;
- Borgaasaalthoom;
- Fraalxshildadoon.

The Jill names and dragon-like transformations are hard implementation evidence.

UNSLAAD does not currently provide ordinary dialogue defining them as temporal repairers.

## Continuity classification

**Jill vocabulary across UNSLAAD and DAc0da: high-confidence shared VICN node.**

What is not yet established:
- that UNSLAAD's Jill bosses perform DAc0da-style temporal censorship;
- that they are the same individual Jills seen/referenced in DAc0da;
- that their transformed forms represent the universal Jill body;
- that all Thunder-Scale/Void-Jill gear is temporal technology.

Cross-retrieve; do not flatten.

## Staff of the Jills

UNSLAAD also contains a named **Staff of the Jills**. Its description reduces time between Shouts by 20%. It is found in a Dragon's Peak reward chest and becomes craftable after **Arkved** is completed.

This strengthens Jill terminology as an intentional UNSLAAD node, but the item does not explain Jill temporal-censorship mechanics and should not inherit DAc0da's definition automatically.
