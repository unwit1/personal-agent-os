# Arkved / Oneiromancer — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

## GLENMORIL

GLENMORIL's Oneiromancer material identifies Arkved as:
- a Third-Era figure;
- someone punished by Vaermina and imprisoned in nightmares;
- a direct connection/key for movement toward Quagmire;
- an unreliable but active participant in the Oneiromancer sequence.

Romion explicitly discusses Arkved in the Oneiromancer mission, and dedicated Arkved negotiation scenes exist inside that arc.

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD contains:
- a side quest named **Arkved**;
- multiple **Arkved the Dreamer** forms.

Arkved directly says:
- he made a deal with **the Oneiromancer**;
- she asked him to guide the player;
- she asked him to collect payment.

He also discusses:
- nightmares;
- dream/reality equivalence;
- frozen Atmoran dreams.

## Continuity assessment

This is a **high-confidence direct cross-work bridge** because:
- the same named Arkved recurs;
- the same distinctive Oneiromancer relationship recurs;
- both appearances are embedded in nightmare/dream-realm navigation.

## Boundary

Do not yet assume:
- every Arkved form is one continuous physical body;
- the exact chronology between GLENMORIL and UNSLAAD is settled;
- every Oneiromancer manifestation is one body/state.

The common Arkved ↔ Oneiromancer relationship is strong; exact embodiment/chronology remains source-aware.
