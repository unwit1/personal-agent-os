# Oracle Iridescent — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

**Oracle Iridescent** now appears independently in both GLENMORIL and UNSLAAD, with a third provenance layer from reused apocryphal/developer material.

## GLENMORIL

In the Oneiromancer material, Romion says old records describe **Zuzu the Oneiromancer** as:
- daughter of a Dumb Old Giant who served as Ysgramor's counselor;
- someone who begged the **Oracle Iridescent** for knowledge;
- someone who then gained the power to come and go in dreams.

Most other records about Zuzu are absent.

Ozwald hypothesizes she intentionally removed information about herself before leaving Nirn.

Thus GLENMORIL treats Oracle Iridescent as:
- a source of rare knowledge;
- directly connected to dream-travel / Oneiromancer power;
- part of an intentionally incomplete historical record.

## Shared VICN node

High-confidence common ground:
- the exact title **Oracle Iridescent** recurs across Vicn works;
- the node is associated with exceptional knowledge;
- dream/memory/identity instability surrounds both appearances;
- both works connect the node to larger Jhunal/Ysgramor/Atmora-adjacent material.

## Identity boundary

Do **not** currently assert:

**GLENMORIL Oracle Iridescent = KINMUNE / Queen Ayrenn**

as a settled fact.

Why:
- GLENMORIL does not name Ayrenn or KINMUNE in the Oneiromancer record;
- UNSLAAD's KINMUNE identity comes from reused external/apocryphal material staged inside the mod;
- the shared title is strong continuity evidence but not sufficient for a total identity merge.
