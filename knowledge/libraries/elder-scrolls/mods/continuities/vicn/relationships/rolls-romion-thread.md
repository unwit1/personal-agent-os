# Rolls-On-Roads / Romion — cross-VICN relationship

Parent continuity: `tes.mod.vicn`

## GLENMORIL evidence

In **Brain-Billy's Sting**, Rolls-On-Roads describes an unnamed former Psijic friend who:

- was an Altmer;
- was eccentric;
- studied the Dwemer;
- later found employment with the Thalmor;
- disappeared from Rolls's life.

When the player describes **Romion** as an Altmer Dwemer researcher with glasses, Rolls strongly suspects Romion may be that old friend.

GLENMORIL originally preserved this as a high-confidence identity hypothesis because Rolls did not directly say the friend's name.

Romion's own GLENMORIL material independently fits the description:

- Dwemer researcher;
- Eton Nir Research Institute;
- Thalmor-associated research;
- later Witch-Hunter association.

## DAc0da evidence

DAc0da removes the remaining naming ambiguity.

Rolls-On-Roads explicitly names **Romion** as:

- an old friend;
- a former colleague;
- an Altmer;
- a Dwemer scholar;
- a fellow apprentice;
- someone who loved machinery;
- someone who shared his interest in the Vanos siblings' Dwemer adventures.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA49`, `00CA4A`, `00CA4C`, `00CA4E`.

## Parent-continuity conclusion

Within the VICN continuity, the combined evidence upgrades:

> **Rolls's unnamed former Psijic friend ↔ Romion**

from a GLENMORIL-only high-confidence hypothesis to a **high-confidence shared-character relationship**.

The precise chronology of every Romion employment stage should still be sourced to the work that states it.

## What can be promoted

Safe parent-level claims:

- Rolls-On-Roads and Romion knew one another before the present stories.
- Their relationship dates to a Psijic/apprentice period.
- Romion is an Altmer Dwemer specialist.
- Rolls remembers him as an eccentric friend/colleague.
- GLENMORIL's Romion biography is consistent with DAc0da's named recollection.

## What should remain source-local

Do not automatically use DAc0da alone to prove:

- every GLENMORIL Romion employment detail;
- exact dates;
- when Romion left the Psijics;
- why the friendship ended;
- that every mention of an unnamed eccentric Psijic elsewhere must be Romion.
