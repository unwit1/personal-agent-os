# Harakk — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

**Harakk** is now a high-confidence recurring VICN node across **UNSLAAD** and **DAc0da**.

The recurrence is strengthened by shared:
- Atmoran context;
- sailors/voyage imagery;
- Snow Whales;
- frozen sea/sky imagery;
- Yngol-adjacent material.

The exact ontology of Harakk is intentionally left unresolved.

## DAc0da

### Stranded Harakk

DAc0da has a named Atmoran route/location:

**Stranded Harakk**

It is a major opening region in the Yngol/Hgelhelm route.

Other implementation names include Harakk-route/ship cells and a Harakk dead-dragon-skeleton record in a branch-dependent Yngol aftermath.

### Yngol and Harakk

In Drowned Nighthawk:
- Yngol remembers a sky filled with **Snow Whales**;
- his voyage belongs to the same frozen Atmoran route;
- in one branch, after reclaiming his dragon identity, he chooses to fly toward **Harakk**;
- Yngol explicitly identifies Harakk as **one of the twin stars**;
- he expects to become stardust and watch over the player's night.

DAc0da's generic Atmoran voice vocabulary also independently includes Harakk.

## Cross-work synthesis

The rare exact name plus the shared Snow-Whale/frozen-voyage context makes this stronger than coincidence.

High confidence:
- Harakk belongs to a recurring VICN Atmoran mythic/cultural vocabulary.
- Snow Whales and voyage imagery surround Harakk in both works.
- DAc0da explicitly gives Harakk a **stellar** meaning.
- UNSLAAD explicitly gives Harakk **sailor/warrior/wreck** meanings.

## Ontology boundary

Do **not** yet collapse all uses into one literal object.

Possible models include:
- a star after which a people/ship/route is named;
- a cultural homeland or company associated with the star;
- a ship or fleet identity that inherits the name;
- deliberately mythic name reuse across several categories.

Current evidence does not decide among them.

Do not assert:
- Wreck of Harakk = the same physical site as Stranded Harakk;
- Harakk sailors = DAc0da's Yngol party;
- Harakk Warrior's Final Note was written in DAc0da's timeline;
- the twin star is literally a ship.
