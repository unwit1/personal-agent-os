## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD gives the clearest direct Elder-Wood testimony.

### Orlando the Knowledgeable

Orlando says:
- he and the **Black Owl** come from the same place;
- that place is in the far-off icy north;
- they come from the depths of a forgotten **Elder Wood**;
- "we miss our people."

This is direct origin/kinship testimony.

### Hermit

The Hermit independently dreams of:
- frozen mountains;
- an **elder wood**;
- priests praying for the end of endless winter;
- a pilgrim whose Shout clears the sky;
- a reflected golden dragon whose name he does not know.

Because this is dream material, it should remain a separate witness from Orlando's origin statement.

### Relic family

UNSLAAD also contains a broad **Elder Wood** equipment tradition:

The equipment family proves the phrase is not an isolated line of dialogue.

## Current synthesis

High confidence:
- Elder Wood is an intentional recurring VICN node.
- It is tied to the far-northern Owl/Orlando origin complex.
- UNSLAAD expands the phrase into a material/relic tradition.
