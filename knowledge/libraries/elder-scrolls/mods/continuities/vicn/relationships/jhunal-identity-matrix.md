Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

This page answers one narrow question:

**How should Lorekeeper relate the many Jhunal/Owl identities without flattening them into one body?**

It complements:
- `jhunal-saarthal-owls-thread.md`
- `orlando-the-knowledgeable-thread.md`

## 1. Original / deific Jhunal

Status:
- **referent exists as a remembered god/identity**
- exact body/continuity unresolved

### UNSLAAD evidence

Jhunal the Gray says he has used the name **Jhunal** for thousands of years until people can no longer distinguish him from their god.

He explicitly implies:
1. there was a distinction between himself and the god Jhunal;
2. collective forgetting erased that distinction.

### Consequence

Do not globally define:
**Jhunal the Gray = original god Jhunal**

The source itself says the distinction once existed.

## 2. Jhunal the Gray

Source:
- UNSLAAD

Direct identities/titles:
- Jhunal the Gray
- **Gray Owl** — explicitly self-identified

Strong evidence:
- Bell-Tower alias resolves the speaker to a Jhunal-the-Gray form;
- that actor calls himself Gray Owl;
- several Jhunal-the-Gray forms wear **Gray Owl's Mask** through default outfits;
- post-Mq07 Gray-Owl craft text calls the Gray Owl the **second CHICK** and an Atmoran mage seeking the "dragon";
- he says he has used the name Jhunal for millennia.

Contradictory/qualifying evidence:
- Hermit calls Austella's influential Jhunal figure a **pretender**;
- Gray Owl himself says the original distinction from the god has been forgotten;
- Dragon's Peak contains separate physical inspection representations for **Jhunal the Gray** and **Gray Owl**.

Current model:
- **high-confidence title/narrative identity link**
- **multiple physical representations/body states**
- **not proven identical to original god Jhunal**

## 6. Orlando the Knowledgeable

Sources:
- VIGILANT
- GLENMORIL
- UNSLAAD

Direct recurrence:
- exact named identity appears in all three works.

GLENMORIL:
- Orlando-family actor/body appears on the Gray-Owl side;
- Orlando was once regarded as an **incarnation of Jhunal**;
- he belongs to the Owl/Atmora network.

UNSLAAD:
- Orlando is in Owl Faction;
- distinguishes himself from the **Black Owl** while saying they share an origin;
- connects himself to Elder Wood, Blue Star, EGG, and prior meetings.

Current model:
- Orlando is a **recurring Owl/Jhunal-associated manifestation/identity node**.

Do not globally assert:
- Orlando = Black Owl;
- Orlando = Jhunal the Black;
- Orlando = Jhunal the Gray;
- Orlando = original god Jhunal.

"Incarnation of Jhunal" is a GLENMORIL source-local claim and should remain attributed.

## 10. Retrieval policy

If asked:

### "Is Gray Owl Jhunal?"
Answer:
- **Yes at the UNSLAAD narrative/title layer:** Jhunal the Gray explicitly identifies himself as Gray Owl.
- **No simple god-identity conclusion follows:** he also says he used the Jhunal name until people forgot the distinction from the god.

### "Are all the Owls Jhunal?"
Answer:
- several colored Owls are directly named Jhunal variants;
- exact shared-body/deity ontology remains unresolved.

### "Is Orlando Jhunal?"
Answer:
- GLENMORIL says Orlando was once regarded as an incarnation of Jhunal;
- other works preserve Orlando as a distinct recurring identity;
- do not globally collapse Orlando into every Jhunal/Owl form.

### "Is VIGILANT's Jhunal the same as UNSLAAD's?"
Answer:
- continuity relationship is very strong;
- Laza/dragon/Saarthal/Owl evidence independently repeats;
- one uninterrupted physical body is not proven.

---

## 11. Core rule

The best current model is:

**one intentional Jhunal/Owl identity complex with direct local identities, repeated titles, fragmented bodies, incarnational claims, and unresolved global ontology.**

Lorekeeper should merge the **relationship network**, not the **bodies**.
