# Hare / word-rewriting — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

This thread connects a rare **Hare + words that reshape identity/form** motif across **DAc0da** and **UNSLAAD**.

The exact hare actors are not yet proven to be one individual.

## DAc0da

### Hare Mound

DAc0da contains:
- location: **Hare Mound**
- insight: MESG 00475B
- interactable: Hare Mound
- nearby named encounter: **Hare's Servant**

The insight is framed as an inscription by **Hans the Fox**.

It says, in summary:
- Hans sealed a cursed hare of Forelgrim;
- Frostwood plague is spreading through Atmoran forests;
- the forest beast should not be listened to;
- **its words will reshape you**.

This is unusually specific language about speech/words altering form or identity.

## UNSLAAD

### Heavy-eyed hare

In the Gral / Mindok / Owl Forest cycle:

- the Owl Forest withers;
- Woodland Men move among the dead trees;
- Gral increasingly listens to a **heavy-eyed hare**;
- the hare teaches that things do not precede words;
- words separate things from Sithis;
- it tempts Gral toward a world before that separation.

The same story later transforms Gral's body into:
- ink;
- quills;
- parchment.

Thus the hare appears inside a direct **words <-> bodily/form rewriting** narrative.

### Strange Hare

UNSLAAD also has NPC:

Implementation:

This creates a hard implementation edge from a Hare form into the wider:
- Owl;

network.

### Mask of the Hare

UNSLAAD also contains:
- **Mask of the Hare**

It becomes reconstructable after **The Final Journey**.

No direct wearer is currently resolved.

## Cross-work synthesis

High confidence:
- both works deliberately use **Hare** imagery in Atmoran/mythic contexts;
- both connect the Hare motif to **words that alter or redefine form/reality**;
- Herma-Mora / Woodland-Man-adjacent material surrounds both clusters;
- Hans the Fox is directly tied to the DAc0da Hare-Mound inscription, while UNSLAAD independently reuses Hans/Pelinaalilargus elsewhere.
