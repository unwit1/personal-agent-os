# Jhunal / Saarthal / Owls — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

This thread is independently supported by UNSLAAD, VIGILANT, and GLENMORIL.

## UNSLAAD

Jhunal the Gray says:
- the other Owls were last together at **Saarthal** during the **Night of Tears**;
- a barrier around Lizz is one of the **Arcanas of the Owls**;
- the Arcana of Magnus he uses was stolen from Saarthal.

UNSLAAD also directly links:
- Jhunal the Gray ↔ Gray Owl;
- Gray Owl ↔ Atmora / Saarthal / higher planes;
- Owl research ↔ dragon-soul/vessel experiments.

## VIGILANT

VIGILANT independently contains:
- **Jhunal the Owl**;
- Arcana of Jhunal / Arcane Fire;
- testimony that Jhunal stole a **fire rune / Wisdom of Magnus** from Saarthal;
- environmental support through Burnt Casimir carrying the Arcana.

This makes the repeated **Jhunal + Saarthal + Magnus-fire theft** claim especially strong across works.

## GLENMORIL

GLENMORIL independently contains:
- **The Night of Tears**;
- **The Owls**;
- Jhunal the Black;
- Jhunal the White;
- Gray Owl material;
- Orlando describing himself as an Atmoran mage once regarded as an incarnation of Jhunal.

## Continuity model

High confidence:
- Owl/Jhunal identities are deliberately recurrent across Vicn works;
- Saarthal / Night of Tears is a shared origin/history node;
- Magnus-associated fire/arcana theft is independently repeated in VIGILANT and UNSLAAD.

Unresolved:
- exact body identity among Gray/Black/White Owl;
- exact relation to VIGILANT's Jhunal the Owl;
- whether Orlando is incarnation, successor, mask, vessel, or another category;
- exact relation to an original/deific Jhunal.

## Retrieval rule

Cross-link these identities densely, but do not flatten:
- Jhunal the Gray;
- Jhunal the Black;
- Jhunal the White;
- Jhunal the Owl;
- Orlando;
- generic Owl actors

into one body/person without source-specific evidence.
