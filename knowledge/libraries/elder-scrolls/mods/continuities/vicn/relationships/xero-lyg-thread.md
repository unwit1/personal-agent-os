# Xero-Lyg — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: `tes.mod.vicn`

This page tracks **VICN-plugin uses** of Xero-Lyg separately from external/apocryphal source texts.

## GLENMORIL

In **Jazel Phantom**, Ja'zel directly attributes a **dark Radiance that dwells in Flesh** to:

> **Xero-Lyg, the Black Star beside the Serpent constellation**

He also says Xero-Lyg's myth strangely coincides with **Namira**.

This is Ja'zel's source-local cosmology and remains speaker-attributed.

GLENMORIL therefore directly connects:

`Xero-Lyg -> Black Star -> Serpent -> dark Radiance -> Flesh`

## DAc0da

Unique NPC **Crabbimarco**, speaking as a self-proclaimed Lyg necromancer, says:

- **Xero-Lyg** and her son were involved in a slave revolt;
- that revolt ruined his attempt to seize power;
- he exploited the point when the world was liberated/ascended;
- he is now unstable and expects to become attached to this kalpa.

DAc0da evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00492C`.

DAc0da therefore supplies an independent **Lyg historical/kalpic** use of Xero-Lyg.

## DAc0da's separate Blue-Star / Radiance material

Elsewhere DAc0da independently contains:

- Kagrenac's **Blue Star** whose radiance spills from the future into the past;
- Dumac's question about a hidden Blue Star;
- the Sea of Radiance / Mnemo-Li material.

DAc0da does **not** directly identify any of those with Xero-Lyg.

That distinction is important because GLENMORIL explicitly calls Xero-Lyg a **Black Star**, whereas DAc0da's major star/radiance thread is framed around a **Blue Star**.

## External source layer

The broader Elder Scrolls apocryphal library independently contains Xero-Lyg texts such as:

- **Invocations of Xero-Lyg**
- **Un-Stars**
- **Aesoterica**
- **The Tract of Merid-Nunda**

These external texts can inform source comparison, but repeated vocabulary inherited from shared apocrypha is not by itself evidence of a new VICN-specific relationship.

## High-confidence continuity claim

High confidence:

- both DAc0da and GLENMORIL independently use the exact named entity **Xero-Lyg** inside their own dialogue;
- GLENMORIL gives a Black-Star/Radiance/Flesh model;
- DAc0da gives a Lyg/slave-revolt/kalpic-instability account.

This is a real cross-work entity bridge.

## Current synthesis boundary

Do not yet assert that:

- DAc0da's Xero-Lyg is certainly the same manifestation/state as GLENMORIL's Black Star;
- DAc0da's **Blue Star** is Xero-Lyg;
- Xero-Lyg is Mnemo-Li;
- Xero-Lyg is Radiance itself;
- Crabbimarco's comic/distorted history is objectively complete;
- Ja'zel's Xero-Lyg/Namira comparison is objective cosmology.

The correct parent-layer model is:

> **shared named entity, independently interpreted by multiple VICN works, with ontology still unresolved.**
