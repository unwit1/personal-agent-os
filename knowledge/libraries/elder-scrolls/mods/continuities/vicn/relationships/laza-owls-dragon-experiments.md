# Laza / Owls / dragon experiments — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

Laza is one of the clearest three-work continuity nodes linking VIGILANT, GLENMORIL, and UNSLAAD.

## VIGILANT

VIGILANT directly establishes:
- Laza as an implemented Coldharbour/main-quest figure;
- a dedicated Laza book cluster;
- three color-Owl books:
  - Laza and the White Owl
  - Laza and the Gray Owl
  - Laza and the Black Owl.

Most importantly, **Jhunal the Owl** directly says:
- he used Laza as an obedient shepherd;
- Laza harvested dragons for him;
- Jhunal wanted dragon souls;
- Jhunal's larger aim involved becoming a dragon.

This is direct participant testimony from VIGILANT's Jhunal.

## GLENMORIL

GLENMORIL preserves the exact text **Laza and the Owls**.

It sequences Laza through:

1. **White Owl**
   - shepherd -> wolf -> wind transformation imagery;
   - warns that the wind of Order may become a wolf again.

2. **Gray Owl**
   - tells Laza to blind himself;
   - directs him toward the bottom of **Kyne's Spring**.

3. **Black Owl**
   - tells him to plunge a dagger into the Spring;
   - frames corruption/transformation and loss as bringing him closer to those he lost.

GLENMORIL separately resolves:
- White Owl -> Jhunal the White;
- Black Owl -> Jhunal the Black;
- Gray-Owl material -> Orlando/Jhunal identity network.

These later identities are cross-work continuity evidence, not substitutes for the unrecovered exact VIGILANT book prose.
