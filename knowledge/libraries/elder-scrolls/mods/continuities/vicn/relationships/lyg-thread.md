# Lyg — cross-VICN continuity thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD contains two independent Lyg traditions.

**Herkel** describes Lyg as:
- an adjacent place;
- accessible through a mirror made from Merid-Nunda's essence;
- a place where fate can be changed;
- dangerous to remain in because of the Grabbers.

**Khev the Dreugh King** uses Lyg in his Nu-Mantia theology and later says his voice is dead "like Lyg."

These two UNSLAAD witnesses are related by the shared place-name but should remain separately attributable.

## DAc0da

DAc0da's Ghost Choir material independently contains the exact terms:
- LYG;
- Xero=LYG;
- a **mass erasure** in LYG;
- a "memory" project.

DAc0da also independently uses **Adjacent Place** and **Grabbers** terminology elsewhere in its corpus.

## High-confidence continuity claim

The recurrence of the rare exact place-name **Lyg**, together with Adjacent-Place/Grabber vocabulary across UNSLAAD and DAc0da, is strong evidence that the two works intentionally participate in the same VICN cosmological vocabulary.

## What is not yet established

Do not yet state as fact that:
- DAc0da's mass erasure caused Khev's "dead like Lyg" condition;
- Lyg is intrinsically a memory-erasure realm;
- every adjacent place is Lyg;
- Merid-Nunda, the Grabbers, Ghost Choir, and Khev participate in one single event;
- the UNSLAAD mirror and DAc0da's LYG references describe the same point in time.

These are priority comparison hypotheses for later source resolution.
