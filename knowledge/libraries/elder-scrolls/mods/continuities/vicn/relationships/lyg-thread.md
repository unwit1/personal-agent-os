# Lyg — cross-VICN continuity thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD contains two independent Lyg traditions.

**Herkel** describes Lyg as:
- an adjacent place;
- accessible through a mirror made from Merid-Nunda's essence;
- a place where fate can be changed;
- dangerous to remain in because of the Grabbers.

**Khev the Dreugh King** uses Lyg in his Nu-Mantia theology and later says his voice is dead "like Lyg."

These two UNSLAAD witnesses are related by the shared place-name but should remain separately attributable.

### UNSLAAD GC9 Insight

UNSLAAD also contains MESG 034C6340 / **zzzCrbMsgInsightGC9**.

Its visible communication log reports MEHRUNES activation by the Magne-Ge, **memory evaporation in the LYG sector**, disposal of the current timeline, and purging of contaminated memories.

The visible prose does not name Ghost Choir 9; the **GC9 association comes from the record EditorID**.

## DAc0da

DAc0da's Ghost Choir material independently contains the exact terms:
- LYG;
- Xero=LYG;
- a **mass erasure** in LYG;
- a "memory" project.

DAc0da also independently uses **Adjacent Place** and **Grabbers** terminology elsewhere in its corpus.

### Crabbimarco / stolen names

DAc0da unique NPC **Crabbimarco** explicitly calls himself a necromancer of **Lyg**.

He says he once served the **King of Dreugh**, but cannot remember that king's name because **the Grabbers took it from him**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00492C`.

He also names **Xero-Lyg and her son** as participants in a slave revolt that ruined his attempt to seize power.

This gives the DAc0da Lyg thread a direct internal relation:

`Lyg -> Grabbers -> stolen name/memory -> Xero-Lyg`

The comic/distorted register of Crabbimarco's dialogue remains a reliability qualifier, but the rare vocabulary is explicit.

### In Grabbers' Hands

DAc0da quest **In Grabbers' Hands** independently implements a name/nymic recovery mechanic:

- Boss alias script: `DcdNymicStealerAliasScript`;
- quest property: `NymicObj`;
- concrete nymic object: `zdcdItemNamePrisoner`;
- objective: retrieve the Book of the Prisoner and **restore your name**.

The compiled Papyrus body is unavailable, so the exact runtime rename call remains unresolved.

Together with Crabbimarco, DAc0da now supplies both **dialogue testimony** and **mechanical quest structure** linking Grabbers to stolen names.

## High-confidence continuity claim

The recurrence of the rare exact place-name **Lyg**, Adjacent-Place/Grabber vocabulary, DAc0da's concrete **stolen-name/nymic** mechanics, and an UNSLAAD implementation-tagged **GC9** memory-erasure record makes the UNSLAAD ↔ DAc0da relationship substantially stronger than a thematic resemblance.
