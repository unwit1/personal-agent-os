# Hjalti / Alcaire / changing names — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

This thread connects recurring Hjalti material in UNSLAAD, GLENMORIL, and DAc0da while preserving each work's own testimony.

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD gives three internally consistent references.

### Aisha
Aisha says an earlier male visitor:
- was someone she disliked;
- said he was **from Alcaire**.

### Ulliss
Ulliss independently says:
- the visitor was called **Hjalti at first**;
- he had a different name every time he visited;
- he eventually stopped returning.

### Mifa Xanadu
Mifa says:
- a little boy named **Hjalti** commissioned custom armor;
- paid in gold;
- left centuries earlier.

Together, UNSLAAD deliberately associates:
- Hjalti;
- Alcaire;
- changing names;
- ancient repeated appearances.
