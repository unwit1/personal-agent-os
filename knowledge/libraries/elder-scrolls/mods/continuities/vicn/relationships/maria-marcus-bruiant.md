# Maria / Marcus Bruiant — Vicn cross-mod bridge

Parent continuity: `tes.mod.vicn`

Sources:
- GLENMORIL — **The Cat and the Chick**
- VIGILANT — Marcus Bruiant's dated journals

## GLENMORIL

Ja'zel names Maria's child's father:
**Marcus Bruiant**.

Orlando says Maria:
- was raised by Chick Traders;
- became involved with a noble from Cyrodiil;
- was nearly freed;
- became pregnant;
- was abandoned;
- returned to prostitution.

Orlando says the noble returned home after paying heavily over the dispute.

## VIGILANT

Marcus Bruiant's 4E 199 journal says:
- he repeatedly traveled to **Markarth**;
- had an affair there;
- paid a **Khajiit slaver** 10,000 gold to acquire/free the woman;
- learned she was pregnant;
- threw her out;
- paid support and a large civic donation after she appealed to the Jarl.

VIGILANT separately establishes that Marcus's son **Julius already existed** during this period.

## Reconstruction

The shared proper name Marcus Bruiant, Markarth setting, Khajiit-slaver link, pregnancy, abandonment, and financial settlement produce a very strong cross-mod correspondence.

High-confidence Vicn reconstruction:
**Maria is the unnamed Markarth woman in Marcus Bruiant's VIGILANT journal.**

Therefore Maria's child is **not Julius**.

## Provenance rule

- VIGILANT's journal does not name the woman Maria.
- GLENMORIL names Marcus but does not reproduce the journal.

The identification belongs at the **Vicn parent-continuity layer** and should not be rewritten as though either source independently states the full merged identity.
