# Ja'cobee / Aisha / Ulliss — cross-VICN thread

Parent continuity: tes.mod.vicn

This thread is supported directly by GLENMORIL and UNSLAAD.

## GLENMORIL — Last Chick Trader

GLENMORIL's **The Last Chick Trader** quest directly stages:

- **Ja'cobee the Last Chick Trader**
- **Ulliss**

in one dialogue sequence.

Ulliss says:
- she was walking with **Aisha** when a booming noise and snow transported her into the scene;
- Aisha is a cat;
- Aisha has become attached to Ja'cobee because he is lonely;
- Ja'cobee warns Ulliss not to touch the EGG.

Ja'cobee independently says:
- he is the **last Chick Trader**;
- the EGG is not completely broken;
- he broke/crushed a CHICK before it could mature;
- the current dream began when the EGG cracked;
- the EGG/Owl secret is central to his duty.

This gives GLENMORIL a direct named network:

**Ja'cobee ↔ Ulliss ↔ Aisha ↔ EGG / Chick Trader**

## UNSLAAD

UNSLAAD independently contains all three named characters and expands their history.

### Ja'cobee
UNSLAAD says:
- Ja'cobee is continuous with the earlier Fluffy form, but memory/self-recognition is unstable;
- Ja'cobee later raises **Lizz**, Ulliss's daughter;
- he eventually sends Lizz back through time to Ulliss;
- he says there are two Ja'cobees / "this side" and "other side" language;
- his death/passage is associated with Aisha.

UNSLAAD also contains direct EGG evidence:
- Fluffy remembers an Egg;
- Orlando says Ja'cobee broke its shell;
- the player crushed its contents.

### Aisha
UNSLAAD presents Aisha as:
- Ulliss's longtime companion;
- closely tied to Fluffy/Ja'cobee;
- later associated with Ja'cobee's peaceful death/passage.

### Ulliss
UNSLAAD gives Ulliss a much larger biography:
- Austella's daughter;
- artificial dragon-soul/ice origin according to Jhunal;
- multiple body states;
- mother of Lizz;
- central member of the post-story family group with Aisha and Fluffy/Ja'cobee.
