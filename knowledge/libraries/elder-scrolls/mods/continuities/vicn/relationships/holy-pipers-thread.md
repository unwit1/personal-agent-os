# Holy Pipers — cross-VICN organization thread

Parent continuity: `tes.mod.vicn`

The **Holy Pipers** are a direct recurring institution across VIGILANT, GLENMORIL, and DAc0da.

This thread preserves substantial differences between their source-local states instead of treating every appearance as one simultaneous organization profile.

## VIGILANT

VIGILANT directly establishes the **Society of the Holy Pipers** through:

- named Holy Piper NPCs;
- a direct anti-Piper mission message;
- **Report: Sacred Anatomancer**;
- the **Sacred Anatomancer** quest.

VIGILANT evidence says:

- the Sacred Anatomancer swayed Vigilants of Stendarr;
- he organized a splinter group called the Society of the Holy Pipers;
- the group became involved in kidnapping, torture, and flesh/anatomancy practices;
- Piper doctrine invokes Radiance, the Song of Life, Blue-Star imagery, and targeted extermination.

Safe VIGILANT relation:

`Vigilants of Stendarr -> schism/recruitment -> Society of the Holy Pipers`

## GLENMORIL

GLENMORIL independently treats the Holy Pipers as an **excommunicated/extremist Vigilant branch**.

Its material includes:

- kidnappings around Falkreath;
- Laelnoah and other victims;
- Piper's Hill;
- perception/information hazard language;
- Sacred Anatomancer leadership traditions;
- Psijic concern that merely knowing/listening can expose people to Piper influence.

GLENMORIL therefore appears to represent a later or further-developed Holy-Piper operational state, though exact chronology must remain source-supported.

## DAc0da

DAc0da's **Vigilant Athanasius** directly identifies himself as:

> Keeper of Stendarr of the Holy Pipers branch.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00554C`.

Athanasius describes his branch's ordinary mission as:

- traveling the land;
- spreading Stendarr's teachings;
- helping the less fortunate;
- rescuing people in distress.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00555E`.

He says most Holy Pipers are not fighters and frames his own combat competence as a legacy of former Imperial Legion service.

## Why the DAc0da evidence matters

DAc0da prevents the parent continuity from defining **Holy Piper** as synonymous with “anatomancy cultist” in every context.

The same institutional name can include, or once have included, a benevolent Stendarr-oriented branch represented by Athanasius.

That creates several plausible but unresolved models:

1. DAc0da's branch predates the Sacred Anatomancer's corruption/schism;
2. multiple Holy-Piper branches coexist;
3. Athanasius belongs to a surviving benign tradition that shares the name;
4. temporal distortion exposes a different point/fate-line in the organization's history.

No processed source yet selects one model conclusively.

## Radiance continuity

The Holy-Piper recurrence also carries a direct cross-work **Radiance** bridge.

### VIGILANT

Holy-Piper/Sacred-Anatomancer liturgy explicitly invokes **Radiance** as something obstructed by designated enemies and tied to the group's Song-of-Life doctrine.

### DAc0da

Athanasius is both:
- explicitly a **Holy Piper**;
- the central character of **The Sea of Radiance**;
- a speaker who says he has **attained Radiance**.

The same DAc0da arc includes **Letter to a Friend**, which directs a seeker of Radiance toward VIGILANT's **Altano** to learn compassion through suffering.

This triangulation makes Radiance a high-confidence **institution-adjacent recurring concept** across VIGILANT and DAc0da.

It does not prove that the Anatomancer's Radiance doctrine and Athanasius's attained state are technically identical.

## Chronology caution

A simple “DAc0da shows the Holy Pipers before they became corrupt” chronology is **not currently supported**.

VIGILANT's **Report: Sacred Anatomancer** dates the Anatomancer's reappearance and organization of the Society of the Holy Pipers to **winter 4E 198**.

DAc0da's Athanasius says he fought in the **Great War** and later joined the Holy Pipers. His Sea-of-Causality appearance is tied to a Solitude naval expedition launched from DAc0da's Skyrim-era present, but DAc0da's setting is heavily affected by time breaches and displaced actors.

Therefore the current parent models should remain:

1. multiple Holy-Piper branches/states can coexist;
2. Athanasius may represent a benign branch not identical to the Anatomancer's Society;
3. DAc0da may expose a temporally displaced or alternate state;
4. the exact institutional relationship is unresolved.

The evidence currently **disfavors treating chronology alone as proof** that Athanasius belongs to a pre-4E-198 organization.

## High-confidence parent claims

Safe to promote:

- Holy Pipers recur by exact name across VIGILANT, GLENMORIL, and DAc0da.
- They are Stendarr/Vigilant-associated.
- Sacred Anatomancer-linked Holy Pipers become an apostate/anatomancy organization in VIGILANT.
- GLENMORIL continues/extensively expands the extremist/perception-hazard organization.
- DAc0da independently contains a Keeper of Stendarr who calls himself part of a Holy-Pipers branch and describes benevolent rescue/teaching duties.

## Current synthesis boundary

Do not assert without further evidence:

- Athanasius served the Sacred Anatomancer;
- Athanasius participated in kidnappings or anatomancy;
- DAc0da's benevolent description disproves the VIGILANT/GLENMORIL apostate state;
- every Holy Piper across all works exists at one chronological moment;
- the institution's entire history can be represented by one fixed moral alignment.

The strongest current model is a **recurring VICN institution with source-dependent historical/branch states**.
