# Pan-Argonia

**Quest:** `zDcdMqArgoEnd`  
**Title:** Pan-Argonia  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:00CA5B`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Structural role

This is an alternate ending state centered on Rolls-On-Roads and the Hist.

The quest aliases:

- `Monk` -> `DAc0da.esm:000D77` — **Rolls-On-Roads**
- `HistMarker` -> a dedicated scene/location marker

There are only four direct dialogue responses, but they are highly consequential.

## Hist influence

Rolls-On-Roads apologizes and says he can hear the **Hist inside him whispering**. He says he must save the Argonians. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA65`.

This is a branch-specific state and should not be retroactively imposed on ordinary Rolls-On-Roads dialogue.

## Reactivating Numidium

Rolls-On-Roads says that with the player's **heart** he will reactivate Numidium and claims he will be able to control/handle it now. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA67`.

This presents an explicit alternate outcome in which the Numidium crisis is not ended but appropriated for a new project.

## Hist devotion

He addresses the Hist directly, saying he drinks its milk and yearns for the "milk" of its words. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA69`.

The metaphor is preserved as source wording; the exact metaphysical mechanism is not explained here.

## Pan-Argonian project

Rolls-On-Roads declares:

- neither elves nor men;
- only "we, the ancient ones";
- roots spread throughout all of Nirn.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA6B`.

This is an explicit supremacist/eradication-oriented alternate ending spoken by a Hist-influenced Rolls-On-Roads.

It should remain clearly branch-scoped and attributed to this altered state.

## Relation to MQ04 Hist anomaly

Inside Numidium, MQ04 already showed strange Hist roots constraining a Jill and Rolls-On-Roads described those Hist as unfamiliar and possibly mad.

Pan-Argonia may be related to that anomaly, but the four dialogue lines do not directly establish the causal chain. The relationship should therefore remain a high-priority comparison rather than a settled identity claim.
