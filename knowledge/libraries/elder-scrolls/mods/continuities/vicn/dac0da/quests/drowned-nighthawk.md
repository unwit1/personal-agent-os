# Drowned Nighthawk

**Quest:** `zDcdSqYngol`  
**Title:** Drowned Nighthawk  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:004B1C`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

This is one of DAc0da's largest side quests. It follows temporally displaced **Yngol, son of Ysgramor**, through repeated rescue decisions, Atmoran/Snow-Elf history, memories that do not fit his expected past, a golden-dragon identity, and a confrontation with Akashiya-Samon.

## Structural progression

The QUST record repeatedly gives the player a choice to help or abandon Yngol. It eventually branches into:

- battles around Yngol's historical contradictions;
- persuasion/duel stages with Samon;
- a recovered dragon identity;
- a final choice over Yngol's end;
- an optional dragon-soul absorption.

Quest aliases define multiple Yngol states plus a separate golden-dragon actor:

- `DAc0da.esm:00316B` — Yngol
- `DAc0da.esm:004DAA` — Yngol state 2
- `DAc0da.esm:004DAB` — Yngol state 3
- `DAc0da.esm:005013` — **Yngol the Tsunaltir**
- `DAc0da.esm:005036` — **Tsuunalinfaxtir**

## Yngol's identity and historical frame

Yngol directly identifies himself as **Yngol, son of Ysgramor**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004B9B`.

He says an enchanter who now goes by **Ahzidal** gave him a runic bracelet intended to protect him from elven magic. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004BA2`, `004BA4`.

Yngol describes his voyage as an expedition toward Mereth that was struck by an extreme storm and ghosts. He remembers a voice from the sky, interprets it as Stuhn's guidance, sees a school of snow whales in the sky, and then loses the remainder of the event. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004BAA`.

## Jylkurfyk and the Snow-Elf conflict

Yngol identifies **Jylkurfyk** as Atmora's largest port city and says a fleet of eighty ships had recently sailed from it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C12`.

He initially believes the Snow Elves attacked the port because they feared Atmoran retaliation and says Ysgramor's main force is already sailing to conquer Mereth. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C14`.

He explicitly frames his hostility toward the Snow Elves through Saarthal and the Night of Tears. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C16`.

Later scene dialogue introduces a contradictory Atmoran perspective: speakers accuse Ysgramor of deceiving the people, say civilians wanted peace and migration away from Atmora's civil war, condemn purges of elves and mixed-blood people, and claim Mereth had been peaceful before the Atmoran expedition brought war there. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DC5` through `004DE0`.

These statements are conflicting in-world testimony and should remain attributed rather than collapsed into one definitive history.

## Dream / alternate-memory material

Yngol says he dreamed of a snowy but warm land without strife where he worked as a blacksmith for a long time and was asked by a mysterious boy to forge armor. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004BA7`.

Later he begins remembering people and facts he says he somehow "knew all along and yet wasn't supposed to," including Hgelhelmson and multiple memories of Periff. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FAA`, `004FAC`.

This is direct evidence that the side quest is exposing alternate or censored memory states, not merely retelling a single historical timeline.

## Yngol and dragon identity

Yngol says Atmoran tradition taught that people once took dragon forms in an earlier kalpa and later shed their scales. He speculates that the golden dragon's scales are also his own. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004CCE`.

He later says Ysgramor told him that Alduin's scales slumber within them and that they must learn their name and shed it; Yngol instead decides to reclaim his dragon name and scales. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DF5`.

Near the climax, Yngol identifies the golden dragon as the other half of his being and recalls its name:

**Tsuunalinfaxtir.**

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FED`, `004FEF`.

The quest actor `DAc0da.esm:005036` is independently named **Tsuunalinfaxtir**, confirming that this is not only figurative dialogue.

## Samon's interpretation

Samon says the golden dragon came from an **Adjacent Place** and that aspects of an old kalpa are entering through the time distortion. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DF9`.

He calls the dragon the center of the local distortion and says it must be removed if the player intends to correct time. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DFD`.

When told the dragon is Yngol's other half, Samon compares the transformation to practices of **Ka Po' Tun** and considers it dangerous. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FB4`.

Yngol, in contrast, interprets Samon as one of the "People of the Serpent" sent because Yngol is approaching death. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FC2`.

## Final state

After reclaiming the dragon, Yngol says its power is burning him from within and will reduce him to dust. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005001`, `005004`.

Depending on the player's choice, he can seek a final warrior's death or choose to fly toward **Harakk**, one of the twin stars, saying he will at least become stardust and watch over the player's night. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00500E`, `00503C`, `005040`.

This ending should be represented as a branching outcome, not one mandatory canonical fate within the mod.
