# Abnur Tharn

**Quest:** `zDcdSqAbnur`  
**Title:** Abnur Tharn  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:00525E`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Actor and disguise

The quest's `Abnur` alias points to `DAc0da.esm:005253` / `zDcdUqAbnurTharn01`, whose displayed name is **Mysterious Battlemage**.

The dialogue repeatedly has him start to reveal his identity or Nibenay connection and then stop, so the disguise is deliberate rather than a missing FULL name.

## Time-breach connection

Abnur says large eastern and western time breaches are forming and that he may be responsible for the **western breach**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005271`.

Later he says he may have been pulled toward the distortion because of **Mannimarco** and is willing to break local causality rather than encounter him again. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052BC`, `0052BE`.

This directly links his displacement to the same breach system used in **Patchwork**.

## Reaction to the Dragonborn

After witnessing the player absorb a dragon soul, Abnur asks whether the player is a descendant of Reman and recognizes the player as Dragonborn. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005282`.

He says divine selection alone would not make someone secure on the Ruby Throne: political support from the people would still be necessary. He then suggests the player may have some different role. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005282`.

This is Abnur's political/historical judgment inside DAc0da, not a neutral rule of succession.

## Time God / Prisoner-style arrival

After seeing the player's improbable intervention, Abnur says he now understands why mystics describe the Time God as insane and complains that the fate of the world is being entrusted to a person who appeared from nowhere. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00527D`.

This does not explicitly use the word Prisoner in his dialogue, but it belongs in retrieval alongside DAc0da's Prisoner/Many-Paths material because it describes the same anomalous hero-arrival pattern.

## Mannimarco

Abnur says there is not enough time in a day to describe Mannimarco's vileness and believes Mannimarco is why he was drawn to the time distortion. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052BC`.

A nearby reaction identifies magic ahead as Mannimarco's. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052B3`.

The full Mannimarco/Mnemolichite arc continues in **Echoes of Mnemolichite**, where a second Abnur actor is present and should be joined in the next pass.

## Epilogue connection

The later `zDcdEpiQAbnur` quest delivers Abnur's more-than-thousand-year-old will to the player. The will explicitly remembers the strange sea and towering Dwemer giant, proving that at least one DAc0da outcome allows Abnur to retain the event long enough to record it before death.
