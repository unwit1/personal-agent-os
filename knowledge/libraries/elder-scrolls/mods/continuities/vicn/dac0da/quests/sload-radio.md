# Sload Radio

**Quest:** `zDcdSqSloadRadio`  
**Title:** Sload Radio  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:00CE40`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

This compact quest is a sequence of Sload broadcasts and is useful primarily for organization/terminology evidence.

## Thras and Ul'vor Kus Alliance Fleet

The broadcast repeatedly identifies its source as the **Thras and Ul'vor Kus Alliance Fleet**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE49`, `00CE50`, `00CE57`, `00CE5E`.

The speakers insist they are not enemies and are present to "help," while repeatedly demanding immediate surrender. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE4B`, `00CE4D`.

## Captain N'Danda

One message says **Captain N'Danda** guarantees the lives and dignity of those who surrender. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE54`.

This is currently only a named organizational/personnel seed; no dedicated NPC identity has yet been resolved.

## Aid / coercion language

The broadcasts promise:

- hot meals;
- beds on Sload floaters;
- rescue from freezing;
- medical treatment;
- relief from fear and pain.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE59`, `00CE5B`, `00CE60`.

The language is explicitly paired with surrender demands, making these messages direct examples of Sload coercive propaganda in DAc0da.

## B'Korgen therapy

The fleet advertises **B'Korgen therapy** as a treatment capable of handling any injury and asks listeners to come aboard and "be reborn." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE5B`.

The exact medical/transformative mechanism is not defined here.

## K'Tora psychotherapy / mindshapers

Another broadcast openly advertises Sload **mind magic** as a way to escape fear and pain and says **K'Tora's psychotherapy** can help a person "discover a new you" and become a **mindshaper**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE60`, `00CE62`.

This is a strong terminology node to retrieve beside Echoes of Mnemolichite's brainwashing and Sload experimentation.
