# The End of All Wishes

**Quest:** `zDcdSqHalfElf`  
**Title:** The End of All Wishes  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:0050DC`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Structural outline

Direct objectives:

1. talk to a hidden child;
2. offer a flower to a golden dragon;
3. report back to the child;
4. talk to a priest;
5. defeat the priest.

The primary aliases are:

- `Child` -> `DAc0da.esm:0050D6` — **Girl**
- `Priest` -> `DAc0da.esm:0050B1` — **Beynhaal**

## Hidden child / long ears

The child hides because she has long ears while the people around her do not. Her brother told her it was unsafe outside. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0050F0`.

She describes her brother as pure white like herself, but without such long ears, and says a king valued him enough to make him a warrior. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0050F3`.

When asked why she has long ears, she says her brother blamed **Herma-Mora** and promised she could be cured, while other people say the cause is strong **Snow Drake blood**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0050F6`.

The quest does not independently resolve which explanation is true.

## Port-town purge / departure

The child says the king took away all the long-eared people and then departed south with many boats. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0050F9`.

She then remembers Snow Drakes and Ice Giants coming from the sea and says her brother defeated them. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0050F9`.

This strongly belongs beside DAc0da's Yngol/Jylkurfyk material, which already contains conflicting testimony about Atmoran treatment of elves and mixed-blood people.

## Golden dragon and flower

The child asks the player to deliver a flower to a shining golden dragon. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0050FC`.

She believes the flower will cheer the dragon god, which will blow away the winter and end the fighting. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0050FF`.

The quest objective confirms that the flower is actually offered to a golden dragon, but this pass does not assume the dragon is identical to Tsuunalinfaxtir without direct form/alias evidence.

## Sacrifice

When the player returns, the child is gone; the record states that she became an offering/sacrifice. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005104`, `005105`.

Beynhaal says she was offered as a shrine maiden to the mountain where their god sleeps, supposedly to end the winter. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005109`.

He says people hope the Snow Elves caused the winter and therefore consider it fitting that someone of their blood be sacrificed to end it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00510C`.

This is direct evidence of scapegoating and ritual sacrifice in the quest's Atmoran setting.

## Beynhaal's loss of faith

Beynhaal admits the sacrifices do not work: the dragon never responds and the winter never ends. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00510F`.

He says the sacrifices continue partly because the people must be shown that the cult is doing something, otherwise they will lose faith. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00510F`.

He also says he personally sacrificed his mortality, parents, children, and loved ones and has been left in a dried-up immortal form. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005111`.

His final attitude is that because so much has already been lost, everyone else in the land may as well pay the same price. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005111`.

## Ending

If the player attacks him, Beynhaal welcomes death as the fulfillment of a wish and says there is nothing warmer than death in Atmora. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005117`, `00512A`.

If not, he says he will seek a place to die elsewhere. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005115`.

The quest therefore links DAc0da's Atmoran historical contradictions with persecution, failed religious sacrifice, endless winter, immortality, and the golden-dragon thread.
