## Direct quest progression

The objectives establish a long multi-party side arc:

1. optionally meet and release Vanus;
2. head west;
3. choose whether to help Yu'qbar;
4. continue northwest;
5. fight a Yaghra/Sload chimera;
6. choose whether to save a wounded survivor;
7. heal Athanasius;
8. regroup with Vanus;
9. infiltrate the Sload research facility;
10. continue through the Sload capital;
11. fight a necromancer army;
12. reach the ritual site;
13. defeat Mannimarco's legion;
14. defeat the God of Worms;
15. talk with the surviving companions;
16. close the remaining temporal discrepancy.

This side quest is structurally integrated with DAc0da's time-breach system rather than being an unrelated dungeon chain.

## Vanus as a reconstructed memory

Vanus first appears in a turtle form and directly identifies himself as **Vanus Galerion**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052D4`.

He says he lost a duel to the death with Mannimarco, left the world, and later awakened as a turtle. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052EB`.

At the end of the quest he says this turtle body contains copied/transferred memories and personality, and eventually describes himself as a memory that will return to the "sea of memories." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006056`, `006057`, `00605D`.

When asked whose memory he is, he admits he does not know and speculates that he could even be **Mannimarco's memory** because of their old attachment. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006062`.

This means DAc0da does **not** require the turtle Vanus to be treated as the ordinary historical body of Vanus Galerion. He is an instantiated memory/personality copy produced inside the mnemolichite distortion.

## First mnemolichite model

Vanus initially calls mnemolichite a hypothetical mineral mined by the Dreugh that has become real under the **Numidian Effect**. He says memories are rapidly crystallizing in the surrounding waters. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052F2`.

He says the material can react to thought and memory strongly enough to instantiate a remembered mage who traps him in a cage. His summary is that **perception defines existence**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052F0`.

Yu'qbar gives a more literal Dreugh account: mnemolichite is memory dissolved in water and crystallized, useful for recovering lost knowledge, but unstable forms can turn memory into existence according to mortal perception. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00531E`.

These are not fully compatible explanations and should remain separate testimony layers.

## Republic of Hahd and the Mnemolichite Maid

Yu'qbar identifies himself as a Templar of the **Republic of Hahd**, an underwater state near the Dellese Isles of High Rock. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005316`.

He says the Sload stole the **Mnemolichite Maid** while it was being transported to the Emirates of Nahd. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00531C`.

He describes the Maid as a very pure giant mnemolichite crystal, a relic of the Dawn-Era Ehlnofey War, recovered by Dervish the Hero of Hahd from a drowned Ehlnofey city in the southern Eltheric Ocean. Mankar-hul says it remembers old Aldmeris. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005320`.

These are Yu'qbar/Hahd traditions. The quest later destabilizes their literal status.

## Sload research

The Sload and allied necromancers kidnap and brainwash people lost in the sea. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052F5`.

Their research facility splices captives with Yaghra through necromancy, producing chimeras. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00540A`, `005480`.

The linked `zDcdSqWormSub02` quest culminates in a boss alias pointing to `DAc0da.esm:003104`, **Worm's Midwife**, during the Sload-capital battle.

## Mannimarco / God of Worms plan

Vanus eventually learns that the Sload are exploiting:

- the time breach produced by the Numidian Effect;
- mnemolichite's cognitive/perceptual effects;
- Mannimarco's corpse;
- the divine **God of Worms / Necromancer's Moon** state.

He says the goal is to **retroactively rewrite the laws of Nirn** by using Mannimarco's corpse as a vessel through which the God of Worms can manifest on Nirn. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005536`.

Vanus describes the God of Worms as the divine form Mannimarco created during the Warp in the West and says the Jills separated that divine state from Mannimarco's mortal form. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00553D`.

The `zDcdSqWormSub03` alias table structurally confirms both:

- `Worm` -> **Mannimarco the God of Worms**
- `WormM` -> **Necromancer's Moon**

The subquest ends with the God of Worms manifesting after the portal fails to close. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006008`, `00600A`.

## Mnemolichite becomes ontologically unstable

Vanus later corrects his own earlier description.

He says "mnemolichite" as **crystallized memory** is a mistranslation of the Dreugh concept; properly translated, it describes the process by which perceptual information becomes fixed memory and is not originally a tangible substance. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005546`.

He then speculates that surface-world misunderstanding itself may have caused mnemolichite to materialize and **retroactively altered Dreugh history and society** so that the mineral had always existed. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005546`.

Yu'qbar independently says the sea is where memories eventually dissolve and that imagination has power there; he describes mnemolichite as a template where everything is a reproduction and there may be no original. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00547D`.

Mankar-hul's teaching, as reported by Yu'qbar, says mnemolichite is a materialized idea that reconstructs existence from memory according to perception, but produces warped results because memories are uncertain. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005554`.

## Mnemo-Li theories

Vanus first reports an unconfirmed theory that memory crystallization occurs because **Mnemo-Li** protects memories. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052F2`.

Later, after continued exposure, he theorizes that Mnemo-Li was an artificially created Magna-Ge whose divinity was manufactured after creation. He connects Dreugh dislike of Mnemo-Li, blood imagery, and mnemolichite, and proposes that mnemolichite may be like a scab covering a wound across time and space caused by contradictory causality. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005713`.

These statements are explicitly Vanus's evolving research/speculation, not settled narration.

## End of the Maid

After the God of Worms falls, Yu'qbar says the Mnemolichite Maid has returned to the sea and assumes Dervish came to retrieve her. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006021`.

Athanasius instead says there never was such a thing: people thought there was, and reality acted accordingly. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006034`.

Vanus says the Maid is now outside their perception and has therefore ceased to exist. He thinks it was delaying the distortion from the time breach until the party was able to close it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00603B`.

This contradiction is intentional and should be retained as the central epistemic problem of the quest.

## Resolution

Vanus says the remaining task is to repair the time breach by reducing temporal discrepancies. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00603D`.

Once the discrepancy is removed, mnemolichite itself begins to dissolve and even the copied Vanus form prepares to disappear back into the sea of memories. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006053`, `00605D`.

The arc therefore functions as a local demonstration of DAc0da's larger rule that contradictory perception, memory, and causality can temporarily become real under Numidium-induced temporal instability.
