## Actor

The quest's `Sheogorath` alias points to `DAc0da.esm:00CDD7` / `zDcdUqSheogorathCheese`.

This record does not carry a FULL name, but the quest/alias/editor ID structurally establishes the intended Sheogorath role.

## Premise

Sheogorath refuses to focus on the Numidium crisis and instead insists that the urgent problem is assembling guests for a **cheese party**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CDE4`.

He initially says the missing guest is the **Totem of Tiber Septim**, which he had promised cheese after work. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CDE7`.

## Totems

The player can bring a sequence of named totems:

- Totem of Sarcasm
- Totem of Obsession
- Totem of Innocence
- Totem of Divinity
- Totem of Lamentation
- Totem of the Prisoner
- Totem of Entanglement

The quest gives each one a Sheogorath reaction, but it does not directly identify every totem with a specific historical person.

### Divinity

Sheogorath says the Totem of Divinity has **Reman's face**, not Tiber Septim's, and mentions **Shoni-Etta** / "Reman's bread." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CDF8`.

### Lamentation

He reacts to the Totem of Lamentation as a crying figure, invokes **Mara**, and asks whether Mannimarco pinched it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CDFB`.

### Prisoner

He says the Totem of the Prisoner has **no face** and accuses it of hiding in a shell. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CDFE`.

That imagery is useful for retrieval beside DAc0da's explicit Prisoner material, but the joke should not be promoted into a precise metaphysical doctrine by itself.

### Entanglement

He tells the Totem of Entanglement that it does not know who it is and says it has never been a person before. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE01`.

This is a potentially meaningful identity/entanglement node but remains Sheogorath's dialogue.

## Ending

Once all the guests are present, Sheogorath immediately declares that because the party has begun it is already over and leaves. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE09`, `00CE0B`, `00CE0D`.

The file should be treated as a retrieval index for DAc0da's abstract totem vocabulary rather than a literal cosmological explanation.
