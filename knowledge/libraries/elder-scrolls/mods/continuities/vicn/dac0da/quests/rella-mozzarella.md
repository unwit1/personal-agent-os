# Rella Mozzarella

**Quest:** `zDcdMqCheeseEnd`  
**Title:** Rella Mozzarella  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:00C9F1`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Structural role

This is an alternate ending/censorship route.

Objectives:

1. talk to Sheogorath;
2. talk to Rolls-On-Roads;
3. talk to Sheogorath again;
4. **escape the Jills' censorship**.

Aliases include alternate forms of Sheogorath and Rolls-On-Roads.

## Half-plane

Sheogorath describes the location as a half-plane that will disappear in the next moment and jokes that he already no longer remembers it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00C9FE`.

This is consistent with a space being consumed/removed by the Jillian correction process.

## Blind God

Sheogorath refers to an entity as the **Blind God**, says the Jills are about to attack/devour it, and describes it as a godlike jealous figure who "ran from" a party. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00C9F5`, `00CA01`.

The processed records do not explicitly identify this Blind God. No external identity should be assigned without additional evidence.

## Jills devouring the past

Sheogorath says a Jill has bitten Rolls-On-Roads and would have swallowed him completely if Sheogorath had not intervened. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA04`.

Most importantly, he describes the swallowed result as being **"cleaned of his past"** and jokes that the Jill would then possess/reminisce over Rolls-On-Roads' childhood and adolescence. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA04`.

This gives DAc0da a vivid model of Jillian censorship as consumption/appropriation of personal history rather than merely rewriting public records.

## Rolls-On-Roads' interpretation

Rolls-On-Roads says the process is the **correction performed by the Jills** and warns that they will soon devour:

- him;
- the player;
- their entire journey.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA17`.

He tells the player to flee and says the Jills are no longer "on our side." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA19`.

This should be read beside the ordinary MQ05 route, where Rolls-On-Roads previously said Jills are not simple allies or enemies but actors fulfilling their temporal function.

## Escape

Sheogorath offers the player an escape route out of the collapsing/censored reality. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA0D`.

He refuses to let Rolls-On-Roads accompany the player because the Jills are still "biting down" on him. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA13`.

This is a branch-specific fate and should not replace the ordinary **Censored Fate** ending.
