## Final manifestation

Dialogue says the ritual has already begun and the party must hurry. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005FE9`.

After Mannimarco's forces are defeated, an attempt is made to close the portal. It fails, followed immediately by the declaration that **the God of Worms has already manifested**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005FFF`, `006008`, `00600A`.

The parent quest later states that the God of Worms has fallen and the Sload/necromancer plot has failed. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006022`.

## Identity handling

DAc0da explicitly distinguishes:

- a Mannimarco-associated mortal/body component;
- **Mannimarco the God of Worms** as a combat actor;
- **Necromancer's Moon** as a separate alias/form.

Vanus claims that the Jills separated Mannimarco's divine form from his mortal form during the Warp in the West. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00553D`.

That explanation remains Vanus testimony, but the plugin's separate forms show that DAc0da itself operationally distinguishes these states.
