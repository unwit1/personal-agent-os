## Identity

The MQ05 `Monk` alias points directly to NPC `DAc0da.esm:000D77`, whose FULL name is **Rolls-On-Roads**.

He is an Argonian member/operative of the Psijic Order and acts as the main Psijic guide during DAc0da's Numidium crisis.

A second NPC form, `DAc0da.esm:004930` / `zDcdUqPsijicArgonianMemospore`, also bears the name Rolls-On-Roads and is used for memospore/holographic communication. A later fragment form `DAc0da.esm:00B75A` is likewise named Rolls-On-Roads.

## Role in the Numidium crisis

Across the numbered main quests, Rolls-On-Roads:

- recruits the player into the Psijic effort against Numidium;
- explains repeated fate-line attempts and alternate versions of the player;
- opens and maintains portals;
- describes the Jills as temporal repair actors;
- guides the player through time breaches and into Numidium;
- applies the mirror-logic ward against zero-sum defenses;
- interprets Numidium's internal records;
- relays Ritemaster/Psijic theories about the Prisoner;
- witnesses Hist–Jill conflict and the Akulakhan/Nerevarine anomaly;
- says the Jills will "censor" the event after the breach is closed.

Evidence is distributed through `zDcdMq00`–`zDcdMq05`; see the individual quest reconstruction files for record-level citations.

## Post-censorship state

In **Censored Fate**, Rolls-On-Roads no longer recognizes the player as his Numidium partner. He says he was sent to Solitude Lighthouse merely to investigate Numidium fragments that had briefly reacted to the location. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA3B`, `00CA3F`.

He still has an unexplained sense that he has recently accomplished something important. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA50`.

## Dragon Break / memory testimony

Rolls-On-Roads says Dragon Break correction can produce:

- ordinary memory loss;
- memories retained without comprehension;
- later forgetting;
- severe physical injury;
- death;
- unusually high numbers of missing people.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA54`, `00CA55`, `00CA57`.

These remain character testimony within DAc0da.

## Romion

Rolls-On-Roads names **Romion** as an old friend and former colleague. He describes Romion as an Altmer Dwemer scholar and says the two were apprentices together who consumed stories of the Vanos siblings' Dwemer adventures. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA49`, `00CA4A`, `00CA4C`, `00CA4E`.

GLENMORIL independently contains the named Dwemer scholar Romion. This is therefore a high-confidence retrieval bridge across the Vicn continuity, while source-specific biography remains separate.
