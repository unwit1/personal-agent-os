# Yngol

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary quest:** `zDcdSqYngol` — Drowned Nighthawk  
**Status:** evidence-backed character dossier

## Direct identity

DAc0da's first Yngol actor is `DAc0da.esm:00316B` / `zDcdUqYngol01`, named **Yngol**.

He directly identifies himself as **Yngol, son of Ysgramor**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004B9B`.

Later quest states include `DAc0da.esm:005013`, named **Yngol the Tsunaltir**, and a separate dragon actor `DAc0da.esm:005036`, named **Tsuunalinfaxtir**.

## Ahzidal

Yngol says a famous Saarthal enchanter gave him a protective runic bracelet. He says that enchanter now uses the name **Ahzidal** and crossed from Mereth to assist the returning Atmorans. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004BA2`, `004BA4`.

This is DAc0da's Yngol testimony and should remain separate from official-source Ahzidal chronology until reconciled.

## Atmora / Jylkurfyk

Yngol identifies Jylkurfyk as Atmora's largest port and places an eighty-ship expedition toward Mereth in his immediate historical frame. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C12`, `004C14`.

He initially supports Ysgramor's war against the Snow Elves and explicitly cites Saarthal and the Night of Tears. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C16`.

The quest later confronts him with opposing Atmoran testimony accusing Ysgramor of deception, war-making, and purges. This is a deliberate contested-history node rather than a single unchallenged account.

## Golden dragon / Tsuunalinfaxtir

Yngol becomes increasingly convinced that a golden dragon is connected to his own identity.

He says older Atmoran teachings claimed people once had dragon forms in an earlier kalpa and shed their scales. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004CCE`.

He later says Alduin's scales slumber within them and chooses to reclaim his dragon name/scales. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DF5`.

Near the climax he identifies the golden dragon as the other half of his being and names it **Tsuunalinfaxtir**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FED`, `004FEF`.

The separate dragon actor bearing that same name supports the identity link structurally.

## Memory instability

Yngol experiences memories and knowledge that do not fit a simple linear past. He describes a long blacksmithing dream and later remembers facts he says he somehow always knew but was not supposed to know. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004BA7`, `004FAA`, `004FAC`.

This places Yngol squarely inside DAc0da's fate-line / memory-correction framework.

## End states

After reclaiming the dragon power, Yngol says it is burning him from within. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005001`, `005004`.

The player can support a final duel/death outcome or persuade him toward another ending in which he takes flight toward Harakk and expects to become stardust. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00500E`, `00503C`, `005040`.

These are branch-dependent outcomes and should not be flattened into one fixed fate.
