## Family and origin

Yngol's recovered-memory dialogue directly says Haalj is **Hgelhelm's son** and was **born to a Snow Drake**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FAA`.

The same recollection says Haalj and his father were among the **500 Companions**.

Yngol further remembers:

- their ship as **Drumbeater**;
- its captain as **Morgan the Red**;
- every crew member dying except **Olaf the Dog**.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FAA`.

Because Yngol describes names and memories as suddenly returning and vanishing, these details should be recorded as explicit Yngol memory rather than omniscient narration.

## Opposition to Ysgramor's war

Scene resolution identifies Haalj as the speaker arguing with an alternate Yngol-state.

Haalj says **Ysgramor deceived them**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DC4`.

His account says people wanted peace and passage to Mereth to escape an Atmoran civil war, but the conflict was instead carried into Mereth. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DC8`.

He condemns the purge of:

- elves;
- half-breeds;
- children.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DCC`, `004DD0`.

He says Mereth had been peaceful and accuses Ysgramor's faction of bringing war there. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DD8`, `004DDD`.

## Reliability boundary

Haalj's testimony is deliberately placed against Yngol's competing version, in which the Snow Elves began the war and Ysgramor acted for his people.

DAc0da also separately includes Snow-Elf orders for an attack on Jylkurfyk and a Dagon contract, so Haalj's testimony should **not** be flattened into the sole objective history.

His importance is that DAc0da supplies an internal Atmoran/mixed-blood dissident account from someone Yngol later remembers as belonging to the 500 Companions.

The precise trigger and branch condition for the mutation remain script/quest-state details and should not be assumed from the actor form alone.
