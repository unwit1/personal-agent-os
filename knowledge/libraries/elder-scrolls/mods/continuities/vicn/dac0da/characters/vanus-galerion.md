# Vanus Galerion

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:005183` / `zDcdUqVanus`  
**Memory/ghost form:** `DAc0da.esm:006067` / `zDcdUqVanusGhost`  
**Primary quest:** `zDcdSqWorm` — Echoes of Mnemolichite

## Identity state

The turtle actor directly identifies himself as **Vanus Galerion**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052D4`.

He acknowledges that he used to be an Altmer and dislikes being a turtle. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052D8`.

However, the ending makes clear that this form is a **memory/personality reconstruction**, not necessarily Vanus's original historical body.

## Mannimarco

Vanus says Mannimarco was a fellow Psijic student: talented, but drawn into necromancy. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052ED`.

He says he lost a duel to the death with Mannimarco and only later awakened in the turtle form. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052EB`.

When the quest ends he says he does not know whose memory he actually is and even speculates he could be Mannimarco's memory because of their former attachment. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006062`.

## Rolls-On-Roads

Vanus says Rolls-On-Roads released him from his cage, recognized him immediately, and recruited him to assist the player. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00534F`.

Vanus usually dislikes requests from Psijics but makes an exception because Rolls-On-Roads appears honest and their objectives align. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00534F`.

## Mnemolichite research

Vanus drives most of the arc's evolving mnemolichite theories.

His major stages are:

1. hypothetical Dreugh ore made real by Numidium (`0052F2`);
2. perception can instantiate remembered entities (`0052F0`);
3. Dreugh ontology treats perception as the beginning/catalyst of existence (`005473`);
4. "crystallized memory" is a mistranslation and the original term describes a perceptual-memory process (`005546`);
5. misunderstanding may have retroactively created a physical mineral and rewritten Dreugh history (`005546`);
6. mnemolichite may be a time-space "scab" associated with contradictory causality and Mnemo-Li (`005713`).

These are progressive research hypotheses and should not be flattened into one authoritative definition.

## Sload / God of Worms investigation

Vanus infiltrates Sload logistics and learns that highly pure mnemolichite is being transferred to an island ritual site. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00544D`.

He later discovers a record saying the body of the King of Worms was transported with it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005476`.

His final interpretation is that the Sload intend to use the time breach, mnemolichite's cognitive effects, Mannimarco's corpse, and the divine God-of-Worms state to rewrite Nirn's laws retroactively. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005536`.

## Ending

Vanus says both memories and personality were transferred/copied to the turtle body, which is why he persists longer than some other mnemolichite constructs. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006056`, `006057`.

He ultimately accepts that the turtle form is a memory that will disappear back into the sea of memories and describes himself as one point in a cycle of sleeping and waking. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00605D`.
