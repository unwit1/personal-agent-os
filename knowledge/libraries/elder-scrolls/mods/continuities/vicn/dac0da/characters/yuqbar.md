# Templar of Hahd Yu'qbar

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:000D74` / `zDcdUqDreughElder`  
**Displayed name:** **Templar of Hahd Yu'qbar**  
**Primary quest:** `zDcdSqWorm` — Echoes of Mnemolichite

## Identity

Yu'qbar introduces himself as a member of the **Templars of Hahd**, led by **Mankar-hul**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005316`.

He says the Republic of Hahd is an underwater state near the Dellese Isles of High Rock. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005316`.

## Religion / terminology

Yu'qbar calls **Maztiak** the jailer king and identifies Maztiak with **Arkayn**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005319`.

He says Mankar-hul is Maztiak/Arkayn's most beloved and will oppose the corruption of the **Vermae**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005319`.

He repeatedly invokes Arkayn and treats necromancy as blasphemy against Arkayn. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005551`.

These are Hahd/Dreugh religious terms inside DAc0da and should not be silently normalized to external identities beyond the source's own equivalences.

## Mission

Yu'qbar says the Sload stole the **Mnemolichite Maid** during transport to the Emirates of Nahd. His unit was destroyed, leaving him the only survivor, but he continues the mission alone. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00531C`.

The linked `zDcdSqWormSub01` quest gives the player an explicit choice to aid him against Yaghra; if abandoned, he can die apologizing to Mankar-hul for failing his mission. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00530C`, `00530E`.

## Hahd model of mnemolichite

Yu'qbar describes mnemolichite as memory dissolved in water and crystallized. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00531E`.

He later says memories ultimately dissolve in the sea, imagination is power there, and his own existence is the result of someone else's tale. He describes mnemolichite as a template with reproductions but no discoverable original. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00547D`.

He attributes to Mankar-hul the teaching that mnemolichite is a materialized idea able to reconstruct existence from memory, imperfectly because memories are uncertain. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005554`.

## Mnemolichite Maid tradition

Yu'qbar says the Maid is a pure giant crystal recovered by **Dervish, Hero of Hahd**, from a drowned Ehlnofey city in the southern Eltheric Ocean, and that Mankar-hul says it remembers old Aldmeris. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005320`.

At the quest ending, Yu'qbar says the Maid has returned to the sea and supposes Dervish retrieved it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006021`.

This ending conflicts with Vanus and Athanasius, who give different ontological explanations.

## Ghost form

The Revenant subquest also defines `DAc0da.esm:006087` / `zDcdBossGhostDreughElder`, displayed again as **Templar of Hahd Yu'qbar**.

The existence of both ordinary and ghost aliases is structurally important for branch/death-state handling and should not be collapsed without quest-condition context.
