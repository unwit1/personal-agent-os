# Beynhaal

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:0050B1` / `zDcdUqBeynhaal`  
**Primary quest:** `zDcdSqHalfElf` — The End of All Wishes

## Role

Beynhaal is a priest/cult functionary involved in sacrifices intended to end an endless winter.

He says the hidden child was offered as a shrine maiden to the mountain where their god sleeps. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005109`.

## Snow-Elf scapegoating

Beynhaal says people hope the Snow Elves are responsible for the winter and therefore see it as fitting to sacrifice someone carrying their blood. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00510C`.

He explicitly says he no longer cares whether that explanation is true; he would sacrifice anything to make the winter end. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00510C`.

## Institutional despair

Beynhaal admits the offerings have produced no response and do not end the winter. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00510F`.

He nevertheless says the cult must continue visibly doing something or people will lose faith. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00510F`.

This makes him direct evidence for institutionalized sacrifice persisting after its practitioners have lost confidence in its efficacy.

## Immortality and accumulated sacrifice

Beynhaal says he gave up:

- mortality;
- parents;
- children;
- loved ones.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005111`.

He says those sacrifices accomplished nothing and left him in a dried-up immortal form. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005111`.

His despair leads him to conclude that everyone in the land should eventually pay the same price.

## Death wish

If challenged to combat, Beynhaal reacts with relief and welcomes being made into a sacrifice himself. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005117`.

His death line says there is nothing warmer than death in Atmora. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00512A`.

This is character testimony and emotional framing, not a general cosmological statement about Atmora.
