# Akashiya-Samon

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:003152` / `zDcdUqTsaesciSamurai`  
**Ghost NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:00CE92` / `zDcdUqTsaesciSamuraiGhost`  
**Status:** evidence-backed character dossier

## Identity

Akashiya-Samon directly introduces himself by name and identifies himself as a **Dragonguard serving Reman**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004489`.

He refers to Reman as the true Dragonborn and describes the Dragonguard as Reman's protectors and dragon hunters. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00448C`.

## Arrival in the Sea of Causality

Samon says that during an expedition to Skyrim his party was caught in a storm. He saw a golden dragon pass above/through it, jumped into the sea to pursue the dragon, and later washed up in the ship graveyard. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004493`.

Because this encounter occurs within DAc0da's Numidium-linked temporal instability, Samon is a chronology/time-displacement node.

## Drowned Nighthawk

Samon reappears throughout `zDcdSqYngol`, where his golden-dragon hunt intersects with temporally displaced Yngol.

He says the golden dragon arrived from an **Adjacent Place** and that aspects of an old kalpa are entering through the local time distortion. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DF9`.

He identifies the dragon as the center of the distortion. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DFD`.

When the player explains that the dragon is Yngol's other half, Samon compares the transformation to practices of **Ka Po' Tun** and insists the dragon must be destroyed. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FB4`.

## Dragon-hunting cosmology

Samon says dragons are fragments or maddened forms of the Time God and also calls them spies of **Tosh Raka**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005053`.

He says simply killing a dragon does not truly destroy it; only a dragon bloodline such as Reman's can liberate its soul and return it to the god of time. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005053`.

He describes Tosh Raka as one manifestation of the Time God that hates Akatosh and frames the Dragonguard's hunts as preparation for a future conflict. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005055`.

These are Samon's source-scoped beliefs within DAc0da.

## Relationship to the Dragonborn

Samon recognizes the player as a Dragonborn like Reman after seeing the player absorb a dragon soul. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005066`.

In another branch he says the player's role/power is called **Dovahkiin** in the dragon language but still insists Reman is his only master and the "true Dragonborn." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005069`, `00506B`.

## Death / ghost epilogue

The epilogue uses ghost NPC `DAc0da.esm:00CE92`.

Samon says his soul returned to Reman after his earlier duel and that Reman ordered him to wait for the player in the far future. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE9D`, `00CEA1`.

He also says he served Reman's descendants for generations until the betrayal of **Versidue-Shaie**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEA2`.

This provides a much longer DAc0da chronology for Samon than the initial MQ01 encounter alone.

## Paarthurnax / warrior ethics

In his follow-up epilogue Samon treats dragon hunting as a means rather than an end and tells the Dragonborn to take responsibility for choosing whether to fight. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEB5`, `00CEBB`.

He considers Paarthurnax an old, tired monk for whom there is little honor in fighting, and uses his own history as a former enemy accepted by Reman to argue that past crimes do not automatically settle the choice. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEB3`, `00CEB7`.

His responses differ depending on whether Paarthurnax lived or died; these are branch-specific character judgments, not neutral lore facts.
