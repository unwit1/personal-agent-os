## Identity and self-description

Crabbimarco calls himself:

- the great Crabbimarco;
- the god of all crabs;
- the most powerful necromancer in **Lyg**.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00491F`, `004921`, `004925`.

This is intentionally comic/distorted dialogue, but the actor is a concrete unique NPC placed in the Sload laboratory.

## Anus Falerion

When asked about his past, Crabbimarco recounts a magical duel against a sea-cucumber mage named **Anus Falerion**, whom he calls both old friend and nemesis. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004928`.

He says:

- the battle filled the sea with magical destruction;
- Crabbimarco had already become a lich;
- Anus Falerion accepted mortality and was defeated;
- Crabbimarco therefore became the strongest mage "in both name and truth."

The names and duel clearly parody/distort Mannimarco and Vanus Galerion, but DAc0da does **not** directly say Crabbimarco literally is Mannimarco or that Anus Falerion literally is Vanus. Preserve the Lyg-parody/distorted-analogue framing.

## King of Dreugh / stolen name

Crabbimarco says he once served the **King of Dreugh**, but no longer remembers that king's name because **the Grabbers took it from him**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00492C`.

This is one of DAc0da's clearest direct statements that Grabbers can steal a **name** from an individual.

It strongly complements **In Grabbers' Hands**, where a nymic-stealer boss removes the player's name and the quest requires restoration.

## Xero-Lyg

Crabbimarco says his plans collapsed when slaves revolted and blames:

- **Xero-Lyg**
- her son

for ruining his attempt to seize power. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00492C`.

He then says he exploited the moment when "the world was liberated and ascended," became unstable, and expects eventually to be **attached to this kalpa**.

This is direct DAc0da Lyg/Xero-Lyg material and should be retrieved with GLENMORIL's independent Xero-Lyg references, while keeping each work's source claims separate.

## Metaphysical significance

Crabbimarco's dialogue clusters:

- Lyg;
- Grabbers;
- stolen names;
- Xero-Lyg;
- kalpic attachment;
- altered/distorted versions of familiar mage identities.

The comic register should not cause these repeated rare-vocabulary links to be discarded, but neither should every joke be treated as literal cosmological narration.
