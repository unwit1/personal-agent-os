## Creator claim

When asked who created him, Mecha-Kanra answers:

> Uncle Yagrum.

He then says Yagrum is **Kagrenac's apprentice**, describing him as chubby, many-legged, spider-like but more like an octopus. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00D04E`.

This is Mecha-Kanra's statement. The name strongly evokes Yagrum Bagarn, but the library should not silently identify them beyond the speaker's exact **Yagrum** wording unless another DAc0da record makes the equivalence explicit.

## Sotha Sil mission

Mecha-Kanra says **Sotha Sil asked him to come here** and that he is:

> inspecting the Prisoner's memory modules

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00D051`.

This is a major direct link among:

- Sotha Sil;

- the Anu[s]midium / Numidium interior.

It gives DAc0da a mechanical-memory model that is distinct from, but compatible with, the broader mnemolichite and Prisoner material.

## Brain-Billies

Mecha-Kanra recommends eating **Brain-Billies** to avoid forgetting.

When asked what they are, he says:

- they are spirits around the area;
- they **sting your memories**;
- the sting causes forgotten things to be remembered.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00D051`, `00D053`.

This exact rare term also appears independently in **GLENMORIL** dialogue associated with the Augur of the Obscure, making it a strong direct Vicn cross-retrieval node.

Do not assume the full mechanism is identical between works until the GLENMORIL usage is compared line-by-line.
