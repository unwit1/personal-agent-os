# Sindwen the Wintercaller

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:003191` / `zDcdBossAtmoraWintercaller`  
**Displayed name:** **Sindwen the Wintercaller**  
**Quest:** `zDcdSqYngol` — Drowned Nighthawk

## Role

Scene-speaker resolution identifies Sindwen as the Snow-Elf commander/antagonist encountered in the Jylkurfyk sequence.

She asks where **Ysgramor** and **Ylgar** fled and disputes Yngol's claim that a fleet of eighty ships has already gone to Mereth. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C90`, `004C96`.

She says the Snow Elves' **Eye of the Sea** can see a thousand miles and argues that such a fleet should have been visible. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C96`.

## Dagon and Auri-El

Sindwen threatens to send Yngol's soul to **Lord Dagon**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C9A`.

In branch dialogue she condemns survivors who consorted with “the lowly,” including elves, and says humans/Children of Lorkhan should be destroyed in the name of **Auri-El**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004CA1`, `004CA3`.

These statements establish a militant ideological position but should not be generalized to every Snow Elf.

## Written-order context

DAc0da BOOK `DAc0da.esm:0049F6`, **Orders to Wintercaller Unit**, separately orders a Wintercaller force to attack **Jylkurfyk**, kill Ysgramor and his sons if found, and kill Saarthal citizens.

The order claims the operation is required by a **contract with Lord Dagon**.

The shared **Wintercaller** designation and Sindwen's direct invocation of Dagon make her a strong participant in the same military/religious context.

However, the book does not explicitly name Sindwen as its author or recipient, so the library should say she is associated with the Wintercaller/Dagon operation rather than silently assigning the document personally to her.

## Contested-history role

Sindwen supplies the Snow-Elf militant side of DAc0da's deliberately contradictory Atmoran history.

That record should be read alongside:

- Haalj's accusation that Ysgramor exported an Atmoran civil war to Mereth;
- the separate anti-elven purge letter from Jylkurfyk;
- Yngol's belief that the Snow Elves initiated the conflict;
- the Wintercaller attack order itself.

DAc0da preserves all of these rather than resolving them into one simple account.
