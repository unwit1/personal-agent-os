# Vigilant Athanasius

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:005251` / `zDcdUqAthanasius`  
**Ghost NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:006089` / `zDcdBossGhostAthanasius`  
**Primary quests:** `zDcdSqWorm`, `zDcdSqWormSub04`

## Identity

Athanasius introduces himself as **Keeper of Stendarr of the Holy Pipers branch**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00554C`.

He says the Holy Pipers travel, teach Stendarr's doctrine, and aid people in need. His group accompanied the Solitude navy into the strange sea to rescue survivors. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00555E`.

## Earlier life

Athanasius says he is a former Imperial Legion soldier who fought in the Great War and was present when the Imperial City was retaken. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005560`.

He says the devastation of the Imperial City changed him and that Stendarr's ideal of protection without discrimination led him into his current vocation. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005563`.

## Mnemolichite ending

At the end of Echoes of Mnemolichite, Athanasius gives one of the most radical interpretations of the Maid: **there never was such a thing; people thought it existed, so it behaved accordingly.** Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006034`.

He then begins questioning his own identity and reality. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006036`.

## The Sea of Radiance

The follow-up quest `zDcdSqWormSub04` — **The Sea of Radiance** — resolves Athanasius as its primary alias.

He says mnemolichite has shown him a **spark of Radiance at the end of the sea of possibilities** and claims to have found a "tower of blood" associated with Mnemo-Li. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00614A`.

He says he has attained Radiance, will traverse all paths, and is becoming "we." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00614C`, `006153`.

The quest also defines `DAc0da.esm:006135`, **Malmanius the Child of Radiance**, whom Athanasius calls his son and "another form of myself." He says this state has saved the child from the slaughter in the Imperial City. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00614F`.

This should be treated as a Many-Paths/perceptual transformation event, not ordinary biological parenthood or resurrection unless later evidence establishes otherwise.
