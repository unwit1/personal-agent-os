# Zurin Arctus

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

**Status:** evidence-backed DAc0da character/state dossier

## Concrete boss identity

The `Boss` alias of **VS Zurin Arctus** points directly to NPC `DAc0da.esm:00AF11`, whose FULL name is **Zurin Arctus** and short name is **Zurin**.

This is stronger structural identification than relying only on the following MQ04 conversation.

## Approach to the lift

The boss quest's opening dialogue belongs to Rolls-On-Roads.

He says the party has reached the main lift toward Numidium's center and begins activating it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B5F3`.

He then senses something approaching at very high speed and erects a barrier. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B5F5`, `00B5F7`.

After sustaining the attack, he says the hostile magic feels as though it is **decaying** and concludes that the attacker must be a **lich**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B60D`.

He then realizes the initial attacks were only measuring the barrier's strength. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B60F`, `00B611`.

## Zurin's own lines

During the confrontation, Zurin says:

- he is not merely the hatred driving him;
- he holds a responsibility toward **Hjalti**;
- he wants to finish what "we" started;
- he questions who Hjalti truly was.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B61F`, `00B621`, `00B625`.

These lines tie the boss directly to DAc0da's Hjalti/Tiber/Mantella conflict without requiring an inferred speaker.

## Equipment/state

The boss form carries:

- **Wilt-Flower Greatsaber (Dual)** (`DAc0da.esm:009BEC`).

DAc0da also provides summon/skull records explicitly named for Zurin Arctus, showing that this boss-state can be represented as a reusable/summonable combat form after the encounter.

## Mantella link

After the encounter, MQ04 explicitly identifies the defeated figure as Zurin Arctus and then discusses the Mantella as a replacement for the Heart of Lorkhan containing Zurin's soul.

See `analysis/mq04-numidium-tertius.md`.

## Identity boundary

DAc0da uses several overlapping labels and states around:

- Zurin Arctus;
- the Underking;
- Hjalti;
- Mantella;
- earlier time-breach manifestations.

This dossier records what the boss quest directly establishes. It should not collapse every Underking manifestation into this exact NPC form unless the relevant quest/alias evidence supports it.
