## Memospore appearance

The Crab Spirit appears inside the memospore scene during **Negative Legacy**.

The Augur says he received a memospore from the "lizard friend" and begins playing it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004945`, `004948`.

The Crab Spirit then addresses the intended recipient as:

> Vestige

and starts explaining how to fight **Molag Bal**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00494A`.

Before the message can continue, the Augur cuts it off and says the recording was the wrong one and was meant for **an old friend**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00494C`.

## Interpretation

This scene should **not** be used to identify DAc0da's Skyrim player as the Vestige.

The scene explicitly establishes that the recording/message is being played for the wrong recipient.

The safe evidence is:

- the Augur possesses memospores addressed to people in other histories/times;
- one such memory/message addresses a **Vestige** fighting Molag Bal;
- the intended recipient is described by the Augur as **an old friend**;
- the Crab Spirit is the speaker/avatar used in that recording.

## Placement

The Crab Spirit has a placed reference `DAc0da.esm:004938` in the **Zurin Island** region, but its narrative speech is delivered through the MQ02 memospore scene rather than ordinary local conversation.

## Cross-era significance

The scene is a deliberately cross-era Elder Scrolls reference inside DAc0da's memory/time framework.

It is useful evidence that memospores can preserve or replay information intended for a different person/time-line, while the exact identity of the Augur's "old friend" should remain unresolved unless another record names them.
