## Scene role

Scene-speaker resolution identifies Hgelhelm as the outcast who finds the dreaming Yngol.

He says Yngol can simply be left to freeze rather than murdered directly. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004B41`.

Samon objects that this is no proper way for a warrior to die and later calls Hgelhelm a hypocrite who reeks of spilled blood.

Hgelhelm warns the player to stay away from the port. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004B45`.

## Conflict with Yngol

When Yngol wakes and continues forward, Hgelhelm says he had hoped Yngol could remain asleep in peace and ultimately considers offering Yngol's head to the Snow Elves. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004BEF`, `004BF8`.

He expresses deep frustration with both Yngol and the player and says that the elves do not know Atmoran culture, so he will at least send the player to **Shor**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004BFD`, `004BFF`.

## Relationship to Haalj

Later Yngol dialogue explicitly identifies **Haalj Hgelhelmson** as **Hgelhelm's son**.

Yngol says:

- Haalj was born to a **Snow Drake**;
- Haalj and Hgelhelm were among the **500 Companions**;
- they sailed on a ship called **Drumbeater**;
- its captain was **Morgan the Red**;
- none of its crew survived except **Olaf the Dog**.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004FAA`.

This information comes to Yngol as recovered/unstable memory and should retain that framing.

## Historical significance

Hgelhelm sits directly inside DAc0da's contested Atmoran-history layer:

- associated with the 500 Companions;
- father of a mixed Snow-Drake child;
- alienated from Yngol/Ysgramor's faction;
- willing to side against Yngol during the Jylkurfyk crisis.

His precise reason for becoming an “Outcast” is not separately narrated in the processed records.
