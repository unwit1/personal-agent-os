## Structural identity

The `Boss` alias in **VS Dumac** points directly to **Dumac's Tonalframe**.

This exact actor naming is important: DAc0da does not label the encounter actor simply "King Dumac."

It supports the main-quest dialogue in which Rolls-On-Roads identifies the armor as Dumac's but warns that the figure may be a materialized shadow/retained spirit rather than the historical body.
