# N'Danda

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**NPC:** `DAc0da.esm:00306C` / `zDcdBossSload`  
**Displayed name:** **N'Danda**  
**Primary story layers:** `zDcdMq01` — The Sea of Causality; `zDcdSqSloadRadio`

## Role in The Sea of Causality

The SCEN index resolves the Sload speaker in the MQ01 encounter as **N'Danda**.

He recognizes that the player is not affected by Sload mind magic and initially dismisses the possibilities as either resistance or stupidity. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004507`.

When he recognizes the player as the **Doom-Driven**, he immediately orders a retreat. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00450A`.

He then:

- sends a subordinate to stall the player while he calls reinforcements (`004514`);
- orders that the player's body not be damaged too badly because it will be needed later (`004519`);
- calls for a floater to return (`00451C`);
- attempts to bargain for his survival by promising freedom and long life (`004524`).

This makes N'Danda a concrete operational participant in the Sload capture/body-use program rather than only a radio name.

## Sload Radio

A later alliance broadcast says **Captain N'Danda** guarantees the lives and dignity of those who surrender. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE53`.

This title identifies the earlier MQ01 Sload as a captain within the Sload/Ul'vor Kus operational structure.

## Orders to N'Danda

BOOK `DAc0da.esm:00CEDD`, **Orders to N'Danda**, instructs him to move south after the clash of two liches near Numidium.

The order treats shipwrecked human/elven survivors as abundant **material** and directs N'Danda to collect that material and retrieve the scattered/ashen lich.

This written order aligns with his MQ01 insistence that the player's body should remain usable.

## Character interpretation

N'Danda is therefore directly associated with:

- Sload mind magic;
- capture rather than simple killing;
- preservation of bodies as usable material;
- Sload floaters/logistics;
- later alliance propaganda and surrender broadcasts.

The exact experiments planned for the player in MQ01 are not stated in that scene alone and should be joined with Echoes of Mnemolichite's later Sload/Yaghra experimentation rather than assumed to be identical.
