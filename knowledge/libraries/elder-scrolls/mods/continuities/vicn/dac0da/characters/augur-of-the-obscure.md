## Non-linear continuity

When encountered through the side quest, the Augur greets the player as though the meeting has happened before and says the previous version of the player died five minutes earlier. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0047F0`.

When the player asks him to leave, he says every attempt to leave is followed by the player reaching back in and calling him again. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0047F4`.

This matches the main quest's earlier evidence that the Augur experiences time non-linearly and can perceive alternate versions/locations of the player.

## Adjacent Place origin

In his generic dialogue, the Augur says he is a **traveler from an Adjacent Place** who is merely visiting. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490A`.

He says his skull-like appearance is a "trick of the light" and that his intended shape is more like a throw pillow, implying that the perceived form is not his underlying ontology. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490A`.

This is direct DAc0da evidence that Adjacent-Place entities can appear through a locally legible/perceptual form.

## Adjacent Places

When asked what an Adjacent Place is, he compares it to a sticky spot on the backside of paper and then describes it as a place where entities can fight using weapons of pure mathematics. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490C`.

He immediately warns that **Grabbers** can bite off one's head there. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490C`.

## Grabbers

The Augur says a single entity is a **Grabber** and multiple are **Grabbers**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490E`.

He compares their behavior to vampires sucking blood, except Grabbers "suck out all sorts of things." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490E`.

He also associates large gatherings of them with a "Magna-Ge striptease show"; the exact meaning is not resolved by this dialogue and should remain quoted-source vocabulary rather than normalized interpretation.

## View of mortals

The Augur calls ordinary embodied people **meat-brains** and says he enjoys watching them "trample all over possibilities." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004911`.

That wording places him conceptually next to DAc0da's Many Paths / possibility framework, though the quest does not define a formal mechanism connecting the terms.
