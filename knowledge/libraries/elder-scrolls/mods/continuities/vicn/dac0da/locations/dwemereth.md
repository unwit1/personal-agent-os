# Dwemereth

**Worldspace:** `DAc0da.esm:0097E3` / `zDcdDwemereth`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

Dwemereth is DAc0da's custom Dwemer/Numidium machine-world exterior.

## Named cell structure

Meaningful custom cell families include:

- `DcdDwemerethStart`
- central/lower/upper clusters (`CL`, `CU`)
- northeast, northwest, southeast and southwest clusters
- `DcdMantellaTower01` through `DcdMantellaTower06`

The existence of a six-cell Mantella Tower turns the Mantella from dialogue-only metaphysics into a literal navigable machine-world structure.

## Relation to Numidium interiors

Associated named interiors include:

- Numidium Cavitas
- Numidium Arteria
- Numidium Vena
- Numidium Iecur
- Numidium Diaphragm
- Numidium Vena Cava
- Numidium Neural Circuit
- Numidium Cockpit
- Anu[s]midium

This anatomical naming directly supports the main-quest description of Numidium as a vascular/machine-body environment.

## Direct boss anchors

- GC9 / O.Y.A.R.S.A -> **Numidium Vena**
- Arcanist -> **Numidium Iecur**
- Zurin Arctus -> **Numidium Vena Cava**
- Dumac's Tonalframe -> **Mantella Tower 01**
