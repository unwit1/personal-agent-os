# Sea of Causality

**Worldspace:** `DAc0da.esm:000DBB` / `zDcdGhostSea`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

The **Sea of Causality** is DAc0da's primary custom exterior worldspace and contains most of the temporally displaced side material surrounding the Numidium event.

These are mechanically separate cell clusters within one worldspace.

## Story progression represented geographically

The worldspace physically interleaves multiple displaced histories:

- MQ01 shipwreck/Sload/Yaghra/Agent material in **Ship Graveyard**;
- Samon's golden-dragon pursuit;
- Yngol/Atmoran history through **Harakk**, **Frost Sea**, **Jylkurfyk**, and Kalpic-Ship layers;
- Vanus/Yu'qbar/Sload research through **Road to Coral**, Yaghra caves, and **Neo-Thras**;
- Mannimarco/God-of-Worms climax at **Revenant Gate**;
- Zurin/Underking anomaly at **Zurin Island**.

The level design therefore supports DAc0da's central idea that incompatible times and stories have been physically collected into one unstable sea.
