# Mozarella Spacetime

**Worldspace:** `DAc0da.esm:00B765` / `zDcdBlindRealm`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

Mozarella Spacetime is the custom worldspace used by DAc0da's Sheogorath/Jillian-censorship alternate-ending material.

## Direct actor placement

Both:

- Sheogorath `DAc0da.esm:00C994`
- Rolls-On-Roads fragment/bit `DAc0da.esm:00C995`

are placed in `DcdBlindRealm07`.

## Narrative role

This world is associated with **Rella Mozzarella**, in which:

- Sheogorath describes the place as a disappearing half-plane;
- Jills are actively censoring/devouring the surrounding reality;
- Rolls-On-Roads is being bitten/consumed;
- the player is offered an escape.

The worldspace is therefore branch-specific and should not be treated as a normal location accessible in the standard Censored Fate chronology.
