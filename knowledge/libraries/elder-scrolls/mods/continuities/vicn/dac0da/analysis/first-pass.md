# DAc0da first record-derived lore pass

Continuity: `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Structural picture

DAc0da is organized around a Numidium/time-collapse main plot rather than a conventional linear Skyrim quest chain. The six numbered main quests run from **The Call of Landfall** through **Censored Fate**, with side arcs for Yngol, the Augur, Abnur Tharn, Mnemolichite/Worm material, Sheogorath, Sloads, and several epilogues.

The main quest objectives themselves establish recurring **time breaches**, entry into the **Numidium**, and a later effort to **close** those breaches. Those are direct QUST-record facts.

## First high-value lore nodes

The opening dialogue pass adds stronger source-scoped claims:

- A Psijic-associated Monk describes the Numidium as collapsing time around itself on a scale the Psijic Order cannot repair. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA37`.
- The Monk says there are other versions of the player in other times/spaces that cannot perceive one another. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA32`.
- The mission is framed as banishing the Numidium from the current "present", not defeating it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA35`, `DAc0da.esm:00AA64`.
- Multiple dialogue variants count repeated failed attempts at changing the outcome, eventually describing the event as becoming fixed "beyond fate." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA4D` through `00AA51`, `0044EE`, `00AA53`.
- The Monk attributes the saying "Each Event is preceded by Prophecy. But without the Hero, there is no Event" to **Zurin Arctus**. This is an in-world attribution made by DAc0da dialogue, not an automatically imported Bethesda-canon quotation. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA5B`.
- The Monk identifies **Jills** as female dragons tasked with repairing the flow of time and says the mission is to help them correct time after closing the breach. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA69`, `DAc0da.esm:00AA6B`.
- The Monk reports that the whole Psijic Order is working to slow the Numidian effect and that Artaeum is in upheaval. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA6D`.

## Claim-strength cautions

Several opening statements are explicitly speculative or speaker-limited:

- The claim that this Numidium is a remnant of the Warp in the West is presented as **Psijic speculation**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA62`.
- The Monk reports that the Ritemaster asked Daedric Princes for help and they refused, but the suggested reasons—moving on to another kalpa or fearing for their own existence—are introduced as possibilities, not established facts. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA66`.
- Repeated-attempt counts are conditional dialogue variants (54th, 112th, 283rd, 379th, 484th, 643rd), so they should be modeled as state/fate-line evidence rather than flattened into one ordinary chronology.

## Early character/time-displacement node

**Akashiya-Samon** identifies himself as a Dragonguard serving Reman, calls Reman the true Dragonborn, and describes pursuing a golden dragon before washing up in a ship graveyard. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004489`, `00448C`, `004493`.

The surrounding quest is already operating inside Numidium-linked temporal instability. This makes Samon a strong time-displacement candidate, but the exact transport mechanism should remain unresolved until scenes/scripts and later Samon quests are joined.
