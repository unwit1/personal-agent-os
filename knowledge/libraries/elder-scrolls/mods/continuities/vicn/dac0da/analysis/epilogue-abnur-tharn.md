# Abnur Tharn epilogue

**Quest:** `zDcdEpiQAbnur`  
**Title:** Epi Abnur  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:00CEE1`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Quest structure

The epilogue explicitly instructs the player to:

1. talk to a courier;
2. read **Abnur Tharn's will**.

The courier alias points to `DAc0da.esm:00CEE2`, **Messenger of House Tharn**.

## House Tharn delivery

The messenger says House Tharn discovered a will more than a thousand years old that directs part of the estate to the player. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEEF`.

The messenger says they were told they would recognize the intended recipient on sight despite the insulting description used in the will. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEEB`.

## The will

Two BOOK records contain variants of **The Last Will and Testament of Abnur Tharn**:

- `DAc0da.esm:00CEDF` — `zDcdLetterTharnWillA`
- `DAc0da.esm:00CEE0` — `zDcdLetterTharnWillB`

Both versions have Abnur remember the strange sea and towering Dwemer giant as an event from long before his deathbed. He leaves the player the **Shield of Varen Aquilarios**.

The will identifies Varen as the husband of Abnur's sixteenth daughter, Clivia Tharn, and says Varen was once regarded by people as a possible second coming of Reman even though the gods did not choose him as such.

The two variants differ in the admonition attached to the bequest:

- one says the player deserves the shield and should learn wisdom by knowing their place;
- the other explicitly says even a powerful Dragonborn is not worthy of the Ruby Throne and warns against self-destruction through banal ambition.

Both variants instruct House Tharn to find the intended recipient in Skyrim in **4E 201**, using archival clues tied to Reman-era Imperial Mananauts.

These are DAc0da's Abnur-Tharn statements and chronology; official ESO chronology/source material should be compared separately.
