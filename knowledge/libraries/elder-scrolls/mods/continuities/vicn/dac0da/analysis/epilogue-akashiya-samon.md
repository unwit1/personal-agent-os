# Akashiya-Samon epilogue

**Quest:** `zDcdEpiQSamon` — Epilogue Samon  
**Follow-up:** `zDcdEpiQSamonAfter` — Epi Samon Gen  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Ghost form

The epilogue alias points to `DAc0da.esm:00CE92`, `zDcdUqTsaesciSamuraiGhost`, named **Akashiya-Samon**.

Samon explains his transparency as a consequence of the player's earlier duel and says his soul returned to Reman. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE9D`, `00CEA1`.

## Reman's prediction

Samon says Reman took great interest in the player's story and ordered him to wait in the temple until the player's arrival in the far future. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE99`, `00CEA1`.

Samon does not know why; he concludes that Reman must have foreseen something. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEA4`.

This is direct evidence that DAc0da attributes some form of future foresight or long-range planning to Reman, but the mechanism remains unstated.

## Later service

An alternate Samon response says he served Reman's descendants for generations until **Versidue-Shaie** betrayed the Emperor. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEA2`.

This extends Samon's chronology well beyond his first Reman-era introduction and should be reconciled against his Yngol-side-quest appearances before a final timeline is constructed.

## Dragon-hunting philosophy

In the follow-up dialogue Samon says dragon hunting is a **means, not an end**, and frames the Dragonborn's purpose as returning dragon souls to Akatosh. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEB5`.

He says the historical Dragonguard could only kill dragons but the player is not bound to repeat that limitation. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEB5`.

## Paarthurnax

Samon gives conditional advice about Paarthurnax.

If the player is undecided, he emphasizes that the warrior must personally choose whether to fight and accept responsibility for that choice. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEBB`.

He describes Paarthurnax as an old, tired monk for whom there is little honor in fighting. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEB3`.

He also says past atrocities do not automatically settle the question, using his own past as an enemy of Reman who was later accepted into service. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEB7`.

If the player killed Paarthurnax, Samon can condemn the act as lacking mercy or challenge attempts to justify it through future risk or judgment. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEC0`, `00CEC2`, `00CEC4`.

If Paarthurnax was spared, Samon accepts the decision and says difficult choices carry responsibility for preventing later harm. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEC9`, `00CECB`, `00CECD`.

These are Samon's values and judgments, not neutral truth claims about Paarthurnax.

## Delphine

Samon considers Delphine skilled but too strong-willed to obey the Dragonborn and compares the problem to Reman's ability to control difficult vassals. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CEB0`.

This remains character opinion.
