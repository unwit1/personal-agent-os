# DAc0da MESG insight pass 01

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm`  
**Record class:** MESG

DAc0da contains **66 MESG records**. Many are ordinary UI prompts, but a substantial minority are lore-bearing inscriptions, diagnostics, historical fragments, or system-level concept definitions.

## Atmora / serpent / Star-Men material

### Aeonstone — `00471C`

A pale-green ore from **southern Elsweyr** is said to store and amplify magical energy. The message asks who brought it to Atmora.

This is a direct DAc0da bridge between Atmora and aeonstone/Kaalgrontiid-adjacent material.

### Tsa Rat — `00471D`

A serpent-folk creature/figure appears in Atmora from time to time. The message distinguishes serpents arriving:

- from the west, riding serpents;
- from the east, wearing snakeskin.

The **Tsa rat** acts as a decoy to mislead both groups and keep them from Atmora.

### Star Men — `00471E`, `00471F`

A group called **Star Men** arrive riding serpents in a reversed-sun image. After seeing **Ysmir, king of Men and dragons**, they understand the cycles of stars and choose to serve him.

Another record says the Star Men lost sight of the constellations during a long voyage, then realized they had moved so quickly that the constellations had not yet risen.

These are DAc0da insight texts; exact identity with Tsaesci, Akaviri, or other star-travel groups requires separate evidence.

## Herma-Mora / Forelgrim

### Hans the Fox seal — `00475B`

The text reads as an inscription by **Hans the Fox**, sealing a cursed hare of **Forelgrim**.

It warns that the plague of **Frostwood** is spreading across Atmoran forests and says not to listen to the forest beast because **its words will reshape you**.

### Serpent warning — `00477B`

A short litany warns against speaking of, praising, or seeking **the Serpent**, ending with the reciprocal image that whoever “fills the Serpent” will be filled by it.

## Jill / Hist diagnostics

### Fallen Jill — `004B7C`

The observer asks whether a Jill fell because it could not withstand Numidium's Dragon Break.

The Jill is no longer active, and hostility is sensed from **plant roots tangled around its body**, identified as the **Hist**.

### Hist isomer: Milk Tree — `004B7D`

A Dwemer device on a body transmits:

> Hist isomer: **Milk Tree** invaded through the **Wheelian rip**. It is currently advancing on planet **KYNRT**. Urgently requesting reinforcements.

This substantially strengthens the MQ04 Hist/Jill anomaly:

- the roots are not merely scenery;
- DAc0da names them a **Hist isomer**;
- the isomer is called **Milk Tree**;
- its movement is described as an invasion through a **Wheelian rip**;
- the target/location is rendered as **planet KYNRT**.

The message does not define Wheelian rip, KYNRT, or the relationship between this Hist isomer and ordinary Hist.

## Beynhaal / masks / Jhunal-Owl bridge

### Beynhaal parchment — `005121`

A parchment in Beynhaal's hands pleads with the Dragon God to raise its Voice in anger at the people's wickedness.

### Smiling masks — `005122`

The message says people:

- gave everything;
- gained nothing;
- donned smiling masks;
- mocked even themselves.

It then says this masked smile was passed on to:

- the **giants of Jhunal**, who use it to mock their own ignorance;
- the **Owl sorcerers**, who use it to mock the ignorance of god.

This is a direct rare-vocabulary DAc0da bridge into VICN's wider **Jhunal/Owl** network.

### Snow-Drake doll — `005132`

The record questions whether a doll's tail mocks a Snow Drake or sincerely wishes for draconic strength and states that the sacrificed girl has gone somewhere hatred and wishes cannot reach.

## Hahd / mnemolichite

### Nahd book title — `005148`

A text is titled **The Dervish of Dellesia and the Mnemolichite Maid** and attributed to a “humble Nahd of Nahd.”

This supports Yu'qbar's Dervish/Maid tradition as something represented in written culture inside DAc0da.

### Mnemolichite Maid dissolution — `0060F6`

The message says crystallized memories are crumbling and that the **Mnemolichite Maid** appears to have returned to the **sea of memories**.

This is system/observation evidence that sits beside the mutually inconsistent character interpretations at the quest ending.

## Pan-Argonia bad-ending summary

MESG `DAc0da.esm:00CA5C` explicitly labels itself:

**Bad Ending: Pan-Argonia**

It says:

- the player's soul reactivates Numidium;
- Rolls-On-Roads takes control;
- he builds a **histo-neural network**;
- man and mer are exterminated;
- Argonians unify Tamriel;
- pre-Duskfall prosperity/primordial chaos returns;
- Hist roots transcend stars, time, and space;
- a never-ending war with the Jills begins.

Because the message itself calls this a **Bad Ending**, it is explicit branch/failure-state material and must not be used as the normal DAc0da chronology.

## Retrieval rule

MESG records should be treated according to their framing:

- **insight/inscription** -> in-world observational or attributed text;
- **bad-ending summary** -> explicit branch summary;
- **system/UI explanatory message** -> direct mod-design concept evidence;
- **ordinary prompt** -> mechanics only.

Do not flatten all MESG prose into omniscient narration.
