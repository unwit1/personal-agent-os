# Hist, Jills, Akulakhan, and the Fifth-Era anomaly

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary evidence:** late `zDcdMq04` dialogue plus MESG diagnostics

## Hist versus Jill

Inside Numidium, the Monk recognizes roots as Hist but says the voice is unfamiliar even to him as an Argonian. He speculates that this Hist may have gone mad. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B6ED`.

The roots are physically shown clamping down on a Jill. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B6F6`.

A separate insight record makes the encounter more specific.

MESG `DAc0da.esm:004B7C` describes a fallen Jill and asks whether it could not withstand Numidium's Dragon Break. It says plant roots tangled around the body are identified as **Hist** and emit hostility.

MESG `DAc0da.esm:004B7D` reports through a Dwemer device:

- **Hist isomer: Milk Tree**
- invaded through a **Wheelian rip**
- currently advancing on planet **KYNRT**
- urgently requesting reinforcements

This upgrades the scene from a generic "Hist roots versus Jill" image to a named DAc0da conflict involving a **Milk Tree Hist isomer** and a dimensional/temporal invasion route.

## Wheelian-rip provenance boundary

The phrase **Wheelian rip** is also present in the Michael Kirkbride KINMUNE material already preserved in the repository's apocryphal source library, where Hist-Jillian warfare spills through such a rip.

DAc0da therefore deliberately imports/adapts the same rare vocabulary into its own Hist/Jill encounter.

The safe provenance model is:

- KINMUNE terminology/original prose -> external developer/obscure-text source;
- DAc0da's **Milk Tree / KYNRT / specific Numidium encounter** -> Vicn's adaptation/integration.

Do not treat the repeated vocabulary as proof that every KINMUNE event occurs unchanged in DAc0da.

## What remains unresolved

The ESM does not yet define:

- what makes this Hist an **isomer**;
- whether **Milk Tree** is an individual Hist, a network, a ship-like form, or a category;
- what exactly **KYNRT** denotes;
- whether the Wheelian rip is the same mechanism as an Adjacent Place, time breach, or Many-Paths transition;
- why this Hist faction is attacking the Jill.

These should remain explicit research questions.

## Akulakhan and the Nerevarine

After the central breach closes, Numidium's records display Akulakhan and identify its rider as the Nerevarine. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B71F`, `00B728`.

The Monk flags this as contradictory because his expected history has the Nerevarine destroying Akulakhan, not piloting it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B72A`.

He further notices that the rider's armor appears inconsistent with the Third Era and explicitly raises the possibility of another reality/fate. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B72C`.

## DAc0da's Fifth-Era Numidium theory

The Numidium record set includes a Fifth-Era date. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B733`.

The Monk's synthesis is extraordinary and should be stored as **his reconstruction**, not objective chronology:

1. the Numidium originates in the Tiber War;
2. its siege of Alinor somehow continues across an impossible time span;
3. in the Fifth Era it battles a Nerevarine driving Akulakhan;
4. Numidium wins;
5. a fracture in space-time associated with that battle displaces part of Numidium into the present line;
6. this follows destruction of Nirn in that alternate Fifth-Era history.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B741`, `00B745`.

The Monk himself acknowledges uncertainty and hopes his interpretation is correct. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B747`.

## Jillian censorship

After the crisis, the Monk says the Jills will begin "censoring" the events: optimizing them, minimizing discrepancies, and reassigning participants to appropriate coordinates. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B743`, `00B74F`, `00B751`.

This provides DAc0da's explanation for how wildly divergent fate-line events can occur without remaining ordinary history.

The alternate **Rella Mozzarella** route further depicts Jills as literally biting/swallowing a person's past, but that imagery is branch-specific and should not replace the main MQ05 description.
