# DAc0da written-lore pass 01

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

This pass covers the highest-value DAc0da-specific BOOK records and separates them from obvious source-reuse candidates.

## Snow-Elf orders: attack on Jylkurfyk

**Orders to Wintercaller Unit** (`DAc0da.esm:0049F6`) says:

- Ysgramor, Yngol, and Ylgar survived Saarthal and returned to Atmora;
- they are believed to be regrouping forces for a return invasion;
- a Wintercaller unit is ordered to attack the Atmoran military port of **Jylkurfyk**;
- Ysgramor and his sons are to be killed on sight;
- all Saarthal citizens are to be slain;
- the order claims this violence is required to honor a **contract with Lord Dagon**.

This is extremely important DAc0da source testimony because it gives the Snow-Elf side an explicit aggressive operation against Atmora and introduces a Dagon contract.

It should be read beside, not substituted for, the conflicting Atmoran testimony in **Drowned Nighthawk**.

## Snow-Elf awareness of the Jylkurfyk purge

**Search Request Denied** (`DAc0da.esm:0049F7`) says:

- Snow-Elf ambassadors/relatives in Atmora are missing;
- an **anti-elven purge** is underway in Jylkurfyk;
- even people of mixed Snow-Elf/Atmoran blood are being targeted;
- authorities consider recovery hopeless and order the petitioner to stop filing search requests.

This directly corroborates the purge references encountered in the Yngol/Atmora quest material.

Together, the two letters deliberately prevent a simple victim/aggressor account: DAc0da supplies evidence both for Snow-Elf military aggression and for Atmoran anti-elven persecution.

## Radiance -> Altano / VIGILANT

**Letter to a Friend** (`DAc0da.esm:006133`) is associated with **The Sea of Radiance**.

The writer says they are departing for a world without sorrow or pain and tells their friend how to find the path to Radiance:

- go to nightmare-plagued **Dawnstar**;
- find a Vigilant named **Altano**;
- there, learn compassion;
- suffer, because there is no Radiance without suffering.

This is a direct named DAc0da -> **VIGILANT** continuity bridge.

The existing VIGILANT library independently identifies Altano as a central Vigilant character with a full journal and main-quest chain. DAc0da's letter should therefore be co-retrieved with Altano's VIGILANT dossier, while its Radiance interpretation remains DAc0da-specific.

## Abnur's note

**Tharn's Note** (`DAc0da.esm:00CA5A`) has Abnur identify himself as Grand Chancellor/overlord of Nibenay and say he has wandered unstable currents of time since the battle with Kaalgrontiid on Dragonhold.

He identifies the nearby Dwemer colossus as possibly Numidium and warns future readers away from a powerful undead sorcerer to the north.

This note provides written corroboration for DAc0da's temporally displaced Abnur side quest.

## Sload operational order

**Orders to N'Danda** (`DAc0da.esm:00CEDD`) reports:

- two liches fought at the foot of Numidium;
- a red-robed lich won;
- the other was blasted south and scattered ashes;
- the surviving red-robed lich is left alone while Sload floaters are ordered to avoid him;
- the battle raised the seafloor;
- human and elven fleets were caught in the battle;
- the Sload view the resulting survivors/captives as abundant **"material"**;
- N'Danda is ordered south to collect that material and retrieve the ashen lich.

This provides written evidence for the opportunistic logistics behind the Sload kidnapping/experimentation seen elsewhere.

The lich identities should not be assigned from color/location alone without joining quest/form evidence.

## Temporal Tomes

Three late BOOK records are explicitly framed as alternate-reality texts.

### Karnwasten Temporal Tome — `DAc0da.esm:00CE64`

Describes a reality where Sea Sload rule over Summerset and reshape subjects through:

- mindshaping;
- B'Korgen fleshcrafting;
- Yaghra-assisted bodily modification;
- Z'maja-associated shadow training.

This strongly overlaps **Sload Radio** terminology and provides a fuller alternate-world context for K'Tora/B'Korgen-style transformation programs.

### Traitor's Vault Temporal Tome — `DAc0da.esm:00CE65`

Describes a world where Mannimarco wins during the Planemeld, Nirn becomes a necromantic catastrophe, and surviving Psijics attempt to remove Artaeum from Nirn.

This should be stored as a **Many Paths / alternate-reality document**, not ordinary ESO chronology.

### Garden of Sacred Numbers Temporal Tome — `DAc0da.esm:00CE66`

Describes an alternate reality overwhelmed by unknown entities from beyond the world after sacred-number defenses fail.

It is useful evidence for DAc0da's broader library of failed possibility-lines, but its unnamed entities should not automatically be identified with Grabbers or any other group.

## Source-reuse boundary

The Daggerfall/Totem-letter block and the MK/apocryphal Nahd-fiction block should remain separate from the DAc0da-authored/quest-integrated evidence above.

The correct ingestion behavior is:

- preserve that DAc0da **contains/uses** the text;
- link to the existing official/apocryphal source where known;
- do not treat embedded reused prose as a new Vicn claim merely because it appears in `DAc0da.esm`.
