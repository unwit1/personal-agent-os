# Patchwork — quest reconstruction

**Quest:** `zDcdMq03`  
**Title:** Patchwork  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:004981`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Direct quest structure

Objectives in the QUST record:

1. Talk to the Monk.
2. Fix one of the large time breaches.
3. Talk to the Monk again.
4. Break into the Numidium.
5. Optionally continue the briefing.

The quest therefore turns DAc0da's temporal cosmology into an explicit gameplay rule: reduce temporal contradictions enough to create a safe route into Numidium.

## Underking and Numidium provenance uncertainty

When the player says they have just faced the **Underking**, the Monk says time is too distorted to explain the encounter cleanly. He recalls that histories place the Underking's death during the Warp in the West and wonders whether the present Numidium might be from the Third Era. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00498A`.

The Third-Era-origin idea is explicitly framed as the Monk's question/speculation, not a settled fact.

## How DAc0da time breaches work

The Monk says the Numidian effect is spreading too quickly for accurate teleportation and that the main breach is inside Numidium. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004992`.

He identifies two large breaches, east and west, and asks the player to close at least one. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004996`.

The clearest rule is stated directly: make cause and effect "add up" again by finding whoever is out of place in the current time and eliminating them. If the result matches the present, the breach shrinks on its own. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004999`, `00499E`.

This makes DAc0da's breach mechanic explicitly **causal/chronological**, not merely spatial.

## Historical contradictions used as breach contents

The Monk reacts to two possible breach resolutions:

- **Yngol:** he recalls the historical story of Ysgramor's son being lost in a storm at sea and says the contradiction is comparatively small. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049A5`.
- **Mannimarco:** he says the Sload were attempting to incarnate Mannimarco and that a successful incarnation would have caused the plan to fail. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049A8`.

The Yngol response also invokes a theory of Valenwood's **spinners** and says the Psijic Order can correct the remaining contradiction, with the Jills handling the rest. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049A5`.

## Entering Numidium

Once distortion is reduced, the Monk opens a portal into Numidium and warns that it has self-defense mechanisms. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049B0`.

He casts a ward intended to protect against Numidium's **zero-sum attacks**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049B2`.

The Monk says the ward applies **mirror logic from Alinor**, claims a related technique proved useful during Tiber Septim's Numidium campaign, and says the historical Mirror Logicians were wiped out in battle with Numidium. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049C4`, `0049C6`.

He further claims the Psijic Order's version of mirror logic is more advanced. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049C8`.

When asked what zero-sum is, he calls it one of Numidium's defenses against foreign substances and refuses a deeper explanation, asserting that knowing less improves survival odds. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049CB`, `0049CD`.

These statements are direct DAc0da dialogue but remain source-scoped cosmological claims rather than automatic Bethesda-canon facts.

## Transition

Patchwork ends by opening the route into Numidium, leading into **Numidium Tertius** (`zDcdMq04`).
