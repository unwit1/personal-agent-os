# DAc0da apocryphal / obscure-text provenance pass 01

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary records:** `zDcdNahdFiction_*`

## Why this pass exists

DAc0da embeds a substantial body of pre-existing Elder Scrolls developer/obscure/community apocrypha as readable BOOK records.

The correct library model is:

1. record that **DAc0da contains and deliberately deploys** a source;
2. retain the source's original authorship/provenance;
3. treat placement, selection, adaptation, and quest-context as the DAc0da/Vicn evidence;
4. do not silently reclassify embedded outside prose as Vicn-authored lore.

## `DAc0da.esm:0085FC` — Djaf: Arena of Lyg

**Editor ID:** `zDcdNahdFiction_001_Mojonation1487`  
**Origin:** community apocrypha by **mojonation1487**  
**Status:** verified external community-text reuse

The editor ID itself names `Mojonation1487`.

External provenance independently supports that attribution: mojonation1487 reposted **Djaf: The Arena of Lyg** as the progenitor of the LDZ project and described it as the first realized appearance of Ghost Choir 9.

An earlier illustrated-post discussion also has mojonation1487 explicitly describe the Lyg worldbuilding as their own interpretation, while Michael Kirkbride replies separately about Lyg and Ghost Choir 9.

DAc0da's BOOK text follows the same Aechneil / Bleed / Djaf / Ghost Choir 9 narrative.

### Library treatment

- prose/content origin: **mojonation1487 community apocrypha**
- inclusion and use in DAc0da: **Vicn narrative curation/integration**
- do not attribute the Djaf prose to Michael Kirkbride merely because it shares Ghost Choir 9 terminology.

## `DAc0da.esm:0085FD` — Ghost Choir 9

**Editor ID:** `zDcdNahdFiction_002_MK`  
**Origin status:** **strongly attributed to Michael Kirkbride; original standalone publication location not yet recovered**

The DAc0da text is titled:

> Ghost Choir 9 — An in-character text concerning the Imperial Blade-Seneschal Stringform Multivox Warframe

It contains the nine-name identity including Captain Starkweather, Cat, Duke, Copper, Reinholdt, Mira, Nuttergun, Kohl, and John Satisfaction.

External material confirms:

- Ghost Choir 9 is an idea associated with Michael Kirkbride;
- Kirkbride's **Landfall: Day One** uses Ghost Choir 9 directly;
- mojonation1487 separately describes Ghost Choir 9 as an RPG idea by MK while saying they themselves wrote about the concept in their own works.

A later external provenance search compared the **actual full DAc0da BOOK body** against public material.

Findings:

- the DAc0da text is **not** the mojonation1487 `Djaf: Arena of Lyg` story; it is a separate first-person/in-character GC9 transcript that partially recalls the Djaf arena state;
- Michael Kirkbride's **Landfall: Day One** independently uses Ghost Choir 9, confirming the concept in Kirkbride's own published C0DA material;
- a later Italian Elder Scrolls lore translation reproduces the exact distinctive DAc0da transcript and labels it **“Ghost Choir 9 (Michael Kirkbride)”**;
- exact-phrase searches for unique strings such as the `ODDSEMBLIO` sequence, “CHOIR 9 SLEEVESHELL,” and the nym-pause/mem-web passage did **not** recover an older primary publication page.

### Library treatment

Current provenance confidence:

- `_MK` editor attribution: direct DAc0da metadata;
- exact text: independently reproduced in a later translation explicitly attributed to Michael Kirkbride;
- Ghost Choir 9 concept: independently present in Kirkbride's `Landfall: Day One`;
- original standalone publication URL/date for this exact transcript: **unresolved**.

Therefore classify the text as **strongly Michael-Kirkbride-attributed obscure material**, while preserving the narrower unresolved field: **original publication provenance**.

Do not confuse it with mojonation1487's separate `Djaf` prose simply because both contain Captain Starkweather and the Ghost Choir 9 identity.

## DAc0da narrative significance

These source distinctions do **not** make the texts irrelevant to DAc0da.

Vicn's choice to place them together in the DAc0da/Nahd corpus creates a retrieval cluster around:

- Lyg / Adjacent Places;
- Ghost Choir 9;
- Biters / names;
- KINMUNE / Dominion futures;
- Tsaesci naming metaphysics;
- zero sum;
- adjacentia;
- Numidium / future-era technology;
- Pelinal/Morihaus;
- Dwemer zero-language.

What belongs to Vicn is the **curation, placement, adaptation, and narrative reuse** of these sources inside DAc0da. Their original prose claims retain their original source labels.
