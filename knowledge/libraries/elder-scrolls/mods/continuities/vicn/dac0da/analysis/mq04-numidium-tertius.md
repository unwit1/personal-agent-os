# Numidium Tertius — quest reconstruction

**Quest:** `zDcdMq04`  
**Title:** Numidium Tertius  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:00B21B`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Direct quest structure

The QUST record sends the player through the Numidium itself. Objectives include reaching the upper structure, dealing with defense systems, optionally avoiding or fighting **Ghost Choir 9** and an **Arcanist**, passing Mantella-related defenses, and finally closing the central time breach.

This is the densest DAc0da main-quest lore section processed so far.

## The Numidium as an interior world

Immediately after entry, the Monk estimates that the teleport created only about a two-hour time difference. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B21F`.

He describes the entry area as the base of Numidium's legs, containing heat exhaust and waste-disposal systems, and later compares transport channels to blood vessels leading toward a heart/central region. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B22F`, `00B2BE`.

The Monk believes much of the interior may still be essentially Kagrenac's original construction even though Numidium was rebuilt after the Dwemer. He says the exterior was reconstructed while the interior may have remained. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B2FA`.

The interior repeatedly uses **music/rhythm** as a design principle. The Monk states that Dwemer design is rooted in music and instructs the player to follow machine rhythms. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B248`, `00B36B`.

## Alternate-fate Agents and Memospores

The group encounters an Agent body that, according to the Monk, did not exist there moments earlier and must have arrived from another fate line. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B237`.

A nearby glowing fragment is identified as a **Memospore**, described as an information form used for remote communication through the Dreamsleeve. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B239`.

The Monk later describes the Agent as a candidate for the **Prisoner** and a Hero of Daggerfall who vanished during the Warp in the West. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B38A`.

He treats the Agent and Underking as evidence that the current crisis may be a fragment of the Warp in the West; that conclusion is presented as a Psijic theory, not objective narration. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B38E`.

## Ghost Choir 9

The linked Ghost Choir material describes an organization processing multiple "Agents," performing nymic analysis/reassignment, and reacting to strong Jillian interference. It also explicitly discusses the quantum vibration of a **Prisoner** and the difficulty of determining whether an Agent fully "existed." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B423` through `00B444`.

The Ghost Choir 9 battle dialogue names several members and identifies a target as an anomaly assigned to **AKA**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B44D` through `00B45F`.

The dying warning says the Jills are not on the player's side. The Monk later responds that this is technically true because the Jills are simply performing their function rather than acting as allies. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B5AE`, `00B3B0`.

## Apocryphal invasion

Within Numidium, the Monk identifies extensive **Apocryphal erosion**, Watchers, fate entanglement, and an Arcanist. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B4F2` through `00B4FD`.

He says forces of Apocrypha have stolen Numidium's calculation results and believes Hermaeus Mora wants knowledge inside the construct. His theories about Mora's exact goal remain conjectural. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B503`, `00B50C`.

He also says the Numidium isolated the Apocryphal contamination into an Adjacent Place, suggesting that its defenses can compartmentalize metaphysical intrusion. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B50F`.

## Canceled Dwemer projects and a "dreaming" Numidium

The party finds records of canceled Dwemer projects, some of which appear to be running despite their canceled status. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B5B1`, `00B5B3`.

Examples named by the Monk include a void exploration ship, a lunar base, and a dreaming animunculus. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B5C7`.

He interprets the dormant Numidium as calculating a canceled project "as if it's dreaming." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B5D7`.

## Zero-sum machinery

A red floodlight is described as a Dwemer zero-sum device that emits an opposite wavelength intended to obliterate the target. The mirror-logic ward prevents immediate disappearance but not separation from time and space. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B5CF`.

This gives DAc0da a concrete machine implementation of the zero-sum concept in addition to the earlier general defense description.

## Zurin Arctus and the Mantella

A linked encounter contains a lich attacking the party's barrier, followed by the statements that the speaker is not only their hatred, bears responsibility toward Hjalti, wants to finish what they began, and questions who Hjalti truly was. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B60D`, `00B61F`, `00B621`, `00B625`.

Afterward, the player identifies the defeated figure as **Zurin Arctus**, and the Monk accepts that identification. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B65A`.

The Monk describes the **Mantella** as:

- the green gem powering Numidium;
- the "Crux of Transcendence";
- a replacement for the Heart of Lorkhan created by Zurin Arctus;
- a vessel containing Zurin's soul;
- the source of the Underking's continuing immortality until he can reclaim it.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B670`.

He warns against destroying the Mantella, claiming the released energy would obliterate western Skyrim. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B68F`.

## Dumac

A Dwemer warrior near the Mantella is identified by armor as King Dumac. The Monk then qualifies that it is not necessarily the historical body but a materialized shadow retained by an unusually strong spirit. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B69A`, `00B69C`.

## Hist and Jills

Near the central breach the Monk recognizes **Hist roots** inside Numidium but says they feel unlike familiar Hist and may be mad. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B6ED`.

The roots are shown constraining a Jill, producing the explicit question of why the Hist and Jills are fighting. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B6F6`.

This is direct scene evidence for conflict; the cause remains unresolved.

## Prisoner role in closing the breach

The Monk says the player's contact can close the time breach because the Ritemaster believes the quantum vibrations of the player's **Prisoner** status stabilize the event. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B711`.

He further says the Jills, not the Psijic Order, assigned the player this role and intend to use the player's actions as a basis for correcting time and inconsistencies. This is his interpretation and he explicitly admits uncertainty about the mechanism. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B711`.

## Akulakhan / Nerevarine anomaly

After the breach closes, Numidium exposes historical data that the Monk finds contradictory:

- Akulakhan appears despite its expected Third-Era destruction (`DAc0da.esm:00B71F`);
- Numidium's systems show it fighting Akulakhan (`DAc0da.esm:00B721`);
- the rider is recognized as the Nerevarine (`DAc0da.esm:00B728`);
- the Nerevarine is shown piloting Akulakhan rather than destroying it (`DAc0da.esm:00B72A`);
- the Nerevarine's armor appears inconsistent with the Third Era (`DAc0da.esm:00B72C`);
- the record extends as late as the Fifth Era (`DAc0da.esm:00B733`).

The Monk's synthesized theory is that this Numidium originated in the Tiber War, continued its siege of Alinor across an impossible span into the Fifth Era, then fought and defeated a Nerevarine-piloted Akulakhan. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B741`.

He interprets the construct's arrival in the present as a fragment displaced after the destruction of Nirn in that Fifth-Era line and the Numidium/Akulakhan battle's fracture of space-time. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B745`.

These are **DAc0da's reconstructed alternate-fate history**, explicitly filtered through the Monk's interpretation of garbled Numidium records. They must not be flattened into base Elder Scrolls chronology.

## Ending state

The Psijic Order reports the other breaches repaired. The Monk says the Jills will "censor" the event by optimizing what happened and minimizing discrepancies, after which participants will be reassigned to their appropriate coordinates. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B74D`, `00B74F`, `00B751`.

This closes the operational MQ04 arc and leads to **Censored Fate**.
