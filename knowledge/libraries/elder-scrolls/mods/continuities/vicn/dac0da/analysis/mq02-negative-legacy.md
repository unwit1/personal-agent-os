# Negative Legacy — quest reconstruction

**Quest:** `zDcdMq02`  
**Title:** Negative Legacy  
**Source:** `DAc0da.esm:004931`  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

## Direct quest structure

Objectives in the QUST record:

1. Go to the meeting place.
2. Optionally talk to the Augur.
3. Eliminate time breaches.
4. Defeat `Boss01`.

Quest aliases include **Augur**, **MonkHolo**, **Zurin**, **ZurinTA**, and **Boss01**, making the Zurin/Underking material structurally part of this quest rather than a disconnected editor-ID coincidence.

## Augur and non-linear time

The Augur greets the player as though the same conversation has happened repeatedly. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00493F`.

He explicitly says his perception of time is not linear and that he can identify when and where the player is. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004942`.

He contrasts this with the player's linear time perception, saying the player cannot recognize another version of themself standing nearby. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004945`.

This independently reinforces MQ00's multiple-"you" / repeated-fate-line framing.

## Memospore and blocked meeting point

The Augur says he received a **memospore** from a lizard friend and plays a stored message. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004945`, `004948`.

The useful message says the planned meeting point is at the foot of Numidium but cannot currently be approached because something is blocking time travel. It suggests a powerful entity emerged from a time breach and must be removed. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00494E`, `004950`, `004952`.

The Augur leaves before the **Walk-Brass** can erase him from existence, directly tying his concern to Numidium. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004954`.

## Misrouted Vestige memospore

The SCEN record `DAc0da.esm:004946` resolves the speakers in the memospore sequence precisely:

1. **Augur of the Obscure** announces that he is playing the memospore.
2. **Crab Spirit** delivers a message addressed to a **Vestige** and begins offering advice about fighting **Molag Bal**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00494A`.
3. **Augur** immediately interrupts and says it was the wrong one, intended for **an old friend**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00494C`.
4. The following scene actions switch to the `MonkHolo` alias, resolved as **Rolls-On-Roads**, for the actual Numidium/time-travel message.

The scene action table explicitly binds the Vestige/Molag-Bal line to the quest's **Crab** alias rather than the player or Rolls-On-Roads.

This should therefore be modeled as a **misrouted stored memory/message**. It is a cross-era easter-egg/retrieval clue, not evidence that DAc0da identifies the Skyrim player as ESO's Vestige.

## Underking / Hjalti encounter

Later scene dialogue recognizes a soul/power as **Hjalti Early-Beard**, blames Hjalti for the speaker's condition, invokes an old friendship and a serpent at Hjalti's feet, and ends with the words "My Mantella... My heart..." Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00496F`, `004971`, `004973`, `00497D`.

The speaker should not be identified solely from these lines. However, the following main quest, **Patchwork**, has the player explicitly say they "just faced the Underking," and the Monk responds to that identification. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00498A`.

Together with the QUST aliases `Zurin` and `ZurinTA`, the evidence strongly links this encounter to DAc0da's Underking/Zurin Arctus thread. Exact form/identity state should still be resolved from scene and NPC records before creating a final character equivalence claim.
