# Citizen Dialogue — Solitude reaction layer

**Quest:** `DAc0da.esm:000D9F` / `zDcdMq00Citizen`  
**Title:** Citizen Dialogue  
**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`

This quest contains **18 ambient reaction INFO records** around the appearance of the giant/Numidium near Solitude.

The lines are public reactions and rumors, not omniscient narration.

## Common civilian reaction

Citizens repeatedly describe:

- an enormous giant visible from Solitude;
- fear that it may begin moving;
- missing people who went to investigate it;
- reports from the court mage that it is **Dwemer-built**.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:000D8A`–`000D8F`, `000D99`–`000D9B`.

One citizen speculates whether it came from **Atmora**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:000D8A`.

That is explicitly civilian speculation, not location provenance.

## Khajiit / travel reaction

Khajiit-flavored lines say the speaker no longer cares about the civil war and intends to return to Elsweyr after seeing the giant, while another jokes about a cat growing that large. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:000D90`, `000D91`.

These are atmosphere rather than new metaphysical evidence.

## Redguard reaction

A Redguard-flavored line fears that, if the giant moves, it could head toward the speaker's home in the **Alik'r**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:000D93`.

Again, this is fear/speculation rather than prediction.

## Dunmer/Nerevarine association

A Dunmer-flavored line asks whether the object is a **Dwemer giant** and says the speaker heard that the **Nerevarine destroyed it long ago**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:000D95`.

This is valuable as a public-memory/confusion node:

- the speaker associates the new colossus with a Dwemer giant;
- the remembered destruction is attributed to the Nerevarine.

It should **not** be treated as proof that the speaker correctly identifies the visible construct as Akulakhan, Numidium, or another specific giant.

DAc0da later directly stages a Nerevarine/Akulakhan possibility-line inside Numidium, making this ambient line useful foreshadowing/retrieval context.

## Thalmor / Alinor association

Thalmor-flavored lines express panic about headquarters and ask whether the **“nightmare of Alinor”** is being replayed. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:000D96`–`000D98`.

This is strong ambient evidence that the visible Dwemer colossus evokes institutional/cultural memory of the Numidium's historical Alinor catastrophe.

The line remains a frightened Thalmor reaction rather than a complete historical account.

## Narrative function

The quest establishes that before the player receives the full Psijic explanation:

- ordinary residents already know something enormous and Dwemer-related has appeared;
- investigators have disappeared;
- different cultural observers map the event onto different inherited histories.

This is consistent with DAc0da's broader use of **memory, interpretation, and historical association** around Numidium.
