# Prisoner and Many Paths

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Status:** evidence-backed concept seed

## Prisoner

DAc0da's Monk calls the **Prisoner** a template for a Hero and, in some accounts, a vessel of possibilities able to carve out fate. He also warns that a Prisoner can be destructive rather than benevolent. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B38C`.

An Agent associated with the Warp in the West is described as another candidate for the Prisoner. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B38A`.

Ghost Choir dialogue describes a Prisoner's "quantum vibration" as becoming more pronounced after de-assignment and making an Agent difficult to sense or define. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B437`, `00B439`, `00B43B`.

The Ritemaster's theory, relayed by the Monk, is that the player's Prisoner-state quantum vibration can stabilize a time event. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B711`.

This is DAc0da's own explicit metaphysical model and should remain distinct from other Elder Scrolls or developer-text usages until compared claim by claim.

## Many Paths

A dialogue branch names a theory called **Many Paths**. It describes Aurbis as containing a web of possibilities and myriad realities. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA70`.

The same explanation says realities are mirror images/reflections of one another and describes Psijic work as studying these possibilities to avert catastrophic fates threatening Tamriel. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA70`.

This provides a direct conceptual framework for the earlier "other yous," fate lines, alternate Agents, and temporal contradictions.

## Relation to Jills

The Monk believes the Jills assigned the player a stabilizing role and use the player's actions as a base from which to correct inconsistent histories. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B711`.

This does not make the Jills allies in an ordinary sense. Ghost Choir material and the Monk both frame them as function-driven actors correcting time according to their own task. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B5AE`, `00B3B0`.
