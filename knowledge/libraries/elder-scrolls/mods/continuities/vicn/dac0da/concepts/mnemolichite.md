# Mnemolichite

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary quest:** `zDcdSqWorm` — Echoes of Mnemolichite  
**Status:** evidence-backed concept dossier with conflicting in-world models

Mnemolichite should **not** be represented as one objectively defined substance. DAc0da deliberately supplies incompatible models and then makes those incompatibilities part of the phenomenon.

## Model 1 — Dreugh mineral / crystallized memory

Yu'qbar says mnemolichite is memory dissolved in water and crystallized. It can store lost knowledge, function as a light source, and—when unstable—turn remembered content into existence in response to mortal perception. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00531E`.

The Hahd tradition describes an especially pure crystal called the **Mnemolichite Maid**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005320`.

## Model 2 — Numidian crystallization

Vanus initially calls it a hypothetical Dreugh mineral that has become real because of the **Numidian Effect**, with memories rapidly crystallizing in the local sea. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052F2`.

He observes a remembered mage materializing from it and summarizes the danger as: perception defines existence. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052F0`.

## Model 3 — perceptual process, not substance

Vanus later says that "crystallized memory" is a mistranslation of the Dreugh term.

His corrected model says mnemolichite originally describes the process by which **perceptual information becomes fixed memory**, not a tangible ore. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005546`.

He then proposes a recursive effect: surface-world misunderstanding of that term may itself have materialized the mineral and retroactively rewritten Dreugh history so that a physical ore, mining culture, relics, and institutions now appear to have always existed. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005546`.

## Model 4 — materialized idea / uncertain reconstruction

Yu'qbar attributes another model to Mankar-hul:

- mnemolichite is a materialized idea;
- it reconstructs existence from memory according to perception;
- reconstructed things are warped because memory is uncertain.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005554`.

Yu'qbar elsewhere says memories dissolve into the sea, imagination has power there, and mnemolichite functions as a template in which reproductions exist without a recoverable original. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00547D`.

## Relation to Mnemo-Li

Vanus reports an early theory that Mnemo-Li protects memories and thereby causes memory crystallization. He explicitly calls this an unpublished research-stage hypothesis. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052F2`.

Later he speculates that:

- Mnemo-Li is a Magna-Ge;
- she was artificially created;
- her divinity was manufactured after creation;
- mnemolichite may be analogous to a scab covering a wound across time/space caused by contradictory causality.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005713`.

These are Vanus's theories, not settled DAc0da narration.

## Mnemolichite Maid

Yu'qbar's tradition says the Maid is:

- a highly pure giant crystal;
- a Dawn-Era / Ehlnofey-War relic;
- recovered by Dervish, Hero of Hahd;
- found in a drowned Ehlnofey city in the southern Eltheric Ocean;
- believed by Mankar-hul to remember old Aldmeris.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005320`.

By the ending, three mutually incompatible statements coexist:

- Yu'qbar says the Maid returned to the sea;
- Athanasius says it never existed and only acted real because people believed it did;
- Vanus says it passed outside perception and therefore ceased to exist.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:006021`, `006034`, `00603B`.

All three should be preserved.

## Practical effects seen in the quest

Mnemolichite or its perceptual field can:

- instantiate remembered people;
- make imagined constraints physically effective;
- create/copy personality and memory;
- reconstruct matter imperfectly;
- produce apparently historical institutions and relics;
- interact with time breaches;
- support an attempt to rewrite the laws of Nirn retroactively.

This makes mnemolichite a central DAc0da bridge between **memory, perception, causality, Many Paths, Mnemo-Li, and Numidium**.
