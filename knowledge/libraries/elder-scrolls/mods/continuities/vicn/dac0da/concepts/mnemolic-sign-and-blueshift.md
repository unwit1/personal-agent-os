# Mnemolic Sign and blueshift

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Evidence type:** late-game MESG / gameplay-system records

DAc0da contains a late mechanical layer that turns mnemolichite into a resource/progression system. These records are especially valuable because their explanatory text states a compact ontology in the game's own UI.

## Ghost Hand

MESG `DAc0da.esm:00D13F`, `zDcdMsgSkyshardMenuSkillUp`, is titled internally around the skill-up interface and displays:

> Ghost Hand — “Registered by C0DA.”

It then says:

- **The Digitals are fingers.**
- these fingers **count, spin tales, and record**.

The menu tracks a quantity called **Mnemolic Sign**, including total and used amounts, and lets the player exchange it for:

- Health
- Stamina
- Magicka
- Carry Weight
- Perk Points

This establishes **Mnemolic Sign** as a mechanically quantifiable residue/resource associated with mnemolichite/memory recording.

## Mnemolichite absorption

MESG `DAc0da.esm:00D140`, `zDcdMsgSkyshardMenuAbsorb`, gives the heading **Mnemolichite** and states:

- **ideas take on substance through perception**;
- mnemolichite is **not a physical entity existing in space**;
- it is a **phenomenon of the mind within the flow of time**;
- **Aldmeris** once “evaporated” in accelerating time;
- it became **rain and then the sea**;
- it has now **returned here**.

The menu tracks:

- Amount Absorbed
- Mnemolic Sign
- **Blueshift Rate**

This is the most direct system-text definition in the plugin and should be retrieved alongside the deliberately conflicting in-world theories from Vanus and Yu'qbar.

## Blueshifting

MESG `DAc0da.esm:00D4C2` reports:

> Blueshifting ... [progress]

MESG `DAc0da.esm:00D4C3` awards:

> 1 Mnemolic Sign

The mechanical sequence therefore treats **blueshifting** as the process that converts/advances absorbed mnemolichite toward Mnemolic Sign.

The records do not yet define the exact metaphysical meaning of “blueshift” beyond this mechanic.

## Relation to Aldmeris

The UI text explicitly connects mnemolichite to **Aldmeris** through an evaporation -> rain -> sea -> return image.

This sits well beside:

- Yu'qbar's claim that mnemolichite is memory dissolved in water;
- Mankar-hul's alleged memories of old Aldmeris;
- Vanus's theory that perceptual/memory contradictions materialize;
- DAc0da's repeated Blue-Star / radiance imagery.

But the UI does not directly state that Aldmeris **is** Mnemo-Li, the Blue Star, or Radiance.

## Evidence boundary

Because these are gameplay-system messages, they may express DAc0da's underlying design ontology more directly than unreliable character testimony, but they still belong specifically to **Vicn's DAc0da continuity**.

Do not promote this Aldmeris/mnemolichite model into official Elder Scrolls canon.
