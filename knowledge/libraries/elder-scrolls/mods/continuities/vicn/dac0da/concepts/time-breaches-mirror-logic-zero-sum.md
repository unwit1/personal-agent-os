# Time breaches, mirror logic, and zero-sum

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Status:** evidence-backed concept seed

## Time breaches

DAc0da treats time breaches as causal contradictions associated with the Numidian effect.

The operational rule given by the Monk is that a breach can shrink when actors or outcomes that do not belong in the current period are removed so that cause and effect once again match the present. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004999`, `00499E`.

The main breach is said to lie inside Numidium. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA69`, `004992`.

The Jills are described as responsible for repairing the remaining flow of time after the player and Psijics reduce the contradictions. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA6B`, `0049A5`.

## Mirror logic

Before entering Numidium, the Monk applies a defensive ward that he describes as an application of **mirror logic from Alinor**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049B2`, `0049C4`.

He says a mirror-logic technique was used during Tiber Septim's campaign with Numidium, that historical Mirror Logicians were wiped out, and that the Psijic Order possesses a more advanced form. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049C4`, `0049C6`, `0049C8`.

These are DAc0da claims and should be compared against existing Elder Scrolls/developer-text source material rather than silently merged with it.

## Zero-sum attacks

The Monk describes a **zero-sum attack** as one of Numidium's defensive mechanisms for rejecting foreign substances. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049CB`.

He deliberately withholds a fuller explanation and claims that ignorance statistically improves survival against such an attack. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0049CD`.

Until later records clarify the mechanism, this library should distinguish:

- canonical/developer-text uses of "zero-sum";
- DAc0da's explicit Numidium defense mechanic;
- interpretation connecting the two.

No equivalence beyond the source wording is assumed here.
