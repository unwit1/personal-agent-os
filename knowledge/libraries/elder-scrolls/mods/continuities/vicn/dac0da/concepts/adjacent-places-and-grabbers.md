# Adjacent Places and Grabbers

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Status:** evidence-backed cross-Vicn concept seed

## DAc0da evidence

The Augur of the Obscure directly identifies himself as a traveler from an **Adjacent Place**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490A`.

He describes an Adjacent Place metaphorically as something like a sticky spot on the reverse side of a sheet of paper and says entities there can fight with weapons of pure mathematics. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490C`.

He warns that **Grabbers** inhabit or threaten such spaces. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490C`, `00490E`.

DAc0da independently uses the same term in the Numidium interior: Rolls-On-Roads says Numidium isolated Apocryphal contamination into an **Adjacent Place**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B50F`.

Samon also says Yngol's golden dragon arrived from an **Adjacent Place**, connecting the term to displaced old-kalpa material. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004DF9`.

## Perceptual embodiment

The Augur says his skull-like form is a local visual trick and that his intended shape is something else entirely. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00490A`.

This suggests that at least some Adjacent-Place entities may be represented through local perception rather than carrying an ordinary physical form across realities.

## Cross-Vicn bridge

UNSLAAD independently states that:

- mirrors can lead through **Lyg**, described as an adjacent place;
- fate can change there;
- worlds should not be mixed freely;
- lingering too long can attract **Grabbers**.

This is a direct rare-vocabulary bridge between DAc0da and UNSLAAD rather than a broad thematic resemblance.

The shared vocabulary should be co-retrieved, while source-specific mechanics remain separately attributable until the works are reconciled line by line.
