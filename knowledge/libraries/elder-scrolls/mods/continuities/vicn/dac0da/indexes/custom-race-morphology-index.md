## Evidence rule

A custom RACE record proves that DAc0da implements a distinct body/morphology category.

It does not by itself prove:

- biological ancestry;
- metaphysical identity;
- chronology;
- cultural membership.

For named transformations like **Haalj the Half-Drake**, combine the RACE record with character dialogue and quest-state evidence.
