# Psijic Order — DAc0da role

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary member:** Rolls-On-Roads  
**Other former member:** Vanus Galerion

This page records what DAc0da specifically claims or implements about the Psijic Order. It is not a replacement for the official Elder Scrolls Psijic dossier.

## Plugin faction record

DAc0da defines custom FACT `DAc0da.esm:000D63` / `zDcdFactionPsijic`, displayed as **Psijic Faction**.

This gives direct implementation support for Psijic actor affiliation in addition to the dialogue evidence below.

## Rolls-On-Roads' assignment

Rolls-On-Roads says the Psijic Order sent him to the **here and now** to recruit the player and prevent a catastrophe analogous to the Warp in the West. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA17`–related MQ00 dialogue.

He says the Order determined the player has the aptitude required for the Hero/Event role. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00AA79` scene / associated MQ00 dialogue.

In Censored Fate he says he was sent by the Psijic Order to survey the Solitude-lighthouse area after Numidium fragments in the Order's possession began reacting toward it. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CA3B`.

## Limits of Psijic time repair

Rolls-On-Roads says Numidium's time collapse can occur on a scale **even the Psijic Order cannot repair**. Evidence: MQ00 dialogue near `DAc0da.esm:00AA0C`.

Later, smaller contradictions are described as being within Psijic corrective capability, especially when Jills can handle the remaining correction.

## Mirror logic

A magic-effect description says DAc0da's anti-zero-sum barrier was created by the mirror logicians of Alinor and **improved by the Psijic Order**.

Rolls-On-Roads says Psijic mirror logic is more advanced than Alinor's version and will protect the party from a zero-sum attack. Evidence: MQ03 dialogue.

This is DAc0da-specific mirror-logic lore.

## Institutional overconfidence

Inside Numidium, Rolls-On-Roads explicitly says:

> We, the Psijic Order, were naive in our thinking.

He says they were overconfident that they were the only organization capable of handling the incident, then encounters evidence for organizations from other fate-lines. Evidence: MQ04 dialogue.

This is an important Many-Paths institutional implication: alternate histories may contain differently developed analogues of organizations the current Order assumes are unique.

## Jills versus Psijics

Rolls-On-Roads eventually concludes that it was the **Jills**, not the Psijic Order, who assigned the player the stabilizing role. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00B711`.

The Order studies and responds to temporal disasters, but DAc0da does not place it above the Jillian correction mechanism.

## Vanus

Vanus says Mannimarco was a fellow student from the time Vanus belonged to the Psijic Order. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:0052ED`.

He later accepts a request from Rolls-On-Roads despite usually disliking requests from Psijics. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00534F`.

## Many Paths

DAc0da's explanation of **Many Paths** says the purpose of the Psijic Order is to study possibilities and avert catastrophic fates threatening Tamriel.

This is a strong DAc0da framing of the Order's mission and should remain source-scoped.
