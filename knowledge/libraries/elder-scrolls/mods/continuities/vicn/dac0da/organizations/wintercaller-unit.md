# Wintercaller Unit

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary region:** Jylkurfyk / Atmoran route  
**Associated named actor:** Sindwen the Wintercaller

## Written order

BOOK `DAc0da.esm:0049F6`, **Orders to Wintercaller Unit**, explicitly addresses a **Wintercaller unit**.

The order states that:

- Ysgramor, Yngol, and Ylgar survived Saarthal;
- they are believed to be regrouping forces in Atmora;
- the Wintercaller unit is ordered to attack the military port of **Jylkurfyk**;
- Ysgramor and his sons are to be killed on sight;
- all Saarthal citizens are to be slain;
- the order claims this is necessary to honor a **contract with Lord Dagon**.

The order further warns that failure to honor the contract will be paid for in blood.

## Generic encounter force

DAc0da separately defines:

- **Wintercaller Soldier**
- **Wintercaller Mage**

plus Wintercaller weapons, spells, effects, and summon forms.

This makes Wintercaller a repeated organized combat designation, not only Sindwen's personal title.

## Sindwen

**Sindwen the Wintercaller** is a named commander/antagonist in Drowned Nighthawk.

She directly invokes **Lord Dagon** against Yngol and also speaks in Auri-El-centered anti-human terms. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:004C9A`, `004CA1`, `004CA3`.

The shared title, battlefield, and Dagon connection strongly associate Sindwen with the Wintercaller operation.

However, the written order does not explicitly name Sindwen as its author or recipient.

## Historical boundary

DAc0da simultaneously preserves:

- Wintercaller military aggression against Jylkurfyk;
- an Atmoran anti-elven/mixed-blood purge in Jylkurfyk;
- Haalj's accusation that Ysgramor moved an Atmoran civil war into Mereth;
- Yngol's belief that the Snow Elves initiated the conflict.

The Wintercaller Unit is therefore evidence for one armed faction inside a deliberately contested historical reconstruction, not proof that one side alone caused the war.
