# Thras and Ul'vor Kus Alliance Fleet

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary quest:** `zDcdSqSloadRadio` — Sload Radio  
**Associated commander:** Captain N'Danda

## Direct identification

Four Sload-Radio scenes explicitly identify the broadcaster as the:

**Thras and Ul'vor Kus Alliance Fleet**

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE49`, `00CE50`, `00CE57`, `00CE5E`.

## Public message

The fleet repeatedly claims:

- it is not an enemy;
- it is present to help;
- people should surrender immediately;
- surrendering people will receive safety, food, beds, medical treatment, and relief from fear/pain.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE4B`, `00CE53`, `00CE59`, `00CE60`.

The broadcasts are coercive propaganda because humanitarian language is paired directly with surrender demands.

## Named leadership / personnel

One broadcast says **Captain N'Danda** guarantees the lives and dignity of those who surrender. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE53`.

N'Danda independently appears as an operational Sload commander in **The Sea of Causality** and has a later zombified actor form.

## Medical / mental-control programs

The fleet advertises:

- **B'Korgen therapy** for injuries and “rebirth”;
- Sload mind magic to remove fear and pain;
- **K'Tora's psychotherapy**;
- becoming a **mindshaper**.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00CE5B`, `00CE60`, `00CE62`.

These terms should be retrieved beside DAc0da's Sload/Yaghra experimentation and the alternate Karnwasten Temporal Tome.

## Alliance structure

The name itself establishes cooperation between **Thras** and **Ul'vor Kus**.

A separate embedded text says new masters from Ul'vor Kus are taking roles as governors and frames that transition as a cultural/political transformation.

That material requires source/provenance handling before it is promoted as ordinary chronology, but it supports Ul'vor Kus as more than a throwaway ship name.

## Boundary

The fleet is a concrete DAc0da organization because it repeatedly self-identifies by one name and acts through coordinated broadcasts and named command personnel.

Do not generalize all Sload in DAc0da into this alliance unless a quest, actor, or document explicitly connects them.
