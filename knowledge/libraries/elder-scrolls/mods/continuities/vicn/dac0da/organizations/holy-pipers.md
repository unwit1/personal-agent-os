# Holy Pipers

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary DAc0da witness:** Vigilant Athanasius  
**Cross-Vicn status:** direct institutional bridge to VIGILANT and GLENMORIL

## DAc0da evidence

Athanasius directly identifies himself as:

> Keeper of Stendarr of the Holy Pipers branch.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00554C`.

When asked what the Holy Pipers are, he says their main task is to:

- travel the land;
- spread the teachings of Stendarr;
- provide assistance to the less fortunate.

Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00555E`.

He says his Holy-Piper group accompanied the Solitude navy into the strange sea to rescue people in distress. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00555E`.

He also says most Holy Pipers are **not fighters**, while he himself is a former Imperial Legion soldier. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005560`.

## Cross-Vicn significance

The same named organization already exists independently in the VIGILANT and GLENMORIL corpora.

Relevant persisted sources include:

- `knowledge/libraries/elder-scrolls/mods/continuities/vigilant/entities/factions/holy-pipers.md`
- `knowledge/libraries/elder-scrolls/mods/continuities/vicn/glenmoril/quests/the-pipers.md`
- `knowledge/libraries/elder-scrolls/mods/continuities/vicn/glenmoril/relationships/holy-pipers-perception-thread.md`

That difference should be preserved rather than flattened into a single moral/organizational state.
