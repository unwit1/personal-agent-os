# Republic of Hahd

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary witness:** Yu'qbar  
**Status:** evidence-backed but ontologically unstable in-world institution

## Location

Yu'qbar describes the Republic of Hahd as an underwater state near the **Dellese Isles of High Rock**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005316`.

Vanus initially believes Hahd is an imaginary undersea state invented as a joke by Firsthold Orrery Keepers, but changes his position after meeting Yu'qbar. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00535A`.

That uncertainty matters because the same quest later suggests mnemolichite can retroactively create histories and institutions.

## People and institutions

Directly named Hahd-associated entities include:

- **Yu'qbar** — Templar of Hahd
- **Mankar-hul** — leader/religious authority
- **Dervish** — Hero of Hahd
- **Maztiak / Arkayn** — jailer king / divine figure
- **Emirates of Nahd** — destination for Mnemolichite Maid research
- **Templars of Hahd**

Yu'qbar says Hahd's forces attempted to recover the Mnemolichite Maid after its theft by the Sload. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:00531C`.

## Mnemolichite ontology problem

Hahd possesses an internally coherent history around mnemolichite, including ancient relics, scholars, scriptures, military recovery missions, and neighboring states.

Vanus later proposes that a surface-world mistranslation/misunderstanding of the original Dreugh idea may have caused physical mnemolichite to materialize and **retroactively altered Dreugh history and society**. Evidence: `DAc0da.esm:005546`.

That means DAc0da leaves open whether:

1. Hahd existed independently and always possessed mnemolichite traditions;
2. Hahd existed but its history was rewritten around mnemolichite;
3. some portion of Hahd's apparent history/institutions is itself a mnemolichite construction.

The library should preserve the institution as directly experienced while marking its historical provenance as unstable.
