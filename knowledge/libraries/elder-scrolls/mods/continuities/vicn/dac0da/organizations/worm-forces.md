# Worm / Black-Worm forces

**Continuity:** `tes.mod.vicn.dac0da`  
**Primary arc:** Echoes of Mnemolichite / The Revenant  
**Central figure:** Mannimarco

## Evidence type

DAc0da does not define a custom FACT record for this organization.

Instead, the organized Worm force is visible through repeated actor roles, equipment nomenclature, quest structure, and named subordinates.

## Repeated combat roles

DAc0da defines:

- Worm Spellsword
- Worm Knight
- Worm Assassin
- Worm Berserker
- Worm's Sacrifice
- Worm's Midwife

Named Worm Anchorites include:

- **Bolor Savel the Worm Anchorite** — `DAc0da.esm:0043CE`
- **Mercator Hosidus the Worm Anchorite** — `DAc0da.esm:0043D0`
- **Camilla Lollia the Worm Anchorite** — `DAc0da.esm:0043D1`

Dedicated equipment also uses:

- **Black Worm Shield**
- **Black Worm Sword**
- **Black Worm Greatsword**
- **Worm Anchorite Armor**
- **Worm Anchorite Sword / Greatsword**

This is sufficient to model a coordinated Worm/Black-Worm military-necromantic force.

## Mannimarco hierarchy

The arc culminates in forms explicitly named:

- **Mannimarco the God of Worms**
- **Necromancer's Moon**

The Revenant quest also unites Worm forces with Sload operations and the mnemolichite/time-breach ritual.

## Sload relationship

The Sload and Worm forces cooperate during Echoes of Mnemolichite:

- Sload research/capture infrastructure;
- necromancer armies;
- transport/use of Mannimarco's body;
- ritual intended to manifest the God of Worms.

The evidence supports an operational alliance in this event.

It does not automatically prove permanent political unity between every Sload group and every Mannimarco-aligned necromancer.

## Naming boundary

Because DAc0da itself most visibly exposes “Worm,” “Black Worm,” and “Worm Anchorite” through actor/item labels rather than a formal custom faction record, this page deliberately uses **Worm / Black-Worm forces** rather than asserting a new DAc0da-specific official organization name.
