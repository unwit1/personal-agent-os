# Recovered Act 4 side-quest dialogue — direct VIGILANT 1.8.2 source

This file preserves direct response text for the nine side quests previously marked missing or partial.

## More Skooma — QUST 021273E0

**Player: What are you doing here?**

Ja'zhan:
> Ja'zhan is thinking about how to get out of here. But the sewer is full of leeches and the big guard is watching the prison entrance.
>
> Well, Ja'zhan will not be able to get out any time soon. And there are only a few bottles of skooma left. Ja'zhan's end is near.

Later:
> Thanks to you, Ja'zhan was finally able to get some fresh air again. In thanks, Ja'zhan will bargain with you, for cheap.

## The Black Worm — QUST 02129061

Later:

**Player: Not until you tell me who you are.**

## Funeral — QUST 02129BCA

Martha:
> I'm looking for my family's graves. But I can't find them...

> Would you really? The names on them are Johan, Simon, and Tlass. Please tell me if you find them.

> I'm sorry, but no. However, I'm certain they are not in this area. I've been searching here for years...

When the graves are found:
> Oh, thank you so much. I can finally meet my family again.

Later:
> Thanks to you I was able to see my family again. I can't thank you enough for all you've done.

> I think I'll stay here for a while. I just want to indulge in the nostalgia for a while longer.

Hello:
> I wish you a safe journey. And please, don't push yourself too much.

## Knight of Julianos — QUST 021265FB

Sir Henrik:
> Oh, are you freeing me? I don't even know what happened that I ended up trapped here. What rotten luck...

> Oh, it's you again. What a coincidence.

> Oh, well met again. Julianos himself must be guiding your steps.

> Oh, you again. I knew you'd come here.

> See you at the Mathmalatu Priory. You can always count on me.

On his imprisonment:
> I don't quite remember. There was something about a slave trader and cheap booze...

> I only wanted some alcohol from the kitchen. But that stingy cook... I thought one would be more relaxed about this after death...

On the slave trader:
> When I woke up again, he was no longer there. What a bastard, to lock me up in this hole...

On his friends:
> I will look for my friends. They must be in trouble, too.

> Well, we were supposed to meet at the Mathmalatu Priory. But who wants to listen to that grumpy old man there?

> I found traces of two knights. One of them was apparently limping... Something must have happened to him.
>
> I'm worried about them, but I hurt my back when I got here. Could you check upstairs for me?

On Varla:
> His name is Varla. A child of an Ayleid and a human. He used to be a lord of the eastern part of the Alessian Empire when he was alive.
>
> They say he was killed when his subjects rebelled against him because of his brutality. I don't think much of peasant revolts, but it was justified in this case.

On the prison tower:
> It's an ancient prison, from the Ayleid dynasty. Even in the time of the Alessian and Reman Empire, this place was full of sorrow.

When told two knights were released:
> ...I see. It was probably a good thing to do so, but it's still sad. Please, leave me alone for a bit.

## Knight of Zenithar — QUST 021306FA

Sir Ralvas:
> Hey, you there, have you seen my head anywhere? I'd like to know what's going on around me.

> Huh, where is my head...

> I can't feel my head. Why can't I feel my head?

On where it was lost:
> It was already gone when I noticed it was missing. I probably lost it somewhere when I was separated from my friends and stumbled through the darkness without any light...

> Ah, yes! I remember I walked into a cat along the way. My body got strangely light at that moment. I must have lost it then!

On speaking without a head:
> I project my voice through my stomach instead of the throat. Like all of us Dunmer.

Ja'zhan:
> There is a strange helmet. Ja'zhan saw it shining like gold in the sewer and sto... picked it up. Yes, he just picked it up.
>
> But it is just cheap copper, badly decorated and there is no one to buy it. For 100 gold it is yours.

When the head is returned:
> Oh, finally! I don't know who you are, but thank you. I can finally go back to the priory.

After attaching it backward:
> But you really are a strange one... Why are you standing behind me?

Final:
> Well, I got separated from my friends. I think I'll return to the Mathmalatu Priory for now.
>
> If you ever need my help, find me there. I won't lend you any gold, but I will gladly lend you my strength.

## Kyne's Dragon — QUST 0221ED0C

Kahkaankrein:
> You... mortal child... you are not like the others here...

On speech:
> Yes. After losing my wings, the people here tried to tame me.
>
> I forgot the language I was born with. Now I can barely remember my own name...

On imprisonment:
> A blood curse took my wings. It was... it was a very long fall. I remember nothing since falling into the black sea.
>
> The last thing I saw... the Owl, horrible grin of the Owl...

On the Alessians:
> They were trying to take my blood. But, for better or worse, that curse made it impossible.

On the other dragons:
> There were. We all served Kyne. But they are all gone now...
>
> They could not bear the pain of the blood curse. They were deceived by the Owl and turned to stone...

On Kyne:
> Yes. We abandoned lord Alduin and chose to live with Kyne and the mortals.
>
> It was our mission to protect Kyne's garden, but we failed...

On Laza:
> They were mortal children who served Kyne with us. But they were not allowed to enter the forest nor meet Kyne.
>
> I often saw them herding sheep outside the garden. I wonder what became of them...

On Molag Bal:
> Loathsome beast of Sithis... He stole Kyne's animals and twisted, corrupted their forms.
>
> We killed him again and again but he always returned the very next day as if nothing had happened. A terrible monster...

On the white flower:
> Ah, it smells so nice. It reminds me of Kyne's garden. I remember crossing the vastness of the sky with my friends...
>
> Thank you, mortal child... Kyne yet lives. This is good. My friends will find comfort in this...

On the Feather of Kyne:
> Oh, this feather... Is it not Kyne's feather? And still pure... A miracle, in this wasteland.
>
> I beg you... would you give me this feather, please? With its help, I could return to Kyne...

If given:
> I do not know how to thank you. There is such beauty in you mortals... My former lord Alduin never understood this.
>
> We have failed, but our way was not wrong. Kyne still lives in the hearts of the people.

Departure:
> Ah, I hear the song of the wind... My friends are calling me... The song of Kyne... I longed for this...

## Knight of Kynareth — QUST 02334C8F

Sir Juncan:

On another Vigilant:
> Vigilants... Weaklings like you? One such tried his luck with the flying Worm a while ago.
>
> I heard some screaming, and then nothing. If you don't want to meet the same end, you should learn your place in this world.

On the flying Worm:
> A flying Daedroth. How can he even fly with such an unshapely body?

On Melus:
> Avoid him. What kind of person spends all their time talking to a corpse?
>
> But even for madmen like him, there is something like inner peace. You should not disturb him.

Later:
> I'm guarding sir Gregory on his artistic pilgrimage. He's quite a stubborn fellow.
>
> From here on you'll have to be prepared for anything. Remember, death is the end for you.

On Varla:
> I heard rumors he's insane. A half-breed of a human and an elf. He hunted elves at first and then finally humans.
>
> He was neither one or the other because of his blood. That's why he went mad. It's a pity, really.

On the barrier tower:
> There is a barrier tower to the east, but don't go there. The smell of rotten blood is coming from there.

On the slums:
> It should be called the "undead den". That priest, Pepe, says they lead to the prison tower and then to the Imperial City.
>
> I don't trust that priest. Maybe he's not lying, but he is hiding something.

On Sir Gregory:
> He's not bad with the sword. However, unlike me who can only fight, he has a sense of art and taste.
>
> He's a funny one, but a good fellow. Though sometimes he's too stubborn for his own good.
