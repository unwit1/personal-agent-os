# Report: Sacred Anatomancer — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 024E7F91` — **Report: Sacred Anatomancer**
- report author: **Thorondir**

Exact text:
- `books/024E7F91-report-sacred-anatomancer.txt`

## Reported origin

Thorondir says the Sacred Anatomancer:
- was once a fisher near Lake Ilinalta;
- killed his own family after losing his mind;
- fled to Riften and disappeared into the Ratway;
- reappeared in winter 4E 198.

These are report claims, not omniscient narration.

## Holy Pipers

The report says the Anatomancer:
- swayed Vigilants from across the world;
- formed a splinter group called the **Society of the Holy Pipers**;
- organized kidnappings and torture;
- harvested organs and entrails;
- produced works compared to Ayleid flesh art.

This directly establishes the VIGILANT implementation of the Holy Pipers as an Anatomancer-led splinter movement.

## Multiplicity problem

Thorondir reports that dozens of apparently identical Anatomancers have already been defeated and keep reappearing.

He infers:
- the Anatomancer may split himself into copies;
- a Daedric contract is likely;
- the responsible Prince is unknown.

Important evidence split:

Direct/observational:
- repeated Anatomancer instances;
- repeated defeat/reappearance.

Thorondir's inference:
- copying/splitting mechanism;
- Daedric contract;
- unknown Prince as source of power.

## Current prisoner

Thorondir says:
- the present Anatomancer is imprisoned;
- no one should approach him;
- he need not be fed;
- he has no organs remaining;
- despite being hollow, he still lives.

This is direct report testimony about the prisoner.
