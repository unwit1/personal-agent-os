# Bruiant Mansion / Child of Oblivion — document reconstruction

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

This reconstruction prioritizes dated documents, quest objectives, and structurally attributed dialogue. It does not assume that every supernatural appearance is a literal continuation of the corresponding dead person.

## Before the haunting: 4E 199

Marcus Bruiant's journal records an affair during repeated travel to Markarth. He describes paying a Khajiit slaver 10,000 gold to obtain the woman he was seeing, then throwing her out when she became pregnant. When she appealed to the Jarl, Marcus paid her support and a large civic donation rather than publicly contest the matter.

This is first-person evidence of Marcus's infidelity, exploitation, and concern with reputation. The ESM material analyzed so far does **not** establish that this unnamed woman is directly responsible for the later mansion curse.

## Julia and Julius disappear: 4E 200

Marcus records that his wife **Julia** and son **Julius** disappear on 9 Sun's Height, 4E 200. Julius is found in the forest ten days later, terrified and unable or unwilling to speak. Julia remains missing.

A Vigilant named **Jacob** later arrives with Julia's ring and reports that her body was found in Lake Ilinalta. The separate `Ilinalta Report 4E 200` corroborates that identification and describes a corpse still burning internally, with acidic/poisonous blood. The report says the curse was unusually easy to dispel and that the remains were purified and scattered.

Julia's surviving letter simply tells Marcus she is leaving him and taking Julius. Her journal is no longer readable: every page has been overwritten with repeated "Die" text.

## Escalating deaths and the arrival of Bal

After Julia's disappearance/death, servants begin dying in apparently accidental or self-destructive ways. Marcus also begins hearing a crawling sound in the halls and later experiences recurring nightmares involving Julia, Julius, and a burning red Stone.

On 28 Frostfall, 4E 200, Marcus hires a new Wild Elf butler named **Bal**. Julius becomes unusually attached to him. Independent servant notes report that Bal tells Julius stories involving black magic and buried myths, while Julius is seen muttering strange incantations and leaving an unnaturally glowing black doll in the pond.

The quest does not structurally identify the butler Bal with the separate `zzzCOMolagBal` NPC form. Lorekeeper therefore keeps **Bal (the butler)** and **Molag Bal (the Daedric Prince manifestation)** as linked but distinct entities unless later script/scene evidence explicitly merges them.

## Bartholo's investigation: 4E 201

Marcus seeks help from the Temple of Stendarr and Vigilant **Bartholo** is assigned to the mansion. For roughly a month, the incidents stop. Julius avoids Bartholo.

The deaths then resume: Lamthor is killed by frenzied dogs; Patricia hangs herself; Summil attacks Marcus and is later eaten by skeevers. Bartholo can suppress the frenzy spell on the dogs but becomes increasingly exhausted and withdrawn. By 6 First Seed he will not leave his room, while Julius—who had previously avoided him—plays outside it.

Gwyneth's later briefing states that the Temple eventually receives Bartholo's request for reinforcements only after a months-long delay caused by the civil war. By the time the player is sent, Gwyneth fears it is already too late.

## Player investigation / Child of Oblivion

`QUST 02065932` / **Child of Oblivion** orders the player to speak to Gwyneth, travel to Bruiant Mansion, investigate and solve the incident, defeat Julius, escape the mansion, and ultimately choose between dying in the flames as a martyr or accepting Molag Bal's corruption in exchange for help.

The companion guide quest `zzzCOGuide` names five **Blood Curses**—Shivers, Corruption, Froth, Chains, and Jealousy—and concludes with an objective to **Free Julius**. This wording is retained alongside the main quest's **Defeat Julius** rather than being normalized away.

## Julius

Marcus's final note says Julius is "no longer human" and calls him a **Child of Oblivion**, blaming himself. The plugin contains multiple Julius forms, including `zzzCOJuliusChildOblivion`, a later `zzzCODregsOfSithisJulius`, and other encounter/representation forms.

Molag Bal's structurally attributed end-of-quest dialogue says the flames consuming the mansion are driven by **Julius's hatred**, which he describes as stronger even than Lamae's. This is Molag Bal's testimony, but the burning escape sequence is also directly reflected in the quest objective.

## Bal and Molag Bal

The butler Bal greets the player, claims to be merely a shadow/remnant, leads the player toward Bartholo, and becomes hostile. Orlando separately calls Bal "tainted" and offers an alternate Spinner's Needle route that bypasses the mansion by stabbing him before entry.

Near the end of the normal route, a separate Molag Bal form offers salvation from Julius's flames in exchange for corruption. His speech repeats VIGILANT's larger imagery of **bread, hunger, alms, salvation, and domination**, calling himself bread for the starving and contrasting his intervention with the supposed weakness of the Aedra.

## Successor aftermath

The later `zzzCOSubQ01` / **Successor** quest returns attention to the mansion. Gwyneth reports that the Temple Keeper went there and did not return. The quest includes a Julius form speaking "Curse me, bless me" and `Dregs of Sithis` dialogue invoking sleep, dream, Kyne, and the end of the dream. The exact temporal relation and Keeper identity require scene/stage reconstruction.
