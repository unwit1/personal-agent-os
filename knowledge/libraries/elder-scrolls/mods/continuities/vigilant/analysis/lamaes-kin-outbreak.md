## Curse model stated by the book

The document distinguishes ordinary vampirism from **Lamae's Kin**.

It claims ordinary vampirism is a curable disease with magical elements, while Lamae's Kin is:
- a painful bodily curse;
- bloodborne;
- observed to transmit orally / through drinking blood rather than through wounds;
- associated with dreams or visions of a woman named Lamae;
- associated with growing emotional instability;
- eventually associated with insanity and overt vampirism;
- without an established cure in the document's medical framework.

The document says the Mages Guild recommendation is immediate incineration of infected people.

These are in-universe medical claims, not automatically objective metaphysical law.

## 2E 582 Lake Ilinalta case

The text cites records of **priest Ainam** for the last confirmed outbreak.

Date and place:
- 7 Frostfall, 2E 582;
- a small fishing village near Lake Ilinalta.

Reported sequence:
1. villagers are found killed;
2. a local boy of about ten is identified as the immediate vampire perpetrator;
3. the boy is found amid the remains, covered in pale tumors;
4. he remains partly conscious and names family members;
5. Mages Guild responders later find him fully violent;
6. they incinerate him;
7. the ashes are placed under Arcane University control;
8. the ashes are later allegedly lost during turmoil involving a winged Daedroth;
9. small fires around Lake Ilinalta continue for months.

## Why the outbreak matters

The case gives VIGILANT a historical bridge among:
- Lamae's curse;
- Skyrim/Lake Ilinalta;
- Mages Guild containment;
- recurring fire imagery;
- preserved/lost remains;
- later Blood Matron events.

It also establishes that the curse can appear generations/centuries apart from Lamae's own direct bodily presence.

## Relationship to Facis

Facis's direct dialogue says:
- Lamae was like a mother to him;
- Lamae's kin are linked to her through hearts and minds;
- they knew her suffering;
- they wanted her release.

That subjective/relational description complements the medical book's statement that infected people dream of Lamae and become psychologically affected.

Together they support a model in which **Lamae's Kin is not treated by VIGILANT as ordinary vampirism alone**.

## Source cautions

Do not automatically conclude:
- every vampire who dreams of Lamae is the same biological lineage;
- the unnamed 2E 582 boy survived incineration;
- the lost ashes directly caused later Lake Ilinalta events;
- the winged Daedroth is a specific named character;
- every later fire at Lake Ilinalta was supernatural.

The book reports or implies these elements but does not mechanically prove every causal link.
