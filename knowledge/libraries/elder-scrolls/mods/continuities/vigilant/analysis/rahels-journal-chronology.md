# Rahel's Journal — chronology and reliability map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 0201C7F7` — **Rahel's Journal**

Exact text:
- `books/0201C7F7-rahels-journal.txt`

## 12 Second Seed, 4E 180

Rahel says Jacob has been gone for two days after going beneath Windhelm to hunt vampires.

This directly overlaps the Windhelm Maiden Statue crisis and confirms:
- Rahel and Jacob were together before the expedition;
- Jacob was already a Vigilant;
- Rahel expected him to return.

The journal does not describe the expedition itself.

## 8–10 Frostfall, 4E 182

After a gap of more than two years, Rahel wakes in the ruins of their house.

She concludes Jacob has betrayed her.

Two days later an **unnamed follower of Molag Bal**:
- gives her a mace;
- causes power and magical knowledge to surge into her mind when she touches it;
- orders her to spread the **seeds of discord**.

Direct evidence:
- Rahel receives the mace;
- the visitor is described as a follower of Molag Bal;
- Rahel understands her new mission as spreading discord.

Unresolved:
- what happened to Rahel during the missing two years;
- whether she knew Jacob had bargained away her soul;
- whether the mace itself contains the knowledge or merely triggers it;
- identity of the follower.

## 23 Midyear, 4E 191

Rahel says she gives a lycanthropy-causing potion to a little girl whose father is ill.

She later hears screaming and admits that she still feels guilt.

This is important because the journal presents her as both:
- an active perpetrator;
- psychologically conflicted rather than emotionally empty.

## 11 Hearthfire, 4E 200

Rahel meets a grieving man who lost his daughter.

She teaches him a Daedra-summoning ritual after telling him it may let him contact the dead girl.

Rahel reflects on how hopeful her victims look before their situations collapse.

The document does not name the man or daughter.

Do not map them onto another family tragedy without independent evidence.

## Undated final entries

The handwriting becomes hasty and dates disappear.

Rahel reports:
- someone stole her mace;
- she wants revenge on the thief;
- summoning **Vernaccus** was a mistake;
- Vernaccus used a blast spell inside a small shack;
- guards and Vigilants are likely to notice;
- she abandons Vernaccus to delay pursuit.

The source does not identify:
- the thief;
- the stolen mace's later holder;
- the exact shack/city;
- whether Vernaccus survives.

## Chronology model

The journal supports this sequence:

**4E 180 worried spouse → unexplained two-year gap → 4E 182 abandoned/betrayed Rahel → Molag Bal follower and mace → decades of deliberate discord work → 4E 200 late corruptive operation → undated flight**

This chronology should be kept separate from the later **Bal-Rahel / Rahel-BalGhost** ontology, which comes from scene structure and supplementary evidence rather than the journal alone.

## Reliability boundary

High confidence:
- dates and acts Rahel records;
- her guilt;
- the Molag Bal follower and mace;
- lycanthropy incident;
- Daedra-summoning deception;
- Vernaccus summoning.

Unresolved:
- missing 4E 180–182 period;
- direct mechanics of Jacob's bargain;
- identity of the follower;
- later Bal-Rahel transformation mechanism.

## Retrieval edges

- **Rahel → waits for → Jacob**
- **Rahel → receives → Daedric mace**
- **Molag Bal follower → orders → spread discord**
- **Rahel → causes → lycanthropy incident**
