# Fervidius Tharn as a canon anchor in VIGILANT

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source: user-provided `Vigilant.esm`

## Summary

VIGILANT directly embeds two texts attributed to **Fervidius Tharn**, the established Alessian/Marukhati Arch-Prelate:

- `BOOK 02054ED3` — *On the Detachment of the Sheath from the Integument*
- `BOOK 024A8AF7` — *Vindication for the Dragon Break*

These are not best treated as VIGILANT-original lore books. They are copies of established Elder Scrolls/ESO material, preserved inside the mod and redistributed through VIGILANT's Alessian/Coldharbour spaces.

The important VIGILANT evidence is therefore primarily **placement and juxtaposition**, not authorship of the underlying prose.

## Exact ESM copies

The exact decoded VIGILANT versions are preserved at:

- `books/02054ED3-on-the-detachment-of-the-sheath-from-the-integument.txt`
- `books/024A8AF7-vindication-for-the-dragon-break.txt`

The VIGILANT copies closely track the established ESO texts, although small transcription/version differences can exist. For example, the VIGILANT copy of *Vindication* uses **“inconclusively proven”**, while some later external transcriptions use **“conclusively proven.”**

Lorekeeper should therefore keep:
- **text origin:** official/ESO Fervidius material;
- **specific VIGILANT transcription:** exact ESM copy;
- **VIGILANT placement/context:** mod-specific evidence.

## On the Detachment of the Sheath

The text is attributed in the ESM to **Arch-Prelate Fervidius Tharn**.

Its core claims concern:
- detaching a “sheath” from the Aurbic integument;
- invocation of **Mnemoli**;
- a **Hurling Disk**;
- a circle turned sidewise becoming a **Tower**;
- twisting a sheath into the **middle dawn**;
- reaching **untime and unplace**.

Within established Elder Scrolls lore, this text is one of the strongest conceptual sources connecting Marukhati metaphysics to the conditions associated with the Middle Dawn.

VIGILANT does not need to invent that conceptual vocabulary; it imports it directly.

### VIGILANT placements

The book-placement index records copies in:

- **Narfin's Inquisition Court**
- **Mathmalatu Priory**
- **Bruiant Mansion North Wing**
- an unresolved/unnamed cell `0251D5D5`
- `CHCourtBridged02`

These placements move the canonical Marukhati text into VIGILANT's own inquisition, priory, and historical-memory environments.

## Vindication for the Dragon Break

The ESM attributes this text to:

**Fervidius Tharn, Arch-Prelate of the Maruhkati Selective**

Its core program includes:
- Akatosh as a unitary spirit;
- removal of the **Aldmeri Taint**;
- restoration of Ak-at-Osh to “humanadic purity”;
- preparation of the **Staff of Towers**;
- chants of Proper-Life;
- ritual Dance;
- winding the Roll of Time backwards.

This is established Elder Scrolls material concerning the Marukhati Selective's Dragon Break project.

### VIGILANT placements

The book-placement index records copies in:

- **Narfin's Inquisition Court**
- **Mathmalatu Priory**
- **Baptist's House**

Again, the prose originates outside VIGILANT, but the mod deliberately places it in locations tied to Alessian religious authority and Coldharbour memory.

## Relationship to Amicus Tharn

VIGILANT also introduces **Amicus Tharn**, an Alessian/Marukhati ideologue whose four *Petition to House Tharn* volumes discuss:
- rewriting Belharza;
- reshaping divine identity through belief;
- separating Auriel from Akatosh;
- a human-only heaven;
- destroying unwanted histories;
- rejecting Prisoner freedom;
- the Stone of Fire.

Because VIGILANT embeds Fervidius Tharn's established Marukhati writings in the same continuity, Amicus's Tharn surname and ideology are best read as a **deliberate insertion into the older Tharn/Marukhati tradition**.

What remains unsupported is any exact genealogy between Amicus and Fervidius.

## Canon bridge

Fervidius functions as a useful **canon bridge** between licensed TES history and VIGILANT's invented material.

### Established substrate

The following concepts are imported from licensed Elder Scrolls material:
- Fervidius Tharn;
- the Marukhati Selective;
- anti-Aldmeri purification of Akatosh;
- the Staff of Towers;
- the ritual Dance;
- Middle Dawn / Dragon Break metaphysics;
- Mnemoli and untime/unplace language.

### VIGILANT extension

VIGILANT then builds around that substrate:
- Pelan/Pepe's Stone corruption;
- Amicus Tharn;
- the *Petition to House Tharn* sequence;
- Marukh's corpse/Stone imagery;
- Belharza's manipulated historical identity;
- Coldharbour reconstructions of Alessian history.

These extensions should remain explicitly tagged `tes.mod.vigilant`.

## Reliability / provenance

- **Official-source text:** the underlying Fervidius writings.
- **A-level VIGILANT structural evidence:** the fact that those books are embedded in the ESM and where VIGILANT places them.
- **Interpretive bridge:** reading Amicus/Pelan material as consciously extending the same Marukhati metaphysical tradition.
- **Unresolved:** any literal family relationship between Amicus and Fervidius.

## Current conclusion

VIGILANT uses Fervidius Tharn as a **canonical historical anchor**.

Rather than rewriting his established texts, the mod places them beside its own Alessian material so that Amicus, Pelan, Marukh, Belharza, and the Stone feel like hidden or apocryphal layers beneath the later Marukhati history already known from TES lore.

That makes Fervidius one of the clearest places where VIGILANT's continuity is designed to interlock with licensed Elder Scrolls history without replacing it.
