# Letter from Taranis — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 02013BE9` — **Letter from Taranis**

Exact text:
- `books/02013BE9-letter-from-taranis.txt`

## Direct statement

Taranis writes to **Carene** that:
- he met an unnamed female summoner;
- she says she can free him from the **sword's curse**;
- she demands one job in exchange;
- he believes this may let him live with Carene and their daughter again.

## What it establishes

This is the clearest direct evidence that:
- Taranis is afflicted by a curse associated with a sword;
- he seeks release from that curse;
- an unnamed summoner offers a bargain;
- the bargain requires service;
- his goal is reunion with Carene and Lilian.

## Identity boundaries

The letter does **not** identify:
- the cursed sword;
- the summoner;
- the job;
- the exact magical mechanism of the curse;
- whether the summoner is trustworthy;
- whether the bargain actually frees him.

Do not map the summoner onto a known witch or Daedric figure without direct evidence.

## Relationship to Carene's warning

Carene's separate letter warns Taranis about someone claiming to be able to lift the curse and asks him to be cautious.

Together, the documents establish:
- Taranis sees the offer as hopeful;
- Carene sees it as dangerous.

This contrast is more reliable than assigning motives to the unnamed summoner.

## Later consequence

Carene later says in *Art of Mercy* that Taranis was killed.

The letter does not explain:
- who killed him;
- whether the bargain caused his death;
- whether he completed the requested job.

Keep those later consequences source-separated.
