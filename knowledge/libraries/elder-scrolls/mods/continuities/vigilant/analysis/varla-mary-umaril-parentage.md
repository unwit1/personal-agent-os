# Varla – Mary – Umaril parentage reconstruction

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

This analysis compares:
- Varla's ESM-backed memory/Coldharbour identity;
- Mary's ESM-backed memory forms;
- the *Pelinal the Bloody* memory;
- a123999 supplementary commentary.

## Direct ESM evidence: Varla

Varla's VIGILANT implementation establishes:
- `NPC_ 020E6A48` / Varla the Man-Hunter;
- `QUST 024F69C2` / VS Varla;
- direct linkage to `QUST 0213965A` / **Knight of Hounds**.

In *Knight of Hounds*:
- Emperor Belharza addresses Varla as an **Ayleid orphan**;
- Belharza says he raised Varla;
- he treats Varla as a son despite **no blood relationship**;
- their break occurs when Varla refuses indiscriminate killing of Ayleids.

This proves foster/adoptive Belharza–Varla family language inside the memory.

It does not state Varla's biological parents.

## Direct ESM evidence: Mary and Umaril memory context

`QUST 022A532E` / **Pelinal the Bloody** contains:
- a separate Mary NPC, `NPC_ 022A0679` / `zzzCHSlaveMary`;
- Mary placed in Burosel;
- a memory narrative centered on Pelinal, the aftermath of **Umaril**, and a **pregnant captive**.

The currently persisted memory analysis treats the scene as a manipulated/unstable memory rather than neutral historical footage.

The direct ESM layer does **not** yet contain a line:
- “Mary is pregnant by Umaril,”
- “Varla is Mary's son,”
- or “Varla is Umaril's son.”

But it creates a strong compatible structure for such a parentage claim.

## Supplementary resolution

The a123999 VIGILANT background article explicitly says:
- **Mary is Varla's mother**;
- **Umaril is Varla's father**;
- Mary had been Umaril's slave/material for his artwork;
- Varla later served in eastern Cyrodiil under the Alessian Empire;
- learning the truth of his birth contributed to his later collapse/madness.

Source:
- `sources/supplementary/a123999-vigilant-backgrounds.md`

This gives a coherent intended-background explanation for two otherwise separate ESM clusters:

1. **Pelinal the Bloody**
   - Umaril aftermath
   - enslaved/vulnerable Mary
   - pregnant-captive motif

2. **Knight of Hounds / Varla**
   - Ayleid orphan
   - raised by Belharza
   - no biological relationship to Belharza

The fit is strong, but the biological parentage remains **supplementary commentary**, not a direct line currently persisted from the ESM.

## Mary identity layer

The Mary in *Pelinal the Bloody* is structurally assigned to:

VIGILANT's broader Mary/Moura dossier also links:
- Moura = Mary;

The exact relation among these bodies is already modeled as a multi-manifestation identity cluster.

Therefore the parentage claim should attach to **Mary's historical/memory identity layer**, not automatically to the Shoggoth Mother boss body.

## Varla's Ayleid identity

Varla's status as an Ayleid orphan is especially compatible with Umaril parentage because Umaril is represented through Ayleid/Auroran history.

But compatibility is not proof.

The current evidence classification is:

- **Varla is an Ayleid orphan raised by Belharza:** high-confidence memory testimony.
- **Mary is the pregnant/vulnerable woman in Pelinal the Bloody:** Mary is structurally present; exact pregnancy relation requires scene-text recovery.
- **Umaril is Varla's father and Mary his mother:** supplementary a123999 resolution.
- **Belharza is Varla's biological father:** contradicted by Belharza's own no-blood-relation framing.

## Later Varla breakdown

Ritho remembers Varla as originally brave/honorable and later becoming fearful and hunting his own people.

Sir Henrik says Varla eventually ruled eastern territory and died after revolt.

The supplementary source says learning his birth contributed to his madness.

This is compatible with the ESM testimony but not directly established by those speakers.

Store:
- **ESM:** behavioral change and later Man-Hunter reign.
- **supplementary:** revelation of Umaril/Mary parentage as a causal trigger.
