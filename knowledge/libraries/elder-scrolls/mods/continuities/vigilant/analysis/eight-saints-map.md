# The Eight Saints of Cyrod — mapping pass 1

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`  
Primary source: `BOOK 020CB0DF` / **The Eight Saints of Cyrod**

This page is being built in small passes. This first pass covers only **Sard** and **Moura/Mary**.

## Sard

The book describes Sard as a woman of eternal youth with one red and one blue eye, formerly a handmaiden of Alessia. It says she later served Belharza and then Marukh, remained young because of Alessia's blessing, and rapidly aged to ash after Marukh's death, leaving only jewel-like eyes.

Current classification: **VIGILANT-specific saint biography**.

The names Alessia, Belharza, and Marukh are licensed Elder Scrolls figures, but this exact Sard biography is not being promoted into base canon. It should be treated as an internal VIGILANT historical tradition unless separately corroborated.

Follow-up targets:
- identify every Sard NPC/BOOK/quest form in the ESM;
- locate Sard's Charnel and connect placed artifacts/notes;
- determine whether the red/blue eye imagery recurs in dialogue or items.

## Moura / Mary

The book explicitly says Moura's true name was **Mary** and describes her as a healer from High Rock who purified Lake Ilinalta of vampiric contamination. It says she was later executed after conflict with the Alessian Order during the Thrassian plague and that her name was erased from later Imperial records.

This maps strongly onto already extracted VIGILANT dialogue:

- Inquisitor Pepe remembers **burning Mary** and later questions whether that was where everything went wrong.
- *A Vision in Malada* stanza 5 describes a Mara-associated woman, an inquisitor, gathered firewood, and execution by flames.
- *Judgement* describes the Alessian execution of a Mara follower whose healing miracle lacked Order approval.

Current classification: **high-confidence VIGILANT identity cluster: Moura = Mary**, with the saint text, Pepe testimony, prophecy, and Judgement all referring to the same core persecution episode unless later scene evidence contradicts it.

Reliability remains layered:
- Mary/Moura's identity is textually explicit in *The Eight Saints of Cyrod*.
- Pepe's role in her burning is his own testimony.
- the theological meaning of the event is contested within VIGILANT's own narrative.


## Jhunal

The saint text presents Jhunal as an Atmoran mage who joined Marukh, spread rune knowledge through eastern Cyrodiil, created **Marukh's Torch**, refused to surrender it to the Order, and was exiled. It further claims his disciple Cosmas later used the art at Malada and Pailune and that later records credited Cosmas rather than Jhunal.

The ESM independently gives Jhunal a much larger footprint:
- a speaking NPC, **Jhunal the Owl** (`0225B23C`);
- a **Piece of Jhunal** form (`0225B23F`);
- **Jhunal's Library** (`CELL 02259C5B`);
- a library key, rings, Arcana of Jhunal, and Bone of Jhunal;
- Saklas dialogue explicitly treating "Jhunal" as both an old Nord divine name and a First Era historical person's name.

Current classification: **high-confidence VIGILANT identity cluster, ontologically unresolved**. The plugin strongly links the historical saint, the Owl, the Library, and the fragmented forms, but does not require Lorekeeper to assume they are a single simple physical continuity.

## Nenyond

The saint text describes Nenyond as an eastern Cyrodiilic lord and comparatively moderate Marukhati Selective who spent his fortune constructing an underground priory. He and Manthar disappeared after Dawn Era ruins were discovered beneath it; Silorn later vanished while searching for them, after which the ruins were sealed.

The ESM independently implements:
- **Nenyond's Underground Priory** (`0210EBFD`);
- **Funeral Temple** (`021114AF`);
- **Nenyond's Key** (`0212D026`);
- a direct door relationship between the priory and Funeral Temple.

No Nenyond NPC form or first-person dialogue has been found in the current VIGILANT inventory.

Current classification: **VIGILANT-specific saint biography with strong location corroboration but weak personal/first-person evidence**.
