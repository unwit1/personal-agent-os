## Core account

Ja'zhan recounts a wealthy retired knight of the Alessian Order who:
- retired after the cleansing of Malada;
- ruled a large estate;
- treated subjects as inferiors;
- maintained large kennels and mounted kennel guards.

After a nine-year-old servant child accidentally injures one of the knight's hounds, the knight orders:
- the child imprisoned overnight;
- the child stripped;
- a staged hunt;
- the hounds released to kill the child in front of the child's mother.

## Witness boundary

The title says **What Khajiit Heard**, and the opening says Ja'zhan heard about the knight.

Later wording shifts into vivid narration.

Therefore the safest source classification is:
- Ja'zhan-authored second-hand account;
- not direct eyewitness confirmation of every detail.

## Malada connection

The knight is said to have retired after the **cleansing of Malada**.

This creates a direct textual connection between:
- Alessian violence;
- Malada;
- elite entitlement.

It does not identify the knight with another Malada actor.
