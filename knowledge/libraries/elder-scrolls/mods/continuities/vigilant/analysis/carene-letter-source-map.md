# Letter from Carene — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 02013BE8` — **Letter from Carene**

Exact text:
- `books/02013BE8-letter-from-carene.txt`

## Direct statement

Carene writes to **Taranis** after he has told her that someone may be able to lift his curse.

She says:
- she has a bad feeling about the offer;
- she wants Taranis to promise to be careful;
- Lilian made him a flower crown;
- she prays he returns before the flowers wither.

## What it establishes

The letter directly establishes:
- Carene knows Taranis is pursuing a possible cure;
- she distrusts or fears the person/offer involved;
- the family expects Taranis to return home if the curse can be resolved;
- Lilian's flower-crown motif is independently confirmed.

The letter does **not** identify:
- the person offering the cure;
- the sword;
- the exact nature of the curse;
- the job Taranis has been asked to perform;
- what Carene specifically fears will happen.

## Relationship to Taranis's letter

Taranis's own letter supplies the details Carene omits:
- the person is an unnamed female summoner;
- she claims she can remove the sword's curse;
- she requires one job in exchange.

Together, the two documents preserve a useful perspective split:

- **Taranis:** hopeful and eager for reunion.
- **Carene:** wary and fearful of the bargain.

Neither source establishes the summoner's identity or motive.

## Flower-crown corroboration

Lilian's own letter says she made a flower crown for her father.

Carene independently mentions the same crown.

This corroborates:
- Lilian/Taranis parent-child relationship;
- the family's expectation of Taranis's return;
- the crown as a mundane emotional motif.

Do not assign magical significance to the crown without further evidence.
