# Laza and the Gray Owl — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

- `BOOK 021E7B14` — **Laza and the Gray Owl**

Exact text:
- `books/021E7B14-laza-and-the-gray-owl.txt`

## Scene

Laza meets the **Gray Owl** at the edge of the **Old Forest**.

The Owl observes Laza's **burning eyes** and gives instructions centered on blindness, pursuit, blood, and Kyne's fountain.

## Eye sequence

The Gray Owl instructs Laza:

- blind the **right eye** → the eyes of the sky are also blinded;
- blind the **left eye** → the eyes of the forest are also blinded;
- blind **both eyes** → no one can catch Laza;
- but if both eyes are blinded, Laza can catch no one either.

This establishes a direct exchange:

**freedom from being hunted ↔ loss of ability to hunt**

## Alternative senses

After blindness, the Owl tells Laza to:
- use nose and ears;
- avoid the smell of blood;
- follow the Owl's call.

This shifts Laza from sight toward:
- scent;
- hearing;
- obedience to the Owl's guidance.

## Destination

The final destination is explicit:

**the bottom of the fountain of Kyne**

This is direct VIGILANT evidence linking:
- Laza;
- Old Forest;
- Gray Owl;
- blindness;
- blood avoidance;
- Kyne's fountain.

## Burning-eye motif

The book opens with Laza having **burning eyes**.

This image should be indexed alongside other VIGILANT fiery-eye motifs, but not automatically treated as the same phenomenon.

The text itself does not identify:
- the source of the burning;
- whether the eyes are literally aflame;
- whether this is Stone-related.

## Gray Owl identity boundary

Later Vicn works provide direct continuity evidence connecting the Gray Owl to **Jhunal the Gray** and the wider Orlando/Jhunal network.

Within this VIGILANT source, the speaker is only **Gray Owl**.

Preserve the later identity as a continuity-resolution layer rather than back-projecting the personal name into the original text.
