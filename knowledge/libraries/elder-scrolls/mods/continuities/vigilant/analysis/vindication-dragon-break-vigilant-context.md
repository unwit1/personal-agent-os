## Core ritual program

The text presents a Marukhati program in which:
- Akatosh is treated as a unitary spirit;
- the Selectives seek to purge the perceived **Aldmeri Taint**;
- the **Staff of Towers** is prepared;
- participants chant **Proper-Life** until achieving **monothought**;
- the Selectives **Dance** forward and backward;
- the Roll of Time is wound backwards.

The text frames this as a deliberate ritual intended to alter Akatosh/time.
