## Sarcophagus-cycle connection

The Coldharbour main-quest testimony from Inquisitor Pepe says:
- people repeatedly climb from a sarcophagus;
- one prior living arrival was believed to be a **slave trader**;
- that person's eyes burned like fire.

That creates a strong source-network connection:

**Slave Trader Note 1 sarcophagus image ↔ Pepe's slave-trader sarcophagus testimony**

The note itself does not explicitly state:
- that the body is the Slave Trader;
- that the sarcophagus is the player's opening sarcophagus;
- whether the body is alive, dead, sleeping, or awaiting emergence.

Keep the exact referent unresolved.

## Itinerary role

Within the eight-note trail, Note 1 functions naturally as an opening waypoint because:
- the note sequence begins with a sarcophagus;
- Pepe's testimony also positions the Slave Trader at the sarcophagus cycle;
- Note 8 later ends on the dead Slave Trader in a cemetery.

This supports an itinerary reading, but does not prove the notes are chronological autobiography.
