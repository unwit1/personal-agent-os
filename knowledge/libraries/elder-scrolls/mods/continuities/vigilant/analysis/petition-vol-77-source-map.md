## Doctrine-memory collapse

Amicus challenges Pelan to recall the Seventy-Seven Doctrines.

Both realize they remember none of them.

Amicus says:
- records may remain;
- readers and writers will forget them.

This makes memory loss itself part of the volume's subject.

## Marukh contradiction

Pelan recalls:
- the cliff covered in blood;
- Marukh already dead;
- other Imga tracing blood stains and producing more than seventy-seven interpretations.

Amicus then points out a contradiction:
- Pelan returned to the Imperial City with Marukh;
- yet Marukh was supposed to be dead.

Do not silently repair this chronology.

The contradiction is evidence of unstable/manipulated memory.

## Burning Stone

Pelan remembers Marukh:
- pointing at Akatosh;
- saying Auriel is unworthy;
- vomiting a burning Stone;
- becoming a mangled corpse again.

Amicus says the Stone chose them.
Pelan insists it chose him.

This directly links:
- authority;
- memory;
- selection;
- Stone-Fire.

## Final doctrine

Amicus concludes that domination is kindness for people who cannot bear freedom and says they must be **burned twice**.

This should be stored as Amicus's ideology, not neutral narration.
