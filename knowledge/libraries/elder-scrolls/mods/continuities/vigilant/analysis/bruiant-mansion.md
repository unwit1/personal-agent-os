# Bruiant Mansion / Child of Oblivion — document reconstruction

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`  
Primary quest: `QUST 02065932` / `zzzCOMq01` / *Child of Oblivion*

## Structural cast

The quest aliases bind Gwyneth, Julius, Julia, Bal, a killable Bal variant, Molag Bal, and Marcus as explicit roles. The quest deliberately treats the butler Bal and later Molag Bal manifestation as separate aliases/forms, so Lorekeeper does not collapse them into one literal actor without further script evidence.

## 4E 199 — Marcus' affair

Marcus' journal records an affair in Markarth while married to Julia. He spends heavily to acquire/free a woman from a Khajiit slaver, learns she is pregnant, later throws her out, and pays a settlement/donation after she appeals to the Jarl.

The same journal says Marcus bought a Dwemer spider as a souvenir for Julius, proving Julius already existed. The unborn child from this affair should not be silently identified with Julius.

## 4E 200 — Julia and Julius disappear

Marcus records Julia and Julius disappearing on 9 Sun's Height. Julius is found in the forest ten days later, terrified and unable to speak. Julia remains missing until Jacob reports that her burned body was found in Lake Ilinalta.

`BOOK 0206592D` / *Ilinalta Report 4E 200* corroborates the discovery and says Julia's corpse was still burning from within, her blood had become acidic/poisonous, and a curse was present. The report says the curse was strangely easy to dispel, as though its caster allowed it.

Julia's surviving letter says she is leaving Marcus and taking Julius. Her journal is overwritten almost entirely with the word "Die."

## Late 4E 200 — Bal and the deaths

After Julia's disappearance/death, Marcus hires a new butler named Bal, whom he calls a Wild Elf. He notes Julius is unusually fond of Bal.

Lamthor warns Marcus that Julius has become stranger since Bal arrived and was seen muttering incantations to the dogs which Lamthor believes Bal taught him.

Deaths and apparently impossible accidents accumulate. Marcus repeatedly hears crawling in the hallways and has nightmares involving Julius, Julia, a room of rust/blood, and a burning red stone.

## 4E 201 — Bartholo

Marcus asks the Temple of Stendarr for help. Gwyneth later says Thorondir considered the deaths coincidences, but Bartholo disagreed and went alone.

Bartholo's journal records a quiet first month followed by renewed deaths. He detects Magicka traces leading to a basement totem which he associates with Molag Bal. He describes a powerful blood curse as concentrated hatred and compares it to ancient Ayleid curses. He infers that the caster is paying a high price.

Bartholo temporarily dispels one totem, estimates four or five more, and requests reinforcements. The next day the curse has returned, exits vanish into fog, and he believes he is trapped. The delayed reinforcement letter is what reaches Gwyneth and launches the player investigation.

## Julius as the Child of Oblivion

Julius' readable note contains only a plea to his father not to do "this."

Marcus' final note says Julius is no longer human, calls him a **Child of Oblivion**, blames himself, and says he must take responsibility.

Molag Bal gives another perspective: he tells the player Julius' hatred is extraordinarily powerful and explicitly compares it to Lamae's hatred. Molag Bal is a manipulative speaker, so this remains attributed testimony.

The quest objectives ultimately require the player to **Defeat Julius** and escape.

## Bal and Molag Bal

Butler Bal welcomes the player, calls himself a shadow/remnant, and minimizes his identity. Orlando's alternate route calls Bal tainted and says the Spinner's Needle can pierce his curse.

The primary quest nevertheless uses separate aliases for `Bal`, `BalKillable`, and `MolagBal`. The current model is association without literal identity until scripts/transformations establish the mechanism.

## Evidence hierarchy

- strong structural evidence: quest aliases, objective order, dated documents, structurally attributed dialogue
- in-world documentary testimony: Marcus, Bartholo, Ilinalta report, Lamthor note
- damaged/limited first-person evidence: Julia's journal/letter and Julius' note
- potentially manipulative testimony: Molag Bal and Orlando
