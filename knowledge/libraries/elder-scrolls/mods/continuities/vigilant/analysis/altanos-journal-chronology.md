# Altano's Journal — chronology and evidence map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 0201C7F6` — **Altano's Journal**

Exact text:
- `books/0201C7F6-altanos-journal.txt`

## Document condition

The surviving journal is incomplete.

The text explicitly says:
- the first several pages were torn out;
- remaining pages are scorched;
- the rest becomes charred and unreadable after the final surviving entry.

This means the document provides a bounded early-life chronology, not a complete autobiography.

## Surviving chronology

### 6 Morning Star, 4E 197

Altano records:
- he does not know who he is;
- he cannot remember his name or place of origin;
- his first memory is waking in a snowfield;
- earlier journal pages have already been torn out.

This is direct first-person evidence for the amnesia state.

It does **not** establish what caused the amnesia.

### 22 Morning Star, 4E 197

Altano says:
- an old man named **Jacob** rescued him from the snowfield;
- Jacob was a Vigilant of Stendarr;
- Jacob gave him the name **Altano**.

This directly establishes the Jacob → Altano rescue/naming relationship.

### 4 Rain's Hand, 4E 197

Altano says three months have passed since he joined the **Vigilants of Stendarr** and that his memories have still not returned.

This places his institutional entry very early in his surviving identity.

### 8–13 Frostfall, 4E 197

The journal records:
- reports of witches gathering south of Whiterun;
- a planned Vigilant witch hunt;
- a successful operation with minor injuries and no deaths;
- Altano being unable to sleep afterward because he keeps seeing the face of the witch who tried to kill him.

The text does not name the witch.

Do not identify her with a later named witch without separate evidence.

### 9 Sun's Dawn, 4E 198

Altano says the Vigilants encountered and chased a **werewolf**, but it escaped.

He specifically remarks that it was strangely timid.

The werewolf is unnamed.

### 15 Sun's Dawn, 4E 198

Altano records meeting an unnamed **Silver Hand woman** searching for a specific werewolf.

She reacts angrily when Altano says he could not recognize an individual werewolf in transformed form.

Altano calls her childish.

The current document does not identify:
- the woman;
- the werewolf she seeks;
- whether that werewolf is the same timid werewolf from six days earlier.

Those are plausible links, not direct facts.

## Evidence significance

The journal gives the strongest direct chronology for Altano's early known life:

**unknown past → wakes amnesiac in snowfield → rescued/named by Jacob → joins Vigilants → participates in witch hunt → encounters werewolf/Silver Hand thread**

This is important because later VIGILANT material presents Altano as a confident mentor and eventual betrayer, while the journal shows that his known identity began only a few years earlier and was constructed around Jacob and the Vigilants.

## Reliability

High confidence:
- dates as written in Altano's surviving journal;
- Altano's claimed amnesia;
- Jacob rescue/naming;
- Vigilant membership;
- the witch-hunt, werewolf, and Silver Hand encounters as Altano records them.

Unresolved:
- cause of amnesia;
- identity before 4E 197;
- who removed the missing pages;
- who or what scorched the journal;
- identities of the unnamed witch, werewolf, and Silver Hand woman;
- whether the missing pages contained information from before the snowfield awakening.
