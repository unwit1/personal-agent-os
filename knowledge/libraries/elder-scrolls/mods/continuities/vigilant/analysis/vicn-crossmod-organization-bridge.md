# Cross-Vicn organization bridge — VIGILANT gaps clarified by later works

Parent continuity: `tes.mod.vicn`

This bridge records concepts that are only partial or supplementary in the current VIGILANT ESM extraction but are directly implemented in later Vicn works.

It prevents two opposite errors:

1. treating later Vicn evidence as if VIGILANT itself stated it;
2. treating a VIGILANT source gap as if the concept were merely fan speculation.

## Holy Pipers

### VIGILANT layer

Current VIGILANT evidence:
- a123999 supplementary commentary names the Holy Pipers;
- no direct Holy-Piper-named faction record has yet been recovered from the persisted VIGILANT ESM layer.

### GLENMORIL layer

GLENMORIL directly establishes:
- Holy Pipers were an inquisitorial Stendarr branch;
- they were excommunicated;
- they escalated into kidnapping/torture;
- Stendarr is said to have cursed them;
- they occupy Piper's Hill;
- Laelnoah and other victims are tied to them;
- the Sacred Anatomancer is associated with/identified as their leader in later intelligence.

Conclusion:

**Holy Pipers are directly real within the Vicn continuity even though current VIGILANT-only extraction remains source-incomplete.**

## Chick Traders

### VIGILANT layer

Current VIGILANT evidence:
- a123999 names Chick Traders as an Elsweyr/Anequina slave-trading organization;
- VIGILANT also contains an unnamed Slave Trader thread and chick-themed books, but no direct organization-name link has yet been recovered.

### GLENMORIL layer

GLENMORIL directly implements:
- **Ja'zel the Chick Trader**;
- Chick Trader transactions;
- the sale/claiming of orphans;
- Civil War profiteering;
- later Anequina/prince/royal-guard origin traditions;
- intentionally substituted Chick/Egg/Chicken vocabulary.

Conclusion:

**Chick Traders are directly real within the Vicn continuity, but VIGILANT's fiery-eyed Slave Trader should remain a separate unresolved identity until explicit linkage appears.**

## Sacred Anatomancer

### VIGILANT layer

Direct VIGILANT anchors:
- `BOOK 024E7F91` — *Report: Sacred Anatomancer*;
- Dungeon placement;
- gatekeeper/trial dialogue cluster.

Missing:
- exact report text;
- full quest/alias resolution.

### GLENMORIL layer

Direct later evidence:
- Sacred Anatomancer biographies rewrite themselves;
- race/origin/founding stories contradict one another;
- some versions say he founded the Holy Pipers;
- other versions say they summoned him;
- anatomancy voices use repeated-self / radiance / birth language.

### UNSLAAD layer

Direct later evidence:
- concrete **Sacred Anatomancer** NPC;
- asks for the player's **name**;
- explains: **“I want to see its Radiance.”**

Conclusion:

**Sacred Anatomancer is a recurring Vicn identity/concept with deliberately unstable biography and later direct Name→Radiance dialogue.**

Do not force all manifestations into one continuous body.

## Source discipline

For questions about these concepts:

### Ask: “Does VIGILANT itself directly say this?”
Use only the VIGILANT ESM-derived layer.

### Ask: “Is this real in the wider Vicn continuity?”
Allow direct GLENMORIL/UNSLAAD evidence.

### Ask: “What is the intended relationship?”
Use source-separated synthesis:
- VIGILANT direct;
- later Vicn direct;
- supplementary commentary.

## Non-bridge: Black Hand

No equally strong later GLENMORIL/UNSLAAD/DAc0da Black Hand identity reconstruction has yet been found.

Therefore Black Hand remains:
- partial VIGILANT Sithis-system evidence;
- a123999 supplementary five-child/Night Mother interpretation.

Do not infer cross-Vicn confirmation where none has been recovered.
