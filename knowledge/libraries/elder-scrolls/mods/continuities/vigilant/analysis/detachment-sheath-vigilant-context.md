## Core concepts in the text

Fervidius argues that:
- a defined “sheath” within the Aurbis can be detached from the surrounding whole;
- this is accomplished through invocation of **Mnemoli**;
- the **Beseeching Alesstic** performs a transformation of thought;
- the **Hurling Disk** recapitulates the Tower principle;
- twisting the sheath into the **Middle Dawn**, “to the number of seventeen,” brings it to **untime and unplace**;
- the detached state may eventually be reabsorbed when its sustaining force is exhausted.

These are claims within the official text.
