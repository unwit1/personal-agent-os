## Political exclusion

Pelan challenges plans to remove citizenship from:
- elves;
- giants;
- minotaurs;
- other non-human peoples.

He frames Alessia's Empire as a multi-racial project built through cooperation.

Amicus instead advocates a human-only Empire.

## Metaphysical program

Amicus says the Order intends to:
- separate Auriel from Akatosh;
- erase elven history;
- cause non-human races to perish;
- create freedom for humans alone;
- involve the Blue Star;
- reforge the Heart of Shezarr;
- remake the Aurbis with artificial Flowers.

These are Amicus's in-world claims/plans.

Do not treat them as established achievable cosmology.

## Freedom paradox

Amicus argues:
- gods enslave mortals;
- therefore humanity must free itself from gods;
- yet his proposed solution is coercive racial exclusion and extermination.

Pelan explicitly rejects this reasoning.

The contradiction is a core ideological feature of the volume.
