## Surviving excerpt

The book identifies itself as a work on **Ayleid flesh art** and says the surviving passage comes from a section titled **“The Captivity of the Nedes.”**

The excerpt describes extreme violence by Ayleid slaveholders against Nedic captives, including:
- destruction of homes;
- killing men;
- violence toward women and children;
- prolonged torture and execution;
- cruelty directed specifically at pregnant women and infants.

The narrator's purpose is condemnatory. The text argues that calling such cruelty “bestial” would insult actual beasts.

## Jhunal connection

This book is one of the strongest documentary links between **Jhunal** and the recurring VIGILANT motif of **flesh art**.

Jhunal the Owl separately says in direct dialogue that:
- he knew a “secret art of flesh”;
- he attempted to use that art in experiments involving dragon souls;
- the Black Worm learned secret arts from him and later experimented on him.

The authored book therefore establishes:

**Jhunal → writes about → Ayleid flesh art**

and the dialogue establishes:

**Jhunal → claims practical knowledge of → secret flesh art**

These are related but not identical claims.

The excerpt does **not** establish:
- that Jhunal invented Ayleid flesh art;
- that the atrocities described are magical procedures;
- that the exact practices in the book are the techniques Jhunal later used;
- that every later bodily-transformation event in VIGILANT derives from this text.
