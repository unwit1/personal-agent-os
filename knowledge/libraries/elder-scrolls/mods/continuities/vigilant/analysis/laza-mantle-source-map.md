# Laza — mantle / hunter source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

- `BOOK 020DB22F` — **Laza**

Exact text:

## Complete instruction sequence

The text instructs the reader to:
- **take on the name of Laza**;
- become an **immortal hunter**;
- chase the Stone;
- hunt souls burned by the Stone;
- crush the Stone;
- bring peace to captive souls and the Old Forest.

## Laza as a name or mantle

The phrase **“take on the name of Laza”** is direct evidence that Laza is not necessarily only one ordinary personal name.

Safe ontology:
- **Laza** can function as an assumed name, title, role, or mantle;
- a bearer of the name is expected to become a hunter;
- the role is defined in relation to the Stone and captive souls.

This does not prove:
- every character called Laza is a different person;
- there are definitely multiple simultaneous Lazas;
- the original historical Laza ceased to exist.

It only establishes that the text presents the name as something that can be taken on.

## Hunter mission

The mantle's purpose is explicitly anti-Stone:
- pursue the Stone;
- hunt the souls marked/burned by it;
- destroy the Stone;
- release or pacify captive souls.

This is important because other VIGILANT Laza material depicts:
- shepherd imagery;
- Owl manipulation;
- dragon hunting;
- Kyne's garden/fountain;
- Stone-related transformation.

The mantle text should therefore be co-retrieved with those sources without assuming it describes the exact same phase of one person's life.

## Old Forest

The text explicitly connects the mission to the **Old Forest**.

That provides a direct bridge to the Gray/Black Owl books, which also place Laza near the Old Forest and Kyne's fountain.

## Relationship to plural-Laza testimony

Kahkaankrein later describes Laza in plural terms as mortal children/shepherds connected to Kyne.

The mantle text independently makes plural or successive bearers more plausible because the name can be **taken on**.

However:
- plural testimony + transferable name supports a multi-bearer model;
- it does not define the exact number, chronology, or identity of those bearers.
