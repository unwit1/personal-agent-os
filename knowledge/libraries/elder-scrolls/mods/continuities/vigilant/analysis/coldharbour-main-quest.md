# Coldharbour main quest — first structural reconstruction

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

## Main spine

- `QUST 0212F24E` / `zzzCHMQ00` — **Coldharbour**
- `QUST 021363DB` / `zzzCHMQ01` — **Aetherius**
- `QUST 0208F2F4` / `zzzCHMQ02` — **Exsultate Jubilate**

## Coldharbour

The quest aliases make **Inquisitor Pepe** the principal speaking guide. Other major bound roles include Menta Na, Abbot Cosmas, a Marukh form, Laza of Order, and travel/area markers.

Pepe greets the player after they awaken from a sarcophagus and identifies the surrounding ruin as Mathmalatu Priory, formerly a monastery of the Alessian Order. His directions form a usable story path: leave the priory/waterfront; survive the wasteland; pass through the sewer/prison district; reach Malada via Marukh's followers; confront the Marukh/Stone material; kill Dro'Zel and take his heart/key; then reach the central Tower before Greymarch breaks through.

Pepe describes the wasteland as a failed Alessian/Molag Bal order threatened by Jyggalag's Greymarch. He says Molag Bal has consumed souls to maintain a barrier against the Army of Order, but that the barrier is failing.

## Pepe as narrator

Pepe is highly informative but not neutral. His dialogue indicates personal involvement in the history he narrates. He speaks of burning Mary, killing a Sload, serving the Eight Divines, meeting Marukh in Colovia, witnessing Marukh with an apparition of Alessia, and later concluding that the Alessian rulers became enslavers rather than shepherds.

He also says the Stone was lost during the War of Righteousness and connects its loss to the collapse of the Alessian Empire. These remain VIGILANT-specific claims from Pepe.

At the end of *Coldharbour*, Pepe gives the player the route through Dro'Zel to the Tower and then observes that the Army of Order has broken the barrier and Greymarch has begun.

## Aetherius

The `Aetherius` aliases bind Molag Bal, a `MolagTrue`/return form, multiple Pepe ghost forms, Stendarr, Volar the Deathbringer, and Alessia.

Pepe's ghosts repeatedly warn the player to stop. One states that the Stone is the key to the gates of the soul and an awakening bell that rouses the Oblivion inside. Another says Molag Bal uses the bodies of powerful dead figures as vessels and specifically refers to Alessia's body.

Alessia's single resolved line warns the player to run before "the Wolf" arrives.

A Molag Bal form initially asks the player to stop. After the gate opens, the `MolagTrue` form says the player succeeded in opening Aetherius, declares it within his grasp, says Stendarr is dead, and credits the player for making this possible.

The record structure therefore supports a reversal: the player's progress toward the Tower/gate is ultimately used by Molag Bal to access Aetherius. Exact causal mechanics still require stage/script analysis.

## Exsultate Jubilate

The final main quest binds `NPC_ 0208DE94` / Molag Bal. Its resolved dialogue frames the confrontation in dream/identity terms: every dream ends, including ownerless dreams, followed by a demand to answer who Molag Bal is and who the player is.

This connects the main quest to VIGILANT's wider identity/memory motif: Orlando's ownerless dreams, Altano's missing identity, Coldharbour memory variants, and Belharza's unstable-form testimony.

## Canon boundary

Pepe, Molag Bal, Alessia, and the memory figures are in-world speakers in a dream/memory-heavy mod continuity. This page reconstructs what VIGILANT makes happen and what those speakers claim; it does not convert Pepe's Alessian history or Molag Bal's metaphysics into official Elder Scrolls canon.
