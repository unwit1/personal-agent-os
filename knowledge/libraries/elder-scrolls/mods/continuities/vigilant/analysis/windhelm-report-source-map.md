# Windhelm Report 4E 180 — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

## Timeline

### 8 Second Seed, 4E 180

The report records:
- all prisoners in the jail disappeared;
- the guards were also missing.

### 9 Second Seed

Authorities:
- capture a vampire in the dungeons;
- suffer two deaths and more than a dozen injuries;
- order the dead cremated;
- request help from the Vigilants of Stendarr.

### 10 Second Seed

The dead are cremated.

The report says there were no signs of vampirism among them.

### 11 Second Seed

The Vigilants arrive.

Their investigation concludes:
- the **Maiden Statue** is the source or route of the vampire attacks.

Authorities interrogate the captured vampire about the hidden door behind the statue.

### 12 Second Seed

The captive vampire explains how to open the door, but dies during interrogation.

The statue reacts to vampire blood.

A team of:
- **7 Vigilants**
- **13 guards**

enters through the statue.

Half a day later:
- screaming comes from the statue;
- it seals itself;
- vampire blood no longer reopens it;
- the team's status is unknown.

### 13 Second Seed

Jacob is found unconscious outside the city walls.

According to the report, Jacob says:
- the investigation team was killed in a tunnel collapse;
- the collapse also destroyed the vampires' route.

The official investigation is then terminated.

## What the report directly establishes

High-confidence documentary facts:
- the official sequence of events;
- Maiden Statue reaction to vampire blood;
- expedition size;
- Jacob found alive/unconscious outside Windhelm;
- Jacob's reported tunnel-collapse explanation.

## What the report does not establish

The document does not mention:
- Blood Matron;
- Lamae;
- Molag Bal;
- Aredhel's surrender;
- Joshua's flight;
- Jacob's later bargain;
- Blood Mist Castle.

Those come from separate private/testimonial sources.

## Contradiction with Aredhel

Aredhel later says Jacob was killed in battle.

This directly conflicts with the report's public record of Jacob found alive.

The report should therefore be stored as:
- **official/public documentary account**
rather than automatically treated as the complete truth of the expedition.

## Interrogation boundary

The vampire **did not survive interrogation**.

The report does not explain:
- whether the death was deliberate;
- whether torture caused it;
- whether the captive died from injuries or another mechanism.

Do not add a cause not present in the text.
