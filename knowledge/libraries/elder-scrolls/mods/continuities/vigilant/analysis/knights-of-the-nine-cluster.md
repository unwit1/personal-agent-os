## Why this needs a cluster rather than nine equal dossiers

The current ESM-derived repository evidence is uneven.

Several knights have strong VIGILANT evidence:
- **Sir Berich** — dialogue about Jhunal, Saarthal, and the eternal fire rune.
- **Sir Caius** — substantial Mary/Moura and Thrassian-plague testimony.
- **Sir Casimir** — represented environmentally as Burnt Casimir with Arcana of Jhunal.
- **Sir Torolf** — speaking actor in *Knight of Arkay*.
- **Sir Henrik** — direct testimony about Varla preserved in Varla's dossier.
- **Sir Gregory** — at least one preserved note describing Mary/healer cult imagery.

Direct source recovery now resolves **Sir Juncan** and **Sir Ralvas** as full VIGILANT quest actors.

The remaining roster member without comparable direct VIGILANT implementation in the current pass is:
- Sir Amiel Lannus

The cluster is therefore now **mostly direct ESM-corroborated**, while the shared Molag Bal soul-fragment mechanism remains supplementary.

## Soul-fragment claim

The a123999 background source says Molag Bal stole **part of the souls** of these Knights.

This is a strong intended-background explanation for why historical Knights can appear in VIGILANT's later Coldharbour material.

However, the currently persisted ESM corpus does not yet provide one common quest/script record establishing the soul-fragment mechanism for all nine.

Therefore:

- **supplementary layer:** shared Molag Bal soul-theft mechanism.
- **ESM layer:** individual appearances, documents, scenes, and remains must be verified knight by knight.

## Relation to official Knights of the Nine

Several names come from the established *Knights of the Nine* tradition.

Lorekeeper should distinguish:
1. licensed/base-canon biography;
2. VIGILANT's reuse of the name/identity;
3. VIGILANT supplementary claim about Molag Bal retaining soul fragments;
4. Coldharbour manifestation-specific evidence.

Do not assume every official event in a knight's original biography is reenacted or referenced by VIGILANT.
