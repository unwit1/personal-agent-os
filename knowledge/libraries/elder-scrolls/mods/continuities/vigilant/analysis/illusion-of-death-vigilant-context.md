## Textual content

The fragment describes Marukh's encounter with the shade of Alessia / Al-Esh.

It says:
- Marukh undergoes a Century of Penance;
- he experiences bodily affliction;
- the shade of Al-Esh speaks to him;
- he records her words in his own blood;
- the Seventy-Seven Inflexible Doctrines are etched into the rock;
- Marukh interprets Alessia's postmortem persistence, and Pelinal's witness across death, as evidence that death is an illusion;
- Proper-Life and Ehlnofic Annulment are linked to persistence beyond death;
- the drive to expunge corruption is framed as capable of overcoming the Arkayn Cycle.

These are claims within the official source text.

## VIGILANT relevance

- the Seventy-Seven Doctrines;
- Pelan/Pepe;
- death/persistence;
- reconstructed or repeated identities;

The official text therefore acts as important **canonical substrate** for VIGILANT's themes.

But source order matters:

**official text provides vocabulary/concepts → VIGILANT adds its own characters, manifestations, contradictions, and memory structures**

Do not reverse that relationship.
