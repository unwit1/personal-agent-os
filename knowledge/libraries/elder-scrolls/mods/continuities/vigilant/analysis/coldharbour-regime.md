# Molag Bal's Coldharbour regime in VIGILANT

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

This page models the political/metaphysical power structure governing VIGILANT's Act 4 Coldharbour.

It is intentionally **not** stored as an ordinary faction dossier, because Coldharbour contains:
- captives;
- reconstructed historical figures;
- memory bodies;
- Soul-Shriven;
- local rulers;
- monsters;
- Daedric servants;
- people resisting or merely surviving within Molag Bal's realm.

Presence inside Coldharbour does not equal political membership.

## Molag Bal as controlling power

Inquisitor Pepe repeatedly frames Molag Bal as the underlying author/controller of the Coldharbour tragedy.

Pepe calls him the **“playwright”** and describes the inhabitants as puppets within the larger system.

This is character testimony, but the main quest structurally confirms Molag Bal's centrality through:
- multiple Molag Bal forms;
- the Aetherius quest;
- the final Exsultate Jubilate quest;
- Adabal/Stone possession;
- endgame gate logic;
- Coldharbour's broader Daedric setting.

## Soul economy

Pepe says Molag Bal consumes souls to maintain a barrier protecting the remaining Coldharbour territory from Jyggalag's Army of Order.

This produces a coercive war economy:

**captured/available souls → consumed by Molag Bal → barrier against Forces of Order**

The current ESM strongly corroborates the existence of:
- the Order invasion;
- Greymarch quest;
- barrier/Order references;
- Coldharbour soul/manifestation systems.

The exact mechanical process of converting souls into barrier strength remains testimony/script-level detail.

## Rule through domination rather than ordinary citizenship

VIGILANT repeatedly uses:
- enslavement;
- soul capture;
- coercive salvation;
- hunger;
- submission;
- false mercy;
- puppet/playwright imagery.

The regime is therefore best modeled as **domination infrastructure**, not a normal state with a clean citizen/faction roster.

A person inside it may be:
- servant;
- captive;
- reconstructed dead;
- local ruler;
- enemy;
- soul-shriven encounter;
- memory figure;
- temporary collaborator.

## Local rulers

Pepe says **Varla the Man-Hunter** rules territory after swearing allegiance to Molag Bal.

Varla's later implementation supports a local territorial identity:
- boss form in Varla's Hall;
- Coldharbour dialogue threatening trespassers;
- testimony that he ruled eastern territory.

This suggests Molag Bal's regime can contain subordinate/local powers rather than direct uniform control of every space.

Do not generalize Varla's allegiance to all Coldharbour bosses.

## Alessian ruins and inherited institutions

Much of Act 4 is physically organized through:
- Mathmalatu Priory;
- Inquisition Courts;
- Marukhite priory spaces;
- Malada;
- saint charnels;
- Funeral Temple;
- Belharza's Hidden Charnel.

These are Alessian/historical institutional spaces embedded inside Molag Bal's realm.

Their existence does not mean the Alessian Order and Molag Bal's regime are the same institution.

A better model is:

**historical institutions/remains → captured, reconstructed, or embedded within → Coldharbour**

## Soul-Shriven layer

The Feral Soul-Shriven system provides a generic encounter ecology for many named identities.

Soul-Shriven should be treated as:
- Coldharbour ontological state;
- encounter implementation;
- reusable body/identity mechanism.

It is not a political membership category equivalent to “servant of Molag Bal.”

Some Soul-Shriven may be hostile actors inside his realm without being willing adherents.

## War with Jyggalag

The Forces of Order invade the realm during VIGILANT's new Greymarch.

Pepe describes:
- Battle of Weye;
- widespread Order conquest;
- a Silver Cocoon manifestation route;
- the remaining region held behind Molag Bal's barrier.

Structural support includes:
- `zzzCHGreymarchQuest`;
- Order aliases/targets;
- Silver Cocoon cells;
- Throne of Order;
- main-quest Greymarch links.

Thus the Act 4 geopolitical layer is:

**Molag Bal's domination regime → defending a shrinking Coldharbour domain against → Jyggalag's Forces of Order**

Neither side should be simplified into “the good faction.”

## Aetherius objective

The Aetherius quest complicates Molag Bal's defensive posture.

After the player opens the gate, the `MolagTrue` form says:
- Aetherius is now within his grasp;
- the player made this possible;
- Stendarr is dead.

This shows that Molag Bal is not merely defending Coldharbour from Order.

He is also using the player's progression toward his own expansion/access into Aetherius.

## The Stone / Adabal

The later Adabal object is structurally tied to Molag Bal:
- `NPC_ 0212339D` / Molag Bal carries `MISC 021353DF` / Adabal.

Pepe and memory sources also connect Molag Bal to the Stone's theft/use.

The exact metaphysics are deliberately unstable, but the Stone is one of the regime's central power/temptation objects.

## Relationship to player agency

VIGILANT repeatedly contrasts Molag Bal's “helping hand” and salvation rhetoric with Prisoner freedom.

Molag Bal offers:
- escape;
- relief;
- bread/salvation;
- power;
- acceptance;

but frames them through submission and domination.

This places the regime in thematic opposition to the player's capacity to refuse imposed narratives.

## Relationship map

- **Molag Bal → dominates → Coldharbour**
- **Molag Bal → consumes souls to sustain → defensive barrier** — Pepe testimony
- **Varla → swears allegiance to → Molag Bal** — Pepe/local testimony
- **historical institutions → embedded/reconstructed within → Coldharbour**
- **Soul-Shriven → exist within → Coldharbour encounter ecology**
- **Forces of Order → invade → Molag Bal's domain**
- **Molag Bal → uses player progression to reach → Aetherius**
- **Molag Bal ↔ associated with → Adabal / Stone complex**

## Reliability

High-confidence structural:
- Molag Bal forms and quest ownership;
- Coldharbour/Aetherius/final quest structure;
- Forces of Order conflict implementation;
- Varla boss/location;
- Adabal carried by a Molag Bal form.

Attributed:
- exact soul-consumption/barrier mechanism;
- precise territorial history;
- “playwright/puppet” metaphysics;
- exact nature of local allegiance.

## Canon boundary

Molag Bal and Coldharbour are established Elder Scrolls concepts.

The specific Greymarch war, barrier economy, Varla allegiance, Aetherius access plot, and institutional reconstruction described here are VIGILANT-continuity material.
