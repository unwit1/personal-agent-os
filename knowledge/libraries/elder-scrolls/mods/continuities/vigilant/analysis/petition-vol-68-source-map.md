## Unlisted prisoners

Pelan says people not on execution lists are being killed and asks who they are.

Amicus answers that:
- no one knows their origins;
- their greatest offense is seeing a truth other than the one the Order wants them to see.

## Prisoner concept

Amicus describes the **Prisoner** as able to:
- follow prophecies in Books of Stars and Frost;
- resist the Order's gradient-dancing;
- create the world/future they want moment by moment.

Pelan asks whether this is the Shezarrine they sought.

Amicus rejects the Prisoner's salvation because it would destroy the Order's constructed system.

## Freedom as threat

The central opposition is:

**Prisoner freedom → existential threat to constructed Alessian order**

Amicus explicitly says the Order does not want that freedom.

## Stone of Fire

The volume ends with Amicus saying all crowns are illuminated by the **Stone of Fire**.

This is direct terminology.

Do not automatically equate Stone of Fire with every other Stone reference without source-specific evidence.
