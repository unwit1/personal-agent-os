## 1 — Kyne beyond the far shore

Direct text says Kyne waits beyond frothing darkness at the end of the far shore.

This belongs to the same wider VIGILANT imagery in which:
- Kahkaankrein longs to return to Kyne;
- the dragons of Kyne's Garden are lost/cursed;
- children, animals, and souls can be handed over to or return toward Kyne.

The stanza itself does not say which traveler reaches that shore.

## 2 — cowardly Vigilant

The strongest current candidate is **Joshua**, but this remains a candidate rather than a resolved mapping.

Joshua's exact final note directly says:
- he had believed he could face any vampire;
- when confronted by “her,” he could not even stand his ground;
- he ran;
- he abandoned his companions;
- he frames himself as a failed mortal servant of Stendarr.

That is a close thematic match for “a cowardly Vigilant sinks into the darkness.”

Against a high-confidence identification:
- the vision never names Joshua;
- Joshua's note does not use the book's “sinks into darkness” language;
- the following “hollow soul” line is not structurally tied to Joshua in the current persisted extraction.

Current classification:
**Joshua = plausible stanza-2 Vigilant candidate, medium-low confidence.**

## 2 — hollow soul and red Stone

The second sentence says a hollow soul sees in the red Stone a shadow of what it once was.

Potentially relevant VIGILANT systems include:
- Soul-Shriven / hollowed Coldharbour identities;
- the recurring Stone itinerary;
- red/burning Stone imagery in Marukh's visions;
- identity loss and reconstructed bodies.

No current record uniquely binds those systems to one named actor here.

Keep this clause unresolved.

## 3 — red woman, sacrificed child, inherited curse

The stanza describes:
- a red woman sacrificing her child;
- a mother being cursed;
- the curse passing mother → child;
- innocence increasing susceptibility;
- a tainted child sinking into darkness;
- the child being given over to Kyne.

This resembles several VIGILANT motifs but no current chain satisfies every clause.

Relevant but insufficient parallels include:
- Lamae/Blood Matron maternal and blood-curse imagery;
- the *Lamae's Kin* book's bloodborne curse and infected child case;
- repeated child-sacrifice/innocence motifs;
- Kyne-related return/salvation imagery.

Do **not** identify the red woman as Lamae solely from the blood/mother vocabulary.

Likewise, do not identify the tainted child with the unnamed 2E 582 Lake Ilinalta boy: the *Lamae's Kin* report does not say he was given to Kyne or that his curse descended directly from his mother.

## Relationship to A Vision in Malada

Both Marukh visions use compressed Stone/calamity language, but they should not be mechanically merged stanza-for-stanza.

*A Vision in Malada* currently has several strongly mapped referents because later dialogue reproduces its details.

*City of Stone* is less resolved and should remain a separate prophecy/evidence map until more direct scene or script evidence appears.
