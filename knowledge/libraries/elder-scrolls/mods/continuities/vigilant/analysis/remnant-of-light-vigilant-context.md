## Direct narrative

The translated Ayleid tract says:

- **Anumaril** gives **Lord Filestis** the **Remnant of Light**;
- Filestis is asked to take it toward the cold western/sunset limit of Tamriel;
- Filestis departs with his people and is followed or pursued by Ayleid emigrants;
- they reach a land of **Cold Rocks**;
- the Remnant makes the inhospitable land productive and produces healing **food-stone**;
- the emigrants raise or raze a mountain/pinnacle so the Remnant can illuminate the wider land;
- after Filestis dies, his helpmeet brings him to the Remnant;
- Filestis then becomes **strong-with-radiance** and “Danced for eight more choruses.”

The translation deliberately preserves uncertain alternatives such as “minute or eon,” “clan or livestock,” and “raised or razed.” Lorekeeper should retain those uncertainties rather than silently selecting one reading.
