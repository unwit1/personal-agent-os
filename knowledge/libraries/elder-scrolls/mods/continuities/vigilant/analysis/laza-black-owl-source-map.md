# Laza and the Black Owl — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 021E7B15` — **Laza and the Black Owl**

Exact text:
- `books/021E7B15-laza-and-the-black-owl.txt`

## Scene

Laza meets the **Black Owl** at the **fountain of Kyne**.

By this point Laza has **bloody eye sockets**, directly continuing the Gray Owl's blinding instructions.

## Ritual instruction

The Black Owl tells Laza to:

- plunge a dagger into the fountain;
- wash away the **Nedic maiden's blood**;
- lose the shepherd's staff;
- grow fangs;
- taint the fountain's waters;
- remove Kyne;
- move one step closer to those Laza lost.

This is the most explicit corruptive act in the three Owl books.

## Direct transformation structure

The source presents:

**blinded Laza → dagger into Kyne's fountain → staff lost → fangs gained → fountain tainted → Kyne gone → closer to the lost**

This directly reconnects the Black Owl episode to the White Owl's shepherd/wolf imagery:
- staff = shepherd;
- fangs = wolf.

## Nedic maiden

The Black Owl specifically refers to **the Nedic maiden's blood**.

The text does not name the maiden.

Do not identify her with:
- Alessia;
- Mary/Moura;
- Lamae;
- another named Nedic woman

without separate evidence.

## Kyne boundary

The line says **Kyne will be gone** if the act is performed.

The exact meaning remains unresolved:
- departure;
- displacement;
- removal from the fountain;
- symbolic severance;
- destruction of a local manifestation.

Do not normalize it into literal divine death without corroboration.

## Black Owl identity boundary

Later Vicn material provides strong continuity evidence for **Black Owl → Jhunal the Black**.

Within this exact VIGILANT book, the speaker is only **Black Owl**.

Preserve the later identity separately.
