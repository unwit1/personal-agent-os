# A Vision in Malada — evidence map

Continuity: tes.mod.vigilant
Source: BOOK 02054ED0 / zzzCHBookVisionAtMalada

This page treats the thirteen numbered visions as a cross-story prophecy/index and maps them only where the ESM provides concrete echoes. A repeated image is not automatically proof of identity. Confidence applies to the referent of the stanza, not to the objective truth of Marukh's vision.

## 1 — the blood that rusts Arkay's scythe

Current mapping: Lamae Bal — high confidence.

The stanza begins with a woman's pure blood being taken, says blood cannot be stopped by Arkay's scythe, and describes her love answering her tears with a dagger.

VIGILANT independently gives Lamae the exact emotional image:
- INFO 0203C193 — Lamae asks whether the listener will answer her love with a dagger to the heart.
- INFO 0203BC24 — Molag Bal says Arkay will never touch the pair after their union/baptism.
- INFO 0203AE23 — Facis describes the successful release of Lamae's soul back into Arkay's embrace.
- the Blood Matron quest line explicitly frames Lamae's vampirism as a Molag Bal curse.

## 2 — burned pilgrim → burned priest → dimmed Auri-El

Current mapping: unresolved — low confidence.

The ESM contains multiple pilgrimages, burned priests, Alessian burnings, and anti-Auri-El rhetoric, but no unique character currently satisfies all three clauses. Relevant context includes Alessian pilgrimages to Mathmalatu, the blood-pilgrimage material in Maram/Aria's notes, Belharza's anti-Auri-El dialogue, and the Order's history of burning priests/servants.

## 3 — father, mother, daughter

Current mapping: unresolved — low confidence.

The stanza presents a three-person family succession through the Stone and says their blood grows hot while their hearts grow cold. VIGILANT has several cursed family structures, but none presently provides a deterministic father → mother → daughter Stone chain. Do not collapse this into the Bruiants: their child is Julius, not a daughter.

## 4 — bard, mad cat king, red mist

Current mapping: Dro'Zel — high confidence.

Three independent signals align: Dro'Zel is repeatedly identified as the mad king of Senchal; The Mad King memory is organized around a mysterious bard's story; and INFO 0212B2EA in Madness says the speaker repeatedly kills the returning bard and repeatedly covers the bard's hometown in red mist.

## 5 — inquisitor, Mara, and the firewood

Current mapping: Mary/Moura + Inquisitor Pepe — high confidence.

The same language recurs directly:
- INFO 0212D057 — memory-Pepe says the people will willingly place firewood at his victim's feet before burning her.
- BOOK 02054ED7 / Judgement says the masses willingly gathered firewood for a Mara follower condemned by the Alessian Order.
- Mary/Moura is described as a Mara healer killed by the Alessians during the Thrassian plague.
- Pepe later asks himself whether everything went wrong when he burned Mary.

The exact identity relationship among Mara, Mary/Moura, and the memory apparition remains part of VIGILANT's deliberately unstable saint-history.

## 6 — grave keeper and returning grave robber

Current mapping: Melus Petilius — high confidence.

The later NPC literally named Gravekeeper is not the best match. In QUST 021C1EA1 / Paladin Melus, Melus says in INFO 02332600 that the graverobber always returns no matter how many times he crushes his skull; INFO 02332601 repeats the image. That near-verbatim action is stronger evidence than the unrelated title/name Gravekeeper.

## 7 — jump and bottomless darkness

Current mapping: The Falling Khajiit cluster — medium-low confidence.

BOOK 02114FF7 / The Falling Khajiit contains both core images: Ja'zhan's cousin commits suicide by jumping from a tower/building, while the prefatory Khenarthi saying describes a soul falling deeper and deeper into a bottomless abyss. The book does not explicitly say the cousin possessed the Stone or explain the stanza's female pronoun and subsequent transfer.

## 8 — one brother kills another; Stone burns the survivor

Current mapping: unresolved — low confidence.

VIGILANT contains several important brother pairs, especially Simon/Tlass around Johan, but no recovered line yet gives the complete sequence of murder by a brother in a square, the survivor taking the Stone, and the Stone burning his hand.

## 9 — brother killed by a slave trader; fiery eyes; gate

Current mapping: unnamed fiery-eyed slave-trader figure — **high confidence for the role, unresolved personal identity**.

The prophecy itself directly states that the stanza-8 brother is killed by an unnamed slave trader; a gate opens; the burning Stone looks into the trader's eyes; and he steps through the gate.

INFO 0212F271 independently strengthens that identification: Pepe remembers another living person emerging from the sarcophagus before the player, believes he was a slave trader, and says the man's eyes burned like fire. The eight Slave Trader notes form a deliberately cryptic trail through Act 4 locations/events.

What remains unresolved is not stanza 9's role, but the trader's personal name/base actor and the exact ownership/acquisition chain for all eight notes.

The documentary sequence and known placements are reconstructed separately in `analysis/slave-trader-note-trail.md`; the normalized identity node is `entities/characters/slave-trader.md`.

## 10 — Vigilant trades love for a false heartbeat

Current mapping: Jacob and Rahel — high confidence for the bargain.

INFO 0200EA82 / Old Regrets is almost a prose expansion: Jacob says he was defeated and dying; Molag Bal offered to revive him; the price was his wife's soul; Jacob accepted and regrets it. That is a direct analogue for a Vigilant sacrificing his love and receiving a false heartbeat.

The clause that she became the Stone and was thrown into the north is not stated so literally. Rahel becomes a major Molag-Bal-associated figure and the story occurs in Skyrim, but that Stone-identity step remains an inference.

## 11 — jealous woman, flames, and a child following

Current mapping: possible Julia and Julius Bruiant — medium-low confidence.

The Bruiant tragedy supplies several matching elements: Marcus's infidelity precedes Julia becoming cold and leaving with Julius; Julia's recovered corpse is supernaturally burned; Julius disappears with/follows his mother and returns changed; the mansion guide has a Blood Curse of Jealousy; and Julius's endgame is associated with consuming flames. No recovered Julia line says she swallowed the Stone, so this remains thematic rather than settled.

## 12 — the nameless man brings the Stone to the Temple

Current mapping: Altano — high confidence.

Altano's journal begins with him waking in a snowfield with no memory of his name or origin. In Old Regrets, after defeating Bal, Altano asks for Bal's Mace and says he will return to the Temple to consult the Keeper. Reyda later asserts that the Stone is Molag Bal and recognizes Stone-contact by the fire behind the player's eyes. Altano's betrayal culminates in the Temple's destruction, and the mod later contains the Horn of Stendarr (Rusty).

The prophecy's Stone and the carried Mace are not mechanically proven to be the same record, but the nameless-man → Temple → failed transcendence → rusted-horn sequence maps strongly onto Altano.

## 13 — reject twice, accept on the third; hunger and a pale helping hand

Current mapping: player/Prisoner under Molag Bal's temptation — medium confidence.

The motifs converge repeatedly around the player:
- The Endless Fall: Molag Bal explicitly reacts to the player turning down his merciful offer.
- Child of Oblivion: the objective makes the player choose death in Julius's flames or accept Molag Bal's corruption.
- Molag Bal calls himself bread for the starving, offers salvation, and says he will keep extending his helping hand.
- the mansion escape includes a command to go through the gate.
- Pepe later tells the player that they have held and swallowed the Stone, even once being more than enough.
- Aetherius dialogue calls the Stone the key to the gates of the soul.

The ESM does not yet prove the exact arithmetic—two prior refusals followed by a third acceptance—as one deterministic route. That likely requires the external quest-fragment PEX logic or a more complete branch-state reconstruction.

## Structural interpretation

The book is best treated as a prophetic Stone itinerary through VIGILANT's stories, not simply a First Era chronicle. Several stanzas appear to point forward into Fourth Era Vigilant events, and stanza 13 appears player-facing.

Future work should focus on stanzas 2, 3, 7–9, 11, and 13, preferring scene/PEX evidence over semantic resemblance.
