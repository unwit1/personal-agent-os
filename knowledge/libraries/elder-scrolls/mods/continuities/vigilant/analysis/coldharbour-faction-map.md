# VIGILANT faction and institution relationship map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

This page separates historical institutions, contemporary pre-Coldharbour organizations, and Act 4 Oblivion factions so Lorekeeper does not flatten every religious or armored group into one chronology.

## Contemporary Vigilant frame

### Vigilants of Stendarr

Narrative role:
- player-entry institution;
- Altano's organization;
- anti-Daedric/vampire investigative frame;
- moral language of mercy and judgment.

Altano's betrayal and the Temple disaster help break the story out of the ordinary Vigilant frame and toward Coldharbour.

## Alessian historical memory

### Alessian Order

Broad institution associated with:
- Marukh;
- Pelan;
- saints/inquisitors;
- monasteries and courts;
- Malada;
- Mary/Moura persecution;
- Belharza conflict.

VIGILANT reconstructs this history through memory, hagiography, ruins, relics, and surviving/repeated figures.

### Marukhati Selective

Best modeled as a radical development within the wider Alessian tradition.

Distinctive markers:
- Fervidius Tharn official texts;
- Proper-Life;
- anti-Aldmeri purification of Akatosh;
- Staff of Towers / Middle Dawn concepts;
- Selective armor;
- Amicus Tharn's ideology.

Do not label every Alessian as a Selective.

### Alessian Inquisition

Best modeled as the judicial/doctrinal-enforcement apparatus within the wider Alessian tradition.

Key anchors:
- Inquisitor Pepe / Pelan;
- *The Grand Inquisitor*;
- Narfin's Inquisition Court;
- First and Second Inquisition Courts;
- *Judgement*;
- Mary/Moura persecution.

This is distinct from the Selective: **Inquisition = enforcement machinery; Selective = radical theological current.**

### Brothers of Marukh

Best modeled as a Marukhite religious-military community within the wider Alessian tradition.

Key anchors:
- Abbot Cosmas;
- licensed *Cleansing of the Fane* substrate;
- Malada;
- Holy Brothers of Marukh Priory;
- Marukh's Underground Priory.

This group should not be collapsed with the Inquisition or Marukhati Selective merely because all are Alessian/Marukhite.

## Parallel historical martial tradition

### Order of Diagna

Represented through:
- Gaiden Shinji;
- Aredhel and Blade of Diagna equipment;
- Judo of the Order of Diagna;
- crafting bridges.

This is not structurally part of the Alessian Order.

The traditions overlap in Coldharbour geography and encounters because Act 4 collects figures from many historical layers.

## Coldharbour regime

### Molag Bal's domain

Molag Bal is the controlling Daedric power behind Coldharbour and is described by Pepe as the “playwright” of the tragedy.

VIGILANT's Coldharbour contains:
- captured/reconstructed souls;
- historical figures;
- Alessian ruins and institutions;
- memory bodies;
- soul-consuming defensive infrastructure.

This should not be modeled as a normal political faction with uniform membership.

It is better treated as a **domination regime / realm structure**.

Detailed reconstruction: `analysis/coldharbour-regime.md`.

## Invading Order

### Forces of Order

Associated with:
- Jyggalag;
- the Greymarch quest;
- Silver Cocoon;
- Throne of Order;
- Order army/knight systems;
- Saklas's mining testimony.

Current conflict:

**Forces of Order invade Coldharbour ↔ Molag Bal's regime resists them**

Pepe says Molag Bal consumes souls to sustain a barrier against Order.

This opposition does not make either side morally aligned with the player.

## Soul-Shriven are not one historical faction

VIGILANT uses:
- `FACT 025738DF` / Soul-Shriven;
- Soul-Shriven abilities;
- `QUST 0251532C` / Feral Soul-Shriven;
- `ACTI 0251532D` / Soul-Shriven trigger system.

Named historical identities can appear through this system, including Judo and multiple Tharns.

Lorekeeper should distinguish:

**Soul-Shriven = ontological/gameplay state or encounter class**

from:

**Order of Diagna / House Tharn / Alessian tradition = historical or institutional identity**

A character can belong to both layers without the Soul-Shriven system becoming that historical faction.

Detailed encounter-system reconstruction: `analysis/feral-soul-shriven-system.md`.

## Alessian Sleeper is also a form-class

Pelan/Pepe and other forms use **Alessian Sleeper Race**.

The race/body class helps encode transformation, but shared race implementation alone does not prove common political membership or one historical unit.

Model it primarily as:
- transformed body class;
- Alessian-associated manifestation type.

## Simplified relationship graph

### Historical/institutional

- **Marukh → source/founder tradition → Alessian Order**
- **Alessian Order → contains enforcement arm → Alessian Inquisition**
- **Alessian Order → contains Marukhite community → Brothers of Marukh**
- **Alessian Order → later radical development → Marukhati Selective**
- **Fervidius Tharn → Arch-Prelate → Marukhati Selective**
- **Amicus Tharn → VIGILANT ideologue aligned with → Selective**
- **Order of Diagna → separate martial tradition → Gaiden / Judo / Aredhel cluster**
- **Vigilants of Stendarr → separate Fourth Era institution → Altano / player**

### Act 4

- **Molag Bal → dominates → Coldharbour**
- **Forces of Order → invade → Coldharbour**
- **Molag Bal regime ↔ wars with ↔ Forces of Order**
- **Alessian ruins/figures → embedded within → Coldharbour**
- **Diagna figures → embedded within → Coldharbour**
- **Soul-Shriven system → can instantiate → named historical identities**

## Temporal caution

Act 4 should not be read as evidence that:
- Gaiden Shinji historically served alongside Pelan;
- Judo served the Alessian Order;
- the Vigilants of Stendarr existed in the First Era;
- Fervidius personally encountered Amicus in the depicted spaces;
- every Coldharbour NPC belongs to Molag Bal politically.

Coldharbour collapses/reconstructs people, memories, relics, and institutions from different periods.

## Canon boundary

Several institutions originate in established Elder Scrolls lore:
- Vigilants of Stendarr;
- Alessian Order;
- Marukhati Selective;
- Order of Diagna;
- Jyggalag and Forces of Order;
- Soul-Shriven.

VIGILANT's specific cross-era encounters, faction relationships, Coldharbour war, character memberships, and institutional afterlives remain mod-continuity implementation unless independently corroborated.
