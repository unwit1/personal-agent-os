# Ja'zhan testimony trilogy

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Direct books:
- `BOOK 02054ED4` — **What Khajiit Saw**
- `BOOK 02054ED5` — **What Khajiit Heard**
- `BOOK 02054ED6` — **What Khajiit Said**
- author on all three: **Ja'zhan**

## Function of the trilogy

The three texts form a coherent progression:

1. **Saw** — domestic/private cruelty toward an adopted child.
2. **Heard** — aristocratic/Alessian cruelty turned into spectacle.
3. **Said** — Ja'zhan's explicit rejection of a doctrine of harmony that accepts innocent suffering.

The sequence therefore moves from witness/anecdote to historical accusation to moral conclusion.

## Child-victim motif

The books repeatedly center very young victims.

This connects Ja'zhan's writing to VIGILANT's larger concern with:
- innocence used as sacrificial material;
- religious systems rationalizing pain;
- the Alessian Order's violence;
- the moral insufficiency of abstract salvation when individual victims are ignored.

The trilogy should be retrieved alongside:
- Mary/Moura burning testimony;
- Alessian Inquisition material;
- Malada/Marukhati history;
- Ja'zhan's own character dossier.

## What Khajiit Saw

The book attacks the contradiction between:
- public benevolence/culture;
- private abuse.

The unnamed five-year-old Khajiiti child is not currently linked to another named character.

Do not infer that she is:
- Mary/Moura;
- Martha;
- Lilian;
- Lalanoah;
- another later Vicn child

without direct evidence.

## What Khajiit Heard

The unnamed Alessian knight:
- served through the cleansing of Malada;
- retired wealthy;
- ruled an estate with hundreds of subjects;
- kept many hounds and armed kennel guards;
- had a nine-year-old servant child hunted to death after the child accidentally injured a hound.

This is direct in-universe testimony attributed to Ja'zhan.

The knight's personal identity is unresolved.

## What Khajiit Said

The final text rejects Alessian “harmony” because the tears of tortured children remain unheard and unpaid.

The three images of children:
- cold confinement;
- red mist;
- hanging/rope

may deliberately echo separate historical episodes.

Only the first clearly repeats the child from *What Khajiit Saw*.

The red-mist and rope referents should remain open unless separately resolved.
