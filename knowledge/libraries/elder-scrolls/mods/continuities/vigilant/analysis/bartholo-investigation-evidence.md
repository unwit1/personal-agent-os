# Bartholo's investigation — evidence map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

- `BOOK 0204C5CD` — **Bartholo's Journal**

Exact text:

## Assignment

Bartholo records that Marcus Bruiant visits the Temple of Stendarr on 8 Morning Star, 4E 201 asking for help with the mansion's deaths.

Keeper Thorondir thinks the incidents may be coincidences.

Bartholo disagrees strongly enough to investigate.

This establishes an early split between:
- institutional skepticism;
- Bartholo's suspicion of supernatural causation.

## Quiet period

A month after arriving, Bartholo says no new accidents have occurred.

He considers the possibility that:
- his fears were unfounded;
- or hostile spirits/Daedra are hiding because of his presence.

This is explicitly uncertainty in his own reasoning.

## Magicka trace and basement totem

After Lamthor, Patricia, and Summil die, Bartholo says he detects faint Magicka around the bodies.

He follows the traces to a basement storeroom and finds a hideous totem.

Direct observation:
- magical traces;
- physical totem;
- powerful curse on the totem.

Bartholo's interpretation:
- the totem is probably associated with Molag Bal;
- the curse resembles ancient Ayleid curses;
- the totem caused the mansion's misfortune.

The first layer is observational evidence; the second is expert attribution.

## Failed dispelling

Bartholo initially believes he successfully lifted the curse.

He reports:
- the totem rusts;
- its magical power appears to fade;
- dispelling it nearly exhausts him;
- he estimates four or five more similar totems may exist.

The next day he realizes the dispelling was incomplete.

The totem's glow returns, an eerie sound begins, and Bartholo feels the curse approaching.

## Spatial collapse / entrapment

In his final entry Bartholo says:
- doors and windows disappear;
- the environment fades into thick fog;
- there is no route out;
- he believes himself trapped.

This is direct testimony of a localized reality/space failure from Bartholo's perspective.

It does not independently explain whether:
- the mansion physically changed;
- he entered a pocket space;
- he was hallucinating;
- a curse altered perception.

## Caster-cost inference

Bartholo says the curse's hatred and power imply the caster is paying a high price.

That is a magical inference, not direct observation of the caster.

Do not convert it into proof of:
- who cast the curse;
- what price was paid;
- whether Julia, Julius, Bal, or another actor created it.

## Source hierarchy

For the mansion curse:

1. **Bartholo's observations** — Magicka traces, totem, curse, failed dispel, fog/entrapment.
2. **Bartholo's interpretations** — Molag Bal association, Ayleid similarity, number of remaining totems, caster cost.
3. **Later quest/dialogue** — may corroborate or reinterpret those conclusions.

This separation is important because Bartholo is competent but not omniscient.
