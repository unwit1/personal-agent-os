# Judgement — source and doctrine map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

## Core case

The document asks why the Alessian Order:
- burned a follower of Mara who cured a boy infected with the Thrassian plague;
- hanged the boy who was cured;
- did **not** execute Archmage Syrabane for treating the plague with the Warlock's Ring.

Its answer is institutional authorization.

According to the text:
- a miracle without Alessian permission is heresy;
- the Mara healer lacked permission;
- the healed boy is condemned because his cure came through unauthorized religious action;
- Syrabane's treatment is accepted because the Order approved the use of the Warlock's Ring.

## Institutional principle

The document gives a very clear rule:

**outcome does not determine legitimacy; authorization does.**

A successful cure can still be heresy if performed outside Alessian authority.

This makes *Judgement* one of the strongest direct documents for how VIGILANT portrays Alessian religious control.

## Popular participation

The text says:
- citizens willingly gathered firewood for the healer's execution;
- citizens threw stones at the boy on the gallows;
- this collective participation proves the Order's righteousness.

That reasoning belongs to the document's ideology.

It should be recorded as:
- **Order claim:** public participation validates the judgment.

It should not be normalized into:
- the punishment was actually just;
- the crowd acted freely;
- the crowd's behavior proves consensus in any neutral sense.

## Mary / Moura relationship

The document does **not** name the healer.

However, it closely parallels the wider Mary/Moura cluster:
- follower of Mara;
- healing a plague victim;
- condemnation by the Alessian Order;
- death by burning;
- Thrassian plague context.

Independent VIGILANT sources explicitly connect Mary/Moura to those elements.

Current status:
- **Judgement healer ↔ Mary/Moura: strong parallel / likely same historical persecution tradition**
- **exact identity from this book alone: unresolved**

Do not use *Judgement* as independent name confirmation.

## Syrabane and the Warlock's Ring

The text invokes:
- Archmage **Syrabane**;
- the **Warlock's Ring**;
- Alessian recognition of that ring as a relic associated with St. Alessia.

These references function inside the document's legal argument.

The book does not prove that every historical detail in its explanation is neutral or accurate.
