Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

- `BOOK 0204C5A8` — **Marcus' Final Note**

## 4E 199 — Markarth affair and exploitation

Marcus repeatedly travels to Markarth, which he calls the **City of Stone**.

He records:
- infidelity against Julia;
- paying a Khajiit slaver 10,000 gold for the woman he was seeing;
- expelling her after learning she was pregnant;
- later being forced by the Jarl to support her financially and make a large civic payment.

The woman is never named in these journals.

Do not identify her with Julia, a known witch, or a later Stone-linked woman without additional evidence.

## 4E 200 — family disappearance and mansion deterioration

Marcus records:
- Julia and Julius disappear on 9 Sun's Height;
- Julius is found alone in the forest ten days later, unable to speak;
- Julia remains missing;
- Jacob later brings Julia's ring and says her burned body was found in Lake Ilinalta.

The separate Ilinalta report independently corroborates Julia's identification and abnormal corpse condition.

Marcus then records a series of deaths in the mansion:
- Heather falls to her death;
- Clifford drowns;
- M'zaq dies in a bizarre gardening accident.

He also begins hearing something crawling through the halls.

## Bal enters the household

On 28 Frostfall, 4E 200 Marcus hires a Wild Elf butler named **Bal**.

He notes that Julius becomes unusually attached to him.

The journal itself does not identify Bal as Molag Bal.

Keep:
- **Bal the butler**
- **Molag Bal**
as related but distinct until direct source evidence merges them.

## Red Stone nightmare

On 29 Evening Star, 4E 200 Marcus describes a recurring nightmare:

- a room of rust and blood;
- Julius with eyes blazing like fire;
- Julia placing a **single red stone** into Marcus's hand;
- the stone burning his arm to ash.

This is direct dream content.

It is not proof that historical Julia literally handed Marcus the Stone.

The imagery should be co-retrieved with:
- red Stone material;
- fiery-eye motifs;
- Julia/Julius;
- Bruiant Mansion curse analysis.

## 4E 201 — Bartholo's arrival and renewed deaths

Marcus seeks help from the Temple of Stendarr.

He records:
- Bartholo being assigned;
- roughly a month of peace;
- Julius avoiding Bartholo;
- Lamthor being killed by frenzied dogs;
- Patricia's apparent suicide;
- Summil attacking Marcus and later dying;
- crawling sounds returning.

Marcus notices Bartholo becoming exhausted and withdrawn.

His perspective does not reveal the cause.

Bartholo's journal supplies the separate investigator layer.

## Final note

Marcus's final note says:
- Julius is **no longer human**;
- Julius is a **Child of Oblivion**;
- Marcus blames himself.

This is Marcus's conclusion at the end of the documentary trail.

It should not by itself define the precise metaphysical transformation mechanism.

## Reliability model

Marcus is useful because his journals are:
- dated;
- first-person;
- continuous across the escalation.

But he is not omniscient.

Direct:
- his own affair and payments;
- household disappearances/deaths as he observed them;
- Jacob's visit as Marcus remembers it;
- Bal's employment;
- the nightmare;
- Bartholo's arrival;
- Marcus's final belief about Julius.

Unresolved/inferred:
- cause of each servant death;
- identity of the crawling presence;
- Bal = Molag Bal;
- whether the dream is prophetic, induced, or symbolic;
- exact mechanism making Julius a Child of Oblivion.
