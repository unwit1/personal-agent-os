# VIGILANT narrative structure — record-derived pass

This reconstruction uses QUST numbering, objectives, dialogue ownership, and the two Spinner's Needle quests rather than an external walkthrough.

## Vigilant / Altano sequence

The contiguous `zzzAoMMq00`–`zzzAoMMq10` family follows:

`Vigilant of Stendarr → Bloodsucker → He Who Cannot Be Touched → Lazy Afternoon → The Eye of Madness → Dine and Dash → Thus Spoke Khajiit → Old Regrets → No Mercy → The Endless Fall → The Landing`

The player is recruited by Altano, investigates vampires and Daedra, pursues a summoner, confronts increasingly coercive interpretations of Stendarr's mercy, obtains Bal's mace, is ordered to kill a witch and her child, and ultimately confronts Altano after his betrayal. The optional `Art of Mercy` quest revisits consequences if Carene and Lilian survive.

## Blood Matron sequence

The numbered `zzzBMMq01`–`03` chain is:

`Empty Cells → Remnants → The Blood Matron`

The final quest explicitly progresses from defeating Lamae Bal to freeing her from Molag Bal's curse.

## Bruiant Mansion / Child of Oblivion

`zzzCOMq01` / **Child of Oblivion** begins with Gwyneth briefing the player on the missing Vigilant Bartholo and the Bruiant family. Its objectives proceed through the mansion investigation, defeating Julius, and escape.

## Act-number evidence

`Spinner's Needle 1` and `Spinner's Needle 2` both contain an optional objective explicitly labeled **Skip to Act 4**. One route targets Altano and the other targets Bal at the Bruiant Mansion; both return the player to Orlando and then direct the player into the wasteland/dream layer.

This makes the Coldharbour transition an explicit Act 4 boundary in the ESM. Treating the three major pre-Coldharbour clusters above as the preceding acts is strongly supported, though exact act tags for every side quest still need transition-script verification.

## Emerging through-lines

**Mercy versus coercion:** Altano repeatedly describes killing as Stendarr's mercy, while the quest structure includes refusal, negotiation, and consequence paths.

**Hunger, bread, salvation, domination:** Molag Bal presents submission as rescue from suffering/hunger/despair. Marukh calls Adabal "bread for the starving," closely echoing Molag Bal's rhetoric. This is a thematic link, not yet proof of metaphysical identity.

**Memory and unstable identity:** Altano begins without a remembered past; Orlando opens ownerless bloody dreams; Coldharbour uses memory variants; Molag Bal discusses modifying memory and repeating dreams; Belharza describes Morihaus and the player as having unstable forms.

**History as testimony:** Pelinal, Morihaus, Belharza, Alessia, Marukh, and Molag Bal are presented through conflicting memories and speakers. Claim provenance must remain attached.
