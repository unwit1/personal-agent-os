## Core testimony

Ja'zhan condemns abusive Cyrodiilic nobles who foster Khajiiti children while presenting themselves as cultured and benevolent.

He describes one specific victim:
- a five-year-old Khajiiti girl;
- repeatedly beaten;
- locked outside in a latrine during winter;
- humiliated and forced to consume her own waste.

The foster parents remain unnamed.

## Source perspective

This is Ja'zhan's authored testimony and moral judgment.

High-confidence source-local facts:
- Ja'zhan presents the described abuse as a concrete case;
- the victim is a five-year-old Khajiiti child;
- the abuse is framed as hidden beneath respectable noble status.

Unresolved:
- identity of the child;
- identity of the foster parents;
- exact date/location;
- whether Ja'zhan witnessed every act personally or learned part of it through another channel.
