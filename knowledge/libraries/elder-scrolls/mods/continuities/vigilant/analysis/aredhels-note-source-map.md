# Aredhel's Note — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 02038AAD` — **Aredhel's Note**

Exact text:
- `books/02038AAD-aredhels-note.txt`

Structural association:
- carried in the death list of early **Aredhel** (`zzzBMCorruptVigilant05`) in the Blood Matron questline.

## Direct claims

Aredhel says:
- **Jacob was killed in battle**;
- **Joshua fled in terror**;
- the remaining Vigilants surrendered to the **Blood Matron**;
- the Blood Matron shared her blood with them;
- Aredhel alone accepted it willingly;
- the others still resisted their new bloodthirst;
- those resistors might be disposed of;
- the Blood Matron's steward **Bal** granted them a temporary reprieve;
- they have until the Blood Matron's next awakening;
- Aredhel intends to persuade the others to accept the blood.

This is first-person testimony from a participant.

## Conflict with the official Windhelm report

The Windhelm report later records Jacob found alive outside the city walls.

Aredhel says he was killed.

The conflict is direct and should remain explicit.

Possible models include:
- literal death followed by revival;
- near-death perceived as death;
- incomplete/false official reporting;
- unreliable or distorted memory.

Jacob's later Molag Bal bargain and supplementary authorial commentary make revival plausible, but **Aredhel's note itself does not explain the contradiction**.

## Joshua corroboration

Aredhel says Joshua fled.

Joshua's own Final Note independently says:
- he abandoned his companions;
- he ran because he could not stand before “her.”

This makes:
**Aredhel → Joshua fled**
one of the best-corroborated claims in the note.

## Blood Matron blood

Aredhel distinguishes herself from the other captured Vigilants.

She says:
- all were given Blood Matron blood;
- she accepted willingly;
- the others resisted.

This directly explains Aredhel's early corrupted-Vigilant identity.

It also shows the corruption was not experienced uniformly:
- Aredhel embraces it;
- the others continue resisting.

## Bal as steward

Aredhel explicitly calls **Bal** the Blood Matron's **steward**.

This is an important direct role statement.

It does not by itself prove:
- Bal = Molag Bal;
- Bal = the later Bruiant Mansion butler;
- Bal = every other Bal-labelled form.

Keep the Windhelm/Blood Matron steward role source-local unless another record merges identities.

## Awakening cycle

Aredhel says the group has until the Blood Matron's **next awakening**.

This pairs naturally with Jacob's warning that opening the Maiden Statue means **“her awakening.”**

Together, the two documents create a strong awakening-cycle motif, but neither explicitly supplies a full mechanical schedule or identity map.
