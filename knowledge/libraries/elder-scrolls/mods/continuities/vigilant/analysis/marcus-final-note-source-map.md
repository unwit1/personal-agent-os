# Marcus' Final Note — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 0204C5A8` — **Marcus' Final Note**

Exact text:
- `books/0204C5A8-marcus-final-note.txt`

## Direct statement

Marcus says:
- he cannot stop crying;
- all he has left is hate;
- Julius is **no longer human**;
- Julius is a **Child of Oblivion**;
- Marcus believes the situation is his fault;
- Marcus says he must take responsibility.

## What it establishes

This is the clearest surviving statement of Marcus's final understanding of Julius.

Direct:
- Marcus believes Julius has ceased to be human;
- Marcus uses the title **Child of Oblivion**;
- Marcus blames himself.

The note does not explain:
- what exactly transformed Julius;
- what Marcus intends to do as “responsibility”;
- whether Marcus's conclusion is metaphysically precise;
- whether the phrase Child of Oblivion is a formal category, a quest title, or Marcus's own label.

## Emotional contrast

Earlier Marcus repeatedly notes that he cannot cry over deaths around him.

The final note reverses that pattern:
- now he cannot stop crying;
- grief and hate coexist.

This is useful character evidence but should not be turned into a supernatural mechanic unless another source establishes one.

## Relationship to quest structure

The main mansion quest later uses **Child of Oblivion** as the title for the Julius-centered crisis and contains Julius-specific forms.

That strongly supports the note's terminology as deliberate implementation language.

Still, the note itself remains Marcus's perspective, not an omniscient explanation of Julius's ontology.
