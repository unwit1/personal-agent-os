## Direct warning

Jacob addresses an unknown future reader and says that if the **Maiden Statue** opens, then **“her awakening”** has occurred.

He warns the reader:
- never to approach her;
- not to look at her;
- not to listen to her voice.

He describes the awakened figure as:
- the **defiler of Arkay**;
- a **mistake of Stendarr**;
- capable of dragging victims to **Blood Mist Castle**;
- associated there with the **Lord of Corruption**.

These are Jacob's direct claims.

## What the note establishes

The note strongly establishes that Jacob left the Windhelm expedition with knowledge of a threat far larger than the official report records.

The official Windhelm report only says:
- the Maiden Statue was tied to vampire attacks;
- an expedition entered;
- the route collapsed/sealed;
- Jacob later appeared outside the city.

Jacob's note adds:
- a specifically gendered awakened figure;
- sensory danger from sight/voice;
- Blood Mist Castle;
- Lord of Corruption language.

This difference is important evidence that the public/official record is incomplete relative to Jacob's private knowledge.

## Identity boundary

The note never names **“her.”**

Later VIGILANT evidence strongly links the Windhelm disaster to:
- Blood Matron material;
- Lamae's cluster;
- Aredhel;
- Joshua;
- Jacob's Molag Bal bargain.

But this note alone does **not** prove:
- “her” = Lamae Bal;
- “her” = the Blood Matron;
- “her” = any specific later NPC form.

Keep the identity unresolved at the note level.

## Lord of Corruption boundary

The phrase **Lord of Corruption** is strongly compatible with Molag Bal within the surrounding VIGILANT storyline.

However, the note uses the title rather than the proper name.

Current best practice:
- **Jacob note → Lord of Corruption**
- wider storyline → strong Molag Bal association
- do not rewrite the note as if it literally said “Molag Bal.”

## Blood Mist Castle

The note explicitly names **Blood Mist Castle** as the destination to which the awakened figure can drag victims.

This makes Blood Mist Castle part of Jacob's threat model and should be indexed with:
- Maiden Statue;
- Blood Matron;
- vampire expedition;
- Jacob;
- Lord of Corruption.

## Reliability
