# Julia's Letter to Marcus — source map

Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

Primary source:
- `BOOK 02054ECF` — **Julia's Letter to Marcus**

Exact text:
- `books/02054ECF-julias-letter-to-marcus.txt`

## Direct statement

Julia tells Marcus:
- she is leaving him that day;
- she is taking Julius with her;
- goodbye.

The message is short, unambiguous, and first-person.

## What it establishes

The letter directly establishes Julia's **intentional departure** from Marcus with Julius.

That is important because Marcus later records Julia and Julius as having **disappeared**.

From Marcus's perspective they vanish.

From Julia's surviving letter, at least the initial departure was deliberate.

Therefore the chronology should distinguish:

**Julia chooses to leave with Julius → later Julius is found alone → Julia is later found dead under supernatural conditions**

The supernatural crisis begins somewhere after or during that departure, not necessarily before Julia left the mansion.

## Placement

The VIGILANT placement index places Julia's letter in:

- **Hidden Room** (`CELL 0204F6C8`)

This is useful environmental evidence that the letter is intentionally discoverable in a concealed mansion-related space.

Placement does not tell us:
- who hid it;
- when it was hidden;
- whether Marcus ever read it;
- whether Julia left it for Marcus to find immediately.

## What the letter does not establish

The letter gives no reason for leaving.

It does not explicitly mention:
- Marcus's infidelity;
- the Markarth woman;
- Bal;
- witchcraft;
- fear of Julius;
- any curse;
- plans for a destination.

Those connections may be inferred from broader chronology, but should not be attributed to Julia's text.

## Relationship to later evidence

Marcus's 4E 200 journal says:
- Julia and Julius disappear;
- Julius is found in the forest;
- Julia remains missing.

The Ilinalta report later identifies Julia's burned, cursed corpse.

Together, the sources establish a transition from:
**voluntary departure → separation of mother and son → Julia's death**

The missing middle remains unresolved.

## Retrieval edges

- **Julia → takes → Julius**
- **Julia's Letter → placed in → Hidden Room**
- **later Julius recovery → follows → Julia's departure**
- **Julia's death → follows → unresolved separation event**
