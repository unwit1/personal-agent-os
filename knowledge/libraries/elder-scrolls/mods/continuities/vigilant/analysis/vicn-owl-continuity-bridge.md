## VIGILANT baseline

Direct VIGILANT evidence establishes:
- **Jhunal the Owl** as a speaking NPC;
- Jhunal says he used **Laza** as an obedient shepherd to harvest dragons;
- Jhunal wanted dragon souls and dreamed of becoming a dragon;
- VIGILANT contains separate White/Gray/Black Owl book records;
- the three books are deliberately distributed together across several Coldharbour locations.

The exact VIGILANT prose now directly establishes the White → Gray → Black sequence, but the books still identify the speakers by color/title rather than giving explicit personal names such as “Jhunal the White.”

## GLENMORIL — direct Owl texts

GLENMORIL contains two highly relevant exact books.

### The Three Owls

`texts/books/007A48-the-three-owls.txt`

The book gives three distinct guiding functions:

- **White Owl** — shows a “pure” path leading back whence the traveler came, but the path is endless and leads to moonless night.
- **Black Owl** — shows a “foul” path toward what the traveler desires; difficult and muddy, but illuminated by moonlight.
- **Gray Owl** — shows a road that travelers must not take; a road for the Owls, drenched in suffering and leading into wilderness.

This is direct GLENMORIL text, not VIGILANT text.

### Laza and the Owls

`texts/books/007A49-laza-and-the-owls.txt`

This later Vicn text directly sequences Laza through all three Owls:

1. **White Owl**
   - frames shepherd → wolf → wind transformation;
   - warns that “wind of Order” can become a wolf again.

2. **Gray Owl**
   - tells Laza to blind his eyes;
   - tells him to follow the Owl's call;
   - directs him toward the bottom of **Kyne's Spring**.

3. **Black Owl**
   - tells the blinded Laza to plunge his dagger into the Spring;
   - tells him to wash away a Nedic maiden's blood;
   - frames loss of staff / growth of fangs / disappearance of Kyne as bringing Laza closer to those he lost.

This is very strong cross-continuity evidence for the Laza/Owl sequence.

The exact VIGILANT Owl books are now separately persisted above. GLENMORIL should therefore be treated as later continuation/corroboration: it preserves the same three-stage narrative with some translation/version wording differences, rather than serving as a substitute for missing VIGILANT prose.

## Direct VIGILANT / GLENMORIL textual continuity

The recovered VIGILANT Owl books and GLENMORIL's later `Laza and the Owls` text preserve essentially the same three-stage narrative:

1. White Owl — shepherd/wolf/wind transformation
2. Gray Owl — self-blinding and route to Kyne's fountain/spring
3. Black Owl — dagger into the water, Nedic maiden's blood, loss of staff, growth of fangs, corruption of Kyne's water

This is now direct textual continuity across two Vicn works.

Wording differs slightly between translations/versions:
- VIGILANT uses **fountain of Kyne**;
- GLENMORIL uses **Kyne's Spring**.

The narrative sequence itself is unmistakably shared.
