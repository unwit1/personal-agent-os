## Complete surviving message

Julius writes only a brief plea to his father not to do **“this.”**

This is one of the very few direct first-person pieces of evidence from Julius.

## What it establishes

The note directly establishes:
- Julius is addressing **Papa**, naturally mapping to Marcus Bruiant within the family context;
- Julius is objecting to or pleading against an action by his father.

That is all the text itself safely establishes.

## What remains unresolved

The word **“this”** has no explicit referent in the note.

Do not silently identify it with:
- Marcus's affair;
- Marcus's treatment of Julia;
- actions toward the unnamed Markarth woman;
- an attempted killing;
- a ritual;
- the mansion fire;
- Julius's transformation;
- Marcus's final decision.

Any of those would require placement, stage, script, or surrounding-record evidence not currently attached to this note.

## Why the note matters

Most of Julius's story is told by other people:
- Marcus;
- Lamthor;
- Bartholo;
- Molag Bal;
- quest objectives and encounter structures.

The note is therefore disproportionately important because it preserves Julius's own voice.

It also reinforces the need to distinguish:
- **what Julius says**
from
- **what frightened adults and supernatural actors say about Julius**.
