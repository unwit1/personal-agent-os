What Khajiit Said
by Ja'zhan
[pagebreak] 

This one does not need what the Alessians call harmony.

For it is not worth even a single tear of the tormented kitten 
Locked in a cold dark latrine, 
Desperately scratching on the wall with small claws, 
Tearfully praying to the great Alkosh.

For it is not worth even a single tear of the tormented kitten 
Lost in the searing red mist, 
Running desperately on small paws, 
Begging for mercy the great S'rendarr. 

For it is not worth even a single tear of the tormented kitten 
Struggling with a rope, 
Gasping desperately with a small throat, 
Crying loudly to the great Lorkhaj. 

Why is it not worth it? 
Because the tears went unheard, never repaid. They must be atoned for, otherwise there is no such thing as harmony. 

This Khajiit will never accept it. No matter how much Skooma they offer him, he will not accept this. Khajiit will declare so even on a chopping block. If little kittens have to suffer for atonement, then no truth is worth this price.
