The Great Gate

Marukh's holy corpse rose and carved the Seventy-Seven signs into the air. The markings shone brilliantly, and the faithful around Marukh continued praying in ecstasy, despite the burning in their eyes. When Marukh spread the air apart with his hands, a crack appeared in reality and light flowed out of it like a flood. Amid the flood of light, the faithful screamed with joy and were instantly reduced to ashes. My limbs, too, crumbled to cinders. But I was more relieved than terrified, happy to be a part of the Aetherius I had only ever dreamed of.

But I soon realized that it was all a deception. What I saw beyond the light was a wasteland that was far from paradise. A godless wilderness with no meaning to life. Do the arrogant gods still want to give us more trials? No one can live like Shezarr. Who can walk through the frozen wasteland without being bound by name and fate? Who can fall into an endless abyss and not have any doubts?

We have to strive to become something to keep on living. That's why we are supposed to have faith. But if we can follow our path without hesitation, if we escape the search for the meaning of life, if we refuse to fear the judgment after death... then why would we worship gods like you? You abandoned us. You left us to die alone, without mercy. You took away even the hope the weak need to live. So I swear this by my black soul, I will cleanse this taint that is the blood of Anu from this world forever.
