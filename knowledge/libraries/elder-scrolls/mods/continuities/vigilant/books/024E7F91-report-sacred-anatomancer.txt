About the Sacred Anatomancer

Report by Thorondir
</p>



[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
The Sacred Anatomancer was a simple fisher living in a fishing village by lake Ilinalta until the day he lost his mind and slaughtered his own family. After that he fled to Riften and disappeared into the Ratway, only to reappear in the winter of 4E 198. He started swaying Vigilants of Stendarr from all over the world to his side and began organizing a splinter group he called the Society of the Holy Pipers. They began kidnapping and torturing innocent people, harvesting their guts and organs to create evil works of art reminiscent of the Ayleid flesh art.

We've been working with the other followers of Stendarr on all fronts to defeat this organization, and yet the number of the Holy Pipers is not only not decreasing, but it even appears to be growing with each day. Even stranger, we've already defeated dozens of the very same Anatomancer who must be somehow splitting himself into copies, and these keep reappearing as if there's no end to them. His powers must result from a contract with a Daedric Prince, but we still don't know which Daedra we're dealing with, so we cannot take effective countermeasures. The only thing we know at the moment is that a new Anatomancer will not appear unless we kill him.

I know that this is just postponing the problem, but for now, I've imprisoned him here. No one is to approach this prisoner. There's no need to ever feed him. We know he has no organs left; he's quite literally hollow. And somehow, he still lives.
