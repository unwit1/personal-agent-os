<font face="$HandwrittenFont">Seeing bodies floating on the surface of lake Ilinalta is not an uncommon sight ever since the civil war began. However, there is something unusual about the body we found this time, which is why we should increase our patrols around the lake.

Victim: Julia Bruiant (identified by the ring she was wearing) 
Cause of death: Severe burns 

A curse was placed on her corpse, and it was still burning from within when we found it. Additionally, its blood turned acidic and poisonous, killing everything coming in contact with it.
Lifting the curse proved unnaturally simple. There was no resistance to the dispelling, suggesting that the caster of the curse let it be lifted. Since there was a possibility that the caster may want to use the corpse in further rituals, we purified it with the light of Stendarr and scattered the ashes.
