Prophet Marukh the Imga saw the following vision in the city of Dwemer in the north: 

1 
Kyne waits beyond the frothing darkness, at the end of the far shore.

2 
A cowardly Vigilant sinks into the darkness. 
A hollow soul sees in the red Stone a shadow of what it once was.

3 
A red woman sacrifices her child and curses a mother. 
The curse flows from mother to child, the more innocent the better. 
The tainted child sinks into the darkness and is given over to Kyne. 

The vision of the prophet Marukh ends here.
