What Khajiit Saw
by Ja'zhan
[pagebreak]

More than a few Cyrodiilic nobles pretend to be civilized and take it upon themselves to raise Khajiiti cubs. Such parents are then referred to as the "ladies and gentlemen of honor and culture".

Khajiit would once again like to clarify that many humans possess a certain trait in common, and that is the abuse of kittens. Not just kittens, but anything young. This one cannot understand it. With anything else, these ruthless abusers seem like an example of benevolent and cultured nobles to this one... But it is a lie. They love to torment kittens, and from this twisted point of view, they think they love them. It seems to this one that the childish innocence of the kittens inspires such abuse that it would make even the Ayleids of old flinch. Khajiit shudders at the thought.
 
A beast is lurking inside every human being. This one is often mistaken for a beast, too, but that is very rude. Do not make this mistake, yes? And Khajiit only looks like a beast because he was infected with various diseases like brain rot and yellow tick fever because of his lustful nature.

Back to the matter. These educated foster parents subjected a certain five-year-old kitten to all kinds of torture. Ja'zhan cannot imagine why they would do such a thing, but they beat and beat and beat her and did not stop beating her, even when the kitten's skin split. And as if this would not be disgusting enough, they tortured her even worse. They locked the kitten in the outside latrine during winter when it was cold enough that even the Nords were freezing.

Why did they do this? To punish her for not telling them she had to use the toilet in the middle of the night. As if a little kitten sleeping soundly could do something like that. So they smeared the little kitten's face with her own mess and forced her to eat it. No Khajiit would ever do such a thing to a kitten. And these two parents slept soundly while the poor kitten locked up kitten wept in vain.
