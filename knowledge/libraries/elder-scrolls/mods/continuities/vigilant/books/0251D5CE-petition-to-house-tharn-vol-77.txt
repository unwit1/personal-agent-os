Pelan: "Such blasphemy. This goes against all doctrines of Marukh."

Amicus: "You keep bringing up these doctrines and refuting me at every opportunity. But how many of them do you truly remember? I remember none of them. And yet, I somehow know what to do. At some point, my arms and legs began moving as if they had a mind of their own."

Pelan: "That's not true...! No, I don't remember any of them, either. How did I not notice this before?"

Amicus: "This record may remain, but none will remember it. Those who read, those who wrote it, all will forget it. You saw that 'cliff' on which Marukh drew his doctrines, didn't you? Were those truly the words of St. Alessia?"

Pelan: "The cliff was covered in blood and Marukh was already dead. The other Imga traced the blood stains on it and gave me their interpretations. Only one image was painted on the cliff, but there were more than seventy-seven interpretations of it."

Amicus: "So there was no truth, only interpretations. Ah, but you returned to the Imperial City together with Marukh. And yet, Marukh was supposed to be dead. Do you remember his words when he pointed to the statue of Akatosh?"

Pelan: "'Auriel is not worthy.' And then he vomited up a burning stone and turned into a mangled corpse again."

Amicus: "That Stone chose us. And what chooses us, that we also choose."

Pelan: "I was chosen. It chose me."

Amicus: "Indeed, we were chosen. It chose us. For those who cannot bear to be free, domination is the only true kindness. So close your eyes, cover your ears, and shut your mouth. Trust in the warmth of the Stone-Fire. For we must be burned twice."
