# Forces of Order — VIGILANT continuity

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Type: Jyggalag-aligned invading force / Coldharbour war faction
- Associated Prince: Jyggalag
- Major implementation anchors:
  - `QUST 0213AEE3` / `zzzCHGreymarchQuest`
  - **Throne of Order** (`CELL 02125B35`)
  - Silver Cocoon traversal chain
  - Order aliases/targets/explosions in `zzzCHMQ00`
- Major witnesses: Inquisitor Pepe, Saklas

## Official substrate

VIGILANT uses established post-*Shivering Isles* lore as its starting point:
- Jyggalag is the Daedric Prince of Order;
- the Greymarch was associated with his return and the Forces/Knights of Order;
- the Hero of Kvatch broke Jyggalag's old Sheogorath curse cycle;
- Jyggalag was left free to roam Oblivion.

The mod's new conflict begins from that licensed endpoint.

## VIGILANT's Coldharbour invasion

Inquisitor Pepe says Jyggalag, now free, has invaded the Coldharbour wasteland with an immense Army of Order.

He describes:
- a catastrophic Battle of Weye;
- a brilliant silver cocoon through which Jyggalag manifested;
- most of the land falling to Order;
- Molag Bal sustaining a defensive barrier by consuming souls;
- the barrier eventually failing as Greymarch begins.

These historical details are Pepe's testimony.

The **current conflict**, however, is strongly corroborated structurally by:
- a dedicated Greymarch quest;
- Order quest aliases and objects;
- Order-themed NPCs and bosses;
- Silver Cocoon route cells;
- the Throne of Order;
- main-quest references to Greymarch state.

## Silver Cocoon and Throne of Order

The Coldharbour door graph contains a distinct route:

**First Corridor → Second Corridor → Large Void → Throne of Order**

This is one of the clearest implemented territories associated with the invading faction.

The route's existence proves physical level design and traversal relationships, not the exact chronology or access conditions of every stage.

## Saklas and Order mining

Saklas says the Forces of Order are **mining for something**.

He also says:
- this activity had been interrupted in the First Era by Dragon Breaks;
- Jyggalag has a library/ship;
- a singularity may eventually be resolved;
- Jhunal's Library should be found before the Forces of Order can mine it.

These claims connect the faction to:
- buried First Era knowledge;
- Jhunal's Library;
- Dragon Break history;
- the broader Staff-of-Towers/Middle-Dawn conceptual layer.

But the mining target is not identified by current evidence.

Do not equate Order mining with:
- Staff of Towers excavation;
- Adabal recovery;
- a specific Tower segment;
- Jhunal's relics;

unless later records say so directly.

## Relationship with Molag Bal

VIGILANT's Act 4 conflict is not a simple player-versus-Jyggalag war.

Pepe says Molag Bal maintains a barrier against the Army of Order and consumes souls to do so.

This creates a temporary structural opposition:

**Molag Bal's Coldharbour regime ↔ defending itself against ↔ Forces of Order**

That does not make Molag Bal benevolent. The same source frames his defense as domination fueled by consumed souls.

## Greymarch terminology

VIGILANT deliberately reuses **Greymarch** for the new invasion.

Lorekeeper should distinguish:
- **old Greymarch cycle in the Shivering Isles** — established licensed history;
- **VIGILANT Greymarch across Coldharbour** — mod-specific post-*Shivering Isles* extension.

## Canon boundary

Jyggalag, Order, and the old Greymarch are established Elder Scrolls concepts.

The invasion of Coldharbour, Battle of Weye, Silver Cocoon campaign, Order mining, and this specific Greymarch are VIGILANT-continuity extensions.
