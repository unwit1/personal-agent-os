# Marukhati Selective — VIGILANT continuity

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Type: radical Alessian theological/political current
- Established-lore anchors embedded by VIGILANT: Fervidius Tharn, *Vindication for the Dragon Break*, *On the Detachment of the Sheath from the Integument*, Proper-Life, Staff of Towers / Middle Dawn vocabulary
- Major VIGILANT-associated figure: Amicus Tharn
- Material culture: Marukhati Selective armor, masks, hoods, battlemage presentation

## Established substrate

VIGILANT directly embeds official-source texts attributed to **Fervidius Tharn**, Arch-Prelate of the Marukhati Selective.

These texts supply established Elder Scrolls concepts including:
- purification of Akatosh from the “Aldmeri Taint”;
- Proper-Life;
- ritual Dance;
- the Staff of Towers;
- reversal/manipulation of Time;
- Middle Dawn / Dragon Break metaphysics;
- Mnemoli, the Hurling Disk, and untime/unplace imagery.

Because VIGILANT imports those texts directly, those concepts should retain their official-source provenance rather than being credited to the mod.

## VIGILANT extension through Amicus Tharn

Amicus Tharn is the clearest VIGILANT-original ideologue who extends this tradition.

Across the four *Petition to House Tharn* volumes, Amicus argues for:
- fabricated Belharza and rewritten memory;
- reshaping divine identity through belief;
- separating Auriel from Akatosh;
- removing non-human peoples and histories;
- reforging reality into a human-only heaven;
- rejecting Prisoner freedom because it threatens imposed order.

This ideology strongly resembles and radicalizes the Selective's established anti-Aldmeri theological program.

The ESM later equips Amicus with **Marukhati Selective Armor**, gauntlets, mask, and hood.

That material presentation makes the link structural rather than merely thematic.

## Fervidius and Amicus

VIGILANT places Fervidius Tharn's official texts inside the same Alessian/Coldharbour continuity that contains Amicus Tharn.

This creates a deliberate bridge:
- **Fervidius** = established historical Marukhati Tharn;
- **Amicus** = VIGILANT-created Tharn ideologue using Selective equipment and compatible theology.

However, the ESM does not establish exact kinship.

Do not encode:
- Amicus as Fervidius's son;
- Amicus as direct ancestor/descendant of later Tharns;
- any precise House Tharn genealogy.

## Dragon Break and Tower layer

VIGILANT uses canonical Selective texts as a conceptual framework for its own Stone/Tower story.

Established imported layer:
- Staff of Towers;
- ritual Dance;
- Middle Dawn;
- Proper-Life;
- Akatosh/Auriel purification.

VIGILANT-specific layer:
- Marukh commanding that a red Stone be made into a Tower;
- Adabal / Stone-Fire objects;
- Pelan's corruption by prolonged Stone possession;
- Amicus's Stone ideology;
- later Saklas testimony that Forces of Order mining had once been interrupted by Dragon Breaks.

The ESM does **not** contain a physical artifact explicitly identified as the Staff of Towers.

Therefore:

**Marukh's Stone/Adabal ≠ Staff of Towers unless future direct evidence establishes that equation.**

## Material culture

The ESM uses a distinct **Marukhati Selective** armor family.

Amicus Tharn wears:
- Marukhati Selective Armor;
- Marukhati Selective Gauntlets;
- Marukhati Selective Mask;
- Marukhati Selective Hood.

Named Soul-Shriven Tharns such as Ortutay and Marosi also use Selective equipment in Alessian/Marukhati spaces.

This supports a broader Selective visual identity in Coldharbour but does not prove genealogy or a single chronological unit.

## Relationship to broader Alessian Order

Lorekeeper should preserve a parent/branch relationship rather than synonymy:

- **Alessian Order** = broad religious-political tradition associated with Marukh and later centuries.
- **Marukhati Selective** = radical theological current associated especially with Akatosh purification and temporal metaphysics.

VIGILANT intentionally places both in the same historical memory architecture, but not every Alessian saint or inquisitor is automatically a Selective.

## Reliability

High-confidence:
- official Selective texts are embedded in the mod;
- Amicus uses Selective equipment;
- Amicus's ideology overlaps strongly with Selective themes;
- Selective equipment appears in other Coldharbour Alessian contexts.

Unresolved:
- exact institutional membership of every Alessian figure;
- exact chronology from early Order to Selective;
- Amicus's genealogy;
- whether the mod intends one continuous organization or layered Coldharbour reconstructions.

## Relationship map

- **Alessian tradition → radical development → Marukhati Selective**
- **Fervidius Tharn → established Arch-Prelate → Selective**
- **Amicus Tharn → VIGILANT ideologue aligned with → Selective program**
- **Selective texts → provide vocabulary for → Dragon Break / Tower metaphysics**
- **VIGILANT Stone plot → extends but is not identical to → Staff of Towers ritual**

## Canon boundary

Fervidius, the Selective, Staff of Towers, Proper-Life, and Middle Dawn ritual vocabulary come from licensed Elder Scrolls sources.

Amicus's program, his exact role, his relationship to the Stone, and VIGILANT's hidden Stone/Tower history are mod-continuity extensions.
