## Supplementary description

The a123999 VIGILANT background article describes the **Holy Pipers** as:
- an extremist branch derived from the Templars/Vigilants of Stendarr;
- excommunicated;
- heavily associated with torture and coercive “purification.”

This fits VIGILANT's broader themes around mercy becoming coercion and religious certainty becoming violence.

However, the current repository does not yet preserve a direct VIGILANT ESM faction record, NPC roster, quest alias, unique scene, item set, or location explicitly named **Holy Pipers**.

## Current classification

**Supplementary organization node / direct-source gap.**

Lorekeeper may use the a123999 description as intended-background commentary, but should not present Holy Pipers as fully reconstructed from the shipped ESM.

## Relationship to Vigilants of Stendarr

The supplementary article frames Holy Pipers as an extremist offshoot related to Stendarr worship.

Do not automatically treat:
- every harsh Vigilant as a Holy Piper;
- Altano as a Holy Piper;
- the main Temple of Stendarr as Holy Piper-controlled;
- Holy Pipers as identical to the ordinary Vigilants.

Safe relation:

**Stendarr-derived religious tradition → extremist/excommunicated offshoot → Holy Pipers** — supplementary.
