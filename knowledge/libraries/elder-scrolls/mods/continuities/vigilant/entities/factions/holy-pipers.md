## Direct VIGILANT status

The Holy Pipers are now directly confirmed by the supplied VIGILANT 1.8.2 ESM.

They are not merely a supplementary organization.

### Thorondir's report

*Report: Sacred Anatomancer* says the Anatomancer:
- reappeared in winter 4E 198;
- swayed Vigilants of Stendarr to his side;
- organized a splinter group called the **Society of the Holy Pipers**;
- led them into kidnapping and torture;
- used victims' organs and guts for evil works compared with Ayleid flesh art.

Exact source:
- `books/024E7F91-report-sacred-anatomancer.txt`

The message offers a direct anti-Piper mission and frames them as defilers of Stendarr's name.

## Song of Life / Piper doctrine

The Sacred Anatomancer quest contains explicit Piper liturgy.

One dialogue prompt is:

**“Pipers! What is our mission?”**

The response orders extermination of:
- **Hamah and her bloodline**
- **Laza and those with the blood curse**
- **the Bard and those who drown out the song**

The reasons given involve:
- protecting “our life”;
- removing what blocks “our Radiance”;
- silencing what interferes with “our song.”

Another Piper exchange invokes:
- Love, I and We;
- threes and repeating threes;
- Blue Star;
- redshift;
- the Song of Life.

These are direct VIGILANT doctrinal/liturgical lines.

## Relationship to Vigilants of Stendarr

Thorondir's report directly says the Anatomancer was **swaying Vigilants of Stendarr** and organizing a splinter group.

Therefore the safe VIGILANT relation is:

**Vigilants of Stendarr → recruitment/schism → Society of the Holy Pipers**

This is now direct VIGILANT evidence.

It still does not mean:
- every harsh Vigilant is a Holy Piper;
- Altano is a Holy Piper;
- the ordinary Vigilants share Piper doctrine.
