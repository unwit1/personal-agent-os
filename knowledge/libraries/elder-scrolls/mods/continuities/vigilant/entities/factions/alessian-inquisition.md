## Institutional role

VIGILANT presents an **inquisitorial apparatus** inside the wider Alessian tradition.

This is best modeled as a sub-institution rather than a synonym for the entire Alessian Order.

The broader Order includes:
- saints;
- abbots;
- priests;
- political ideologues;
- military/religious communities;
- later Marukhati developments.

The Inquisition represents the Order's **judicial, doctrinal-enforcement, interrogation, punishment, and execution** side.

## Pelan / Inquisitor Pepe

Pelan survives into VIGILANT's later material as **Inquisitor Pepe**.

The memory sequence explicitly includes:
- *The Grand Inquisitor*;
- early still-humanoid Pepe/Pelan;
- the execution/persecution memory involving Mary/Moura;
- later transformation into the Alessian Sleeper body.

Pepe's own later testimony is deeply critical of the institution he once served.

He remembers:
- burning Mary;
- serving the Eight Divines;
- participating in Alessian history;
- later concluding that Alessian rulers became enslavers rather than shepherds.

This makes Pepe both:
- a former/continuing embodiment of inquisitorial authority;
- and one of its strongest retrospective critics.

## Judgement

`BOOK 02054ED7` / *Judgement* provides one of the clearest textual examples of Alessian judicial ideology.

The document argues that:
- a Mara follower who cured a plague victim without Order permission committed heresy;
- the healer was therefore burned;
- the healed boy was also executed;
- Syrabane's healing was acceptable because it had institutional approval;
- popular participation in punishment was treated as evidence of righteousness.

The key institutional principle is:

**miracle versus heresy is determined by authorization, not outcome.**

This is powerful evidence for an inquisitorial system centered on control of legitimate religious action.

The healer is not named in this document, so the text should be treated as a strong parallel to Mary/Moura's persecution rather than as independent name confirmation.

## Mary / Moura persecution

Multiple witnesses converge on the Order/Inquisition burning Mary/Moura after her plague healing:
- Ja'zhan says he witnessed the “maiden of Mara” burned;
- Sir Caius says the Alessians burned Mary;
- Pope Megus says “we” should not have burned her;
- Pepe remembers burning Mary;
- *The Grand Inquisitor* structurally places Mary the Dark Maiden in the Mara role opposite Pepe.

This is one of the strongest cross-source examples of inquisitorial persecution in VIGILANT.

## Denunciation and historical rewriting

Amicus's petition program advocates:
- denying Belharza's identity;
- fabricating replacement history;
- killing witnesses who preserve unwanted memory;
- exterminating non-human peoples;
- rejecting anomalous Prisoner figures.

The presence of an Amicus-linked **Denounced One** representation in First Inquisition Court ties ideological historical control to an explicitly inquisitorial environment.

The ESM does not prove which inquisitorial authority “denounced” Amicus or exactly when.

## Relationship to Marukhati Selective

The Inquisition and the Marukhati Selective overlap historically and ideologically, but they should not be collapsed.

Safe distinction:
- **Inquisition** = enforcement/judicial machinery;
- **Marukhati Selective** = radical theological/metaphysical current;
- **Alessian Order** = broader religious-political tradition containing multiple currents and offices.

Some individuals or spaces may participate in more than one layer.
