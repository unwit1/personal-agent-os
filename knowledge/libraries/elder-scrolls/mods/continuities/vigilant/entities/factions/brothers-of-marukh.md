# Brothers of Marukh — VIGILANT continuity

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Type: Alessian/Marukhite religious-military community
- Major associated figure: Abbot Cosmas
- Major associated historical site: Malada
- VIGILANT location anchor: **Holy Brothers of Marukh Priory** (`LCTN 023870E4`)
- Related VIGILANT site: **Marukh's Underground Priory** (`CELL 02101AA1`)

## Licensed historical substrate

VIGILANT's Brothers of Marukh are grounded in the licensed text *The Cleansing of the Fane*.

That source describes **Abbot Cosmas** leading the Brothers of Marukh against the Ayleid temple **Malada**, using holy fire and destroying relics/books.

This establishes:
- the name Brothers of Marukh;
- Cosmas as an abbot associated with them;
- Malada as a historical target;
- a militant religious role.

VIGILANT reuses these obscure historical anchors as explorable Act 4 material.

## Abbot Cosmas in VIGILANT

VIGILANT contains:
- `NPC_ 0210330D` / Abbot Cosmas boss form;
- `QUST 024F57B8` / **VS Abbot**;
- a main-quest Abbot alias;
- `ACHR 02114871` placing Cosmas in **Marukh's Underground Priory**.

The boss form currently has no large uniquely resolved dialogue corpus.

Therefore Cosmas's historical motives should come from licensed-source provenance and VIGILANT structure rather than invented boss exposition.

## Holy Brothers of Marukh Priory

The ESM contains:
- `LCTN 023870E4` — **Holy Brothers of Marukh Priory**

A named Feral Soul-Shriven variant:
- **Radokhan Tharn**

is triggered in:
- `CELL 0206E57A` / `CHicCourtMarukh01`

whose location is the Holy Brothers priory.

Radokhan uses:
- Alessian Battlemage Armor;
- Stonefire Wand.

This gives the Priory a material Alessian/Tharn battlemage layer, but does not prove Radokhan was historically one of Cosmas's original Brothers.

## Marukh's Underground Priory

The nearby/related Marukh complex contains:
- Abbot Cosmas;
- Jhunal's corpse/relic cache;
- Ortutay Tharn Soul-Shriven variant;
- Marukh-related route material;
- access toward Malada.

This creates a strong **Marukhite institutional geography** across priory spaces.

However, “Holy Brothers of Marukh Priory” and “Marukh's Underground Priory” are separate structural locations and should not be silently treated as the exact same building or period.

## Relationship to Malada

Malada is one of the strongest bridges between licensed Alessian history and VIGILANT.

Licensed substrate:
- Cosmas and the Brothers attack the Ayleid fane at Malada.

VIGILANT:
- implements a multi-cell Malada complex;
- places Alessian/Marukhite figures and records around it;
- uses Malada as a major Coldharbour route and memory/history site.

This makes the Brothers a useful institution node for connecting textual TES history to VIGILANT's worldspace reconstruction.

## Relationship to wider Alessian Order

Safe ontology:

**Alessian Order → broader tradition**
  
**Brothers of Marukh → particular Marukhite religious-military community within that tradition**

This is distinct from:
- the Alessian Inquisition;
- the later Marukhati Selective;
- generic Alessian saint cults.

The groups overlap in ideology/history but perform different narrative and institutional roles.
