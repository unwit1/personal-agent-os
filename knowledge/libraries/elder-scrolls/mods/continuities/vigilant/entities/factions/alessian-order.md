# Alessian Order — VIGILANT continuity

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Type: religious-political institution / historical memory system
- Major associated figures: Marukh, Pelan/Inquisitor Pepe, Amicus, Belharza, Jhunal, Abbot Cosmas, Manthar, Nenyond, Silorn, Sard, Dulsa, Moura/Mary
- Major associated spaces: Mathmalatu Priory, Marukh's Underground Priory, Inquisition Courts, Malada, Funeral Temple, saint charnels
- Major documentary sources: *The Eight Saints of Cyrod*, *Petition to House Tharn* vols. 11/23/68/77, *Judgement*, embedded Marukhite/Alessian texts

## Role in VIGILANT

The Alessian Order is one of VIGILANT's central historical institutions.

It is not represented as a single stable organization with one doctrine across all eras. Instead, VIGILANT presents the Order through:
- saint biographies;
- inquisitorial memories;
- ideological debates;
- surviving/reconstructed members in Coldharbour;
- monasteries, courts, charnels, relics, and books;
- contradictory testimony about Alessia, Marukh, Belharza, and the Stone.

Lorekeeper should therefore model the Order as a **historically changing institutional network**, not as a monolithic speaker.

## Marukh and early formation

VIGILANT follows established Elder Scrolls tradition in treating Marukh as the prophetic source of the Alessian religious movement.

The mod's embedded official texts supply licensed concepts including:
- Marukh's relationship to the shade/vision of Alessia;
- the Seventy-Seven Doctrines;
- Proper-Life;
- anti-Aldmeri purification;
- the later Marukhati theological program.

VIGILANT adds its own hidden-history layer in which Marukh is tied to a red Stone/Adabal, ritual sacrifice, and the command to make the Stone into a Tower.

Those additions remain VIGILANT-specific.

## The Eight Saints institutional memory

`BOOK 020CB0DF` / *The Eight Saints of Cyrod* presents a saintly institutional history around:
- Pelan;
- Moura;
- Sard;
- Dulsa;
- Silorn;
- Manthar;
- Nenyond;
- Jhunal.

The book supplies an internal canon of heroic/religious biography, but later environmental evidence and dialogue repeatedly complicate it.

Examples:
- Pelan survives into Coldharbour as Inquisitor Pepe and describes himself as corrupted by the Stone.
- Jhunal is said to have died, yet appears as Jhunal the Owl, fragments, a corpse/relic cache, and distributed relics.
- Silorn, Manthar, and Nenyond persist through remains, transformed forms, or later encounters.

Therefore the saint text should be treated as **institutional hagiography**, not an omniscient historical record.

## Pelan and Amicus: internal ideological conflict

The four *Petition to House Tharn* volumes preserve an internal dispute between Pelan and Amicus.

Pelan increasingly objects to:
- manufactured history;
- replacement of Belharza;
- extermination of non-human peoples;
- destruction of witnesses;
- rejection of Prisoner freedom.

Amicus advocates:
- reshaping divine and historical identity through belief;
- separating Auriel from Akatosh;
- erasing unwanted histories and peoples;
- creating a human-only heaven;
- imposing order over freedom.

This is important because VIGILANT does not portray the Alessian Order as ideologically unanimous.

Current model:

**Alessian institution → internal split between reformist/resistant figures and increasingly radical Marukhati ideologues.**

## Belharza and exclusion

Belharza's testimony says the Order stripped him of meaningful power, persecuted non-human peoples, mutilated him, and created a replacement/puppet identity.

Licensed Elder Scrolls sources support a contested tradition of anti-minotaur/proto-Alessian persecution, but VIGILANT's exact mechanisms are mod-specific.

The Order is therefore both:
- grounded in established exclusionary Alessian tradition;
- extended by VIGILANT into a much more explicit program of historical fabrication and bodily/political replacement.

## Mary / Moura persecution

Multiple VIGILANT witnesses independently remember Mary/Moura as a healer associated with Mara who was burned by Alessians.

Important witnesses include:
- Ja'zhan;
- Sir Caius;
- Pope Megus;
- Pelan/Pepe;
- the saint tradition;
- *Judgement* / Grand Inquisitor memory material.

This convergence makes Mary's persecution one of the strongest recurrent institutional crimes attributed to the Order in VIGILANT.

Claims about Mary's literal divinity, later forms, and relationship to Molag Bal remain separate questions.

## Geography and institutional persistence

Coldharbour contains multiple spaces framed as Alessian institutional ruins or continuations:
- Mathmalatu Priory;
- Marukh's Underground Priory;
- First and Second Inquisition Courts;
- Narfin's Inquisition Court;
- Malada complexes;
- saint charnels and ossuaries;
- Funeral Temple;
- Belharza's Hidden Charnel.

This environmental density makes the Alessian Order more than historical backstory: Act 4 is physically organized through its surviving architecture, memories, relics, and ideological aftermath.

## Relationship to the Marukhati Selective

The Alessian Order and Marukhati Selective should be linked but not collapsed.

The broader Alessian tradition precedes and contains many figures who are not necessarily Selectives.

VIGILANT later introduces unmistakably Marukhati Selective material through:
- Fervidius Tharn's official texts;
- Selective armor;
- Amicus Tharn;
- anti-Aldmeri/Akatosh purification ideology;
- Dragon Break / Middle Dawn vocabulary.

Current ontology:

**Alessian Order → broader religious-political tradition**
  
**Marukhati Selective → radical later/internal doctrinal faction or development within that tradition**

## Reliability

High-confidence structural:
- repeated Alessian-named locations, quests, equipment, books, and characters;
- Mathmalatu Priory explicitly described as a former Alessian monastery;
- multiple named institutional actors;
- embedded official Alessian/Marukhati texts.

Attributed/contested:
- exact First Era chronology;
- specific responsibility for every atrocity;
- whether Coldharbour manifestations are literal historical survivors, reconstructed souls, dreams, or memory bodies;
- metaphysical truth of the Order's Stone/Tower claims.

## Relationship map

- **Marukh → prophetic founder/source → Alessian tradition**
- **Alessian Order → preserves → Eight Saints tradition**
- **Alessian Order → persecutes → Mary/Moura, Belharza/non-human groups** — VIGILANT testimony
- **Pelan ↔ disputes ideology with ↔ Amicus**
- **Amicus → radicalizes → historical/divine rewriting program**
- **Alessian institutions → persist as → Coldharbour ruins, courts, priory complexes, relic networks**
- **Alessian tradition → later/radical branch → Marukhati Selective**

## Canon boundary

The Alessian Order itself, Marukh, Proper-Life, exclusionary doctrine, and the later Marukhati tradition are established Elder Scrolls concepts.

VIGILANT's exact saint biographies, Stone plot, Mary persecution reconstruction, Belharza mutilation/replacement, Pelan/Amicus debates, and Coldharbour institutional afterlife remain `tes.mod.vigilant` material unless independently corroborated.
