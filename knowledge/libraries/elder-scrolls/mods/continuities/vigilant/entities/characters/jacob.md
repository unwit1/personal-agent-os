## Senior Vigilant and Altano

Altano's journal says an old Vigilant named **Jacob** rescued him from a snowfield on 22 Morning Star 4E 197.

Jacob:
- listened to the amnesiac man's situation;
- gave him the name **Altano**;
- became the institutional bridge through which Altano entered the Vigilants of Stendarr.

This makes Jacob part of Altano's origin story long before Jacob becomes central to *Old Regrets*.

## Windhelm incident — 4E 180

The Windhelm report and Rahel's journal establish a detailed earlier crisis.

### Rahel's journal

On 12 Second Seed 4E 180, Rahel writes that Jacob has been gone for two days after going underground in Windhelm to hunt vampires.

### Windhelm report

The official report records:
- prisoners and guards disappearing;
- a vampire captured beneath Windhelm;
- Vigilants requested from the Temple of Stendarr;
- the **Maiden Statue** identified as the source/path of the vampire attacks;
- seven Vigilants and thirteen guards sent through the statue;
- screaming from the sealed statue;
- the team presumed lost;
- on 13 Second Seed, Jacob found unconscious outside the city walls.

Jacob tells the authorities that the investigation team died in a tunnel collapse that also destroyed the vampires' route.

This report makes Jacob the only named apparent survivor of the expedition.

## Jacob's warning note

Jacob later leaves a warning that if the **Maiden Statue** opens, “her” awakening has occurred.

The note describes the awakened figure as:
- a defiler of Arkay;
- a mistake of Stendarr;
- a danger whose voice and gaze should be avoided;
- capable of dragging victims to **Blood Mist Castle** for the **Lord of Corruption**.

This provides strong evidence that Jacob knew far more about the Maiden Statue threat than appears in the terse Windhelm official report.

The note does not explicitly name the threatened “her.”

## Aredhel's conflicting account

Aredhel's note gives another version of the expedition's collapse.

It says:
- **Jacob was killed in battle**;
- Joshua fled;
- the others surrendered to the **Blood Matron**;
- the Blood Matron shared her blood with them;
- Aredhel accepted willingly;
- the others resisted their vampiric bloodthirst.

This conflicts with the Windhelm report's presentation of Jacob as a surviving investigator.

Rather than choose one account, Lorekeeper should preserve the conflict.

Possible explanations include:
- the official report being incomplete or false;
- Jacob dying and later returning;
- Aredhel using “killed” loosely;
- memory/identity distortion;
- events occurring in more than one phase.

The current ESM evidence does not settle the mechanism.

## Old Regrets bargain

In `INFO 0200EA82`, Jacob gives the most important first-person account of his fall.

He says:
- he was defeated and dying;
- Molag Bal offered to revive him;
- the price was **his wife's soul**;
- Jacob accepted;
- he deeply regrets that choice.

This gives VIGILANT a direct bargain structure:

**Jacob near death → Molag Bal offers life → Jacob sacrifices Rahel's soul → Jacob survives/returns → regret**

This account can potentially reconcile Aredhel's statement that Jacob died with later Jacob appearances, but the exact timing and metaphysical mechanics remain unstated.

## Supplementary intended-background resolution

The supplementary VIGILANT background article by **a123999** gives a more definite interpretation of the Windhelm contradiction.

It says:
- Jacob and his fellows **lost their lives** when they encountered the half-sleeping Lamae;
- Jacob later sacrificed his wife's soul to Molag Bal;
- Jacob received a **new body** and continued acting as a Vigilant.

Source:
- `sources/supplementary/a123999-vigilant-backgrounds.md`

This strongly supports the literal-death-and-revival model at the supplementary-commentary layer.

Lorekeeper should still preserve the in-plugin documents separately:
- official Windhelm report says Jacob was found alive;
- Aredhel says Jacob was killed;
- Jacob says Molag Bal revived him in exchange for Rahel's soul.

The commentary helps explain authorial intent but does not make the report cease to exist.

## A Vision in Malada — stanza 10

The current prophecy map assigns stanza 10 to **Jacob and Rahel** with high confidence for the bargain.

The stanza's core image—a Vigilant sacrificing his love for a false heartbeat—closely matches Jacob's own account.

The further Stone-transfer clauses are less explicit and remain only medium-confidence.

## Later Vigilant activity

By 4E 197 Jacob is still active enough to rescue Altano.

In 4E 200, Marcus Bruiant records a Vigilant named Jacob arriving with Julia's ring after her burned body was found at Lake Ilinalta.

This indicates Jacob's Vigilant activity spans at least:
- Windhelm crisis in 4E 180;
- Altano rescue in 4E 197;
- Bruiant/Julia investigation in 4E 200.

That long span is compatible with Jacob surviving Molag Bal's bargain, but the ESM does not give a simple date for the bargain itself.

## Reliability

High-confidence documentary:
- Jacob rescued/named Altano;
- Jacob was a Windhelm Vigilant investigator;
- official report says he was found alive after the Maiden Statue expedition;
- Jacob authored/was associated with a warning about the Maiden Statue;
- Jacob is the speaking center of Old Regrets.

High-confidence testimony:
- Jacob says Molag Bal revived him in exchange for his wife's soul.

Conflicting testimony:
- Aredhel says Jacob was killed in battle.

Unresolved:
- exact date of Jacob's death/bargain/revival;
- how the official Windhelm account relates to Aredhel's version;
- precise identity behind the Maiden Statue warning.
