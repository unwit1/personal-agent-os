## VIGILANT portrayal

In *After the Storm*, Morihaus initially mourns Pelinal and recalls Pelinal teaching that Ada change things through love. He says this makes surrendering to rage harder and resolves to keep watch for Paravania.

The same structurally attributed memory sequence then turns sharply: lines order captives and citizens killed, describe learning bloodlust and rage from Pelinal, and declare Cyrod belongs to the victors. The contradiction is important evidence. It may represent change, corruption, fractured memory, juxtaposed temporal moments, or dream logic; the current record pass does not choose between them.

A later Morihaus memory in *The Final Night* says he always knew "she" would become a star and expresses pain at that outcome. Scene/relationship reconstruction is required before resolving the unnamed referent.

Belharza offers a separate interpretation: he identifies Alessia and Morihaus as his parents and describes Morihaus' appearance as unstable, proposing that "Morihaus" may be an accumulation of anecdotes and spirits held in a divine vessel. This remains Belharza's VIGILANT-specific testimony.
