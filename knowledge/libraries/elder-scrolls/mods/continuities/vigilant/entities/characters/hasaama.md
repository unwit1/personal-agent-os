# Hasaama

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Primary relationship: beloved/companion of Dro'Zel
- Major context: **The Mad King** memory and **Madness** side quest
- Additional implementation clue: named inventories/relic reuse

## Dro'Zel relationship

Dro'Zel's memory material directly addresses or refers to **Hasaama** as a beloved figure.

The bard's tragic tale of Polydor and Eloisa leaves Dro'Zel emotionally destabilized, and Hasaama is part of the personal context around that reaction.

## Madness

In the *Madness* side quest, Dro'Zel asks Hasaama whether he should kill the recurring bard again.

He says the bard keeps returning even after being killed and dismembered.

This makes Hasaama a direct witness/listener within Dro'Zel's recurrence crisis.

## Relic/implementation clue

VIGILANT reuses certain saint/relic items in inventories associated with Hasaama.

Those inventory links are useful implementation evidence but should not be converted into a detailed biography without direct dialogue or quest-alias support.
