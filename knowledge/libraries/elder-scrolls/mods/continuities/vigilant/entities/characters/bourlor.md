- Quest: `QUST 021279A1` / **Archer of Kyne**

- Direct source: translated VIGILANT 1.8.2 ESM

## Archer of Kyne

The quest directly binds Bourlor to the **Archer** alias.

Despite the quest title, Bourlor says he has been practicing **knife throwing** for centuries and has still never hit anything.

He wants another attempt at **Vernaccus**.

## Bow memory

When the player returns Bourlor's bow, he says:

> This bow brings back memories... Nothing has changed since then...

and agrees to help the player.

This makes the bow a memory/identity anchor even though the current pass has not yet mapped its exact item FormID.

## Vernaccus

The quest's **Prey** alias is Vernaccus.

Vernaccus identifies himself as:
- **Bane of Kyne**
- someone previously humiliated by the player
- now seeking revenge.

This creates a direct Bourlor ↔ Vernaccus conflict inside **Archer of Kyne**.

## Relationship map

- **Bourlor → Archer of Kyne**
- **Bourlor → hunts/challenges → Vernaccus**
- **Bourlor → bow restores → memories / willingness to fight**
- **Vernaccus → title → Bane of Kyne**

Bourlor and this quest relationship are VIGILANT-continuity material.
