## Pepe before Colovia

Arasil says that **Pepe had once been a priest of the Eight Divines** and describes him as kind-hearted before his journey into the jungles of Colovia.

Arasil says Pepe changed after returning from that journey.

This testimony is important because it independently supports the Pelan/Pepe reconstruction.

## Link to Pelan

*The Eight Saints of Cyrod* says **Pelan** found Marukh in the Colovian jungles.

Pepe himself later says he traveled into the Colovian jungle after hearing about the Imga prophet and met Marukh there.

Ritho explicitly identifies Pepe as **Bishop Pelan**.

Arasil's account therefore fills in the character-state transition around that same event:

**priest of the Eight Divines / kind-hearted Pepe-Pelan → journey to Colovia → return in a changed state**

The ESM does not establish that the entire later monstrous transformation happened immediately on his return. Other evidence shows Pepe remains physically human in early memories and only later uses the Alessian Sleeper body.

Accordingly, Arasil's “changed” should be interpreted first as a broad personal/spiritual change, not automatically as the final bodily transformation.

## Relationship to the Stone timeline

The detailed Pelan/Pepe reconstruction indicates:
- early Pepe/Pelan remains humanoid;
- he becomes entangled with the Stone;
- centuries later he says holding it too long left him a soulless monster;
- later memory and present-day forms use the Alessian Sleeper body.

Arasil's testimony therefore belongs near the beginning of the transformation arc, but does not by itself identify the exact mechanism.

## Reliability

High-value testimony:
- Pepe had been a priest of the Eight Divines;
- he was perceived as kind-hearted;
- a significant change followed the Colovian journey.

Supported by other evidence:
- Pelan/Pepe's Colovian connection;
- Ritho's Pelan identity line;
- memory-form progression;
- Pepe's own later testimony.

Unresolved:
- what specific change Arasil witnessed;
- whether the change was doctrinal, psychological, metaphysical, physical, or a combination.
