# Bal — Bruiant Mansion butler

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Forms: `0204DC3A` / `zzzCOEvilButler`; `02064870` / `zzzCOEvilButlerKillable`
- Role: Bruiant household servant/guide associated with the mansion curse

## Evidence

Marcus' 4E 200 journal says he hired a new butler named Bal, whom he describes as a Wild Elf, and notes that Julius became unusually fond of him. Lamthor separately warns Marcus that Julius' behavior has worsened since Bal arrived and alleges Bal taught Julius strange incantations.

When the player reaches the mansion, Bal welcomes the player and attempts to lead them inside. He calls himself a shadow/remnant and dismisses questions about his name. A scene in `zzzCOMq01` uses the separate killable Bal alias.

Orlando's optional *Spinner's Needle 2* route calls Bal deeply tainted and says the Spinner's Needle can pierce his curse. Stabbing Bal is the alternate route's trigger for skipping to Act 4.

## Relationship to Molag Bal

The main quest defines `Bal`, `BalKillable`, and `MolagBal` as three separate quest aliases/forms. The story strongly invites a connection, but Lorekeeper currently records **association without literal identity** until scripts, transformation references, or stages establish the mechanism.
