## Pelan = Pepe identification

Ritho provides the strongest explicit identity statement connecting **Pelan** to **Inquisitor Pepe**.

When asked about Pepe, Ritho identifies him as **“bishop Pelan.”**

This is important because most other Pelan/Pepe evidence is circumstantial or biographical:
- Pelan's saint biography says he found Marukh in Colovia;
- Pepe says he met Marukh in the Colovian jungle;
- Pelan is said to survive for thousands of years;
- Pepe speaks from a millennia-spanning perspective;
- Arasil says Pepe changed after returning from Colovia.

Ritho's line turns those parallels into a direct in-world identification.

Current Lorekeeper classification:

**Pelan = Bishop Pelan = Inquisitor Pepe is high-confidence within VIGILANT.**

## Structural role

Ritho is not merely a passing dialogue source.

The Act 4 orchestration layer includes a dedicated boss quest:
- `zzzCHBossQuestRitho` — **VS Ritho**

That boss quest is included in the Act 4 starter infrastructure alongside other major Coldharbour encounters.

This establishes Ritho as an implemented major encounter whose testimony is embedded in the active Coldharbour layer.

## Reliability

Ritho is especially valuable for one narrow claim:
- Pepe's older identity/title as Bishop Pelan.

The statement is direct identification by a named in-world character, but it still remains character testimony. Its reliability becomes high because multiple independent structural and biographical details converge on the same identity.

Ritho's line does **not** by itself explain:
- why Pelan becomes known as Pepe;
- when the name changes;
- how the Stone causes his bodily transformation;
- whether every Pelan relic belongs personally to Pepe.
