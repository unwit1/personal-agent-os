# Lamae Bal / Lamae Beolfag — VIGILANT continuity

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Key forms: `02037468` `zzzBMLamaeBal`; `02035D7D` `zzzBMLamaeBeolfag`; `02080E93` `zzzCHLamaeMemory`; `022C8784` `zzzCHLamaeMemoryMad`
- Role: central figure of the Blood Matron arc and later Coldharbour memory material

## Blood Matron arc

The numbered Blood Matron chain culminates in `QUST 02038526` / *The Blood Matron*. Its objectives explicitly require the player first to defeat Lamae Bal and then to **free Lamae Bal from the curse of Molag Bal**.

Lamae's direct dialogue mixes love, violence, possession, and fear of rejection. She compares the player's cold eyes to "him" and asks whether her love will again be answered with a dagger; other lines insist on eternal togetherness. These establish her subjective emotional state without making every implication a historical fact.

## Documentary evidence

`BOOK 020DB22D` / *Lamae's Kin* describes a VIGILANT-specific form of vampirism as a painful bloodborne curse distinct from ordinary curable vampirism. It says afflicted people begin dreaming of a woman named Lamae and become unstable before progressing into violent vampirism. These are the book's claims, not automatically objective medical history.

## Coldharbour

A separate `zzzCHLamaeMemory` form has structurally attributed dialogue in the Coldharbour corpus. It is linked to the Blood Matron figure while retaining the memory-context boundary.
