- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: household servant/victim in the Bruiant Mansion crisis
- Primary evidence: Marcus' 4E 201 journal; Bartholo investigation

## Death

Marcus records Patricia's apparent suicide during the renewed mansion deaths.

The broader reconstruction says she hangs herself.

Bartholo later reports faint Magicka around the bodies involved in the renewed incidents.

## Evidence boundary

The sources establish:
- Patricia dies by apparent hanging/suicide;
- the death occurs during the curse crisis;
- Bartholo detects magical traces around the cluster of deaths.

They do not establish:
- Patricia willingly chose death;
- who magically influenced her;
- whether Julius, Bal, the totems, or another actor directly caused it.
