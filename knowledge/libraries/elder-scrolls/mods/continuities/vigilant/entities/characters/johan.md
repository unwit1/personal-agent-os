## Johan the Fool memory

The Coldharbour memory reconstruction says that after Martha's death:
- a traveling bard calling himself **Bal** approaches Johan;
- the bard gives Johan a mace;
- Johan is directed toward collecting sinners' souls;
- later Johan is granted a final wish to burn what he built;
- the sequence ends with Johan being consigned to Oblivion.

This is memory testimony inside VIGILANT's explicitly unstable Coldharbour sequence.

## Bard identity boundary

Therefore:

- bard = uniquely resolved Molag Bal actor = not structurally proven by that alias alone.

The larger motif strongly parallels Molag Bal's mace/soul economy, but provenance remains explicit.
