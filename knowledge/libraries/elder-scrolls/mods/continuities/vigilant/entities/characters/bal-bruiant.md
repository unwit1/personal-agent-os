# Bal — Bruiant Mansion butler

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- NPC form: `0204DC3A` / `zzzCOEvilButler`
- Role: Wild Elf/Ayleid-associated servant at Bruiant Mansion; supernatural agent connected to Julius's corruption
- Identity policy: **not automatically merged with Molag Bal**

## Arrival and influence

Marcus hires Bal in 4E 200 after Julia's death. Julius becomes unusually fond of him. A servant note complains that Bal teaches Julius stories about black magic and buried myths, while another reports Julius practicing strange incantations.

## Player encounter

Bal greets the player at the mansion, says he is merely a "shadow" or "remnant," claims Bartholo is waiting inside, and eventually becomes hostile. Separate dialogue says Bal summoned the Legion and reported that Marcus had been killed by political opponents.

Orlando identifies the butler as deeply tainted and offers a Spinner's Needle route that requires stabbing Bal **before entering the mansion**, which skips directly toward Act 4.

## Relationship to Molag Bal

The `Child of Oblivion` quest has separate aliases/forms for **Bal** and **MolagBal**. That is strong structural reason not to collapse them. The name, corruption imagery, and narrative role clearly link the butler to Molag Bal's plot, but the exact ontology—servant, remnant, avatar, disguise, or something else—remains unresolved pending VMAD/scene analysis.
