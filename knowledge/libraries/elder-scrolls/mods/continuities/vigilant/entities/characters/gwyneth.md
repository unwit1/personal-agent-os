## Story role

Gwyneth is a research-oriented Vigilant associated with the Temple library. Her ambient dialogue directs the player toward material on Daedra, witches, vampirism, Namira, Vivec/Molag Bal, and related subjects.

During *The Endless Fall*, Gwyneth survives the Temple disaster and says **Altano summoned the attacking Daedra**, urging the player to stop him. In *The Landing*, she describes herself and the player as the Temple's only survivors and proposes that the player become its new Keeper.

Gwyneth then connects later arcs. During *Empty Cells* she retrieves historical case documents. In *Child of Oblivion* she briefs the player on the missing Vigilant Bartholo and the Bruiant family—Marcus, Julia, and Julius—and prepares documents for the investigation.

A Coldharbour-related generic line has Gwyneth discuss a damaged tablet which a peddler claimed belonged to the Alessian Order. She says she can only partially read it and that it appears to be a song about the return of Shezarr. Lorekeeper stores this as peddler claim → Gwyneth partial reading, not objective history.
