# Summil

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: cook/servant at Bruiant Mansion
- Major documents:
  - `BOOK 0204C5AE` — **Summil's Note**
  - `BOOK 02054EC4` — **Summil's Bloody Note**
- 4E 201 event source: Marcus' journal

## Breakdown

Marcus records on 1 First Seed, 4E 201 that:
- Summil, the cook, suddenly attacks Marcus with a meat cleaver;
- Bartholo saves Marcus;
- Summil is restrained and confined in the storeroom.

Later reconstruction records Summil dying after the incident, with the mansion deaths forming part of Bartholo's Magicka-trace investigation.

## Source gap

The two Summil note records are physically placed in Bruiant Mansion South Wing, but their exact prose is not currently persisted in the browsable book layer.

Therefore do not reconstruct their wording from later summaries.
