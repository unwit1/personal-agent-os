## Established Elder Scrolls substrate

Umaril the Unfeathered is an established enemy of Pelinal Whitestrake and a major figure in the Alessian/Ayleid mythic-historical tradition.

VIGILANT reuses that established identity rather than inventing Umaril.

Lorekeeper should keep official Umaril biography separate from the mod's added Mary/Varla material.
