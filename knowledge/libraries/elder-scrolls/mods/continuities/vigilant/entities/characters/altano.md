## Record-backed biography

Altano's surviving journal says his first remembered experience was waking in a snowfield on 6 Morning Star 4E 197 with no memory of his name or origin. Jacob, an older Vigilant, rescued him and gave him the name Altano. The journal says he joined the Vigilants within months but never recovered his earlier memories.

At the beginning of the main Vigilant chain, Altano recruits the player and serves as guide and mission partner. His assignments move from vampires and Daedra toward increasingly coercive moral judgments.

In *The Eye of Madness*, after the player reports that Balor is not hostile and has no evil intentions, Altano still insists Balor must die because he might someday lose control. Refusal is answered with the threat that the player cannot remain a Vigilant.

By *Old Regrets* and *No Mercy*, Altano asks the player to bring him Bal's mace and orders the killing of a witch and her child. The quest itself contains optional objectives to negotiate with or fight Altano.

In *The Endless Fall*, Gwyneth says Altano summoned the Daedra that devastated the Temple. Altano later addresses a lord, claims to have corrupted many souls in hopes of gaining a place in that lord's realm, and tries to have the player killed.

In *The Landing*, a dying Altano says that after the battle with Bal he heard a compelling voice, followed it, gave in to temptation, and asks forgiveness. This is Altano's own explanation; it does not automatically prove that all of his earlier harsh choices were mind control.

## Arc

The ESM supports: **amnesiac initiate → confident mentor → coercive enforcer → corrupted betrayer → remorseful dying figure**.
