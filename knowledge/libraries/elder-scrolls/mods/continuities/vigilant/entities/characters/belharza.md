# Belharza — VIGILANT continuity

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Forms: `02510B22` `zzzCHMntrBelharza`; `02137E63` `zzzCHBelharzaMemory`; `020E5E2E` `zzzCHEmperorBelharza`; `0251D61C` `zzzCHBelharzaBull`
- Roles: Emperor/memory figure; later Man-Bull prisoner/restoration figure

## Knight of Hounds memory

The Emperor Belharza memory presents a ruler directing campaigns and supporting the Alessian Order's use of Malada. It escalates into explicit extermination orders against Ayleids, including noncombatants and children. Belharza addresses a knight named Varla as a son-like figure and breaks with him when Varla will not kill an Ayleid woman.

This is not silently harmonized with later Man-Bull testimony; the contrast may be character history, altered memory, or part of VIGILANT's critique of Alessian history.

## Man-Bull testimony

The later Belharza says the Alessian Order stripped him of effective power and moved toward a human-only order that persecuted non-humans as well as elves. He claims the Amulet of Kings prevented his murder, but that his body/power were mutilated and a puppet was made from his horn.

He identifies himself as the son of Alessia and Morihaus. He says childhood rumors caused doubt until the Amulet of Kings recognized him after Alessia's death.

His account of Morihaus is unusual: he remembers his father's appearance and touch changing and proposes that Morihaus may be the result of many rebels' anecdotes/spirits accumulating into a divine vessel. He applies similar instability to the player and connects it to Akatosh. These are Belharza's interpretations, not Lorekeeper conclusions.
