# Lamthor

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: Bruiant Mansion butler/servant
- Primary authored source: `BOOK 02054EC6` — **Note to Master Marcus**
- Death: 20 Sun's Dawn, 4E 201 in Marcus' journal

## Warning about Julius and Bal

Lamthor writes to Marcus that:
- Julius has acted increasingly strangely since the Wild Elf's arrival;
- Julius was playing with the dogs while muttering unusual incantations;
- Lamthor believes the Wild Elf taught him;
- Lamthor asks Marcus to dismiss the Wild Elf.

The observation of Julius and the dogs is direct.
The claim that Bal taught Julius is Lamthor's inference.

## Death

Marcus records that Lamthor is killed by his own dogs.

The dogs are frenzied and Bartholo calms them with magic.
Bartholo says someone cast a frenzy spell.

This creates a strong narrative echo with Lamthor's earlier dog/incantation warning.

It does not prove Julius or Bal caused Lamthor's death.
