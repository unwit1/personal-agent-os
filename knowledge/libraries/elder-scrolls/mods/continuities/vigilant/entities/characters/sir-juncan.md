## Direct quest identity

The **Knight of Kynareth** quest directly binds both its boss and sane aliases to Sir Juncan.

This resolves the former source-gap stub.

## Flying Worm

Juncan says:
- another Vigilant challenged a “flying Worm”;
- he heard screaming and then silence;
- the creature is a flying Daedroth despite its awkward body.

The exact identity of this “flying Worm” should remain separate from the **Black Worm** unless direct evidence connects them.

## Melus

Juncan warns the player not to disturb Melus.

He says:
- Melus spends his time talking to a corpse;
- even madmen have something like inner peace;
- the player should leave that peace undisturbed.

This directly cross-links:
- Knight of Kynareth
- Paladin Melus

## Sir Gregory

Juncan says he is guarding **Sir Gregory** on an artistic pilgrimage.

He describes Gregory as:
- competent with a sword;
- possessing art/taste that Juncan lacks;
- funny and good;
- stubborn.

This upgrades the Gregory cluster from note-only evidence to direct knight testimony.

## Varla

Juncan says he has heard that Varla:
- is insane;
- is a human/elf half-breed;
- hunted elves and later humans;
- went mad because he belonged fully to neither bloodline.

This is Juncan's rumor/explanation, not neutral fact.

It should be stored beside:
- Henrik's political account;
- Ritho's moral-change testimony;
- a123999's Mary/Umaril parentage layer.

## Pepe and the slums

Juncan says:
- the slums lead toward the prison tower and Imperial City according to Pepe;
- he does not trust Pepe;
- Pepe may not be lying but is hiding something.

This is useful testimony about Pepe's reputation among other Coldharbour figures.
