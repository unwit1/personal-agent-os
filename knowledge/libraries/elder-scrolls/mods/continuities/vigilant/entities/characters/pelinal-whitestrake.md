## VIGILANT portrayal

The first Pelinal memory presents Pelinal reflecting on himself as a storm/rage that enabled repeated battle and atrocity. He explicitly includes destroyed villages and killed noncombatants, then reinterprets the rain after battle as Kyne mourning the innocents rather than comforting him.

The sequence ends with Pelinal rejecting further bloodshed and describing an image of domestic peace—a wheat field, a woman, and a child playing with a wolf—as what he finally understands the wind of Kyne to mean.

Following memory material feeds into *After the Storm*, where Morihaus' recollections juxtapose Pelinal as a teacher about love with later orders and bloodlust. Lorekeeper keeps remorseful Pelinal, violent memory fragments, and boss representation connected but not flattened into a simple chronology until scene ordering is complete.
