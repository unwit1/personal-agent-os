# Alessia — VIGILANT continuity

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Forms: `020708BE` `zzzCHStAlessiaMemoryGhost`; `022BC383` `zzzCHMemoryStAlessiaOld`; `0213206F` `zzzCHStAlessia`
- Memory contexts: *Temptation of Marukh*, *The Final Night*, and related Coldharbour material

## Marukh memory

A ghost/memory form of Alessia addresses Marukh and commands him to fill the Stone, restore the Adabal, and place it in the Tower. She tells him this will end an endless war and frames the task as his mission.

Because the same broader sequence includes explicit memory manipulation and Molag Bal-linked material, this voice is recorded as a VIGILANT memory representation rather than unqualified historical Alessia.

## Final Night

An older Alessia memory form appears in *The Final Night*. Its resolved lines are sparse and personal: she welcomes someone back, asks whether "her" was found, expresses hope that a way will be found, and says goodbye. Scene ordering is needed before assigning unnamed pronouns.

## Belharza's testimony

Belharza identifies Alessia as his mother and Morihaus as his father. He describes her ideals as something he failed to preserve fully and says his later struggle against the Alessian Order is partly an attempt to preserve a remnant of what she wanted. This is Belharza's testimony, not neutral narration.
