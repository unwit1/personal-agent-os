On 17 Frostfall, 4E 200, Marcus records that:
- Heather falls down the stairs;
- she breaks her neck;
- her home was in or near Silvenar, Valenwood;
- Marcus plans to pay to return her body.

Marcus describes her as clumsy, but says the accident was extreme even by her standards.
