# Julia Bruiant / Julia the Crimson

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Quest alias form: `NPC_ 020430BE` / `zzzCOBossWitchCrimson` / **Julia the Crimson**
- Role: wife of Marcus Bruiant; mother of Julius; central absent/dead figure behind the mansion crisis

## Surviving evidence

Julia's short letter to Marcus says she is leaving him and taking Julius. Her surviving journal has been overwritten almost completely with the word "Die," leaving its original contents unreadable.

Marcus records that Julia and Julius vanished in 4E 200. Julius was later found alone in the forest. A Vigilant named Jacob subsequently brought Marcus Julia's ring and reported that her body had been found at Lake Ilinalta, burned in a supernatural manner.

`BOOK 0206592D` / *Ilinalta Report 4E 200* independently describes Julia Bruiant's corpse as suffering severe burns while continuing to burn from within. It says her blood had become acidic/poisonous and that a curse on the corpse was unnaturally easy to dispel, as if the caster allowed it.

The *Child of Oblivion* quest later contains a unique alias named `Julia` bound to `zzzCOBossWitchCrimson`, establishing that VIGILANT reintroduces a Julia representation/form during the mansion story. The precise relationship between the dead historical Julia, curse, and boss form requires stage/placement/script reconstruction before describing resurrection or transformation mechanics.

## Reliability note

Julia herself provides almost no intact testimony. Her dossier therefore distinguishes her letter, corrupted journal, Marcus' account, the Ilinalta report, and the later quest form rather than merging them into one narrator.
