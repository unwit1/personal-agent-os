## Documentary role

*Lamae's Kin* attributes its last known Second Era outbreak account to **the priest Ainam**.

The book says Ainam's records place the outbreak:
- on the **7th of Frostfall, 2E 582**;
- in a small fishing village near **Lake Ilinalta**;
- ten years after the Akaviri invasion.

The exact institution, temple, or order to which Ainam belonged is not named in the persisted text.

## Outbreak account attributed to Ainam

The cited record says:
- the village population was killed;
- a local boy about ten years old was found among the remains;
- his body was covered in pale tumors;
- he was still partly conscious and muttered family names;
- by the time the Mages Guild arrived, he had become violently vampiric;
- the Guild incinerated him;
- his ashes were placed under Arcane University supervision;
- those ashes were later allegedly lost during turmoil involving a winged Daedroth near the end of the Second Era;
- small fires repeatedly appeared around Lake Ilinalta for months afterward.

These are claims transmitted through the book's author using Ainam's records; they are not equivalent to a directly witnessed scene.
