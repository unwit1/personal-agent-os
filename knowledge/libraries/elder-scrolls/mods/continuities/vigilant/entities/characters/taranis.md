# Taranis

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: cursed husband of Carene and father of Lilian; documentary catalyst for the *No Mercy* family tragedy
- Primary document: `BOOK 02013BE9` — **Letter from Taranis**
- Supporting document: `BOOK 02013BE8` — **Letter from Carene**
- Direct NPC FormID: **not yet persisted in the current normalized layer**

## Family

The exact family letters directly establish:
- **Carene** as Taranis's partner/wife;
- **Lilian** as their daughter.

Taranis explicitly looks forward to living with Carene and “our daughter” again.

Carene's letter says Lilian made Taranis a flower crown and warns him to be careful.

## The sword curse

Taranis's letter says he is afflicted by **the sword's curse**.

He reports meeting a female summoner who claims she can:
- free him from that curse;
- in exchange for one job.

Taranis treats this as the possibility of finally returning to normal family life.

The current direct document does not name:
- the sword;
- the summoner;
- the requested job.

Those identities should remain unresolved until a direct record supplies them.

## Carene's corroboration

Carene's direct No Mercy dialogue says she learned alchemy/medicine from Glenmoril witches because she was trying to break her husband's curse.

Her letter to Taranis separately refers to:
- his search for someone who could lift the curse;
- her fear that the proposed cure is dangerous.

This independently corroborates the curse/cure setup from the family side.

## No Mercy / Art of Mercy consequences

The family crisis feeds directly into:
- `QUST 0200EA8A` / **No Mercy**
- `QUST 024D0376` / **Art of Mercy**

Carene's Art of Mercy dialogue says Taranis was killed.

That death is central to why surviving Carene can remain grateful for being spared yet refuse to forgive the player.

The persisted direct layer does not currently provide:
- Taranis's death scene;
- his killer as a deterministic actor;
- a normalized NPC base;
- the exact chain connecting the unnamed summoner's job to his death.

Do not fill those gaps from inference.

## Documentary role

Taranis is currently best represented as a **documentary/off-screen character** whose importance comes from the family documents and the consequences narrated by Carene.

His source value is high despite the missing actor record because he explains:
- why Carene sought forbidden Glenmoril knowledge;
- why the family became vulnerable to suspicion;
- what Lilian was waiting for;
- why the later mercy route remains morally costly.
