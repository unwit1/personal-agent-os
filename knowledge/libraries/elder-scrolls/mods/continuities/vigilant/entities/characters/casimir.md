# Casimir

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Implemented remains: `CONT 023B3504` — **Burnt Casimir**
- Location: **Prayer Hall** (`CELL 020CF815`)
- Associated relic: `ARMO 023B3500` — **Arcana of Jhunal**
- Speaking NPC form: not identified in the current persisted corpus

## Environmental evidence

VIGILANT physically implements Casimir not primarily as a speaking character, but as **Burnt Casimir**, a container/remains record in the Prayer Hall.

The container carries **Arcana of Jhunal**.

This creates a strong structural association between Casimir's destroyed state and Jhunal's magical tradition.

## Sir Berich's account

Sir Berich says that Casimir got too close to a central eternal fire rune and was overtaken by it.

Berich also attributes that rune to one of the **Wisdoms of Magnus** allegedly stolen by Jhunal from Saarthal.

Taken together, the environmental and testimonial evidence supports:

**Casimir encountered the Jhunal-associated fire-rune complex and was destroyed or transformed by it.**

The exact mechanics remain Berich's explanation rather than direct neutral narration.

## Arcana of Jhunal

Because **Arcana of Jhunal** is physically inside Burnt Casimir, Casimir is one of the strongest context anchors for that relic.

This does not by itself prove:
- Casimir owned the Arcana before the accident;
- the Arcana caused the burning;
- the Arcana is the fire rune itself;
- the Arcana is identical to Marukh's Torch.

Those are plausible relationships that require stronger source support.
