## No Mercy

Carene says she prepares salves and medicine and earns her living through alchemy. She admits learning from the witches of Glenmoril but denies being one of them; her stated reason was to break her husband's curse.

Altano orders the player to kill the witch and her child, while the quest objectives include optional negotiation with Altano and optional combat against him. Carene therefore becomes a concrete test of the difference between Altano's formulation of "Stendarr's mercy" and the player's choice.

## Art of Mercy

The later `zzzAoMMqGoodEnd` / *Art of Mercy* preserves consequences if Carene and her daughter survive. Carene is grateful they were spared but says she cannot forgive the player because her husband Taranis was killed. Multiple dialogue branches allow the confrontation to continue through anger, vengeance, or violence.

Sparing Carene is therefore not presented as erasing prior harm.
