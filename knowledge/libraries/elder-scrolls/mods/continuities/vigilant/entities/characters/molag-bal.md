## Child of Oblivion

The Molag Bal form attached to *Child of Oblivion* presents himself as both tempter and rescuer. He says Julius' hatred will burn the player and offers a way out, contrasting his offered salvation with the weakness of the Aedra. The same dialogue expresses hatred of creation/Mundus and a desire to consume the Aetherius perceived inside mortals.

A recurring VIGILANT pattern emerges: domination/submission is framed as relief from suffering, hunger, freezing, despair, or death.

## Coldharbour memories

The memory-tail form is structurally attributed across *Temptation of Marukh* and *The Nameless Bard*. In Marukh's sequence it shifts between a dying first-person wanderer and an overt tempter demanding that the Stone be filled with souls. A branch lets the player challenge the figure with the claim that it used to be mortal; the figure says it does not remember what happened long ago rather than providing a clean historical confirmation.

In *The Nameless Bard*, this form narrates ancient suffering, transformation, repeating dreams, and eventually explicitly identifies itself as **Molag Bal, Prince of domination and enslavement**. Because VIGILANT presents this through memories/dreams and material that explicitly involves memory manipulation, Lorekeeper records it as central VIGILANT testimony rather than literal base-canon history.

## Altano and Lamae links

Altano's arc links his fall to Bal's mace and later temptation; his dying dialogue says he heard a compelling voice after the battle with Bal. *The Blood Matron* explicitly asks the player to free Lamae Bal from **the curse of Molag Bal**.
