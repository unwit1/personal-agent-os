## Direct VIGILANT prophecy

*A Vision in Malada* gives the clearest direct narrative statement.

Stanza 8 ends with a brother taking the burning Stone after killing his own brother.

Stanza 9 then says:
- the surviving brother is killed by an **unnamed slave trader**;
- a gate opens;
- the burning Stone looks into the slave trader's eyes;
- the slave trader acknowledges it and passes through the gate.

This makes the slave trader a direct participant in the Stone itinerary, not merely an inference from later commentary.

## Pepe's independent memory

`INFO 0212F271` provides a second VIGILANT anchor.

Pepe remembers:
- another living person emerging from a sarcophagus before the player;
- that person being, in Pepe's understanding, a **slave trader**;
- the man's eyes burning like fire.

The prophecy and Pepe testimony therefore converge on an **unnamed fiery-eyed slave trader associated with a gate/Stone transition and sarcophagus survival**.

They do not yet supply a personal name or NPC FormID.

That literary ending is notable because a123999's supplementary VIGILANT background article says the Slave Trader is the soul of the protagonist from Vicn's earlier *LST Bravil Underground* and that his life ended with writing a poem.

This is meaningful authorial/background corroboration, but the earlier-work identity remains **supplementary**, not a direct VIGILANT personal-name resolution.
