## Direct report biography

*Report: Sacred Anatomancer*, written by **Thorondir**, says the Anatomancer:

- was originally a simple fisher near Lake Ilinalta;
- lost his mind and slaughtered his own family;
- fled to Riften and disappeared into the Ratway;
- reappeared in winter **4E 198**;
- began recruiting Vigilants of Stendarr;
- organized a splinter group called the **Society of the Holy Pipers**;
- led that group into kidnapping and torture;
- harvested organs and guts to create works compared to Ayleid flesh art.

This is direct VIGILANT ESM text.

Exact source:
- `books/024E7F91-report-sacred-anatomancer.txt`

## Copies and hollow bodies

Thorondir says defenders had already defeated **dozens of the very same Anatomancer**.

The report's interpretation is that he somehow:
- splits himself into copies;
- keeps reappearing;
- may derive this power from an unidentified Daedric pact.

The report also says the imprisoned Anatomancer:
- needs no food;
- has **no organs left**;
- is literally hollow;
- remains alive anyway.

These claims are written by Thorondir and should remain attributed where metaphysical mechanism is concerned.

Structurally, the ESM does contain multiple Anatomancer-related NPC forms and placements, which strongly supports the repeated-body motif without proving Thorondir's exact theory.

## Quest structure

`QUST 024D4C3D` contains objectives including:

- **Find the Anatomancer**
- **Prove yourself to the Anatomancer**
- **Talk to the Anatomancer**
- **Help the Anatomancer (Evil)**
- **Snatch a two-legged sheep**
- **Return to the Anatomancer**
- **Put the sack into the grinder**
- **Activate the grinder**
- **Listen to the Song of Life**
- **Kill the Anatomancer (Good)**
- **Examine the Anatomancer's corpse**
- **Divine the future from the guts**

The quest therefore explicitly supports both:
- a cooperation/evil route;
- a kill/examine/good route.

## Pale Feet

The quest's **Anatomancer** alias directly points to:
- `NPC_ 024D3A1A`
- EditorID `zzzAoMSq02Anatomancer`
- display name **Pale Feet**

Pale Feet's dialogue defines **anatomancy** as:
> divination from the guts

He asks the player to bring:
- a “sheep walking on two legs”;
- preferably a young victim “so full of future.”

The victim NPC is:
- `NPC_ 024D5E66` / **Urchin**

Associated quest items include:
- `MISC 024D5E6C` / **Sack with Urchin**
- `MISC 024D5E6D` / **Pale Feet's Guts**

This is direct structural evidence that the quest's Anatomancer identity can be represented through a named manifestation that is not displayed simply as “Sacred Anatomancer.”

## Radiance of Life

The quest repeatedly links anatomancy to **Radiance**.

Direct lines include:
- “Let's squeeze out the future. Let's squeeze out the Radiance of Life...!”
- “The radiance of life...”
- trial dialogue saying the player may already carry **the Radiance**;
- a gatekeeper instruction to “Seek only the Radiance.”

This makes Radiance a direct VIGILANT term, not something introduced only in later Vicn works.

## Blue Star

The trial sequence explicitly tests knowledge of the Stone and then says:

- if the Stone were **blue**, the player's story would already be over;
- after correct answers, the player is told they deserve to see the **Blue Star**.

This is direct VIGILANT evidence linking the Anatomancer trial to the Blue Star vocabulary later developed across Vicn.

Do not automatically equate:
- Blue Star;
- Radiance;
- Mnemo-Li;
- the Stone

without source-specific evidence.

## Orlando as vessel

The trial dialogue tells a player who has not completed the trials:

- follow the puppet **Altano** for a detour;
- follow the **vessel named Orlando** for a shortcut.

This is direct VIGILANT evidence that the Anatomancer system calls Orlando a **vessel**.

It should be cross-linked with later GLENMORIL/UNSLAAD Orlando/Owl material but not collapsed into one body theory.

## Laza and the Blood Matron feast

The final trial question asks whether anyone was late to the Blood Matron's feast.

One player answer names:
- **Laza**, “the only one of the nomads who survived.”

The response says that answer is “wrong in all the right ways” and confirms there was someone present who should not have been.

This gives direct VIGILANT support for:
- Laza as exceptional survivor;
- Blood Matron / nomad incident;
- anomalous participation in that historical sequence.

It strengthens the earlier a123999 supplementary claim about Laza's survival.

## Bard / Jyggalag

The trial asks whether Molag Bal sang of the bard's name.

The Anatomancer rejects that claim and says:
- Molag Bal does not know the bard's name;
- the bard was created as a **nameless puppet**;
- this is why **Jyggalag came to exterminate him**.

This is direct VIGILANT testimony tying:
- Nameless Bard;
- puppetry;
- Jyggalag;
- extermination.

It remains the Anatomancer's explanation rather than neutral omniscient narration.

## Song of Life

The quest's “Song of Life” material includes repeated themes of:

- repetition / iteration;
- “Love, I and We”;
- a dreaming god;
- a “faithless fourth philosophy”;
- threes and multiples of three;
- the Blue Star;
- redshift;
- names being discarded;
- sleeping hearts fused into one;
- flesh dispersed;
- the “sleepless Heart.”

These are direct lines but highly symbolic.

They should be preserved as **anatomancy/Piper liturgy**, not decoded into one literal metaphysical formula.

## Holy Piper mission

The quest directly includes:

**“Pipers! What is our mission?”**

The response orders extermination of:

1. **Hamah and her bloodline**
2. **Laza and those with the blood curse**
3. **the Bard and those who drown out the song**

This is direct VIGILANT evidence for Holy Piper enemies/targets.

The response specifically frames:
- Hamah's bloodline as threatening “our life”;
- Laza / blood curse as blocking “our Radiance”;
- the Bard as drowning out “our song.”
