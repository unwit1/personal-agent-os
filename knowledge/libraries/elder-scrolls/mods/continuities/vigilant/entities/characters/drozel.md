## Main Coldharbour route

Inquisitor Pepe directs the player to the mansion north of the Imperial City, identifies its resident as the mad king of Senchal, **Dro'Zel**, and says the player must kill him and take his **heart and key**. Pepe says the key opens the way toward the center of the city and the heart is needed to enter the Tower.

Bandit Kanra independently repeats the rumor that a cat named Dro'Zel can enter the Imperial City Tower.

These claims establish Dro'Zel as a route-gating figure in VIGILANT's Coldharbour progression even before interpreting his memory material.

## The Mad King memory

Dro'Zel's memory dialogue revolves around his beloved **Hasaama** and a disturbing bard's story of **Polydor and Eloisa**. He says the tale left him in a rotten state and resolves to stop the bard from telling it again.

The memory does not, by itself, establish the full causal path to Dro'Zel's later madness.

## Madness side quest

In `zzzCHSubQuest05` / **Madness**, Dro'Zel says the bard repeatedly returns even after Dro'Zel kills and dismembers him. He asks Hasaama whether he should kill the bard yet again. This connects the king's madness to VIGILANT's recurring pattern of repetition, returning roles, and stories that cannot be permanently killed.

The identity/nature of the bard must remain separately provenance-tagged because several bard roles in the memory sequence are dynamically filled or tied to Molag Bal imagery.
