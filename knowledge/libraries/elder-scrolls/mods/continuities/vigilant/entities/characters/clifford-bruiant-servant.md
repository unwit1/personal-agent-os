# Clifford — Bruiant Mansion servant

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: long-serving Bruiant family butler
- Primary evidence: Marcus' Journal, 4E 200

## Death

On 19 Sun's Dusk, 4E 200, Marcus records that Clifford:
- drowned in the courtyard pond;
- was thought to have died because of a heart illness;
- had served the Bruiant family since Marcus was a child.

Marcus describes Clifford as almost family.

## Evidence boundary

The journal records the medical explanation as what healers told Marcus.

Because the mansion is already deteriorating supernaturally, the death is narratively suspicious, but the source does not prove a supernatural cause.

Do not silently convert:
- reported heart illness
into
- confirmed curse murder.
