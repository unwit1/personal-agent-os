## Relationship to Jacob

Rahel is Jacob's wife.

Jacob later says that when he was dying, Molag Bal offered to revive him in exchange for **his wife's soul**.

Jacob accepted.

This makes Rahel central to one of VIGILANT's clearest coercive-bargain stories.

## 4E 180 — waiting for Jacob

Rahel's journal begins on 12 Second Seed 4E 180.

She writes that Jacob has been gone for two days after going underground in Windhelm to hunt vampires and that she fears he will not return safely.

The Windhelm report independently confirms a Vigilant expedition through the Maiden Statue during those same days and identifies Jacob as the apparent survivor found outside the city.

This is strong chronological corroboration that the journal and report concern the same crisis.

## 4E 182 — aftermath

Rahel's next surviving entry is 8 Frostfall 4E 182.

She wakes at their ruined house and concludes that Jacob no longer lives there and has **betrayed** her.

The journal does not explain:
- what happened during the missing two years;
- whether Rahel knows the terms of Jacob's Molag Bal bargain;
- whether “betrayed” refers directly to the soul bargain or to Jacob's disappearance.

That gap should remain open.

## Molag Bal's follower and the mace

On 10 Frostfall 4E 182, Rahel says a follower of Molag Bal visits her and gives her a mace.

Touching it gives her:
- a surge of power;
- magical knowledge.

The follower orders her to **spread the seeds of discord**.

This marks a clear transition from abandoned/betrayed spouse to active Daedric agent.

The journal does not identify the follower by name.

## Spreading discord

Rahel records later acts carried out under this corruptive mission.

### 4E 191
She gives a lycanthropy-causing potion to a little girl with a sick father, then hears screaming from the house.

Rahel says she still feels guilt despite trying to become numb to what she does.

### 4E 200
She teaches a grieving man a Daedra-summoning ritual by telling him he can contact his dead daughter.

She remembers his hopeful smile and reflects uneasily on how often her victims smiled before disaster.

These entries portray Rahel as:
- knowingly causing suffering;
- psychologically conflicted;
- still capable of guilt.

## Lost mace and Vernaccus

In the undated final entries:
- someone steals Rahel's mace;
- she becomes enraged and vows revenge;
- she says summoning **Vernaccus** was a mistake;
- Vernaccus uses a blast spell in a small shack;
- Rahel expects city guards and Vigilants of Stendarr to notice;
- she leaves Vernaccus behind to buy time.

This places her corruption work into the same broader Fourth Era world where the Vigilants remain active.

## Bal / Rahel identity — supplementary resolution

The current ESM scene index already contains a combined **Rahel/BalGhost** role in `SCEN 0200EA62`.

Two supplementary sources sharpen that connection:

- a123999's VIGILANT background article says Rahel became a servant of Molag Bal after Jacob bartered her soul;
- the official English voice-addon credits label the voiced role **Bal-Rahel**.

A community dialogue transcript of *Old Regrets* additionally presents Jacob recognizing Bal as Rahel during the confrontation and a later Rahel spirit exchange.

Current Lorekeeper classification:

**Rahel ↔ Bal is high-confidence as a VIGILANT identity cluster at the supplementary/transcript layer.**

What remains unresolved is the exact metaphysical implementation:
- whether “Bal” is Rahel's transformed body;
- a Molag-Bal-controlled manifestation;
- a role/name adopted after corruption;
- whether the later Rahel/BalGhost form is the same body or a post-death spirit.

Do not collapse **Bal/Rahel** into **Molag Bal** himself.

## A Vision in Malada — stanza 10

Stanza 10 is mapped strongly to Jacob and Rahel because Jacob's first-person account matches the image of:
- a dying Vigilant;
- sacrificing his love;
- receiving continued life / a “false heartbeat.”

The stanza's additional claim that the sacrificed woman becomes the Stone or is transferred north is not stated as directly in Rahel's journal.

Therefore:
- **bargain mapping = high confidence**
- **Rahel = Stone identity = unresolved / medium at best**

## Victim and perpetrator

Rahel's dossier should preserve both layers.

She is:
- a victim of Jacob's bargain and Molag Bal's manipulation;
- later an active perpetrator who deliberately spreads curses, lycanthropy, and Daedric danger.

The journal itself shows guilt and moral conflict, but does not absolve her actions.
