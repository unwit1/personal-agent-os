# Marcus Bruiant

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- NPC form: `0204BA63` / `zzzCOMarcus`
- Role: noble of Chorrol; husband of Julia; father of Julius; owner of the Bruiant Mansion
- Primary evidence: journals from 4E 199, 4E 200, 4E 201 and a final note

## Family and status

Gwyneth says Marcus belongs to the Bruiant family, made its fortune breeding military dogs for the Legion, and holds a Council seat. She identifies Julia as his wife and Julius as their son.

## 4E 199

Marcus' journal records an affair in Markarth. It describes spending heavily to acquire/free a woman from a Khajiit slaver, continuing the affair, learning she is pregnant, then expelling her and paying a settlement/donation after she appeals to the Jarl. The same journal mentions buying a Dwemer spider for Julius, demonstrating that Julius already existed and is not the unborn child from this affair.

## 4E 200–201 crisis

Marcus records Julia and Julius disappearing, Julius returning traumatized and mute, and learning from Jacob that Julia was found dead at Lake Ilinalta under bizarre burning/cursing conditions. He later hires Bal as a new butler and notes Julius' unusual attachment to him.

As servants die, Marcus hears unexplained crawling and has recurring nightmares involving Julia, Julius, blood/rust, and a burning red stone. He eventually seeks help from the Temple of Stendarr, leading to Bartholo's investigation.

Marcus' final note states that Julius is no longer human, calls him a Child of Oblivion, blames himself, and says he must take responsibility.

## Reliability

Marcus is a detailed dated diarist but also self-serving, frightened, guilty, and increasingly distressed. Lorekeeper preserves his chronology while treating causal interpretations and self-justifications as testimony.
