# Orlando the Knowledgeable

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- NPC form: `024419E1` / `zzzAomOrlandOwl`
- Role: enigmatic spectator/guide associated with the Spinner's Needle routes
- High-confidence dialogue coverage: 23 INFO records

## Story role

Orlando appears in both **Spinner's Needle 1** and **Spinner's Needle 2**, two optional routes whose quest objectives explicitly advertise that they can **skip to Act 4**.

In the first route he offers a needle that lets the player enter "blood-stained" dreams that no longer have a dreamer. He identifies Altano as possessing such a dream and instructs the player to stab Altano before he reaches the Temple. He then says there is no turning back and directs the player toward a wasteland and "dreaming dead."

In the second route he appears outside the Bruiant Mansion, calls himself a spectator, directs suspicion toward the butler Bal, and says the needle can pierce any curse. Again, completion sends the player forward into the wasteland/dream layer.

## Interpretation boundary

Orlando is structurally a route-breaker into VIGILANT's deeper dream/memory layer. The normalized records do not yet establish his ultimate identity or motive, so his self-description and claims remain attributed testimony.
