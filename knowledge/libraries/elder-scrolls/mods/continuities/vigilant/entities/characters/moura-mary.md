## Saint tradition

*The Eight Saints of Cyrod* says Moura was a healer born in High Rock whose true name was **Mary**. It credits her with exceptional healing powers and with cleansing Lake Ilinalta of vampiric contamination in a single night.

The same text says she was executed after conflict with the Alessian Order during the Thrassian plague and that later Alessian records erased her name.

That establishes `Moura = Mary` directly within VIGILANT's saint tradition. The historical details remain an in-universe account rather than neutral narration.

## The Grand Inquisitor: Mara = Mary the Dark Maiden

The strongest structural identity link comes from `QUST 0212C4F4` / **The Grand Inquisitor**.

Its aliases include:

- alias `Mara` → unique actor `020F9649` / **Mary the Dark Maiden**
- alias `Inquisitor` → memory form of Inquisitor Pepe

This means VIGILANT itself assigns the NPC later known as **Mary the Dark Maiden** to the role **Mara** inside the execution memory.

Pepe's dialogue in this quest addresses the silent figure as Mara, calls her a witch and heretic, and repeatedly says he will burn her at the stake. He also accuses her of interfering with Alessian authority and presses the Stone upon her.

The memory therefore provides structural evidence for the following identity cluster:

**Moura / Mary → the woman called Mara by the masses → the condemned woman in The Grand Inquisitor → Mary the Dark Maiden**

This does not require every appearance to be the same uninterrupted physical body. VIGILANT's Coldharbour memories and Stone manifestations are explicitly unstable media.

## Mary the Dark Maiden / Shoggoth Mother

`NPC_ 020F9649` is named **Mary the Dark Maiden** but has the EditorID `zzzCHBossShoggothMother`.

The same NPC is used by:

- `QUST 0212C4F4` / **The Grand Inquisitor**, under the alias **Mara**
- `QUST 024F69C0` / **VS Shoggoth Mother**, under the alias **Boss**

A placed reference, `ACHR 020F964C`, puts this form in **Bed of Corruption**.

VIGILANT also supplies:

- a summon form, `NPC_ 0212A7B0`;
- `BOOK 0212A7B4` / **Fragment of the Stone: Mary the Dark Maiden**, which provides the associated conjuration.

This is strong implementation evidence that Mary's later monstrous manifestation is not merely fan interpretation; the plugin intentionally reuses the same named actor base across the Mara memory and Shoggoth Mother boss framework.

The **mechanism** of that transformation remains unresolved without the relevant Papyrus/script bodies and broader scene logic.

## “Mary's children”

Coldharbour testimony repeatedly links Mary to plague-mutated creatures.

### Sir Caius

Sir Caius says the leech creatures were once mortals twisted by the **Thrassian Plague and Mary's blessing**, endlessly rotting and regenerating until they lose human form.

He also describes Mary as a priestess of Mara and healer capable of curing the Thrassian Plague, says the Alessians burned her as a witch, and after a later event remarks that **Mary died again**.

This is highly important testimony, but Caius is still an in-world witness rather than an omniscient source.

### Inquisitor Pepe

Pepe says some people call the leech creatures **“Mary's children.”** He also reports a hostile rumor that Mary consorted with Molag Bal and gave birth to the monsters.

That Molag Bal story is explicitly framed as something **“some say”** and should not be promoted to fact.

Pepe separately remembers burning Mary and wonders whether that was where everything went wrong.

### Atima

Atima speaks of Mary as a present caregiver:
- Mary made her a doll;
- Mary is kind to her;
- Mary is frightened of fire.

The fear-of-fire detail strongly echoes Mary's historical execution, but it remains character dialogue rather than proof of a specific metaphysical mechanism.

## Historical witnesses

Several NPCs independently preserve parts of Mary's persecution tradition.

### Ja'zhan

Ja'zhan says:
- Mary saved a boy from the plague;
- he personally saw the Alessians burn the **“maiden of Mara”** at the stake;
- she did not scream and eventually collapsed into ash.

### Pope Megus

Pope Megus remembers Mary as an exceptional healer, wonders whether she truly was a daughter of Mara, and says **“we”** should not have burned her.

### Sir Gregory's note

`BOOK 023399E3` / *Sir Gregory's Note 2* describes a statue of the healer **Mary** accompanied by a wolf statue. Gregory says the site appears connected to an old offshoot of Mara worship.

This is useful evidence for Mary's later saint/cult memory, but it is second-hand description.

## Pelinal the Bloody memory

Mary also appears as a separate NPC base:

- `NPC_ 022A0679` / `zzzCHSlaveMary` — **Mary**
- `QUST 022A532E` / **Pelinal the Bloody** assigns this actor to alias **Mary**
- `ACHR 022A2CD0` places her in **Burosel**

Six dialogue records resolve directly to this Mary form. Her lines are brief and vulnerable rather than explanatory.

This is best treated as another **memory representation of Mary**, not automatically as evidence that the Bed of Corruption boss body and the Burosel memory body are literally the same physical incarnation.

## Prophetic and documentary echoes

### A Vision in Malada

Stanza 5 describes an inquisitor, gathered firewood, a woman associated with Mara, and rising flames.

The mapping to Mary/Moura and Pepe is high confidence because *The Grand Inquisitor* independently recreates the same firewood/execution structure.

### Judgement

`BOOK 02054ED7` / *Judgement* describes an Alessian case where a follower of Mara heals a plague victim without Order approval and is condemned to burning.

It does not name Mary directly, so it remains a **strong parallel account**, not an independent name confirmation.

## Evidence model

The current evidence supports several layers rather than one flattened biography:

1. **Explicit identity:** *The Eight Saints* states Moura's true name is Mary.
2. **Historical persecution:** multiple witnesses and texts connect Mary to plague healing and Alessian execution.
3. **Memory identity:** *The Grand Inquisitor* assigns **Mary the Dark Maiden** to alias **Mara**.
4. **Coldharbour manifestation:** that same actor base is the **Shoggoth Mother** boss in Bed of Corruption.
5. **Recurrence motif:** Caius says Mary “died again,” while Atima speaks of Mary as an active caregiver.
6. **Separate memory form:** *Pelinal the Bloody* uses a distinct Mary NPC in Burosel.
7. **Contested theology:** claims that she was literally Mara's daughter, consorted with Molag Bal, or biologically birthed the monsters remain attributed rumors/testimony.

Current classification: **high-confidence Moura/Mary/Mara identity cluster with multiple memory and Coldharbour manifestations; exact ontology unresolved**.

## Canon boundary

Mary/Moura's detailed biography, persecution, connection to Pepe, Shoggoth Mother manifestation, “Mary's children,” and recurring memory forms belong to `tes.mod.vigilant` unless independently supported by licensed Elder Scrolls sources.
