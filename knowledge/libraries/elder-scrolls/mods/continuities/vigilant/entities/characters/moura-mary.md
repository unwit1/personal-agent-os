# Moura / Mary

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: saint/healer persecuted by the Alessian Order
- Identity: **Moura = Mary** according to `BOOK 020CB0DF` / *The Eight Saints of Cyrod*

## Saint tradition

*The Eight Saints of Cyrod* says Moura was born in High Rock, that her true name was Mary, and that she possessed exceptional healing power. It credits her with cleansing Lake Ilinalta of vampiric corruption in a single night.

The same book says she was executed after conflict with the Alessian Order during the Thrassian plague and that later Alessian records erased her name.

## Pepe's testimony

Inquisitor Pepe later remembers **burning Mary** and questions whether that act marked the point where everything began to go wrong. This links Pepe personally to the saint-persecution tradition, though it remains his testimony.

## Prophetic echo

*A Vision in Malada* stanza 5 describes:
- a Stone reaching an inquisitor;
- people gathering firewood beneath a woman;
- Mara weeping while the flames rise.

That imagery aligns closely with Mary/Moura's persecution and Pepe's execution dialogue.

## Related source

`BOOK 02054ED7` / *Judgement* describes an Alessian case in which a follower of Mara heals a plague victim without Order approval and is burned for heresy. The text does not name Mary directly, so Lorekeeper records this as a **strong thematic/historical match**, not an independent name confirmation.
