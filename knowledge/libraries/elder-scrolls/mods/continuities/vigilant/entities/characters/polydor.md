# Polydor

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`

## Direct evidence

Dro'Zel's memory dialogue refers to a bard's tragic story of **Polydor and Eloisa**.

The tale affects Dro'Zel strongly enough that he says it left him in a rotten state and resolves to prevent the bard from telling it again.
