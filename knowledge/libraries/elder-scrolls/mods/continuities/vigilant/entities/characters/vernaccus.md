# Vernaccus

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Direct NPC: `NPC_ 020ECCB3` — **Vernaccus**
- Major side quest: `QUST 021279A1` — **Archer of Kyne**
- Opponent: Bourlor
- Self-title: **Bane of Kyne**

## Archer of Kyne

Direct recovered dialogue gives Vernaccus a distinct speaking role.

He:
- greets the player as a Vigilant of Stendarr;
- identifies himself as Vernaccus;
- calls himself the **Bane of Kyne**;
- says the player humiliated him previously;
- promises revenge.

Bourlor separately says he has spent centuries practicing knife throwing because he wants another attempt at Vernaccus.

This creates a direct Bourlor ↔ Vernaccus conflict.

## Rahel chronology

Rahel's journal independently names **Vernaccus** in an earlier Fourth Era episode.

She says:
- summoning Vernaccus was a mistake;
- he used a blast spell inside a small shack;
- guards and Vigilants would notice;
- she abandoned him to buy time.

The identical uncommon name creates a strong identity cluster.

The exact mechanism linking the earlier summoned Vernaccus to the later Coldharbour/Act 4 NPC is unresolved.

Do not assume ordinary uninterrupted bodily survival.
