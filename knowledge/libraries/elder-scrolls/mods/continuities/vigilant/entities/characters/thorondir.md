## Sacred Anatomancer report

He records:
- the fisher/family-murder origin story;
- the Riften/Ratway disappearance;
- the 4E 198 reappearance;
- creation of the Society of the Holy Pipers;
- organ/flesh-art practices;
- repeated Anatomancer copies/reappearances;
- imprisonment of the current hollow-but-living Anatomancer.

His explanation that the power must come from a Daedric contract is explicitly an inference.
