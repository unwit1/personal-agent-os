# Sir Caius

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: witness to the Mary/Moura and Thrassian-plague tradition
- Exact NPC FormID: not yet persisted in the current browsable repository corpus

## Mary as healer

Sir Caius describes **Mary** as a priestess of Mara and a healer capable of curing the Thrassian Plague.

He also says the Alessians burned her as a witch.

This independently reinforces the Mary/Moura dossier's core persecution pattern:

**healer associated with Mara → plague relief → Alessian condemnation → burning**

Caius's account agrees in broad outline with:
- *The Eight Saints of Cyrod*;
- Ja'zhan's eyewitness testimony;
- Pope Megus's retrospective regret;
- *The Grand Inquisitor* memory structure.

Agreement across these sources increases confidence that VIGILANT intentionally treats Mary's healing and execution as a durable continuity fact, while details of her divinity or later transformations remain separate questions.

## Leech creatures / “Mary's children”

Caius says the leech-like creatures were once mortals changed by the **Thrassian Plague and Mary's blessing**.

He describes them as endlessly rotting and regenerating until their human form is lost.

This is significant because it gives a non-Pepe explanation for the creatures associated with Mary. Pepe reports that some people call such beings **“Mary's children,”** but also repeats a more sensational rumor that Mary consorted with Molag Bal and birthed them.

Caius's account should therefore remain distinct:

- Caius frames the creatures as transformed mortals;
- Pepe reports competing folk explanations;
- neither testimony alone proves the exact biological/metaphysical mechanism.

## “Mary died again”

After a later event, Caius remarks that **Mary died again**.

The wording is strong evidence that, from Caius's perspective, Mary's Coldharbour existence is a recurrence rather than a simple continuation of one ordinary mortal life.

That observation aligns with the plugin's multiple Mary manifestations:
- Moura/Mary saint tradition;
- the Mara role in *The Grand Inquisitor*;
- Mary the Dark Maiden / Shoggoth Mother;
- the separate Mary form in *Pelinal the Bloody*.

It does not, by itself, establish whether these are resurrection, memory embodiment, reincarnation, dream forms, or another Coldharbour process.

## Reliability

Caius is best modeled as an informed in-world witness.

High-value testimony:
- Mary as plague healer;
- Alessian burning;
- leech-creature transformation account;
- perception that Mary later “died again.”

Unresolved:
- exact mechanics of Mary's blessing;
- whether the leech creatures are literally Mary's children;
- exact ontology of Mary's repeated appearances.

## Relationship map

- **Sir Caius → remembers → Mary as healer/priestess of Mara**
- **Sir Caius → attributes → leech-creature condition to plague + Mary's blessing**
- **Sir Caius → reports → Alessians burned Mary**
- **Sir Caius → observes → Mary later “died again”**

## Canon boundary

Caius's testimony about Mary, plague-mutated creatures, and recurrence belongs to `tes.mod.vigilant`. The Thrassian Plague and Mara are established Elder Scrolls elements; the specific Mary narrative and transformation model are VIGILANT's extension.
