# Sir Berich

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: witness/interpreter of the Jhunal fire-rune tradition
- Exact NPC FormID: not yet persisted in the current browsable repository corpus
- Key associated location: **Prayer Hall** (`CELL 020CF815`)

## Jhunal and the eternal fire rune

Sir Berich describes a central eternal fire rune as one of the **Wisdoms of Magnus**.

He claims that **Jhunal stole** this knowledge from Saarthal and attempted to use the flame to drag down Magnus.

This testimony is one of the strongest links between:
- Jhunal's magical tradition;
- Saarthal;
- a destructive fire-rune complex;
- the later **Arcana of Jhunal** relic.

Berich's account should remain attributed testimony. The ESM preserves the relevant relic and burned remains nearby, but it does not independently prove every historical statement he makes about Magnus or Saarthal.

## Casimir

Berich also says **Casimir** came too close to the flame and was overtaken by it.

The physical implementation strongly supports a destructive event:

- `CONT 023B3504` — **Burnt Casimir**
- placed in **Prayer Hall** (`CELL 020CF815`)
- contains `ARMO 023B3500` — **Arcana of Jhunal**

This creates a direct environmental chain:

**Berich's fire-rune warning → Burnt Casimir → Arcana of Jhunal**

The structure strongly ties Casimir's destruction to the Jhunal-associated magical complex, although exact causation still comes through Berich's testimony.

## Relationship to Marukh's Torch

*The Eight Saints of Cyrod* attributes a secret art called **Marukh's Torch** to Jhunal.

Berich's fire-rune account is clearly related thematically and through Jhunal, but the current ESM evidence does **not** explicitly establish:

- eternal fire rune = Marukh's Torch;
- Arcana of Jhunal = Marukh's Torch;
- Wisdom of Magnus = Marukh's Torch.

Lorekeeper should keep those as connected but unmerged concepts until direct evidence appears.

## Reliability

High-confidence structural evidence:
- Burnt Casimir exists in the Prayer Hall.
- Arcana of Jhunal is carried by the Burnt Casimir container.
- Berich is an in-world speaker associated with this scene/thread.

Attributed historical claims:
- Jhunal stole the fire wisdom from Saarthal.
- the rune is one of the Wisdoms of Magnus.
- Jhunal intended to drag down Magnus.
- Casimir was overtaken specifically because he approached the flame.
