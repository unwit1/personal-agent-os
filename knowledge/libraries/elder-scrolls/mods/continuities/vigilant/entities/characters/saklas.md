- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Quest: `QUST 0251FD3E` / **Bald Man-Ape**
- Alternate form: `NPC_ 0251FD5F` — **Saklas the Man-Ape** (“owled” form in the existing extraction)
- Jhunal Library placement: `ACHR 0251FD60` in `CELL 02259C5B` / **Jhunal's Library**
- Key testimony: `INFO 0251FD4F`, `INFO 02531D0F`

## Jhunal investigation

Saklas's **Bald Man-Ape** quest directly investigates Jhunal.

He says the name **Jhunal** repeatedly appears in the souls of Alessian priests. He recognizes Jhunal as the name of an old Nord deity, but says the evidence also points to a **First Era person** bearing the same name.

Saklas then identifies **Jhunal's Library** as a target in the Imperial City and argues that it should be found before the Forces of Order can mine it.

The quest structurally supports this research path:

- alias `MapLib` points to `REFR 0225B236` / **Jhunal's Library** map marker;
- Saklas has a separate later/alternate “owled” form;
- `ACHR 0251FD60` places that Saklas form inside Jhunal's Library.

This makes Saklas one of the strongest in-world investigators tying the old Alessian Jhunal tradition to the surviving Owl/Library complex.

## Order mining and Dragon Breaks

Saklas also provides evidence about the active Greymarch.

In `INFO 02531D0F`, he says the **Forces of Order are mining for something** and refers to Jyggalag's library/ship. The existing Greymarch reconstruction treats this as character testimony rather than objective narration.

In `INFO 0251FD4F`, Saklas says this mining process had stopped in the First Era because of past **Dragon Breaks** and refers to a singularity that may eventually be resolved.

These claims are important because they connect three major VIGILANT systems:

1. the present invasion by Jyggalag's Forces of Order;
2. First Era Dragon Break history;
3. the search for Jhunal's Library and other buried/archival knowledge.

The current ESM evidence does **not** establish that the thing being mined is the Staff of Towers, Adabal, or another specific artifact.

## Owl transformation evidence

Saklas has a distinct alternate form identified in the existing extraction as an “owled” **Saklas the Man-Ape**, and that form is placed in Jhunal's Library.

This is especially notable because:

- Jhunal himself survives as **Jhunal the Owl**;
- Laza is surrounded by a White/Gray/Black Owl textual cycle;
- Saklas's investigation leads directly into Jhunal's Library.

However, the ESM-only structural layer does not safely establish the exact runtime event that changes Saklas into this form, who causes it, or whether “owled” represents bodily transformation, possession, disguise, metaphysical imprinting, or another mechanism.

The quest VMAD confirms stage transitions tied to Saklas's normal and alternate aliases, but the executable transition logic resides in external Papyrus `.pex` bodies that have not yet been ingested.

## Reliability

Saklas is useful but should be modeled as an **investigator and theorist**, not an omniscient narrator.

High-confidence structural facts:
- he owns the Bald Man-Ape quest role;
- his quest targets Jhunal's Library;
- an alternate Saklas form is physically placed in the Library;
- his dialogue explicitly links Order mining to earlier Dragon Breaks.

Testimony requiring caution:
- what the Forces of Order are actually mining;
- the exact nature of the singularity;
- the historical interpretation of the Dragon Break interruption;
- the ontological meaning and cause of his alternate Owl-associated state.
