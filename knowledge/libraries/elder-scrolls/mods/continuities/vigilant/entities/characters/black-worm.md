## Direct quest identity

The quest **The Black Worm** directly binds its `blackworm` alias to the NPC **Black Worm**.

This resolves the earlier structural uncertainty about whether Jhunal's “Black Worm” and the side-quest title had an instantiated Black Worm actor.

The quest definitely contains a Black Worm actor.

Whether that actor is specifically the same Black Worm who mutilated Jhunal remains strongly likely but still requires cross-record linkage if strict identity proof is desired.

## Prison dialogue

The Black Worm is imprisoned and repeatedly pressures the player to release him.

He says:
- “Open this cell, please. I want to get out of here.”
- refuses to explain who he is;
- refuses to explain why he is imprisoned;
- uses flattering/manipulative language after release/progress.

The dialogue is intentionally evasive.
