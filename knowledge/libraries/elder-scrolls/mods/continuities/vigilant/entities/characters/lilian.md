## Family

The direct family documents establish:
- **Carene** as Lilian's mother;
- **Taranis** as Lilian's father;
- Carene and Taranis as a couple;
- Lilian as their daughter.

Carene's letter to Taranis says Lilian made him a flower crown and hopes he returns before the flowers wither.

Taranis's letter to Carene says he hopes to live with Carene and **their daughter** again.

## Letter from Lilian

Lilian's own short letter addresses her father as “Papa.”

She says:
- she made flower crowns;
- one was for him;
- she wants him to get well;
- she wants him to come home.

The letter is important because it gives the No Mercy family a direct child perspective rather than leaving Lilian as only a quest objective.

Exact source:

## No Mercy

`QUST 0200EA8A` / **No Mercy** places Carene and Lilian at the center of Altano's order to kill a supposed witch and her child.

Carene's direct dialogue says she:
- practices alchemy;
- learned from Glenmoril witches;
- did so to try to break her husband's curse;
- denies being one of those witches.

The family letters reinforce that domestic context.

Lilian should therefore be represented as:
- a named child;
- daughter of the cursed Taranis;
- not independently established as a witch by the persisted direct evidence.
