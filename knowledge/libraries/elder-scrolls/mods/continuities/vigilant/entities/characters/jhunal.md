# Jhunal

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Historical role: mage associated with the Alessian Order and Marukh
- Speaking form: `0225B23C` / `zzzCHJhunalOwl` — **Jhunal the Owl**
- Additional form: `0225B23F` / `zzzCHEncJhunalFission` — **Piece of Jhunal**
- Location: `CELL 02259C5B` / **Jhunal's Library**

## Saint tradition

`BOOK 020CB0DF` / *The Eight Saints of Cyrod* describes Jhunal as a mage from Atmora who was banished from Skyrim and later joined Marukh's Alessian Order.

The book credits him with:
- extensive knowledge of magical runes;
- helping Marukh's influence spread through eastern Cyrodiil;
- creating a secret arcana called **Marukh's Torch**, said to be capable of burning an entire city;
- refusing to give that art to the Alessian Order and being exiled for it;
- passing the knowledge to his disciple **Cosmas**, who later used it at Malada and Pailune.

The same book claims later Alessian records credited Cosmas rather than Jhunal because Jhunal had been expelled. This is a VIGILANT-specific historiographic claim, not base-canon fact.

## Jhunal the Owl

The plugin contains a speaking NPC explicitly named **Jhunal the Owl**. Seven INFO records resolve to this form with high structural confidence.

His dialogue presents a long-lived, fragmented figure who:

- says the Black Worm learned secret arts from him, then used Jhunal himself as a test subject and tore him to pieces;
- says it took hundreds of years to recover to his current size;
- says he used **Laza** to harvest dragons;
- says he tried to tear a dragon's soul out and failed;
- says he wanted a dragon's soul so he could become a dragon;
- describes black souls as the essence of men, elves, and the Hist, calling their striving the **"Will to Shezarr"**;
- calls the red Stone a false imitation of Lorkhan's blood that can distort souls and interfere with the Tower.

These are Jhunal-the-Owl's own metaphysical claims and should remain attributed testimony.

## Other testimony about Jhunal

Sir Berich says a fire rune was one of the **Wisdoms of Magnus** that Jhunal stole from Saarthal. Berich says the flame burns forever, claims Jhunal tried to use it to drag down Magnus, and condemns Jhunal for spreading the stolen knowledge and causing destructive consequences.

Saklas the Man-Ape independently says the name **Jhunal** repeatedly appears in the souls/memories of Alessian priests. He finds it notable that Jhunal is both the name of an old Nord deity and apparently the name borne by a person in the First Era. Saklas sends the player to find **Jhunal's Library** before the Forces of Order mine it.

This makes VIGILANT's Jhunal deliberately ambiguous: deity-name, historical mage, surviving/owl form, and fragmented remains all overlap without the ESM proving that every representation is ontologically identical.

## Canon boundary

VIGILANT's identification of a First Era person named Jhunal, his expulsion from the Alessian Order, Marukh's Torch, the owl/fission forms, and his metaphysical theories are all mod-continuity claims unless separately corroborated by licensed TES sources.
