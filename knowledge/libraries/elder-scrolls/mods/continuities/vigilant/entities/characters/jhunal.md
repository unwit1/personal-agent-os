# Jhunal

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Historical role: mage associated with the Alessian Order and Marukh
- Speaking form: `0225B23C` / `zzzCHJhunalOwl` — **Jhunal the Owl**
- Fragment form: `0225B23F` / `zzzCHEncJhunalFission` — **Piece of Jhunal**
- Primary location: `CELL 02259C5B` / **Jhunal's Library**
- Direct high-confidence dialogue: 7 INFO records

## Saint tradition

`BOOK 020CB0DF` / *The Eight Saints of Cyrod* describes Jhunal as a mage from Atmora who was banished from Skyrim and later joined Marukh's Alessian Order.

The book credits him with extensive knowledge of magical runes; helping Marukh's influence spread through eastern Cyrodiil; creating a secret arcana called **Marukh's Torch**, said to be capable of burning an entire city; refusing to give that art to the Alessian Order and being exiled; and passing the knowledge to his disciple **Cosmas**, who later used it at Malada and Pailune.

The same account says Jhunal died before Cosmas inherited his art. The later ESM evidence makes that “death” difficult to read as a simple final bodily endpoint.

## Jhunal the Owl

The plugin contains a speaking NPC explicitly named **Jhunal the Owl**. Seven INFO records resolve to this form with high structural confidence.

His dialogue says that the **Black Worm** learned secret arts from him, then used Jhunal as a test subject and tore him to pieces; that it took Jhunal hundreds of years to recover even to his present size; that he used **Laza** to harvest dragons; that he tried and failed to rip a dragon's soul from its body with the “secret art of flesh”; that he hoped to acquire a dragon soul so he could become a dragon; that he calls black souls the essence of men, elves, and the Hist and names their striving the **“Will to Shezarr”**; and that he considers the red Stone a false imitation of Lorkhan's blood capable of distorting souls and interfering with the Tower.

These remain Jhunal's own metaphysical claims, not neutral Lorekeeper facts.

## Fragmentation inside Jhunal's Library

The physical placement strongly supports Jhunal's statement that he was torn apart.

All four Jhunal-related actor placements are inside **Jhunal's Library**:

- `ACHR 0225B23D` — **Jhunal the Owl**
- `ACHR 0225B241` — **Piece of Jhunal**
- `ACHR 0225B242` — **Piece of Jhunal**
- `ACHR 0225B243` — **Piece of Jhunal**

The Library therefore presents one speaking Jhunal plus three literal fragment actors in the same space.

The cell is also visually coded as a true archive/library: the ESM places dozens of book-tower statics there, an open Jhunal-book static, a dragon statue, storage, and a dedicated merchant chest.

Current interpretation: **the Library is the primary environmental presentation of Jhunal's fragmented surviving state**, not merely a location named after a dead scholar.

## Saklas and the Library quest

`QUST 0251FD3E` / **Bald Man-Ape** gives Saklas a direct Jhunal-research route.

Saklas says the name Jhunal repeatedly appears in the souls of Alessian priests; Jhunal is also the name of an old Nord deity; evidence nevertheless points to a First Era person bearing the same name; and Jhunal's Library is somewhere in the Imperial City and should be found before the Forces of Order mine it.

The quest contains:

- alias `MapLib` → `REFR 0225B236` / **Jhunal's Library** map marker
- a separate **Saklas the Man-Ape** “owled” form (`NPC_ 0251FD5F`)
- `ACHR 0251FD60`, placing that later Saklas form inside Jhunal's Library

The ESM VMAD shows stage transitions tied to Saklas's normal and “owled” aliases, but without the external PEX bodies it is not safe to state the exact transformation sequence or cause.

Its inventory contains:

This “corpse” is important evidence but should not be treated as proof that the Owl form is a different person. VIGILANT simultaneously presents a corpse/relic cache, surviving Owl, and literal Pieces of Jhunal.

## Golden ring and Jhunal's Egg

Jhunal's second ring is elsewhere:

- `ARMO 020CB0AE` — **Jhunal's Golden Ring**
- carried by `CONT 02224F3D` / **Jhunal's Egg**
- the Egg is placed as `REFR 02224FF1` in **Golden Sanctuary** (`CELL 02224E78`)

Pope Megus provides a strong contextual link. When asked about the golden egg, he says **the Owl** gave it to him and described it as a vessel for blood, flesh, and bones from which new life would spring. Megus also calls it a treasure of Saarthal while doubting whether it truly works.

Thus the Golden Ring is not merely a generically scattered relic: it is inside an object explicitly named for Jhunal and attributed to the Owl by a high-confidence speaker.

## Arcana of Jhunal and the fire-rune trail

`ARMO 023B3500` — **Arcana of Jhunal** is held by:

- `CONT 023B3504` / **Burnt Casimir**
- placed as `REFR 023B3507` in **Prayer Hall** (`CELL 020CF815`)

Sir Berich's nearby dialogue says a central eternal fire rune is one of the **Wisdoms of Magnus** that Jhunal stole from Saarthal. Berich claims Jhunal tried to use the flame to drag down Magnus and condemns him for spreading the stolen knowledge.

Berich also says Casimir got too close to that flame and was overtaken by it.

The physical placement of **Arcana of Jhunal** on Burnt Casimir therefore links a Jhunal-named relic to the destructive fire-rune tradition. However, the ESM does **not** explicitly prove that this item, that rune, and the saint text's **Marukh's Torch** are all the same spell/artifact. Lorekeeper keeps those as related but unmerged evidence.

## The Art of the Ayleids

`BOOK 0218B1BA` / **The Art of the Ayleids** is explicitly credited **“by Jhunal.”**

The surviving excerpt discusses Ayleid flesh art and extreme cruelty toward Nedic captives. Its authorship fits Jhunal the Owl's later claim that he practiced a “secret art of flesh,” but the book remains an in-universe authored source.

The ESM places copies in **Bruiant Mansion South Wing**, **Slave Trader's House**, **Prison Tower**, and **Fort Welkynd**. This gives Jhunal's writings a reach beyond his own Library.

## Laza and the Owl

Jhunal directly says Laza was a simple, obedient shepherd whom he used to harvest dragons.

Other VIGILANT sources repeatedly connect **Laza** with an Owl, including the *Laza and the White Owl / Gray Owl / Black Owl* texts and several speakers who describe Laza as deceived by “the Owl.” Jhunal himself admits exploiting Laza for dragon hunting.

The structural evidence makes Jhunal a strong candidate for at least part of the Owl tradition surrounding Laza, but the color-coded Owl texts should not all be collapsed into Jhunal without direct identity evidence.

## Supplementary Owl identity commentary

The supplementary VIGILANT background article by **a123999** explicitly identifies **Jhunal the Owl as the Gray Owl** and describes him as one of multiple “Jhunals” associated with Atmora.

The same source says Jhunal deceived Laza into attacking **Kyne's Garden** and catching dragons.

This lines up strongly with the ESM-level testimony in which Jhunal admits using Laza to harvest dragons, but it adds identity/intent information not directly present in the currently persisted ESM dialogue.

## Ontology / reliability

VIGILANT presents several apparently incompatible Jhunal states at once:

1. a First Era Alessian mage whom *The Eight Saints* says later died;
2. a corpse/relic container in Marukh's Underground Priory;
3. a surviving **Jhunal the Owl** who says he was torn apart and spent centuries recovering;
4. three literal **Pieces of Jhunal** in his Library;
5. bones, rings, a key, an Arcana, an Egg, and authored writings distributed across Coldharbour.

Current classification: **high-confidence Jhunal identity cluster with deliberate bodily fragmentation and unresolved ontology**.

These records support continuity of identity and motif, but they do not justify a simple “died → resurrected once” chronology.

## Canon boundary

VIGILANT's First Era Jhunal biography, Marukh's Torch, Black Worm experimentation, Laza manipulation, dragon-soul ambitions, flesh art, relic trail, Owl identity, and soul/Tower metaphysics are mod-continuity claims unless separately corroborated by licensed TES sources.


## Cross-Vicn Owl resolution

Later direct Vicn ESM evidence materially sharpens the Owl identity problem.

See:
- `analysis/vicn-owl-continuity-bridge.md`

Key later evidence:
- GLENMORIL directly resolves **White Owl → Jhunal the White**.
- GLENMORIL's dedicated **The Black Owl** quest uses **Jhunal the Black** as speaker.
- GLENMORIL pairs a **Gray Owl actor from the Orlando family** with Jhunal the Black.
- UNSLAAD has **Jhunal the Gray explicitly self-identify as the Gray Owl**.
- UNSLAAD's Gray Owl independently lists **Laza** among failed experiments and repeats the dream of becoming a dragon.

This strongly corroborates the VIGILANT Jhunal/Laza/dragon-experiment cluster.

It also means the earlier low-provenance comment-thread shorthand **“Black Owl = Orlando”** should not be used as a global identity rule. Later record-level evidence places Orlando-family material primarily on the Gray-Owl side while Black Owl material is directly spoken by Jhunal the Black.

The exact VIGILANT color-book identities remain source-limited until their own text is recovered.
