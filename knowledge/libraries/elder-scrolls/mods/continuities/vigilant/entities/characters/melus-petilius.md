Melus says that a graverobber repeatedly returns no matter how many times Melus crushes his skull.

The same image is repeated across the two preserved INFO anchors above. This makes the recurrence motif one of Melus's strongest identifiable narrative features.

The ESM-level evidence currently supports:

**Melus Petilius → guards/defends graves → repeatedly kills a returning graverobber → the graverobber nevertheless returns.**

The exact metaphysical reason for the recurrence is not established by the currently persisted source layer.
