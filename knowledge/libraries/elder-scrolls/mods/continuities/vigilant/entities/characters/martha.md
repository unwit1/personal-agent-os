## Funeral quest

Martha is the direct subject of the Act 4 **Funeral** side quest.

She is searching for the graves of her family and cannot find them despite searching for years.

She names the family members as:
- **Johan**
- **Simon**
- **Tlass**

The player can locate their graves in:
- **Chapel of Arkay's Cemetery**

Martha then says she can finally meet her family again.

## Living / skeletal forms

The quest contains two explicit aliases:
- **Sister** → Martha
- **SisterDead** → Martha Skeleton

This confirms a deliberate Martha identity transition/variant.

The exact stage at which the skeletal form replaces or accompanies the first form still depends on runtime stage fragments.

## After the reunion

Martha thanks the player and says she will remain for a while to indulge in nostalgia.

Her ambient farewell is gentle:
- she wishes the player a safe journey;
- tells them not to push themselves too much.

This sharply distinguishes the quest from the more grotesque **Funeral Temple** cluster elsewhere in Act 4.
