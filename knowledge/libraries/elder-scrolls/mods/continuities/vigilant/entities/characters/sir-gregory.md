# Sir Gregory

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: documentary witness to Mary/Moura shrine tradition
- Primary preserved document: `BOOK 023399E3` — **Sir Gregory's Note 2**
- Knights cluster: supplementary membership in VIGILANT's Knights of the Nine group
- Exact NPC FormID: not yet persisted in the current browsable repository corpus

## Mary shrine testimony

Sir Gregory's preserved note describes:
- a statue of the healer **Mary**;
- a **wolf statue** accompanying it;
- the site as apparently connected to an old offshoot of **Mara worship**.

This is one of the direct documentary anchors in the Moura/Mary dossier.

It supports the existence of a later religious/cult memory around Mary without independently proving:
- Mary literally is Mara;
- the wolf is literally a divine avatar;
- every shrine tradition accurately preserves First Era events.

## Wolf / Korn context

The supplementary a123999 background article says:
- Mary was accompanied by **Korn**;
- Korn is a wolf/avatar of Mara.

This provides an intended-background interpretation for Gregory's observed wolf statue.

Source:
- `sources/supplementary/a123999-vigilant-backgrounds.md`

Lorekeeper should therefore preserve:

- **ESM/document:** Mary shrine + wolf statue + Mara-offshoot interpretation.
- **supplementary:** wolf = Korn, an avatar of Mara.

## Knights of the Nine layer

The a123999 article includes **Sir Gregory** among VIGILANT's Knights of the Nine and says Molag Bal stole/retained portions of their souls.

That shared-fate claim is supplementary rather than directly established by Gregory's surviving note.

See:
- `analysis/knights-of-the-nine-cluster.md`

## Reliability

High-confidence documentary:
- Sir Gregory authored a note concerning a Mary/healer shrine;
- Mary statue and wolf statue are linked in his description;
- Gregory interprets the site as Mara-related.

Supplementary:
- the wolf is Korn;
- Korn is Mara's avatar;
- Gregory shares the Knights' Molag Bal soul-fragment fate.

Unresolved:
- Gregory's exact VIGILANT NPC form;
- exact location/date of the note;
- whether Gregory directly interacted with Mary or only studied later remains.
