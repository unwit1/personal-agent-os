## Varla testimony

Sir Henrik says that **Varla**:
- once ruled an eastern part of the Alessian Empire;
- became notorious for brutality;
- was eventually killed after his subjects revolted.

This is one of the direct ESM testimony anchors used in Varla's dossier.

Henrik's account is valuable because it supplies a political endpoint distinct from:
- Belharza's earlier foster-son/orphan memory;
- Ritho's personal memory of Varla's moral collapse;
- supplementary commentary about Varla's parentage.

## Relationship to Varla's chronology

The current combined sequence is:

1. *Knight of Hounds* memory — Varla is an Ayleid orphan raised by Belharza.
2. Ritho remembers Varla as once courageous/honorable and later changed by fear.
3. Henrik says Varla ruled eastern territory and died after revolt.
4. supplementary a123999 commentary says discovery of Varla's Mary/Umaril parentage contributed to his later madness.

Henrik confirms the **ruler/revolt/death** layer, not the parentage cause.
