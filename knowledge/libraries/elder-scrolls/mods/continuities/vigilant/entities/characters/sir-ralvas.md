## Direct quest identity

This resolves the former source-gap stub.

## The missing head

Ralvas's quest is built around his missing head.

He says:
- the head was already gone when he noticed;
- he was separated from his friends;
- he stumbled through darkness;
- he remembers colliding with a cat and suddenly feeling lighter.

Ja'zhan then offers a strange gold-looking “helmet” he found in the sewer for 100 gold.

The quest makes the joke structurally explicit:
- Ja'zhan's “helmet” is the route to recovering Ralvas's head.

## Headless speech

When asked how he can speak without a head, Ralvas says:

> I project my voice through my stomach instead of the throat. Like all of us Dunmer.

This should be treated as Ralvas's comedic in-world statement rather than a general biological rule about Dunmer.

## Restoration

When the player returns the head:
- Ralvas thanks them;
- the head is initially attached facing the wrong way;
- the player guides him through rotating it;
- he eventually returns toward Mathmalatu Priory.

Final intention:
- return to the priory;
- offer strength rather than money if the player needs him.
