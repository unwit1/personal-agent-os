# Pope Megus

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: Coldharbour religious witness and relic-context speaker
- Exact NPC FormID: not yet persisted in the current browsable repository corpus

## Mary / Moura testimony

Pope Megus is one of several independent VIGILANT witnesses who remember **Mary / Moura** as an extraordinary healer.

He wonders whether Mary truly was a daughter of Mara and says that **“we” should not have burned her**. This places Megus on the side of retrospective remorse concerning the Alessian persecution tradition.

Lorekeeper should preserve two distinct layers:

- **strong witness value:** Megus remembers Mary as a healer and the community/order burning her;
- **contested theology:** his question about Mary being a literal daughter of Mara is belief/speculation, not established ontology.

His testimony therefore reinforces the historical-persecution cluster without independently proving Mary's divine parentage.

## Jhunal's Egg

Megus is also a major witness for the **Jhunal the Owl** relic network.

When discussing the golden egg, he says **the Owl gave it to him** and explains that the Owl described the object as a vessel for blood, flesh, and bones from which new life would spring.

Megus also calls the egg a **treasure of Saarthal**, while expressing doubt about whether the claimed rebirth function actually works.

The structural record independently supports this testimony:

- `CONT 02224F3D` — **Jhunal's Egg**
- placed as `REFR 02224FF1` in **Golden Sanctuary**
- contains `ARMO 020CB0AE` — **Jhunal's Golden Ring**

This makes Megus an important bridge between:
- Jhunal's surviving Owl identity;
- the Egg/rebirth motif;
- Jhunal's distributed relics;
- the broader VIGILANT pattern of unstable bodily survival and reconstruction.

Megus's uncertainty matters: the repository should not upgrade the Owl's claimed rebirth mechanism into objective fact merely because the Egg exists.

## Relic economy

The VIGILANT relic system includes a death-item list named `zzzCHDeathItemPopeMegus`.

That list also participates in the broader distribution of saint relics such as **Nail of St. Dulsa**. The existence of reusable death-item/relic lists means relics carried by or associated with Megus should be interpreted partly as gameplay distribution unless a specific placement or dialogue context establishes stronger narrative meaning.

## Reliability

Megus is useful as a **historical and religious witness**, but not as an omniscient narrator.

High-confidence:
- he is a named in-world witness used by VIGILANT;
- he remembers Mary as a healer who was burned;
- he directly attributes the golden Egg to the Owl;
- Jhunal's Egg and Golden Ring exist structurally in the plugin.

Attributed/uncertain:
- Mary being literally a daughter of Mara;
- the Egg's claimed ability to generate new life;
- “treasure of Saarthal” as a historically verified provenance rather than Megus's received tradition.

Megus's Mary testimony, relationship to Jhunal's Egg, Saarthal provenance claim, and relic context belong to `tes.mod.vigilant`. References to Mara, Saarthal, and Alessian history draw on Elder Scrolls concepts, but VIGILANT's specific relationships remain mod-continuity material unless independently corroborated.
