# Vigilant Bartholo

- Continuity: `tes.mod.vigilant`
- Role: Vigilant sent to investigate the Bruiant Mansion before the player
- Primary document: `BOOK 0204C5CD` / *Bartholo's Journal*
- Associated conjuration item: `BOOK 02113C50` / *Fragment of the Stone- Vigilant Bartholo*

## Investigation

Gwyneth says Bartholo was assigned to the Bruiant Mansion before the player joined the relevant investigation. Thorondir considered the mansion's deaths coincidences; Bartholo disagreed and went alone.

Bartholo's dated journal records an initially quiet month, followed by renewed deaths. He detects Magicka traces leading to a basement totem which he associates with Molag Bal. He identifies a powerful blood curse, describes it as concentrated hatred, compares its style to ancient Ayleid curses, and concludes that its caster is paying a high price.

He temporarily dispels the totem but estimates four or five others may exist and requests reinforcements. In his final entry the curse has returned, the house is engulfed in fog, exits have vanished, and he believes he is trapped.

The reinforcement request is delayed by the civil war and reaches Gwyneth months later, providing the immediate hook for the player's mansion investigation.

## Reliability

Bartholo is an investigator making magical inferences under deteriorating conditions. The presence of the totem/curse is strong first-person evidence; its Molag Bal attribution, Ayleid comparison, number of remaining totems, and assumptions about the caster are Bartholo's expert interpretation.
