- **64** unique BOOK records have at least one placed reference.
- Spell/conjuration tomes are omitted from the narrative list below but remain in the raw 179-BOOK corpus.

## Interpretation rules

- Physical placement is implementation evidence that the mod makes the document available in that cell; it does not make every statement in the document objectively true.
- Reprinted official TES books retain their original text provenance even when VIGILANT places them in a story-significant location.
- Multiple placements of the same title can be narratively meaningful distribution, but should not be interpreted as multiple independent witnesses.
- Books with no placed REFR may still be script-granted, container-only, unused, or summoned; absence from this index is not evidence that the BOOK record is inaccessible.
