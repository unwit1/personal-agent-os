## Structural notes

- The ESM exposes a numbered main-quest chain with a visible gap around `MainQuest1`; this index does not invent a missing quest or assume the numbering maps one-to-one to release-order chapters.
- `ArnimaMainQuest12` (`Periphery`) is the densest main-chain quest in the source records by journal/objective surface and has several support quests (`...12Bog`, `...12Outpost`, `...12Damian`).
- `ArnimaMainQuest14` (`Iris`) has separate management quests for Arnima, Divide, Evermore, and Deepcrag, indicating a broad world-state/finale orchestration layer.
- `ArnimaMainQuestEpilogueBadQuest` (`New World`) is preserved as an explicit epilogue/bad-outcome quest record, but its source-record entry has no journal or objective text; downstream interpretation should rely on dialogue/scene evidence before making narrative claims.
