## Continuity cautions

- These quests present the Reach conflict from an Orc-aligned route and should be indexed as in-world/player-route evidence, not as a neutral correction of the Breton route.
- The sequence explicitly invokes Orsinium, Malacath, Breton-Orc violence, the Last King/other-realm episode, and the survival of the Orc community. Those subjects should retain route provenance in later entity and claim records.
- `Conatus` is the fifth numbered Orc main quest in the ESM. No later `ArnimaOrcMainQuest6+` record appears in the current QUST corpus, so this index stops there rather than inferring a continuation.
