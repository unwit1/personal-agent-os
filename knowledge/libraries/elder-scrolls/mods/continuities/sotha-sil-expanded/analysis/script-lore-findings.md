# Sotha Sil Expanded — script lore pass 01

The full 543-script scan exposes several lore-bearing systems and one important internal identity tension that a book/dialogue-only pass would miss.

## sse:script:sotha-end-upload

A direct endgame script voice identifying itself as Sotha Sil says Almalexia destroyed his corporeal shell but that he remains alive within the walls, gears and circuits of the city after a hasty, imperfect transfer; it offers completion of the upload/merge or severing the lifeline.

Evidence: SCPT Sotha_EndScript @216440.  Evidence mode: `direct_scripted_endgame_text`; reliability: `high_for_this_source_layer`.

Qualification: This conflicts/tensions with the included readme continuity boundary and later Gestalt copy/partial-upload framing. Preserve both layers; do not collapse them into a single resolved identity claim.

## sse:script:magic-schools

A six-part scripted magic-school discourse distinguishes Illusion as perception, Alteration as ontic change, warns that Destruction begets destruction, treats Daedric Conjuration as dangerous because immortal beings remember and resist binding, states Restoration cannot revive the dead, and describes Mysticism as paradoxical, solitary, and a Psijic way of life.

Evidence: SCPT MagnusLine1, SCPT MagnusLine2, SCPT MagnusLine3, SCPT MagnusLine4, SCPT MagnusLine5, SCPT MagnusLine6.  Evidence mode: `scripted_philosophical_instruction`; reliability: `medium_high_in_universe`.

## sse:script:pocket-cabal

The Pocket Cabal presents itself as a sentient magical/spell-twisting entity tied to Vehk, sanity theft, thought-books of the extreme east, and a prior containment that weakened when Sotha Sil fell into ruin.

Evidence: SCPT _pocket_linecontrol, SCPT pocket_cabalscript.  Evidence mode: `self_report_by_hostile_entity_plus_quest_script`; reliability: `medium`.

Qualification: Hostile/self-aggrandizing speaker; factual particulars need corroboration.

## sse:script:elder-scroll-experience

Reading the Elder Scroll is scripted as an overwhelming temporal/knowledge experience in which time is simultaneously nothing and everything and fragmentary knowledge remains afterward.

Evidence: SCPT sotha_elderscrollscript @214959.  Evidence mode: `direct_player_experience_script`; reliability: `high_for_depicted_experience`.

## sse:script:trans-aetherial-portals

Fyr-related portal machinery explicitly references a missing trans-aetherial component and scripted destinations including Tel Fyr, Gnisis, Sadrith Mora, Ald Sotha, Vivec and Mournhold.

Evidence: SCPT fyr_portalscript, SCPT fyr_portalscript1, SCPT fyr_portalscript2.  Evidence mode: `direct_implementation_text`; reliability: `high`.

## sse:script:powerworks-emission

The Grand Powerworks core emits an unidentified harmful wave/field that weakens the player, functionally modeled as a hazardous energy emission without asserting a modern physical radiation mechanism.

Evidence: SCPT _power_radiation @78514.  Evidence mode: `direct_player_experience_script`; reliability: `high`.

## sse:script:life-creation-mechanism

A Life Creation Mechanism can detect restoration and announce restoration of all life systems, giving direct implementation evidence for Clockwork City life-support/life-production machinery.

Evidence: SCPT Garden_Finish @114015.  Evidence mode: `direct_system_message`; reliability: `high`.

## sse:script:watch-enforcement

Clockwork City Watchmen enforce district access and can physically detain an exhausted player and take them to prison.

Evidence: SCPT Sotha_clockworkwalk @202532, SCPT sotha_slumdoor.  Evidence mode: `direct_implementation_text`; reliability: `high`.

## sse:script:pneuma-loot

Numerous death/loot scripts treat pneuma as a recoverable component from fabricants, and a Fyr-related interaction explicitly offers to deconstruct pneuma.

Evidence: multiple fabricant template/death scripts, SCPT fyr_fabpseak.  Evidence mode: `repeated_direct_implementation`; reliability: `high`.

## sse:script:cube-flower

Consuming the Cube Flower teaches Recycle Magicka, Purge Body, Transfer Rust, and Sonic Boom, linking a Clockwork botanical/construct item to direct spell acquisition.

Evidence: SCPT Sotha_CubeFlower @210612.  Evidence mode: `direct_implementation_text`; reliability: `high`.

## Sotha Sil / Gestalt identity tension

The current package contains three source-layer framings that should remain simultaneously retrievable rather than forcibly harmonized:

1. `Sotha_EndScript` speaks in first person as Sotha Sil and says the corporeal shell was murdered but the self remains within the city after an imperfect transfer.
2. The included project readme preserves Tribunal’s ending and says Sotha Sil remains dead.
3. Later endgame/dialogue material frames Gestalt as a copy/partial upload or regent and leaves open whether it becomes “Seht.”

Lorekeeper should represent this as an unresolved internal tension with source-layer provenance, not choose a single answer.
