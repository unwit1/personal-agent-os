# Sotha Sil Expanded — unique book findings

This pass covers lore-bearing books in the verified 3.1 source that are not merely duplicates of Wheels of Lull material.

## Sotha Sil's Last Words

`_sotha_lastwords` presents itself as the final messages between Sotha Sil and Divayth Fyr. Within this continuity it characterizes Sotha Sil as reflective about mortality, Tribunal divinity, Red Mountain, the Dwemer, Vivec's riddling nature, Almalexia's warrior identity, and the ambiguity of the Clockwork City's location. The text has Sotha Sil identify himself as **the Mage** and **the Clockmaker**.

Evidence class: attributed correspondence compilation. It is useful for this continuity's characterization and metaphysics, but should not be silently treated as Bethesda-canon final dialogue.

## A Type of Zero

`_sotha_zero` is explicitly framed as a traditional Dwemeri children's rhyme. It plays with unknowability, mapping Nirn, moons, numbers, folklore, and “a type of zero still to be discovered.”

Evidence class: literary/traditional rhyme. Use for Dwemer epistemic themes, not literal history.

## Temple Zero texts

`_cot_book1` (*The Null Manifesto*) identifies its speakers as the confraters of **Temple Zero**. Its doctrine places the temple conceptually at Convention, at the point where “Is” meets “Isn't,” treats Truth as prior to masks/gods/worldly sovereignty, and invokes Marukh as a guide.

`_cot_book2` (*King Oktug's Last Birthday*) is a mytho-historical narrative connecting Marukhati/Ald-Cyrod imagery, tower-dancers, early mannish hegemony, and elaborate esoteric terminology. It should remain provenance-scoped until names/events are independently cross-checked.

## A Traditional Sload Tale

`_cot_book3` is a literary Sload teaching story. Its framing associates great girth/age with knowledge and deceit and depicts corpse proxies, soul commerce, undead servants, old Thras, and extreme caution as culturally intelligible behavior.

Evidence class: cultural tale; useful for self-portrayal and motifs, not objective history.

## An Exploration of the Hist

`_cot_book4` is written as an Imperial field/linguistic account. Its narrator explicitly admits failure to establish stable one-word/one-meaning mappings in Argonian speech and records Huleej's claim that a “word” need not mean only one thing. The text treats the meaning of **Hist** as potentially broad and then presents multiple competing interpretations of a recited passage.

Evidence class: scholarly account with explicit uncertainty. Retrieval should preserve the ambiguity rather than flattening it into a single translation.

## Orc Creation Story

`_cot_book5` is explicitly a sectarian myth from an anti-Gortwog, Malak-oriented perspective. It retells creation and the Trinimac-to-Malak transformation through deliberately scatological symbolism and frames the transformed Orcs as a people of iron.

Evidence class: sectarian creation myth. Strong evidence for the beliefs/voice of this group, weak evidence for literal cosmology.

## Deliberately unreliable/gag material

- `_sotha_guarbook` (*A Child's Book of Guar Secrets*) makes absurd claims and ends by admitting the narrator knows nothing about guars. It is excluded from factual creature claims.
- `_sotha_truth` (*Book of Inevitable Truths*) is a one-line gag/insult and has no lore-evidence value.
- `sotha_scrollbook` (*Transmeta Interivatives*) is carried primarily by embedded images; text-only extraction is insufficient, so it remains flagged for visual-source review.

See `source-reliability.json` for machine-readable retrieval policy.
