# Sotha Sil Expanded 3.1 — source/lore analysis

The uploaded 3.1 package matches the current Nexus release for mod 42347. `Sotha Sil Expanded.ESP` is a TES3 plugin with 5,082 records excluding its TES3 header, exactly matching the declared record count. Its masters include `OAAB_Data.esm`, matching the 3.x dependency line. The included readme credits Trainwiz as project lead.

## Corpus

The source contains 49 BOOK, 250 DIAL, 1,147 INFO, 344 NPC_, and 460 CELL records in the core narrative layer, plus a large implementation layer including scripts, activators, creatures, equipment, factions, spells, and related structural records. Forty-four journal topics were deterministically indexed.

## Major findings

- The endgame centers on **Gestalt**, described as a copy of Sotha Sil's mind partially uploaded into the Clockwork City. The source preserves both save and destroy outcomes.
- The included readme explicitly preserves Tribunal's ending: Sotha Sil remains dead. Gestalt must therefore be treated as a copied/simulated continuation, not silent resurrection of the original Sotha Sil.
- Archcanon dialogue describes the surviving Gestalt as potentially becoming “Seht” while retaining uncertainty about what it has become.
- The Chronographer quest culminates in fabricants departing the city aboard a voidship to travel through time and space, chart/upkeep time, and give Imperfections to those who need them.
- Fabricant **pneuma** is directly described as a soul-battery. *Wind Up and Wound Down* describes Rewound as fabricant bodies whose mechanisms restart after pneuma/soul loss.
- The plugin's computational lore includes light-refractory computing capable of simulating a real mind that emotes, thinks, and learns.
- The books contain extensive shared text with Wheels, including How Two Moon, Musings on Power, De Rerum Mutabilitatis, Wind Up and Wound Down, KINMUNE, Harquebuses, Tatterdemalion, and The Cacophony.

## Evidence boundary

This is the current 2025 edition. Exact/current text identity with Wheels is now verified, but that does not itself establish that each text existed in the 2012/2014 historical Sotha releases.
