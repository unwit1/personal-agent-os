# Wheels of Lull Claim Registry

This registry normalizes high-value lore claims without collapsing evidence classes.

Evidence classes include quest journal narration, deterministic dialogue testimony, cryptic testimony, in-universe research notes, scholarly texts, travel accounts, and literary/apocryphal books.

Claims are scoped to `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull` unless explicitly promoted after external comparison. The tension registry records where a claim may conflict with, extend, or require special handling relative to broader Elder Scrolls canon; it does not silently choose one version.
