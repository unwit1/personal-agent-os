# Fabricants

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

## Evidence

- Llavados, Numinar, Avarri, Subori, Hammar, and other Chronographers describe themselves or are described as fabricants.
- `QUST 0500590D` — the player journal explicitly records Llavados as a fabricant whose glowing object is his soul/life force.
- `INFO 050488DB` — Llavados says experimentation on themselves and one another is culturally accepted despite casualties.
- `BOOK 05371591` — Wind Up and Wound Down provides a technical account of fabricant pneuma, unwinding, and Rewound bodies.

## Classification

A people/construct category rather than a single political faction; kept here because it is a central social grouping in the continuity.
