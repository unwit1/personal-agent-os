# Chronographers

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

## Identity and function

- `INFO 0500FC52` — Numinar identifies the Chronographers as fabricant servants of Seht who monitor time/space and maintain world mechanisms and Towers.
- `QUST 0500590E` — the player journal independently records that Numinar's order maintains mechanisms that run the world.
- Major named members include Century Numinar, Millisecond Llavados, Minute Avarri, Decade Subori, Medeliu Hammar, Decade Belarus, guards, and miners.
- The NPC corpus shows a recurring shared faction membership among major Chronographer NPCs (`05000996` and `0535720A`), preserved as implementation evidence even though the FACT records themselves were not part of the structured ingestion target.
