# Wheels of Lull

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

## Role

The Wheels of Lull are treated in the mod as underlying world mechanisms that can be manipulated, monitored, and in Archeron's case exploited.

## Evidence

- `INFO 0500FC52` — Numinar describes the Towers as pinpoints of the Wheels of Lull and says the Chronographers monitor the world's mechanisms.
- `INFO 0503E1A9` — Llavados says Archeron has an unusual understanding of the Wheels and has "hacked" himself into their mechanisms.
- `QUST 05005912` — the player journal states Archeron used the Wheels of Lull to make himself invincible.
- `QUST 05005913` — the player retrieves a Wheel of Lull and uses it during the final sequence.

## Scope

These are continuity-supported mechanics inside Wheels of Lull. They are not promoted into general Bethesda canon without separate evidence.
