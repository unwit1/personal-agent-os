# Memodermis

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

## Evidence

- `BOOK 05367181` — Message from Eternity describes Archeron extracting Memodermis beneath his fortress and calls it liquidized material of the Wheels.
- `BOOK 055400D7` — Archeron's Research Notes describe Memodermis as the "lifeblood of the world" / liquid equations, responsive to memories and shapeable by experimentation.

## Evidence class

Archeron's notes are experimental testimony, not neutral narration. Eternity's message is independent in-universe testimony and therefore useful corroboration that the material exists in this continuity.
