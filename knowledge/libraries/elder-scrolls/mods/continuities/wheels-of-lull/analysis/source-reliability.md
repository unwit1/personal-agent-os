# Source reliability matrix

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Reliability means usefulness for a class of claim, not an overall truth score.

## Direct plugin implementation

**Strongest for:** record identity, actor forms, aliases, quest ownership, scene role assignment, location/cell structure, message text, and condition logic.

**Limitations:** implementation can reuse voices or proxy forms for technical reasons. A shared voice class is not proof that every line comes from one person.

**Rule:** direct FormID/alias/SCEN bindings outrank voice-only attribution.

## Player quest journal

**Strongest for:** chronology, objectives, and what the player currently believes.

**Limitations:** journal narration can be provisional. MQ06's apparent-Fyr material is explicitly revised by the Archeron reveal.

**Rule:** use for event order and story-state, not as automatic final metaphysical truth.

## Millisecond Llavados

**Strongest for:** Memory, deepest world-mechanism layers, Chronographer secrets, Archeron's integration with the Wheels.

**Limitations:** admits his madness is partly a facade; speaks cryptically and withholds information.

**Rule:** high-value insider testimony, always attributed for cosmological claims.

## Century Numinar

**Strongest for:** current Lull-Mor operations, equipment, investigation procedure, Chronographer work.

**Limitations:** incorrectly identifies the player as the saboteur before the Archeron reveal.

**Rule:** strong operational source; deductions remain revisable.

## Archeron

**Strongest for:** his intentions, experiments, disguise, laboratory work, and self-authored notes.

**Limitations:** self-serving, grandiose, deceptive; associated forged documents exist.

**Rule:** separate observed actions from Archeron's theories and boasts.

## Memory

**Strongest for:** her own prophetic statements.

**Limitations:** extremely terse and symbolic; not conventional historical reportage.

**Rule:** preserve exact line-level wording and avoid over-expansion.

## Medeliu Hammar

**Strongest for:** the metaphysical model the mod chooses to place in his mouth.

**Limitations:** his Dwemer, Numidium, Talos, Oversoul, Tower, and creation claims are character theory rather than developer narration.

**Rule:** index aggressively, attribute always.

## Yagrum Bagarn

**Strongest for:** his own recollection and mechanical analysis.

**Limitations:** explicitly says he does not know for sure what happened to the Dwemer.

**Rule:** his uncertainty is evidence and must be preserved, especially when contrasted with Hammar's confidence.

## Wailway locomotive

**Strongest for:** first-person experience of being transformed and existing as an engine.

**Limitations:** most lines are contextual speaker resolution rather than condition-proven; ancient political claims are remembered testimony.

**Rule:** identify as high-confidence contextual testimony, not exact CTDA speaker proof.

## Chronography volumes

**Strongest for:** in-universe scholarly model of Chronographer history and purpose.

**Limitations:** explicitly use phrases such as "it is said" and "believed."

**Rule:** preserve scholarly uncertainty.

## Archeron's Diary / Research Notes

**Strongest for:** Archeron's plans, experiments, and interpretations.

**Limitations:** private research can still be mistaken; his metaphysical conclusions are not omniscient.

## Literary, forged, and meta texts

- `0552BAD3` *Forged Thalmor Orders*: explicitly forged.
- `0525D2A0` *Thomas the Screaming Tank Engine...*: literary/fairy-tale/satirical framing.
- `0549E1A5` *Cheater*: meta/punitive note.

**Rule:** never promote these as neutral historical documents.
