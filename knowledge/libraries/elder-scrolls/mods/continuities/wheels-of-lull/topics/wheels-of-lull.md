# The Wheels of Lull

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

The mod treats the Wheels of Lull as real underlying mechanisms connected to the operation and stability of the world. Different sources approach them from different levels of confidence.

Archeron says he has studied the Wheels alongside aetherial travel and soul gems. His diary describes knowledge of the Wheels/extensions and a plan involving Snow-Throat's Stone. Llavados says Archeron has "hacked" himself into the Wheels, explaining why ordinary attacks no longer work. Hammar provides the broadest metaphysical explanation, calling the Wheels literal clockwork underlying creation.

The deepest post-Archeron location is described by Llavados as the core of Nirn, Magnus' workshop, Sotha Sil's central mainframe, and Memory's room.
