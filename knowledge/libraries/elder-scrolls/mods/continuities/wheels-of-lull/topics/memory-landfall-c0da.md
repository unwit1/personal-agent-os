# Memory, Landfall, and c0da

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Memory's dialogue is the mod's densest prophetic cluster. Her lines are intentionally terse and should be retrieved at the source-record level rather than paraphrased into a single definitive theory.

Llavados supplies the explanatory frame: Memory is Sotha Sil's final creation, a child of mer, machine, and Lullian magic, intended to help future people survive a disaster that destroys the world and build a new world afterward. He says the Towers sustain her.

Memory says to "Know Love to avoid the Landfall," identifies herself as both mage and clockmaker, links oceans/thoughts/salvation, predicts a Thalmor victory, predicts "screaming brass," and says "The c0da does not bend. The Digitals have seen to that." Llavados separately predicts meeting the player again at Landfall.
