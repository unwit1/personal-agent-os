# Fabricants, pneuma, and the Rewound

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Fabricants are machine-bodied inhabitants associated with Sotha Sil and the Clockwork City. The mod repeatedly presents them as sapient people with specialized mechanical bodies, occupational roles, and replaceable/transportable components.

Llavados' early sequence distinguishes his visible body from the glowing component the player carries deeper. Underwater dialogue treats a working pneuma as something that can make a non-fabricant appear fabricant-like to Trademarks.

The book *Wind Up and Wound Down* describes fabricant death in terms of unwinding. It says some bodies can restart after pneuma/soul departure as "Rewound": behaviorally repetitive, potentially hostile machines that require slowing or stopping their mechanisms to defeat.

## Boundary

The book's model is an in-universe technical account; gameplay implementations and dialogue corroborate parts of it but do not make every theoretical statement omniscient.
