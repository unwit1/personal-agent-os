# Trademarks and the ocean

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

The mod presents Trademarks as engineered sea creatures/constructs associated with Sotha Sil and underwater access to his work.

Belarus says mortals call them sea monsters, krakens, or giant squid and that Sotha Sil built them as guardians. He describes the ocean as Sotha Sil's domain and says its waters are "our thoughts." A working pneuma can apparently trick the Trademarks into recognizing the player as a fabricant.

The book *Trademarks* provides a separate authored account that says Sotha Sil designed the creatures with inspiration from Dreugh and modified them for land/sea operation and electrical attack.
