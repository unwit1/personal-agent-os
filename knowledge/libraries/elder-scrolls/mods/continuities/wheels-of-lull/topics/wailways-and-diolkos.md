# Wailways and the Diolkos

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

The Wailway locomotive gives a first-person account of a Reman-era transportation project in which souls were bound into engines. It identifies the Diolkos/Resource Men as the industrial organization responsible and describes political punishment through fleshforging into machinery.

The locomotive remembers being a poet, says other engines hoped loyal service might earn them death, and asks the player to destroy it. It later says Reman sold it to a dark-elven clockmaker who studied it.

A separate book, *Thomas the Screaming Tank Engine And His Screaming Friends*, is presented as a fairy-tale/satirical treatment associated with Wailway failures. It is useful cultural evidence but low-reliability evidence for exact history.

## Reliability

First-person locomotive testimony is primary for its remembered experience. The literary book is not treated as objective documentation.
