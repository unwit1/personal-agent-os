# Power, energy, and metaphysical infrastructure

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

BOOK `05371592`, *Musings On Power*, is framed as mutterings of an unnamed **High Foremer**, recorded by the scribe **Alammu 73912**. It presents a compact theory of power that treats magical, cosmological, political, and infrastructural energy as versions of the same underlying problem.

## Source theory

The High Foremer describes power/energy as the agent that permits motion and change, arising from the interaction of Anu and Padomay. The text distinguishes several environments and sources:

- Aetherius contains effectively limitless power, but the speaker calls it unusable in that state.
- Oblivion is described as tending toward entropy.
- Everything in the Grey Maybe requires power.
- Named sources/examples include Magnus, AnuLorkhan, and mundane kinetic energy.

The text then gives a transmission principle: moving power itself costs power, and greater distance from a source produces greater loss.

From that, the speaker derives an engineering rule: major infrastructure should be built close to its power source. The same rule is then generalized to magic, politics, and theosophy. Large systems are portrayed as especially vulnerable when their supporting power fails.

## Lore significance

This gives the Wheels continuity an explicit **energy/infrastructure metaphysics** that helps explain why the setting repeatedly treats cosmic mechanisms like maintainable engineering systems.

It also provides a useful interpretive bridge for:
- world mechanisms and Towers;
- Sotha Sil-style machinery;
- large magical constructions;
- the mod's recurring theme that metaphysical systems can fail like infrastructure.

Those connections are interpretive unless a second source explicitly links them; the BOOK itself does not name the Towers or Wheels.

## Core evidence

- BOOK `05371592` — *Musings On Power*.

## Reliability and canon boundary

This is an attributed in-universe theoretical text, not omniscient narration. The identity and authority of the "High Foremer" are not independently established in the current corpus, and no verified pre-Wheels source witness has been found.

Treat its model as **Wheels-continuity metaphysical theory**, not as automatic base Elder Scrolls cosmology.
