# Snow-Throat, Towers, and Stones

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Snow-Throat is treated as a Tower whose Stone and continued operation matter to world stability. Numinar sends the Dragonborn to investigate surface actors capable of affecting it and uses data gathered through mechanism "slots" to locate the disturbance.

Hammar gives a broader theory: Towers are artificial or natural constructs that stabilize Nirn; each has a Stone that keeps it active, and destroying a Stone deactivates the Tower. He supplies his own list of Towers he considers active or inactive.

Llavados adds a continuity-specific consequence: he says the Towers also sustain Memory, so destruction of Snow-Throat would kill her.
