# Hammar's Numidium, Dwemer, Oversoul, and Tower theories

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Medeliu Hammar presents a coherent but explicitly character-authored metaphysical model.

He says the Dwemer regarded creation as a prison and ultimately merged into Numidium, becoming components of the Brass God. He describes Numidium as the collective souls of world-deniers and says a Shezarrine can guide it.

Hammar also describes Talos as a collection/oversoul of Shezarrines and the Dragonborn as participating in an Akatoshian Oversoul. His Tower model says stabilizing Towers possess Stones and that destruction of a Stone deactivates a Tower.

Finally, he describes many iterations of the world associated with Magnus and treats the Wheels of Lull as literal clockwork underlying creation.

## Core evidence

- `INFO 053D6624`, `053D6626`, `053D6628` — Dwemer/Numidium.
- `INFO 053D662B`, `053D662D` — Talos/Dragonborn Oversoul claims.
- `INFO 053D6631`, `053D6633` — Towers/Stones.
- `INFO 053D663A` — world iterations and Wheels.

## Canon boundary

These claims are important because the mod says them, not because the library has adjudicated them as Bethesda canon. Retrieval must label Hammar as the source.
