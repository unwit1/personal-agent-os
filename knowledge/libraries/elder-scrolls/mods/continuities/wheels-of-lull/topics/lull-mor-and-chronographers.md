# Lull-Mor and the Chronographers

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Lull-Mor is the primary Chronographer settlement/subsection reached beneath Skyrim. Its inhabitants include fabricants with occupational/time-unit titles such as Century, Minute, Decade, and Millisecond.

Numinar explains the group operationally: they maintain and repair world mechanisms and respond to threats to Towers. Later he says Chronographers fix problems and move on. His personal history says he was an overclocker/mechanic and that after the Clockwork City died he was shipped out with the other Chronographers.

The Chronography books provide an in-universe scholarly history of the wider Pearlescent Chronographers, including helicarriers, harquebuses, time manipulation, and an "integrity preservation" interpretation of their purpose.
