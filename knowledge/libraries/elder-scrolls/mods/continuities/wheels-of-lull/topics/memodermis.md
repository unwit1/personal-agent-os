# Memodermis

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Memodermis is described as material extracted from or associated with the Wheels of Lull. *Message from Eternity* says Archeron is extracting "liquidized bits" of the Wheels beneath his fortress.

Archeron's *Research Notes* call memodermis the "lifeblood of the world" and "liquid equations." He reports that it reacts to memories, can be shaped, may have been used by Sotha Sil in the Imperfect, and was used in Archeron's creation of the giant automaton Ramon.

## Core evidence

- BOOK `05367181` — *Message from Eternity*.
- BOOK `055400D7` — Archeron's *Research Notes*.
- Related Archeron quest/laboratory records — implementation context for his experiments.

## Reliability

The existence of the material is strongly embedded in the mod. Archeron's theoretical interpretation of what it fundamentally is remains his research conclusion.
