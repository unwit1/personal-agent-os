# Chronography and the Pearlescent Chronographers

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

The three *Chronography* volumes form an in-universe historical/scholarly source about the Pearlescent Chronographers.

Volume I associates them with fabricants from the Clockwork City and says they left under the influence of the Armour of Oscillating Quartz. Volume II describes helicarriers, harquebuses, time shifts/stops, and proposes "integrity preservation" as their purpose: nudging events so known future events still occur. Volume III says it is believed Sotha Sil reforged 1,200 fallen followers after the Battle of Mournhold into crystal automatons called the Armour of Oscillating Quartz.

The wording matters: claims framed as "it is said" or "believed" retain that uncertainty.

## Reliability

Treat the volumes as in-universe scholarship, not developer narration. They are especially useful for how the continuity conceptualizes Chronographer history and mission.
