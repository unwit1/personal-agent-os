# Sybandis

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Two in-universe books present Sybandis as a "Stillborn Nirn" or prototype world associated with early testing of Magnus' schema.

*Sybandis* claims the Aedra built a prototype world before Mundus, without Earthbones/divine law and with static time, and that its problems influenced revisions to the final creation.

*Journeys Through Sybandis* is framed as a travel account by Cocius Ratori. It says Psijics mystically lock the place, Daedra avoid it, travelers carry pieces of real existence into it, and interactions with its mechanisms momentarily animate the otherwise unfinished world.

## Core evidence

- BOOK `0543D472` — *Sybandis*.
- BOOK `0543D473` — *Journeys Through Sybandis*.

## Reliability and canon boundary

These are mod-continuity texts making large cosmological claims. The second is explicitly a personal travel account. Neither should be silently promoted into base Elder Scrolls creation lore.
