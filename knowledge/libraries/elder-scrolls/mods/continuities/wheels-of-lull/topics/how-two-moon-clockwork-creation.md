# How Two Moon and artificial world-building

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

BOOK `05371594`, stored as *How To Moon* but internally titled **How Two Moon** and attributed to **Andy**, proposes a method for creating a new clockwork world.

## Proposed creation model

The surviving fragment claims that decoded Dreamsleeve material suggests the traumatic birth of the world might be reenacted without repeating its original trauma.

The author proposes avoiding both Padomaic and draconic modes of thaumaturgy and instead using the metals and machinery of "Our Lord." The intended result is a deliberately manufactured, perfect clockwork world drifting through Oblivion and free from what the author regards as fleshy imperfection.

The text frames this as a way to avoid the destructive consequences of personality/selfhood that accompanied prior creation.

## Lore significance

The book adds another artificial-creation program to the Wheels continuity: rather than merely repairing the current world, its author imagines **manufacturing a replacement world through clockwork engineering**.

This resonates strongly with the continuity's recurring themes of:
- Sotha Sil and engineered creation;
- world-as-machine metaphors;
- replacement or successor worlds;
- Sybandis as a prototype/artificial world.

However, *How Two Moon* never names Sybandis. The connection is therefore thematic and should not be normalized into a claim that the proposed clockwork world **is** Sybandis.

## Epistemic cautions

Several terms are opaque or allusive, including the "C-signs," "Pigmius," "Scarab," and "Scarab-framer." The fragment does not define them, and they should remain unresolved rather than being forcibly mapped onto outside metaphysical systems.

## Core evidence

- BOOK `05371594` — *How To Moon* / internal title *How Two Moon*.

## Reliability and canon boundary

This is an in-universe speculative proposal by "Andy," not a demonstrated construction record. Its historical source origin is unresolved and no verified pre-Wheels witness has been found.

Treat it as a Wheels-continuity creation theory, not as an objective account of how Mundus or Sybandis was made.
