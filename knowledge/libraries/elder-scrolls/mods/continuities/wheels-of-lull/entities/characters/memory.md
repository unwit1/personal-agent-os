# Memory

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`  
Primary NPC: `05342C02` (`_Lull_IMPORTANT_Memory`)  
Custom voice: `05005909 _lull_memory`

## Continuity summary

Memory is a mysterious girl who intervenes after the Dragonborn's fall during the traitor/Archeron sequence and tells the player to get up because they are needed.

Llavados later identifies her as Sotha Sil's final creation and calls her a child of mer, machine, and Lullian magic. He says she is designed to make survival possible after a future world-destroying disaster and that the Towers sustain her along with Nirn.

Memory's own ambient dialogue is compact, prophetic, and intentionally cryptic. She identifies herself as "the mage" and "the clockmaker," invokes Landfall, says the Thalmor will ultimately win, speaks of salvation in the oceans/thoughts, predicts "screaming brass," and says that "The c0da does not bend. The Digitals have seen to that."
