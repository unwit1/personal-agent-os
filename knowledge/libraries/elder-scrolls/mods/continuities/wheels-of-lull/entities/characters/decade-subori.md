## Continuity summary

Subori operates and repairs the Upgate, Lull-Mor's transport connection toward the surface. She says she learned transport-gate work while employed by Pevlar when she was young and later became responsible for repeated Upgate maintenance.

She is openly frustrated by the repair burden and by Llavados' behavior. Her dialogue supplies mundane operational detail that grounds Lull-Mor as a maintained working settlement.

Subori also boasts of a longer Clockwork City career: she says she played for the Cacophany, served as secretary to the High Foremer, and has a helicarrier named after her.
