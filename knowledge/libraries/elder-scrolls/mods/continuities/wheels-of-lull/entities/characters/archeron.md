## Continuity summary

Archeron is a former Psijic-associated Thalmor Justiciar, researcher, experimenter, and principal antagonist. His diary says he stole Sotha Sil's notes from Psijic archives, works with the Thalmor, knows of the Wheels of Lull and their extensions, and seeks access to the Stone associated with the Throat of the World.

His laboratory dialogue emphasizes vivisection, biological alteration, aetherial travel, soul gems, and the Wheels of Lull. Research Notes describe memodermis as the "lifeblood of the world" or liquid equations that react to memory; he links it to Sotha Sil's work and records creation of a giant automaton named Ramon.

During the Brass Forest/Bottom of the World sequence, the apparent Divayth Fyr is revealed to be Archeron in disguise. He explicitly jokes about disguising himself as the one person the fabricants would revere. Later combat dialogue claims control of the World Mechanism and strength comparable to Sotha Sil.
