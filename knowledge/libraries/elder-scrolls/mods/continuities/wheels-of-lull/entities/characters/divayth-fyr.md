# Apparent Divayth Fyr

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`  
NPC form used by the presentation: `0500592E` (`_Lull_ACTOR_Fyr`)  
Shared custom voice: `05005905 _lull_archeron`

## Continuity role

The mod presents a figure as Divayth Fyr during the Brass Forest sequence, alongside Yagrum Bagarn. The figure gives biographical and Clockwork City dialogue in Fyr's persona.

The later MQ06 reveal revises the player's understanding: Archeron explicitly states that he disguised himself as Fyr, and the quest journal likewise treats the apparent Fyr as Archeron in disguise. The plugin reinforces this through a shared voice class and separate scene aliases/forms for the apparent Fyr role and Archeron role.

Accordingly, this dossier is a **story-state identity**, not evidence that the canonical Divayth Fyr personally performed every action attributed to this NPC form.
