# Medeliu Hammar

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`  
NPC: `05005925` (`_Lull_ACTOR_Hammar`)  
Custom voice: `05005903 _lull_hammar`

## Continuity summary

Hammar is a Telvanni-associated long-lived mage who offers the Dragonborn "primers" on deeper metaphysics and sometimes practical assistance. His dialogue functions as a concentrated in-universe theory source.

He claims that the Dwemer merged into Numidium, that Numidium is a collective embodiment of furious world-deniers, that Shezarrines can guide it, that Talos is a collection/oversoul of Shezarrines, and that the Dragonborn participates in an Akatoshian Oversoul. He also explains Towers as stabilizing constructs with Stones and supplies a list of Towers he considers active or inactive.

Hammar further describes the Wheels of Lull as literal clockwork underlying the world and speaks of repeated world-design iterations associated with Magnus.
