# Millisecond Llavados

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

Custom voice: `05005902 _lull_llavados`

## Continuity summary

Llavados is a fabricant Chronographer who first recruits the Dragonborn by presenting himself as damaged, erratic, and apparently unable to explain the crisis clearly. Early dialogue frames him as "Broken"; later he explicitly says much of the gibbering and rambling is a facade and that he knows substantially more than the other Chronographers believe.

His body can be shut down while a glowing life-force/pneuma-like component is carried onward. He repeatedly acts as a guide through deeper layers of the world mechanism and is one of the continuity's most important sources for Memory, the Wheels of Lull, the Towers, and Landfall.

Llavados says Memory is Sotha Sil's final creation, a child of mer, machine, and Lullian magic. He says a future disaster will destroy the world and that Memory is intended to help people survive and build a new one. He also says the Towers sustain Memory as well as stabilizing Nirn.

After Archeron's defeat, Llavados describes the deepest location as the core of Nirn, Magnus' workshop, Sotha Sil's central mainframe, and Memory's room. He says Archeron's soul has become integrated into the mechanism and predicts another meeting at Landfall.

## Reliability notes

Llavados is an insider and primary witness to Chronographer operations, but his presentation is intentionally deceptive and cryptic. His direct testimony is high-value continuity evidence while remaining testimony rather than neutral omniscient narration.
