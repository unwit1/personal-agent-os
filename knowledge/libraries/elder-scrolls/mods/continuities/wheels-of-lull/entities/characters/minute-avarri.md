## Continuity summary

Avarri is Lull-Mor's smith and equipment specialist. He says he was already a good smith but wanted greater ability, encountered an intelligence called the Stoker while serving the Chronographers, and bound it into his mind as part of a bargain to learn clockwork smithing.

The Stoker speaks through Avarri's sequence and identifies itself as a foundry-running, steel-shaping intelligence confined to the blacksmith's mind. Their relationship is adversarial but functional.

Avarri constructs protective equipment, clockwork weapons, ammunition, and multiple harquebus variants when the player provides materials listed in associated crafting manuals.
