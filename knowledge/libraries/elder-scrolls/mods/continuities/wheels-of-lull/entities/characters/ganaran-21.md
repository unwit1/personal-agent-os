# Ganaran-21 / Arms Merchant

Continuity: `tes.mod.wheels-of-lull`

## Continuity summary

Ganaran-21 is a fabricant who conducts a clandestine augmentation trade using an FRF as his speaking interface. He explicitly says the FRF is talking for him and identifies himself as a fabricant.

He deals in recovered fabricant body parts and offers permanent augmentations to the player. Named products include an Aetherial Glass Eye, Secondary Mystic Heart, Quartz Ligaments, and other mechanical enhancements.
