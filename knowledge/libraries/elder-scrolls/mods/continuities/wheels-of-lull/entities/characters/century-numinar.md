## Continuity summary

Numinar is the senior operational authority encountered in Lull-Mor. He explains the Chronographers' relationship to the world mechanism, assigns surface investigations, and equips the Dragonborn with tools used to perceive and manipulate hidden parts of the mechanism.

He describes Snow-Throat as a Tower whose condition matters to world stability. He identifies the Greybeards, College of Winterhold, and Thalmor as candidate causes of the disturbance. His Artophysical Manipulator and visor are tools for finding and acting on "slots" or folds in the world mechanism.

His judgment is not infallible. During the sabotage crisis he concludes that the Dragonborn is the traitor; Archeron's later reveal shows that Numinar reached a correct general conclusion about an outsider but identified the wrong person.

Numinar also says he was once an overclocker/mechanic and that after the Clockwork City died he was shipped out with the other Chronographers.
