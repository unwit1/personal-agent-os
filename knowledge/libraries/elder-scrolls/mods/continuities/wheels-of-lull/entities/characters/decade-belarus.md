## Continuity summary

Belarus identifies himself as the operator of Contingency Station Alpha, an underwater installation on the ocean floor. He says pressure is dangerous to fabricants and helps the player navigate the underwater route.

Belarus describes the Trademarks as creatures mortals call sea monsters, krakens, or giant squid and says Sotha Sil built them to guard his work because access to Sotha Sil is comparatively easy under the sea. He describes the ocean as Sotha Sil's domain and says its waters are "our thoughts."

He proposes using a working pneuma to fool the Trademarks into accepting the player as a fabricant and mentions a Dwemer craft that crashed near the station from a northern outpost.
