## Continuity summary

The Wailway locomotive is a sapient engine that says it was once a poet and was transformed into an engine by the Diolkos/Resource Men as punishment for political dissent.

It describes a Wailway locomotive as "a soul bound to an engine of movement" used to pull goods and passengers. It portrays the Wailway project as a Reman-era nightmare in which the dead were eternally enslaved to industrial machinery.

The locomotive says other engines hoped useful service might eventually earn them a final death. It asks the Dragonborn to destroy its mechanical shell and release it. It also says Reman later sold it to a "dark elven clockmaker" who studied it before abandoning it.
