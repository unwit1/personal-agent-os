## Continuity summary

The mod portrays Yagrum Bagarn as a visiting Dwemer expert who accompanies the apparent Fyr presentation and later participates in tracing the saboteur.

He calls himself the last living Dwemer / Last Living Dwarf, says he has searched for years without finding another Dwemer, and states that he was exploring the Outer Realms during the Battle of Red Mountain. He says that on returning he found his people vanished and the Blight descended on Resdayn, while admitting he does not know for certain what happened.

His mechanical analysis of the sabotage is more concrete: he identifies tampering on Sotha Sil's machines and concludes that the infiltrator used a backdoor but does not carry fabricant signatures.
