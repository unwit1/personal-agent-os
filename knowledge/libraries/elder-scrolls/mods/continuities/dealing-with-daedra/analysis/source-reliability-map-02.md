# Source reliability map — pass 02

## Practical manuals
Examples: Witch-Hunter's Primer, Restoration Instructions.
Use for institutional practice, terminology, norms, procedures, and what practitioners believe. Biological, historical, or metaphysical claims remain author-attributed unless corroborated.

## Experimental reports
Examples: Patron Notes, Essence Extraction Results, Servitor Experiment Log, Elemental Essences.
Use strongly for what experiments were attempted and observed. Treat interpretations of gods, souls, Aetherius, Towers, species origins, or metaphysical identity as hypotheses unless independently corroborated.

## Reference/theology books
Examples: The Daedric Princes and Their Servants, Sexuality in the Daedric and Aedric Spheres, The Daedric Alphabet.
Useful for the continuity's intellectual culture and taxonomy. Do not silently promote classifications into objective metaphysical facts.

## Character dialogue
High confidence for a speaker's beliefs, role, actions, relationships, and first-hand admissions. Lower confidence for rumors, ancient history, rival-faction claims, political interpretation, or cosmic theory.

## Cross-source convergence
When independent books/dialogue/mechanics support the same event or institution, promote the continuity fact while retaining separate uncertainty for the explanatory theory.

Examples:
- Rorikstead pact: multiple independent sources -> high-confidence continuity fact.
- Mageblight: Grados + public PSA -> strong existence/symptom claim.
- Syndicate origin: Operative Green + precursor mage -> strong shared event skeleton, incomplete participant mapping.
- probable Thalmor research program: internal textual markers converge strongly, but the facility is never explicitly named -> strong inference, not direct fact.
