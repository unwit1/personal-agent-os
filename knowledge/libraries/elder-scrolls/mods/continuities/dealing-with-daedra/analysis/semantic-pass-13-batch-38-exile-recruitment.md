# Semantic pass 13 — batch 38: Exile Witch alternative-magic recruitment

This batch analyzes BOOK 05455B16, 05577222, and 0545AC48.

## Public apprenticeship pitch

The Exile Witch advertises specialized magical education with:
- no College training required;
- no prior magical experience required;
- tuition;
- a cave at the northwest corner of Lake Ilinalta.

A second recruiter has written over/after the advertisement, telling prospective apprentices to reject "slave labour" for betters and instead join a camp south of the Guardian Stones/northwest of Helgen for freer magic, drinking, and camaraderie.

The second group's identity is not stated and should remain unresolved.

## Targeting Nelacar

The Exile Witch writes directly to Nelacar.

She knows of his conflict with the College of Winterhold and offers to pay him to redirect aspiring students toward her instead.

This is deliberate recruitment through institutional grievance.

## Low-magicka ritual pedagogy

The instruction note explains the technical selling point:
- no inherent magical ability is required;
- rote memorization is sufficient;
- magically infused candles provide the magicka for ritual casting.

The cost is concealed from the public advert but explicit in instruction:
- tallow must be harvested from a sapient being;
- rendered with lavender and salt;
- Anise is suggested as an easy isolated target;
- bounty targets are offered as a less morally objectionable source.

## Significance

The apprenticeship is structured as an **alternative education market**:
- target people excluded from or hostile to the College;
- promise accessibility to low-magicka students;
- charge tuition;
- externalize the magical cost into harvested human/mer tallow.

The rival graffiti/recruitment note shows that even alternative occult education has competitors.
