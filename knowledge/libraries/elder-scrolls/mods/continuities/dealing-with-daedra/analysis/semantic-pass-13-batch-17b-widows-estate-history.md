# Semantic pass 13 — batch 17b: Widows estate history and relocation

This batch analyzes three short coven notes: BOOK 052F2996, 052FCC9D, and 052FCCA0.

## Flight from Vigilant pressure

Note 052F2996 warns Serra that her hideout was overrun and that Vigilants are pursuing the writer.

The writer abandons their own post and offers two fallback destinations:
- Eldi's house south of Riften;
- Lysara at the old estate.

The note is meant to be burned after reading.

This establishes a dispersed network of safe locations and named associates rather than a single fixed cult site.

## Old estate

Note 052FCC9D, addressed to Eldi, says:
- Serra is missing and may not have escaped;
- the old estate remains standing and mostly furnished;
- the writer's uncle and aunt were executed;
- the Jarl of Morthal then seized the deed;
- the property lies at the base of the Solitude Arch;
- a hidden cavern entrance exists behind a cupboard in the basement.

The writer worries that repeated traffic would eventually alert the Vigilants.

## Property laundering / respectable front

Note 052FCCA0 proposes exploiting the Jarl's ownership of the deed.

The plan is to have someone outside the family purchase the property, reducing scrutiny.

The writer also asks whether the property can be converted into a store to create both revenue and a veneer of respectability, and plans to find a recruit with enough money to make the purchase.

The note is truncated at the end, so no missing conclusion should be reconstructed.

## Significance

These notes show the Widows/coven network adapting to law-enforcement pressure through:
- distributed safe houses;
- burn-after-reading communications;
- hidden architecture;
- third-party property purchase;
- legitimate storefront cover;
- recruitment based partly on available capital.

This is an earlier/parallel version of the same institutional logic later formalized in the estate finance-front system.
