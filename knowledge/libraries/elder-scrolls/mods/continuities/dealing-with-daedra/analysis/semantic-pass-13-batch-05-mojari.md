- consumables and personal favors function as trust currency;
- route access can be revoked/revalidated when identity becomes uncertain;
- the favor requested is grounded in Mojari's personal history rather than an institutional fee.

The "face changing elf" reference is evidence for Mojari's belief/encounter context; this batch does not independently identify that elf.
