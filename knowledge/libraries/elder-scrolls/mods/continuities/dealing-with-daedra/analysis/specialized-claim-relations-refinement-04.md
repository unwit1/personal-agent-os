# Dealing with Daedra specialized-claim relation refinement — pass 04

This pass prevents specialized named claims from being miscounted as independent confirmations when they restate or expand numeric claims.

## Semantic alias

`dwd-051` and `dwd-eraamion-mysticism-model` express the same Eraamion Anti-Magic/Mysticism proposition and both cite INFO `057A6224`.

They remain separate historical claim records, but retrieval should deduplicate them as one proposition with **no independent-evidence bonus**.

## Book of Curses page details

The twelve specialized page mechanic/placement claims refine `dwd-086`, the six-physical-page summary.

Shared ACTI evidence is not counted twice. REFR/CELL placement evidence and page-specific mechanics remain useful detail evidence.

## Scope boundaries

- `dwd-completed-book-grants-12-curses` qualifies `dwd-086`: twelve completed-book curses do not imply twelve physical contest pages.
- `dwd-debilitation-not-physical-page` explicitly preserves Debilitation as completed-book repertoire rather than a seventh physical page.

## Independent Drelas corroboration

`dwd-page-despair-placement` is deterministic ACTI/REFR/CELL placement evidence locating the Page of Despair in Drelas' Cottage.

That evidence is independent of the INFO dialogue used by recovered claim `dwd-203`, so it provides real additional corroboration that the alchemist Drelas is the Book-of-Curses Drelas.
