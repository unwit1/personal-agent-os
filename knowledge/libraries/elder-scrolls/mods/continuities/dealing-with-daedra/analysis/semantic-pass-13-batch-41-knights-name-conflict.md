# Semantic pass 13 — batch 41: Knights Mentor / Knights Templar terminology conflict

Dialogue and item text use two different names for what appears to be the same Julianos militant progression.

## Knights Mentor

Keeper Harorn explicitly says:
- he is the ranking Keeper of the **Knights Mentor** in Skyrim;
- the Knights Mentor are a knightly order dedicated to Julianos;
- their militant role is defending knowledge/free thought through force when necessary.

This is the dominant narrative/dialogue name.

## Knights Templar

Several promotion/service records instead say **Knights Templar**:
- dealskeepersign — promotion to Keeper of the Knights Templar;
- dealsgallantsign — promotion to Gallant of the Knights Templar;
- dealschevaliersign — promotion to Chevalier of the Knights Templar;
- dealschruchwritgallant — bravery/charity on behalf of the Knights Templar.

The ranks overlap the Knights Mentor progression.

## Treatment

There is not enough evidence to decide whether "Knights Templar" is:
- an older development name left in item text;
- an in-world alternate/formal name;
- a textual error.

Lorekeeper must preserve the conflict rather than silently rewriting the source.

For ordinary retrieval, "Knights Mentor" remains the primary organization label because it is explicitly explained in dialogue. "Knights Templar" should be indexed as a conflicting/possible alias.
