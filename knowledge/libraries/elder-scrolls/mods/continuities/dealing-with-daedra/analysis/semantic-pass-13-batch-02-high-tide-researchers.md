# Semantic pass 13 — batch 02: High Tide researchers

This small batch resolves three specialists associated with the High Tide: the Synod Researcher, an unnamed Redguard weapons expert, and an exiled former Reaver leader.

## Synod Researcher

NPC 05656DE4 says the Synod expedition came to Solstheim to recover the design of the wrecked Patchwork Airship. Rieklings killed the researcher's assistants and stole supplies. The researcher offers scroll bounties for riekling kills and for recovering a staff, Welkynd Stone, and Julianos amulet, and describes the Welkynd Stone as especially important. INFO 0577880D also claims Synod branches throughout Cyrodiil from Bruma to Leyawiin.

The speaker openly uses exterminatory language about rieklings. Preserve this as character/institutional behavior rather than neutral description.

## Redguard weapons expert

NPC 05660FEF is a Hammerfell weapons expert researching Dwemer technology for reverse engineering before another Thalmor invasion. He searches Solstheim for an artifact described as "the black doom, which cast fate itself as arrows from a bow."

His Dwemer Automaton Deactivation Rod works on mainland-Skyrim automata but not Solstheim machines. He infers that control sequencers vary by regional/design tradition and doubts the same rod would work on Morrowind or Hammerfell variants. The observed compatibility failure is direct evidence; the generalized sequencer model remains expert inference.

## Former Reaver leader

NPC 0565BEEA says he once led Solstheim's Reavers. He portrays the older Reavers as a coded warrior culture, claims they began as a Nordic clan and became increasingly Dunmer, and says five lieutenants overthrew him before splitting into competing Reaver Lord bands.

He hires the player to kill the five and explicitly intends to exploit the resulting power vacuum to restore his version of the Reavers. His immediate role and plan are direct evidence; the remote clan history and idealized old code remain partisan testimony.

## Cross-cutting observation

The High Tide cluster places institutional salvage, anti-Thalmor Dwemer reverse engineering, and Reaver restoration politics in one social hub without making the three actors a single faction.
