# Semantic pass 13 — batch 05: retiring smuggler and Velehk Sain charts

This batch covers nine exact-Subject INFO records for Patron NPC 055C3423 plus the associated written Instructions (BOOK 0527D222).

## Recruitment

The Patron quietly recruits the player for an illegal and potentially dangerous job.

He says he is about to retire and does not want to risk himself so close to leaving the trade. He explicitly judges the player to look more desperate.

He asks that anyone looking for him be told he is dead.

## Target

The initial spoken description is deliberately vague: infiltrate around the back of the College of Winterhold and recover a map.

The written Instructions make the objective precise:
- recover charts used by Velehk Sain to avoid the Imperial Navy;
- sources say Velehk Sain is somewhere within the College of Winterhold;
- an earlier team attempted entry through natural caverns but ran out of climbing spikes;
- trail markers were left to the climb;
- disguising oneself in robes and going at night is recommended;
- killing Sain is desirable because he is a shipping hazard, but recovering the charts is the priority.

The instructions also say the smugglers will shift berth after the 12th, with coordinates attached.

## Continuity with Plautis

Plautis Vulpin later accepts Velehk Sain's charts, immediately recognizes their value, pays for them, and offers additional smuggling work.

The records therefore support a continuity of operation:
1. retiring smuggler recruits a disposable infiltrator;
2. player retrieves the charts;
3. Plautis becomes the active smuggling contact using the recovered navigational intelligence.

This does not prove Plautis personally authored the original instructions unless another source says so.

## Lore significance

The charts are not merely treasure. They are maritime intelligence: routes developed by a notorious pirate to evade the Imperial Navy become a commercial asset for Skyrim smugglers.
