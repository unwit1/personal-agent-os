# Semantic pass 13 — batch 22a: public recruitment across institutions

This batch compares public recruitment material from the School of Julianos, Witchhunter's Guild, and Vigilants of Stendarr.

## School of Julianos

Bulletin 0559A9EB presents the School as worshipping Julianos through learning, education, and knowledge.

It specifically recruits young adults with aptitude/drive and offers food and board at the School east of Dragon Bridge.

Letter 054E38FB to Grelod extends that outreach to Honorhall wards approaching adulthood and offers supervised visits.

This is direct evidence that the School sees education, housing, and youth development as part of religious service.

## Witchhunter's Guild

Bulletin 0559A9ED recruits people seeking adventure, pay, and protection of ordinary citizens from vampires, werewolves, and other abominations.

It says prior combat, weapons, first aid, or alchemy experience is useful but not required; Regalt offers novice training.

The copy also uses personal loss ("Perhaps as your parents did") as an emotional recruitment hook.

## Vigilants of Stendarr

Bulletin 055A9D7D emphasizes Stendarr's mercy during upheaval.

It says Vigilants travel Skyrim healing the sick and comforting people in need.

Armed recruits are also wanted for "more preventative actions".

This source is important because it prevents Lorekeeper from reducing the Vigilants to monster hunters alone: their own public recruitment includes healing and relief work.

## Comparative pattern

The three institutions compete for recruits through different value propositions:
- School: education, board, intellectual/religious vocation;
- Witchhunters: pay, adventure, practical defense;
- Vigilants: mercy, healing, service, plus armed prevention.

These are recruitment self-presentations, not neutral evaluations of institutional behavior.
