# Semantic pass 13 — batch 25: Gentleman's Club worker recruitment

This batch analyzes the Club's public recruitment bulletin and the personalized offer to Susanna.

## Public worker recruitment

BOOK 057D9048 advertises the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club as a high-class establishment south of Kynesgrove with influential Skyrim clientele.

It seeks attractive young women with fashion/social skills to work:
- games;
- bath-house;
- bar;
- patron networking.

The public pitch emphasizes status, social access, and networking rather than the Syndicate's covert operations.

## Personalized Susanna offer

BOOK 052F2990 addresses Susanna directly.

The writer:
- praises her beauty;
- says she is wasted in her current city/tavern work;
- promises better pay;
- emphasizes access to influential patrons;
- directs her to the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club.

The mod's technical edit list says a note was added to Susanna's inventory/dead actor record, strongly tying this specific offer to Susanna.

This establishes that the Club actively scouts tavern/service workers rather than relying only on open applications.

## Evidence boundary

The note proves Susanna was targeted with a recruitment offer.

It does **not** prove she accepted, worked at the Club, or that her canonical/base-game murder was caused by the Syndicate.

That distinction is especially important because the note is found through her actor/inventory placement.
