# Semantic pass 13 — batch 07: Lorenz–Herod debt chain

This batch deepens the already identified Ratway favor economy using 13 INFO records for "Snap-blade" Lorenz (NPC 056BC76A), six for Bookie Herod (NPC 056BC76B), and route/reminder messages.

Lorenz possesses a ruined but still valuable Elven dagger and offers it in exchange for clearing his gambling debt to Herod. He accepts either negotiated debt forgiveness or Herod's violent removal.

Herod threatens Lorenz over the debt but offers a nonviolent settlement: locate the hidden stash left by Herod's predecessor. He believes the long-resident "Sewer King" knows its location and will forgive Lorenz's debt if the information is obtained.

Herod later recognizes the linked manipulation of the skeever pit and Sewer King, but accepts the exchange because the stash is worth more than the lost debt/betting revenue.

MESG 05857B43 exposes how this favor chain can propagate: the dagger can become fabricated evidence against another Ratway resident, whose death yields an item needed for another trade, ultimately feeding back into Mojari's route-access bargain.

## Interpretation

Debt is not isolated bookkeeping here. It becomes a transferable social obligation that can be discharged with information, violence, equipment, or services. One person's liability becomes another person's quest currency, producing a network of interdependent exchanges.
