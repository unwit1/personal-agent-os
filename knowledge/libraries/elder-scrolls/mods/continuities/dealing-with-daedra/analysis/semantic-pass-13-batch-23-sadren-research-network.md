# Semantic pass 13 — batch 23: Sadren Sarethi's research network

This batch analyzes four household/correspondence sources associated with Sadren Sarethi.

## Calcelmo correspondence

Sadren writes to Calcelmo from Skyrim after learning of his Dwemer scholarship even while she was in Cyrodiil.

Her immediate question is whether Falmer use necromancy. She asks for information about Falmer populations in unconquered Dwemer ruins around Markarth.

Calcelmo replies that he has not observed Falmer necromancy, though he recognizes shamanic/ceremonial roles. He points Sadren toward Arkngthamz and offers financial support in exchange for exclusive publication rights to any Dwemer findings.

This is a direct scholarly correspondence network connecting Sadren's Wispmother/Falmer research to Markarth's Dwemer scholarship.

## Research procurement

Sadren's shopping list pays above ordinary vendor rates for:
- Glow Dust and Wisp Wrappings;
- Stalhrim from Solstheim;
- empty Wisp cores, with authentic cores valued over synthetic ones;
- Falmer shaman staves.

She identifies Captain Gjalund in Windhelm as a regular Solstheim transport contact.

The list shows that her research is materially dependent on long-distance procurement and comparison specimens, not only local field observation.

## Household organization

A separate task list assigns ordinary domestic labor: armor maintenance, cleaning, linens, gardening, cooking, water, laundry, and firewood.

This is useful social context for Sadren's live-in-assistant arrangement. The research household combines dangerous experimental work with conventional servant/domestic duties.

## Evidence boundaries

Calcelmo's statement that he has not seen Falmer necromancy is negative expert testimony, not proof that no Falmer necromancy exists.

Sadren's willingness to pay more for authentic Wisp cores supports her belief that authentic/synthetic cores differ in research value; it does not independently prove a metaphysical distinction beyond the artifacts' provenance.
