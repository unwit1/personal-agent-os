# Relationship map — social and coercive pipelines

All links are scoped to tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra.

## Widows of Mephala

- Lysara Carius -> prospective initiate: teaches betrayal through intimacy.
- Lysara -> Solitude inn: directs unmarried initiate there to find a spouse.
- Lunglef -> initiate: genuine courtship/marriage branch created through drinking and vows.
- initiate -> Lunglef: spouse becomes the first intended Mephalan heart sacrifice.
- Lysara -> Jaree-Ra/pirate narrative: proposes it as cover story for the spouse's disappearance/death.
- Widows -> Mara/Dibella/Sanguine practice: studies their spheres as tools for improving later Mephalan betrayal.

## Sanguine-affiliated branch

- Special Patron -> Sanguine: openly invokes Lord Sanguine and recruits toward a Sanguine followers' party.
- Special Patron -> Svana task: supplies special brew and directs sexualized/intoxication-based social manipulation.
- Special Patron -> Love Shack: provides access/key.
- Love Shack -> Wiloke: party host/contact offering imported Morrowind drink.
- Wiloke drink sequence -> trafficking state: mechanically advances to blackout/travel outcome.
- trafficking outcome -> Morrowind slave brothel: player is moved, dispossessed, and placed under Madam control.
- Madam -> labor/release: release is conditional on completing the brothel work/economic requirement.
- House Dres -> brothel: Madam says some equipment/toys were made by House Dres.

No direct edge is promoted from Sanguine personally to the trafficking order.

## Cult of the Return

- Janorana Curone -> needy/outcast population: soup, bedroll, storage, charity.
- Janorana -> Hrotggi Asgikverssen: funnels receptive charity recipients toward induction.
- Hrotggi -> recruit: converts social support into service-based religious probation.
- cult complex -> armed/alchemical/quartermaster infrastructure: charity and theology coexist with practical state-like functions.

## Family / flesh cult

- Jurger Stonearm -> Thaer's Family: military defender and resource organizer.
- Forsworn -> Family: enemies whose bodies can become offerings/material.
- Kolskeggr Mine -> Family: temporary resource target capable of supplying weeks of material.
- flesh extract -> Family members: ration/resource and magical-biological commodity.
- God-Mother -> sacrifice collection: deeper membership grants permission to gather offerings.

## Rift Bank

- Aedwatch -> Teller/Curator: finance and collection apparatus.
- Rift Bank -> public clientele: deposits, exchange, storage, and temporarily suspended lending.
- Aedwatch collection -> unusual/Dwemer items: accepted.
- Aedwatch collection -> Daedric-affiliated items: rejected by Curator policy.
- Dagon Merchant -> bank visitors: occult recruiter using the bank as a contact surface.

No evidence currently establishes that Aedwatch sponsors the Dagon Merchant.

## Brethren

- High Priest -> initiated members: theology, oaths, sacrifices, hereditary transformation promise.
- Brethren Priest -> captives: forced labor and threat of farm/blood-table assignment.
- service -> initiation: coercive outsiders can potentially rise into the religious hierarchy.
- Gilded Mine -> Brethren: combines mine labor, food production, ritual hierarchy, and Deep-One transformation in one site.
