## Cross-cutting result

The first fourteen notes show how the mod distributes information through imperfect public channels:
- job adverts;
- family accusations;
- commercial correspondence;
- warnings;
- rumors;
- institutional recruitment.

The wording itself frequently marks uncertainty ("suspected", "supposed", "report has not been verified"). Retrieval should retain those epistemic qualifiers.

## Note 13

The terminated-business letter is the strongest non-pointer source in this batch.

The writer says runners found the partner's men dead and vampires occupying the hideout, losing two additional men in the discovery.

The writer mocks the failure because the infestation developed close to heavily armed, silver-wielding fanatics.

The exact identity of those fanatics is not named in the note and should not be silently assigned.
