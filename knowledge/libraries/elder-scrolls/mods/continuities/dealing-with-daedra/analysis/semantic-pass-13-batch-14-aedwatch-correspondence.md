# Semantic pass 13 — batch 14: Aedwatch correspondence network

This batch analyzes six short bank letters signed "K.A." and stored under Rift Bank/Aedwatch editor IDs.

The institutional context, signatures, and subject matter strongly associate K.A. with Mr. Aedwatch. The library treats them as Aedwatch correspondence while preserving the initials in source-sensitive retrieval.

## Civil-war financing posture

Letter 057E327F tells Ulfric that financing may be possible but refuses ideological rhetoric as sufficient evidence. The writer demands concrete proof that the cause is viable, beginning with securing nearby Fort Greenwall.

Letter 057E3281 tells General Tullius that Legion financing is too risky while the Rift is Stormcloak-held and the bank is exposed near a fortress.

Together the letters show a financier attempting to remain transactionally flexible while avoiding a commitment that could expose the bank to whichever army controls the area.

## Witchhunter pricing

Letter 057E3283 rejects Regalt's request for preferential silver rates.

The writer says the Witchhunters' public-safety use of silver is admirable but subordinate to the bank's bottom line.

## Syndicate confrontation

Letter 057E3285 rejects an attempted extortion by an unnamed organization represented by the editor ID as Syndicate correspondence.

The writer claims far greater capital/influence and invites the group to test the bank's trained security.

## Thieves Guild / Mercer channel

Letter 057E3286 tells Mercer that the new branch will buy large quantities of silver, gold, and gems if identifying marks/settings are removed.

This establishes willingness to buy anonymized high-value material from Mercer without the letter explicitly stating the goods are stolen.

## Dark Brotherhood / Astrid channel

Letter 057E327D tells Astrid that Maven Black-Briar is seeking an assassination against one of the writer's business contacts.

The writer offers to pay more for Astrid to ignore the contract and separately expresses interest in acquiring Astrid's famous dagger for the collection.

## Significance

Aedwatch's bank is not politically or criminally isolated.

The correspondence network touches:
- both sides of Skyrim's civil war;
- Witchhunters;
- the Syndicate;
- Mercer/Thieves-Guild channels;
- Astrid/Dark Brotherhood channels;
- Maven Black-Briar.

The common behavior is transactional risk management rather than ideological allegiance.
