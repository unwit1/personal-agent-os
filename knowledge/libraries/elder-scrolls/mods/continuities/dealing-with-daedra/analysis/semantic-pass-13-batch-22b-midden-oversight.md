# Semantic pass 13 — batch 22b: College oversight of the Midden

This batch reconciles faculty notice BOOK 052032E0 with the previously analyzed unauthorized Midden Student.

## Faculty policy

The College notice says:
- the Midden is off limits to new students;
- it is extremely hazardous;
- it contains remnants of dangerous experiments lacking proper approval or oversight;
- upperclassmen may romanticize those experiments as sources of power/glory;
- anyone accessing or perpetuating the experiments will be severely reprimanded.

This is explicit institutional policy, not rumor.

## Student case

Student 057E83A9 secretly conducts an experimental charm project in the Midden, fears investigation, and can accept private research funding/bribery.

The faculty notice independently confirms that this behavior violates College rules.

## Significance

The Midden's dangerous experimentation should not be treated as evidence that the College institution approves all occult research found beneath it.

The setting is explicitly a repository of **unapproved/abandoned experiments** with formal access restrictions.

This distinction matters when using Midden evidence for Mannimarco, Hermaeus Mora, Mageblight, Atronach Forge, or other mod-added occult material.
