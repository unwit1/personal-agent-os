# Semantic pass 13 — batch 29: Witchhunter silver-production discipline

This batch analyzes smelter notice BOOK 050E592A.

## Dedicated silver production

The notice says the smelter is intended for processing silver into weapons that can save hunters from abominations.

It warns that improper use leaves hardened pig-iron in the furnace, contaminating later silver batches with enough impurities to make them functionally useless.

The writer institutes a zero-tolerance policy for careless misuse.

## Operational significance

The Witch-Hunter's Primer already recommends silver against several supernatural targets.

This notice adds the infrastructure layer:
- silver must be processed locally/in quantity;
- purity matters enough to affect field usefulness;
- shared equipment discipline is treated as a life-safety issue.

Aedwatch's refusal to give Regalt preferential silver rates elsewhere also shows that silver supply has a real economic cost.

## Evidence boundary

The notice is practical workshop instruction. It establishes the organization's belief that impure silver weapons are ineffective, but it does not quantify the metallurgical/magical threshold.
