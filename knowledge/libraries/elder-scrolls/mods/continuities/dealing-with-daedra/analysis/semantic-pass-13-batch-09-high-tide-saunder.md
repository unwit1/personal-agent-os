# Semantic pass 13 — batch 09: Captain Saunder and the High Tide

Four exact-Subject INFO records resolve to Captain Saunder (NPC 05656DE3).

Saunder identifies the High Tide as his ship and says it was once a merchant vessel but now operates as a floating inn.

Cabin space is scarce. Rather than simply charging a higher rent, Saunder conditions a long-term cabin on a favor: resolve his dispute with the Raven Rock guards. Successful intervention grants a room while a gated area remains crew-only.

## Interpretation

The High Tide is not just environmental scenery. It is an adaptive commercial vessel with:
- a former merchant identity;
- current lodging business;
- scarce cabin capacity;
- restricted crew space;
- favor-based access to long-term accommodation.

This also explains why otherwise unrelated specialists can share the High Tide as a social hub.
