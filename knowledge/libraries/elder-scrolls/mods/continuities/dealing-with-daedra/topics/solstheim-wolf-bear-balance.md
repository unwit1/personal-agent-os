# Solstheim wolf/bear balance and the Great Hunt

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

This storyline connects the North-Jerall witch tradition to Solstheim's Hircine-aligned werewolves.

## Historical explanation

Beldam Annes says the Glenmoril maintain a three-witch cell at Thrond on Solstheim.

According to her:
- the sisters have been there for more than two centuries;
- they once practiced curing lycanthropes;
- they later became Hagravens;
- they stopped dealing constructively with the werewolves;
- their withdrawal helped destroy the former balance between wolf and bear.

This is Annes's partisan historical account.

## Current imbalance

A Frostmoon/Hircine role-conditioned speaker says werebears have become numerically dominant.

The desired solution is explicitly not extinction.

The objective is to reduce the primary werebear concentration at Snowclad Ruins until the two groups are approximately even again.

## Great Hunt ritual

Direct attack is considered suicidal, so the speaker proposes using the Hagravens' accumulated Hircine power.

The ritual sequence:
1. kill the three Hagravens at the Altar of Thrond;
2. arrange their severed heads on the altar;
3. use the altar to summon spirits of fallen packmates from Hircine's Hunting Grounds;
4. attack Snowclad Ruins with this spectral Great Hunt;
5. smear werebear blood on the Snowclad altar to assert the wolf's dominance.

## Hircinean ethic

After the operation, the speaker says there are still werebears on Solstheim but the numbers are now closer.

The desired result is renewed competition "through skill and cunning" rather than overwhelming numbers.

This suggests a competitive Hircinean ecology:
- predators should struggle;
- stronger hunters should prevail;
- total safety is undesirable;
- but numerical dominance so severe that the contest becomes foregone is also undesirable.

This complements Beldam Annes's broader view of Hircine as a deity of the hunt rather than simply a patron of one were-species.
