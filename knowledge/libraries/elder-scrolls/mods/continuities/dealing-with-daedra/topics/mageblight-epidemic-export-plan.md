# Mageblight epidemic-export plan

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

BOOK 052F7B91 (*Unacceptable Progress*) describes a deliberate plan to infect enough Legion soldiers with "Magebane" that returning troop rotations carry an epidemic into other provinces.

The plan calls for more distribution hubs and faster spread before "the elves" notice.

Public sources call the spreading affliction MageBlight, while plugin mechanics frequently use Magebane for the anti-magicka disease system. The shared context strongly supports treating these as operational/public labels for the same disease family in this storyline.

A public PSA documents contaminated mana potions, ale, and wine as transmission vectors.

A later notice says the Alchemists' Union of Alinor funds free cure for Skyrim natives.

Do not infer from that cure program that Alinor created the disease. The epidemic note does not identify its sponsor, and its fear of elven detection points the other way.

Unresolved:
- who authored/sponsored the epidemic-export order;
- which provinces/populations are the ultimate target;
- whether the plan reaches critical spread.
