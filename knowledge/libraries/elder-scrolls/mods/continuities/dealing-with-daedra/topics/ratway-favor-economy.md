# Ratway favor economy

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Ratway contains an informal barter/favor economy that operates beside Riften's official and Thieves-Guild economies.

## Access

Mojari controls access mechanisms leading toward the Ragged Flagon.

He does not trust strangers and requires proof of friendship through material favors such as Skooma or recovery of special gloves.

Drahff and Hewnon construct a barricade and state that their real target is the Thieves Guild. They may permit unrelated travelers through for a toll or repeated deliveries of drinks.

## Debt chain

Snap-blade Lorenz owes Bookie Herod money.

Lorenz offers a ruined but valuable Elven dagger if the debt can be cleared.

Herod will clear the debt for information about his predecessor's hidden stash.

Sewer King Serrius possesses the clue but asks for the pit-fighting skeevers to be killed so the Ratway's hungry vagrants can eat.

Gian the Fist possesses the gloves Mojari wants and trades them for substantial money or revenge against Knjakr.

## Economic interpretation

The storyline treats non-monetary resources as currencies:
- trust;
- route access;
- Skooma and alcohol;
- gambling information;
- food;
- debt relief;
- rare equipment;
- violence/revenge;
- labor and favors.

The sequence shows a functioning social economy among vagrants, gamblers, criminals, workers, and blockers whose problems are tightly interdependent.

It also shows that the Thieves Guild does not simply control all Ratway space: other residents can physically obstruct its access and pursue their own interests.
