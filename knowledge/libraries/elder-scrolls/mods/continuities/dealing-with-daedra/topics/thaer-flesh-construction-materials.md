# Thaer's flesh-construction material theory

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Thaer's research notes treat creature construction as a modular engineering problem.

Functional components and selected analogues:
- Osseous Marrow / shape and structure -> dragon bone.
- Dermis Membrane / desire to consume -> slaughterfish tissue.
- Essence of Breath / movement and spirit -> ash-beast ash/material.
- Blood Liqueur / nutrient and life -> troll fat.

Earlier notes show some of these choices as provisional; later notes list the selected materials directly.

Thaer reasons by perceived embodied properties: dragon bones preserve immense structure, slaughterfish exhibit extreme consumption, ash creatures may embody an animating principle, and troll fat represents regeneration/nutrient reserves.

A separate Deep-One research note uses a detailed mine survey supplied by Aslfur to search for underground phenomena and possible sites connected to Thaer's research.

The construction process is a continuity practice. The metaphysical equivalence between components and source creatures is Thaer's experimental theory.
