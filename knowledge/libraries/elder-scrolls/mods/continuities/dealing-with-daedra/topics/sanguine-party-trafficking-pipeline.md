# Sanguine-affiliated party and trafficking pipeline

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

This storyline begins through an explicitly Sanguine-affiliated Special Patron at the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club.

He invokes Lord Sanguine, supplies intoxicating/special drink in a social-sexual recruitment task, and grants access to a gathering place for Sanguine followers called the Love Shack.

At that party, Wiloke provides a special drink imported from Morrowind.

Repeated consumption triggers a scripted transition:
- blackout/travel messages;
- relocation to a Morrowind room;
- removal/storage of the player's possessions;
- activation of the Madam and brothel-client sequence.

The Madam then explicitly identifies the destination as a slave brothel and ties release to labor/earnings.

## Evidence boundary

High-confidence fact:
- a Sanguine-affiliated party route mechanically leads into a Morrowind trafficking/enslavement scenario.

Not established:
- that Sanguine personally ordered the trafficking;
- that every party participant knows the destination;
- that Wiloke's subjective intent is proven by his drink dialogue;
- that House Dres institutionally owns the brothel merely because the Madam says some equipment was made by House Dres.

This distinction should be preserved in any lore answer.
