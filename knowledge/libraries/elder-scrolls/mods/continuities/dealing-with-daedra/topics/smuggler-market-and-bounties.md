# Smuggler market and bounties

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Smuggler documents define a concrete illicit commodity market.

Desired goods include Dwemer technology, Dragon-Cult jewellery, dragon material, ebony, malachite, and Dwarven metal.

Payment can be made in untraceable silver ingots. Stolen goods are accepted at lower prices when identifying marks remain.

The network explicitly seeks to circumvent the East Empire Trading Company's ebony/malachite monopoly.

Current berth information is distributed through a Winterhold Inn contact.

A separate bounty list targets Haldyn, Hargar, Volf, and Safia, with signature personal items serving as proof.

This is a decentralized maritime black market rather than a single guild hierarchy.
