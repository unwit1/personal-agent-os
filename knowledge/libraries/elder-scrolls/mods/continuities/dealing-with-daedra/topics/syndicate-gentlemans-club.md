# Syndicate and Eastmarch Gentleman's Club

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Club is both a real entertainment/service business and a laundering/contact front for the Syndicate.

Evidence includes:
- Carsten's membership, employment, Service Coupon, and covert-job dialogue.
- The Syndicate bulletin, which threatens rivals while directing contact through the Club.
- Welcome to the Syndicate, which tells members to distinguish ordinary bandits from protected allied groups.
- Operation notes directing elimination of competitors to increase demand for Syndicate services.
- delivery forms proving an ordinary logistics layer feeding the Club.
- an independent necromantic letter reporting a Gatekeeper trading relationship with the Club.

The organization seeks political and social leverage as well as profit. Carsten encourages the player to become Thane because official influence becomes an asset to the network.

The result is an organized-crime hub that connects mundane commerce to occult procurement, favors, recruitment, and magical enhancement.
