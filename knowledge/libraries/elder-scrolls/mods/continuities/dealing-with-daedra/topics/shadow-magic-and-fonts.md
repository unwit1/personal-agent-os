# Shadow magic and Fonts of Shadow

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: Keeper Harorn and School/Knights Mentor messages.

Keeper Harorn defines Shadow magic as manipulation of possibilities produced by conflicting forces, not literal darkness.

Mechanically/institutionally:
- spells use a target's ambient Shadows instead of ordinary caster magicka;
- a target cannot immediately supply the same resource again after a Shadow spell;
- Knights Mentor signet rings collect encountered Shadows;
- collected Shadows can be returned to a Font of Shadow;
- multiple Fonts are said to be connected across Tamriel.

A dream/message describes branching possible outcomes as multiple Shadows and inspires new applications of the magic.

Another message states that Daedric understanding can reveal how the signet ring harnesses latent Shadows and allow it to be modified to improve Shadow casting, though doing so damages its divine-intervention function.

The resource mechanics are strongly represented in the continuity. The deeper claim that Shadows are literally possibility-reflections is Knights Mentor metaphysical doctrine.
