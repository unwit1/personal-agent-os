# Widows of Mephala: marriage-betrayal pipeline

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Widows turn ordinary intimacy into a ritual resource.

Lysara instructs a recruit to:
1. obtain a spouse;
2. consummate the marriage;
3. murder the spouse;
4. remove the heart;
5. offer it through Mephalan ritual.

If the player is unmarried, Lysara sends them toward the Solitude inn.

The Lunglef branch supplies a real marriage rather than a fake ceremonial prop. Lunglef remembers vows, consummation, and the relationship as a sincere happy event.

A message after the drunken marriage explicitly notes that his heart will be a fitting sacrifice.

Lysara later proposes a cover story around the lighthouse and Jaree-Ra's pirates so the spouse's death can appear to be part of a kidnapping/disappearance.

## Broader training model

Lysara also tells initiates to study Mara, Dibella, and Sanguine because understanding affection, sexuality, and indulgence improves later betrayal.

The path ultimately replaces those accumulated relationship-oriented blessings with a Mephalan one.

The Widows' magic is therefore not simply "kill a lover". It is a structured process of creating trust, extracting social/material value, then converting the relationship itself into sacrificial betrayal.
