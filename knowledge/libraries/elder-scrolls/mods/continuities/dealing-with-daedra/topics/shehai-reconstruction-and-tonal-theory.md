# Shehai reconstruction and tonal theory

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: Torn Journal Page 052CED64.

An anonymous researcher attempting to recreate the lost Shehai from written accounts proposes studying the Thu'um and Dwemer Tonal Architecture for shared principles.

This is an explicit in-world **comparative tonal-magic hypothesis**.

The plugin also implements Manifest Shehai/Shehai spells, weapons, enchantments, a perk, and a spell tome, so some reconstructed Shehai technique is mechanically real in this continuity.

Keep the two levels separate:
- reconstructed Shehai technique: mechanically supported;
- universal theory linking Sword-Singing, Thu'um, and Dwemer tonal systems: research hypothesis.
