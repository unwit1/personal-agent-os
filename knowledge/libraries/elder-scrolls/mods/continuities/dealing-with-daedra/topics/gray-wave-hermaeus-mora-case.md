# Gray-Wave / Hermaeus Mora case

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Gray-Wave case is reconstructed through multiple documents rather than one omniscient source.

## Census evidence

Census of Prominent Families records:
- substantial Gray-Wave property and wealth;
- a hunting lodge east of Rorikstead;
- a Vigilant inheritance challenge over suspected Daedric activity;
- the final 37 children being recorded as stillborn.

## Legal evidence

Legal Filing Archive summarizes Vigilant cases involving:
- sudden magical ability among family members;
- unsanctioned conjuration;
- all family births being routed to the hunting lodge;
- a burglary witness reporting an abominable ritual;
- political/legal obstruction of investigation.

## Vigilant theological dead end

Vigilant Treatise on the Daedric Threat argues that Daedra gain purchase through worship and souls. A reader note observes that newborns could not have meaningfully worshipped, so the ordinary model does not explain the Gray-Wave children.

## Hermaeus Mora deduction

The Daedric Princes and Their Servants describes Seekers as corrupted mortals. A reader annotation concludes that the Gray-Waves likely traded newborns to Hermaeus Mora for forbidden knowledge, with the children transformed into Seekers.

The hunting lodge contains a ritual site and a Seeker, and other dialogue directs the player there for a ritual capable of increasing magical ability.

## Confidence

High confidence:
- Gray-Wave occult activity at the lodge;
- repeated suspicious infant disappearance/stillbirth reporting;
- Hermaeus Mora/Seeker involvement.

Medium-high inference:
- the exact transactional formulation that each newborn was payment for forbidden knowledge and specifically transformed into a Seeker.
