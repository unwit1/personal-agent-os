# Revenant Moon / necromantic strategic network

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: Unsent Letter, BOOK 058C82F4.

The letter maps a coordinated necromantic network operating in Skyrim. Its exact organizational name is not stated plainly, but its terminology includes the Revenant Moon, Anchorites, "His Eminence", and "the Master".

Reported strategy:
- feed the Witchhunters Guild information on rival necromancers while remaining undiscovered;
- oppose the Gatekeepers, whose Soul Cairn patronage competes with the subtler Revenant Moon tradition;
- monitor the Volkihar/Dawnguard conflict and infiltrate or exploit whichever side becomes useful;
- place an acolyte among Fellglow Keep mages and discourage their Soul Cairn experiments;
- ally with Reach necromancers who share principles despite not following "His Eminence";
- search Skyrim for the Master's Amulet.

The same letter independently reports that Gatekeepers have a steady trading relationship with the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club.

The faction appears strongly Mannimarco/Worm-adjacent, but the letter alone does not explicitly identify the organization, so that identity remains an inference.
