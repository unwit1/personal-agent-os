# Gatekeeper recruitment and tattoo trade

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A Gatekeeper Hooded Recruiter uses mortality anxiety as a recruitment filter.

Prospects first perform an ordinary delivery to the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club: a tattoo kit and large quantities of purple ink.

The recruiter describes ink production as a normal revenue stream comparable to church alcohol or craft sales.

After the errand he introduces the deeper promise: the order can prepare a mortal soul to linger after death as a stronger, more self-aware ghost.

This creates a layered recruitment funnel:
1. identify fear of death;
2. assign a low-risk commercial errand;
3. establish trust and organizational legitimacy;
4. introduce postmortem doctrine;
5. invite the prospect toward Yorgrim Overlook and deeper Gatekeeper practice.

The shipment is also independent evidence of Gatekeeper–Gentleman's Club commerce.
