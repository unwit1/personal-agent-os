# Flesh magic

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Thaer's system divides embodied construction into meat, blood, bone, breath, and soul. Conventional artificial flesh constructs may substitute a Daedric vestige for a mortal soul; Thaer's experiments instead begin with existing bodies and rewrite them.

The Family's God-Mother is a protoplasmic experimental organism that generates polyps. Worshippers treat it religiously, but Thaer repeatedly approaches it as a research subject.

Practical flesh magic is described as Restoration-adjacent: extraction resembles alchemy, bodily repair follows biological pathways where possible, and implanted polyps can act as ritual/magical foci even for practitioners with limited conventional magicka.

Primary evidence: INFO 053A95DC-053A95E7, 056DAF73, 056E009E.
