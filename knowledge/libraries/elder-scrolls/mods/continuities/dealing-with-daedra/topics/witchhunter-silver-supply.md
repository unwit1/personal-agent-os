# Witchhunter silver supply

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Witchhunters maintain silver-processing infrastructure for anti-abomination weapons.

A workshop notice warns that pig-iron contamination can make later silver batches functionally useless and imposes a zero-tolerance policy on improper smelter use.

This complements:
- the Primer's recommendation of silver weapons;
- Regalt's attempt to obtain preferential silver pricing from Aedwatch;
- the Guild's paid bounty economy.

Silver is therefore both a tactical material and an organizational supply-chain concern.
