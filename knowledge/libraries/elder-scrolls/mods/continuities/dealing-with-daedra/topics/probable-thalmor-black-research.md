# Probable Thalmor black-research program

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary sources: Patron Notes; Essence Extraction Results.

Two reports describe a coordinated magical research facility. Patron Notes directly cross-references the essence-extraction experiments and contains several institutional markers:

- a "military branch";
- consideration of Mannimarco for "propaganda purposes";
- institutional animosity toward the Hist;
- "Agent Ancano attached to the college";
- a resident astromancer;
- requests for "justiciars" to investigate a surveillance anomaly.

Taken together, these strongly imply a Thalmor/Altmeri Dominion research program. The reports never explicitly call the facility Thalmor, so the identification remains a high-confidence inference.

## Research lines

Patron attunement:
- Ideal Masters;
- Mannimarco;
- isolated Hist;
- Magna Ge;
- unidentified Psijic-like energy entity;
- Dreamsleeve/collective-subconscious target.

Aspect/Aedric extraction:
- Heartstones interpreted as Lorkhan-attuned;
- Taproots interpreted as Y'ffre-attuned;
- Ice Wraith essence compared to Tsun, Zenithar, and Z'en.

The Essence Extraction author kills a Nordic test subject immediately after a Heartstone/Lorkhan trial and orders the result sealed. The report proposes "phase 2, Lorkhic suppression" and population-scale testing in Skyrim.

The experiments and institutional intent are direct source evidence. The claimed divine/metaphysical correspondences are research interpretations.
