# Exile Witch recruitment and alternative magical education

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Exile Witch markets ritual magic as accessible to people without College training or strong innate magicka.

She recruits through:
- public apprenticeship adverts;
- direct outreach to Nelacar, paying him to redirect students away from the College;
- a ritual system where infused candles supply the magicka required for rote casting.

The public advert omits the candle material cost.

Instruction later reveals that the tallow must come from a sapient being, with Anise suggested as an easy target and legal bounty targets offered as an alternative.

A second unidentified group edits the advert to recruit apprentices to a freer camp near the Guardian Stones/Helgen. Its identity remains unresolved.
