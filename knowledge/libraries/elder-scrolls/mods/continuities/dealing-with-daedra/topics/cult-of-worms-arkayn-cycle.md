# Cult of Worms and the Arkayn Cycle

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A Cult-of-Worms recipe claims to partially corrupt the "Arkayn Cycle" inside a mortal body.

The ritual combines:
- a fresh egg as the beginning of life;
- a human skull as the end of life;
- a filled Black Soul Gem as life/soul renewal halted through confinement.

It must be performed at a Worm-God altar in a place of death.

The resulting item is implemented as the Elixir of the Inverted Cycle, with associated Ritual of Arkayn Spite and Worm-God ritual records.

Other factions use the same Arkayn-Cycle concept differently:
- Vigilants defend Arkay's soul/rebirth order;
- Dragon-Cult sources describe Draugr as outside it;
- Worm/Gatekeeper practices attempt to bypass or corrupt ordinary death.

The exact metaphysics remain faction-dependent.
