# Doom-Truth apprenticeship and ritual candles

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A former North-Jerall witch serves a Doom-Truth, which she calls a lesser Daedra, and teaches a low-magicka ritual tradition.

Ritual candles release residual magicka from tallow and combine it with alchemical ingredients. The witch says mer tallow is strongest but human and beast-race bodies also work.

The apprenticeship is deliberately networked:
- Service Coupons from the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club are used as tuition/payment.
- the apprentice is sent to former North-Jerall associates;
- candle-making requires corpse harvesting and mundane supplies;
- a communion/portal ritual eventually introduces the apprentice to the Daedric patron.

The witch says she purchased her own advancement by finding three new mortals for the patron to bargain with, making recruitment itself a form of payment.
