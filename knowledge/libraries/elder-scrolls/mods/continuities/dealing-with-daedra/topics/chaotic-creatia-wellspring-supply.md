# Chaotic Creatia wellspring supply chain

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A smuggler note reports the loss of Skyrim's only known Chaotic Creatia supplier and suspects Vigilant interference.

Grados later locates an active Creatia Wellspring southwest of Dushnikh Yal using Vigilant records and says a previous operation there was purged.

The two source sets converge strongly:
- southern-hold courier origin;
- Daedric material;
- interrupted supply;
- Vigilant action;
- need for a new commercial operator.

Grados considers the Syndicate and smugglers as possible operators but sees serious risks with both.

His description of the wellspring as Creatia leaking from an unknown Oblivion plane, and his comparison to Hist sap/Shivering Isles, remains character research rather than neutral cosmology.
