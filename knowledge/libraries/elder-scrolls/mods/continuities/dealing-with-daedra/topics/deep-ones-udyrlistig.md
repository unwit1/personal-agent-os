# Deep Ones / Udyrlistig

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Brethren describe a hereditary, multigenerational transformation in which mortals and their descendants become progressively more like the Deep Ones. That transformation is treated as an observable continuity fact within their quest line.

The deeper origin account is less secure. A Brethren Priest says the Deep Ones are Udyrlistig descended from primordial trolls who lost power after the Dawn era and later obtained transformative knowledge associated with Hermaeus Mora. The account links Lurkers, interbreeding, and ancient troll lineage.

Other priestly dialogue explicitly admits that parts of the Hermaeus Mora/Lurker model are conjecture. The lore library therefore stores the transformation process separately from the cult's speculative deep history.
