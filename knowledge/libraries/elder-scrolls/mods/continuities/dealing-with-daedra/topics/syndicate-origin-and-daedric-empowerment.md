# Syndicate origin and Daedric empowerment

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Two independent testimonies describe the background of Carsten's criminal network.

## Operative Green

Green says Carsten and Bashnag were friends and cofounders. They found a witch who taught them a secret ritual to summon a Daedra that granted power. Carsten managed the business while Bashnag managed field operations. Bashnag later left with the best spellswords.

Green further says Carsten can arrange the same Daedric empowerment for highly useful employees and that Carsten suspended the practice after Bashnag's breakaway.

## Precursor mage

A surviving mage from an older occult group says:
- a Falkreath witch joined the group and entered the leader's inner circle;
- the leader shared his power-granting ritual with her;
- she shared it with two other members;
- the trio staged a coup;
- a winged horror escaped and massacred most members;
- traitors and survivors formed a new group south of Kynesgrove and pursued a criminal empire.

The geographic endpoint strongly matches the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club/Syndicate. The two accounts therefore strongly corroborate an occult-coup origin for the criminal network.

The exact identities of all three conspirators are not explicitly mapped across both accounts, so that detail remains unresolved.
