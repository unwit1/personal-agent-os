# Dremora soul-collateral contract

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

This storyline is one of the mod's clearest examples of Daedric law behaving like enforceable contract magic.

A summoned Dremora offers power, demands a Sigil Stone, and holds the mortal's soul as collateral.

The Dremora explicitly separates a written blood contract from a verbal delivery/forfeiture condition and exploits that distinction when possible.

Falion's response treats Daedric hierarchy and Nymics as competing legal/metaphysical tools:
- a higher Daedric claimant may supersede a lower claim;
- Molag Bal's vampiric claim could displace the Dremora's;
- a true-name forced summoning can strip the Dremora of ordinary freedom;
- binding the Dremora into a weapon can create leverage for a soul exchange.

The narrative repeatedly rewards careful reading of the agreement rather than treating all Daedric pacts as simple magical curses.

Related evidence:
- Stauf's Skaafin contract also requires exact wording and true name;
- Dark Contract and Break the Pact scroll records generalize written pact mechanics;
- Patron Focus Rings turn pact attunement into an experimental technology.
