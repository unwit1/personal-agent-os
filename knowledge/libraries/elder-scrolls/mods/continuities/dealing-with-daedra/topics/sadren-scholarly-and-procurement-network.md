# Sadren Sarethi scholarly and procurement network

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Sadren's research network reaches beyond her own experiments.

She corresponds with Calcelmo about possible Falmer necromancy. Calcelmo points her toward Arkngthamz and offers expedition funding in exchange for exclusive publication rights to Dwemer findings.

Sadren also purchases Glow Dust, Wisp Wrappings, Stalhrim, Wisp cores, and Falmer shaman staves.

Captain Gjalund is identified as a regular Windhelm-to-Solstheim transport contact.

Her household task list shows that a live-in assistant is expected to perform ordinary domestic labor alongside the research environment.

This makes Sadren a small independent research institution: correspondence, procurement, fieldwork, household labor, and dangerous experimentation are all integrated.
