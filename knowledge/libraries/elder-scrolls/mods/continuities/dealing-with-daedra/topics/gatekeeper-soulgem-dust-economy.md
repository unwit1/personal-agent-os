# Gatekeeper Soulgem Dust economy

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Soulgem Dust is both ritual material and black-market commodity.

## Production/use testimony

A Gatekeeper Cultist Wraith asks for empty white soul gems and describes:
1. shattering/sharding them;
2. mixing them with withered soul-grass from its master's realm;
3. using the resulting material to beckon soldiers through the void.

The exact manufacturing chain between that description and the inventory item named Soulgem Dust is not spelled out as a crafting recipe, but the shared dialogue/economy strongly connects the material.

## Two material states

- Soulgem Dust — ALCH 0559FB56.
- Gravetouched Soulgem Dust — ALCH 056238E4.

The Wraith can convert 8 ordinary Dust into 8 Gravetouched Dust. It calls the altered material darker/purer and better for spirit-weaving enchantment work.

## Currency

Dust buys:
- a limited Wraith blessing/Soul-Cairn affinity;
- Conjuration training;
- other Gatekeeper services.

The Wraith says repeated blessing has a survivability limit.

## Distribution

Looking for Supply (BOOK 055A9D7B) describes Dust as a new, highly desired mage commodity whose source is obscure to ordinary criminals. Shipments are observed near Nightgate Inn.

Other Gatekeeper dialogue says a friendly criminal organization buys their Dust.

This makes Dust a useful relationship tracer between the Gatekeepers, smugglers/criminal logistics, and Skyrim's illicit magical market.
