# Invisible-organism disease and decomposition theory

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: Old Letter 058C82F9.

An anonymous experimenter proposes that microscopic/invisible living organisms cause decomposition and may also cause illnesses such as ulcers and dysentery.

They connect this to a paralytic alchemical preparation:
- low doses can be medicinal;
- the substance may kill the unseen organisms;
- larger doses might preserve corpses.

Ingredients named include swamp fungal pods and imp stool.

This is an in-world research theory supported only by the writer's claimed experiments. It should not be promoted as universal biological fact merely because it resembles modern germ theory.
