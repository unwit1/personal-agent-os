# Mysticism, Recall, and Eraamion's trafficking trap

Continuity: `tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra`

## Mysticism model

Eraamion's anti-magic dialogue presents Mysticism as the older discipline of direct Magicka manipulation.

He treats modern school boundaries as historical fragmentation:
- Restoration inherited warding;
- Destruction inherited shock techniques;
- Conjuration inherited and altered soul trapping;
- Mark/Recall preserve spatial manipulation;
- temporal manipulation once existed but is effectively lost.

Store this as Eraamion's scholarly interpretation.

## Ordinary Recall vs "free" Recall

Eraamion says useful Recall scrolls are profitable enough that he withholds the spell tome.

The alternative he offers to poor/desperate hunters is not a discounted version of the same safe service.

INFO records `059BC98E` and `059BC98F` both hand out `SCRL 059BC982`, whose effect runs the trafficking initialization script.

That script sends the user directly into the Morrowind slave-brothel system, strips and stores inventory, resets the brothel/debt state, and enables the Madam sequence.

Eraamion's own warning references House Dres and says the victims will "probably" be released eventually. He also acknowledges earning a cut from use of the scroll.

## Network implication

This creates a direct network edge:

**Witch-Hunter's Guild specialist -> House Dres-coded trafficking system**

The Guild's physical co-location with Eraamion should not automatically make the whole Guild complicit. The evidence establishes Eraamion's personal business, not institutional authorization.

It also gives the mod another example of mundane/legitimate magical services being used as infrastructure for coercion and trafficking.
