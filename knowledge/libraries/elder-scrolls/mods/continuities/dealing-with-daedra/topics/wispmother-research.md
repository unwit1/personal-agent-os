# Sadren Sarethi's Wispmother research

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Sadren tries to replace hearsay about Wispmothers with direct experimental memory recovery.

Her procedure:
- suppress the mortal assistant's spirit;
- conjure a weakened Wispmother into the body;
- permit temporary memory blending;
- banish the spirit;
- interview the assistant about memories retained from the possession.

Results she interprets include priestly identities in life, necrologic/ritual ingredients, frost and Ice-Wraith affinities, enchanted-ice soul vessels, and a possible connection to magical ice on Solstheim.

She also uses ancient Nord binding artifacts to argue that Snow-Elf lich-like beings could be discorporated, bound to enchanted components, and confined by wards.

The experiment has killed at least one prior assistant and traumatized others. The findings remain character research rather than automatically verified ancient history.
