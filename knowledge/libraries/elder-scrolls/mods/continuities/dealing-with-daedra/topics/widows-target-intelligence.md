# Widows target intelligence

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: BOOK 0569E033.

The Widows maintain target dossiers on unhappy marriages.

Potential recruits/targets include Frabbi/Kleppr, Olda/Horgeir, Ahlam/Nazeem, Nivenor/Bolli, and Beitild/Leigelf.

Each entry pairs personal resentment with a locally plausible cover:
- Forsworn violence in Markarth;
- drunken accident/disappearance near Dragon Bridge;
- poisoned prestige alcohol in Whiterun;
- Ratway robbery in Riften;
- mining accident in Dawnstar.

The Beitild entry explicitly warns against interfering after rumors of Dark Brotherhood interest.

This shows the Widows practicing operational intelligence and deconfliction with other violent actors.

The plans are proposed scenarios, not universal completed events.
