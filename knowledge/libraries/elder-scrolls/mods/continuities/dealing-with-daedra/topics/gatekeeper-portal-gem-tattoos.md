# Gatekeeper portal-gem tattoos

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A Gatekeeper enchanter says the gems used in the organization's rings arrive already enchanted from the portal.

The same gem can be processed differently:
- mounted into a ring for a removable effect;
- ground into tattoo ink for a stronger, effectively permanent effect.

The enchanter offers tattooing if the player retrieves the required supplies from the portal.

The senior Gatekeeper identifies that portal as opening to the Soul Cairn, while remaining uncertain about the exact patron being on the far side.

This supports a Soul-Cairn-linked material supply chain without resolving the patrons as definitely the Ideal Masters.

The tattoo system also explains why the Gatekeeper recruiter can plausibly ship large quantities of tattoo equipment/ink to the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club while simultaneously treating tattoos as part of deeper magical practice.
