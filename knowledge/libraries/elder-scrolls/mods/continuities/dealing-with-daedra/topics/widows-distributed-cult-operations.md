# Widows distributed cult operations

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: BOOK 0538FFE9.

The Widows operational model delegates crime to recruited cultists.

Cultists can seduce or blackmail high-profile figures for favors, or find lovers and sacrifice them for cult resources.

Notoriety both makes missions less reliable and increases recruitment interest.

Influential favors can be used for pardons, political smoothing, or hiring mercenaries against Vigilants.

The recommended strategy cycles through quiet capacity-building, favor accumulation, murder operations, high-notoriety recruitment, and pressure reduction.
