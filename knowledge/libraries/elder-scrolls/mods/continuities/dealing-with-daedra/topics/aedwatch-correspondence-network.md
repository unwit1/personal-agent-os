# Aedwatch correspondence network

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A set of Rift Bank letters signed K.A. shows the bank's principal negotiating with major political, criminal, and mercenary actors.

The writer:
- conditionally considers financing Ulfric but demands evidence of territorial competence;
- refuses to fund Tullius while local Stormcloak control makes retaliation too risky;
- refuses Regalt preferential silver rates;
- rejects Syndicate extortion;
- offers Mercer a market for melted/unmarked precious metals and loose gems;
- offers Astrid more money to ignore a Black-Briar assassination request and asks about purchasing her dagger.

The pattern is consistent with Mr. Aedwatch's established characterization: capital preservation, collection-building, and transactional independence.

These contacts demonstrate communication/business relationships, not ideological membership in any recipient organization.
