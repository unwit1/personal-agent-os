# Book of Curses contest

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Book of Curses storyline is a distributed Daedric contest rather than a simple fetch quest.

## Structure

- A Dremora owns/organizes the contest.
- The Vampire Master is selected as one participant and receives the spine.
- Six curse pages are distributed among dangerous holders/sites.
- The Master uses the spine's remaining paper fragments to scry for the pages.
- The player is recruited and then magically compelled to collect them.
- Near the end, the Dremora removes the compulsion so the player can alter the outcome.

The Dremora's motive is explicitly entertainment/prolonging the game, not liberation.

## Outcomes represented in the plugin

Documented outcomes include:
- page destruction through the Vigilants, which also erases the player's curse knowledge;
- true submission, in which the Master completes/tests the book and renews obedience;
- fake submission after the Dremora frees the player, allowing escape after the Master leaves;
- rebellion/attempted seizure of the book, encouraged by the Dremora.

The exact final state of every possible combat branch should be treated separately from these explicit messages unless verified by scripts/stage conditions.

## Artifact epistemology

Destroying the page's central rune causes the player to forget how to use the curses. This implies the learned curse techniques are at least partly sustained by an external magical information structure rather than being purely ordinary memorized spellcraft.

That interpretation is strongly suggested by the ending effect, but the mechanism is not explained by a character or technical note.
