# Dwemer control-sequencer variation

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: High Tide Redguard weapons researcher.

A Dwemer Automaton Deactivation Rod is reported to work on mainland-Skyrim Dwemer constructs but not on Solstheim constructs.

The researcher interprets the failure as evidence that control sequencers vary by Dwemer design principle.

He further predicts that the Skyrim-compatible rod would likely fail on Morrowind or Hammerfell variants.

Evidence tiers:
- observed continuity fact: the rod fails against Solstheim automata;
- researcher interpretation: differing control sequencers/design principles explain the failure;
- extrapolation: Morrowind/Hammerfell automata probably differ as well.
