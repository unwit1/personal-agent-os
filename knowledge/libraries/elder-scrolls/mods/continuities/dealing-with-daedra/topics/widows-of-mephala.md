# Widows of Mephala

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Lysara Carius rebuilds a hereditary cult of Mephala after the destruction of her family tradition.

The cult's central practice is betrayal of intimate trust. A spouse is sacrificed first; later lovers can be offered through the same logic. The rewards described in dialogue include youth, beauty, magical blessings, and social power.

The Widows combine Mephalan ritual with practical tools: poison, Illusion, seduction, identity alteration, investments/front businesses, recruitment of unhappy spouses, and infiltration of vulnerable social networks.

Lysara also claims the Night Mother is Mephala. That identity claim is not independently established in this continuity and remains explicitly attributed to Lysara.
