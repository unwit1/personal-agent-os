# Cult of the Return: charity and recruitment

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Cult of the Return operates an integrated welfare and conversion system.

Janorana Curone provides food, sleeping space, and basic shelter/storage to the poor and displaced.

She simultaneously frames that assistance as religious ministry and directs receptive people to Hrotggi Asgikverssen for induction.

Hrotggi's next stage emphasizes mundane service—wood, water, food financing, and procurement—before access to deeper doctrine.

This creates a clear funnel:

material aid -> belonging -> religious conversation -> service probation -> occult induction.

The aid is materially real, so the system should not be reduced to a sham charity. Its significance is that genuine welfare provision becomes the social base of a restorationist authoritarian cult.
