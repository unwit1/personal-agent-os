# Order Vampyrum cross-border politics

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Cyrodiilic Order Vampyrum appears across multiple independent sources.

- An intercepted letter reports probing along Skyrim's southern border.
- Isran worries the Order may exploit a Skyrim vampire power vacuum and push north from Bruma.
- Valerica identifies Bruma as a route into Order Vampyrum society.
- The Book-of-Curses Vampire Master plans to negotiate with the Order in Cyrodiil.
- A note signed "The Order" proposes a Bruma meeting on the 75th anniversary of an earlier parlay between vampire groups.

The convergence supports the Order as a durable, diplomatic, territorially aware vampire institution.

Specific invasion expectations remain character assessments rather than certain future history.
