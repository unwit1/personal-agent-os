# Eastmarch Gentleman's Club worker recruitment

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Club publicly recruits attractive young women for games, bath-house, bar, and patron-facing work.

The recruitment pitch emphasizes:
- better pay;
- fashion and social skills;
- access to influential clientele;
- networking with Skyrim's upper crust.

A personalized note to Susanna shows direct scouting of tavern/service workers.

The evidence supports targeted recruitment, not proof that Susanna accepted or that the Syndicate caused her death.
