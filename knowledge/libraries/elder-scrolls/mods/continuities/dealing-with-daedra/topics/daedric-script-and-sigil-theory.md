# Daedric script and sigil theory

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Three sources intersect around magically functional writing.

The Daedric Alphabet:
- maps Daedric letters to Tamrielic analogues;
- speculates about a common language root;
- treats letterform variants as possible dialects;
- notes traditions that names/words have power among Daedra.

The Dagon cultist:
- says his "sigils" are not true higher-Daedra sigils;
- describes them as specialized conjuration writing in Daedric script;
- uses materially Nirnian/"Aedric" components to anchor the effect.

Quinergus:
- uses elemental energy as rune ink;
- explicitly wants to understand why Daedric letters work where Shalidor-style script does not.

Together these establish that the continuity treats script choice as part of magical engineering. The common-language-root explanation remains scholarship, not verified cosmology.
