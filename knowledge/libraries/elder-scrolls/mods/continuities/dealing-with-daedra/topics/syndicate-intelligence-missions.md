# Syndicate intelligence missions

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Syndicate mission briefs show a structured intelligence function.

### Northwatch
Cassus Carius has been captured by the Thalmor. The priority is recovering interrogation transcripts to assess compromise and conduct damage control.

### Mistwatch
A trafficking operation is investigated to identify buyers, competitors, leverage targets, and unknown smuggling channels.

### Treva's Watch
The Syndicate seeks incriminating records from a politically/business-connected Rift family during a succession crisis.

Across these missions the Syndicate collects information as aggressively as money: compromised secrets, customer networks, smuggling routes, and blackmail material are treated as strategic assets.
