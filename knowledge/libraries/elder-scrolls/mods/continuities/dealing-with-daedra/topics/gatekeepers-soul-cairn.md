# Gatekeepers and Soul Cairn commerce

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Gatekeepers operate a transactional necromantic organization that exchanges souls, soul gems, artifacts, and services for power from patrons in the Soul Cairn.

Their own dialogue separates corpse animation from manipulation of animating souls/vestiges and treats necromancy partly as a technical/commercial discipline.

INFO 058C8306 explicitly considers several possible identities for the patrons and treats the Ideal Masters as the strongest guess, not a certainty. This uncertainty must be preserved.

The same lore cluster intersects Vigilant Toruld's later schism: records referring to gifts from "the masters", soul-stealing rings, and Soul Cairn anti-Daedra powers make an Ideal-Master identification plausible across sources.
