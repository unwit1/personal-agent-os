# North-Jerall coven

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Beldam Annes describes the North-Jerall witches as small cells, commonly three members, distributed around Falkreath and northern Cyrodiil. Their tradition emphasizes Hircine, hunting, lycanthropy, alchemy, and practical manipulation of natural/supernatural structures.

Annes contrasts them with Reachmen, Glenmoril, and Darklight traditions. Because she is a partisan actor in active competition with some of these groups, her descriptions are evidence of North-Jerall beliefs and strategic knowledge rather than neutral ethnography.

The player is used to acquire rival techniques, manipulate inter-coven blame, and reduce rival influence in Falkreath.

Primary evidence: INFO 0572C7F8, 0572C7F9, 05708A9C, 05708AAD, 05708AB2.
