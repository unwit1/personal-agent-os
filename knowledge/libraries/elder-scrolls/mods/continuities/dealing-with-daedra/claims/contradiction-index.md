## Retrieval rule

A contradiction edge means "show both sources and their evidentiary type." It does **not** mean "average them together" or "pick the source with the highest confidence."

Mechanically corroborated practice can establish that a magic system works inside the continuity without proving the speaker's explanation of why it works.
