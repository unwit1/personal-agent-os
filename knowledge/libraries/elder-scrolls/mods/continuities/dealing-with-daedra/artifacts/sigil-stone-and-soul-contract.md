# Sigil Stone and soul-collateral contract

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A Dremora summoned through the Exile/Doom-Truth path offers power in exchange for a Sigil Stone.

## Contract structure

The Dremora states:
- delivery of a Sigil Stone is the requested consideration;
- ownership of the mortal's soul is held as collateral;
- death before repayment sends the debtor to Coldharbour;
- the written contract is signed in blood.

When the player later delivers the stone, one branch reveals a trap: accepting a gift from the summoned Dremora broke the conjuration's seals/compulsions, but the Dremora argues the blood contract still gives it ownership of the soul.

The verbal Sigil-Stone forfeiture clause and the written soul-for-power clause are treated as separable.

## Falion's countermeasures

Falion says the Dremora is associated with Molag Bal and suggests two loophole strategies:

1. learn the Dremora's Protonymic/Neonymic, force a summoning, kill its manifested form, and bind it into a Daedric weapon as leverage;
2. become a vampire so Molag Bal obtains a superior claim to the soul, then cure vampirism to leave no remaining claimant.

These are Falion's proposed legal-metaphysical workarounds, not general universal rules outside this continuity.

## Falion's Sigil Stone

Falion also owns a Sigil Stone anchoring an Aetherial slipstream pocket. Removing the stone collapses the pocket.

That distinguishes true Sigil Stones from the Dagon cell's derivative written "sigils".
