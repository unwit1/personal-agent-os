# Synod

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Synod appears through a stranded field researcher and through commentary by College of Winterhold figures.

## Synod self-description

The High Tide researcher calls the Synod the finest magical society in the Empire and says it has branches throughout Cyrodiil from Bruma to Leyawiin.

The researcher is on a confidential mission to recover the design of the wrecked Patchwork Airship.

The expedition collapses after Rieklings kill assistants and steal supplies, including:
- a powerful staff;
- a Welkynd Stone;
- an amulet of Julianos.

The researcher offers scrolls for Riekling kills and recovered belongings.

## College of Winterhold view

Mirabelle Ervine describes both the Synod and College of Whispers as political organizations that seek power through magic.

She claims the Synod:
- is exclusive rather than open;
- favors wealthy or politically useful recruits;
- is attempting to expand influence into Skyrim from Bruma.

She says the College of Winterhold has refused alliance with both Imperial magical societies.

## Reliability

The Synod Researcher is an openly partisan member.

Mirabelle is an institutional rival.

The continuity securely establishes the expedition, presence in Skyrim, rivalry with the College of Whispers, and political competition. Broader value judgments remain speaker-attributed.
