# Rift Bank and Mineral Exchange

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary hub: Rift Bank and Mineral Exchange (CELL 057BF74F).
Principal: Mr. Aedwatch.

The bank is a functioning financial institution rather than merely a quest backdrop.

The Teller describes:
- silver, gold, and gemstone exchange;
- safe-deposit services;
- short-term/7-day deposit products with variable returns;
- ordinary lending, temporarily suspended because provincial instability makes it too risky.

The Teller says Aedwatch built his fortune through gem and gold trading in Alinor before moving to Skyrim during the rise of the Thalmor.

The Curator manages Aedwatch's collection and accepts unusual objects and Dwemer arms/armor while rejecting Daedric-affiliated items.

## Occult contact surface

A Dagon-linked Merchant is physically placed inside the bank and recruits ambitious visitors toward magical power.

This proves that the bank can function as a contact surface for an occult recruiter.

It does **not** prove that Aedwatch, the Teller, Curator, guards, or bank management knowingly sponsor that recruitment.
