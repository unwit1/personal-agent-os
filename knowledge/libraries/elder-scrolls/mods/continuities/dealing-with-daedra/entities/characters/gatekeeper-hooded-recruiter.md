# Gatekeeper Hooded Recruiter

Form ID: 0557C32F
Editor ID: dealscursedconjurer3
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Gatekeepers

The Hooded Figure calls himself "a missionary of a sort".

He targets people exposed to death, arguing that fear of the unknown afterlife makes them receptive to the Gatekeepers' promise of certainty.

Before giving full access, he uses a mundane delivery as a test: a tattoo kit and purple ink are sent to the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club.

After successful delivery he explains that the order prepares souls in life so they can persist as unusually potent/self-aware ghosts rather than pass into an uncertain afterlife.

The character is therefore the clearest surface recruiter for the Gatekeepers, connecting their necromantic theology to ordinary trade and social spaces.
