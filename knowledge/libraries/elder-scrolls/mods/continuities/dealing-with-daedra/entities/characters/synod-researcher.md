# Synod Researcher

Form ID: 05656DE4
Editor ID: dealsinnboatmage
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location: The High Tide

The unnamed researcher is part of a confidential Synod expedition to recover the design of the Patchwork Airship.

Rieklings killed the researcher's assistants and scattered the expedition's equipment.

Important lost objects include:
- Synod Staff;
- Welkynd Stone;
- amulet of Julianos.

The researcher offers scrolls as bounties for Riekling ears and stronger rewards for returning the lost items.

The researcher jokes about or endorses killing the Rieklings until extinction, making the source useful not only for Synod logistics but for the moral culture of this particular field operative.

The researcher also provides the Synod's self-image: an Imperial magical society superior to the College of Whispers and spread across Cyrodiil.
