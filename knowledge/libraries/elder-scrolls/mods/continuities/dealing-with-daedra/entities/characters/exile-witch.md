# Exile Witch

Form ID: 053E12AA
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The exile witch is a former North-Jerall practitioner who left the Falkreath cell and now serves a Doom-Truth, described by her as a lesser Daedra.

She teaches ritual magic as a way for low-magicka practitioners to cast by releasing residual magical energy from specially prepared tallow candles. She states that the best tallow comes from mer, though men and beast races also work.

Her tuition and errands connect several networks: she demands Service Coupons from the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club, sends the player back toward her former North-Jerall associates, and eventually uses a portal/communion ritual to introduce the player to her Daedric master.

She says the price she paid for her own power was recruiting three new people for the patron to deal with. Her model of Daedric bargaining is explicitly transactional: value depends on what the mortal wants, what the patron demands, and whether the mortal judges the exchange worthwhile.
