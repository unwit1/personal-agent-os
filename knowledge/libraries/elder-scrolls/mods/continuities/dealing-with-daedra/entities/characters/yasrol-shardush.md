# Yasrol Shardush

Form ID: 059B7877
Editor ID: dealsdragonqm
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: Cult of the Return quartermaster and smith/enchanter.

Yasrol is an Orc serving the restored Dragon-Cult movement.

When challenged about an Orc worshipping Atmoran gods, he describes Orcs as enslaved in life and afterlife by "the most wretched of the Daedra" but refuses to elaborate.

His practical role is arms, armor, and maintenance training.

Yasrol practices what he calls "elven enchanting", learned while pursuing advanced smithing. He says the Herald tolerates the art because it is useful despite not being a pure Atmoran/dragon tradition.

He gives recruits basic enchanting maintenance, soul gems, enchanted tools, and tactical work such as clearing frost trolls from Cold Rock Pass.

Yasrol is therefore evidence that the Cult of the Return is syncretic in practice even while preaching restorationist purity: useful foreign techniques are retained when they serve the movement.
