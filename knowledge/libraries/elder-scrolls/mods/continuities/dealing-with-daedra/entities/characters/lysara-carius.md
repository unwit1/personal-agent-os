# Lysara Carius

Form ID: 0533EC13
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: restorer of a hereditary Mephalan cult.

Lysara describes herself as the last Carius and says her family served Mephala for generations in exchange for power, youth, and beauty before Vigilants destroyed the tradition.

Her rebuilt cult, the Widows of Mephala, uses betrayal as both theology and magical currency. Dialogue around INFO 0569E028 and 0569E022 links the offering ritual to sacrifice of a spouse and later lovers, with blessings including renewed youth/beauty and other powers.

Lysara studies the spheres of Mara, Dibella, and Sanguine to improve seduction and social manipulation before directing trust and intimacy into Mephalan betrayal. The organization also uses poisons, Illusion, identity changes, investments/front businesses, and recruitment of unhappy spouses.

INFO 0569E011 states Lysara's claim that the Night Mother is Mephala. This is an uncorroborated cult claim and is stored as such rather than as continuity-neutral truth.
