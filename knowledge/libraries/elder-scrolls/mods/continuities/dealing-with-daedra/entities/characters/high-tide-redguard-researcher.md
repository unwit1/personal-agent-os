# High Tide Redguard weapons researcher

Form ID: 05660FEF
Editor ID: dealsinnboatredguard
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location: the High Tide.

An unnamed weapons expert from Hammerfell studies Dwemer technology for military reverse engineering.

He explicitly frames the work around preparing for another possible Thalmor invasion.

On Solstheim he seeks a Dwemer artifact poetically described as "the black doom, which cast fate itself as arrows from a bow"; the recovered object turns out to be a literal bow.

He also owns a Dwemer Automaton Deactivation Rod that works against mainland-Skyrim automata but fails against Solstheim designs.

From that failure he concludes that Dwemer control sequencers vary by design principle and predicts the rod would probably also fail against Morrowind/Hammerfell variants.

Observed failure is strong evidence; the wider regional extrapolation remains his research hypothesis.
