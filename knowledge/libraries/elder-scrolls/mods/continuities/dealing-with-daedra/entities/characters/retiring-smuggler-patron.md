# Retiring Smuggler Patron

Form ID: 055C3423
Editor ID: dealssmugglerpointer
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

An unnamed smuggling contact recruits the player to infiltrate beneath/behind the College of Winterhold.

He is preparing to retire and explicitly avoids taking the risk himself. He asks the player to tell anyone searching for him that he is dead.

The associated written instructions identify the target as Velehk Sain's navigational charts, valued because Sain used them to avoid the Imperial Navy.

An earlier infiltration team had already attempted a natural-cavern route and left trail markers before running out of climbing spikes.

The Patron is best understood as the outgoing contact in a smuggling operation whose later jobs are handled by Plautis Vulpin.
