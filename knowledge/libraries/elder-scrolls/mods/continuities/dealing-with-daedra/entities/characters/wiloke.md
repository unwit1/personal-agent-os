# Wiloke

Form ID: 058F0E14
Editor ID: dealsrecruiter2
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: party contact in the Sanguine-affiliated Love Shack branch.

Wiloke greets the player as a party attendee and offers a "special" drink imported from Morrowind.

Repeated drinking advances the party sequence.

The associated implementation then transitions the player through blackout/travel messaging into a Morrowind slave-brothel state.

This makes Wiloke's drink sequence a deterministic trigger in the trafficking branch.

However, his dialogue alone does not establish whether he knows the final destination or is consciously acting as a trafficker. Preserve that distinction.
