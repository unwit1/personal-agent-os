# Sanguine — Love Shack manifestation

Form ID: 0568EB09
Editor ID: dealsSanguine
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Sanguine appears directly at the Sanguine-followers' Love Shack.

He describes himself as being "in the flesh, for the moment at least", indicating a temporary embodied manifestation without explaining its exact mechanism.

If the player carries the relevant Mephala/Widows context, Sanguine recognizes the influence of the "8-legged one" and encourages the player to enjoy his own sphere in addition to serving their existing mistress.

This makes the scene direct evidence for non-exclusive Daedric devotion in this mod continuity.

No direct Sanguine dialogue in this cluster establishes knowledge of or responsibility for the later Morrowind trafficking outcome.
