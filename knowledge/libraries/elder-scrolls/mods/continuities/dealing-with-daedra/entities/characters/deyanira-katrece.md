# Deyanira Katrece

Form ID: 057D9044
Editor ID: dealsshrike
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: Shrike of Nocturnal and Darkling patron/intermediary.

Deyanira appears through the Darkling ritual and grants Ichor according to one of the three paths: Shadow, Subterfuge, or Strife.

She remembers an older Darkling presence at an Imperial fort on Lake Honrich and orders the player to touch the ruined Nocturnal shrine at Faldar's Tooth.

She also orders the purge of Darklight leaders who abandoned Nocturnal for other practices. Her dialogue establishes that the Nocturnal/Darkling conflict is not merely academic rivalry but treated as apostasy.

Deyanira limits the player to three Ichor infusions, calling three the sacred number. This is Darkling/Nocturnal cult practice within the mod, not a general statement about Nocturnal canon.
