# Cultist Wraith

Form ID: 051D00DD
Editor ID: dealspurewraith
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Gatekeepers
Primary site: Yorgrim Overlook

The Cultist Wraith is a speaking supernatural member/service provider of the Gatekeeper economy rather than a generic hostile summon.

## Trade

It requests empty white soul gems and says the gems are shattered and mixed with withered soul-grass from its master's realm to help summon soldiers through the void.

It also trades in Soulgem Dust.

For sufficient Dust it can:
- bless the mortal with Soul-Cairn/Grave-touch affinity;
- convert ordinary Soulgem Dust into Gravetouched Soulgem Dust;
- train Conjuration.

The Wraith refuses excessive blessing because the recipient would not survive and eventually says it is not permitted to teach more, directing the mortal to the Masters instead.

## Reliability

The trade actions are mechanically corroborated by topic-fragment scripts.

The Wraith's references to its "master's realm" and "the Masters" fit the Gatekeepers' Soul Cairn patron system. Exact identification with the Ideal Masters should still inherit the uncertainty already recorded for the Gatekeepers rather than being treated as a new independent confirmation.
