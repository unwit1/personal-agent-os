# Merithis Giryem

Form ID: 059B7876
Editor ID: dealsdragonalch
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: Cult of the Return mycologist/alchemist.

Merithis is a Dunmer refugee/scholar who rejects both Tribunal and Daedric salvation narratives after the fall of Baar Dau and the Argonian invasion.

He joins the Cult of the Return despite its Atmoran restorationism, showing that the movement recruits through dissatisfaction with existing divine and political systems rather than ethnicity alone.

## Mycology

Merithis specializes in fungi and argues they are neither plant nor animal but a distinct biological branch with unusually broad alchemical potential.

He claims fungi are especially robust under magical alteration and points to Telvanni practice as the best-known large-scale exploitation of mushrooms as structures.

He asks recruits to gather Mora Tapinella and Scaly Pholiota and teaches basic healing-potion work in return.

His presence reinforces the cult's practical infrastructure: food, medicine, fungi cultivation, and alchemy coexist with apocalyptic theology.
