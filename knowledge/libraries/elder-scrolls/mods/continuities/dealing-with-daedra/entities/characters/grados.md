# Grados

Form ID: 0535306B
Editor ID: dealsinfovendor
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Grados is a blind former Penitus Oculatus operative and intelligence-oriented witchhunter whose arc combines pragmatic anti-mage warfare, morally extreme experimentation, and highly speculative cosmology.

## Mageblight

INFO 0564C2D9 describes Mageblight as tiny artificial parasites made using Chaotic Creatia that consume magicka from a host. Further dialogue says ordinary cleansing does not cure it, the organisms weaken as they spread rather than reproduce indefinitely, and the design is intended as a strategic anti-mage weapon. The in-world book PSA: MageBlight (BOOK 0520D4F1) independently corroborates the affliction's existence and magicka-suppressing symptoms.

## Chaotic Creatia

Grados describes Chaotic Creatia as raw base matter used to constitute Daedric bodies, terrain, and objects, and discusses a possible wellspring on Nirn. These are his research explanations, not automatically objective cosmology.

## Towers, Thalmor, and Talos

INFO 0564719D-0564719E attributes to the Thalmor a plan to deactivate/destroy Towers and unravel Nirn. Other dialogue gives Grados's Tower inventory and his theory of Talos as an oversoul involving Hjalti, Wulfharth, and Zurin in a Lorkhanic/enantiomorphic pattern. Grados explicitly presents parts of this material as complicated theory. It must remain character-attributed and cannot be promoted into Bethesda canon.

## Ethical position

Grados repeatedly frames his methods as a lesser evil and acknowledges that they are terrible. His narrative function is therefore not merely exposition: he is a case study in anti-Daedra/anti-Thalmor instrumentalism becoming morally similar to the threats it opposes.
