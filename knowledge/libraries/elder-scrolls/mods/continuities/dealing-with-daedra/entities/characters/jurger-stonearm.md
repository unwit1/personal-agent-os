# Jurger Stonearm

Form ID: 05371969
Editor ID: dealsHirelingVorstag
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: martial/resource organizer for the Family flesh cult.

Jurger says the Forsworn killed his family and that the holy flesh saved him.

He channels that personal history into the Family's material and military needs.

## Forsworn raids

Jurger directs violence against Forsworn and treats their bodies as useful offerings or raw material for the cult.

## Kolskeggr operation

He later organizes a raid to seize or hold Kolskeggr Mine.

The stated objective is resource extraction rather than necessarily permanent conquest: holding the mine for roughly half a day is enough, he says, for the Family to acquire weeks of needed material.

Successful participation can be rewarded with flesh-extract rations and authority to gather sacrifices for the God-Mother.

Jurger shows that Thaer's biological research and the Family's religion require armed logistics, territory, labor, and consumable resources.
