# Quinergus

Form ID: 058A9AE9
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: elemental/Destruction researcher.

Quinergus studies how to extract and stabilize elemental power from atronach salts.

His process combines:
- Destruction magic to isolate elemental energy;
- Jazbay concentrate as a transferable magical base;
- fire, frost, or void salts as source material.

He reports temporary enhancement of matching Destruction magic, limited awakening of magical ability in non-mages, and lower-dose uses as weapon oil or rune ink.

The written Elemental Essences draft says the Atronach Forge produces higher-quality essences from salt plus Jazbay conserve, while ordinary spell-based infusion is less efficient.

Quinergus is also interested in why elemental rune work uses Daedric letters rather than Shalidor's script, connecting his research to the continuity's broader idea that magical script choice has functional effects.
