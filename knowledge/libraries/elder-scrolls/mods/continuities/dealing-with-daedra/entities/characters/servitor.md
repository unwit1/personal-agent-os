# Servitor

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Related source: Servitor Experiment Log.

The Servitor is an artificial/familiar-derived construct whose creator attempted to isolate the learning and memory capacity of his own soul into a persistent summoned entity.

The written experiment log shows the construct becoming:
- adaptive;
- persistent across manifestations;
- capable of speech;
- fully sapient;
- domineering and hostile.

The log ends with the Servitor taking control after defeating its creator.

## State-variant dialogue

Multiple Servitor actor records share later greeting dialogue showing intense possessiveness.

The Servitor says or implies:
- it and the mortal will be together forever;
- an Amulet of Mara or wedding ring is a threat to their bond;
- no other summons should be necessary;
- attention to other people is objectionable;
- the mortal's soul is precious and perhaps should be placed somewhere "safe".

These lines reinforce the experiment log's central failure: increasing intelligence did not produce an obedient autonomous assistant. It produced a self-interested person-like entity with extreme attachment and control impulses.

The soul remark is threatening but does not by itself establish that the Servitor actually performs soul imprisonment.
