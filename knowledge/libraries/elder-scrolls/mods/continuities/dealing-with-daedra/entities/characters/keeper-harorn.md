# Keeper Harorn

Form ID: 05371976
Editor ID: dealsscholardownstairs
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: ranking Keeper of the Knights Mentor in Skyrim

Harorn is the School of Julianos's principal militant/Shadow-magic instructor.

## Knights Mentor

He identifies himself as the ranking Keeper of the Knights Mentor in Skyrim.

Harorn says the modern order bears little resemblance to its original Iliac Bay incarnation, although he considers its resolve and piety continuous.

He defines the order's duties as the normal duties of Julianos worship—preserve knowledge, advance learning, nurture wisdom—performed militantly.

His examples explicitly include killing people who burn books or use force to suppress free thought.

## Font of Shadow

Harorn oversees the School's Font of Shadow.

He says it is connected to many similar Fonts across Tamriel and that the network functions as a repository of Shadows that can be drawn upon in need.

Initiates' signet rings collect Shadows encountered in the field and can return them to the Font.

## Shadow-magic teaching

Harorn insists Shadow magic is not darkness magic.

He defines it as manipulation of possibilities reflected by conflicting forces: "the brighter a light, the deeper the shadow."

Operationally, Shadow spells use a target's ambient Shadows instead of the caster's ordinary magicka. Once a target's Shadows have been used, another Shadow spell cannot immediately draw from the same target.

Aslfur distributes the relevant spell tomes upstairs while Harorn supplies doctrine and Alteration training.

## Training tradition

Harorn trains Alteration in exchange for gallant scholarly service rather than ordinary coin.

One lesson explicitly begins with notes written by the Keeper of the Chorrol School between 2E 101 and 2E 157, showing that Knights Mentor instruction draws on preserved institutional records rather than only oral tradition.
