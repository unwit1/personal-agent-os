# The Herald

Form ID: 059B7872
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: principal theologian/organizer of the Cult of the Return.

The Herald presents the Return as a restoration of the old Dragon Cult rather than a generic Daedric sect. His doctrine links Alduin's expected return, resurrection of dragons, recovery/protection of dragon burial sites, a revived priesthood, and recruitment of socially displaced people.

## Draugrisation

INFO 059EADB8 describes a staged transformation that begins while the subject remains alive. The Herald claims it strengthens the soul's connection to the Dreamsleeve/Aetherius, supplements failing tissue with necromantic animation, eventually leaves the body undead, and allows higher priests to draw life force from supplicants. He also claims memory erosion follows brain necrosis and Dreamsleeve exposure. This is cult doctrine, not objective metaphysics.

## Dragon theology

INFO 059EADB9 and 059EADBB teach that dragon bodies are semi-mortal, dragon bones are indestructible soul anchors, and Alduin's return can reverse the deaths of dragons killed in the Dragon War. The cult therefore protects and recovers burial sites.

## Historical narrative

The Herald attributes the old cult's collapse to internal corruption, human rebellion, and a Solstheim schism involving Hermaeus Mora. These statements are preserved as ideological/historical testimony rather than verified history.

## Reliability

High confidence for what the Cult of the Return believes and does. Medium/low confidence for the Herald's cosmological and ancient-historical explanations unless independently corroborated.
