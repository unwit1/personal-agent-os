# Grystar

Form ID: 057A1104
Editor ID: dealsmeridiaapologist
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: Meridia/Merid-Nunda apologist associated with the Witch-Hunter's Guild.

Grystar rejects the simple category "Daedric Prince" for Meridia.

He calls her Merid-Nunda and argues that she is a Magna-Ge who fled during the Dawn rather than a conventional Daedra. He contrasts her active power over life/undeath with Arkay, whom he dismisses as a dead or ineffective god.

This is sectarian theology, not neutral cosmology.

Grystar nevertheless provides practical anti-undead services and asks the player to clear an abandoned prison full of ghosts, rewarding the work with a spell using Merid-Nunda's starlight.

His placement in the Witch-Hunter's Guild shows the guild's pragmatism: it tolerates a heterodox Daedric/Magna-Ge devotee because his anti-undead expertise aligns with the profession.
