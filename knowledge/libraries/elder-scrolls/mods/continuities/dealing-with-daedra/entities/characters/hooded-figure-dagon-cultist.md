# Hooded Figure — Dagon cultist

Form ID: 058A9B15
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Hooded Figure is a Mehrunes Dagon recruiter, conjuration teacher, and propagandist.

He reframes Dagon as the Prince of destruction, revolution, ambition, energy, change, and natural disasters. He uses this framing to recruit people who want social mobility or personal power.

His initiation requires a permanent scar, brand, or tattoo as a symbolic irreversible act of change. He then teaches "sigils" that he explicitly says are not true higher-Daedra sigils; they are specialized conjuration scribework using Daedric letters and unusual ink.

INFO 058D7622 claims the ink uses strongly Nirnian/"Aedric" material to anchor magic because Daedra are not native to Nirn. This is his technical explanation.

He also describes "Punished" Dremora transformed into useless flesh as punishment and warns the player not to let one die because death would allow it to reform and end the punishment.

His doubt about official Oblivion-Crisis histories is ideological rhetoric and remains attributed.
