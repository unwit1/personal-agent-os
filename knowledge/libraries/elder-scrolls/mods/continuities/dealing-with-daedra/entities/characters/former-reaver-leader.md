# Former Reaver leader

Form ID: 0565BEEA
Editor ID: dealsinnboatreaver
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location: the High Tide.

A deposed Reaver leader describes himself as a once-legendary Solstheim warrior whose five lieutenants overthrew him.

He claims the older Reavers:
- were a coherent warrior organization rather than ordinary bandits;
- had a code;
- possessed hundreds of years of history;
- originated as a Nordic clan but became predominantly Dunmer over time.

According to him, the five coup leaders immediately fought among themselves, split the group, abandoned the code, and styled themselves Reaver Lords.

He hires the player to kill all five and, after their deaths, announces his intention to fill the resulting power vacuum and restore the Reavers.

The coup/revenge plan is direct character history. His romantic account of the older Reaver institution should remain attributed to him.
