# Former Reaver leader

Form ID: 0565BEEA
Editor ID: dealsinnboatreaver
Display name: Reaver
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Associated hub: High Tide

This unnamed warrior says he once led Solstheim's Reavers before five lieutenants overthrew him.

He presents the old Reavers as a long-lived warrior tradition with a code, claims they began as a Nordic clan and became increasingly Dunmer over time, and blames his former lieutenants for fragmenting the group into competing Reaver Lord bands.

He hires the player to kill the five Reaver Lords and intends to use the resulting power vacuum to restore his leadership and the old Reaver identity.

Reliability: high for his stated goals and role in the quest; medium/low for remote Reaver origins and idealized descriptions of the old organization because he is a partisan restorationist source.
