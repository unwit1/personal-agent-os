# Hrotggi Asgikverssen

Form ID: 059B7874
Editor ID: dealshubrecruiter
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Hrotggi is a priest/recruiter for the Cult of the Return.

He refuses to center his former identity, saying that what he was before no longer matters. His job is to explain the cult's public-facing theology and move new arrivals through a sequence of service tasks before they meet the Herald.

## Recruitment theology

Hrotggi calls the dragons the first of the true Atmoran totem-animals and the rightful god-kings of mankind. He avoids speaking their common name because he believes words in their divine language have power and should be reserved to inducted priests.

He predicts their imminent return and says the restored cult is preparing worship, clergy, and mortal infrastructure.

## Initiation through service

His tests are intentionally mundane:
- cut firewood;
- draw water;
- personally finance a week's food supply;
- arrange produce from Rorikstead.

The lesson is humility, sacrifice, labor, and willingness to serve before receiving occult power.

This makes the Cult of the Return's recruitment system materially different from many other cults in the mod: it first offers shelter, soup, bedrolls, and social belonging, then channels the recruit toward increasingly exclusive doctrine and ritual.
