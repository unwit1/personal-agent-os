# Carsten

Form ID: 0537195B
Editor ID: dealssyndicateclubowner
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Carsten manages the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club and serves as a polished interface to the Syndicate.

The Club uses Service Coupons as internal currency for entertainment, bath-house, gambling, bar, and other services. Membership and employment also create obligations that can later be called in as favors.

Carsten consistently maintains plausible deniability. He describes questionable opportunities as matters for his "associates" and calls the Club legitimate while directing the player toward criminal jobs, competitors, bribery, and hidden contacts.

His network reaches into magical supply chains. Dialogue around 0563CE65 and 0563CECF concerns disrupted precursor/"blue goo" production and possible takeover by associates. He can direct the player toward Grados and later toward a ritual agreement that grants magical enhancement.

Carsten also encourages acquisition of formal political influence, including becoming Thane, because status benefits the organization.

Written corroboration includes The Syndicate bulletin, Welcome to the Syndicate, operation notes, warning letters, and multiple signed delivery forms naming Carsten at the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club.
