# Mojari

Form ID: 056B75DB
Editor ID: dealsratwaykhajiit
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Mojari controls access mechanisms in the Ratway near routes toward the Ragged Flagon.

He is suspicious of strangers and repeatedly requires proof that the player remains a "friend" before opening or repairing access.

His requested favors include:
- Skooma;
- personal attention;
- recovery of special gloves from an Imperial fighter.

Mojari says he enjoys testing his claws against other Khajiit but Skyrim's scarcity of Khajiit opponents and Nord disapproval of claws force him to fight with padded gloves.

After losing a wagered fight to an Imperial, he becomes convinced the opponent's special gloves provided an unfair advantage.

Mojari's role is primarily social/economic evidence: Ratway access is controlled through personal trust and favors rather than formal toll collection.
