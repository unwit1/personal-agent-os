# Mojari

Form ID: 056B75DB
Editor ID: dealsratwaykhajiit
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location/network: Ratway

Mojari is a Khajiit route-keeper in the Ratway who controls door/bridge access and demands proof of friendship from strangers.

He first accepts Skooma as a favor. Later he becomes uncertain that the returning person is the same individual because of a "face changing elf" and requires renewed proof: recovery of special gloves from an Imperial who beat him in a claw-fighting wager.

Mojari therefore participates in the Ratway's informal favor economy, where personal trust and desired goods substitute for standardized payment.
