# Leigry

Form ID: 05857B4C
Editor ID: dealscourierboss
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: courier/logistics manager associated with Distribution Center operations.

Leigry is part of the deliberately mundane logistics layer running underneath the mod's occult economy.

A large series of order forms and signed delivery forms route goods through the Windhelm Distribution Warehouse / Distribution Center system to cults, clubs, researchers, and other customers.

Leigry's relevance is structural rather than metaphysical: his network demonstrates that many magical factions depend on repeatable civilian supply chains rather than conjuring all resources from nowhere.

The courier system is therefore valuable provenance for relationships that might otherwise look speculative. A signed order form can independently corroborate that two groups were doing business even when dialogue frames the connection euphemistically.
