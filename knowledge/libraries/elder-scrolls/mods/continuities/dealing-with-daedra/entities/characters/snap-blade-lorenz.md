# "Snap-blade" Lorenz

Form ID: 056BC76A
Editor ID: dealsratwaysmith
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location/network: Ratway

Lorenz is indebted to Bookie Herod and offers a ruined Elven dagger as payment to anyone who resolves that debt. He does not care whether the solution is negotiation or violence.

His dagger can become an input into a longer Ratway favor chain, demonstrating how scarce equipment is used as social currency even when damaged.
