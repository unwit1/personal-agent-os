# Brethren Priest

Form ID: 056E0123
Editor ID: dealsdeeppriest2
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary site: Gilded Mine

The Brethren Priest is one of the clearest sources on both the Deep-One cult's theology and its coercive social structure.

## Captive labor

The priest says outsiders who learn too much are not allowed to leave.

Uninitiated captives can be:
- forced to dig tunnels;
- assigned mushroom cultivation;
- sent to a "farm";
- taken to a "blood-table";
- eventually rewarded with Brethren initiation for effective service.

Fear of worse assignments is explicitly described as a labor-control tool.

## Transformation

The priest says the Deep Ones use flesh magic to give initiates some Deep-One traits.

The more important transformation is hereditary:
- children of Brethren are said to become more Deep-One-like;
- repeated rituals across generations accelerate the process.

## Origin traditions

One branch says the Deep Ones are Udyrlistig descended from primordial trolls who sought Hermaeus Mora's transformation secrets.

Another branch interprets the encountered beings as Lurkers and admits that the priests' Hermaeus-Mora/Lurker account is partly conjectural.

The priest explicitly acknowledges uncertainty, so the hereditary transformation practice should be separated from the speculative ancient origin model.
