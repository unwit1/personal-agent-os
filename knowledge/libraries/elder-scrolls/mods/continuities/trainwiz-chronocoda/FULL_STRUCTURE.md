# Current-snapshot full lore structure

This layer records the full **current-source lore structure** of the verified Aethernautics 3.1 and Sotha Sil Expanded 3.1 packages. Historical edition archaeology is intentionally not an active requirement.

The layer preserves all extracted book text, dialogue responses/topics, quest journals, characters/speakers, places, factions, named implementation objects, script-carried lore strings, script/source references, concept evidence maps, and evidence-scoped relationship graphs.

These source scopes remain separate from Wheels of Lull and from Bethesda canon; the `trainwiz-chronocoda` graph links them without flattening provenance.
