# Current-snapshot semantic synthesis 01

This pass uses the complete verified current snapshots for **Sotha Sil Expanded 3.1**, **Aethernautics 3.1**, and the mature **Wheels of Lull** corpus. It intentionally prioritizes structural understanding over historical edition archaeology.

## Chronographer spine

The strongest cross-continuity structure is a three-stage Chronographer arc.

1. **Sotha Sil Expanded** supplies the formation/departure layer. Fabricants leave the Clockwork City, form or become Chronographers, travel through time and space, and distribute Imperfections.
2. **Aethernautics** supplies an expeditionary layer. Century Adgran leads Chronographers into Rthamaz to recover and launch the aethership Nzhavananear. The surrounding records show an organized Chronographer culture with time-unit ranks/titles and a technological mission.
3. **Wheels of Lull** supplies the world-maintenance layer. Lull-Mor is an operational Chronographer settlement whose members repair world mechanisms, respond to threats to Towers, solve problems, and move on.

This is a strong structural arc, but it is not normalized into a single omniscient chronology. Each stage retains its source scope.

## Sotha Sil, Gestalt, and the Clockwork City

Sotha Sil Expanded explicitly keeps the original Sotha Sil dead. **Gestalt** is a copy/partial upload of his mind into the Clockwork City. The endgame has mutually exclusive save and destroy branches:

- save: Gestalt continues operating/embodying the City and may be becoming “Seht”;
- destroy: Gestalt and effectively the City are destroyed, although some machinery remains.

Wheels later contains Numinar's statement that after the Clockwork City “died” he was shipped out with other Chronographers. That statement fits a failed/destroyed City more naturally than an intact one, but it does **not** justify selecting the Sotha destroy branch as canon. The graph therefore preserves the tension.

## Chronographer origins and the Armour of Oscillating Quartz

The direct Sotha quest material and the later *Chronography* histories are complementary but epistemically different. Sotha depicts departing fabricants becoming/forming the Chronographers. *Chronography* says the Armour of Oscillating Quartz influenced fabricants who became Chronographers, and reports/believes that Sotha Sil had reforged 1,200 fallen followers into that crystal host.

The library now models:
- the fabricant formation event as direct quest/dialogue evidence;
- Armour influence as later scholarly history;
- the 1,200-follower origin specifically as a qualified reported claim.

## Imperfections and temporal integrity

Sotha directly gives the departing Chronographers a role distributing **Imperfections**. Aethernautics/Wheels *Chronography II* interprets Chronographer behavior as preservation of temporal integrity: small interventions nudge events toward futures that must still occur.

Together these sources support a consistent organizational model: Chronographers are not merely “time travelers”; they are interventionist maintainers who treat history as something that can require repair.

## Pneuma, identity, and machine personhood

Sotha dialogue directly calls **pneuma** a fabricant soul-battery. The shared *Wind Up and Wound Down* treatise describes **Rewound** as fabricant bodies whose mechanisms restart after pneuma/soul departure, retaining fragments and loops of prior behavior without the departed soul.

Wheels independently uses pneuma in active dialogue and treats fabricants as sapient people with replaceable/transportable mechanical components. Across the continuity, the important distinction is therefore not “machine versus person” but **body/mechanism versus pneuma/soul/personhood**.

## Travel and large-scale technology

Aethernautics extends the Chronographer system into practical trans-spatial engineering:
- Rthamaz and its Aetherion defenses;
- Aetherium/starmetal power;
- Nzhavananear as an operational aethership;
- destinations including Sotha Sil, Masser, and Tamriel;
- a labeled Reman-era lunar colony on Masser.

This belongs in the same technology graph as Chronographer time/space travel but remains source-scoped to Aethernautics unless corroborated elsewhere.

## Engineered worlds and metaphysical infrastructure

Two shared books provide broader conceptual bridges.

*How Two Moon* proposes manufacturing a perfect clockwork world in Oblivion using Sotha Sil-associated metal/machinery and a reenacted world-birth. This strongly resembles Wheels' larger interest in artificial/prototype worlds, especially Sybandis, but no source identifies the proposal with Sybandis.

*Musings on Power* treats magical, political, cosmic, and engineered power as an infrastructure problem subject to transmission loss. It offers a useful interpretive model for the continuity's world-machinery and maintenance themes, but the text itself does not explicitly name the Wheels.

## Retrieval rule

Queries about this continuity should prefer:
1. direct quest/dialogue/implementation evidence for events and active systems;
2. technical or scholarly in-universe texts with their stated uncertainty;
3. literary/sectarian/speculative texts only with their reliability classification;
4. cross-continuity synthesis only when the relationship is explicitly represented in the graph.
