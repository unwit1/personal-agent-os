## Research summary
A letter reporting the beginning of scholarly work in the Scholarium. Irnard describes libraries containing ancient knowledge, texts in forgotten languages, magical scripts and codes, and the need to cross-check newer histories against ancient accounts to establish truth and trace how stories evolved.
