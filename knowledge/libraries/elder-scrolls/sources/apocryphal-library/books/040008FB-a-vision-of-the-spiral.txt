A Vision of the Spiral

[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Arcane Scribblings/arcane04.png' height='451' width='296'>
[pagebreak]

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/E_letter.png'>verything starts from a point unattainable. Trace a spiral all the way to the centre and you just run out of room to draw a spiral, but the shape keeps going, inward and inward, to a point you can never draw, but technically it's there. If you draw a ring around it, it's definitely in the ring.

Likewise, I couldn't visit Mephala in her realm in order to petition her for the Ebony Blade, of which I had great need. Fortunately, the simple truth of the matter was provided to me in my reading of numerologies. Reaching the Spiral Skein was to be as simple a matter as drawing a ring around the entry point, which was too small to exist.

To this end I drew a spiral on the ground in the blood of a Dunmer I had slain dishonourably. He deserved it, but he had wanted a fair fight. And, I daresay, a fist fight at that.

I wove his entrails into a tight spiral and dropped them in the middle of the blood spiral. And I sat somewhere over the top so that the centre would have to be beneath me, and then I chanted, having written the letters:
<font face='$DaedricFont'>
PALE SPIDER KING MEPHALA 
WHOSE LAWS ARE BOUND ONLY TO YOUR WILL
SHOW ME THE SKEIN SO I MAY SPEAK DIRECTLY
</font>
I burned the offering of Nightshade as was custom, and the heavy fumes of the plant lulled me into a stupour.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Skein I would now see in the vision. There it was, the cavern with the false pillars of an untrue sky. Spiders with the heads of women wove the walls into ever-shimmering gossameres, and I wished I was truly there so I could explore the intricacies of the realm for myself. But I was bound by the vision, and before long I was pulled by strands of fate and fame to the throne of the Spider Queen, Lord Mephala, his hair flowing silver, her legs arched and black.

Lady Mephala, King of Spiders, I began. I have come to trade a favour for a boon. You know what I seek.
<font face='$DaedricFont'>
YOU SEEK MY EBONY BLADE MORTAL
</font>
It is so, Lord Mephala, Queen of Spiders. What might I do to receive this boon?
<font face='$DaedricFont'>
YOU HAVE ALREADY KILLED THE ELF I ASKED YOU TO KILL
THAT WAS THE FIRST STEP
YOU WERE DRAWN TO THIS BY MY INIQUITY
THE NEXT STEP IS TO FRAME TWO FOR THE MURDER
RICH ONES
STRONG ONES
MAKE THEM BELIEVE THE OTHER IS THE MURDERER
</font>
And so my vision returned to me.

I spent the next few days weaving a belief in the mind of a local nobleman that the Dunmer slain had been one of his subjects, executed by the Dark Brotherhood as part of a ploy to terrorise the locals.
I told the Dark Brotherhood that the dead mer was a Black Sacrament, and that they should attend to the scene to learn their new contract.

When they showed up, the guards attacked them. The resulting feud resulted in much bloodshed.

When next I slept, Mephala came to me in person and said
<font face='$DaedricFont'>
YOU NEVER NEEDED TO PAINT THE SPIRAL
I WAS ALREADY COMING TO YOU
YOU WERE MY INSTRUMENT
AND SUCH A TUNE I PLAYED
HERE TAKE YOUR REWARD
</font>
And I awoke beside the sword.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'> could go on at length about the crimes I committed with the Ebony Blade of Mephala, but mostly it was just murder. They were people I wanted dead for my own reasons, but the longer I held onto the blade the more I wondered: Are my reasons ever my own?
