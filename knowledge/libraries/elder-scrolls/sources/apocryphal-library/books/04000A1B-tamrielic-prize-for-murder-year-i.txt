The Tamrielic Prize for Murder
An All-Sanctuaries Competition

Year I: 3E 240


As awarded by the Listener of the Most Secret and Ancient Night Mother's Catacomb

<p align="left">
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'> would like to begin by congratulating everyone who took part in the inaugural murder competition! There were some excellent entries, and if the spirit of this contest is matched at our next contest (currently set for 245!) then we can expect the Night Mother to be most pleased indeed.

Without further ado, let us address this year's top four in reverse order.

In fourth place, TITUS RATHE of the Cheydinhal Sanctuary. The death of Elder Council busybody Andiriel went above and beyond. For those who haven't already heard the story, Titus was able to replicate the effects of the notorious long-lasting laxative poison of the Hidden Crane and apply it is a blade venom. Shot from a simple blowpipe, the dart found its mark - and the unfortunate councillor vacated most of his insides on the lavatory. Now it's against the rules to speculate who performed a Black Sacrament, but we do notice this: his Imperial Majesty Uriel Septim IV was very clearly holding back a smirk when the news was delivered.

In third place, RYLAS ANDASRETH of the Mournhold sanctuary. You don't often think of enchanters as being master assassins, but when they are - the legends write themselves. 
Most of you have never seen an Inverted Arcanix enchantment, nor seen a Tribunal Temple triolith-amulet marked with such, but the look of shock on Temple priest Ules Fathyndal's face as he froze to death while performing a blessing? You don't see that everyday! Well done Rylas.

In second place, UZEEKA of the Wayrest sanctuary. We all love stealth and subtlety in our killings, of course. Well, not Uzeeka apparently! Absolute spectacle. That ancient tower on the walls finally collapsed with a terrible crash, right in the middle of day. How regrettable that the Archmage of the Mages Guild should be underneath! They really don't teach people detection magic anymore, do they? You really picked your mark for this one, too - there were NO stipulations about innocent bystanders! Sithis is cackling in the void after this one!

And in first place... the first winner of the Tamrielic Prize for Murder, for sheer ingenuity in the face of a difficult assignment... it's JA'ZHARTHA of the Corinthe Sanctuary! Tasked with killing not only the Imperial governor of Dune AND the Moth-Priest Antilochus Vario, she was able to dupe them into sharing a Dreamsleeve transmission, before reversing all the wraithbone wards and setting the canticle bark alight. My word, you'd never seen such a mess. Who could have thought moths were even capable such bloodshed? To you, Ja'Zhartha, we proudly grant the first award. Well done to the rest of you, there were a lot of honourable mentions - but fear not, you have five years until the next contest is judged. Get to it, and remember, knives out!
