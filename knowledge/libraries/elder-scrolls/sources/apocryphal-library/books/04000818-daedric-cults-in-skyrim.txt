<p align="center">


DAEDRIC CULTS OF SKYRIM
A Handbook for Vigilants


by
Vigilant Tyranus

</p>



[pagebreak]
<p align="left">

These notes should keep members of the Skyrim Vigil aware of the current local Daedric threats. Some are considered higher priority than others - Least Priority should be considered non-hostile unless reports come in to state otherwise, and Most Priority should be dealt with as soon as resources allow. They are listed alphabetically.
[pagebreak]

AZURA - The Mother of the Rose, as she is known, is considered least priority by the Vigil. She is venerated by the Dunmer and the Khajiit, and we do not wish to provoke either of those nations or peoples. Very few acts against the general population have been attributed to her devotees, and in general her followers seem to abide the law as much as those of the Divines. Her followers should probably be left alone, even if they bear her artefact, AZURA'S STAR, since this soul-gem does not have the capacity, according to our lore, to capture 'black' souls. 
She has one major shrine in Skyrim, atop a peak south of Winterhold. It is hard to miss from anywhere in the realm, with her statue standing high over the hold. It is distasteful to see a shrine to a Daedra take up so much of the skyline, though many have admired the craftsmanship of the Dunmer refugees who built it.
The current High Priestess of Azura in Skyrim is Aranea Ierith. The Vigil has made contact with her in the past, and there has yet to be any reason for conflict.
[pagebreak]

BOETHIAH - The Prince of Plots is a terrible foe of mortals, encouraging good folk to betray one another, and to engage in games of deceit and villainy. He, or sometimes She, is often seen as the progenitor of the Dunmer, or their forbears the Chimer. As such she is sacred to them - Dunmer devotees of Boethiah are to be left alone unless they are reported to be engaging in provocative behaviour, or if they are seen to be in the possession of the artefacts GOLDBRAND or the EBONY MAIL. These artefacts are extremely powerful, and a Vigilant should NOT approach their wielder alone. 
Her largest shrine in Skyrim is in Eastmarch, near the border of Morrowind. A statue of the Prince has been observed there. The altar is called the 'Sacellum'. Her new priestess is a Dunmer, as many are, though we do not know her identity yet. 
Non-Dunmer devotees of Boethiah should be considered Moderate Priority.
[pagebreak]

CLAVICUS VILE - This Prince does not seem like one of the greatest threats to Tamriel, given that most who come to rue his existence are those who play his insidious games. But make no mistake - this Daedra is arguably one of the most powerful, and is the prime suspect in the Lilmoth incident in which a Soul Snare caused the deaths and undeaths of thousands. Cultists of Vile are few, but one attempting to fulfil a deal on the Prince's whim may cause a great deal of suffering, perhaps inadvertently; it matters not. He is also recorded as commanding the fealty of Cyrodiil's Order of Vampires. Some of these vampires have supposedly made their den here in Skyrim, at the ruin known as Haemar's Shame. It is considered high risk. 
His worshippers, vampiric or otherwise, and those performing his tasks are to be considered High Priority. Any seen in possession of the MASQUE OF CLAVICUS VILE should be reported to the Vigil Keeper, as the matter may become complicated due to the masque's beguiling effects. The sword UMBRA is rumoured to have been destroyed, though individuals bearing similarly named swords - and acting in a similar manner to its old namesake's wielders - have been recorded in recent years.
[pagebreak]

HERMAEUS MORA - Often overlooked as a rather bookish monstrosity, the Gardener of Men is nevertheless a terrible foe of mortals. In his relentless pursuit of knowledge, he has inspired many to awful acts. 
It is often forgotten that he is part of the ancient Nordic pantheon, where he is simply known as the Woodland Man. The Nords have built no shrines to him in Skyrim that we have ever found, however - perhaps some lurk in the dimmest reaches among the old Draugr ruins, but he does not seem to have been a popular face of worship.
However, a follower of Mora who is wanted in connection with several crimes across Tamriel is believed to be in hiding within Skyrim. Septimus Signus, an Adept of the College of Winterhold, is suspected of several murders and possibly the outbreak of an unrecognised disease in Colovia. The College has not offered any hint of his whereabouts however, and so the trail has gone cold for now. Devotees of the Demon of Knowledge should be considered Moderate Priority, with those of Signus' talents being High Priority. His artefact is the OGHMA INFINIUM, a tome of great knowledge that is sought by almost every mage. Be cautious among those who seek it.

On neighbouring Solstheim, there has been sparse reports of tentacle-masked figures that have been seen, but none of the local Dunmer population - all devout followers of the Reclamations - have ever confirmed this, nor have any of these figures been seen in Skyrim. Any Vigilants travelling to Solstheim should be ready should his cults be trying to make foothold there.
[pagebreak]

HIRCINE - The Beastfasther, Lord of the Hunt and the Chase, is well represented in Skyrim. Simple hunters may often make prayer to this Prince, though they are considered low priority. Of greater concern is the number of Werewolves in Skyrim. A single werewolf can tear a patrol of unwary Vigilants to shreds in a matter of moments.
Werewolves are, consequently, left to specialists. We tend to communicate sightings of such to our colleagues in the Silver Hand, who have taken their own vigil against the threat. The Silver Hand are not generally to be approached directly, however. It is best left to those in the Vigil who are on the best terms with them. They are rumoured to be investigating the Companions of Jorrvaskr.
We do not know where the shrines of Hircine lie, though they are likely in the wilds around Falkreath and Whiterun holds, where game is to be had. Their worshippers should be considered Moderate Priority, raising to High Priority if it seems that the Silver Hand will not be able to respond.
His closest artefacts are the SPEAR OF BITTER MERCY, the SAVIOUR'S HIDE and the RING OF HIRCINE. Anyone discovering these items should report them to their superior instantly - they are all extremely dangerous, in anyone's hands.
[pagebreak]

MALACATH - The Keeper of the Sworn Oath is usually considered Least Priority. His Orcish worshippers are not to be disturbed unless we get reports of dangerous activity. Non-orc followers are one step higher at Low Priority - they have no cultural reason to devote themselves, so they are more suspicious, but we have no records of any existing in Skyrim. Beyond his ancestral quarrels with the ancient Nords and Dunmer, his cult has generally been of lesser concern.
His shrines are mostly found in Orc strongholds, but his effigy has been reported by nomadic hunters at a grove high in the Velothi mountains.
His artefacts are SCOURGE and VOLENDRUNG. The former is unusual; it is a mace designed to be used against Daedra. Consequently it may be of use to the Vigil, though only high ranked Vigilants should be allowed to use it, and even then only with permission from the Keeper. Volendrung is a Dwarven hammer - how it came to be associated with Malacath is unknown.
[pagebreak]

MEHRUNES DAGON - The Prince of Ambition may have been defeated in the Oblivion Crisis, but the Stormcloak rebellion may be fuelling one of his attributes: Revolution. Needless to say, cults of Dagon should always be considered Most Priority. The Mythic Dawn are largely believed to be extinct, though there have been sightings in the Velothi mountains of some wearing their colours. 
There is one shrine to Dagon in Skyrim, and the mountain path that leads to it is guarded by the Vigil itself. Any who take that route should be watched closely.
Recently, a descendant of the Mythic Dawn, a man called Silus Vesuius, has made his home in Dawnstar. He is seen as an eccentric and a collector of old relics of his ancestors, but has shown no signs of worship Dagon worship yet. He is nevertheless to be watched closely.
His artefact, simply known as MEHRUNE'S RAZOR, has been neutralised. The specifics of this are known only to our upper echelons and another group with whom they have worked. The information is considered too dangerous for any.
[pagebreak]

MEPHALA - The Spider Matron, mistress of murder and assassins. She has no known shrines in Skyrim, except possibly small Reclamation shrines in Windhelm among the Dunmer population. Other than them, she has no known cultists here either - Morag Tong activity in Skyrim is presumed absent at the moment. Given that her assassins tend to be acting on behalf of others, they are best left to other authorities. And so despite her dread aspect, her cult is considered Low Priority, with her Dunmer adherents in the cities being Least Priority.
We aren't expecting to see the EBONY BLADE used in Skyrim, but anyone wielding it should be considered High Priority.
[pagebreak]

MERIDIA - Something of an anomaly among Daedric Princes, in that her aims often align with that of the Vigil. She despises the undead in all their forms, and offers boons to those willing to hunt them down. Thus, short alliances betweem the Vigil and her cult have existed in the years since our founding. Such alliances may only be formed with VERY clear consent from a Keeper. Her cults, though seemingly benign, may have been behind some more sinister actions. Ancient records state that she sided with the Ayleids against the Alessians in the First Era, and restored her fallen champion to life during the Oblivion Crisis to wreak havoc on the Gold Coast.
Her artefact is unknown, but her followers have spoken of a 'Holy Sword' that strikes terror into the undead. Her cults are considered Low Priority.
[pagebreak]

MOLAG BAL - Considered by most Vigilants to be the very worst of the princes, he and his cult are to be considered Most Priority. Most of his worshippers are Vampires, though mortal ones exist too. All will gladly carry out atrocities in their Lord's name. They are to be hunted down wherever possible, with any and all rumours being attended to. His shrines usually turn up in vampire lairs - any that have been reported to us have already been dismantled and cleansed.
His artefact is the MACE OF MOLAG BAL, which has not been seen in Tamriel since the Oblivion Crisis. Any information regarding a weapon matching its description should be passed up the chain of command as soon as possible.
[pagebreak]

NAMIRA - The Queen of the Scuttling Void, and the patroness of cannibals. Her aspect is among the most vile of all the Princes, but her activities usually put her cults at Moderate Priority due to their localised nature. Even her cannibal cults usually cause less death than local bandits. Nevertheless, all rumours of Namira cults should be pursued if resources allow - her actions will cause distress in local communities that have lost people, or the corpses of the buried dead, to her worshippers. Reports of cannibalism should always be reported EXCEPT in Bosmer communities, where the practice is custom and not believed to hav anything to do with Daedra.
We know of no shrines or temples to Namira, but we believe one or two may exists, given that reports of desecrated graves and partially eaten corpses have been passed to us from all the Western holds, most recently The Reach.
She has one known artefact - Namira's Ring - which allows the wearer to consume uncooked flesh, or indeed the flesh of men and mer, without succumbing to disease or illness. If it is located, it should be found and destroyed.
[pagebreak]

NOCTURNAL - The Night Mistress has few followers, and is said not to seek them. Nevertheless she will bargain with mortals, and may be revered by thieves - there are tales of 'Nightingales' who supposedly fulfil a role of twisted warrior-knights among thief-kind. Her 'cults' are considered Low Priority for us, and probably High Priority for local law enforcement. 
Her artefact is the SKELETON KEY, which can open any door - a wretchedly potent tool in the hands of a thief. She also has a Champion called the Gray Fox, who has possibly lived for hundred or even thousands of years - a master rogue, and legendary ringleader of the Thieves Guild, he would be considered a Moderate to High Priority if he were to show up in Skyrim. She has no known altars, though reports of the 'Nightingale' symbol have been reported around the fringes and foothills of Skyrim's borders.
[pagebreak]

PERYITE - The Taskmaster is portrayed in his statues as a profane mockery of Holy Akatosh, bearing four legs rather than two. He sends disease and misery to the people of Tamriel, though not normally fatal ones. He is suspected of involvement in the Thrassian Plague and the Knahaten Flux, but not proven. As such his disciples should be considered High Priority, but diseased individuals seeking his blessing should be considered Moderate to Low. Some of his afflicted have recently crossed over from villages around Jehanna and Evermore to a Dwemer ruin, Bthardamz. They should be monitored carefully, and from a healthy distance.
He has one shrine in Skyrim, located Northwest of Karthwasten. His current priest is Kesh the Clean, whose activities are currently below our concern. He has no known artefacts, but has previously been known to award the SPELLBREAKER shield to his champions, a relic from the Rourken clan's war against Shalidor.
[pagebreak]

SANGUINE - The Master of Revels is a curious creature, and underestimated for his role in Tamrielic history. He has purview over all manner of sins and carnal desires, mirth and merriment - and as such is often dismissed as a harmless prankster. But the Lord's whims can turn the minds of great Jarls, Queens and Generals. What terrors might be unleashed by our sovereigns if they are swayed by his promises?
We have found no traces of any shrines to Sanguine in all of Skyrim - a rumour of a statue of a horned man clutching a rose spotted in the Rift was passed to us, but we have not traced it. His worshippers are usually Low Priority, being drunks and libertines, but if any are spotted wielding a flower called the SANGUINE ROSE, which has the power to call forth Daedric minions, they should consider that person a High Priority target. Similarly, any cults seeking to spread his influence to those in positions of power should be dealt with quickly.
[pagebreak]

SHEOGORATH - The best that can be said about the Prince of Madness is that he doesn't seem actively hostile to mortals - in fact he rather enjoys our company. But he spreads terrible insanity to kings and peasants alike - he is unpredictable to the extreme, and this makes him and his followers a High Priority. 
He has no known shrines in Skyrim, although a statue of a smartly-dressed man has been described on the Solitude Arch in the past. We have not found this shrine yet, and if it exists it probably represents an old nobleman. However, the location is a concern - the Blue Palace once housed Pelagius the Third, known as The Mad. Though his madness is often attributed to his aunt, the Wolf Queen Potema, there is a tale simply titled 'Wabbajack' that appears to be written by the young King of Solitude, in which he attempts to summon Mora but instead calls forth Sheogorath. 
He has no cults in Skyrim that we have found - there are madmen in all areas of Skyrim, though none of them have shown particular reverence to the Prince. If any are found, they are to be considered High Priority. Should the WABBAJACK be spotted, the Keeper should be alerted at once.
[pagebreak]

VAERMINA - The Mistress of Dark Portents brings nightmares to all at some point in their lives. She seeks to take memories from mortals and replace them with visions and dreams of despair. She enjoys bringing mental and spiritual torment, and rewards those who commit sins or atrocities for their own benefit. 
Her vile nature makes her, and her devotees, Most Priority. She has one known temple in Skyrim, Nightcaller Temple, which has been effectively neutralised thanks to a band of orcs, who we steered in that direction after they complained of awful nightmares, and a priest of Mara who was able to seal them within. It is believed that her artefact, the SKULL OF CORRUPTION, may be within - as such, despite the sealed nature of the temple, it should periodically be checked by Vigilants.
