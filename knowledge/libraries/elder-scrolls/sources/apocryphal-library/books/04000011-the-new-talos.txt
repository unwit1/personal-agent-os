The New Talos

By Ingvar the False Prophet

<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>ALOS, god of Mortals! TALOS, god of War!

Some remember him as Tiber Septim. Some remember him as General Talos, the Storm Crown. They are not wrong, for this is who he was. But much changed since then.

When Tiber Septim died, he was elevated to his place among the Divines. Kynareth, Mara and Dibella ordained him with wreaths and declared him the center of the wheel. And so it was that like Reman before him, he became the centre of Cyrodiil. The keystone that kept the Empire in place. And for centuries this was the case.

But all great things have their dark twin. None know for certain how the Underking came to be, but it is said that he was a snake disguised as a man who disguised himself as a wizard, who came unto Tiber as a friend, a friend by the assumed name of Zurin Arctus.

But Tiber did betray Zurin before his conquest of Skyrim, and stole his heart in order to wreak a great conquest of Tamriel. He died a wealthy man, not realising what he had done. 

For Zurin was a great sorceror, and he had bound his heart to Tiber as well as himself. This meant that as long as Tiber was a god, Zurin too could not die, not until he found and devoured the Mantle-Heart he had given to Tiber.

And so he plotted and schemed for centuries, until he connived for the duchies and kingdoms of High Rock to do war with one another. One of the kings revealed the location of his heart, which the kings had been squabbling over because it was a powerful weapon and source of power.

And with his heart, Zurin could finally die. But his soul was twined to Tiber Septim, who was a god. And that meant that two became as one. And so, the NEW TALOS was born.

And then it was that the Elves truly knew their reckoning was at hand. Had the Thalmor or the Dominion ever shown their colours while an Emperor reigned? They had not. But once the New TALOS was born, they pointed at him and said: He Cannot Be. 

But why? Why did they declare TALOS an aberration, and not other gods become mortals, like Tiber or Arkay?

The secret is this: Many years ago, Shor broke himself in two. He became as two warriors roaming the land, and their children would take their mantle. Tiber and Zurin were these two warriors, and when they joined once more, they became Shor again. SHOR IS TALOS! TALOS IS SHOR! Rejoice for he has returned to us!
