<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'>t has become an unspoken doctrine among may Vigilants that the followers of the Reclamations, or the Anticipations as they came to be known in my home Morrowind's now-ancient history, are 'Good' Daedra or at the very least to be considered of minimal danger. Let me assure you that nothing could be further from the truth.

My fellow Dunmer often claim that these false gods were the inspiration for our Chimer ancestors in the distant Merethic, allowing us to find a foothold in a land cloaked in ash and populated with giant insects terrible to our minds. Let us imagine, for a moment, that this baseless conjecture is true.

That was then, this is now! Morrowind has flourished, foundered and fallen since then. I remember when the Tribunal lost their weapons. I remember when Nerevar returned to fight Dagoth Ur. I remember when Mephala's own son let the lie-rock fall from the sky and dash the Red Mountain wide open. These are the acts of Ald Resdayn's ancestor-gods! War and ruin!

Mephala, liar and trickster, prince-princess of plots and caller of smoke. Not a word from the lips of the Ur-Spider can be trusted, and yet the people of Morrowind have, for generations, permitted a whole slew of assassins devoted to her to go about their grim business will full legal sanction.

Azura, most rapturous queen of the ebbing sky and rising sun? Nay! Queen of naught but pleasantries and jealousy, drowning her lovers in sacred pools the moment she feels the imagination of their slights against her. 

Neither of these are aught, of course, next to the Black Star of the East, the three-pointed diamond, the logicker of false triangle-mythamatics, Boethiah the Queen of Betrayal. One hand offered in friendship, the other concealing the sword by which she takes every life she meets. She demands that her followers use any means possible to vanquish friend and foe alike.

Do not be fooled by the peaceful face the Reclamations offer to the world, that of the patrons of the Dunmer. Remember the slavery that the Dunmer imposed on those they disliked, the murders they abided, the destruction of their own lands they unwittingly invited. All Daedra want the same thing in the end.
