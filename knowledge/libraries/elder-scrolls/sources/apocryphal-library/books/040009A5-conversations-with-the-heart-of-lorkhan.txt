<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/H_letter.png'>ow I came to wear the panoply, I cannot recall; much of what was and will be has been lost in the red river of blood-awoken-dreaming, but the old skins and the new are as vivid in hue as the daubing of Diblada (who wrote in sound: may wisdom-weapon bathe in silk) on the fresh-fleansed skins of fire drums.

Those days, or those after or before, I was centred away from myself, but always strove in great endeavour to listen.

Said the Heart;
Hum-drum doom-drum doldrum red-rum. What's started by the scarab is finished by the whale.

Said I;
"It is time to don your armour. Ancestral whispers calling us to the fray."

Said the Heart;
What's started by the pig is finished by the fox. A crown made of metal is better than one made of leaves, but the one you've got is the one you'll wear.

Said I;
"Crowns are just circle-talk made crystal. I have no need of such philosophy."

But, when AKA bites his tail again, I am calm and need not think on such madness. Where I was at this time is for the logic-mongers to cavil over. But on such a time as he lets go, I asked;
"Why, O urge of aeons, is this the fate I bear again and again?"

Said the Heart, which was I;
The greatest of palaces need the strongest of arches need the keystone.

Said I;
"And what palace shall we build once we are done?"

Said the Heart;
One made in loving memory of White and Gold. It pains me more than the weeping of the skies to show you this. 

And when I was put together (which is to say, torn apart) again, It was Perrif who found me clutching the hole in my chest. And sundered thus I bellowed because my wisdom had been stolen by the Old-skin and in its place I knew only his ancient rage. And though I was in the armour of royal understanding it now bore the sigil of Diamond-like-Hate, which was the memory of when I had it, that which glistens beyond all else and beats and pulsates and shatters and hides among the ash and the winds and the fires. 

Said I; 
"Where are you, O fruit of the hills, wellspring of my blood-soaked boots? Over how many elf-banners must I tread to find you now?"

Said the Heart;
What's started by the man is finished by the Heart. White and Gold still stand, but as wrong-headed monument to a sky-ill-spoken.

Said I;
"I witness you not, where are you?"

Whispered the Heart;
Do you not remember this place? Do you not remember who I am?

Said Perrif, anon;
"Pelin-El, fair Pelin-El, why dost thou thunder so? You bellowed my name and that of Reman, whosoever that may be, and even Morihaus was stirred to tremble."

And the blood of elf-kings stained my lips and I knew I had found it. And I was so overjoyed with the knowledge that I devoured the earth. She who gave heart to man, who held in one hand the sword that said; I Am Broken-Tower and the Scythe Unshackled, and in her other hand the banner that said; I am the Pillar of the Sky and the Wind.

And I did proclaim;

"O Aka, for our shared madness I do this! I watch you watching me watching back! Umaril dares call us out, for that is how we made him!", for that was what the enemy called himself, and he was the son of a heart-stealer himself.

And Aka-who-was-Perrif rode on the back of Morihaus, son of the wind and the sky, and she knew and did not have to speak. The Heart beat the chord to the path to the tower, and only stopped when my head rolled across the floor at Umaril's blade.

And then it said;
Cast into eight, yet among those pieces there is no centre. Ask yourself now why the Elves love that number.

And I said;
"I am Diamond become Promise, and the massacre that becomes heaven's sacred peace, and the unspoken word of true enquiry."

Beloved Mor found me then, and rested his head by my side as I remembered the dream once again, and I wondered if the dreamer had woken long ago. So I asked;

"What of my dreams has remained once I have woken?"

And the Heart said;
What is finished by the word is started by the scarab. A waking-dream is too ignorant to die. Only wisdom can offer it a mercy-strike, and even sleep is broken in time by the clamour of the dawn and the glare of the sun.

And I said to Perrif later, as I placed upon her breast my four-angled heart;
"I have found myself among Aka and Kynarada, and left you to gather flesh with my twin, who will bestow the shining mortal-wisdom that brings them great joy, that is, freedom, which even the precincts of Aetherius shall not truly know, thus why our Father, the first and last of all world-skins in those first coruscations before Convention spoke that first and only truth, that which we echoed in our earthly madness. With them, I now bear you to that place. We will shed our true skins, which eat one another in amnesia each Age."
