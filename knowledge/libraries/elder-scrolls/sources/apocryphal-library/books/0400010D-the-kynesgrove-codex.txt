It is at third day of Sun's Dusk when the wronged maiden will paint her hair with troll fat and bathe her face in silver fox blood, and taking cut-head of her lover she will place it on the cairn in the Troll's lair.

For the vengeance and blood-price she seeks, she will utter the words to the gods.

To KYNE:

"Mother Kyne, I have been wronged and wish passage across god-law for the shape I may take, which I will wear forever."

To HIR-KYNE:

"Hunt-lord Hir-Kyne, I call forth the aspect of the hunter that I might wear it as mantle."

To ORKEY:

"Orkey, Father of Serpents, I beseech you for the killing-bite."

To SHOR:

"All-father Shor, I ask for time to pass me by til the blood-price be paid."

And having entreated the gods thus, she awaits the Troll, who she then must slay with a burning knife to the heart.

And then she must remove the skin of the Troll, and sleep inside it until a week has passed and it has become her own skin.

In this pattern she shall become the Udyrfrykte, as shall be any child she bears from troll-father.
