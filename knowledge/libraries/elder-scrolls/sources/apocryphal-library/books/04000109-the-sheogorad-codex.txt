The words here are written so that oral legend will not forget them, nor will they forget the march of Veloth's children up and down the slopes of the Throat of the World.

Veloth met with the three Jills there, whose names are Kyne and Mara and Dibella. Each made a promise to Veloth that they would deliver a child to the Chimer in the coming times.

And so when the time came to make good on their promise, Kyne descended on Resdayn and said,

"I am Kyne Storm-Queen, and as my gift to those Elves who did not betray the great Scarab, I bestow Almalexia onto the world, and she shall be foremost of all your warriors."

And so too did Mara make her perch on the towers of Resdayn, and she bellowed;

"I am Mara the Wolf-Mother, hunter by day and night, and I give to the land Sotha-Sil. He will be a source of wisdom that the Chimer can forever rely upon."

And so too did Dibella lower her scaled head, and she said;

"I am Dibella, sheathed in silk and mystery, and I give to the world Vivec, firey child of beauty. May this scion of divinity bring poetry to the four corners, and be the inspiration of all Veloth's chosen."

To protect the skies of Resdayn, Kyne made a swarm of creatures not quite hawk and not quite lizard, and these would be the Cliff Racers that thwarted the dragons of the land.

To protect the land itself, Mara made a swarm of creatures not quite insect and not quite wolf, and these would be the Nix-Hounds that served as loyal hunters for the Chimer.

And to protect the burrows under the land, Dibella made many things - she made the Shalk, the Brass Spider, the Kwama, and the many crabs and insects that came to call the land their own.
