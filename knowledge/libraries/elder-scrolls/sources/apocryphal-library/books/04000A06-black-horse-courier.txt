THALMOR DEFEATED IN HAMMERFELL
Second Treaty of Stros M'kai Signed</p>

For the last five years, the Dominion has continued its attempts to conquer Hammerfell. But today, with their armies exhausted, the Crowns and Forebears have forced the Thalmor to sign the Second Treaty of Stros M'kai, and their legions are withdrawing back to Alinor.

The news has been met with cheers in the Empire, who still lament the White-Gold Concordat. "Why couldn't we get the Redguard negotiators to deal with the Dominion five years ago, eh?" said Legate Volgar.

It's a good question - what did the Dominion want with Hammerfell anyway? Did the Redguards give anything away in the deal that we don't know about?

The Black Horse Courier continues to investigate.
