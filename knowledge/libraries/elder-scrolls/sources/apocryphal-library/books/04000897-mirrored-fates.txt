Before the Heart of Lorkhan, Nerevar repeated its fate. His heart cut out by a Thrice-Named traitor, he lay in death for aeons until the fates decided he would walk again. This has been documented, and the documents do not match. Compare the Nerevarine to the Shezarrine; do you have two consistent stories of either?

Atop the White-Gold tower, itself a mimic of the Zero Tower, Tiber cut out the heart of his ancient ally. Now Talos too is a god with three heads.

Trinimac basked in his glory until he was struck down by Boethiah. The Tribunal enjoyed the immortality of the gods until Azura sent her champion.

Whatever you think of Talos, perhaps his fate at the hands of the Thalmor will be deserved. 

But in their efforts, will they too try to take the one treasure in this world that can only be taken by trickery and theft? Or will they, like their predecessors, claim that they are the great warrior who took it by right?

It falls to us to observe and determine. Perhaps one cycle will come to end the rest, and we will forego our lust for the Heart of the World.
