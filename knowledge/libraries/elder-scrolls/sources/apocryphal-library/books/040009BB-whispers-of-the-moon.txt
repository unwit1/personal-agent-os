<p align="center">


Whispers of the Moon


Collected poetry of the Ashlander warrior-poet Zershishi Mus-Manul

</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
In the caverns of the ancestors
My mothers and fathers wait in silence
Their eyes as black as coal now
And they weep only ash.

When my last moon has risen
And my last sun has set
Here I will sit in my best bug-silk shroud
All else passed on to my children.

The true ancestors revere death
For that is how the world turns.
I turn my face from the god-walkers
Who fear the wheel and refuse to die.
[pagebreak]
The sky is my crown
And my spear is my sceptre.
The mountain is my throne
And the wind is my cloak.

No kings or emperors 
Have ever had such wealth as I.
Look to old Kogorun
To see what becomes of palaces.

[pagebreak]
In the night I hear the weeping
Of a far-perched cliff-racer
And the grumbling roars
Of the sleeping pale walkers.

But the silt-strider
Her mourning keens the truest
Her grave-song the deepest
A thousand cairns piled at her tears.

One dark day your lament
Will mark the last tears of all the Velothi.
