Martial Techniques: On Fighting Dremora
Volume II

by Haspian Witheric
</p>
<p align="left">

Daedra as a whole do not tire easily. Relying on a trade of blows in the expectation that they will run out of breath is usually a fool's endeavour, but weapons enchanted to sap or steal a rival's stamina may do something to help this end.

When fighting with a single weapon and a shield, expect the fight to be long. You are beating a wall down with a lump-hammer instead of a sledgehammer, but you may endure a few more attacks.

However, this author believes that a two-handed weapon is a more reliable tool against a Dremora. Though there will be less defence, the time one saves by killing their opponent faster may save ones life.

Dremora often go to war with their heads unarmed, leaving a valuable avenue of attack wide open. A blow from a warhammer or battle-axe can cause severe damage to a Dremora and stagger them before they have a chance to retaliate.

Against a Dremora with a shield, be mindful that they are well versed in the use of the shield, though their discipline leaves them open to attacks from a creative opponent. Aiming a blow to their head with a greatsword but bringing it down in an arc to damage their legs or feet can open up an excellent opportunity for a follow-up strike.

Fighting Dremora is a very difficult task, and should not be attempted by those not versed in the arts of war or fighting Daedra. But should the wards against Oblivion fail once more, then avoiding combat may no longer be a possibility, and these instructions may keep you alive to salute another dawn.
