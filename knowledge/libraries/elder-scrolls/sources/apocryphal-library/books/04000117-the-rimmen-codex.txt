When the World Egg was split into twelve, those twelve pieces were not so different from each other in any way that could be quantified. Things being what they were, movement from one to the other was trivial.

The notion of time but them into a circle, so that each world-piece was bordered by two. And from that moment, one could only travel back or forth, never across. Time had given each piece a number between one and twelve. They were ordered in a way that was against their nature.

To those of us in the mundic realm, we are dimly aware of two other worlds that border our own. We perceive Yokuda and we perceive Akavir, two worlds that have more in common with each other than they do with our own. To them, the serpent is an inevitability. To us, it is a demon to be fought.

The persistence of the thirteenth piece of twelve has upset the old chronologians, because it implies that the law of twelve was based on the notion that this world is merely numbered twelve, therefore that must be the total number of worlds that exist.
