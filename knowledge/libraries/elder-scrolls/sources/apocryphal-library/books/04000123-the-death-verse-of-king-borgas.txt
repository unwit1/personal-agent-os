And his shield-thanes and torc-wives
Brought flame-fear to the fallow
And with Kyne-cunning and killing cleaver
Wild-Hunt harrowed, hail!
Shield-Nords shouting, Shor! Shor!
The venom-vines, vicious, parted
And there Borgas, bruised, bloody
Lay dying, death-door dreaming.
And there had fallen a Good King.

With all his armour adorning
Back to the bleak and barren
To the Pale, pierce-cold palace
Til cold-cavern'd Korvanjund
Where now lies a Good King.

And to Brave Borgas, cheer!
Sing-strong, ere Sovngarde
Where fallen and fell
Hail high Hoar-Father!

And his shield-thanes and torc-wives
Brought bale from the barrow
For so passed King Borgas
The last-line of Ysgramor.
