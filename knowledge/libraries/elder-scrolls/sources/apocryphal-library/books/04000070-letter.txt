Old friend,

It seems that our mutual acquaintance has made great process with his contraption. I cannot say with certainty how he constructed it, but I have seen within his laboratory all the trappings of terrible dwemerology, replete with cutting-spheres and anomalous cubes which fold from a lesser size to reveal a greater, against all the sacred geometric proofs of Julianic mathematics. But most unholy of all was the circlet that he has constructed with guidance from most terrible ancient tomes of Numidian logics. This, he claims, acts as an anchor between the world of the Aurbis and those of the roiling masses of Oblivion. No sigils of Daedric nature attired its eerie brass workings, nor anything to indicate a connection to those devilish outer realms, in fact the sheer banal mundanity of the device sent through me a terrible convulsive shiver. I asked him what intention he had for the device, and he merely laughed and told me that all would become clear with the next alignment of the eclipse.

I cannot tell you how unwholesome that ordinary looking brass device made me, or how it felt I was almost looking through it, opaque as it may have been. If things do not improve I may be forced to take matters into my own hands.

I pray for your safety and mine.

- O.D
