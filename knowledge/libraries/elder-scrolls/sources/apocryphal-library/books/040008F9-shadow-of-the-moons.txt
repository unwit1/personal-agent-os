<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'>mong such gardens as I saw - I would walk again today, would that any in The Arena match their beauty. Amid a cloud of fragrant manablooms did I see a swirling kestrel cry, and to hearken to its joyful song I saw a path to evergreens.

Forgotten by the waking world, I saw upon the aching cliffs and saw, across a sea of myths, the weeping shadow of a bird. I walked across the tales of white and green and sang a poem, my way across.

Against that far forgotten cliff I saw the palace, Palace White, wherein she sits, the Queen of Rose, the Lady of Forgotten Dawn.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'>zura looked down on me and said,
<font face='$DaedricFont'>
WHO IS THIS WHO COMES TO ME
WHOSE SCALES ARE LIKE THE DRUM OF TIME
</font>
I said, my scales are not so dark, nor does my heart beat such a tune, a tune to make the years go by, a beat to bring the ages down.
<font face='$DaedricFont'>
BORN OF THE ROOT YOU COME TO ME
COUSIN SHAPE YOU ARE IN WORD
COME TO SEEK THE WEALTH DIVINE
SUNDERED CHILD OF PADHOMAY
</font>
I asked for naught, Azura, queen of Dawn and Dusk and nil-between.
I came to see, and cannot leave, for when I go I will not be.

She smiled and showed me then a door, which took me on a mountain path
Which took me to a place I'd been, I'd been in many sainted dreams
And as I looked upon the sky, I reached up and I took a star.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'>zura's Star is white and gold and other colours in between, but all the tales of shades and tones could not describe the light within. 

It took a lot to draw away the gaze that I had set upon the fate-wrought star that sat within the very palm of my left hand.
Within that star have even sat the souls and tales of many fiends and men and mer (when time allowed) and even poets who sat in towers.

Just when I had decided that I would remain, for evermore, I was alerted by a pair of silver-purple heckling birds.
Their wings were night, their eyes were day, their feathers like the morning sun, their talons like the fading light.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/W_letter.png'>inged Twilights had come to me, and so it was to them I asked,

What business do you have with me? I came to look upon a star.
<font face='$DaedricFont'>YOUR BUSINESS HERE IS DONE </font> said they,
<font face='$DaedricFont'>TO WAKE AND LIVE IS YOUR DUTY</font>

And with that I, and they, did fly into the sky where dragon-flies
Danced with memories and flowers that took the clouds in their brood-hearth.

And I awoke, in my own nest, and I had dreamed the whole affair, for man nor mer nor beast nor orc can visit Moonshadow in life.
But when I opened up my palm, the star was there! In pale-moon glare!
