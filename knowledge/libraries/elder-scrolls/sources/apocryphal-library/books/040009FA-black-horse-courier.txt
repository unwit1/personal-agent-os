PRINCE ATTREBUS BORN</p>

Word from the Imperial Palace this morning was that last night, Prince Attrebus Mede was born to Emperor Titus Mede and Empress-Consort Lavinia Mede.

"This is a joyous occasion for the Empire", announced His Imperial Majesty Titus Mede to our correspondent. "Tamriel has a new heir, and he will be wise and brave and strong, a true ruler the likes of which the Empire deserves."

Following the news, the Empress-Consort declared that this coming Morndas should be held as a day of celebration across the Empire.
