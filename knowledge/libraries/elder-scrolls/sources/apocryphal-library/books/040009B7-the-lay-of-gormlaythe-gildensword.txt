<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/C_letter.png'>ome hearken to me, as I sing this old tale
Of the boldest shield-maiden, Gormlaythe of the Pale
Whose banner was smeared with the blood of the drake
Who died of her deeds for all fair Skyrim's sake.

Ho, there! Gormlaythe the Bold,
Victor of many great battles of old,
Ho there, Gormlaythe Fell-Starred,
May you rest forever in high Sauvenguard.

When Aldoeyn the Black, Dragon-King of the dead,
Raised a great army with he at it's head,
The good folk of Skyrim were rightfully cowed
Even bold Nords found no heads were unbowed.

Ho, now, Gormlaythe the Young,
Just seventeen with no songs to be sung,
Ho now, Gormlaythe the Proud
Would see Skyrim free, this she promised, she vowed.

For many a year did she fight for her lot,
But mortals are small and can breathe fire not,
So she wandered through tundra and forest of pine,
'Til she came to the long-hidden Altar of Kyne.

Ho, there, Gormlaythe the Brave,
Did call to her goddess for Skyrim to save,
Ho there, Gormlaythe the Tongue!
"Go forth my child, let your story be sung!"

With the voice of a dragon did Gormlaythe now hail!
As did One-eyed Halcyon and Felldyr the Frail,
Three heroes of yore, three legends in life,
Joining as one, so to end Skyrim's strife.

Ho, now, Gormlaythe the Fierce!
Her voice slew those dragons her sword could not pierce,
Ho now, Gormlaythe the Dour,
Her last dying moments were her finest hour.

At the peak of Kyne's mountain, the Throat of the World,
The dread-drake King Aldoueyn sat, wings unfurled,
Gormlaythe, with her voice and her sword sheathed in gold,
Slew four of his kin before reaching his hold.

Heark now, Aldoueyn the Cruel!
Eater of Worlds and the Father of Ghouls!
Heark now, Aldoueyn the Dread,
Eater of Dreams and of tears unshed.

Though she knew that her life was now surely to end,
She charged the black drake, with her hope for to spend,
And the tyrant did bite her, and that was her fate;
But her bravery masked what had hidden in wait.

Strike, now, Halcyon the Fleet!
Strike at his underside while he sings heat!
Hail now, Felldyr the Wise!
Sing Foul Aldoueyn from the waking world's eyes!

And Nords from all quarters did come to her pyre,
To bid their farewells as she rose with the fire,
And the smoked was breathed in by the Queen of the Sky
And she breathed it back out to the Halls upon High.

Rest, now, Gormlaythe the Scarred,
Drink ye your fill up in high Sauvenguard,
Rest, now, Gormlaythe Loud-Roar,
Rest until Skyrim needs your voice once more!
