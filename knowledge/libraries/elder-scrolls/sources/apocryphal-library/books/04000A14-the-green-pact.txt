<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>hese are the words spun by Y'ffre of the Green, by whose naming all things of nature are hereby ordered and known.

Y'ffre wove this world from her own form, in doing so she told a story of a world without end. So long as the Green does not come to injury, she may continue to spin this tale so that all may come to know the final understanding.

Y'ffre wove this law from his own beard, which is all the moss and the fronds of the ever-growing Valenwood. He made this law so that the true protectors of this forest may shoulder the burden of the final understanding.

Those Bosmer who make pact with their wisdom to join this Treat of Frond and Leaf do so under the strictures of the following clauses.

Thou shalt not bring harm to the forests of the Valenwood. No trees shall be shorn or uprooted, the soul among the shade of the trees shall not be tilled for pasture, the root-bores of the great oaks shall not be plundered.

Thou shalt not feast upon the living plant-flesh of the wood, for this is the flesh of Y'ffre. Thou mayest only eat those fruit that fall from the tree by the sole whim of the Green, or those plants that have already perished or been split from their root by causes natural.

Thou shalt feast instead only on things of meat-flesh and egg, and shalt nourish thyself to great fullnes on the carcasses of all manner of fish, animals and insect, for these are the gifts of Y'ffre to mortal mer.

Thou shalt not allow the flesh of the departed to rot and wither on the soil, but instead shall, once due time of mourning has passed, devour them and so pass them   back into the great tales spun by Y'ffre. This shall go for thy beloved, thine enemy, and all those beasts hunted and slain for meat and for hide.

Thou shalt not kill game without intent to devour and to use its flesh, for to do so is wasteful and a slight upon the providence of Y'ffre, whose gifts are awarded from the Green in great kindness and should never be squandered.

Thou shalt not take upon themself the graven form of a beast outside of the most sacred ritual of the Wild Hunt. The shapes of animals are divine and sacred unto Y'ffre, and to become unto their likeness outside of those most ancient practices is an affront to The Green.
