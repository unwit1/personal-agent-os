LUCIDITY

by The Reconstituted Man

[pagebreak]

<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> dream is anything but exact. Nothing is exact of course, because this too is a dream, and the world we dream is dreaming us back.

At death we succumb to Aetherius, the dreaming-sleeve of everything, everything therefore nothing. One cannot have too many hands in motion when one is born with only two. 

Exactitudes come in Multitudes. Wake up and the world is as it should be - where dreams are left in their aeon outside aeons. 

Only the gods may be so precise. They offer us gifts of this precision through the stars, weeping tears into their own forgetfulness, as a million kindnesses that we turn into things of colour and imagination, splitting certainty into possibility with the keen-edged tool that is the waking mind.

As tears of varliance fall through the firmament, wonder too what falls back the other way. You already know. You've done it a thousand times before, and you cannot cease to do so.

The gods beg us to dream, because we can. They know only certainties where we know only chance. A chance can be taken, and an exactitude is defined by unmovement.

When you sleep, remember the difference between sound and song.

When you wake, remember the difference between sheet and music.
