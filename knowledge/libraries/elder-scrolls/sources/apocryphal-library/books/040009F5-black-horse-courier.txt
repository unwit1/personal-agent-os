POTENTATE OCATO MURDERED! </p>

Ocato of Firsthold, former battlemage to Uriel Septim VII and Potentate of the Empire, was found dead in the Elven Gardens district of the Imperial City this morning. He had been stabbed in the back with a poisoned dagger.

The whole city has been shocked by the terrible news, and the Elder Council are in flux as there is no obvious successor to Ocato.

Hailing from Summurset Isle, Ocato proved himself a loyal member of the Elder Council in the days leading up to the Imperial Simulacrum. After the event concluded, Emperor Uriel Septim VII appointed him as Battlemage, due to his trustworthy nature, keen intellect and resolute determination.

The Imperial Guard are still on the lookout for a suspect. Currently the Dark Brotherhood are suspected as the most likely perpetrators of this attack, though they have left none of their usual tell-tale signs, according to watch commander Servatius Quinitilius. 

We pray to the Divines that his killers are brought swiftly to justice, and that peace is restored soon.
