Reachfolk in Combat

by Vigilant Carthia

</p>


[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Forsworn are a present and ever-growing problem for the good citizens of Tamriel. They venerate the heathen Daedra and mount attacks on civilised folk in order to take souls to sacrifice to the likes of Vaermina, Hircine and Peryite.

When travelling in the Reach on either side of the Druadach range, be wary. They hide in the hills and make good use of vantage points, and the roads are usually clear enough that a traveller can be seen from a good distance. Attacking from an elevated position gives them a clear advantage in melee and archery, and they will generally also attack en masse.

Good armour is always sensible, especially against the reachfolk. Their weapons are deadly sharp, but often made from fairly crude material such as thorns, sharpened stones or bone. Against light armour or cloth, these can make short work - but with good iron or steel, the spines of these weapons are likely to blunt faster.

However, the speed with which they attack can be disorientating for a warrior unskilled in the use of plate armour. It's common for them to wield a weapon in either hand, and perform rapid spinning attacks that are hard to dodge. A good shield may help the defender, but may also block their line of sight against additional assailants.

A rapid retreat to a bottleneck point is a good strategy, where their numbers or wide spinning attacks will count for much less. But one must still be wary of their mages, especially if their Hagraven masters have joined the fray. Hagravens will often use fireballs, which are deadly against a warrior trying to defend a narrow gap.

Fortunately, the light armour that affords the Forsworn and the Witchmen their mobility also makes them more vulnerable to deadly attacks of your own. A good shot with a bow or a powerful swing can fell a child of the Reach in one, if you are true of arm. Be vigilant always in the Reach, and keep one eye to the hills always, for they are often watching back.
