EMPEROR UNMASKED AS JAGAR THARN!</p>

We bring perhaps the most astounding news of the last five years - and what a five years it has been. The right and true Emperor, Uriel Septim VII, had been replaced by Jagar Tharn using illusion magic.

Though the details are still coming in, it seems that Battlemage Tharn betrayed Uriel and imprisoned him in an astral pocket of sorts, such that would prevent him dying (and thus alerting the Elder Council via the Amulet of Kings). 

With the Emperor and the Amulet no longer within the safety of Mundus, it is no wonder that so many wars had broken out over the last five years. However, it has now been revealed that the Emperor was replaced a whole five years before the Elsweyr-Valenwood war broke out.

Tharn used an item called the 'Staff of Chaos' to affect this plan, and was able to break the staff apart and conceal it in some of the most inaccessible reaches of Tamriel, including the Adamantine Tower and the Red Mountain of Morrowind. How he affected this trickery is as yet unknown, but reports indicate that a single unknown hero was able to unite all eight pieces.

To this 'Eternal Champion', we give you our everlasting gratitude.
