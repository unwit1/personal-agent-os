ARTAEUM DISAPPEARS</p>

Word is slow to come out of Summerset Isle these days, but one interesting tidbit has come up. This one's of special interest to the magickally inclined!

The Isle of Artaeum, home to the mystic Psijiic Order, has disappeared for the second time in known history. It has always been a difficult place to keep track of, never remaining at the spot where the sea charts place it for long, but this is only the second time it has been reported as completely gone.

The last time was in the Second Era, at a time roughly coinciding with the formation of the Mages' Guild. It was gone for some five hundred years before returning. When it did return, none of the Elves present - most of whom were the same as the ones who had vanished centuries prior - would give any information as to where it had gone, nor the fate of its Archmage, Iachesis.

Nor indeed, why it had gone in the first place. Perhaps with the foundation of the Mages' Guild, they no longer believed that their presence was necessary to moderate magickal learning. But why now? With the moons still gone and the Empire on the brink of collapse, more than ever the wisdom of the Artaeum monks would be needed.
