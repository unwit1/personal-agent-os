Aad semblio aurbex, so ecta daba-laserti aad semblio ehlnofera in aedifex. Ae dracofex ehlnonymic Aka, Akhat, Arkay, Rkhet, Orkey, Lorkhan.

Nymici aad Magne-ge ex varlia sune, sur tenebhru gulme Merid-Nunda emhaldi. Varlikhan rhans ohsmo kurhat, saed nouem falvue anagulme Magne kurhat. 

Aad semblio varlia, dela can Ehlnotella semblex. Ahksi-en-ecta hae sur-delebanthe ce mure-bjah.

En ecta aad Ahksi, Ehlnokhan ce nymic vheri ae semblex Mantella. 

Sul-rah, Alta-Mora uhte draco ae, aad soume ehlno ehstante.

Sur-rah, Aldmerex uhte ehlno ae, aad soume draco ehstante.

Pa-rah ae Sel-rah ae Iocu-Tah, ouhs ehlnotella dae reminit.

Nymic ce dhama pohlae-odhae ae [HERMINIT], indra Magna-ge ae ell aad semblio Lkhan in Mundex.

Thode futurahhe inplende odhae elli voleistex. Kherem ce Aka-vir bahneth li diversa futu-rah ce vyrgghe tecaton, eht aad pellegris kheter ghulam scaeo ce futu-rah.

Saed Herma-Mora in Apocra ancaelit ehlle. Aad scaeo prehnde, trophe librex bhorthaju mundis-varliex eht ahbsorghe.

Aad Tella, ce Mantella, ce Ehlnotella.

Aad Semblio, ce Sembex-Magicka, ce illux.

Aad Lahdrox, ce librex kharus.

Aad Almabella, ce saghe Bo-Et-Hra, ce anghille-laskhe Sax-ille.

Eht aad semblio khetere ce aldhyr fyrmamente. Ehl saghe odhae apyrte! Ehl nymic [HERMINIT]!
