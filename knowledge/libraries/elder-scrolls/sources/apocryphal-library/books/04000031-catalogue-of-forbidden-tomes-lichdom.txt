Volume Two: Attaining Lichdom

<p align="left">

Ascendancy: Pathway to Lichdom
This was written by the Nordic lich Gullveig the Ascendant in the second era. It outlines in a rather limited fashion some of the steps required to achieve lichdom, although in the author's arrogance she appears to have neglected to record some of the important steps. Highly dangerous, destroy any copies found.

Achieving Harmony with Death
by Ymirra, master of bone and scythe. An esoteric text that lays out some of the philosophical pretexts of necromancy among the Khajiit. Considered highly dangerous.

The Path of Transcendence
The journal of the lich Celedaen, it has been reproduced several times among necromantic covens. Lists some of the dangers and requirements of the process of lichdom but avoids several specifics deliberately. Still considered highly dangerous.

Whispers of the Veil
A tome believed to contain Mannimarco's personal secrets on how to attain lichdom. No copy has ever been found, and if any still exist it may do so as a single copy. Highest possible priority.
