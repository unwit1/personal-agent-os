Funerary Customs of Elsweyr

by Thomas Holvin 

</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he burial rites of the Khajiit are considered the least important part of the process of life and death. The tales of the Khajiiti afterlife are rich and mysterious, featuring a land beyond the stars - L'esweyr, the 'true' Elsweyr, a land akin to Aetherius for other nations.

Khajiit who live their lives ambitiously and according to the trails of the moons Jone and Jode may be taken by the goddess Khenarthi to this land, while those who divert from their path may become corrupt spirits, 'bent cats', who might become terrible spirits called dro-m'Athra, who serve Namira. Thus there is a spiritual practice of ensuring that those who become 'Bent Cats', should they die, are sent to the dark beyond the moons where Lorkhaj will remake them.

The preoccupation with dro-m'Athra means that things like the undead are a much lesser concern. Burials in Elsweyr are simple cairns at the very least, or proper coffins in family graves more generally. Moon priests tend to these graves to ensure the correct passing of the souls, though the rites of Arkay are not performed. This means that necromancy is rather more common in Elsweyr than other parts of Tamriel, to the point where in the slums of Black Kiergo in Senchal, it is possible to buy any part of a dead body, and those are the least remarkable things that one can buy there.

The planting of waning lilies in the ponds and pools of holy sites in remembrance of the dead is practiced across Elsweyr. These plants are relatives of the sacred lotus, and ceremonial pools filled with these blossoms can create heady aromas in spring and summer.
