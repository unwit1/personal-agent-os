This is where the dream is different, Mystic.

It is hard to keep a watch over the two as they chase one another. I remove one of my eyes and throw it among the mirrors. I have to understand so that the story makes sense. My eye follows them always. Sometimes we three are foes, sometimes we are allies. Sometimes the three of us become a small god, and we create our own shadow by mistake. The layers of snakeskin are thick throughout the world now, and the pieces of the mirror are now so small I can barely see them.
