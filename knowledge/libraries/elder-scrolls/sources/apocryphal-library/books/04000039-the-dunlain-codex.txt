Through the manifold lenses and prisms does MERID-NUNDA thus reveal the prime colours of the forces and all refractions and reflections thereof. Thus do the outer spheres reflect towards the inner, and the undivided light once blended in the ineffability of purpose and purity.

AKHAT is the circle of time and the closed loop of new becomings. He arrives in the form of a great wyrm plated in gold. His only movement is circular provided that the hub is in place. His velocity is unknown to vary because all things are counted by his rotation.

MARA is the foundation from which all things flow from the greater. The divine multiplier and both the source and the essence of multiplication. She is the division that makes two wholes.

LORKHAN or SHEZZAR is the plane on which the circle forms. He is the substance from which the thing is made and that ground on which the act is performed. He is the hub around which AKHAT turns, and his centre is so small as to be absolute.

TRINIMAC is the ideal form of things to be, and the absolute perfection of shape. His form can only be unreasoned by itself, which is the triangular gate and the mirror turned away from itself.

PHYNASTER is the act of motion and the motion of acting. He is the rhythm upon which time exists, and the notion of velocity by which AKHAT is measured.

JHUNAL or JULIANOS or JURNALONUS is the first and last of all things to happen, and the memory of all the things to happen in between. He is the counting and the record.

BOETHIAH is the counter-action to any event, the mirror of possibility, the wheel viewed from the other side so that it appears to be turning withershins. She is the mirror to everything and anything.

SITHISIT is the absence of anything and the slow dissolution of any force that acts upon it. It is the gradual cessation of all things, and the summation of the entropic forces that feed into it.

KYNARET or BLUE MARA or KAAN or WHITE BOETHIAH is the greater scion of all life-bearing miasmas and things that bear life into all other things. She is the centre born anew and the movement of things unaligned from their anchor.

JEPHRE or GREEN MARA or WHITE JHUNAL is the shepherd of things of life into literature coalescent. He is movement of knowledge and cannot exist in present or future.

MERUNADAGON or RED PHYNASTER is the movement without impediment or direction, which are the same thing. He is the outward movement of all forces that a thing has, and the gradual ruination of things built.

ANUMIDUM or NUMIDIUM or BRASS TRINIMAC or GOLD LORKHAN or BRONZE SITHISIT or YELLOW MERUNADAGON is the thing after the final thing that only exists when all other things have stopped. It is the motionless step from the nadir to the zenith that crosses the mirror inaccessible.

HERMAEUS MORA or MORWHA or GREY MARA or MORIHAUS or MORA OBSCURA or BLACK JHUNAL is the guardian of the ink-sea of non-memory that becomes as written-logic through the same lens of being that makes the sands and the sky.

ARKAY or BLACK AKHAT or WHITE LORKHAN or RED TRINIMAC is the constant of the circle once it is attached to itself.

ORKEY or BLACK ARKAY or GREEN AKHAT or MALAKHAT or BLACK TRINIMAC is the sacred and necessary remainder that is left in the with all turns of the gyre.

TSENDAR or STENDARR or ZEN or SRENDR or ZENITHAR or TSUN or GREEN TRINIMAC is the paired force made indivisible by the osmotic third force.

ALMSIVI or AYEMSEHTVEHK or RED TRINIMAC is the seed taken from a distant plant and grown in a new land, and the circle incomplete around the triangle resplendent.

MEPHALA or RED BOETHIAH or GREY JHUNAL is the counter-feint of divine reason and the thing that is written down in order to become its own opposite.

AKULAKHAN or YELLOW AKHAT or YELLOW LORKHAN or YELLOW ANUMIDUM is the memory of forgetfulness and the remembrance of a promise made to itself.

DIBELLA or RED MARA is the force that acts upon itself until it can act upon another. She is the force also that blinds other forces to the existence of each other.

MOLAGABALA or BLUE DAGON or BLACK MEPHALA or RED ANUMIDUM is the force that absorbs all other forces.

MAGNUS or WHITE JHUNAL or GOLD AKHAT is the creator of the archive impenetrable and the conduit of the varliance membrane.

NOCTRA or NOCTURNAL or GREY SITHISIT is the only presence in the empty space and all forces that can act upon it.

SHEOGORATH or BLACK LORKHAN is the removal of all forces on any object in no particular order. It is the order that breaks down until all that exists is one single force, and the collapse of that force under itself.

JYGGALAG or WHITE SHEOGORATH is the order that exists for a moment when all other forces have ceased to act.

SYRABANE or BLUE JHUNAL or SILVER MARA is the birth of shapes and patterns and their inhabitation in places newly created.

NAMIRA or RED NOCTRA is the empty space in any shape that expands with every new vertex added.

VAERMINA or PURPLE JHUNAL is the transmutation of the inside of the wheel to the outer.

UNALA-SE or YELLOW MERID or YELLOW BOETHIAH or SALLOW REGENT or YELLOW MAGNUS is the misconception born of correct observations and a mirror to other mirrors, or the wandering light whose reflection is stasis.

HIRCINE or HIR-KYNE or RED KYNARET is the exact distance between one moving force and another force moving on the same vector, as well as the reduction in that distance.

MNEMO-LI or MEMORY or BLUE MAGNUS or BLUE ALMSIVI is the third integer in the formless inequation when AKHAT does not equal LORKHAN.

XARXES or XARKET or YELLOW JHUNAL or BLUE ARKAY or TUACCA is the records and the writing on the other side.

REMAN or RUDDY-MAN or RED LORKHAN is the first solution to the inequation when AKHAT does not equal LORKHAN.

TALOS or TIBERSEPTIM or WULFARCTUS or RED REMAN or GREEN LORKHAN is the second solution to the inequation when AKHAT does not equal LORKHAN.

PERYITE or RED AKHAT or GREEN MOLAGABALA or YELLOW PHYNASTER or BLACK ANUMIDUM is the basal wheel which rotates in order for other things to have motion.

CLAVICUS VILE or UMBRA or YELLOW MOLAGABALA or GOLD SHEOGORATH is the window that resembles a mirror, and the counter-force that resembles the reflection.

SANGUINE or BLACK DIBELLA or RED SHEOGORATH is the reduction of all unstable forms to an inert layer, easily acted upon by the outside force.

EBONARM or GREY REMAN is the wave released by the opposition of any two forces of comparable strength.

ADABAL or DABAL-A or PELINALA or PELINALESTIA or ALESSIA or PARAVANT or BLUE MALAKAT or RED DIBELLA or GOLD MERUNADAGON or RED ALMSIVI or BLACK NUMIDIUM is the summation of want, and the soothing respite of inconceivable star-violence as told through winsome prophecies, and the tears shed for want of a lost centre.
