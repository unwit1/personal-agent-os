The Spear-Talker is one of the Children of Sithis, and a cousin to the Hist. Some tribes share his tale, others do not. In this tribe, we always have at least one who can read the prey-trails in the root-dark.

His worship is common across all the lands, be they marsh, mountain or meadow. But the best hunters favour the woodland, for that affords the most prey, the most silence, the most shadow. Thus are the greatest hunters, most favoured of Spear-Talker, found in Elden Wood, High Forest and of course the Black Marsh.

They speak of a tall elf or man with antlers. We see him for his truth. All black, and scaled, and clawed. 

If you have been chosen by Spear-Talker, you are blessed indeed. Hunt for the Saxhleel, and bring great flesh-prize back to your tribe-stead.
