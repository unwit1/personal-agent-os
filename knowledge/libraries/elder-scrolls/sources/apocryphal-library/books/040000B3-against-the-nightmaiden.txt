On the dangers posed by Nocturnal

by Keeper Rathoran

</p>
<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/C_letter.png'>onsider the simple thief, going about her illegitimate business under cloud-shrouded moonlight. A bane of the honest mer, for certain - but her dark purpose does not end with her greedy plunder of the citizenry's pockets.

Thieves of all stripes under Tamriel's sky worship a terrible and dark mother. Nocturnal, one of the most ancient beings in existence. Just as the dawn breaks after a long winter's night, so too does Nocturnal exist in the indeterminate aeons that spanned the gulfs of misremembering long before the Dawn Era. She is the very soul of the void, a terror from beyond knowing and reason. 

And why does this simple thief venerate her thus? It is simple; she is darkness, and she brings darkness. Her concealment of the moons makes the dark business of the thief a little easier, with that rogue remaining none the wiser as to her foul mistress' intent.

And what is that intent? Nothing but the total subsumption of existence into darkness eternal. Imagine a world of blindness, where all that live stumble in a never-ending void damped of all light. That is the final blessing of Noctra.

Beggars, bandits, thieves and rogues alike, know this: The dark queen you worship is the end of all things, and your obesiance makes you an enemy of the goodly folk of Tamriel. So know this also: You are the enemy of the Vigil of Stendarr. 

Vigilants, be ready! Thieves are careful and well-practiced opponents. They do not fight in the light of day, and will choose the battleground on which they will be most victorious. The cleverest will avoid combat altogether, but all have one easily exploited weakness; the love of wealth.

O Nocturnal, most ancient of all Daedra, know this. Your worshippers will fall to us through their own greed and their own folly. The light of day will always return to vanquish the once-eternal darkness.
