AE AYLEIDOON ALTADOON GUME GRAVANTIA, Hidden is the weapon against the enemy.

Mercy is this; it is anger turned into a knife against itself. This is remembered as the forbidden sword-stance of the greater kalpics, whereby the sword is struck with itself. This is only used when one must disarm oneself mid-strike.

Mercy, by its own accord, does not demand the same sword-knowledge as the forbidden stance, else acts thereof could only be performed by gods. The sword technique in fact follows on from Mercy, which is the act of turning hate as a dagger against hate.

One who does not hate cannot commit Mercy. Only the lies of biters proclaim otherwise.

Mercy can only be granted by the victor. It is the remit of the warrior who has already won.

From this truth, know the stations of the blade:

One; it is in Padomaic Horizon, and the vanquished is kneeling.

Two; it is in Scarab's Cadence, and the vanquished is prone.

Three; it is in Boethiah's Grip, and the vanquished is spared.

The vanquished may relinquish their hands and feet if they are slow to surrender, and they may come to see this as a second mercy.
