WAR IN THE NORTH-WEST</p>

The Nords of Skyrim have launched an all-out war against neighbouring regions of Hammerfell and High Rock, and the cities of Jehanna, Elinhir and Dragonstar. These territories, the Nords have claimed at length, are the true property of Skyrim. Indeed, the Nords have often claimed the entire Reach as their dominion, to the upset of the local Reachfolk.

With yet another war in the provinces, the Emperor has once again shown little inclination to act directly. What is happening in the Imperial Palace? Imperial Battlemage, Jagar Tharn, was not available for comment but assured the Elder Council last night that his best agent is looking into it.
