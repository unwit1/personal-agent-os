Halsion the Collector, unkissed by wind
Stone-nest hewn by Northern iron
His last bronze chalice borne in silk
Meets the Widow's breath at last
And grief-marches unto a Daedra'd East
Among ash-eaten foremothers to dwell
To conclave with the prophet-driven kin

West-snow grave-now
High-cave wide-shadow!
Snake-bird from North Ice
Circle-stitch doom wheel.

In fire-spit of East-star
Sun-child bled to Prophet
For steel from the Three-Name
War-cross the Doom Drum.

Veloth the Restless, burnt by the wind
Closed eyes from the Ice-West
DAE TRINYMIC ALTADOON GUME BOET-HI-AH
LKHAN CE MALAKHAT 

Halsion the Collector, broken by the East
Awaited the North-steel 
Where fast-grew the Jazbay
And last-set the long-day.
