Poems of Aldmeris


Edited by Varigilamo of Firsthold


</p>
[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
The following poems have been collated from across all the Elven nations. They all represent the memory of distant Aldmeris, through the many varying forms of flower-script that form the birthright of Mer.

May these poems bring you the joy of the half-memory of Old Forgotten Ehlnofey, whose gilded arches and monuments inspire our dreams for eternity.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'>fter I had walked the circuit
For the third, fifth, and seventh times
I laid a crystal flower down
For every star that passed me by.

And when I walked the circuit
For the ninth and final time
I wished those flowers into heaven
To fall as tomorrow's rain.

- The Sequence Immutable
Altmer traditional, author unknown

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'> will not lie; there is pain here.
We do not go to a place of marble towers.
We do not march to a valley of crystal springs.
There are no tears of joy,
Only tears shed in ash and sorrow.

Our brothers still mourn for you,
We must march on from you.
The memory will bring no kindness
Only promises and regrets
Of a land half-spoken.

- Farewell to Aldmeris, By Veloth the Prophet

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'> lay to rest in Skingrad Town
Expecting there to wake
But when I woke I looked upon
A shining crystal lake.
And when I drank its waters pure
I felt it call to me;
A hundred thousand years or more
Of timelost memory.
I walked among the glorious hills 
Among the gleaming towers
And when I woke, in truth this time,
I could still smell the flowers.

- The Dream, By Nalynwe
