Being the recollection of the Dawn Era

Book One

Translated from the Aldmeris by Pallancio of Dusk

<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'>t is the recollection of the sun-blessed and those who came before that I now record with the right and true reverence that one should always have for ones ancestors. These are the recollections of a memory beyond memory, of the time when time came to be, when light and darkness came to meet and what it was that was birthed between them.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Brothers of Light and Darkness

How the Void had come to be there, or not-there, Anu and Padomay could only conjecture. Indeed, they could scarcely ascertain exactly how they had come across it, or indeed exactly from whence they had made the journey, though they both agreed that they had been elsewhere when they had been born.

For Anu and Padomay were brothers, and they knew each other well - but they had entered into a great dark traverse, unlit save by the glow of Anu's flaming torch, reflected by Padomay's sword - which was, it seemed, the only thing for the light to touch. Both laughed in amazement as they realised that the light from the sword was now as bright as the torch itself - and in those times before time, it was impossible to determine exactly which one was which, for the sword always showed the torch's reflection, and Anu always gripped its stem as it it were a weapon.

Being the only sources of light in the Void, the brothers came to realise that they could manipulate the darkness as they desired. With their hands they formed shadows on one another, and were able to animate these shapes for brief moments before they were swallowed up by the darkness. 

Padomay made himself greater in size, to allow these shadows longer to flourish. He made shadows of his own on Anu, and after timeless-time had passed, they realised that there was a substance between them - a mixture of light and dark, a grey shadow. They shone their lights upon this substance, and were astounded when it took shape.

The substance took a shape known to the inaccessible memory of the brothers as the She to their He. They both felt a new thing - they felt love for Her, she who now revealed herself as Nir, the first scion of existence, the very heart of all things.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Birth of Creation

Both Anu and Padomay courted Nir for much of the timeless time, and in those moments all things were possible, for nothing had to be just light or darkness anymore. They spoke of things such as Up and Down, of Here and There, and bathed in the splendour of things which yet had no name. The light of Anu's torch created new things, and the sword of Padomay was able to shape them. Nir marvelled at each new idea, and for eternity all three danced.

Until it came to be that Nir said;

"With what I am and what you are, we could make of this place a true creation, one that shapes and illuminates itself - as such we would not be the only beings here, but would be creators, and would feel the joy of those many whom we created, just as I felt joy when you both formed me from light and dark."

It was clear to the brothers that Nir's idea for creation was the highest of all possible callings, and perhaps the very reason they had come into the Void in the first place. And so they agreed to her plans.

For eternity they spoke of the world they might craft, and the manner in which they might craft it. The brothers came to love Nir deeply, and she loved them in turn; it is the only eternity in all of existence where all beings experienced joy as one.

But the moment came when Nir determined how the world would come to be; she would take the darkness within her, and impregnate it with the light of one of the brothers. Then, she would make of that birthing-spark a form-from-formlessness, a shape from no-shape, capable of self-creation and forming new life.

Thus there came to be the first argument in existence, for neither brother could bear to imagine not being the one to share in Nir's love. Eventually, Nir had to make her choice. She looked into the void at the two brothers, one clutching the torch, the other clutching the sword. At one moment, the two had been identical, but after the creation of Nir, there was now more in the world for the light to catch than just the sword. She saw that the sword was dimmer than the torch, and so she chose Anu, whose light was unquenchable.

Padomay, feeling the first grief in existence, left the pair to their acts and headed deep into the Void. There, he experienced things never before known; the first spark of jealousy, the first smoulder of rage, the first pangs of longing. In the darkness, he believed for a moment he saw something darker than the darkness itself.

Meanwhile, Anu and Nir had performed the rituals of creation, and she had within her the new world. For eternity, the world within her grew and grew. Eventually, time began to flow in all directions - it flowed around Anu and Nir, but not around Padomay.

Anu was comforting Nir at one time.

"O Nir, what wonders might the beings of our new world know?"

"I dream only that they will dream for themselves, beloved Anu."

Deep in the Void, Padomay watched, almost forgotten by Anu and Nir. He felt betrayal at his being spurned, and vowed to win Nir back. And so one moment, he did so.

"O Nir, I love thee so. I would give all of myself to birth worlds within thee also."

"O Padomay, you are handsome and strong, but Anu is the source of all light. Only his love can sustain what we have created."

"You wound me Nir! Was it not my shaping hand that helped your existence form?"

"It was, and I learned it from you. This world is as much yours as Anu's. But the light is what I need - and yours is only a pale reflection."

"You are pale yourself, Nir. I have seen things far brighter and far darker. I have seen the face of dismay and of hate, and I bring it now upon you, so that your world will know it."

And he struck Nir with all the rage and hatred that had built up within him now that he could no longer reflect Anu's light. She reeled, wounded by Padomay's terrible blow, and screamed. Anu, who had been elsewhere, responded at once. He lashed at Padomay with his torch, who parried it with his sword. For some time, they fought until Anu lashed at Padomay's eyes, and forced him outside of time.

Anu rushed to Nir's side. She was alive still, but the blow of terrible darkness was changing her into death, which had never been known before. Anu comforted her more, and shone his great torch to illuminate her face.

As he comforted her, she told him of what she wanted for the world before she came unto darkness.

"I will always think of your love, Anu. Always shine your light on what I have made, so that the world will always remember what we were before this tragedy."

As time went on, Nir was able to give birth to Creation, but as she had foreseen, the effort of it was too much, and what life she had, she gave instead to the world. 

Anu hung his torch above the world to act as a light that would guide it eternally, and this came to be the sun. And then upon the world, as the only living thing in time (since his brother was banished), he breathed his soul into everything, so that everything could have life as he did. Only then, he mused to himself, might he be able to experience the love of Nir - for she too had given herself to make all things.

This new soul of everything was Anui-El, and he was the first living thing in Anu's creation. With Anui-El's birth, Anu retreated through the sun he had made into a place where he could rest and grieve for eternity.
