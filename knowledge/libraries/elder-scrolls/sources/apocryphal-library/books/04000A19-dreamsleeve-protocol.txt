<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/D_letter.png'>reamsleeve communication should not be attempted without the following chants and invocations prior:

1 - Salasa's Greater Ward Against Vaermina
2 - Alvante's Lobe Embalmer
3 - Thirteenth Song of the Ancestral Pupae

After which the security protocol should be painted onto the canticle bark. The security protocol wards are, from lowest to highest:

1 - Moonmoth ancestor wight-ice
2 - Frostmoth ancestor sun-milk
3 - Death's-head ancestor voidbrass
4 - Sphinxmoth ancestor wraithbone
5 - Emperor Moth ancestor sigil stone
5 - Gypsy-moth ancestor diamond-chime

There are other protocols but they are proscribed by the Order. They are only used by miscreants and The Enemy.

Only once these have been performed should the correspondent allow themself into the Chamber Inviolate. 

Once ensconced in the incorrectly-shaped sphere, the correspondent may make use of the keys and hanging-charts and rune-orbits to enforce the nature of the communication. 

To end the communication, reverse the last orbit.

To leave the chamber, make the last Loving Accepted Rebuke of Ancestors and the correspondent will awaken.
