<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/S_letter.png'>he who gave name to the philosophy of the Red Numidan also gave will to the creation of that which lay dormant in inchoate god-form where the sea-caves made lowest nexus.

The path of the Red Numidan was the hunt not just for freedom from the tyrant Molag Bal, but also the enlightenment that would follow. Thus a weapon would be made that would annihilate Bal.

Bal had drawn upon god-guising in order to accrete his rule, and he could not be thwarted by a mortal. She, who was called the Flesh Star, fell from heaven from her wounds but found herself alive in the sea.

She showed the sea-sages her plan, which was to built a new god to slay Bal. This plan was called Red Numidan, which was a name from the ruins of the past. But to the god-weapon they gave another name.

Many gave of themselves to vanquish Bal, and their flesh was changed so that it could be removed and built into the shape of a great warrior. The flesh-changing became part of them, so even to this day they can change from those of the sea to those of the land. But of those who gave their flesh to the Red Numidan's final result, it can be said that they lessened themselves for the greatest cause.

The great Red-Tower-Warrior was thus armed with four shards of the broken sword of Padomay, one in each hand. The two gods fought, and the Red Tower won. Bal had been vanquished, and all came to feast on his flesh.
