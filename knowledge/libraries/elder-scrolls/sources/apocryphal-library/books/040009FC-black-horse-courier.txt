MANE OF ELSWEYR ASSASSINATED IN TORVAL </p>

It is a sad day for the nation of Elsweyr. Last night, Mane Rid'shi'vashta was poisoned with deadly Jarrin Root. He was found this morning by his Lunar Guard.

"This is terrible, terrible news," said Lunar Guard Captain S'hirri. "S'hirri saw nobody enter, nobody leave the Palace. There were no warnings, the magic wards told her nothing. This was a plan long in the making."

Though the Lunar Guard are not dismissing the idea that the Dark Brotherhood was involved, most are currently looking to the Thalmor as the most likely culprits. They have been spreading their talons across the Southern remnants of the Empire for some time now, and now the Elsweyr is the only remaining Imperial province in the south, there seems no more likely candidate.

Imperial control over Elsweyr has been diminishing somewhat over the last few years, with the Mane being one of the true supporters of the Empire. We do not know how long it will be before the moons converge to appoint a new Mane, but we pray to Masser and Secunda that they choose soon.
