Preparations for the Soul Snare



by N'Gasta

</p>
[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/B_letter.png'>efore the process of casting the Soul Snare can begin, the following preparations must be attended to.

First, in the designated area, all shrines to Stendarr must be desecrated. The Watchful Spirit must not be afforded any opportunity to inveigle himself in the proceedings. His followers are jealous rivals of the King of Worms, and seek any opportunity to bar mortals from rightful communion with Mannimarco, Clavicus Vile and Vaermina.

Second, with the watchman's shrines thus toppled, all shrines to Arkay must be extinguished. All his altars must be daubed with the blood of innocent sacrifice, and all candles and offerings dispersed.

(One may overlook the 'hallowed' dead of Arkay - the Soul Snare may not touch them, but they will not interfere with it either. Of course, they remain a useful source of bonemeal.)

Third, all other shrines to the Divines and the Aedra must be similarly sundered and desecrated.

Fourth, should there be any shrines or altars to the Glister-Witch Meridia, these must be utterly reduced to gravel and cleansed in innocent blood.

Fifth, salt should be poured over as much vegetation in the area as can be managed. For very large areas, this may not be practical - magical plagues that affect vegetation will work in its stead. In arid regions, this preparation can be circumvented altogether.

Sixth, black soul gems with the trapped souls of an innocent family must be placed to mark the boundaries of the snare. The shared bond of the family will link the soul gems and, therefore, the perimeter. One remaining gem should be kept apart from the rest - ideally, the eldest of the family.

Seventh, the final stone shall be used as the focus for the Soul Snare ritual, which shall cause all unhallowed dead to rise at the caster's command. Attempts to hallow the dead while the snare is active are doomed to failure, for the King of Worms has foreseen this attempt by his ancient enemy to stall his power.
