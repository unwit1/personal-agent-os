<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/C_letter.png'>ircle-logic is, by its nature, a false and miserly refutation of monolinearity, which is the singular consideration, which is the rejection of the Aldmeri Taint. Therefore, it is in itself a black vestige of the false dualism proposed by the Saliache and their southern kindred.

Therefore; everything that happens, happens only once. An event may be similar to another, but it is never the same event. Time therefore cannot be a cycle, therefore the notion of a Kalpa refers not to cycles but merely to days in the life of Akatosh, which in turn fall in sequence. Each day is different, so the notion that day and night are a cycle is also false.

This is therefore the correct and true order of the universe as it is known;

One; Akatosh and Shezarr come into being. They are equals among all the spheres.

Two; The substance of the Aurbis is formed by the shared endeavours of Akatosh and Shezarr.

Three; The populace of the Aurbis is formed by the shared endeavours of Akatosh and Shezarr.

Four; Some of the populace turn to the falsehoods of two-thought and rebel. They are the first Aldmeri, and they enslave the first Men.

Five; Shezarr walks among Men and shows them how to do battle.

Six; The Aldmeri taint Akatosh with two-thought, enabling them to change the past.

Seven; Shezarr goes missing, allowing Akatosh to fill Heaven once more, disrupting the Aldmeri taint.

Eight; With Shezarr gone, the Saliache enslave Men yet again.

Nine; Akatosh selects his mortal vessel, Alessia.

Ten; Alessia makes war on the Saliache.

Eleven; Alessia finds the Red Diamond and uses it to reconstruct Shezarr.

Twelve; Shezarr, in the form of Pelin-El, helps Alessia to win the war against the Saliache.

Thirteen; Alessia inspires Marukh to form the Exclusionary Mandates.

Articles that post-date the thirteen occurrences can not be calculated, as that information would be required to travel backwards in time, which is the very essence of the Aldmeri taint.
