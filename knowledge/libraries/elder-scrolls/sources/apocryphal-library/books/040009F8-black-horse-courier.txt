ARGONIANS INVADE MORROWIND! </p>

Following the disastrous eruption of Red Mountain, the province of Black Marsh has declared independence under the An-Xileel government, citing that faction's assistance during the Oblivion Crisis as their mandate to rule. With this independence so declared, the Argonians have invaded Morrowind, and have already begun a brutal sack of the lands of House Dres.

"I can't say I blame them", said Gin-Wulm, the master armourer. "After all those years of slavery and mistreatment, House Dres was bound to get it coming to them sooner or later."

Slavery was made illegal in Morrowind by King Hlaalu Helseth in 3E 433, but that clearly hasn't abated the dire sentiment expressed by most Argonians towards their former overlords. The armies are currently advancing towards House Indoril territory, and with the Great Houses still reeling from the volcanic eruption, little is being done to stop them.

The Grand Council has appealed to the Empire to help, but with the legions still recovering from Dagon's onslaught, there are not enough soldiers to deploy. The Elder Council has even raised the question of whether or not Morrowind should be cut loose from the Empire, since the legions were recalled during the Imperial Simulacrum and the Oblivion Crisis.

House Redoran have declared that they will form a bastion against which the Argonian invasion will break. With the commission at Ebonheart destroyed, and Mournhold on the brink of a siege, many are looking to Blacklight to steer the future of that most unfortunate of nations.
