WHERE HAVE THE MOONS GONE?</p>

You don't need us to report it - just look up at night. Masser and Secunda are no longer in the night sky. Where can they have gone?

"Nothing like this has ever happened before," said High Librarian Domitius Caro of the College of Whispers. "We can't even begin to imagine what might have caused this. Is this the work of the gods? Have the Divines punished us?"

The Moons above Nirn have been a source of mystery since the dawn of time. Some believe that they are the remains of gods, with one notorious treatise claiming that they are the body of Shezarr. They are held in high esteem by the Khajiit above all others, who call them Jone and Jode.

"We are still uncertain how to adjust to this", said Priestess Ko'Sashira of the Temple of Azurah. "There is no precedent here. How are we supposed to be Khajiit now?"

While many point to a divine punishment or Daedric trickery, others believe the cause lies closer to home.

"There was to be a new Mane soon. This Ra'zhartha knows", said Ra'zhartha of the White Sands. "And we all know that the last one was the Thalmor's work. They're trying to stop us having our Mane."

His words don't seem to convince many of his fellows, however. The Thalmor are known to be powerful, but surely they can't hide the moons? Can they?
