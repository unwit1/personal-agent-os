HIGH KING OF SKYRIM SLAIN
Jarl of Windhelm Declares Rebellion</p>

The news came down through Pale Pass last night - High King Torygg has been murdered. 

The perpetrator is well known - Ulfric Stormcloak, the Jarl of Windhelm, and leader of the Stormcloak movement. Though his declaration of independence happened some time ago, after he was released from imprisonment following his insurrection in The Reach, he has now openly called for civil war, and the province is split almost down the centre. Eastmarch, Winterhold, The Pale and The Rift all cleave to Ulfric's banner

Jarl Ulfric has declared that Skyrim should be free of the Thalmor and the Empire. Citing the White-Gold concordat and the proscription of Talos worship, he declared that Skyrim should be free of outside interference once and for all, and that the lands should belong to the Nords.

This is a devastating blow to an Empire that already consists now of only three provinces. Will the Throat of the World fall to these rebels?

"Not if General Tullius can help it," said a soldier we spoke to. "He's one of the finest military minds in our generation. And the Nords love him - he fought alongside the Nord legions in the Great War. If anyone can put down this Stormcloak traitor, it's him."
