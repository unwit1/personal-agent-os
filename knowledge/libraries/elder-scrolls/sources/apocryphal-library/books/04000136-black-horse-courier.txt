GENERAL SYMMACHUS DIES IN BATTLE! </p>

Dark tidings from the provinces today - General Symmachus, loyal servant of Tiber Septim himself, had perished last Loredas in battle against a growing peasant revolt in southern Morrowind.

Discontent has been brewing across the Empire as of late, with His Majesty Uriel VII assuring the population that all will be well. However, with the death of one of the Empire's staunchest protectors, unease is filtering across the mountains into fair Cyrodiil. The Royal Family of Morrowind has never been popular since the rise of the Empire, with most Dunmer turning instead to the rule of the Great Houses, or the Tribunal Temple. The exact motives of this band of rebels are not yet known, but for so seasoned a general to fall against a mob is causing special disquiet.

Symmachus is remembered as one of the founders of the Third Empire, as his loyalty to his Celestial Majesty Tiber Septim assisted in the consolidation of Imperial rule in Morrowind. He is remembered for his heroic deeds during the War of the Red Diamond.

Symmachus was married to Queen Barenziah, who has recently travelled to the Imperial City with their children, Helseth and Morgiah, where they have met with welcoming crowds. Our thoughts are with the family during this tragic time, and we encourage the people of our good city to give them the privacy they require.
