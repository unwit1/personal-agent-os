A Wager with the Madgod

by Lord Andulias
</p>
<p align="left">
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'> summoned the Mad God Sheogorath on his summoning day, and made with him a wager. I bet that I could withstand the effects of his madness, and indeed that I, Lord Andulias of Eldenroot, could make him more mad.

The inconceivable prince of chaos stood before me in his Sundas best, a fine silken purple doublet with slashed sleeves, and accepted the wager. His composed yet rather brow-beaten butler, a man (or was he too a Daedra) called Haskill, was conjured forth to judge the contest.

Sheogorath unleashed a barrage of gibberish at me, which I deflected with my calm wit. He would have to do better than that, I told him. Flummoxed, he revealed to me the most terrifying secrets of creation, and showed me the dark void of un-existence from which all life sprang out of dear hope that it might be spared the eternal darkness.

I yawned. I told Sheogorath that I'd seen it all before.

By this point he was gasping and wheezing, watering at the eyes, and demanding to know how I could withstand this onslaught. At this point, Haskill declared me the winner.

"Who are you that can outwit the great Sheogorath?" he demanded.

"Oh you don't understand, milord,"  I replied, " I AM Sheogorath."

"But if you're Sheogorath", said Sheogorath, "then who in Oblivion am I?" 

"You, milord, are Lord Andulias of Eldenroot."

"But that's - wait, if you're me and I'm you, who on Nirn is writing this damn book?" I retorted, and the Madgod looked at me with a smirk. Wait, I thought he was writing it? Wait, where was I.
"Yes, you are writing it!" I said back to the Lord, who was indeed writing it. "Look at all the words you are writing."

"Yes of course!" I said to Sheogorath. "Wait, who won the bet?"
"I did of course!" I sneered back. Or rather, He sneered back, at me. 

"Oh yes of course. Wait, which one am I again?"
"You're Sheogorath."

"Oh yes, of course. Haskill, let us return - wait, if I'm Sheogorath, why are you Sheogorath?"

"Oh don't worry about that right now. I think you're learned your lesson," I replied.
"You're right, of course. Well played, Lord Sheogorath, " I replied. To me.

"Wait, if you're Sheogorath and I'm Lord Andulias, and I'm writing the book but you're the one I was writing about but you're writing it, then who is reading it?"

"Oh, that's just the reader. Nobody you need to get worked up about."
Hang on a minute, you think. Am I in the book now?

"No, you're just reading it. Don't worry about it!", you said to Lord Andulias. And the three of you gorged yourselves on cheese and lungs. What? You're the one writing it.
