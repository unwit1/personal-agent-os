<p align="left">
The Animavorial Rites
A blasphemous treatise from old High Rock detailing the use of souls trapped in black soul gems. A high priority target, any copies must be confiscated and if necessary destroyed on the spot.

Ars Carnibus Ligandi
A tome of such vileness that its contents must not be made known. Report any rumours of this book to the necessary authorities immediately.

De Salibus Animae
"On the Salts of the Soul", attributed to an unknown first era necromancer, describes processes by which soul gems may be broken down into a substance from which larger ones may be created. Contains certain revelations which make the book very dangerous.

N�Gasta! Kvata! Kvakis!
A well-known tome of necromancy by N'gasta the sloadic conjuror of the late second era. Poorly understood but believed to contain hidden knowledge of soul snares. Considered extremely dangerous.
