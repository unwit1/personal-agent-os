Moth Priest Arrested</p>

Syntallus Cordibo, an eminent member of the Cult of the Ancestor Moth, was arrested in the Imperial Court today for claiming, out-loud and during a session of the Elder Council, that the Emperor was not wearing a "false copy of the Amulet of Kings."

This allegation is, frankly, a bizarre one; if the Amulet is fake, what has happened to the real one? We know that it cannot be destroyed, and we know that if the Emperor were to die, then the Elder Council would be alerted by the amulet.

Brother Cordibo was taken away to the cells after claiming that he could "not hear the Oversoul", and that "Alessia, Reman, Talos... if they are not with our Emperor, then where are we?"

Moth-Priests undergo heavy spiritual training in order to decipher the Elder Scrolls, which has a tendency to cause blindness as their career progresses. Brother Cordibo is not yet blind, the Black Horse Courier wishes to note.
