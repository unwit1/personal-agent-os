Come to me, Paramour, and bathe in the assurant wisdoms of Almalexia.

AE MARA ANTHERA, Love is a flower. 

A seed is the nothing that becomes everything. Born blind and unknowing, it longs for a sun it has never known. The first rays of ancient sunlight that kiss its shoots are a fuller hedonism than it has ever known from the memories it drank in everlasting night. It knows nothing but to grow and finally to bloom; it dies at the climax of its ecstasy and is never seen again. 

On her crystal dais, Merid-Nunda crafts a rose of glass. It never opens because it was made that way, and it can never close and never die. It is beautiful only in the moment, and without movement it becomes invisible. It was never love and it was never a flower. The seed is born in misery and its exaltation in death is nothing less than the perfection of all endeavours that births the new seed.

The flower-bud is a promise of beauty to come. The passing moments of patience are rewarded when the blossom is full. The gardener knows that the flower will die with the passing of the season, and so one must spend all the time with it that one can, before it dies and leaves the promise of tomorrow.

Tend to a single flower, embrace it in all its divine wonder, and then go into your garden, which is all of Resdayn. See how the stoneflower looks to the soil, the kresh-blossom looks to the sky, the fire fern sprouts from ash and the coda-flower rises from the mire.
