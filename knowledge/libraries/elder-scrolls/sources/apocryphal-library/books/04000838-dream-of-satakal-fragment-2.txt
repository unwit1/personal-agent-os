The snake man, the cruel builder of the Desert, maker of the first mountain atop a summit of nothing. It is he, Akel - and he mocks me for my loss. I hurl abuse at the snake-man-coil.

"Be gone, Akel, thief and murderer! Begone inky black nothing, snake of no new beginnings, whose scales conceal only hunger! A hunger that can never satisfy you! You took from me my greatest treasure!"
