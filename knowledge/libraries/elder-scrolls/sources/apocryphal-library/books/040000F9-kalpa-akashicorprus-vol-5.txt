Volume IV: On the Subsequent Kalpa

<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he next kalpa is in doubt. The pattern has repeated for an unknown number of instances, and there are those in the world who expect it to happen again. There are those who prepare for the 'Dusk' era (which is, perhaps, the Dawn of the next kalpa) by making themselves ready to 'jump' onto the next, where no doubt their fool-talk will cause the cycle to repeat. But there are some who believe that the next kalpa might not be a repeat, but a new thing hitherto unknown.

According to Professor Elaphagus Queld of Wayrest, plans for the former are already underway.

"The next kalpa is, I think, being prepared for by those who remember the last, or at least who remember the Middle Dawn. You know the kind of people I mean - the Marukhati of Ut-Cyrod, the old Thalmor of second-era Alinor, and other similarly single-minded groups. It's the elves who want to erase men and escape into the next kalpa unscathed, and the Exlcusionists of men who want to erase the 'Aldmeri Taint' and live in a world forever rid of elves. These groups are actively trying to bring about the next apocalyptic event so that they can be the ones to drink the blood of Akatosh and survive the calamity. I don't think it will work out the way they think it will, but it's happened before. Why do you think the Elves have their tale of Aldmeris, a piece of the previous age, while the Men were descended from those who survived by endurance? 

So there it is - the next kalpa, assuming something doesn't change, will be much like this one - populated largely by one group but with a smattering of the other. It's going to go down the exact same way."

Moon-Bishop Sa'Rivrra of Dune describes another way that the next kalpa may emerge.

"Azurah knows many mysteries, any Khajiit can tell you this. But she and her sisters know the secret of the next age - they have shared it with the Dunmer, but Baan Dar whispered its secrets to Khajiit when he stole them.

"The two-faced god of the Dunmer, who is called Vivec among those people, has spoken to his priests of Amaranth - the flower of love, the wounded heart that forgives and rests. It is when one has the power to take the Heart, but instead cuts their hands off so they cannot. It is the mercy-strike against the self.

"There is little true love in this world, especially among gods and the very powerful. It will take one of a singular mind, one cunning and wicked enough to crush all resistance between them and paradise for all. It was Lorkhaj, the Imperials say, who first attempted this so that others might learn. But it has only been succeeded once ever, when the Aurbis was made anew."

The Amaranth is a lofty goal, it seems - but as the Moon Bishop says, it will require one who has all the skills of a murderer and the good will of a saint. Can such an individual bring in the next era, free of the Enantiomorph, before the feuding enemies can re-enact the first death?

And with that, we conclude this series. The fate of the Aurbis will be determined by warriors, thieves, lovers and mages, likely many times, before the Amaranth brings in the new order of being.
