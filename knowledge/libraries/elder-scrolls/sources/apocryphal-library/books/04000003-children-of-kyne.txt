With Shor, the great Storm-Mother Kyne had her multitudes of children, that is us - Men. She breathed us onto the snow and the ice. But she had other children too, and here are their names; remember, they are brother and sister to us, but no less deadly and no more merciful.

All creatures of the land, sea and sky are hers. The gulls and the crows you know by sight and by sound, and many is the warrior who wears the pelts of wolves and bears fought to death. The Slaw-Fish and the Great Crab are her watchers of the ocean, and all forms of beetle and ant from the smallest to the greatest of the ancestor-crabs.

The Storms above are her eldest children. Be they the ones that unleash the thickest snows or the brightest lances of lightning, they are her wisest and fiercest. 

And deep in the forests are the trees she counts as her most favoured. The great Gleamsap Tree of Osternmark is tended by her most loyal servants, those children of the Sky Mother who tend to her duties with more savagery than any wolf or bear.

The Spriggans are they, and though they do not bear the power of the storm they have more mastery than any others of her issue. They are the mirror of man's build, and they mould their barken faces in her image. They invoke her swarms of bees and wasps, and command her bears and wolves and crabs as armies against any who threaten her most sacred groves.

Go wary then into the wilderness, for the children of Kyne will not recognise you as brother or sister unless you bear her token or blessing. Never forsake Kyne and perhaps she will reward you with such.
