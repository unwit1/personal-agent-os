IN THE NAME OF CLAVICUS VILE
We dutifully calculate the worths and values of mortal souls, so that he may weigh against them is mercies and favours, so that when the moment of the final and right bargain comes the worth of each thing may be known.

"O, Clavicus Vile, name ye the price
And we as mortals shall pay it."

IN THE NAME OF MOLAGABAEL
We drive fast the spikes and so turn the screws into the appointed innocents, that their wails might crystallise on the catafalque as a promise monochromatic.

"O, Molagabael, name ye the hour
And we as mortals shall abide."

IN THE NAME OF HERMAEUS MORA
We perform the duties of rogues in the night, and ask not for comprehension of their meaning but instead of the mysteries of the impossible pattern recurring infinitely.

"O Hermaeus Mora, name ye the task
And it shall be written in secret ink."

Make unto the dark gods no circle nor sanctuary that does not honour them thus, for such patrons of the eldritch methodologies are the wards and sentinels against the thousand colours and the gyre inconceivable.

THE THOUSAND COLOURS
The polynymic radiance of the mirror-infinity is a beacon of ignorance in the calming dark, and only with due reverence can the mirrors be turned from view or shattered. For the light of even two such colours may rebuke the summoned dead, and as lettering may form a ward against the better knowledge. Know the enemy as MERID-NUNDA.

THE GYRE INCONCEIVABLE
As ever turns the wheel within the wheel, things fall to ruin and cannot be rekindled. This is the dark law of ARKAY, a shade from another time still lauded as a creator and a wellspring of knowledge by those content to let flesh become soil with no recourse.

Thus the tumult in heaven is mirrored on Nirn - as above, so below. Task yourself in every hour with the confundation of the schemes of the enemy.
