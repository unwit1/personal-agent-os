The Rules of Influence
for Jarls, Merchants and Landowners

by

Nazeem of Whiterun

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/D_letter.png'>o you get to the Cloud District very often? Oh, what am I saying! If you did, you wouldn't be reading this. If you wish to run a successful business someday, hold influence in the court of a powerful ruler, or command the respect of your citizens, you have surely come to the right place. You want to win big in a sea of big fishes, and the shark of Whiterun has come to offer his business advice for you, keen learner.

The first tenet of success is never to outsmart your superiors. You may show disdain to everyone else in the world, but your superiors - be they Captains, Generals, Jarls, Queens - must always look good compared to you. You must work hard to make them look good, and in doing so you will always have their favour.

Friends may become hard to rely on; they will believe that you owe them as much as they owe you. Sometimes it is smarter to promote a rival, who will have more to prove to earn your favour.

It can benefit you to create an air of mystery. Own a large tract of land? Sleep in an inn. Imply that you have the ear of a great Jarl, or attend his court often, but let none see you there. Thus, none will be able to predict your gambits.

Know who you're dealing with. You might gain respect by elevating yourself in the eyes of the common folk, whether by aggrandising yourself or besmirching them; but avoid doing so to an armed warrior who may take that as a challenge to their esteem.

Enlist the help of others, but always take the credit. Thus will you seem to achieve things without effort, and gain great respect.

Learn the rules and customs of the culture where you seek influence; follow them faultlessly, and act as a perfect citizen at all times. Fail in this and you will be seen as an eternal outsider.

Follow these rules, and you too can become wealthy and influential like myself. Of course, this is probably a bit over your head.
