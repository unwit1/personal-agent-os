RETURN OF THE MOONS</p>

We're all very glad to see that the moons are back in the night sky once more. The Void Nights are over, after two dark years.

"It's a strange one", said Domitius Caro of the College of Whispers. "I've been studying the phenomenon since it began. There were no warnings from the Temples of anything like this happening, and we've all but dismissed a Divine occurrence. The Khajiit detractors may have been right after all".

Various outspoken elements in Elsweyr had decried the Void Nights as being the result of Thalmor interference, but their complaints had largely fallen on deaf ears. 

"No, you see? The Thalmor are the ones who fixed it. They returned the moons to us!", said Priestess Ko'Sashira of the Temple of Azurah. "We should be thankful to our friends the Thalmor. They helped us where the Empire did not."

Worrying sentiments indeed. The Emperor has declared a private meeting of the Elder Council to discuss what might happen in Elsweyr next.
