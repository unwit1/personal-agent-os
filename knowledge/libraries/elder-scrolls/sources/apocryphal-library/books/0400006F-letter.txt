To my dearest F,

I dared to use the powers of true seeing upon the item, and I cannot say even now whether or not I am glad that I have done so. For within its fractals and corners I bore witness to things from between the edges of the epochs that even now I struggle to ignore. Such hideous sights put me in mind of the ancient tales of forgotten Lyg, or of the forbidden city of Aukurlyfk in snow-perished Atmora. 

Indeed, it is to the detriment of my sleeping hours, for every night I bear witness to the same bleak tapestry. The aeon-soaked knowledge that comes to me from the half-fancied sigils and bas-reliefs, on the other hand, swells my power in the waking hours, and even my fellow magicians have begun to comment on the alacrity with which my learning has hastened. 

I shall not caution you too heavily against looking upon the crystal, but be well aware of its potential. I dare say it is not of the Aurbis, at least, not of this Aurbis. What I mean by that I can still not truly fathom.

Yours forever in forbidden logic,

L
