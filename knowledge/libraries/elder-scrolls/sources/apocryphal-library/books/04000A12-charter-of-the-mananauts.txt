<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/F_letter.png'>or the good of the Empire, the Elder Council hereby declares the IMPERIAL  MANANAUTS, hereafter referred to as the Mananauts, to be assigned official existence and status in perpertuity, provided that the organisation is deemed to have acted in accordance with its remits and mandates as dictated henceforth.

1 - The Mananauts shall gather those with the skill and expertise to use the approved methods to afford the Empire learning and understanding of those interests, threats and other interests that lie beyond the exact borders of the Mundus.

2 - Those approved methods will be defined in a separate charter, and thus will include but will not be limited to: Scrying, Plane-walking, Extra-mundial flight, Conjuration and Dreamsleeve-manipulation.

3 - Those methods used will not act in counter to the approved methods as described and determined by the magickal decrees of the Elder Council.

4 - The Mananauts will contribute their findings to the research of magickal defences to the Empire, against the threat of Daedric invasion or other extra-mundial threats.

5 - The Mananauts will develop an internal charter demonstrating the correct use of equipment designed to ensure the safety in body, mind and soul of the user while transgressing the liminal barriers between Mundus and beyond.

6 - The Mananauts may request from the Imperial reserves sufficient mana and magickal resource to fund and power their explorations and investigations.

7 - The Empire and the Elder Council may not directly interfere with the methods used by the Mananauts to achieve their goals, beyond the remit of article 3.
