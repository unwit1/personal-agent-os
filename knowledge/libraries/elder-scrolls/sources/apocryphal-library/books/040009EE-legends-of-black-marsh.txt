The realm has been a source of wonder for scholars and archaeologians for centuries. Known to most of Tamriel as Black Marsh, it is a land of many unexplained rumours and happenings. Let us turn our attention to some of its most unusual legends.

The DUSKFALL was an incident in ancient times when the native Argonians abandoned their worship of an ancient dark god, who brought only ruin and death. In those times, they had built great cities, purely to withstand the dark god's wrath. In doing so they became expert builders and crafters of all things. The priests of this dark god were all-powerful, able to wield his worship like a weapon. However, the Argonians eventually turned on these priests, though none know for certain why - but apparently the strange Hist-Trees gave them a reason.
After this, the Argonians let the marshes reclaim most of their pyramid-temples, which they called 'Xanmeers'. These ruins are likely full of strange old works and treasures that could reveal much about the ancient Argonians.

The EYE OF ARGONIA is supposedly a great emerald of extraordinary value. Though most Argonian shamans agree that is exists, none have ever shed any light on its whereabouts. In fact, there are only really two things known about it. One is that it is roughly the size of a fist, eye-shaped, and with a dark seam on the inside that appears like a snake's pupil.
The other is that it is the key to an ancient city, the 'Lost City of Argonia'. Of this city, none know the location, but it is fabled to be a seat of power for the ancient Saxhleel civilisation that existed before the Duskfall. There are many ruined Xanmeers around the realm, though none have ever been revealed as this 'Lost City', nor has anything resembling a keyhole for an eye-stone been recorded.

The dreaded HAYNEKHTNAMET is a Wamasu, or swamp-dragon. It still roams the swamps of Black Marsh, and various villages have taken to bringing the creature sacrifices of food in the hopes that it will grant them bountiful harvests. Many still live in terror of this beast, whose mouth is said to be like a stormcloud.

The GOLDEN SKULL OF BEELA-KAR is the skull of the ancient Argonian sorcerer of the same name. He was a terrible necromancer, and worshipper of the ancient dark god. He despised his fellow Argonians who turned away from his god, and sought terrible reckonings. He was eventually slain, but his skull was taken away by his followers and turned into a dreaded idol. They too were ultimately slain, and the skull was lost. Many fear that it contains dread power that could be used to unleash awful ruin on Black Marsh.

The REMNANT OF ARGON is a great amber gem that had the power to protect the tribe that crafted it from their enemies, the Heartland High Elves, who had invaded Black Marsh. What manner of protection it afforded is long since forgotten, but it seems as if the tribe failed. The stone has been long since lost, probably claimed by the Elves.
