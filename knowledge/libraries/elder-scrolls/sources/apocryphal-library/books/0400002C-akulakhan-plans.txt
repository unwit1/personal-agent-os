AKULAKHAN
Plans and schematics for the Second Numidium

as written by
Dagoth Tureynul
Dagoth Gilvoth
Dagoth Vaner
Dagoth Drals </p>
[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
Under the auspices and watchful designs of DAGOTH UR are these schemas of the SECOND NUMIDIUM so compiled for the use of the appointed labourers.

AKULAKHAN is to be the waking-star of the final dawn, wreathed and robed in majesty of the coming time. It is to be made according to the principles and dimensions first proposed by Kagrenac the tonal architect. Those principles are thus:

I: It shall be the walking motif of a tower of the founding time.
II: Its keystone shall be the wounded-star.
III: It shall be flesh-made-god from the skin of its creators.
IV: It shall be a voice for the land.
V: It shall be the divine mirror of creation.
VI: It shall refuse the world upon which it walks.
VII: It shall refuse the sky underneath it.
VIII: IT SHALL REMEMBER

Of the SKELETON:
The skeleton may be made of any pure material constituting bone-thought or reason. It has been proposed that ancient whalebone may be used. It has likewise been suggested that only adamantium will be suitable. DAGOTH UR has decreed in response that either will be suitable, but has indicated that the bones of the monster-children of Vehk may be apposite. We have sent scouts to Mamaea to harvest what we can.

Of the ARMOUR: 
The outer skin shall be made of lichbrass taken from the surrounds of the Dwemer facility. It shall be panel-shaped and form-sung into pieces that will overlap and disperse dissonance and harmful tones.

Of the FLESH:
The body of AKULAKHAN shall be the harvested corprus-flesh of the House Unmourned. They shall remove their flesh piecemeal and do so again once it has regrown. It shall be sung onto the bone by bellwrights and ash-poets. Thus shall it fulfil the third principle.

Of the HEART:
Unlike the failed Numidium, puppet-tool of traitor kings, AKULAKHAN will use the wounded star as was always intended. The Heart is already in place, and AKULAKHAN shall be built around it.

It shall stand one thousand feet from the zenith of its crest to the foundation of its mystery. It shall be built in the likeness of all ADA. It shall stand two hundred feet from shoulder to shoulder.
