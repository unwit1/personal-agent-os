As recorded by Skatha the Skald

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'>t was in Asvald's time when Kvenel Iron-Tongue, hero of Volunruud who had bested the great wyrm Heyvnehlaat at Osric's Stead, did raise twelve-score banners against Hevnoraak the Undying, Wyrm-Priest of Valthume who had slain Eska the Bear-Mother and Haldis Broken-Spear at the Battle of the Two Beards.

From Valthume, Hevnoraak brought his grim batallion to war, and many were his shield-bearers. Among them was Eisenrath Blackwater, son of Isvild the Bleak, and there was Ulvisk Shattermast who broke the great ship Yltharik, and there was Shigla Whale-Call who fought only with her hands. There was Ulfric Hollow-Axe the Fire-Caller, and his sister Ysvimgr the Sly, and lastly there was Yngnavar Ghost-Bear who lived to vanquish his greaters.

And among those following Kvenel was Hamthir the Violent whose name was granted by every skald, and Ludoric Shor's Fist whose anvil was his weapon, and Sjaeme Yew-Haft whose spear pierced the Great Whale, Valdar the Vigilant whose watch never faltered, Drenga Iron-Shod whose horse could outride the wind, and Calas the Son-Daughter, who fought with a weapon in each hand and whose sex was unknown to all but Mother Mara. Lastly there was Unveld the Wolfslayer, servant of Kvenel, and whose arrows missed but one time in nine.

Many battles were fought in this. Valthume and Volunruud both suffered at the hands of the other, and the war spilled over even into the Reach and the Rift where many died at the peak of Northwind Summit.

It was in this time that Yngnavar attacked Freda, who had not brought her children to battle but to tend the wounded. Freda slew two of Yngnavar's banner-men but died at his hand after the last of her children had made their escape. Thus was this known now as the battlefield of sorrow, where his pride was broken in the sight of the gods. 

Freda's children fled but armed themselves. Later in the war, they met Unveld Wolfslayer, who took them under his wing and they did so become part of his great hunt, which included many hunters both man and beast, and even Alebor Snow-Elf who was son of Hircyne. Unveld vowed that Yngnavar would pay for his crime, and so they did seek to find him.

The hunt was long and wearying, but Unveld had made good his sacrifices to the Hawk-Mother. Yngnavar had fled, knowing that he had brought dishonour from the gods themselves, and hid himself so that none could find him.

After closing in on his hiding place, the children of Freda did do battle with those few warriors still loyal to Yngnavar, and cleared the way for Unveld to track down his quarry.

Kyne guided Unveld through many traps and pitfalls until he confronted Yngnavar, who did not raise his sword but threw it down, and said;

"Unveld, I am dishonoured. I have no worth in the eyes of the gods, and no combat between warriors can restore my valour. Take me before the children I have orphaned, and let them have their justice."

Unveld delivered him thus to the children of Freda, who did cut into his back the Bloodhawk of Kyne, the punishment for those who were bereft of honour. As token decree of the gods, his lands did pass to the children who did mourn for their mother but did prosper in later life. Thus was Yngnavar permitted to enter Sovngarde, though he was far from Shor's table.

It was later in the war when Unveld and his pack did do battle with Ulfric and Ysvimgr. Unveld slew Ithric and Galdir who were the sons of Alfmir Grey-Hammer, but his ninth arrow missed Ysvimgr and she would be his bane. She slew Untheld the Woman-in-Bear-Skin and Bruthwe the Bear-in-Woman-Skin, but fell to Ulfgrim the Triumphant, who was a wolf and was son of Mara. Ulfgrim and Ulfric - who had slain Onvald son of Ysmic and Brannan son of Kierthan o' the Reach - were the last two alive, and neither fought the other but went their own ways.

Hevnoraak was mortally wounded and buried in Valthume, but Valdar the Vigilant learned that he had made treaty with dread Orkey to rise from his anointed death, and so set his eternal watch over the Wyrm-Priest's tomb.
Kvenel was wounded but lived another year, and was entombed in Volunruud with his weapons Okin and Eduj, which had slain a thousand men each.

Freda, Yngnavar and Unveld were buried where they had fallen. One in Valthume, one in Volunruud, the other at Northwind Summit. One by Kvenel himself, one in vigil alongside Valdar, and one at the feet of a great wyrm.
