AN END TO THE BLIGHT</p>

The terrible sickness that emanated from Red Mountain, and caused such trouble to the Imperial colonisation and mining efforts, has been cleared. 

The native Dunmer of the island proclaim that a prophecy was fulfilled, and that the blight was the work of a dark god and enemy of the Tribunal, 'Dagoth Ur', who had been a foe of the Dunmer since the first age.

They also claim he was defeated by the re-incarnation of the First Era military leader, Indoril Nerevar, who had been prophesied to return and liberate the Dunmer from darkness. This 'Nerevarine' has been widely celebrated since the demise of Dagoth Ur.

On a more troubling note, none of the Tribunal gods have released even a single statement on the matter. The inner echelons of the Dunmer Temple have remained incredibly close-lipped about their gods' opinions on the matter - indeed, their words on anything at all.
