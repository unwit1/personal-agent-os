Mortal Taxonomies

by Seeker T'uuhgi Hlaeer




Volume I: Divergences of the Dawn War

</p>
<p align="left">

It is with no small amount of distaste that, under the personal instruction of Hermaeus Mora, I am to write these works in the common tongue of the Mortal plane. Nevertheless, under the unblinking eyes above, I persist.

The Ehlnofey War or Dawn War was a mythi-realistic event following a standard temporal iteration pattern. Before the war, mortal species all wore the same morphology as the common Dreugh of Tamriel's waters. Only by accidental tinkering with the varliance circuit did they inadvertently gain insight into a previous morpheme of mortal being, and splintered themselves into reptilians and what are now deemed humanoids.

Those that spent too long toying with the circuit took on draconic, immortal forms and are not the subject of this manual. The remaining forms soon winnowed themselves down to a scant few who attempted to carve up the remnant of the Aurbis between them. It has been fairly amusing to watch.

Two major philosophical branches of the mortal forms have so far developed, the first claiming that they were born of a supreme god, the second claiming that they were created by same. Ignoring the fact that these two points of view are fundamentally identical, the two factions appear to have diverged considerably from this point, with the former being longer-lived while the latter being more impervious to physical harm.

A sub-group of the reptilians also emerged that did not become immortal, and though they were originally considered to be of least interest, they are displaying more and more signs of potence.
