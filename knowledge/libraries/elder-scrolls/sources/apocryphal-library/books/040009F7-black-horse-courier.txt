VALENWOOD GOVERNMENT OVERTHROWN
Third Aldmeri Dominion Declared! </p>

After weeks of bloody civil war, the Imperial-overseen government of Valenwood has been defeated by a renegade faction backed by the Thalmor. The last survivors of the Legions and those Bosmer still loyal to the throne are in retreat back to Cyrodiil.

After their victory, the newly-formed rebel government of the region declared union with Alinor, who have now declared the Third Aldmeri Dominion.

Emperor Mede has vowed that these attacks must not continue, and has instructed the Elder Council to go on a war footing immediately. Currently the big concern is rescuing loyal factions and families from within Valenwood.
