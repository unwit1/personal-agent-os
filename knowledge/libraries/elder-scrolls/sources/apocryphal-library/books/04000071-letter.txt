To P.P

The workes thus mention'd, as in thy laste correspondence, are notte of men nor of mer, but be in the moste foule and ancient tongue of the Sloade, whose colonys of sunken Thrasse remayne well belowe the wayves of the Abecean. Indede, the sinkinge of their realme has done naughte to quelle their darke and moste cruel plans whiche grow e'er closer to fruitioun. 

Neither Divynes, nor daimons of Oblivioun, clayme responsibilitie for the creatioun of the Sloade, remember ye that - it ys as if the verie seas spawn'd that ill-featur'd race of wretched monsteres, or that certain bleak-reflected starlighte was so caste upon the sea as to conjure these devilles from the bryne. 

Of the tablets, say naughte to anye. 

I remayne thy most humble servante,

Q.
