Numidian Saints

by The Reconstituted Man
</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
Under the close-eyed sky, there labour the eternal saints of the eternal endeavour; here follow their names, blessed they be in the font of ever-flowing brass.

Saint Mehrunes Dagon, the Numidium of yesterday, who wore not brass but his own flesh. In this age he is guardian of the hidden heart.

Saint Alessia, Queen Ut-Cyrod, who built the PEL-IN-AL which was the prototype for the new man.

Saint Kagrenac, the Architect, the Armourer, the Witch of Bronze Music. His were the calculations of divine angles and melodies that birthed the New God.

Saint Zurin Arctus, the Silent Machinist, who rebirthed the Golden One from the remains of the old. Willingly he tore open his own chest to give life to the sacred carcass.

Saint Mannimarco, the Worm Saint, who calculated sphere-reckoning from the circuit.

Saint Uriel Septim, who united the West under the truth of the celestial logic-engine, and laid the gentle Zurin to rest after his era of service.

Saint Voryn Dagoth, who remembered himself from death out from among the existence-circuits and made them into the second iteration.
