Doctryne Thirty-Three:

It is forbayden to trafficke with daimons, nor to enter wyth them wyllinglie or unwyllinglie into any pacte or agreymente to the benyfite of said daimon. To do so ys to transgreyss the sanctitie of AKATOSH to whomb we owe our dulye appoynted mortalitie. The punishment is to be death, and forfyture of landes and tytles from thy next of kinne.
