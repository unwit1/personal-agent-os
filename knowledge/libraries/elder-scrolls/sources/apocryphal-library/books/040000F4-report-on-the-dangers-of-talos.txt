Report to the Anuielectorate



On the Dangers of Talos

Approximated threat level: Existential
</p>
[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
For my attendant colleagues of the Anuielectorate, 1st New Seed 4E 1:

A full assessment of the dangers posed by the emergent entity TalOS (Over Soul) has been prepared by agents of the Thalmor. The findings are recorded chronostratigraphically.

LAYER ONE: 

The primary congealment of the threat-substance is initialised at One-Tower with the subsumption of pneuma of Altmoran warlord, designation SK-1 4787 "King Wulfharth", into the crystallised Heart-of-LKN following re-enactment scenario 84699 "Orkey vs Shor". This process grants the AE-of-LKN a mobile presence beyond One-Tower.

LAYER TWO:

A convergence with Breto-Nordic warlord HR-2 782 "Hjalti Early-Beard" occurs some centuries later during the expansion of the landgrab 2442 "Empire of Falkreath" by Breto-Nordic warlord FL-2 53 "Cuhlecain" with the immediate result that HR-2 782 acquires Tonal capacities that had lain dormant within SK-1 4787. Convergence further expands to accept unknown vector UV-X 909 "Zurin Arctus", who agents are trying to connect with renegade "Dragon Guard" operative HR-1 668 "Chevalier Renald" (See also AK-1 77884 "Sword-Captain Vershu") with no confirmed link yet, save for the appearance of HR-1 668 at the camp of FL-2 53 in the surrounding period.

LAYER THREE:

Following the treaty, RD-0 7658 "Vivec" announces the gift of DW-0 6564 "Numidium" to the convergence. HR-2 782 and UV-X 909 reconctruct the Heart-of-LKN in morpholithic subgradient and trap SK-1 4787 inside (though reports vary, and suggest that UV-X 909 ended up inside, with some even going so far as to say that all pneumae shifted one step to the left). End result, one subgradient Heart-of-LKN "Mantella", one lich bound to former, UD-3 3 "The Underking", and one crowned head of state CY-3 1 "Tiber Septim". Heart-of-LKN is still connected to SK-1 4787, which is in the morpholith-centre of UV-X 909, which is connected to HR-2 782, who wears the morpholith ML-0 1 "Chim-El Adabal" which contains the pneumae of (insert series: CY-1 200 "Alessia" to CY-2 89023 "Reman III") and also is connected to Two-Tower. (End result - whoever wears the Adabal is "connected to the sky and the earth" if you will permit the bad mythopoetry.) "Tiber Septim" enters the Cyrod pantheon as an erroneous Nine of Eight (fulcrum).

LAYER FOUR:

Untime event DB-XX25460 "Warp in the West" occurs, sundering "Mantella" and leaving UD-3 1 in final death sequence. The name "Talos" begins to replace "Tiber Septim" in the mythic.

SK-1 4787 thus freed returns to Red Mountain and aids unknown vector UV-3 77819 "The Nerevarine" in the de-triangulation of Heart-of-LKN.

ML-0 1 is sundered by CY-3 8655400 "Martin Septim" freeing all mortal remnants from the morpholith.

LAYER FIVE:

With all mortal components denatured, the threat of TalOS is no longer bound to a physical centre. The detriangulation of the Heart-of-LKN is doubtless a source of potence for the emergent tulpa. 

If left unchecked, the tulpa could fully take on the form of the Ancient Enemy.

RECOMMENDATION:

Force the incumbent Imperial warlord CY-4 48150 "Titus Mede II" to accept the dissolution of the Imperial state and the complete removal of TalOS from established doctrine.

Failing this, affect immediate invasion of Cyrod. At no point should the true nature of TalOS be made clear to the populace; a campaign to restructure it as "the ludicrous notion that mortals can become gods" should be deployed.
