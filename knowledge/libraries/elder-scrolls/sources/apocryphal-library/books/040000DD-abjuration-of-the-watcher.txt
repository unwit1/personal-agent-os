<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'>gainst Hermaeus Mora I pen this page, for his is the most wretched and loathsome of visages. Fouler fruits had not grown on rotten trees than the betentacled eye that glowers like a corpse-blossom in the inky labyrinth of his own making.

From Oblivion's firey corpse I hurl my spear at thee, Hermaeus Mora, to whom the most snivelling of idiot scholars and failed savants beg for scraps of forbidden wisdom. Why do these caitiffs scorn Julianos, whose paths of logic require only that one remain steady on the road without debasing oneself in the most obnoxious of rituals?

Scabrous is the Eye that sees and knows but fathoms not, ill-guarding aeons of knowledge behind a simple tyrant's need for worship. Little does he differ from Sanguine the Thirster, swapping only wine for ink to distinguish the malignant pair.

To the deepest pits of the void I condemn thee, Hermaeus Mora, Demon of Knowledge. Against thee I turn thine own plagues! Begone from the world of mortals!
