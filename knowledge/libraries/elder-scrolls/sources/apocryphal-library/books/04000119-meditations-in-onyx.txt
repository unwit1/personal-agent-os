The Nisswo will crack the egg of light-thought and look into the nothing-pool of Raj-Sithis. For this, a black rock of onyx shall be taken and halved. The first half shall be burnished until it forms a black mirror. The second half shall be rounded into smooth black pebbles.

The pebbles are for the hand. They are to be so smooth that they can only be felt by their cold. 

The mirror is for the eye and the mind. It is to be so smooth and so dark as to reflect nothing but the face of the holder.

With these stones, enter a dark chamber. It should have a small amount of light, to the side where it will not show in the mirror, but will light up the face just so much that it can be reflected. There will be no noise in the chamber, it must be silent.

Steady the mirror, not in the hand. Hold the pebbles in both hands, and look within.

Rest your eyes on the forehead of your mirror-self. The feel of the hands will be numb with the stones. Soon all you will sense is the mirror self. Try not to let the gaze slip from the forehead.

The face will change, bit by bit. You will see a skull. Expect nothing from the skull, and in time it might offer. Even the most patient Nisswo will sometimes come away with nothing, and the wise will remember that this is a gift.
