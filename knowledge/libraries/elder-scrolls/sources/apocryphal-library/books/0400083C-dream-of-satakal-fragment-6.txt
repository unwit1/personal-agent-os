I pick up a shard of mirror and look within. I can see only my eye, single-slit and brimming with sorrow. I could hold onto this piece forever, I think, and at least I could see the tears of Akel and remember why I, Satak, still weep. But forever is a long time. No, I must do what Akel once did. I must give my treasure away.

I take the shards of the mirror and I throw them into the void. I sing a single light to shine between them, and the light dances between them. I remember who I am now - Satakal. He is with me again. My nemesis. My ally. Myself.
