"Satak, I would pity you but for your wailing rage. I took nothing from you. Do you not remember giving your treasure away? Do you not see that actually I am Satak and YOU are Akel?" The man who was a serpent laughed, and I felt my being boil.

I raise my fist and strike at Akel at the same time he strikes me. The mirror between us breaks - and I am in agony. My scales bleed, and I can no longer see Akel. I realise my mistake at once.
