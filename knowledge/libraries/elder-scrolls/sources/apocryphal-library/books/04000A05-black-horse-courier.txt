AN EMPIRE BETRAYED
White-Gold Concordat Signed</p>

It was with shock that the Empire learned the truth of the White-Gold Concordat this morning. It had been intended to end the war formally, but the terms of the agreement were much the same as those that the Empire denied before the war's start. As of now, the worship of Talos, or indeed his recognition as a Divine, is illegal. Hammerfell has been ceded from the Empire to the Dominion, and the Blades is formally disbanded as a group.

With the document's signing, the Empire gives leave to Thalmor justiciars to perpetuate religious purges of Talos worship in the Empire. The move has angered the populace, who are glad that the war is over but feel betrayed by the enforcements of terms that were fought so hard to repel.

Hammerfell has rejected the terms of the treaty outright and has declared independence, and is now continuing to fight the Aldmeri legions alone.

This is a grim day - what more will the Dominion demand of us in the years to come?
