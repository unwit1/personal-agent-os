The Valaxian War

by Henatas III Archaeolictor

</p>


[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he late Fifth Era was beset by grievances for the Tamrielic Dominion. Even if one discounts the Ald Cyrod revivalist movement and the so-called 'Ice Numidium', various breakaway factions of Altmeri sciurges had already tempted the values of the Aurielic strand by using them according to the Five Deliberately Forgotten Dischordances. The resulting political turmoil shifted the balance of power from Alinor to the exarchs of White-Gold tower in a manner that they tried to pass off as Neo-Ayleidic, but was too obviously a reminder of the latter days of the Empire of Men. 

Things came to a head when Syanical, the Red Prince of Cloudrest attempted a rebellion that caused the newly-surfaced Tower Edge to decoagulate along the Quion-axis, leading to a breach in the void-membrane. All waited with some concern to see what would emerge.

It was not one of the Sixteen Rebuked Lens-Vassals that shone through in the end, but the Ideal Masters of the transient realms known as the Soul Cairn. We still do not know to this day if the Masters actually inhabit the Cairn, but the focus of their power was able to form a Lorkhanic Ouroboros through the tear and the Masters began the solemn process of performing their necromancy on the Earth Bones themselves.

The war lasted many years, and none can truly agree on the number. In that time, three ancient Ada were awakened who had supposedly perished during the creation of Mundus, and many more could have awoken had the war not come to such an end. Aurbin-Sa, formerly unknown, had died to form sound, and so when she awoke all things became silent except when she spoke, at which point all things screamed at once in her voice. Where she walked, all saw thunder and bone and the rotting carcass of a bellowed order.

Easende-Tamu was the Ada who died to form a binding-concept of corpuscular matter that is still being discussed. It is unknown exactly what he did in death, but in unlife things fell into a ruin like none other. It was neither disintegration nor melting nor a simple collapse, but a rending of concept-matter that left entire zones unsure whether or not they existed.

Aulk-Houleyth was the Ada that formed Down, or Gravity. Upon being awakened, things were no longer separated from the sky by its will, and so the skies and the earth slowly became as one. Things began to lift unto the sky as if by their own volition.

It was ultimately thanks to the Men-of-Weir that the crisis ended. They had been repairing the Battlespire in secret for generations and had retrofitted it with Mzulft Varliance Lenses. By invoking Merid-Nunda at the crucial moment, they were able to focus her energy to lay the revenant Earthbones to rest.

Though the Dominion had done their best to fight the Ideal Masters' forces while this was happening and had won many battles, the presence of the Humans at the crucial moment caused many to rethink the Fifteen and One Prohibitions on Mortal Logic, and the cracks that are now beginning to open in the Dominion are traceable to this incident.
