<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he arrival of the swordsmen from Akavir was greeted at the time with concern, since they were skilled with blade and with the art of the kiai. It is generally taken as simple fact that these skills were sufficient to allow them their early victories. This is false.

Whatever the Tsaesci eat, they may become. They became the Velothi mountains long enough to allow their soldiers to pass. They became the rivers long enough to drown Nord warriors. One devoured an island in the Karth, and never spat it out, and so she remains there to this day.

Their surrender to Reman was a ruse; he was killed and eaten and replaced with a fitting facsimile. All the Reman emperors were Tsaesci; and when they needed to remove the shape of Men from the throne, Reman III killed and ate his own advisor, Versidue-Shaie, acting as his own potentate for years more to come.

The 'last' Potentate, Savirien-Chorak, was targeted by the Morag Tong. Naturally, he killed and ate his attacker, and ever since that moment the assassins have been ruled by Tsaesci. In this manner, their leader plans to kill and eat Mephala, their own patron. Many left the order at this time, and formed the Dark Brotherhood.

Though they can breed, they cannot fully reproduce here, since their eggs - when laid in Tamriel - are lain broken in twelve. They must rely on reinforcements from Akavir in order to maintain their numbers for the few times they do die. It is only thanks to this mercy that we have not all been consumed. 

Be wary of the Tsaesci - they no longer appear among us as they did in the times of the Potentate, but here they remain. They dwell among the nobility and royalty of Tamriel's ruling class, where they are protected by the arms of the Empire. Be vigilant, and trust not a word from the mouth of a ruler.
