<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/B_letter.png'>alance  is the Prime Aurbic Principle; it is the primal force that permits the other forces to create what we know as reality. Change and Stasis are weighed on the scale that allows things to proceed while remaining within their limits.

The word 'Balance' comes from the Ehlnofex term BAL ANTIA, meaning 'opposing stones', as the first scales were made depending on how weight-stones balanced each other out. Compare the etymology with 'Enantiomorph' - EHLN ANTIA MORA, "The profane opposes the profound", or "The Earth opposes the Sea". This term is also important for understanding Balance, hence its mention here.

When the Anuic stasis first came collided across the limina of the sphere of Padomaic change, the initial conceived Enantiomorph occurred. In order to 'be', Anu (who had previously never done anything at all) had to change - to act - and Padomay had to limit that action. Thus Anu and Padomay briefly swapped their appointed roles. In doing so, they created the balance of is/is not that allowed the nascent subsaequentia to materialise. 

The dutiful Psijiic will, of course, always seek to act for the betterment of the world, but will also be mindful of the need for balance. Dire acts that bring Oegnithr into the world should be balanced with good acts, but conflict is a necessity of balance. Without it, a pall of stasis would settle upon the Aurbis, and with it the corruption that comes from inaction. Indeed, the very dark acts that force us to balance them are, by that nature, a necessity as they force a balance to be struck.

Sometimes the balance teeters on a dreadful precipice - at these moments, one must act carefully but decisively to restore it.
