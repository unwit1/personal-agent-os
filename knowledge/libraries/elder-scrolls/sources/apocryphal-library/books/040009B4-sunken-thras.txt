<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>hras has, according to our scouts, risen again from the sea between The Chain and distant Yokuda.

 In the First Era, the inhabitants of Thras unleashed upon Tamriel a deadly contagion that wiped out a great many inhabitants. This 'Thrassian Plague' spurred the All-Flags Navy to attack the nation of the Sload and cause it to sink. Their use of the plague may have been in response to their failure to recapture lost lands - much of what is now Summerset Isle has remains that appear to be Sloadic in origin, and so it is postulated that it may have formed part of Greater Thras in the time before the Altmer made it their domain.

Exactly how the islands were 'sunk' is not clear - Bendu Olo's fleet seems to have used 'terrible magicks' to sink the archipelago. This was believed to have been the end of the Sload once and for all, but it was not.

The description of the realm before the All-Flags Navy is that of a semi-circle of sixteen islands, possibly surrounding a lagoon. This lagoon was described by Altmer captives who managed to flee after they had been captured in a Thrassian raid on Skywatch in 1E1301. 

These were the first reports; subsequent attempts by cartographers have shown other islands, and in some cases fewer. The isle of Agonio has been known to change size over time. Recently it has been sighted again.

In the time before the sinking, there was known to be a huge pillar of coral known as the Coral Tower. This appears to have been their attempt to create a myth-tower such as White-Gold and Red Mountain, though it was destroyed by the navy when it arrived.

The sky-port of Subo-Lathla was one of many areas where the famed gasbag-aircraft were launched from. These acted as airborne vehicles for the Sload, allowing them to attack shipping from above.

But it is Ul'vor Kus that has caused the most concern for our cartographers, for it has never appeared on any map. It is a sunken realm, spoken of only in rumours from the last hundred or so years, inhabited by a different kind of Sload who prefer to remain underwater indefinitely. That said, it may be that these Sload are the same ones who were supposedly 'sunken' by Bendu Olo, and that they have been waiting underwater all this time, ready to raise their isles again to the surface.

We are putting out a warning to all shipping - all sightings of Sload should be reported to coastal authorities immediately.
