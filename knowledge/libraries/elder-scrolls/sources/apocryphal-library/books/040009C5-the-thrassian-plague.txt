<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Thrassian Plague was unleashed by the Sload in the twenty-third century of the first era, though the specific date has not been determined - usually it is estimated between 2200 and 2260.

It first struck along the Gold Coast and southern Hammerfell, spreading to Summurset Isle and the Iliac Bay. As much as half the population of these areas is believed to have perished.

The disease was manufactured by the Sloadic necromancers of Thras, and was sent to Tamriel with the use of infected sea-creatures, such as whales and great cephalopods, which died on the shores and spread the disease quickly to the unwary. Soon, whole populations were dying to boils, brittle bones and other terrible afflictions that misshaped the victims.

With the population thus decimated, the Sload launched coastal attacks and enslaved those who had survived the sickness, forcing them into terrible slavery in their archipelago.

With the aid of the god Syrabane, a cure was eventually worked out. The Warlock's God thus aided Bendu Olo, the admiral of the All Flags Navy, in destroying Thras and sinking it below the waves. Exactly what magicks were used in the destruction of Thras, we still do not know; it is postulated that Syrabane had worked out a spell by reverse-engineering the plague, but this has never been confirmed.

The disease had long-term political effects, and the dissolution of the sixteen states of Elsweyr into just two, as well as the collapse of the Alessian Order, are just two of the great upheavals that resulted from the devastation.
