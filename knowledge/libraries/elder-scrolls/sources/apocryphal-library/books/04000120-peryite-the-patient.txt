<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/P_letter.png'>eryite, Father of Disease, Prince of Decay. Patron to the incurable, shepherd to the sick. It is easy to see how this devil has become seen as almost kindly.

Peryite waits. He outlasts. His plagues do not ravage towns and sunder bloodlines, but they kill. One by one. A few people out of a city. This matters not to Peryite - he has time. He has all the time in the world. For is he not made in cruel mockery of the Time Dragon himself? 

Peryite is the very foundation of the orders of ruin. The violence of Molag Bal and the nightmares of Vaermina all turn on wheels powered by the larger, slower wheel - the grinding-stone of the Aurbis, Peryite the Patient.

With more violent and sinister plots always on the horizon, it is easy for the Daedra Hunter to de-prioritise the destruction of Peryite's cults. This is by design. The crueller-seeming Daedra are part of his ploy, a distraction while he slowly grinds the world and the heavens into the dust from which his kindred are born.

Fight this complacency! Strike at the head of the False Dragon, so that it is the Daedra instead who will rue their smug idleness! When their wheel has nowhere left to turn, the Princes will rue their wicked natures.
