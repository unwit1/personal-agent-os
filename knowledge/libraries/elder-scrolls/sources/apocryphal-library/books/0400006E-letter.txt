Dear Friend,

I thank you for your detailed correspondence on the matter. I have indeed been seeking the very tome you described; though I did not dare to believe how much knowledge it could contain. 

If you are correct, as I have no doubt you are, then such a volume would afford to me as complete a knowledge of the diabolical arts as even the Ars Carnes Ligandi of the mad wizard Salkreeve, or the Animavorial Rites whose author is lost to the ages. I would naturally be willing to renumerate you most handsomely if your efforts to locate this work prove fruitful. The coming weeks will be the fulcrum on which this endeavour turns, and even a glimpse of its dread symbols and portents would further my work by great strides.

If you would please, tell none other that you have even seen this tome. I wish it to be mine. Name your price.

Until then I remain your most humble and obd't servant,

T
