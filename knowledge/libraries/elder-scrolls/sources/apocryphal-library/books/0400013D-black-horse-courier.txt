A MIRACLE OF PEACE?</p>

The warring city-states of the Iliac Bay have finally lain down their spears. This is good news, of course - but alongside the good news, there is the weird news.

Almost overnight, the Iliac Bay has gone from a multitide of minor states to just three - or perhaps four. And very few witness records of the battles that led to this have yet been verified, due to their singular nature.

Ykalo, Alcaire, Dwynnen, Satakalaam - these states no longer have kings. But where does, then?

Daggerfal. Wayrest. Sentinel. And most disquietingly of all? Orsiniuim.

Yes, you read that right - Orsinium is now a functional city-state in itself. King Gortwog now rules with the Emperor's mandate.

"Wait, what?" you surely say.

That's right - the Orcs of the Wrothgarian Mountains are now full members of the Empire. Indeed, the Empire has lost absolutey no influence during this sudden changing of the borders - in fact, it seems to have greater sway over the region than ever. Was this, then, some grand ploy of Uriel VII or his Battlemage, Ocato, to divvy up the region among selected rulers in return for greater Imperial oversight?

We're not sure - the scattered witness reports indicate something very unusual happened indeed. Talk of advancing many leagues in a single night, and how a forest burnt in one half and remained in perfect leaf on the other. Entire armies vanishing. And even whispered reports of a giant, red-eyed and armoured figure seen walking out of the Bay.

The ruling families of the newly-expanded kingdoms have been sought for statements.
