COUNT OF ANVIL MISSING!</p>

Corvus Umbranox, count of Anvil, has been reported missing. There have been no sightings of him by any of the city's militia, nor did any of his own palace guard claim anything to be amiss.

His wife, Milona Umbranox, rules the city as countess. Though clearly distressed herself, she has managed to calm the people of Anvil and assure them that her husband will return, and that all is well within the city until then.
