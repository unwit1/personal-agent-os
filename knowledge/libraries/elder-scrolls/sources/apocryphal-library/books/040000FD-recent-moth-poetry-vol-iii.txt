Some-too-many had seen and spoke
Names of gold and brass and white
Of heart-lost pilgrim (speaks no faith)
Some-too-many named and spoke

A blanket of us, a night-kindness
A kiss from a million wings
Breathing in the name ill-spoken
Goodnight, doom-speaker

Awaken in the silk-egg
Hatch from the sky-moon
Born in the void-sea
Return to the world-skin

Whisper not the secrets of heaven
To the bored and the blind
Heart-lost is there
Heart-lost is listening
Heart-lost is wrathful

We give wing-beat to the silence
And drink from the moonflower
