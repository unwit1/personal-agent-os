These words are set to parchment that the turning-stone might not erase the last group of warnings.

The First Warning: Suffer Not the Man of Brass

Among the droves of the ignorant it has become a rite of passage to build a new life not out of the cadaver decayed but from the remnants of what the Dwemer left before they spun themselves into the spindle aetheric. 
Remember: Brass logic is entombment. Those who have learned it well will remember why the animunculi in Skyrim have soul gems and the ones on Vvardenfell do not. Empty gloves become puppets for inappropriate spirits.

The Second Warning: Do Not Call Forth the Marsh Dead

Raising the dead has a host of setbacks and hazards but most are avoidable. With that in mind, do not invoke the carcass that has become mired in marsh or fen, for it has become a thing of another rot, another death that should not be allowed to become alive. Do not conjure necromancy into the marsh-bitten corpse. You will awaken more than the body. 

The Third Warning: Ignore the Ghost Snake

Only those initiated from birth should even consider interacting with the notion of the Ghost Snake. Everyone else should simply acknowledge that it does not exist and should be ignored. If you are not in Morrowind you should not think about it at all. Do not think about the Ghost Snake.

The Fourth Warning: Do Not Question Daedra About Numidium

Those Dremora and other sapient echelons and castes of the Daedra might know something about Numidium and it is absolutely imperative that they do not tell you. If they think you might be about to ask them, they might answer prematurely, so guide conversation away from this if possible. Do not let the Daedra think about Numidium if possible.

The Fifth Warning: Do Not Make Trade with the Ideal Masters

The Ideal Masters are experts at haggling and any shortfall you make in a deal will be met with your own soul. Unless you have a wealth of souls to offer and ask for relatively little in exchange you will be trapped in the Soul Cairn. There are very few deals worth making with the Ideal Masters so just don't make any.
