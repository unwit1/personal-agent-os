TITUS MEDE TAKES THE THRONE </p>

Following the unpopular rule of 'Emperor' Thules the Gibbering, Colovian noble and renowned former soldier Titus Mede has seized the Imperial City and put an end to the Stormcrown Interregnum. He has declared his new dynasty will bring an end to the infighting that has plagued Cyrodiilic politics.

Broadly supported by the Elder Council, Titus Mede has proven popular among the common folk of Cyrodiil. He is a respected warrior and an able politician, and speaks well in public. His detractors call him a 'warlord' whose army of veterans could barely be referred to as soldiers anymore, but these are minority voices.

Following the Empire's recent difficulties and the loss of Black Marsh and Morrowind, it is hoped that he will reverse our fortunes.
