<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>saesci. You have all heard the word by now. The name of these strange visitors from distant Akavir, who now walk in the halls of our very palaces. Perhaps you have even seen them with your own eyes. Seen these men from afar with their painted eyes and their strange weapons and armour. Perhaps even spoken with one.

Why was it, then, that when word came from Pale Pass that 'Snake Men' had surrendered to Reman, first of his line, and we began to hear tales of folk with scaled skin all over, or tails where legs should be, that we have ended up in the presence of mere men?

The hasty excuses came first: "They were merely mistaken for snake-folk because they wore the scaled armour of great beasts from their homeland." Preposterous! How many good mercenaries of Cyrodiil wear newtscale armour? And while it is true that some wear this 'dragon scale' armour, most wear cuirasses of overlaying plates. 

The truth is this: They are snakes in the guise of men! In certain light you will see scales reflect from their skin. They are capable of taking the skins of those they slay in battle, too - and they can change their shape to how they please! How do we know that our new Emperor Kastav has not already been replaced with these fiends? Or that Reman himself was not changed, and that Kastav is one of these things since birth?

Shed scales found in palace rooms. Bodies drained of blood. Sightings of scaled and tailed beasts that slither away as soon as they are seen. Eyewitnesses say that these foreigners can elicit secrets from them with a stare. 

Be vigilant, my fellow citizens, for this terror hides in plain sight, and among the very highest echelons of our society. Anyone you know could be one of these monsters! Be ready!
