Almalexia and Boethiah

by Mathis Melandryl
</p>
<p align="left">

[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'>t has been five years since our blessed Lady of Mercy last stood in her temple, and I am the last to admit now that we shall not see her golden form again.

Five years since the hushed words on the lips of every ALMSIVI fearing citizen of Mournhold was that Our Blessed Lady had fallen in battle with the returned Nerevar. At the time, we knew this to be false; of course a god cannot fall in battle to a mortal, even one returned by Lord Sotha's own Anticipation.

But as we waited in the High Temple, our patience went unrewarded. Her Hands admitted they had not received word from Her, but would not allow us to enter Her Sanctum. 

By the time a year had come and gone, we knew that the Blight had been lifted from Vvardenfell. We knew that The Sharmat's place in the prophecy of the Nerevarine had been acted out.

We had not heard from Lord Sotha in years. The last news we heard was from Lord Vivec. Neither spoke to us of Lady Almalexia. Eventually, Lord Vivec stopped speaking to us as well. We don't know if that was before or because of our current grief, that caused by Mehrunes' hordes that flow like locusts from the House of Troubles.

The last we knew of Our Lady was this.

She appointed the Nerevarine with the duty to seek and repair the weapon of Lord Nerevar himself, the True Flame, his wedding-gift from Her. 

She bestowed upon the Nerevarine the duty of bringing an end to the madness of Sotha Sil, who She proclaimed had taken to sickness of the spirit.

And it was only after these two edicts had been made that the first rumours began to circulate - that Almalexia was dead.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/W_letter.png'>hat we now cleave to as our belief is this:

The Tribunal wrought their power from the vestige of the Scarab, who is Lorkhan. Under Red Mountain, where they met their Mystery, the Heart of the Scarab beat for an aeon under the auspices of Kagrnak. 

As The Sharmat used the Heart to create the Blight, so did our Lords and Lady suffer and turn in nature. For though They were mighty, They were not the Scarab.

Our Lady killed Lord Sotha. We do not know why. She commanded the Nerevarine to do this work - we do not know which struck the blow - but She made the command. To this end, She instructed the Nerevarine to reforge the True Flame. Her own blade, the Hope's Fire, was able to cleave the flesh of Mehrunes Dagon himself - would that we had that blade in our arsenal now. 
A weapon that could kill a god! If She intended to slay Lord Sotha anyway, why bother getting the Nerevarine to remake the sword? Was it merely part of a show?

Or maybe She realised Her place regarding her returned Love, Lord Nerevar. She had betrayed him, for such is the nature of Her Anticipation, Boethiah, the Prince of Plots, the Slayer of Rightful Kings.

She showed Nerevarine how to make the blade not to kill Sotha, but to kill Her. Such was Her regret, such was Her grief at the fault in Mercy.

The Heart of the Scarab was never meant to be used thus. Trinimac! You beseeched our prophet Veloth not to make pilgrimage to Dwemereth, you who took the Heart from Lorkhan from his bloodied chest! You, upon whom Boethiah took the vengeance.  You, whose only pilgrims are the Pig Children of Malach. You, for whom that fate was the greatest MERCY of all.

Betrayal is a Mercy. Rebellion is a Mercy. Treachery is a Mercy.

Do the heathens think the Temple builds its pillars with three sides, because we had three gods? By the saints, it pains me to think that by the end, they may have been right. We had forgotten. 

Every Tower Remembers A Betrayal

Every Sundered King Finds Himself Among His People

The hand that clutches Hopesfire. The hand that wields Goldbrand.

The block, the cut. Return stance, block, cut. Distract, cut again.

Boethiah's place has been reclaimed.
