The dream is the one I've had before, O mystic. But this time it goes on longer.

I stand in a dark room. I reach for something to sparkle in my hand, but it is not there. It is the one thing I treasured the most. I never knew I wanted it until it wasn't there. I weep in a maze that is but one room in a space that isn't there - and nothing is around me, no direction to turn, but it is from behind me that the laughter comes.
