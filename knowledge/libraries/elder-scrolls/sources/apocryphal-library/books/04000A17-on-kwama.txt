The Physiology of Kwama is unlike that of any other known beast. Its larvae is called the Scrib, which hatches from the large, leathery eggs lain by the Kwama Queens. This scrib is not dangerous usually, but it will defend itself with a paralytic venom if attacked.

Here, the physiology becomes unusual indeed. Once the scrib has pupated in whatever underground lair it deems safe, it becomes a pair of creatures that form one.

The 'Forager' is a maggot-like creature with a sharp-toothed maw. They are quite aggressive but cannot usually manage large prey. However, if they do fell a creature, they will devour its flesh and bring it back to their burrow, regurgitating it as food for the rest of its body. 

The other part of its body is the 'Worker' which, while the forager is out  hunting, will dig away at the rock with its powerful burrowing tusk. The worker has far lower intellect than the forager, an only rudimentary sensory organs.

When a burrow is threatened, the forager will leap into the open 'maw' of the body of the worker, and emerge at the rear end. The whole creature will then rise up on what had been its forelegs but are now its rear legs, and thus one encounters the Kwama Soldier. These are much more dangerous than either its constituent parts, being able to walk on its 'hind' legs but also unleash bolts of static magicka.

In a burrow, one of these soldiers will grow into a Queen. These queens are the egg-layers of the burrows, and their eggs will go on to form a new generation of scribs.
