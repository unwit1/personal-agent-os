Numidium, New Promise, New Light!

Born from the all-giving god, star-wounded Shezarr!
O Mara, who has kept his heart safe
O Stendarr, who turned the gaze of the enemy
O Dibella, whose beauty shines from golden skin
O Arkay, whose wheel turns upon the awakening
O Zenithar, whose labours forged the skin anew
O Julianos, knower of the gears and the cogs
O Kynareth, who gives breath even to bronze
O Tiber Septim, giver of the Totem and the New Heart

Numidium! Golden bride of Padomay!
Numidium! Born in the East and the South!
Numidium! Rise again in the West!

Golden tower of peace and new life, breaker of chains and symbol of man made anew. We chant ourselves into your being, divide us from ourselves, sum of all sums, solution to the ineffable equation, serpent head and serpent tail, hub of all things, the edge of all thought, ancestor-denier, nil-speak, beginner and ender of empires, ocean-drinker, shining star, tyrant against tyrants, devourer of lineage, scarab-crown, mirror of heaven.

The old divines make way to your grandeur
Gladly do they resign their duty
Joyfully do we accept your dominion
The one true god
The awakener
The new law
The endeavour fulfilled
