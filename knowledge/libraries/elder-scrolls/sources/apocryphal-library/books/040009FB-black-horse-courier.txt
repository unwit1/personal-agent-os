SENTINEL SACKED BY THALMOR</p>

The word coming from Hammerfell is dire: Sentinel was attacked in the night by concealed Thalmor operatives. Their target was the refugee quarter where many Altmer dissidents had fled following the Dominion's political purges.

The Legions were slow to react, and by the time they arrived, the Thalmor had fled leaving the quarter in ruins. Only a handful of dissidents managed to survive the attack, and they reported horrible use of destruction magic. Already the cityfolk of Hammerfell are calling it the 'Night of Green Fire'.

"This occurrence will not be tolerated," his Royal Majesty King Thassad IV of Sentinel declared. "Those responsible will be hunted down to the ends of Nirn."

The local Redguard population has set to work trying to rebuild, and the last survivors of the purge are being re-settled.
