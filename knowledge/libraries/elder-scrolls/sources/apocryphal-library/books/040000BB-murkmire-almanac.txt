<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Murkmire is the southenmost region of Argonia, and is formed of the marshlands that surround the Keel-Sakka river. The largest settlement is Lilmoth, which sits on the Oliis Bay.
[pagebreak]
Part I: Settlements

Alten-Meerhleel - A minor trading post on the southern coast, east of Lilmoth. Acts as a trade hub for many Argonian tribes.

Bright Throat Village -  a settlement found in the eastern shore of Murkmire. In Jel it is known as Seekhat-Yol. It is the tribestead of the Bright-Throat tribe, or Wasseek-haleel, who are known across the province as skilled diplomats and mediators. They are also well-known for their woodwork, and the instruments and 'seed-dolls' they make are likewise famous across the Black Marsh.

Dead-Water Village - a settlement in western Murkmire, north-west of Lilmoth. It is inhabited by the Dead-Water tribe or the Naga-Kur, a particularly fierce and bellicose lineage of Naga feared by those in the rest of the region.

Lilmoth - a town at the south-eastern tip of Murkmire overlooking Oliis Bay. The town is well-connected to the rest of Tamriel and serves as a transport hub. It has various facilities and services common to other Tamrielic towns and cities, such as a Mage's Guild and a Fighter's Guild. It is ruled by a council of merchants, and as such is a reliable location for traders seeking to do business from across the continent.

Root-Whisper Village - A village in north-west Murkmire, abandoned after assaults by the Barsaebic Ayleids but now being rebuilt.
[pagebreak]
Part II: Ruins

Blackrose Prison - A former Imperial prison in west Murkmire, now inhabited by brigands and marauders.

The Dominus Fatum - A shipwreck on the Deepmire, known to have been a Dres vessel in the First Era. 

The Ixtaxh Xanmeer - The Ixtaxh-Thitithil-Meht, or 'Exact Egg-Cracker', is a partially-sunken Xanmeer pyramid on the south coast of Murkmire. Believed to have been used in ancient pre-Duskfall rites, the exact purpose of the structure is unknown. It appears to have been a temple, though it is uncertain to which gods.

Lakemire Xanmeer - A Xanmeer located north of the Teeth of Sithis, on the Deepmire. It is in relatively good repair and has been known to serve as a dwelling for Argonian shamans in the past.

Teeth of Sithis - A temple to the god of death situated in central Murkmire. It has been claimed that the temple is home to vampires and undead, and is largely avoided.

The Tomb of Many Spears - A cave found in the far northwest of Murkmire, located northeast of Dead-Water Village. Burial place of the Naga warlord Etra of the Many Spears.

Vakka-Bok Xanmeer - A pre-Duskfall temple within Root-Whisper Village.

Xinchei-Konu Monument - An ancient calendar used by the pre-Duskfall Argonians for astromantic purposes. It is to the north of Lilmoth.

Xul-Thukis - An ancient temple to the death-god Sithis just outside Root-Whisper Village. Inhabited by rogues and brigands.
[pagebreak]
Part III: Natural Landmarks and Locations

Blight Bog Sump - A complex of swampy caves east of Blackrose Prison. It is filled by the Keel-Sakka river and is home to a tribe of Argonians known as the Veeskhleel or Ghost-People.

Deepmire - A large swamp that coverd much of northern Murkmire. Home to dangerous fauna including behemoths, bog blights, voriplasms, and wamasu.

Path of the Lily - A winding canyon that leads west of Dead-Water Village, used by the tribe as a place of training and ritual.

Singing Grotto - A small cave southeast of Dead-Water Village.

Swallowed Grove - A great underground jungle believed to have been buried when a sinkhole's aperture collapsed. It is speculated that there may be old ruins buried in the depths. It lies to the north-east of Blackrose Prison.
