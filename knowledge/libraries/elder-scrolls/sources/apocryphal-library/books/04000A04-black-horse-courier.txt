VICTORY IN CYRODIIL!</p>

It has been years now since the destruction of our offices, but with the return of the city to Imperial hands, and the kind provision of a printing press from the Imperial Offices, we can finally report that the War is Over in the Imperial Province. Lord Naarifin hangs from a cage on the walls, and the Dominion has left the Imperial Province.

Occupation under Lord Naarifin was grim - he was a reviled Daedra worshipper, who performed terrible atrocities on the inhabitants of the city. But after a long siege and a brave attack on the city, Emperor Titus Mede II returned to the city from which he retreated only recently to absolutely crush the Aldmeri forces of Cyrodiil. With only the armies in Hammerfell to contend with, it is believed that the war will be over within the year.

"This is a tremendous day for Cyrodiil!", bellowed his Imperial Majesty to the cheering crowd. "Let us never waver in the face of Elven aggression again. Long live Cyrodiil, Long live Skyrim, Long live Hammerfell, Long live High Rock, all loyal lands of men whose brave soldiers have carried our victory this day. And to you, the loyal citizens who fled from the Dominion, I say this; your courage in the face of oppression is a boon to all, and worthy of the highest Imperial honours."

When questioned about his views on the victory, Lord Naarifin said "get me down from here you witless apes! You will soon witness the power of the Dominion in all its glory. Nothing has changed! You will kneel before the true scions of creation." He continued like this at some length, punctated only by grunts as rotten tomatoes struck him.
