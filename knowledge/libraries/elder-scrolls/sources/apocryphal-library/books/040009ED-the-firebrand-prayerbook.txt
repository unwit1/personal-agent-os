The Firebrand Prayerbook

Compiled by the Knights of the Flame

For the Honour and Glory of Anticlere
<font face="$SkyrimBooks">
<font size="17">
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Mara

Mara Mother, Blessed be
To whom no hearts hold secrets
Grant us purity of purpose
And righteousness of thought
So that our deeds may be done
In love for that to which we have sworn
And that our desires may never be tainted
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Stendarr

Father Stendarr
Grant us the wisdom of the blade
So that our sword never strikes in hate
Our spear never pierces in malice
Our mace never smites in spite
And that we know thy blessed mercy
And only strike to protect that which we love.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Ebonarm

Wise Ebonarm
Knower of the final tactic
Make a shield of our faith
And give us faith in our shields
Grant us courage to protect the land
Let us never waver in battle
May we remember the wisdom of our enemies
As we ensure they remember ours.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Akatosh

O Akatosh, King of Heaven
Remind us never to forsake our duty
And to act with honour in all things
As we ensure our realm lasts for all time
For you we protect our king
For our king we serve you
Forever and for all eternity.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Dibella

O Dibella, Queen of the Opal Mists
Show to us the beauty of dawn
Draw us to the majesty of dance
Grant us the steady hand of art
Let our words flow like poems
And remind us that for all our toil
All our battles and all our strife
What it is for which we fight.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Kynareth

O Kynareth, Mother of the Wind
Bring us fortune in our labours
Let our acts honour nature
And our deeds honour creation
Teach us to heal one another
And to know the wealth of the land.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Julianos

Julianos, Father of Logic
Grant us wisdom in our deeds
Let us learn from our errors
And grant us keen sight
May our memory serve us
And may we teach others 
As you have taught us.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Zenithar

Zenithar, Patron of Patrons
May we never shirk our duty
May we always strive 
May we never leave our work unfinished
May we always endeavour
To honour you and ourselves..
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'> Prayer to Arkay

Grandfather Arkay
Beginning and End
Remind us each day
To live our lives well
So that when our time comes
We may enter your embrace
With no regrets in life.
