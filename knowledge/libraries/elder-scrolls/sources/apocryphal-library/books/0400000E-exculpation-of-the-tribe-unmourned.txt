<p align="center">

Exculpation of the Tribe Unmourned

by Hanau-Bassinanit
</p>

[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
There is no cause more dutiful and solemn than that of the SHARMAT.

And there is no sentence more punishing.

This is the way it should be, and the way it will be again.

In my youth I found a cave, and in the cave was an egg. It was hatching, and a Kwama-larva was wriggling out. Soon it would be out hunting and foraging in the grazelands.

I saw that the maggot was struggling against the leathery shell. And so I reached forward and pulled the egg apart, allowing the maggot to writhe free. But it had not fully grown, and now that it had naught to struggle against, it could grow no more. It died soon after because of my kindness.

The shell is the first test in our lives, and it is the most important lesson: All wisdom and all strength is born from adversity. Even the House-Dunmer and their Temple acknowledge this simple truth, and the four corners of the House of Troubles are fought against every day in fond gratitude.

The egg-shell is ignorant, and so is the SHARMAT. They both think they want to contain the world, but they do not, because they cannot look outward. Nevertheless, they are the force that binds us outward. Without them, we cannot see the world they chose to be blind to in ignorance.

Tomorrow a score of our hunters go to Telasero where the Tribe Unmourned have made their temple. We shall pay our respects to them. We shall thank them and lay the petals of fire-ferns by their corpses once they are dead, and lay a wreath of coda-flowers around the severed head of Dagoth Brevalyas.
