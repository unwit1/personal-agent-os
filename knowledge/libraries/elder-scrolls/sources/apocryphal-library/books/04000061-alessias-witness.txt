"When first I gazed upon the Dragon Queen, I knew peace in war. For all the wheels in heaven, I now only cared for one, the one that spun around all that we did and undid. I could stand alone in the presence of my forebears and see reflected in my blade a dozen mirrors looking back."

- Attributed to Pelinal Whitestrake, supposedly written by his severed left arm

"Such magicks as hers I had never seen before. The Hummingbird Queen of Twyll died clutching her throat when she dared challenge her. We found a row of suits of Saliache armour in perfect marching formation on the road, completely empty, and then saw her horned crown silhoutted on the hill. I swear she could turn the moons red."

- Hanne-Ott, a literate slave freed in the early rebellion

"Oh yes, I very much died by her hand. I think it was hers, anyway. Perhaps it was that knight who was with her, whose helm masked nightmares. It is strange, I saw them as two people in life, but in death's memory they are harder to distinguish."

- Abagravia the Unrelinquished, Ayleid Lich, before his second death

"Alestia seemed most worthy of my respect from the moment I met her - I cannot say why. But she was able to respond to my thu'um when I greeted. How she did that, I cannot say."

- Osreid Hammer-Tongue, Nord warrior

"She was different after the war. I suppose that should not surprise me. Where once there had been a liberator, there was now a woman of pure ambition. She always wore that red diamond afterwards. It made her look different."

- Golat, Nedic warrior
