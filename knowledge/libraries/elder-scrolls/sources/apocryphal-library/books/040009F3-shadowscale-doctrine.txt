To hatch under a moon star-marked by the passing whisper of the thief's cloak is the divinity of all promised birthright. The egg cracks exact.

We are taken into the brotherhood of darkness, and we are deep-schooled in blade and venom and the wicked mysteries of the marsh.

We learn the secrets of bone and ash, of blood-spittle and foaming river-mouth pouring upon golden sands that stretch to island paradises, looking upon a deep forest where sacred boughs sag over the jungles that take us home.

We return home steeped in the pallour-made-red that is the final lesson. 

And so we turn our services to the King of Argonia.

And in his name do we slay.

And at his command do we bring death.

And the last thing we say before we bring smiling-dagger-end is;

"Tell me, can you name a single king that Argonia has ever had?"

Those who can answer may be spared.

None have ever answered.

We are kindly travellers, and we are welcoming hosts. Those are the twin balances of the Shadowscale. The only mercy in this world is that which is up for us to provide. And we do so with care and affection. Ours is a burden rarely shared. 

Murder is the most ancient weapon of Argonia.

Kindness is the keenest edge in creation.

Black Marsh is the reflecting pool of the King of Argonia.

Do not see his face when you stare in the water.
