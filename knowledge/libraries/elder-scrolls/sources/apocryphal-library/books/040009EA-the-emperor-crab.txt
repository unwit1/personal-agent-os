<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/W_letter.png'>hen the nation of Resdayn was young, the number of great arthropods was much greater. In modern day Morrowind, only a few invertebrate megafauna still survive, such as netches, silt striders and the elusive greater shalk. The Blight from Red Mountain has winnowed the once vibrant and bizarre monsters of the Star-Cursed East to a few hardy species.

One such beast, now known only by the shells occasionally found in the ash wastes of Vvardenfell and Narsis, was the Emperor Crab. As large as the temples of the Tribunal pantheon, they were capable of immense destruction and could level entire towns. Their shells were almost impenetrable, and many survived by simply outlasting their Chimer opponents until their arrows were spent, then lashing out with huge claws.

They preferred to live in the shallows of the Bitter Coast, and there are old Chimeri legends of these beasts clashing, visible from far away, their huge claws kicking up the surf as the titans lashed out at each other. Those days are gone now.

They were originally more widespread, and would occasionally migrate across the Sea of Ghosts. In the first era, a shell was spotted near Dawnstar in Skyrim, but it was swept out to sea over the coming years.

The last living Emperor Crab was known as Gah-Skar, 'Great Skar' or simply Skar. Its shell can be seen today in the Dunmeri house-capitol of the Redoran, Ald Ruhn. In fact this shell is of such prodigious size that it serves not only as the council chambers for the House, but also spacious housing for all the council members.

During the Oblivion Crisis, the Tribunal Temple invoked the ancient bond-rites. Necromancy is not completely illegal in Morrowind, and in certain cases the Temple may charge its acolytes to practice it. This was one of those cases, and the great council  hall was raised from its eternal repose to do battle with the onslaught from Oblivion. For one last time in living memory, the Emperor Crab's power was witnessed - and it cut a swathe through the ranks of the Dremora and Vermai, but was unable to get as far as the Portal before succumbing to the wounds of Dagon's most powerful Markynaz generals.

Ald Ruhn was mostly destroyed in the aftermath, but those who survived spoke of the majesty of Skar, risen once more for one final battle. We shall never live in such times again.
