The Calabian Fragments

Compiled by Calabius Hulectis

</p>
[pagebreak]
<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/W_letter.png'>hat follow are rare pieces of text that have been gleaned from Adjacent Place. Most are incomplete, and the full length and nature of these texts cannot be divined. Some have been gathered from divination rituals, others by aethernauts who claim to have been to Lyg, and others that have somehow managed to survive passage from that place - or time - to this. Translation of these latter ones has been a matter of almost impossible measure, for the original text comprises glyphs that even our order's most capable magicians have yet to decipher. Assistance was found from other sources.

Fragment One:
"...icath, where haunts the falling sea, O there she might dance in pale... ...idore, for when he knelt by her, the world came up... ...so same, when walls were imbued, not for want, not for need, but for the threads that held us, so dear, so dear! When in love did want and need have sway? When in... ...paradise of moons intrandescent, for shadows meet in kindly array, when at last the tides of memory surged once more?"

Fragment Two:
"The brine was upon his lips even before he dropped the [untranslateable], and seeing what he had done, he clamoured for... ...begged to know from whence the salt would next flow, for he had none left, and when he [untranslateable] the last from Haeli he staggered away until he [untranslateable]... ...to this day."

Fragment Three:
"Where Fire and Light had faltered, [untranslateable] showed us the substance of her being, and it was agreed that such could indeed be made to form a tower. And we all gave some of it, and many of us died, but we made him larger and larger until he might blot out the sky, for his was freedom-weapon. But our father was enraged, and he banished [untranslateable], hurling her into the void..."

Fragment Four:
"...our legs once we were again in the water. And we chewed them, and made them into something new, for now we were awash with memories, and we ate our legs and made them into..."

Fragment Five:
"...forms shall be like that of the [untranslateable] while we dream, and like the [untranslateable] while we wake. In one, steady and ready, calm and unyielding, and in the other, able and fast. For that is how we shall be remade."

Fragment Six: 
"All stood beside the dying king, and begged him to stay alive, but the blow had been deep. We promised him a better world, but as his eyes turned to steel we felt only hatred."
