WAR ON THE HORIZON
Aldmeri Dominion Makes Demands of the Empire</p>

Yesterday in the Imperial City, a delegation from the Aldmeri Dominion arrived with their demands and a covered cart. 

Their demands? Nothing short of the abandonment of Hammerfell, the disbanding of the Blades and the relinquishment of the worship of Talos!

Naturally the demands were dismissed out of hand, at which point the emissaries revealed the contents of the cart - the decapitated heads of all the Blades uncovered in Alinor.

War can only be around the corner now. Be ready for it when it comes.
