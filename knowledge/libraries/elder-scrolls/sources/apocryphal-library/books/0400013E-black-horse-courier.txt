VVARDENFELL OPEN FOR SETTLEMENT</p>

Since the second era, Vvardenfell - the island at the centre of Morrowind - has remained a 'Temple Preserve', an area protected from the influence of outside interests and colonisation. It has thus remained a cultural icon of Dunmer traditionalism, with limited presence even by the Great Houses, and overseen directly by the Temple.

Well, with the decree of King Athyn Llethan, that has changed.

Already, Imperial interests have sought to capitalise on the vast mineral wealth of the region. A great mountain full of Ebony has been earmarked for immediate extraction, just north of the House Hlaalu township of Balmora. 

This move is likely to excite considerable tension with some of the more traditionalist Dunmer, and already there are murmurs of hostility among both the religious and criminal. 

His Imperial Majesty Uriel Septim VII declared that the Armistice rights of the Dunmer would be respected - but with Khajiit and Argonians among the settlers of Vvardenfell, that seems like it too could be a source of some friction.

The Tribunal Temple has expressed itself in a measured fashion, cautioning the Empire from transgressing the lines already drawn in the sand - but welcoming those of any race or former creed to join the Temple if they feel that the words of the Tribunal speak to them.
