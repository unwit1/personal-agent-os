<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he following poems are a collection of those few poems that survived the Atmoran onslaught of the Snow Elves. Many more such poems must have been lost to the fires of Ysgramor, and so the few that remain are considered rare treasures indeed. May that enlighten the reader as they illuminated the poets of ancient Mereth.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/V_letter.png'>alley of the Hyacinths

O Auri-El innumerable,
How is it that your eyes so blue
glisten from a hundred thousand
Hyacinths, as there they grew
In valleys north and south of
ancient Calmavelde
Do you see our beauty through them?
Do you see our children dance?

We left the beautiful flowers
In the valleys where they bloomed
And rode across the sun to where
Our ancestors, who once dined forever
Lay still and calm under the sound of calling birds.

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Queen of War

I am the blemished ore
Yet of steel my heart is made
I am borne of strife and fire
And hatched in halls of flame
The hammer I abide
The cooling sword grows sharp
The Queen of War is born.
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/L_letter.png'>ament for Mereth

Smoke rises over broken shields
Above Abalavar and Falimendil
The Falmer are fleeing from the Atmorans
They are fleeing like lost birds.

In Saradith and Ungalmilde
The farmers and soldiers gather
They greet one another with tears
And hold their children close.

Is this what you have ordained for us,
O Auri-El, bringer of light?
As the lilies of summer wither and fade,
Will nothing last in our name?
