Religion and Faith among Necromancers and the Undead

But it is not just we, the living, who have such creeds. The Undead, and their mortal Necromancer allies, have patterns of faith also. They are vile and repugnant religions, and the vigilant hunter of witches, re-animators and warlocks should do well to be advised of their wicked nature.

MOLAG BAL is the creator of Vampires. He tortured the young Lamae Beolfag in antiquity, twisting her into the first of his dark children. All vampires - except for the now extinct Ash Vampires of Red Mountain, who were not believed to be undead - are descended, by passage of the blood curse, from Lamae.
Molag Bal is thus the centre of much worship among Vampire kind, and they perform wretched deeds, and inflict agony on the living, in his name. Many a vampire has wielded his Mace, and he is reputed to have a form of priesthood among the parasite hordes. The act of turning corrupting mortals into vampires is Bal's direct challenge to Arkay.

MANNIMARCO is the patron of all necromancers. In life, he was an Altmer, a Psijiic monk whose acts drew the ire of the great Vanus Galerion. He was expelled, and became a foul lich. In the years between then and the 'Warp in the West', he grew in power around the Iliac Bay, making deals with Sload necromancers - such as the dread N'gasta, to whom he taught one of the first instances of the Soul Snare spell, which would raise to undeath any who died on Stros M'kai. 
He disappeared during the consolidation of Wayrest, Sentinel and Daggerfall, and was believed at first to have been slain by the Blades - but necromancers began to worship him not long after. Indeed, the most erudite of astrologers noted that there had been a change in the heavens, and a new moon, that the Necromancers called the Revenant, had taken form. Once every eight days, it blots out the light from blessed Arkay and allows necromancers to perform their rituals outside of his blessed sight.

CLAVICUS VILE, in addition to being a patron to many Cyrodiilic vampires, has been associated with the Soul Snare ritual on its two most notorious uses. N'gasta the Sload is reported to have made many pacts with the fiend, and the destruction of Lilmoth is believed to have been caused by undead under his command. He is likely able to bring people back to life - or more likely Undeath - through his wicked pacts. 

THE OLD GODS of the Reachfolk of High Rock, Skyrim and the Dragontail Mountains are mostly Daedra by other names. Their gravesingers can call vines from the earth to infuse the dead with unlife. Stranger still is the ritual of the Briarheart, which replaces the heart of a Reachman with the head of a wild rose. This reanimates them, but reports differ on whether or not spells usually used to target the undead work on them. Indeed the symbolism of a man having his heart removed is very ancient, dating back to the forming of the world when Shezarr's divine centre was ripped out by the elf-giant Trinimac. A creation of magic in the place of a heart causing a kind of semi-life filled with violence might also be familiar to those who have read stories of Pelinal.

SITHIS, the primal void, born of Padomay, has in his care the souls of all murderers, and all those slain by the Dark Brotherhood. Though the patron of Tamriel's most prolific death cult, he would not be considered anything to do with the undead - save those vampires who heed his shadowy call - were it not for his unusual practice of sending ghosts into the world. 
This is understood to be a rare occurrence, but captured Dark Brothers have admitted, under some duress, that the Dread Father might on occasion send forth a ghostly aspect of his wrath to punish wayward assassins. One report even tells that an assassin was blessed with the company of a ghost-murderer, one of Sithis' accomplished killers who was to serve again in death.

THE IDEAL MASTERS are mostly unknown, but fragmentary texts that survived the destruction of the Imperial Battlespire indicate that they inhabit a realm of Oblivion far from the clutches of the Princes. This 'Soul Cairn' as it is known is where the Masters keep the souls of those offered to them, for whatever purpose. They are made of crystal and can communicate without speaking. They are not so much worshipped by necromancers as bargained with - the dark wizards offer them souls in exchange for terrible power. Many have been recorded as making such deals in the past, but few have ever been witnessed who have made use of the vast power that they supposedly offer. Thus it is believed that only a few are capable of making such deals without falling prey to the Masters themselves. Such a focus on careful bargaining, and the theft and trade of souls, has led to the belief that they are somehow related to Clavicus Vile.
