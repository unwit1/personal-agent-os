<p align="center">

Ashes in the Wind

By
Gordregh gro-Zogh

</p>
[pagebreak]
<p align="left">

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'> rest my head upon a bed of stone.
I set my eyes upon the wind-wrecked skies.
I fill my lungs with air of ash and bone.
I breathe out dust and close my weeping eyes.

And in my dreams I see a palace grand
With towers white, all reaching for the clouds
And elven maidens dancing on the sand
Gossamered in silver-laden shrouds.
[pagebreak]

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'>nd when the clarion battle-horns then sound,
I arm myself with silver helm and sword
And ride to fate, to ruin, to pride, to war
Against the wretched Doom-Drum's mortal horde.

The memory grows softer every night,
Of pale-green elven banners all around
Of how I thrust my sword with all my might
And wrought the Man-god's heart unto the ground.
[pagebreak]

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/A_letter.png'>nd took it, held it high for all to see
And fixed it to my pennant, green and grey
Look on me, swathes of Men, my mastery!
Look on me, and be driven far away!

And as I hold my standard in the air
My cheering cohort all in gold arranged
And when I wake, I pray they'll still be there
But when I do, I see that nothing changed.
[pagebreak]

<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/I_letter.png'> walk alone through valleys heaped with dust
I walk alone across the trail of years
Heaped with swords all given low to rust
And naught to drink but wretched salt-wracked tears.

O you who have been scorned and spurned as I
Take you my mantle, and my mighty Scourge
Take you Dwarf-Hammer, hold it, raise it high
As I command, you feel my ancient urge
[pagebreak]
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/S_letter.png'>trike down the foes of those who have been spurned!
Of those reviled, mistreated and maligned!
Remember when the mortal legions burned!
That it was I who pierced the Doom-Drum's hide!

Today, I wake in fields of dreary ash,
The patron of those broken, yet thick-skinned
The story of that youth is shattered glass;
Scattered far like ashes in the wind.
</p>
