FLOATING CITY - Fact or Fiction?</p>

Recent reports from Black Marsh indicate that Lilmoth came under attack recently. Long since regarded as the last bastion of the detractors of the An-Xileel government, the Empire is regarding this as a political purge.

However, strange stories have emerged from locals. They claimed that the dead rose from the ground to attack them - and some said that many of the risen dead were people who they had seen alive and well not hours previously.

While Undead attacks are commonplace across Tamriel, it is rare for them to attack a settlement. Perhaps An-Xileel necromancers sought to overthrow the settlement? Not everyone thinks so.

"There was a great mountain in the sky. Yes, you heard me right," said a local hunter who refused to give her name. "Like the peak of a mountain, upturned. I swore I saw buildings on it. It was trailing lightning over the city. It was terrible."

She wasn't the only one. At least three other survivors also claimed they had seen something big moving in the sky, which had prompted them to flee.

Of course, these have been roundly dismissed by others. "If necromancy is involved, as it seems to be, there can be unusual mental effects on those not directly affected," said Battlemage Dagail of the Synod. "Seeing unusual things during such encounters is not unheard of."

Magister Hierem dismissed the rumours as nonsense. "My aides recently returned from a trip to Black Marsh, and reported no such things," he stated. "These are the imaginations of people in the panic of a political coup. The reality of the situation is that this was a motivated attack by magicians of the An-Xileel."
