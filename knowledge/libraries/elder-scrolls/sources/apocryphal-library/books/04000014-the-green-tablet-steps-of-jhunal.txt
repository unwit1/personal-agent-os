It is so that Hylja the Mystic, who is I, came to the great cave that cannot be found within the completeness of the World Throat, and there was a chasm that was all around and within my hand. Before me in the light of his lantern stood a man robed in grey, and he did hand me the tablet of jade that he held, and this is what was written on it.

One; Truth and exactness are found only among the stars.

Two; For the stars are above, they make us in their image, thus what is above is surely below.

Three; Thus it is so that we speak only truth to Heaven, and hear only lies in return.

Four; Wisdom stolen and hidden is the purview of the Woodland Man.

Five; Without the lie there is no tale, and we have no other place to be. Thus do we repeat the lie out of love for what the gods have given to us.

Six; Time is coiled like a wyrm, and has no true place of beginning or ending. Look to the Oracle Iridescent, the Star-Thane, the Leaper-King and the Hungry Man as proof of this.

Seven; The tower built of stars was a ladder for the sun.

Eight; The tower built of fire was a seed planted by the earth.

Nine; The tower built of snow was finished tomorrow.

Ten; The tower built of gold is the mirror of the sky.

Eleven; Lies always tell their own lies, you must see through all of them but the first.

Twelve; When you learn the first lie, believe it as if you knew it.

Thirteen; Know and become the balance between all forces at war, as the Now is the balance between past and future.

Fourteen; It is because I know this that I am called Jhunal, Father of the Three-Sided Truth.

Fifteen; This is the sixteenth step.
