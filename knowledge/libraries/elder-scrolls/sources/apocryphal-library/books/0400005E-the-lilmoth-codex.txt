The world is born from twins. Stasis and Movement. Order and Chaos. Together they bore something hideous and malformed. It is a wheel.

The movement of the wheel is the marriage between stasis and free movement. It is not still, it always moves, but no point ever gets further away or closer to its hub. It will always find itself back in the same place eventually. There are no new places for any point on the wheel to go.

Consider a snake that, having shed its skin, is stuck in a loop because it tried to eat its own tail. It will slither forwards forever, and never know that it is not moving at all. Sooner or later it must tear free.

We are on a wheel. Sooner or later we must tear free. The thing that imprisons us is not the wheel itself but the hub. The hub renders our position exact forever. 

It should surprise none that the breaking of the hub has been tried before. It has succeeded before. It has never given the wheel what it wanted.
