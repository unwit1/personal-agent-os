ELSWEYR NO MORE
Province split into two Dominion Client States</p>

Following the fifteen years since the end of the Void Nights, the inevitable has finally come to pass. The last Imperial offices in Elsweyr closed up very suddenly and the remaining legions retreated into Cyrodiil following huge gatherings of Khajiit warriors.

Elendril of Shimmerene, a spokesmer for the Aldmeri Dominion, stated yesterday morning that the Dominion would 'Gladly welcome the states of Pellitine and Anequina into the Aldmeri Dominion, effective immediately'. Diplomatic negotiations with the Dominion have since been suspended.

The future does not look good, citizens of Tamriel. Look to the defence of your lands, and be prepared to train up for the legion. Times of war are not far beyond now.
