Thrice-cut from the sheet-silk
Spread over the bone-cast
Wrapped twice round the flesh-spine
Slept twice in the earth-clutch

Woken in the stone-white
Broken on the slab-bright
Sing shade to the sunlight!
Bring crystal-like-eyesight!

Thousand-strong wing-brood
Bathed soft in the silk-shroud
Brought song to the Mugwort
Wrath to the wing-cloud

West to the grave-fast
East to the birth-seam
North to the ice-cast
South to the mind-dream 

Wing-glyph like circle-keen
Spindle-like-worm milk
Spread on the hive-white
Shown on the green-silk

Fed to the shoal-husk
Thoughts for the egg-tongue
We are hatched from the mind-sac
And give birth to the wind-song

Lost in the mind-nest
Blink last the light-eye
Take once more from the cloth-west
Give east to the day-sky
