WAR IN ARNESIAN JUNGLE</p>

Yet more unrest in the provinces - this time, it seems that the death of a Dunmer merchant in Arnesia, northern Argonia, has prompted an invasion of the province by Morrowind's armies. Argonia has long been a source of slaves for the Dunmer of the Great Houses, whose right to practice slavery is one of the controversial, but firmly defended, pillars of the Armistice.

The Merchant, whose name is recorded as Roris, was allegedly captured by 'Argonian Sorcerers' and forced - unsuccessfully - to recant the three god-kings of Morrowind. Already, the native temple is considering whether Roris should be considered a martyr of the faith.

With only pockets of ill-equipped tribesfolk to hold the line against the House armies, the war does not look good for the citizens of Black Marsh. It is a common observation that Morrowind overemphasised the death of Roris in order to affect a great abduction of slaves.
