All except the one where Ruptga first crushed Sep with the stick. That one still lets the light of the world in. It is the sun that shines down on me. A tree grows beneath it. I lie beneath it, and I fall asleep.

And then it is as with all the dreams. I wake up below the stars, in the cool sands of the Alik'r. But something is new and different about tonight, mystic. There is a blue star in the sky. I have never seen it before.
