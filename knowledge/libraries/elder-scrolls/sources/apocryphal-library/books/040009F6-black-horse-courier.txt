SUMMERSET ISLE SECEDES FROM EMPIRE </p>

With the Elder Council distracted by Emperor Thules' recent attempts at reform, eyes were turned away from trouble in the Summerset Isle. The Royal Families of Alinor were overthrown in a coup by the violently secessionist Thalmor faction, who have given the name 'Alinor' to the entire province. 

The Elder Council is struggling to come to a decision on the correct course of action to take. So far, Alinor has not indicated that they are sending a diplomat to the Imperial City.
