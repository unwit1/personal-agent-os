Cults of Tamriel

The Black Worm

by Gwendoline Denwick
</p>
<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Order of the Black Worm is perhaps the most infamous of all cults within Tamriel's history. Renowned for their servitude to its leader, the dreaded necromancer Mannimarco, they have been responsible for several dire acts since their founding in the second era.

Mannimarco, an Altmer of the Psijiic Order, had learned terrible soul magics that could be used to trap mortal souls to further ones magical research. Vanus Galerion attempted to stop him from doing so, but Mannimarco had already built up a network of anchorites and eremites who would aid him in performing his research in secrecy. Thus was born the Order of the Black Worm.

Perhaps the most egregious act of the Order was the assistance of Molag Bal during the poorly-remembered second era event known as the Planemeld. In return for power over the dead, Mannimarco and his followers aided the Prince in attempting to drag the whole of Cyrodiil, and later Tamriel as a whole, into the Oblivion realm of Coldharbour. The plot was eventually thwarted by a gathering of vestige-souls who sought liberty from Bal's torment.

Over the coming years, the Order would oppose the Mage's Guild, all the while presenting Necromancy as a reasonable school of magic to all others. Indeed, necromancy became quite commonplace across Tamriel in the following years.

During the Warp in the West, the Order sought to gain control of the Numidium on behalf of Mannimarco. It seems they were partly successful - They proclaimed afterwards that their lord had become the 'God of Worms', and that he had become a new moon to blot out Arkay's will. Indeed, astrologers did note a new celestial body appear in the sky around this time.

The final known act of the Order of the Black Worm occurs during the Oblivion Crisis, or around that time. Hannibal Traven, Archmage of the Mages Guild, had banned necromancy for good. Though it remained legal in Tamrielic law, it was now persecuted by the Mages, and thus all necromancers were exiled from the Arcane University.

This act granted Mannimarco a fresh gathering of eager faithful, and in that year he was able to launch a full scale assault on the Mages Guild, killing Traven in the process - though the mortal form of Mannimarco was slain as well. To this day it is unknown if he still lives on as the Necromancer's Moon.

The structure of the order was well-documented. There were necromancers, and the most talented would become anchorited. There were also Eremites, who were members who became undead themselves.

Since the order's collapse in the Oblivion Crisis, rogue bands of necromancers have continued to plague the old ruins and caves of Tamriel. Whether there is any leadership among these brigands is unknown, but the Necromancers' Moon is still witnessed by astrologers today.
