In the seventeenth year of Jarl Ingesried's reign, the war between Nord and Dragon had almost come to a close. It was decided by the remaining priests that the knowledge preserved for the time of Alduin's return must be preserved in a sanctuary.

And so the great dragonstone was entrusted with the Red Destroyer, whose unrelenting rage was a force against the rebellion of men. For his loyalty he would receive life everlasting.

He had slain two hundred at the battle of Hanmar's Steading, but he had been mortally wounded by Hakon the One-Eyed. He had the last rites of the Snake spoken to him and he was entombed in the great city whose location overlooks Whiterun. 

To preserve the secrecy of the stone, which has upon it the secrets of dragon burials, the dragon priests devoured the name of both the warrior and the city, so that now the warrior is now only remembered as the Red Detroyer or the Guardian, and the city is now known only by the name of the waterfall that cascades down from the snowmelt.

This knowledge was passed to the court of Jarl Ingesried by a warrior who had turned away from the worship of dragons but had stayed to spy on the priests. The warrior's name shall be stricken from the record as was the name of the city and the guardian.
