<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/V_letter.png'>aermina, the Prince of Nightmares, whose purview and whose reach is the thoughts and dreams of mortal minds.

Be not fooled by her lack of corporeal servitors (for indeed, she does have such, and uses them when needed) - for the horrors she conjures into dream are vivid enough to do just as much harm. Do not take this warning lightly.

Her followers claim that all the world is a dream in the mind of a drunken god, and that as her power grows she will change the world-dream itself to that of her own making, rewarding her followers with great power. Of course, the promises of a Prince should never be trusted. Their gifts are every bit as envenomed as their curses.

Guard well against the Dreamweaver. Her passage can be observed when nightmares come as plagues to a local population, to a small village or township. When such occurences arise, rally the people to hunt down her cabals. There is no hope for the world if her insinuations of the dreamer are true, though it would do well to remember that falsehoods that cause fear are her very worst weapons. Be brave, be resolute, and the Lady of Portents will have no purchase in your mind.
