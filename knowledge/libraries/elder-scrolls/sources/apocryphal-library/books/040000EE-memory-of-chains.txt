<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he Saxhleel are victorious in Mourning Hold and Necrom. Tear is a ruin and belongs to the Marsh. Spears and flags are raised over the mushroom-forts of the east.

"They struck us in the back while we reeled from the Red Year!" weep the Dark Elves. Aye, you struck us in the back while we toiled. Have you forgotten?

"The Hist have commanded them to do this, they lack their own will," comes the angry rejoinder. Nay, what the Hist want us to know, we drink of our own will. What we drink from the Hist is memories. This time they showed us the memory of chains.

"The Argonians have invaded us! They have rampaged over our lands," sputter the last few plaintiffs. Nay, you think we invaded? Have you forgotten? I drop my quill in disbelief!

We were already here, Dark Elf. We were taken here in our multitudes, and forced to work your egg-mines and saltrice-paddies. We were already here, Kuuda. We have devastated in the South and the East. Is that not where most of us were slaves?

It is not that none of us crossed into Morrowind. Of course! Many of us had left after your Hlaalu-king ordered our shackled to be struck from us. Many of us were just coming back to where we had been born, far from the Hist. The Hist to whom we came to drink our first memories and take our true names.

Many of us were just coming home. Many of us had stayed. We were already here, Dark Elf. But yes, it is true we did bring friends. It is also true that our slavery had been broken by the Hlaalu-king, yes. Only five years before, mind you. 

Most of us have come home now. All those born in Morrowind have now come to drink of the Hist. It has been a long home-coming. I rest my eyes in memory.

The egg above Vivec City has hatched, we hear. That is what birthed your crisis. Such woe for you - until it isn't. Your two-faced god has cracked her egg exact, we hear. Good for her. Enjoy the Duskfall, you will be better for it soon. 

I rest my quill in black ink.
