These are the records of those who died in outright Dismay, but for whom great stones were raised anyway.

For Romerius, who fled the goblins from the Pale Pass, who is remembered as having made good his escape until his foot slipped and he did fall and so was his doom made known.

For the elf-children of the Autumn Field, who fled from the blades of their most ancient memory but were cut down also.

And for the nameless Northman who returned from his flight to fight once more, and gave these words: Noble Nord, remember these words of the hoar father: Fear not the specter of death, for he is the herald of glory and your guide to great Sovngarde.

One is buried atop the mound in Bromjunaar, not deep below. The other was entombed in the city whose name has faded, where crones go to die. The last was lain to rest where the greatest of the Tongues lost his life.
