<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>he war between Elves and Men began in Saarthal during the Merethic era. In this instance, the Elves attacked Sarthaal and drove the Men back to Atmora, whereupon they remassed and returned.

In the First Era, with the Snow Elves by now more or less hunted to extinction, war began in Cyrodiil. This time the conflict began at Sardavar Leed, in what is now the Great Forest. The Ayleids were ultimately toppled by Queen Alessia.

The linguistic adjacency of 'Saarthal' and 'Sardavar Leed' would be a good deal easier to overlook were it not for the historically similar events that took place there. In both instances, a bitter war broke out that began with Elven hostility and led to the virtual erasure of that particular group of elves over the eras to come by the armies of Men. This coincidence is too canny to be ignored.

Given the cyclical nature of time, it is likely that there existed - in the Dawn Era, or perhaps even before - a place where the war between the Ehlnofey factions began, a mythical 'Sardalia' or similar of which Saarthal and Sard are but historic echoes.

Tracing back to this mythical place is probably impossible - if it did exist, the shards of it are likely scattered to the far corners of Tamriel by now. But it is not out of the question that such shards could be found, and with the correct divinations it is conceivable that they might reveal old secrets.
