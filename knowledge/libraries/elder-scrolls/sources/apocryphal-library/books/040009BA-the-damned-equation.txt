The 'DAMNED EQUATION'

Being the most exact projection of the divine calculation of Julianos of the Green Tablet Understanding

Know him by his twin nemeses: The Comforter of Men, and He Who Holds the Paper to the Light.

Where JULIANOS said: Let the light be conferred with all who are drawn to it, as is the moth to the flame, so HERMAEAMORA said: Let the words of poets be strung into prophecy made currency.

Where SHEGORRAT said; I shall sunder all things in two, be they hearts or be they men or be they memories of brass. And if that be so, then all things may thus be divided by that number.

ONE is an ill-conceived concept, do not think on it. Dwell on Zero if thou must.

TWO is the house from which the divine problem arose.

FOUR is the Writer of the Scroll, the Seer of the Scroll, the Thief of the Scroll, and the dead man who never read it.

EIGHT is the twin-concept in ill-confusion rending sky from ground, as above, so below.

SIXTEEN is four problems for four corners of a troubled household. 

EIGHT is the force of the razor against the light of the sun.

FOUR is the mirror-hope when the zeroes come apart.

TWO is front and back of the diamond-twin who were never meant to face.

ONE is the empty tower looked upon by skulls.

JULIANOS SAID: All sacred things come in threes. The mad logic of squares is a hidden spiral. The eight towers assemble the sky, the ninth rejects the lust of eternal dream.

SHEGORRAT SAID: All things will come to divide by themself.

HERMAEAMORA SAID: That which can split without reason is the first weapon of divine logic.

JULIANOS SAID: I so arrange the children of the sun so that their confluences be numbered twelve.

HERMAEAMORA SAID: I halve them into Six-and-Six.

SHEGORRAT SAID: I halve them into Three and Three.

JULIANOS SAID: Witness the three-angled truth. You could have split and mended for aeons if not for my vigil. But I am here, and how will you divide that three?

And neither of them would divide it by three, because that was a number of sacred logic and of all the saints of the void, it was only known by that number of them; and they became the Hope and Fortunes of the Changed Ones. 

JULIANOS SAID: Try and divide two, you will not, because in the absence of the audient void, love overcomes rivalry. Two will always make three and not one. This is the last and final understanding of divine numerologia. 

MERUNADAGON SAID: Divide a two into one, and I will split that one, for my word is the razor that is the balance between all things. If I can split a one I can split a three. Witness my word.

JULIANOS SAID: But when one is split in two, there is two. And the two will make three.

MOLAGABALA SAID: I will erase down to one. That one shall look upon the void alone.

JULIANOS SAID: And when it sees the void, who looks back?

MALAKAT said nothing, because he was the fourth of three, and he knew how the conundrum worked, and what would happen to the mathematurge that thwarted it.

JULIANOS SAID: The simplest calculation of zero is remove one from one. It is only a zero you can add to the true integer. The result is the same regardless of the understanding used to misreach it. Thus I declare the triangle to be logically indivisible in all matters, and thus I give strength to the diamond that it maybe never be halved into the unreachable origin. And with that god-truth I give hope to mortal reason which is the wellspring of all new laws. This is my divine proof and it shall not be undone by false houses.

Therefore know this, he said: Two and three are most sacred because they form five, which is all the extremities of creation. Three and Five are eight, which you had tried to reach by the violence of doubling instead of through the wisdom of marriage. Eight and Five and Three are Sixteen, from which all the harmonies and vividness of creation flows back into itself where once it had only known the agonies of self-piteous hunger.

Thus was solved the impractical theorem by the divine witness.
