A skeleton walks into a tavern. It orders a tankard of ale and a mop.

A zombie walks into a bordello. It leaves a tip.

Q: Why were the Dwarves so bellicose? 
A: Because if they weren't they'd have been called Dpeaceves.

Q: Did you hear about the couple who accidentally swapped round the carpenter's resin with Uncle Regrezio's Special Lubricating Love Salve?
A: All their furniture fell apart.

A wizard from Wayrest goes travelling. While on his travels, he finds himself in a tavern. Here, he says: "I say fellows, I have a good joke about folks from Northpoint. Who would like to hear it?"
The bartender glowers and says, "Look mister, I'm from Northpoint. That bloke in the corner is from Northpoint, and my sister over by the fire is also from Northpoint. Are you sure you want to tell your joke?"
"By the Divines!" said the Wayrest wizard, "Not if I'm going to have to explain it three times."

"Build a mer a fire, and he will be warm for a day. Set a mer on fire and he's warm for the rest of his life" - Dunmer Proverb
