<p align="left">
<img src='img://Textures/Interface/Books/Illuminated_Letters/T_letter.png'>o Ismere Fair, when fed with wine,
We make this sweet and sombre lay
For once this frozen realm was thine,
Of Good King Harold's distant line
Until your dying day.
When Aldoeyn, World-Eater roared
And fell unto thy mighty sword
And there was Shor Hoar-Father stooping low
To lend his graven firebrand to thy pyre
And even Kyne Sky-Mother shook her bow
And breathed in all the ashes of thy fire.

But lo! That distant, dark Red Mountain blighted
Where beat the heart of Shor for ever-after!
Under the hammer of the Dwarves dark-sighted
Against whom ashen-elves would be united
'Gainst the Dwarf-lords and their wizard-crafter!
Then Shor, Hoar-Father breathed unto the ground
And said "I make you Askelde-man to bring
My judgement to the elf-thieves in their mound
Where tricksters build them weapons made of sound
Before the altar of their brazen king!

To Resdaine, thus, did King Ismere
Unite his banner-host of old
To once-more strike forth Nordic fear
Into elf-hearts! To persevere 
Where walk the brave and bold.
Verindigoth and Nerevant
Two elf-knights did together wield
The gleaming-blades, Trueflame and Sunder
Against good Ismere's crown of thunder
Never would one to the other yield. 

But heark! That great volcanic chasm which quavered
Down the red hill across an ashen cover!
A giant knight, unholy and ill-favoured
As e'er beneath fair Mara's moon was savoured
By elf-blades shrieking for their devil-brother!
And in that chamber, Nimidune the elven giant
Roared against Atmoran-King defiant
Whose sword of thunder tore the giant in two,
And gave the Heart of Nirn to ancient Shor!
And in that moment all Nord-warriors knew
The cause for which the Ash-King rode to war.

But Nerevant, the elven-knight undaunted
Bravest of his kind, the rightly-vaunted
Though wounded thrice by elven coward
Turned Trueflame upon the Heart-now-Haunted
Thus the Dwarf-king's armies were so scoured.

The ashen-dust of Askelde-King
Across the ands of Eastmatch flew
Where shone the rising dawn of spring
Beyond where the hoary Greybeards sing 
Athwart a sky of blue.
