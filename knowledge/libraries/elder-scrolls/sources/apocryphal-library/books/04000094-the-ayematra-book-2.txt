AE EL MARA LEXIA, great is the nurturing word. 

The truth builds up behind a lie as does the water behind a log-dam, beloved Paramour. What has been spoken falsely shall be sounded a hundred thousand times louder by the truth when the lie breaks. 

O Hortator, you would have cursed at your skin had it turned to ash! Yours is the most princely of mercies, that which I now carry down from Red Mountain with the tears of your ash-wracked mourners. Most sorrowful of all widows am I, for there was never a flower more beautiful than my Hortator. Yours forever is the golden reflection of the sun on your everlasting skin - the beauty never-ageing from the dawn until the dusk.

With peerless sorrow I take the seeds and plant them in the West. O Boethiah, your golden scythe dripping with blood and venom! Take this blade, dimly reflecting yours in the tear-stained waters of Odai, and return it to my husband. 

Look upon my Ordinators, Paramour. Nowhere in the Mourning Hold may any one turn without looking upon the features of Nerevar, the Spear of Resdayn, Eternal Hortator, the Sky-wounded Lover.
