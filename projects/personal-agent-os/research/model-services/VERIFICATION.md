These are independent spot checks of the findings the operator asked to verify. They were made by the coordinating session (Claude Code) against official pages, separately from the Codex research batches. Where a batch disagrees with an entry here, this file wins, unless the batch cites a newer official page. If so, record a `contradicted` finding.
