# Rithrannir

Form ID: 0534DF60
Editor ID: dealsfleshmerchant
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Thaer's Family.

Rithrannir says he was crippled, unable to work, and dying of disease before becoming stronger through the Family's transformation.

He now processes animal remains and Forsworn gear and sells the results in Markarth to raise money for the Family.

His testimony adds a surface-market component to the cult's economy: hidden flesh-magic practice is financed partly through ordinary resale of scavenged/processed material.
