# Regalt

Form ID: 05371973
Editor ID: dealsregalt
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary location: Witch-Hunter's Guild

Regalt is the aging "guild master" of Skyrim's informal witchhunter hub.

## Organization model

Regalt repeatedly puts "guild" in scare quotes. He says the base is an old skooma den used by itinerant bounty hunters rather than a formal chartered organization.

He defines a witchhunter as a bounty hunter specializing in witches, warlocks, Daedra, werebeasts, vampires, and other undead.

Joining requires no formal oath: new hunters are told to behave themselves, take a key, and use a starter chest.

Income comes from:
- standing bounties described in hunting manuals;
- intelligence purchased from Grados;
- killing targets and returning trophies.

## Relationship to other anti-monster groups

Regalt considers the Dawnguard effective but too specialized toward vampires.

He considers the Vigilants of Stendarr more religious than militant and better at investigation than extermination.

These are Regalt's professional comparisons, not neutral rankings of the factions.

## Regional hunting knowledge

The Skyrim hunting manual is explicitly abridged.

Regalt says the full manual contains the same kind of hunting material for monsters found in High Rock and Cyrodiil, but those entries are omitted locally because copying irrelevant material takes too long.

He encourages experienced hunters to cross Skyrim's borders if they want unfamiliar prey.

This is useful continuity evidence that the witchhunter profession and its accumulated field knowledge extend beyond Skyrim even though the local "guild" is informal.

## Training

Regalt is a retired field hunter who says age pushed him out of active hunting.

He trains Block, Heavy Armor, One-Handed, and Two-Handed combat for payment in silver nuggets, with practical instruction focused on economy of motion, armor fit, and unconventional use of large weapons.
