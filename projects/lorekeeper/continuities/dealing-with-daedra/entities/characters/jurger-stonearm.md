# Jurger Stonearm

Form ID: 05371969
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Family / God-Mother cult
Role: military organizer

Jurger says he fought in the Great War, lost his family to Forsworn, and survived a severe wound through the Family's holy flesh. He directs raids against Forsworn and recruits outsiders through combat service. A successful Kolskeggr Mine operation earns flesh extract and Family initiation.
