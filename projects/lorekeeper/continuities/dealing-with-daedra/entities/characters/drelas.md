# Drelas

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Base-game identity extended by mod-added lore.
Primary home: Drelas' Cottage northwest of Whiterun.

Drelas is linked by the mod to both advanced alchemy and the Book of Curses.

## Alchemist

Arcadia says Drelas once ran the shop that became Arcadia's Cauldron and that she learned from him as his assistant.

She remembers him as brilliant but increasingly consumed by transmutation research. He eventually sold the shop to finance more experiments and withdrew to a remote cottage.

Nurelion's letter confirms Drelas corresponded with the White Phial's proprietor about transmutation and Curalmil.

Drelas's unsent response claims he had already achieved a proof-of-concept and wanted Curalmil's work to refine it.

## Book of Curses

The Vampire Master identifies a Dunmer named Drelas at the same northwest-of-Whiterun cottage as a Book-of-Curses page holder/participant.

Another Master line explicitly refers to Drelas among "the other players".

The combined identity evidence is strong enough to normalize these references under one Drelas dossier.

## Reliability

Arcadia is a retrospective personal source and strongly skeptical of transmutation.

Drelas is an invested researcher making extraordinary claims.

The mod does include a "Transmute?" spell/tome, so the proof-of-concept claim has some mechanical convergence, but Drelas's speculation about lost Curalmil research remains speculation.
