# Thaer

Form ID: 0537196A
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: flesh-magic researcher associated with the Family/God-Mother cult.

Thaer is unusually explicit that much of the surrounding religion is useful social framing around an experimental program rather than proof of its theology.

INFO 053A95DC describes five flesh-magic components: meat, blood, bone, breath, and soul. Thaer's system often substitutes a Daedric vestige for a mortal soul in constructed forms. His own work inverts conventional construction by starting with existing living forms.

The God-Mother is an experimental protoplasmic organism derived from a volunteer and capable of producing polyps. The cult worships it, while Thaer treats it primarily as research.

INFO 056E009E classifies practical flesh magic as a Restoration-adjacent discipline. Thaer describes extraction as analogous to alchemy, says magicka can substitute for some biological processes inefficiently, and uses implanted polyps as ritual/magical foci.

Thaer also researches the Deep Ones and hereditary transformation. His conclusions become more tentative when he moves from observed flesh changes to ancient origin stories.
