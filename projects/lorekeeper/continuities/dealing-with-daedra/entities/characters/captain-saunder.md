# Captain Saunder

Form ID: 05656DE3
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: owner/captain of the High Tide

Saunder says the High Tide was once a merchant vessel and now serves as a floating inn.

With only one cabin available, he offers long-term lodging in exchange for resolving his dispute with Raven Rock guards. The ship also retains a crew-only gated area.

His dialogue establishes the High Tide as a functioning lodging business and multi-character social hub.
