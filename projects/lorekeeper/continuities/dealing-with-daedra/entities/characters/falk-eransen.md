# Falk Eransen

Form ID: 0537196F
Editor ID: dealsdeepmineboss
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: public owner/operator of the Gilded Mine.

Falk is the mine's outward-facing manager.

He repeatedly tells visitors that the mine is closed and denies reports that it is exporting ore.

Despite this, he hires outsiders for tasks that can be completed away from the mine, including killing Falmer/Chaurus and recovering related material.

Other sources independently report that the formerly exhausted corundum mine began exporting gold and that its workers became reclusive.

Falk's dialogue is therefore best treated as the public cover story of the Gilded Mine operation, not as reliable evidence that extraction has actually stopped.
