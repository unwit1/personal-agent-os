# Wanders-In-Tatters

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location/network: Ratway

Wanders-In-Tatters values cure-disease potions highly but wants escape from the Ratway more.

He trades a cure-disease potion for a Steel Sword, saying he would rather attempt a new life as a bandit than remain trapped below Riften.

His exchange illustrates the Ratway's scarcity-driven local economy.
