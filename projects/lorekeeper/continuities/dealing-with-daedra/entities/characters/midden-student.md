# Unnamed Midden Student

Form ID: 057E83A9
Editor ID: dealsmiddenmage
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location context: the Midden beneath the College of Winterhold.

The Student is conducting secret, unauthorized magical research.

They attempt to prevent an outsider from passing because discovery would trigger investigation, then offer passage in exchange for testing a variant charm spell.

The encounter can be resolved through:
- threat/violence;
- a 2,000-septim research bribe;
- consent to the experiment in exchange for ordinary Charm scrolls.

The Student's behavior should not be generalized to the College of Winterhold. Their own dialogue indicates secrecy, rule-breaking, and fear of oversight.
