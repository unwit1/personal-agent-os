# Talmeth Dres

Form ID: 05787B56
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: hired Dunmer witchhunter.

Talmeth is a deliberately abrasive specialist whose dialogue provides a heavily biased Morrowind/Dres perspective on Skyrim's supernatural landscape.

He uses bound weapons despite hunting Daedra and abominations, arguing that many supernatural targets resist mundane weapons, bound weapons are comparatively predictable conjuration, and there is poetic value in turning conjured Daedra against Daedra.

Talmeth identifies himself as only notionally connected to Great House Dres after the Argonian invasion and Red Year disrupted ancestral holdings. He came to Skyrim because Morrowind is diminished and the work pays better.

His comments on slavery, Argonians, Khajiit, Reachmen, Orcs, religion, and House Dres are strongly prejudiced character testimony and should not be used as neutral lore descriptions.

His practical witchhunter observations, by contrast, are useful evidence for how this mod imagines a professional monster-hunting trade.
