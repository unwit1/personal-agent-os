# Skegor

Form IDs: 05919A6D and 0591EB77
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Skegor leads a small occult social group built around rebellion, intoxication, taboo-breaking, and accessible magic.

He recruits newcomers with food/drink contributions, then offers forbidden magical instruction in exchange for blood.

## Vestige demonstration

Skegor kills or uses a chicken as a vessel and explains that the result is not necromancy. He says:
- a slain Daedra's vestige persists while waiting to reform;
- his ritual summons a discorporated vestige into the animal body;
- blood weakens the Aetherial skein;
- the ritual circle provides conjuration protection.

He speculates that vampires' blood use, undeath, and Coldharbour connection may help explain why they often become effective necromancers. This is explicitly his speculation.

## Probable Bashnag connection

An intelligence bulletin says one of Bashnag's underlings broke away and began recruiting rebellious youths by showing off learned occult techniques. That description closely matches Skegor's group, making the identification strong but not explicit.
