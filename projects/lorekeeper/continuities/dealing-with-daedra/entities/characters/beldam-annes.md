# Beldam Annes

Form ID: 05371962
Editor ID: dealswitch1
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: senior North-Jerall witch.

Annes defines Beldam as an honorific for the oldest or most knowledgeable witch of a coven/cell. Her North-Jerall tradition operates in small cells and is explicitly associated with Hircine, lycanthropy, hunting, and alchemical use of prey.

INFO 0572C7F9 describes witchcraft as a philosophy rather than a spell school: practitioners manipulate or twist structures already present in the world instead of relying only on magicka to impose change.

INFO 0572C7F8 gives Annes's taxonomy of four regional/traditional witch groupings: North-Jerall, Reachmen witches, Glenmoril, and Darklight. This is valuable in-continuity ethnography but partisan testimony about rivals, not a neutral encyclopedia entry.

Her quest arc uses the player to learn rival techniques, redirect blame, weaken cooperation between competitors, and push Glenmoril influence out of Falkreath. Relevant INFO records include 05708A9C, 05708AAD, and 05708AB2.
