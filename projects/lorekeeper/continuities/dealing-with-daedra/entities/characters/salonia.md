# Salonia

Form ID: 05353071
Editor ID: dealsfoodvendorflesh
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Thaer's Family / God-Mother cult.

Salonia sells fruit and vegetables to the faithful.

She says she miscarried her first child and was told she could never have another, then credits the God-Mother's blessing with restoring her fertility.

She describes her second child as healthy but marked by many "holy mutations" and says the child now lives below as part of the God-Mother. She hopes to bear a third child to offer.

She also says the faithful are unworthy to consume meat, explaining her produce trade.

Her healing/fertility explanation remains devotional testimony; her beliefs and role in the cult's food economy are direct character evidence.
