# Skaafin

Form ID: 05861E2F
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: Clavicus Vile-associated intermediary.

The Skaafin handles wish-style bargains and repeatedly emphasizes their ironic or self-defeating consequences.

In INFO 0589F8C2 it confirms that Clavicus Vile personally granted Rorik's bargain. If the player breaks it, the Skaafin predicts failed soil, economic collapse, starvation, bandit occupation, destruction of the village, and eventual repetition of the bargain by a future founder taking the name Rorik.

Other wishes grant warrior, thief, or mage skill while immediately framing each boon through its likely drawback.

The Skaafin also distinguishes an ordinary mask depicting Clavicus Vile from the actual Masque of Clavicus Vile, showing the mod deliberately differentiates symbolic replicas from unique artifacts.
