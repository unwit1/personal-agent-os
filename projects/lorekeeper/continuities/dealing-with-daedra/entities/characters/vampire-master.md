# Vampire Master

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary NPC variants: dealsvamplord1-8
Primary location: Master's Lair

The Vampire Master is a Vampire Lord, blood-magic teacher, slaveholder, and participant in the Book of Curses contest.

Multiple NPC forms represent the same narrative role across quest states, so the dossier treats them as one character role rather than eight separate people.

## Control model

The Master deliberately rejects ordinary vampiric enthrallment for important servants. He says vampiric thralls are too rigid and may regain will when they turn.

Instead he uses a bespoke Curse of Obedience that leaves enough autonomy for complex work while enforcing standing behavioral commands.

He permits the servant to remain mortal or become a vampire:
- as a vampire, the servant is more dangerous;
- as a mortal, the Master can feed from them.

## Book of Curses

The Master is a selected participant in a contest over the Book of Curses. He was given the spine, uses scrying on the remaining paper fragments, and sends servants to recover individual pages from other holders.

He clearly expects the completed book to give him substantial power.

## Blood magic

He teaches:
- extracting life force;
- concentrating life force in blood before bloodletting;
- absorbing that energy with an enchanted weapon;
- using one's own life force directly to heighten physical performance.

The teaching is mechanically consistent with other Dealing with Daedra systems that treat life force as a transferrable magical resource.

## Later plans

On loyal/deceptive branches he leaves Skyrim after assembling the Book.

He says he intends to go to Cyrodiil to negotiate with the Order Vampyrum and leaves instructions to exploit conflict between the Volkihar and an emerging vampire-hunter organization.

These are the Master's stated plans, not an established later outcome.
