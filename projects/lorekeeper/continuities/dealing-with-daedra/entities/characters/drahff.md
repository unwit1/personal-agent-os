# Drahff

Form ID: 056C19AE
Editor ID: dealsDrahff
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location/network: Ratway
Associate: Hewnon Black-Skeever

Drahff helps maintain a barricade blocking access toward the Ragged Flagon. He says the blockade is primarily directed at the Thieves Guild, not every traveler. A stranger may negotiate passage through payment or repeated drink favors.

The barricade is mechanically consequential: attempting to clear it without their cooperation causes conflict.
