# Sadren Sarethi

Form ID: 0595195C
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: independent Wispmother researcher.

Sadren is an academic researcher whose methods are invasive and dangerous. Her core experiment suppresses a mortal assistant's spirit, conjures a weakened Wispmother into the body, allows memories to blend temporarily, then banishes the spirit and interviews the assistant about acquired memories.

She admits one earlier assistant died and others suffered psychological trauma.

Sadren's findings include:
- a hypothesis that some Wispmothers were priestesses in life;
- ritual/necrologic use of juniper berries and Namira's Rot;
- a nontrivial affinity with Ice Wraiths;
- enchanted ice functioning as a soul vessel;
- a possible similarity to magical ice from Solstheim.

She also claims ancient Nords developed a system for binding Snow-Elf lich-like enemies into enchanted components placed in warded housings. The Pale Blade/Frostmere material is treated by her as evidence for this model.

These are research conclusions from artifacts and memory-sharing experiments, not automatically objective ancient history.
