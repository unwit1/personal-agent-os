# Mr. Aedwatch

Form ID: 05272C96
Editor ID: dealsbenefactor
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Aedwatch is a wealthy magical financier/collector associated with the Rift Bank and Mineral Exchange.

The bank teller says he made a substantial fortune trading gems and gold in Alinor and moved to Skyrim during the Thalmor political rise.

Aedwatch treats fame as an economic asset. He offers the player permanent magicka-enhancing crystals in exchange for publicly associating his name with heroic accomplishments. He will not explain the crystal process and prevents intact crystals from leaving his control.

His curator accepts unusual objects and Dwemer arms/armor for the collection but says Aedwatch is not interested in Daedric-affiliated items.

The Aedwatch-associated note Expectations addresses magically bound servants whose duty is to collect exceptional objects across Skyrim. It explicitly rejects Aedric and Daedric artifacts as too dangerous.

A Dagon cultist claims Aedwatch's flight from Alinor was an evacuation and repeats a rumor tying Aedwatch's father to an older Crystal Tower incident. This is adversarial rumor and is not treated as established biography.
