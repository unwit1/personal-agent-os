# Bjar

Form ID: 0534DF5C
Editor ID: dealsdeepmerchant
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Gilded Mine / Brethren underground society.

Bjar formerly smelted mine ore for export.

He now buys Dwemer arms, armor, and raw metal, smelts or repurposes them, and sends material below to outfit troops.

He asks suppliers not to destroy active Dwemer automata unnecessarily because the constructs kill Falmer.

Bjar's role shows that the Gilded Mine cult converts nearby Dwemer material into military supply while treating surviving automata as useful anti-Falmer pressure.
