# Redguard Dwemer weapons expert

Form ID: 05660FEF
Editor ID: dealsinnboatredguard
Display name: Redguard
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Associated hub: High Tide

An unnamed Redguard weapons expert from Hammerfell researches Dwemer technology for possible reverse engineering before another Thalmor invasion.

He searches Solstheim's Dwemer ruins for an artifact described as the "black doom" that casts fate as arrows. The recovered target proves to be literally a bow.

He also carries a Dwemer Automaton Deactivation Rod. It works on mainland-Skyrim Dwemer automata but fails on Solstheim machines. From that failure he proposes that Dwemer control sequencers differ by regional or design tradition.

Reliability: high for his mission, equipment, observed rod failure, and artifact search; medium for the generalized regional-sequencer theory.
