# Gatekeeper Enchanter

Form ID: 0557C32D
Editor ID: dealscursedconjurer2
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Gatekeepers

An older Gatekeeper cultist specializes in Enchanting and preparation of the organization's rings.

He trains Enchanting for filled Black Soul Gems and commissions books/archaeological work.

Most importantly, he says he mounts prepared gems into Gatekeeper rings but receives the gems already enchanted from the portal.

He can also grind the gems into tattoo ink. According to him, the resulting tattoo produces a stronger effect than the removable ring but is effectively permanent.

This character is the clearest technical source for how the Gatekeepers turn portal-supplied patron material into wearable or bodily magical enhancements.
