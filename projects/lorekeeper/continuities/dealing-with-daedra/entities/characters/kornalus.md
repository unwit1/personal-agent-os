# Kornalus

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Race/source description: Altmer researcher/wizard.

Kornalus experiments with magically enhanced frostbite spiders.

An intercepted Reach letter says he was expelled after causing a spider infestation, then created a force of tame spiders with elemental cloak spells and captured a strategically important fort.

Kornalus's own letter to Servos says:
- he previously relied on the Whiteridge Barrow imbuing chamber;
- without it, his spider enchantments are less potent;
- he has demonstrated the viability of fully grown frostbite spiders instead of small albino specimens;
- the reason for scaling up is greater combat effectiveness.

The two sources independently support the same research program from external and first-person perspectives.

Unresolved:
- identity of Servos;
- identity of the "caller" who expelled Kornalus;
- exact size of the spider force.
