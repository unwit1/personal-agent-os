# Unnamed Witchhunter — Toruld encounter

Form ID: 055AEE8E
Editor ID: dealsvigilantwitchhunter
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Witchhunter's Guild

This Witchhunter appears at the end of Vigilant Toruld's radicalization branch.

She kills Toruld and says she had been hunting him since he killed two women. She specifically accuses him of soul trapping two people.

Those victim details should remain attributed to her unless another source independently confirms them.

## View of the Vigilants

She argues that:
- a proven Daedra-worship case would have generated an official sanction notice filed with the Jarl;
- absent that process, the alleged victims were legally innocent;
- Vigilants are too dependent on Stendarr, atonement, and predictable procedure;
- practical monster hunting requires decisive violence.

Her own contrast is that prayers do not save people from monsters, while armed hunters do.

## Recruitment function

After the confrontation she identifies the Witchhunter's Guild and points the player to its outpost west of Whiterun and north of Lake Ilinalta.

The encounter therefore provides a direct ideological comparison between the Witchhunters and Vigilants rather than only a description from Regalt.
