# Bookie Herod

Form ID: 056BC76B
Editor ID: dealsratwaybookie
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location/network: Ratway

Herod is a bookmaker to whom Snap-blade Lorenz owes money.

He threatens consequences for nonpayment but offers to forgive the debt in exchange for locating his predecessor's hidden stash. He believes the Sewer King knows the location.

When he realizes the player manipulated the skeever pit and Sewer King to obtain the information, he accepts the result because the stash exceeds the value of the debt and lost betting business.
