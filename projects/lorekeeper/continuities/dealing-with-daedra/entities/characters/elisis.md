# Elisis

Form ID: 059EADA3
Editor ID: dealsfleshapprentice
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Elisis is a flesh mage studying eternal youth and health on Solstheim.

She is interested in Black Books because they contain large quantities of knowledge, not because she presents herself as a Hermaeus Mora worshipper.

## Network connections

Elisis says her former flesh-mage tutor still recruits apprentices and directs the player toward Thaer's Markarth-Warrens recruitment route.

She also directs the player toward the abandoned hunting lodge northeast of Rorikstead, saying a ritual site in its basement can greatly improve magical ability.

That referral independently connects practical flesh-mage information networks to the Gray-Wave/Hermaeus-Mora site.

## Synthetic organs

In a later collaboration, Elisis attempts to reproduce the properties of a high-quality elven organ inside raw flesh extract.

She describes the result as a "synthetic organ" and is willing to manufacture more in exchange for completed potions.

She ultimately wants enough alchemical knowledge to remove dependence on both Elynea and Thaer's God-Mother flesh-extract supply.

This makes Elisis important evidence that flesh magic is not merely cult ritual: it can be developed toward repeatable biomedical/alchemical manufacture.
