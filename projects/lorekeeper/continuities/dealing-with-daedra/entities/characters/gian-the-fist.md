# Gian the Fist

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Location/network: Ratway

Gian owns the special gloves Mojari wants.

He offers the gloves either for substantial money or for killing Knjakr, whom Gian says murdered and ate a friend. He requires Knjakr's hat as proof.

Gian therefore links personal revenge to the wider Ratway favor chain that ultimately controls route access.
