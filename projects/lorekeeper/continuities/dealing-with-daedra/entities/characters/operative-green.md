# Operative "Green"

Form ID: 0589A75D
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: Syndicate field boss/handler.

Green represents the operational side of the Syndicate and contrasts himself with Carsten's business-management style. He permits coded notes, pays in trade or money, manages smuggling/recovery work, and coerces captured interferers into service through fabricated or accumulated criminal leverage.

Green calls Carsten one of Skyrim's most powerful men even before counting his Daedric powers.

## Carsten and Bashnag origin testimony

Green says Carsten and Bashnag were friends and cofounders. According to rumor, they found a witch who taught them a secret Daedric summoning ritual that empowered both men. Bashnag ran field operations while Carsten ran business. After a falling-out, Bashnag left with the strongest spellswords.

Green says Carsten later granted similar power to especially useful employees, but stopped after the Bashnag split.

This is insider testimony with a stated rumor component. A separate precursor mage independently describes a Falkreath witch, a shared power ritual, a coup, catastrophic Daedric release, and survivors forming a criminal empire south of Kynesgrove, providing strong partial corroboration.
