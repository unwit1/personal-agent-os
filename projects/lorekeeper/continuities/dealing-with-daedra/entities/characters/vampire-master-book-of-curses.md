# Vampire Master — Book of Curses arc

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary dialogue manager: QUST 0550C6B0

Several NPC forms represent the Master across quest states. Treat them as one narrative role unless a branch specifically distinguishes a form.

## Power-for-freedom bargain

The Master tells the recruited player that he is a Vampire Lord and that the agreed trade is power for freedom (INFO 0553A0F9).

He initiates the player into blood magic by carving symbols into their flesh in a ritual circle (0553A0FA).

## Curse of Obedience

When the player tries to leave, the Master reveals that he applied a Curse of Obedience while they slept (0553A0FE).

Quest spell 05525C54 defines the active restrictions: the victim cannot harm the Master or fellow slaves, must keep his secrets, and must follow orders.

The Master explicitly prefers this curse to ordinary vampiric enthrallment because he needs autonomous agents and believes vampire thralls may regain their will if transformed.

## Blood-magic instruction

As the player progresses, the Master teaches/rewards:
- life draining;
- blood concentration that recharges enchanted weapons when spilled;
- self-powered physical enhancement;
- additional curse magic from the reconstructed Book of Curses.

His teachings consistently frame blood magic as applied manipulation of life force, with Health functioning as both magical resource and risk.

## Evidentiary note

The Master's practical techniques are backed by spell records. His theoretical explanation of life force should remain attributed to him unless corroborated by other continuity sources.
