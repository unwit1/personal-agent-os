# Aslfur the Wise

Form ID: 05371975
Editor ID: dealsscholarupstairs
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: overseer of the Skyrim School of Julianos

Aslfur describes himself as both scholar and priest, charged with overseeing the School.

## School network

He says the Skyrim School is the only School of Julianos in the province, but several exist in Cyrodiil and High Rock.

Many additional schools were destroyed during the Oblivion Crisis. The surviving institution is still reconstructing cultural, historical, and geographical records lost in that destruction, including material concerning Bruma.

Aslfur emphasizes that proper regional documentation requires exhaustive fieldwork and that the School has relatively few members.

## Library role

He calls the School the largest general library in Skyrim, while acknowledging that:
- the Bards College has a stronger artistic collection;
- the College of Winterhold has a stronger magical collection.

The School permits public reading but does not loan its books.

More committed students/members can receive food, lodging, and access to rarer works.

## Membership and service

Membership is earned through demonstrated scholarly service rather than simple payment.

Aslfur's recommended work includes:
- restoring damaged books;
- collecting current hold/court records from stewards;
- teaching children and the next generation;
- advising Jarls;
- mastering skills so knowledge can be passed on;
- travelling between courts to learn, teach, and consult.

Players seeking more militant service are directed downstairs to Keeper Harorn and the Knights Mentor.

Aslfur therefore represents the School's civic/archival wing while Harorn represents its militant counterpart.
