# Lunglef

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
State-specific NPC records: 05698D6B and 05698D6C.

Lunglef is the spouse generated by the Widows of Mephala marriage/betrayal branch.

He first appears around the Solitude inn and responds to the player as an earnest romantic prospect. Drinking accelerates the courtship.

After the marriage branch fires, Lunglef remembers:
- exchanging vows;
- the player saying "I do";
- a heavily intoxicated but genuine wedding;
- consummating the marriage.

He describes it as the happiest moment of his life and treats the relationship as sincere.

That sincerity is narratively important because Lysara's ritual depends on betrayal rather than a merely staged marriage.

The player is later expected to kill Lunglef and remove his heart as the first spouse-sacrifice to Mephala.

Lunglef is therefore less a lore expositor than an evidentiary anchor proving that the Widows' ritual deliberately manufactures and exploits genuine trust.
