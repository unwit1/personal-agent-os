# Plautis Vulpin

Form ID: 0534DF59
Editor ID: dealssmugglervendor
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary vessel: The Spurned Lover

Plautis is a professional smuggler and shipping intermediary whose dialogue maps the mod's mundane black-market logistics.

He moves people and cargo between Skyrim, High Rock, Solstheim, and coastal routes while competing with the East Empire Trading Company and local commercial fleets.

## Blue-goo route

Plautis knows the route supplying Grados's precursor/"blue goo" project.

He reports that the usual courier fled Skyrim after the Vigilants discovered the operation and escaped hidden in a shipping container bound for High Rock.

Plautis refuses to take over the production operation because the risk and need to establish roots in Skyrim exceed the profit.

## Wider network

He pays for Velehk Sain's charts, investigates disrupted overland shipments, checks exchange rates at the Rift Bank, and tracks a Windhelm shipping family whose expansion undermines smuggler demand.

Plautis is useful evidence that occult materials in the mod depend on conventional transport, currency exchange, bribery, maritime competition, and refugee/smuggling infrastructure.
