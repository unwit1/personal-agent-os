# Vigilant Toruld

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: Vigilant of Stendarr whose anti-Daedra campaign develops into a Soul Cairn schism.

Toruld begins as a conventional Vigilant trainer/organizer. His later dialogue shows a collapse of faith in Stendarr and a decision that mortals need stronger patrons to fight Daedra.

INFO 055B916C-055B916E describe unnamed powers in the Soul Cairn that command armies of souls and oppose Daedra. Toruld adopts these powers while insisting they are not Daedra. He later justifies permanent enslavement of souls as punishment or strategic necessity.

Other records refer to rings from "the masters" that steal souls and raise the dead, while Gatekeeper research independently identifies the Ideal Masters as the best guess for Soul Cairn patrons who barter in souls. The identification of Toruld's patrons with the Ideal Masters is therefore a strong cross-source inference, but it is still an inference.

Toruld's arc deliberately mirrors the corruption he opposes: anti-Daedric absolutism becomes coercive necromancy and soul slavery.
