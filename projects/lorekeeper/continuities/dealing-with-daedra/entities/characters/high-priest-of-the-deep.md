# High Priest of the Deep

Form ID: 0537196D
Display name: High Priest
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Affiliation: Brethren / Deep Ones

The High Priest oversees sacrifices, administers oaths, and preaches Deep-One doctrine. He directs initiates to the Bible of the Deep and says repeated offerings earn gold and access to oaths of secrecy, loyalty, and lineage. He says the oaths progressively change the initiate and that the lineage oath culminates in a child.

Operational instructions are high-confidence cult practice; broad metaphysical claims remain sectarian theology.
