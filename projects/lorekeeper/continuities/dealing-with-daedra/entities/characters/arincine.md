# Arincine

Form IDs: 057D9026 and 057D9028
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Role: surviving Darkling witch of Nocturnal.

Arincine says the Darklings were once a substantial witch tradition devoted to Nocturnal. As Nocturnal's power waned, their magic weakened and many converted to Hircine. She says her former Darklight coven ultimately followed that path, while she left rather than convert.

After the decline, she attaches herself to Riften and the Thieves Guild/Ragged Flagon as the closest surviving Nocturnal-centered social structure.

Her initiation test uses theft, debt payment, and method of execution to classify the recruit into three paths:
- Strife — violence/assassination from darkness.
- Shadow — stealth/theft.
- Subterfuge — misdirection, social manipulation, fear, and confusion.

The ritual is performed at the Shadow Stone at night and summons a Shrike. Ichor then changes the recruit's magical relationship to darkness/night.

Arincine later comments on Nocturnal's power waxing again and suspects Mercer's abnormal luck/power and Guild leadership are related to the earlier decline. This is her inference, not independently established by the mod's records.
