# Frysla

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary arc: Book of Curses / Vampire Master recruitment

Frysla acts as the recruiter's public-facing entry point into the Vampire Master's blood-magic household.

She approaches prospective recruits by identifying dissatisfaction and desire for power. Her offer is unusually explicit for a Daedric/occult recruitment path: magical ability in exchange for freedom and service to her boss until released.

Key evidence:
- INFO 0553A10D — frames the player as someone who wants power.
- INFO 0553A10F — offers magical ability.
- INFO 0553A111 — says the price is "Freedom".
- INFO 0553A112 — compares the service obligation to joining an army.
- INFO 0553A116 — sends the recruit to a magical rune behind the stables.
- INFO 0553A109 — later insists that she warned the player about the price.

After the Master accepts the player, Frysla treats enslavement as a legitimate bargain rather than deception.

Frysla's role is therefore recruitment and normalization: she makes the initial exchange sound voluntary and familiar, while the Master's later Curse of Obedience makes withdrawal impossible.
