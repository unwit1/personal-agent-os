# Morrowind brothel Madam

Form ID: 058F0E16
Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Madam manages the slave-brothel state reached from the Sanguine-affiliated party branch.

She explicitly tells the player:
- they are in Morrowind;
- the location is a slave brothel;
- the player's new "job" is sexual labor;
- food and treatment depend on compliance/work;
- release is tied to earning/completing the required value;
- some equipment used by staff/clients was made by House Dres.

The implementation stores the player's possessions and restores/releases them only after the slavery-release state is completed.

Her dialogue is direct evidence for the coercive labor system. It does not by itself identify the wider ownership structure of the brothel or prove direct House Dres institutional control.
