# Gatekeepers

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary hub: Yorgrim Overlook
Key speaker: senior Gatekeeper Cultist (Form ID 051D00DC)

The Gatekeepers are a necromantic service/trade organization built around exchange with powerful Soul Cairn patrons.

They explicitly describe their crypt as "a place of business" rather than a temple. Members offer souls, filled Black Soul Gems, unusual soul-trapping tools, and important artifacts in exchange for enchanted equipment, necromantic instruction, and power over death.

## Core practices

- corrupt Grand white soul gems into Black Soul Gems;
- fill Black Soul Gems with mortal souls;
- study enchanting from recovered magical items;
- learn patron-taught conjuration through Grave-Draught;
- create or command wraiths;
- bargain for armor, staves, and necromantic abilities;
- research historical necromancers, artifacts, and soul technologies.

The senior cultist distinguishes:
- ordinary reanimation using Daedric vestiges;
- reanimation using mortal souls;
- direct soul manipulation;
- their own altered patron-infused wraith system.

## Organization culture

The Gatekeepers are strikingly transactional. The cell leader says members can leave once they decide they have gained enough, because satisfied customers are part of a good business relationship.

This does not make the system benign: murder, soul enslavement, black-soul-gem production, and deliberate cultivation of novice killers are normalized as supply-chain problems.

## Patron identity

The cell leader says the patrons live in the Soul Cairn but admits he does not know exactly what they are. His best guess is one or more Ideal Masters.

That identification remains a strong faction hypothesis rather than an explicit revelation.
