# Witchhunters "Guild"

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Key source: Regalt, Witch-Hunter's Primer.

Despite the name, Regalt says the organization is not a formal guild. Its base is an old skooma den used by itinerant bounty hunters.

The profession specializes in witches, warlocks, Daedra, werebeasts, vampires, and undead. Income comes from standing bounties, trophies, private jobs, intelligence, and salvage.

Regalt distinguishes the profession from:
- Dawnguard: effective but overly specialized toward vampires;
- Vigilants of Stendarr: more religious/investigative than exterminatory.

The Primer supplies the profession's field doctrine. It favors ambush, ranged weapons, anti-magicka measures, wards, silver, cure-disease supplies, and identification of targets through physical trophies, ritual tools, rumors, and local disappearances.

The manual is also culturally biased. Its claims about creatures and rival professions should be treated as hunter doctrine rather than neutral natural history.
