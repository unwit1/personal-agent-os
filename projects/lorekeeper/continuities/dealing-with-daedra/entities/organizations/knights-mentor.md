# Knights Mentor

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Key source: Keeper Harorn.

The Knights Mentor are a militant order dedicated to Julianos. Harorn says the modern order differs substantially from its original Iliac Bay incarnation, but preserves its religious purpose.

Their duties are the same as other Julianos servants—preserve knowledge, advance learning, nurture wisdom—but carried out militantly. Harorn explicitly authorizes force against those who burn books or suppress free thought.

## Font of Shadow

The Skyrim School contains a Font of Shadow connected to similar fonts across Tamriel. These fonts act as repositories for "Shadows", which the order can draw on during need.

Members carry signet rings that capture Shadows and return them to the Font.

The order teaches Shadow magic through the School's book-slip system and trains members in Alteration after gallant service.

The metaphysics of Shadows are documented separately under shadow-magic-and-fonts.md.
