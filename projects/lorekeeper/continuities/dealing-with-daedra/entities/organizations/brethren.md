# Brethren

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary hub: Gilded Mine.

The Brethren are a subterranean religious society centered on Deep Ones/Udyrlistig, hereditary transformation, ritual sacrifice, mine labor, and controlled membership.

## Elite theology

The High Priest presents the Deep Ones as benevolent and says their lineage offers an escape from ordinary aging across generations.

Initiation is structured through three escalating oaths involving:
- secrecy;
- loyalty/service;
- lineage and deeper biological/religious commitment.

Sacrifices and service are treated as routes to stronger blessings.

## Coercive operational reality

The Brethren Priest gives a darker account of how outsiders are handled.

People who discover the cult are not free simply to leave.

They may be assigned to:
- tunnel digging;
- mushroom tending;
- the farm;
- the blood-table.

Good service can eventually earn initiation.

The organization therefore combines sincere religious aspiration with a coercive labor hierarchy. Elite doctrine and captive experience are both necessary to understand the faction.
