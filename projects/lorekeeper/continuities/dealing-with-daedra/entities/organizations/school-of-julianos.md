# School of Julianos — Skyrim

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Key source: Aslfur the Wise; Restoration Instructions.

The School exists to gather, preserve, restore, and disseminate knowledge. It combines temple, library, archive, educational institution, and advisory service.

Activities include:
- restoring damaged manuscripts;
- teaching students and children;
- collecting current records from hold courts;
- advising Jarls;
- maintaining rare collections;
- granting membership through scholarly service;
- rebuilding historical/cultural/geographic archives destroyed during the Oblivion Crisis.

Aslfur says this is the only School of Julianos in Skyrim, with several counterparts in Cyrodiil and High Rock. He describes its collection as Skyrim's largest general library, contrasted with the Bards College's arts focus and the College of Winterhold's magic focus.

Restoration Instructions emphasizes preservation of original material, matching historical substrates and inks, marking speculative reconstruction, and maintaining copies where originals are too damaged. That document strongly corroborates the School's archival mission.
