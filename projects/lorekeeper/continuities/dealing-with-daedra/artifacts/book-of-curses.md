# Book of Curses

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Completed artifact record: BOOK 054FD090
Full-book spell container: SPEL 054FD08E

The Book of Curses is a scattered magical artifact whose pages are distributed among multiple competitors.

An unnamed Dremora says it created or at least initiated the contest over the Book. The Vampire Master is one selected participant and receives the spine rather than a page.

## Six physical contest pages

Direct ACTI -> REFR -> CELL resolution establishes the physical page set:

- Agony — ACTI 055117BA — Anise's Cellar.
- Despair — ACTI 055117BB — Drelas' Cottage.
- Vulnerability — ACTI 055117BD — Harmugstahl.
- Binding — ACTI 055117BE — Rannveig's Fast.
- Petrification — ACTI 055117BC — Southfringe/Bashnag's base.
- Unlife — ACTI 055117BF — Ansilvund Burial Chambers.

This is the authoritative six-page set. **Debilitation is not one of the six placed pages.**

The Unlife page's hidden completion perk grants all six physical-page curses together:
Agony, Despair, Vulnerability, Binding, Petrification, and Unlife.

## Completed Book

Reading BOOK 054FD090 teaches SPEL 054FD08E, whose hidden full-book effect applies PERK 054FD069.

That full-book perk grants twelve curse abilities:

1. Despair
2. Rage
3. Draining
4. Agony
5. Binding
6. Vulnerability
7. Unlife
8. Exhaustion
9. Petrification
10. Mortality
11. Debilitation
12. Obedience

The completed artifact therefore expands the six-page repertoire with Rage, Draining, Exhaustion, Mortality, Debilitation, and Obedience.

BOOK 053349F5, Book of Curses: Debilitation, is a separate spell tome teaching Debilitation directly; it is not the Vulnerability page and is not a physical contest page.

## Curse economy

The associated curse spells consistently use Health or Health/Stamina rather than ordinary Magicka, and many warn that casting can kill the caster.

The artifact's curse tradition is therefore implemented as a life-force economy rather than ordinary spellcasting.

## Completion and destruction

When the Master receives the final page he declares the Book complete.

In endings where he keeps it, he tests the assembled artifact on captive farmers before leaving for Cyrodiil.

In the Vigilant route, destroying the remaining page/rune in Stendarr's Beacon's holy fire causes the player to lose memory/ability to use the page-derived curses. This suggests that access to the page/rune network participates in retaining the curse knowledge, although the deeper mechanism is not explained.
