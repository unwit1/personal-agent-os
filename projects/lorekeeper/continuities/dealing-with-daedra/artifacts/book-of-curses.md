# Book of Curses

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Completed artifact record: BOOK 054FD090
Full-book spell container: SPEL 054FD08E

The Book of Curses is a scattered magical artifact whose pages are distributed among multiple competitors.

An unnamed Dremora says it created or at least initiated the contest over the Book. The Vampire Master is one selected participant and receives the spine rather than a page.

## Core page curses

The page set represented in the artifact hunt includes:

- Agony — damages a target over time.
- Binding — binds/traps a target's soul.
- Petrification — turns a target to stone.
- Despair — forces surrender of hope.
- Debilitation — cripples speed and stamina.
- Unlife — curses a target into unlife.

The associated spell records consistently describe the caster paying with Health or Health/Stamina rather than ordinary Magicka. Many explicitly warn that casting can kill the caster.

This makes the book's curse tradition a life-force economy rather than ordinary spellcasting.

## Related curse technology

The Vampire Master separately applies Curse of Obedience as a quest curse. Its explicit behavioral clauses make it a more specialized application of the same broader curse tradition.

Other later curse spells in the plugin extend the design language to Rage, Vulnerability, Draining, Exhaustion, and Mortality.

## Completion

When the Master receives the final page he declares the Book complete.

In endings where he keeps it, he tests the assembled artifact on captive farmers before leaving for Cyrodiil.

In the Vigilant route, destroying a key page in holy fire removes the player's memory/ability to use the page-derived curses, implying that possession/connection to the pages is part of how the curse knowledge is retained.
