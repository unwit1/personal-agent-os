# Anti-dragon gem collective

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Implementation anchor: REFR 059E5C47 (dealsgemtalkref)
Primary evidence: dealsgemlore* messages and dealsgemmonolog.psc.

A giant gem in the Cult of the Return arc contains or channels a chorus of voices that collectively reject dragon rule.

## Doctrine

The voices claim:
- dragons are powerful animals, not gods;
- the Dragon Cult uses hierarchy and undeath as systems of control;
- dragon priests receive privileged scraps while ordinary followers become chattel;
- true power over life and death was deliberately monopolized;
- mortals should meet returning dragons as equals or inferiors rather than gods.

They describe their alternative as meritocratic but openly admit that all members must contribute energy and that weaker members may lose more than they gain.

## "Masters least Ideal"

The collective says it pried secrets from "the Masters least Ideal" and used those secrets to construct a "perfect afterlife".

The wording strongly indicates the Ideal Masters, but this remains the collective's own claim about the origin of its techniques.

## Joining

The joining event mechanically:
- connects the player's soul to the gem;
- replaces part of the ordinary Dreamsleeve connection;
- causes ongoing life-force siphoning;
- grants necromantic memories;
- grants fragmented old Dragon-Cult memories;
- turns the player against the Cult of the Return;
- unlocks life-force and dragon-memory systems.

The exact identity and historical origin of the chorus are not stated. They appear to be ancient anti-dragon heretics, possibly former priests or cult insiders, but that final identification remains inference.
