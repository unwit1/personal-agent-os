# Dagonist recruitment network

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Dagon-aligned Hooded Figure recruits around ambition and willingness to cross boundaries.

Recruitment material targets:
- socially dispossessed people seeking power;
- taboo researchers;
- leaders of fast-growing organizations;
- existing occult/criminal suppliers.

The recruiter repeatedly uses a trapper's shack near the Lord Stone as a temporary contact point.

A letter to Bashnag warns that ambition is admirable but excessive betrayal risks falling into Mephala/Webspinner territory.

This supports a distinction inside the cult's theology:
- Dagon -> ambition, change, revolutionary power;
- Mephala -> betrayal as a separate sphere that can pull followers away.

The network is socially broad but ideologically narrow: ambition is the common qualification.
