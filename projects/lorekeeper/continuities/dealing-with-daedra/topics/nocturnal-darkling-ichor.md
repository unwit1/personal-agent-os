# Nocturnal, Darkling Ichor, and the Shade Perilous

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary speaker: Arincine.

## Nocturnal's nature

Arincine says Nocturnal is exceptionally difficult to classify because she conceals her nature.

She associates Nocturnal with luck, darkness, shadow magic, passage through inaccessible places, and corvids.

She repeats but explicitly does not endorse as settled fact the claim that Nocturnal is the Ur-Dra—the first Daedra to form in the Void.

She describes the Evergloam as a large realm containing many pocket-realms and repeats a tradition that it may have an unusually direct connection to Nirn.

## Shade Perilous

Arincine identifies the Shade Perilous as a pocket-realm of the Evergloam.

A liquid from that realm is used in Darkling initiation.

She says the liquid:
- may be a form of Chaotic Creatia;
- contains "darkling mana";
- attunes the drinker to Nocturnal.

She also claims other Daedra have fought over the Shade Perilous because of the substance's value, including temporary alliances between frost and fire atronachs.

That history remains character testimony.

## Darkling as religious identity

Arincine says "Darkling" was traditionally a broad name for a mortal witch or cultist devoted to Nocturnal.

She places the cult's strongest period in the Second Era and says the term has largely fallen out of use among mortals.

This is distinct from her separate discussion of Shrikes as Daedric beings associated with Nocturnal.

## Corroboration

The Ichor's exact cosmological composition is not independently proven.

However, gameplay messages and Darkling initiation mechanics do corroborate that consuming it changes the player's magical affinity with darkness/night and Nocturnal's power.
