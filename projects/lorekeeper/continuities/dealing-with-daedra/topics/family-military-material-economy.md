# Family flesh cult: military and material economy

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Thaer's research and the God-Mother cult depend on material supply rather than pure spellcasting.

Jurger Stonearm represents the armed side of that economy.

He directs attacks on Forsworn, treats bodies as offerings/material, and organizes a temporary operation at Kolskeggr Mine.

The objective can be satisfied by holding the mine long enough to gather sufficient resource for weeks of Family use.

Rewards include flesh-extract rations and deeper permission to collect sacrifices.

Combined with Thaer's experimental notes, this shows a multi-layer resource system:
- mines and natural resources;
- corpses/biological material;
- flesh extract as ration/commodity;
- armed acquisition;
- God-Mother ritual requirements;
- research inputs.

The Family is therefore both cult and production system.
