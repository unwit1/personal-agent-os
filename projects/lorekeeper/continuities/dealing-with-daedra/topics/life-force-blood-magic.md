# Vampire blood magic and life-force manipulation

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Vampire Master teaches a blood-magic system that overlaps conceptually with several other mod traditions.

## Techniques

The Master describes life force as magical potential carried through blood and tissue.

He teaches:
- sensing power in nearby blood;
- pulling life force out of victims;
- compressing diffuse life force into the blood vessels before bloodletting;
- absorbing concentrated power through an enchanted weapon;
- pushing the caster's own life force from blood into flesh for temporary strength, breathing, and adrenaline.

The most efficient techniques use the caster's own life force and can be lethal.

## Cross-continuity-within-mod parallels

This model closely parallels:
- the anti-dragon gem collective's quantified life-force pool;
- Draugr servant upkeep paid in life force;
- soul-gem filling from life force;
- Thaer's flesh-magic distinction between blood, breath, and soul;
- curse spells that consume Health/Stamina instead of Magicka.

These are separate traditions, but together they establish a strong Dealing with Daedra worldbuilding pattern: vitality is repeatedly treated as a manipulable magical substrate distinct from ordinary Magicka.
