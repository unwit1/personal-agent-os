# Witch-Hunter's Primer and bounty economy

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: BOOK 05259723, Witch-Hunter's Primer (abridged).

The Primer establishes a semi-formal monster-hunting economy in Skyrim. It claims standing bounties on draugr, vampires, hagravens, and werewolves, while some other creatures are excluded from regular bounties.

The manual pairs each target with:
- trophy requirements;
- combat advice;
- habitat/finding heuristics;
- legal/economic assumptions.

It recommends stealth, ranged attacks, silver, anti-magicka tactics, warding, poisons, and cure-disease supplies. It treats mages engaged in illegal experiments as lawful kill targets if the hunter can document the illegality.

The text is not neutral natural history. It contains professional prejudice, cynical jokes, political avoidance, and explicit economic incentives. Use it primarily to reconstruct the witchhunter profession and its worldview.
