# Kornalus elemental-spider program

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Kornalus develops tame frostbite spiders with magical elemental properties.

His own letter says the Whiteridge Barrow imbuing chamber previously allowed stronger magical properties and that he is attempting to scale the technique to fully grown frostbite spiders.

An intercepted correspondent later reports Kornalus controlling a large force of tame spiders with elemental cloak spells and occupying a fort.

This supports a progression from imbuing research to military deployment.

The exact imbuing mechanism and force size remain unspecified.
