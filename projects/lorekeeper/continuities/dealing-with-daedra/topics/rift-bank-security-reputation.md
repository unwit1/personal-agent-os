# Rift Bank security reputation

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Rift Bank and Mineral Exchange publicly advertises business funding, precious-metal/gem services, secure storage, and customer accounts from an old Dwarven storehouse east of Fort Greenwall.

A Thieves-Guild-oriented note initially proposes casing the bank for robbery.

Brynjolf then edits the notice to warn that the security is too strong and records failed attempts ending in death or life imprisonment.

This independently corroborates Aedwatch's claim that his facility has unusually strong security.
