# Circulation-independent healing

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: Old Letter 058C82FB.

An anonymous researcher produces a proof-of-concept healing spell that does not require natural circulation to spread its effects.

The modified spell is described as less efficient and poorly refined because the application is understudied.

The source demonstrates that circulation-independent magical healing is possible in this continuity.

It does not identify the intended patient type or project. Do not silently assign the research to Thaer, vampires, undead, or constructs.
