# Widows estate finance front

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: operational note BOOK 0538FFE8.

The post-estate Widows operation requires a respectable source of income.

Rather than open a store near the cult site, the operational plan uses the estate as a remote financier:
- contact businesses by courier;
- offer investment capital;
- receive returns by courier;
- build a reputation through successful investments;
- balance safe and risky ventures;
- use School of Julianos historical economic records to improve decision-making.

The same courier route handles basic estate supplies and writing materials.

This financial system serves two purposes simultaneously: generate real revenue and provide a respectable public-facing activity that does not invite customers into the estate.

The School of Julianos is an information source in this arrangement, not a demonstrated participant in the cult.
