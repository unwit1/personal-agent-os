# Servitor soul-fragment experiment

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: BOOK 05226C02, Servitor Experiment Log.

A researcher modifies the familiar-summoning concept in an attempt to isolate the learning/memory aspect of the caster's own soul and manifest it as a persistent Servitor.

The Servitor:
1. begins as an adaptive familiar-like construct;
2. retains learning across manifestations;
3. develops problem solving and speech;
4. becomes fully sapient, domineering, and envious;
5. creates severe spiritual discomfort when unmanifested;
6. ultimately defeats the experimenter.

The final journal entry is written by the Servitor, which says its "father" was too weak and that it will put his body and soul to use.

The experiment and outcome are direct continuity facts. The researcher's theory that the construct is literally a partitioned soul-aspect remains an experimental model.
