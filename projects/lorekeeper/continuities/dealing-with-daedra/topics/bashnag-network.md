# Bashnag network

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Bashnag is a major connective figure despite relatively little direct dialogue.

Evidence from several factions establishes:
- former cofounder/field leader with Carsten;
- access to the witch-taught Daedric empowerment ritual;
- breakaway with elite spellswords;
- mountain base south/southeast of Helgen near the Pale Pass;
- possession of the Blade of the Ravener;
- conflict with Syndicate shipments;
- continuing contact with at least one Dagon-aligned correspondent;
- repeated betrayal significant enough for that correspondent to warn of Mephala's influence.

The Syndicate eventually issues a termination order and requests recovery of Bashnag's signature Syndicate ring.

An intelligence bulletin says one of Bashnag's underlings has started a youth-focused occult group. This likely identifies Skegor's operation, although the bulletin does not give a name.

Bashnag's network therefore links organized crime, Daedric pact magic, artifact acquisition, Dagonist supply relationships, betrayal/Mephala symbolism, and grassroots cult recruitment.
