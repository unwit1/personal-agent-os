# School of Julianos hold-record network

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The School of Julianos does not only preserve old manuscripts.

A repeated role-conditioned dialogue branch has the School collector visit hold stewards and Jarls for the latest official records.

The officials complain about the endless volume, hand over the newest records, and note that more will exist within days.

This makes the School's archive a continuously updated governmental record network.

Combined with Aslfur's institutional description and Restoration Instructions, the School performs at least four evidence-management functions:
- historical manuscript conservation;
- transcription/restoration;
- current hold-record collection;
- preservation of administrative and social history.

The School is therefore closer to a combined temple-library-archive than a conventional static book collection.
