# Gilded Mine cover and possible lure operation

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Gilded Mine has two sharply different public/private realities.

## Public story

Owner Falk Eransen says the mine is closed and denies reports of active ore exports.

## External evidence

Thaer's annotated mine survey says:
- the Gilded Mine was formerly a corundum mine;
- it went dry;
- it then suddenly began exporting gold;
- the miners became reclusive.

A captured bandit independently says his boss heard the mine was exporting gold and sent an infiltration team.

## Easy entry, blocked exit

The bandit says the front door was unlocked and an ore-chute gate was broken. Once the group entered, the route out was blocked.

He interprets both the gold rumor and easy access as bait.

The captured group then became inputs into the Brethren's coercive system: sacrifice, farm assignment, captivity, or potential initiation.

## Classification

"Renewed secret gold exports" has cross-source support.

"Deliberate victim-lure operation" is a strong inference from the captive's testimony plus Brethren practices, but no Brethren leader directly admits to using the gold rumor as bait.
