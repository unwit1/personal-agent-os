# Midden unauthorized experiments and College oversight

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A College faculty notice explicitly describes the Midden as hazardous and filled with remnants of experiments that lacked proper approval or oversight.

New students are forbidden from entering, and anyone accessing or perpetuating those experiments faces severe reprimand.

The unnamed Midden Student's secret charm research independently demonstrates a violation of this policy.

Therefore:
- occult material found in the Midden can be evidence that such research exists;
- it is **not** automatically evidence of College endorsement.

This evidence boundary should apply to future Lorekeeper retrieval involving mod-added Midden shrines, experiments, or notes.
