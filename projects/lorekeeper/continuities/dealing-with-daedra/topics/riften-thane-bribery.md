# Riften Thane bribery branch

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The mod adds a corruption route to Riften's Thane-status progression.

The INFO records allow either of the two possible Riften steward world-state actors to deliver the same branch.

The steward:
- asks for a substantial "donation";
- sets the price at 5,000 septims;
- repeatedly starts to say "my" before correcting to "Riften's" needs;
- promises to mention the player's generosity to the Jarl;
- says a player unable to pay is apparently not Thane material.

After payment, the steward jokes that the money should buy a new outfit—then hurriedly reframes it as clothing for poor orphans.

## Interpretation

This is not evidence that every Riften government in every Elder Scrolls continuity is literally pay-to-Thane.

It is a direct continuity fact that Dealing with Daedra provides a bribery route through the Riften steward office regardless of the relevant civil-war political state.

The branch fits the mod's broader theme that formal institutions can be turned into transactional networks through money, favors, reputation, or occult leverage.
