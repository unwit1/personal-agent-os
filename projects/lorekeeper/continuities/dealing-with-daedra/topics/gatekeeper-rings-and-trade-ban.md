# Gatekeeper rings and EETC trade ban

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Gatekeeper gem-studded rings occupy several roles at once:
- recruitment objects;
- access/recognition tokens at Yorgrim Overlook;
- claimed gifts from the Gatekeepers' unnamed "masters";
- magical tools associated by captured users with soul theft and raising the dead.

A Gatekeeper recruiter calls the rings "purely ceremonial", but a Vigilant report and a Revenant-Moon intelligence letter both describe them as magically powerful recruitment tools.

Lorekeeper should therefore preserve the recruiter's wording as deliberate public-facing minimization, not merge it into the technical description.

The same recruiter says the East Empire Trading Company has imposed a trade ban on the organization because of "religious differences". Gatekeeper supply consequently uses "Independent Traders" and other nonstandard channels.

The existence of a ban is direct Gatekeeper testimony. Its motive is only the recruiter's interpretation.
