# Mageblight public-health evidence

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Mageblight is supported by three evidence layers.

## Grados

Grados explains the affliction as an artificial magicka-parasitic organism produced through Chaotic Creatia and designed as an anti-mage weapon.

That explanation remains his technical account.

## Public PSA

The written PSA independently describes:
- a spreading disease/curse-like condition;
- suppression/disruption of magicka;
- symptoms matching Grados's project.

## Apothecary witnesses

Generic apothecary-role dialogue adds field corroboration.

One apothecary says the unusual cure:
- is unlike any potion they understand;
- genuinely works;
- is supplied for free.

Another says:
- multiple people have presented the same complaint;
- something is clearly spreading;
- a blindfolded man in special Imperial-style armor asked how many cases had appeared;
- he also purchased specialist potions and appeared to be a witchhunter.

The description strongly matches Grados but is stored as a strong contextual inference rather than a named identification.

## Evidentiary result

High confidence:
- Mageblight exists in the continuity;
- cases are spreading among ordinary people;
- an effective unusual cure exists and is distributed;
- an organized investigation is occurring.

Still source-attributed:
- Grados's explanation of its exact Chaotic-Creatia construction and strategic purpose.
