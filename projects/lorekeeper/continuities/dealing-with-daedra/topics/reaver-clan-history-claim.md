# Reaver clan history — former leader's account

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A deposed Reaver leader claims the Solstheim Reavers once formed a long-lived warrior clan with a code rather than being loose bandits.

He says the organization began as a Nordic clan and became increasingly Dunmer across generations.

He attributes the current fragmented "Reaver Lord" structure to a coup by five lieutenants who removed him and then turned on one another.

After their deaths he plans to rebuild the organization.

This file intentionally stores the historical account as **former-leader testimony**, not neutral Elder Scrolls history. No independent source in this batch verifies the claimed centuries-old Nordic origin.
