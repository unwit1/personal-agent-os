# Velehk Sain chart smuggling

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A Skyrim smuggling network seeks the charts Velehk Sain used to evade the Imperial Navy.

The operation:
- has intelligence placing Sain within the College of Winterhold;
- previously sent an infiltration team through natural caverns;
- supplies climbing gear and disguise advice;
- prioritizes the charts over killing Sain;
- relocates its berth on a schedule.

Plautis Vulpin later accepts the recovered charts and treats them as valuable operational intelligence.

The episode shows the mod's smuggling network acquiring historical pirate knowledge as a reusable logistics advantage rather than treating Velehk Sain only as a supernatural curiosity.
