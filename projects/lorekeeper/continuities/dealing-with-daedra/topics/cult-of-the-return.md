# Cult of the Return

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A restorationist Dragon-Cult movement led doctrinally by the Herald.

Core themes:
- Alduin is expected to return and restore dead dragons.
- Dragon bones function, in cult teaching, as anchors for dragon souls.
- Burial sites therefore become strategic religious assets.
- Draugrisation transforms living adherents into progressively undead servants/priests.
- Higher priests use supplication/life-force relationships to preserve function.
- The movement recruits poor, exiled, and socially marginal people into a renewed hierarchy of soldiers, laborers, and clergy.

The movement combines charity and social organization with authoritarian eschatology. Its ancient-history and metaphysical explanations are preserved as cult doctrine, not as verified TES history.
