# Elemental essence research

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary sources: Elemental Essences (draft copy); Quinergus dialogue.

Quinergus extracts raw elemental power from atronach salts using Destruction magic and stabilizes it in a Jazbay-based mixture.

Reported uses:
- temporary amplification of elemental Destruction spells;
- limited magical awakening in non-mages;
- weapon oils;
- elemental rune ink.

His notes explain that ordinary alchemy exposes the salts' familiar properties but does not isolate the desired elemental effect cleanly. Destruction-based extraction produces a more stable transferable energy.

For highest-quality output, he recommends the Atronach Forge with elemental salts and Jazbay conserve. He admits he does not know exactly how the Forge works.

The research later turns toward scrollcrafting and conjured atronachs as a renewable source of salts.
