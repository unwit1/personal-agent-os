# School of Julianos manuscript culture

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The School's archival practices are unusually well documented.

Restoration Instructions directs restorers to:
- identify the original material and historical context;
- match vellum/paper, inks, pigments, and gold leaf where possible;
- transcribe fragile text before intervention;
- preserve original fragments;
- mark conjectural reconstruction;
- avoid presenting repaired guesses as original text;
- store restored works appropriately.

This provides strong institutional evidence that the School values provenance and textual criticism rather than merely accumulating books.

Aslfur's dialogue broadens the mission to field collection, teaching, court advising, and reconstruction of regional archives lost during the Oblivion Crisis.
