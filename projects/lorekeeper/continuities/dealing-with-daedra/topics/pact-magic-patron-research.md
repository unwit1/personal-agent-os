# Pact-magic patron research

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: Patron Notes, BOOK 050435C6 / backup 054509F3.

The report describes an experimental patron-attunement ritual using a silver ring, garnet, the subject's blood, salt, magical heat, and a physical token carrying the intended patron's metaphysical signature. The bond is first encoded into the ring and then transposed onto the subject.

Candidate non-Et'Ada patrons include:
- Ideal Masters — accessible through soul trade but considered dangerously treacherous.
- Mannimarco / God of Worms — considered viable for individual necromancers and potentially useful politically.
- an isolated/disconnected Hist — experimentally interesting, with severe uncertainty.
- Magna Ge — approached through meteoric material and Ayleid-well-like optics.
- an unidentified Psijic-like energy being — hostile but proof the connection process may work.
- a "phantasmagorical" collective-subconscious/dreamsleeve target reached through hallucinogenic work.

The experiment program is a continuity fact. The document's cosmological interpretations are research claims with varying confidence, not base-canon determinations.
