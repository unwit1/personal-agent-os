# Widows estate provenance and safehouses

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Coven correspondence describes a network under Vigilant pressure.

Named fallback points include Eldi's house south of Riften and Lysara's old estate.

The estate:
- survived the execution of the writer's uncle and aunt;
- was officially seized by the Jarl of Morthal;
- sits near the base of the Solitude Arch;
- contains a hidden cavern entrance behind a basement cupboard.

A later note proposes having an outsider purchase the deed and possibly converting the property into a store for revenue and respectability.

The plan combines safehouse logistics, property laundering, hidden space, and a legitimate business front.
