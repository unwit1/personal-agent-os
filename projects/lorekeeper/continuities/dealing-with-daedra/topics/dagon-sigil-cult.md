# Dagon cell and sigil craft

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Dagon cell recruits through ambition and personal transformation rather than simple apocalyptic destruction.

The Hooded Figure emphasizes Dagon's spheres of revolution, ambition, energy, change, destruction, and natural disaster. Permanent marking is used as an initiation symbol.

The cell's "sigils" are explicitly fake or derivative rather than true Daedric sigils. They operate more like specialized conjuration scrolls written with Daedric letters. The cultist claims strongly Nirnian/"Aedric" material in the ink helps anchor the magic to Nirn.

The cult also traffics in specific Daedric identities/names for conjuration and teaches unbound or atypical summoning methods.

The cultist's suggestion that official Oblivion-Crisis history is Imperial propaganda is stored only as Dagonist rhetoric.
