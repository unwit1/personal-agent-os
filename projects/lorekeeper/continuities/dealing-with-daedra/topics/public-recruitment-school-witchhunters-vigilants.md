# Public recruitment: School, Witchhunters, Vigilants

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Public notices show three non-Daedric institutions recruiting through distinct social roles.

### School of Julianos
Education, knowledge, youth development, food, and board. Outreach includes Honorhall wards approaching adulthood.

### Witchhunter's Guild
Paid adventure and practical defense against abominations. Novices can enter without prior combat/alchemy training and learn under Regalt.

### Vigilants of Stendarr
Healing the sick, comforting people in need, religious service, and armed "preventative" roles.

These sources should be used as each institution's self-description. They add important noncombat functions to the School and Vigilants and formal novice recruitment to the Witchhunters.
