# Widows of Mephala: unhappy-spouse network

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Widows storyline uses three already-unhappy married women as potential social targets:
- Nivenor;
- Frabbi;
- Olda.

Their dialogue is conditionally shared but individual grievances are preserved.

## Nivenor

She complains about Bolli giving away money.

A hypothetical scenario points toward the Ratway as a dangerous place connected to his charitable behavior.

## Frabbi

She points to constant shouting and Kleppr's relationships/associations.

A hypothetical reframes his social position as something that could put him in danger.

## Olda

She complains about Horgeir slipping away to drink with their remaining money.

A hypothetical highlights nearby wilderness hazards and sharp drops.

## Recruitment method

The player does not immediately issue an explicit murder order in these exchanges.

Instead the dialogue:
1. elicits resentment;
2. asks whether the wife has considered "doing something";
3. outlines a plausible dangerous scenario connected to the husband's existing habits;
4. lets the target fill in the implication.

Later shared dialogue can move the women into a mourning state.

This is highly consistent with Mephala's betrayal theme: the Widows cultivate situations where intimate trust can collapse from inside an existing relationship.

Quest-state variation means the library should not record all three husbands as universally dead.
