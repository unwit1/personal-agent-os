# Drelas transmutation research

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Drelas and Arcadia represent opposing interpretations of transmutation.

Arcadia says no alchemist she knows has successfully transmuted even lead into gold and describes Drelas's obsession as financially ruinous.

Drelas tells Nurelion in an unsent letter that he has achieved a proof-of-concept and wants access to Curalmil's surviving private work.

Nurelion rejects the premise and refuses to disclose what he knows about the White Phial's resting place.

A mod-added "Transmute?" spell and associated tome provide mechanical evidence that some experimental transmutation effect exists in this continuity.

Evidence tiers:
- direct history: Drelas pursued transmutation and spent heavily on it;
- direct disagreement: Arcadia/Nurelion reject his claims;
- researcher claim: Drelas says he has a proof-of-concept;
- mechanical convergence: a Transmute? spell/tome exists;
- unresolved: whether Curalmil ever researched the same technique.
