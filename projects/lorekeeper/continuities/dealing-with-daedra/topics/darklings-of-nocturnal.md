# Darklings of Nocturnal

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Darklings are a witch tradition devoted to Nocturnal.

According to Arincine, their power declined as Nocturnal's power faded, pushing many practitioners toward Hircine. She describes Darklight as the final coven to defect.

Initiation:
1. prove character through theft/debt tasks;
2. choose or reveal affinity with Strife, Shadow, or Subterfuge;
3. perform a night ritual at the Shadow Stone;
4. summon a Shrike;
5. drink Nocturnal-aligned Ichor.

Deyanira Katrece then provides up to three infusions and sends the initiate to recover old Nocturnal sites and punish defectors.

Messages corroborate direct gameplay effects: Ichor increases affinity with darkness/night, and one message describes a blockage in "darkling mana" flowing from the Evergloam while Nocturnal's power is diminished.

This makes the Darklings one of the mod's clearest examples of a cult whose theology, faction history, ritual procedure, and mechanical effects are all represented.
