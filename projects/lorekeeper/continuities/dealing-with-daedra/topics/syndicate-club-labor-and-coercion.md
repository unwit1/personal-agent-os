# Syndicate/Club labor and coercive recruitment

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

An internal Syndicate bulletin explicitly offers payment for recruiting orphanage graduates to the Eastmarch Gentleman's Club and says targets need not be willing if they can be acquired without witnesses.

This is direct evidence of coercive worker acquisition.

A separate Club instruction sheet describes an organized escort system:
- signature amulet marks seller status;
- service occurs across bedrooms, bar/gambling hall, and bath/sauna;
- work is logged in a ledger;
- wages include gold/basic supplies;
- shared worker housing is available;
- disease triggers Cure Disease treatment in place of normal pay to protect patrons.

The same venue therefore supports both ordinary/voluntary labor processes and coercive Syndicate recruitment.
