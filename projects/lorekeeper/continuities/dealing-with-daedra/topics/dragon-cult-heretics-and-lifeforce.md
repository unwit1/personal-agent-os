# Dragon-Cult heretics, life force, and dragon memories

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary evidence: anti-dragon gem messages plus dealsgemmonolog.psc, dealsdraugrsoultrap.psc, dealsfillsoulgemdraugr.psc, and dealscostfordraugrthrall.psc.

The restored Dragon-Cult storyline contains an ideological break point: the player can reject the Herald and join a chorus of anti-dragon voices stored in a giant gem.

## Competing models of immortality

The Herald's cult offers hierarchical Draugrisation under dragon authority.

The gem collective offers "mindful unlife" and claims to have built a superior afterlife using knowledge obtained from the Ideal Masters.

Both systems consume energy from adherents. The gem collective openly frames this as a merit system in which strong members can gather more life force than they lose.

## Mechanical life-force economy

The gem path implements life force as a persistent resource tied to base Health, Stamina, and Magicka.

- killing victims while using the relevant drain system permanently increases all three and stored life force;
- stronger and mortal victims provide larger amounts;
- creating/maintaining Draugr servants consumes life force;
- filling ordinary soul gems can consume stored life force in fixed amounts;
- Dragon Souls can be exchanged for a surge of life force.

This is unusually strong corroboration because the dialogue's metaphysical language is backed by explicit gameplay systems.

## Dragon-memory theft

The joined player receives memories explaining enough about Draconic soul passage between Nirn and the Dreamsleeve to adapt life-force theft into partial memory harvesting from slain dragons.

The collective's deeper claims about the true nature of dragon souls or the Dreamsleeve remain doctrine/learned interpretation, but the memory-harvesting mechanic is a continuity fact.
