# Mageblight and Chaotic Creatia

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Mageblight is one of the strongest cross-source lore clusters in the mod.

Grados (INFO 0564C2D9 and related dialogue) describes an artificial parasite produced from Chaotic Creatia that feeds on a host's magicka. He says it resists normal cleansing, weakens as it spreads rather than reproducing without limit, and is useful as an anti-mage weapon.

The written source PSA: MageBlight (BOOK 0520D4F1) independently describes the disease/curse, its progression, magicka suppression/sensitivity, and parasitic nature. The affliction's existence within this continuity is therefore stronger than Grados's testimony alone.

Grados's broader statements about Chaotic Creatia — raw matter of Oblivion, Daedric bodies, terrain, and possible Nirnian wellsprings — remain character research/explanation unless independently corroborated.
