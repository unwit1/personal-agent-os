# Stauf and Skaafin contract magic

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary sources: The Tragedy of Stauf; Stauf's Research Notes.

The dramatized Tragedy recounts court wizard Stauf bargaining with a Daedra named Seleh'potsih'pem for one year of knowledge, power, and charm, ultimately losing his soul.

The surviving research notes provide a reproducible ritual framework for contacting a Skaafin of Clavicus Vile:

- Daedra heart -> attune toward Oblivion.
- Gold coin -> refine toward the Fields of Regret.
- True name of the target Skaafin -> identify the contracting entity.
- Filled common soul gem -> power the link.
- Salt -> weaken Mundus.
- Four-way crossroads at midnight -> ritual site/time.
- Skaafin signs first; mortal adds blood and signs second.

The source stresses that exact contractual wording is essential.

This cluster reinforces a recurring theme in the mod: Daedric bargains can be approached as technical legal-magical systems with names, signatures, power sources, correspondences, and carefully constrained wording.
