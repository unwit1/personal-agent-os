# Comparative sexual spheres

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra
Primary source: Sexuality in the Daedric and Aedric Spheres, by Milanie Carius.

The essay compares five divine spheres through the concepts of focus and intent.

- Dibella: physical intimacy directed toward fertility, youth, beauty, and creation.
- Sanguine: physical intimacy directed toward gratification and overindulgence.
- Mara: intimate bonds directed toward stable family formation and nurturing.
- Mephala: intimate bonds deliberately made one-sided so trust can be exploited.
- Molag Bal: sexuality as domination and deprivation of agency, perverting the other four frameworks.

This is sophisticated in-world comparative theology, not direct divine revelation.

It is particularly relevant to the Widows of Mephala because it gives an intellectual vocabulary for transforming love, trust, and marriage into instruments of betrayal.
