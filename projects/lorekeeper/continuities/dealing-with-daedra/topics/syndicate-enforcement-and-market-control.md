# Syndicate enforcement and market control

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

Syndicate mission briefs reveal several distinct forms of control.

- Bashnag: kill a former associate after an unspecified affront and recover his Syndicate ring.
- Liar's Retreat: enforce missed payments from an operation in which the Syndicate owns a stake.
- Faldar's Tooth: destroy an unrelated illicit-entertainment competitor so demand shifts toward Syndicate services, then harvest its client records.
- Fellglow Keep: kill a monitored Thalmor researcher and deliver sealed notes intact to an unnamed client.

The Fellglow target strongly matches Grados's independently stated mission against a Thalmor researcher at the same site. Treat Grados as a plausible client/aligned requester, not a confirmed identity.

The Syndicate behaves like a diversified criminal enterprise with investment, enforcement, market strategy, and information-security functions.
