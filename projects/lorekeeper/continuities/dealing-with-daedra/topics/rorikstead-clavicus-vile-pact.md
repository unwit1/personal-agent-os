# Rorikstead and the Clavicus Vile pact

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

This continuity gives an explicit supernatural explanation for Rorikstead's fertility.

BOOK 05861E27, Note from Rorik, describes poor soil and an older First Era settlement called Rorik's Steading. It says a bargain was made at a Clavicus Vile shrine: fertile land in exchange for the life of any fertile woman who gave birth there. The current Rorik later rediscovered and repeated the bargain.

BOOK 05861E28, Suicide Note, supplies a victim-side perspective: a widower records his wife's death during childbirth and her suspicion that the village's repeated deaths and Rorik/Jouane secrecy were connected.

INFO 0589F8C2, spoken by a Skaafin, independently discusses breaking Rorik's bargain and predicts that removing it would cause the soil to fail, the settlement to collapse, and the cycle eventually to begin again.

Because this has multiple distinct in-world sources, it is a high-confidence fact of the mod continuity. It is not a claim about Bethesda canon.
