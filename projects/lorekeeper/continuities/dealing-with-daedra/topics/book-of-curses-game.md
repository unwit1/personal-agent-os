# Book of Curses game

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

The Book of Curses is not merely a fetch-quest artifact. It is the prize in an explicitly staged contest.

## Participants

Known:
- Vampire Master — receives the spine and sends servants after pages.
- unnamed Dremora — says it set the game in motion.
- several page holders including Anise, Drelas, Bashnag, and a necromancer in Ansilvund.

The exact total participant roster and the Dremora's superior/patron are not stated.

## Rules and interference

The Master uses scrying on paper shreds from the spine to track page locations.

The Dremora later intervenes by breaking the player's obedience curse specifically to keep the contest uncertain. This demonstrates that participant control itself is not a protected rule of the game.

The contest can end with:
- one participant assembling the artifact;
- the player attempting to seize it;
- the player voluntarily returning to service;
- the player deceiving the Master;
- the final page destroyed so no contestant can complete the artifact.

## Interpretation

The storyline treats agency as the contested resource.

The Vampire Master tries to turn a contestant into a perfectly constrained tool while preserving useful autonomy.

The Dremora restores choice not out of compassion but because genuine uncertainty makes the game entertaining.

The Vigilant branch rejects the competition itself by destroying the prize's completion path.
