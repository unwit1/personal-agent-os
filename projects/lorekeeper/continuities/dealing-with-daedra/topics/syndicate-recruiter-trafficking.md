# Syndicate recruiter trafficking

Continuity: tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra

A Syndicate warning note identifies a group of men as organizational "recruiters" and says an unrecognized victim would have awakened in one of the organization's less savoury establishments deep in Morrowind.

The associated script mechanically depicts:
- heavy sedation;
- transport by cart with other sleeping people;
- recovery after the handoff.

This establishes coercive capture/trafficking as part of at least one Syndicate recruitment channel.

Do not automatically equate this destination with the Sanguine-party Morrowind slave brothel. The two systems have similarities, but the records do not explicitly identify them as the same establishment.
