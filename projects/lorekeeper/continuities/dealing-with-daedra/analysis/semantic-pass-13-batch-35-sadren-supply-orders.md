# Semantic pass 13 — batch 35: Sadren estate supply orders

Source: BOOK 05951987 (task/order list).

Sadren's estate uses a courier-based procurement network across Skyrim.

Four equal-value orders are listed:
- Adrianne / Warmaiden's, Whiterun — iron chain;
- Haskill / Witchhunter's Guild — refined silver;
- Taarie / Radiant Raiment, Solitude — silk ream;
- Wylandriah / Mistveil Keep, Riften — Welkynd shards.

Each order is 280 septims plus a 20-septim courier fee and is delivered to the Sarethi estate in Whiterun Hold.

## Significance

This expands Sadren's research economy beyond rare field specimens.

The estate also buys:
- ordinary metal hardware;
- refined anti-supernatural material;
- luxury/fine textile;
- magical crystal material.

The supplier list crosses merchant, Witchhunter, court-mage, and clothing networks.

The orders prove commercial contact, not ideological affiliation between Sadren and the suppliers.
