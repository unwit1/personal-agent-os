# Semantic pass 13 — batch 06: Sanguine at the Love Shack

This batch covers the five exact-Subject INFO records for Sanguine (NPC 0568EB09).

## Direct manifestation

When asked whether he is Sanguine, the figure answers affirmatively in substance: "In the flesh, for the moment at least" (INFO 05693C65).

Within this continuity the Love Shack sequence therefore contains a direct manifested/avatar appearance of Sanguine, not merely worshippers invoking his name.

The phrase "for the moment" is compatible with a temporary manifestation, but the dialogue does not explain the exact metaphysical mechanism of the body/avatar.

## Recognition of Mephala's influence

On a branch associated with the Widows/Mephala path, Sanguine says he can smell "the stink of the 8-legged one" on the player (056D5E3A).

He then tells the player to enjoy the exercises as ends in themselves rather than only as means, and says the player's mistress probably will not mind some worship of him "on the side" because there is enough sin to go around (056D5E3B–056D5E3C).

This is useful cross-cult evidence:
- Sanguine recognizes another Prince's influence on the player;
- he does not frame devotion as exclusive;
- he encourages overlapping indulgence/devotion.

The "8-legged one" identification as Mephala is contextually strong because the branch concerns the player's mistress and the Widows of Mephala, but the epithet itself should remain recorded as Sanguine's wording.

## Evidence boundary

Sanguine's direct appearance strengthens the Sanguine affiliation of the Love Shack.

It still does not establish that Sanguine ordered or knew about the later Morrowind enslavement branch. His five lines do not discuss that outcome.
