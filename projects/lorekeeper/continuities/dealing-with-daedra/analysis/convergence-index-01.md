# Convergence index — pass 01

Contradiction tracking is most useful when paired with independent corroboration.

## Mageblight

Grados explains Mageblight as an artificial magicka-parasitic organism. The public PSA independently describes the affliction and its magicka-suppressing symptoms.

Result: existence/symptom cluster is high-confidence continuity fact; Grados's wider Chaotic-Creatia theory remains his explanation.

## Rorikstead pact

Rorik's confession, a victim-side suicide note, and Skaafin dialogue independently support the Clavicus-Vile fertility bargain.

Result: one of the strongest multi-source continuity facts in the corpus.

## Syndicate origin

Operative Green's insider history and testimony from a survivor of the precursor occult group independently converge on a witch-shared empowerment ritual, coup, catastrophic Daedric event, and criminal successor group south of Kynesgrove.

Result: event skeleton is high confidence; exact mapping of every conspirator remains incomplete.

## Gray-Wave / Hermaeus Mora

Census, legal filings, Vigilant analysis, Daedric reference material, a ritual site, and Seeker placement converge.

Result: Gray-Wave occult involvement with Hermaeus Mora is high confidence; the precise fate of each child remains inferred.

## Darkling ritual

Arincine's doctrine, Deyanira Katrece's Shrike dialogue, ritual instructions, and Ichor gameplay effects converge.

Result: ritual sequence and Nocturnal affiliation are continuity facts even though Arincine's history of Nocturnal's decline remains testimony.

## Gatekeeper soul economy

Gatekeeper dialogue, Soul-Cairn patron exchanges, wraith implementations, Black-Soul-Gem mechanics, and independent reporting of their trade network all converge.

Result: the organization and its soul economy are direct continuity facts; patron identity remains unresolved.
