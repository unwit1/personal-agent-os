# Semantic pass 13 — batch 08: Gian and Wanders-In-Tatters

This batch resolves two compact Ratway exchange nodes.

## Gian the Fist

Gian (six INFO records) possesses the special gloves sought by Mojari. He refuses to give them away cheaply and offers two routes: substantial cash, or killing Knjakr, whom Gian describes as a murderer/cannibal who ate his friend. Gian requires Knjakr's hat as proof.

This closes a major link in the Mojari favor chain: Gian's personal revenge produces the gloves that can be exchanged for route access.

## Wanders-In-Tatters

Wanders-In-Tatters (five INFO records) treats cure-disease potions as extremely valuable in the Ratway but values escape even more. He trades a potion for a Steel Sword so he can leave and attempt a new life as a bandit.

## Interpretation

Both cases reinforce the Ratway's scarcity economy. Ordinary items change value according to local constraints:
- gloves become route-access currency;
- a victim's hat becomes proof-of-service;
- cure-disease potions become high-value survival goods;
- a basic Steel Sword becomes an escape tool worth more than medicine.

These are local exchange facts, not universal Skyrim prices.
