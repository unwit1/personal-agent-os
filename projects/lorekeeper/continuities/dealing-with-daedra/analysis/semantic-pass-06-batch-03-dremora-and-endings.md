# Semantic pass 06 — batch 03: Dremora intervention and endings

This batch covers only the late Book of Curses branch point and its documented endings.

## Game-master intervention

The Dremora (05520B3C) says it created the Book of Curses game and wants the contest to continue (INFO 0553A0E6).

It removes the Vampire Master's Curse of Obedience from the player (0553A0E7), creating a real branch in what had previously been magically compelled service.

The Dremora offers three broad responses:
- return to the Master anyway;
- rebel, kill the Master, and claim the book;
- destroy the page through the Vigilants.

A fourth tactical response allows the player to pretend nothing changed and wait for an opportunity to escape (059052CA).

## Vigilant ending

Generic Vigilant dialogue accepts the player's confession, promises a sanction team against the vampire and Daedric tome, and orders the remaining page burned in the purging flame at Stendarr's Beacon (0553A0F1–0553A0F2).

Message 05525C7C records the result:
- the page/rune is destroyed;
- the player suffers severe mental pain;
- the player loses the remembered ability to use the curses;
- a Vigilant sanction team moves against the Master.

This strongly links at least some curse knowledge to the surviving page/rune network rather than ordinary learned spell memory.

## True-submission ending

Message 0552AD84:
- the player waits in the cage;
- the Master assembles the Book of Curses;
- kidnapped farmers are used as test subjects until all stop screaming;
- the Master renews the Curse of Obedience;
- free will is stripped again;
- the Master gives final orders and leaves for Cyrodiil.

The quest-specific post-ending obedience spell (05525C5B) orders the player not to harm the Master, to kill/undermine his enemies, and to await his return.

## Fake-submission ending

If the Dremora already removed the curse and the player conceals that fact, message 05782A3F describes a parallel scene:
- the Master completes and tests the book on farmers;
- he orders the other women to follow him;
- he leaves for Cyrodiil;
- the player maintains the act until certain he is gone and remains free.

This is mechanically and narratively distinct from genuine renewed enslavement.

## Wider vampire politics

On a loyal return branch, the Master says he intends to travel to Cyrodiil to negotiate with the Order Vampyrum and orders the player to exploit conflict between the Volkihar and emerging vampire hunters (0553A0E0–0553A0E1).

This is direct evidence of his strategic interests, but not evidence that negotiations with the Order Vampyrum actually succeed.
