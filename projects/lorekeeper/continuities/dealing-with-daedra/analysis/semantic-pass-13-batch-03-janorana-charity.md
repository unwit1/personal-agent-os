# Semantic pass 13 — batch 03: Janorana Curone and charity recruitment

This batch resolves the eight exact-Subject INFO records for Janorana Curone (NPC 059B7875), the Cult of the Return's soup-kitchen matron.

Janorana offers food, bedrolls, and storage to uninducted people in need. She explicitly distinguishes charity recipients from inducted members and directs inducted members elsewhere for food. Her religious explanation identifies the humble, meek, downtrodden, outcast, and social/religious pariahs as the people still capable of purification/salvation, and she directs interested recipients to Hrotggi for doctrine and conversion.

The soup kitchen is simultaneously real material charity and a recruitment/contact surface. The evidence does not require reducing every charitable act to insincere manipulation: Janorana actually distributes food and shelter. But she directly joins bodily relief to religious messaging and explicitly identifies marginalized recipients as the cult's preferred salvageable population.
