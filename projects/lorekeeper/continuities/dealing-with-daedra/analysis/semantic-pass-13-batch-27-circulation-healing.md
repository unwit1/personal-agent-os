# Semantic pass 13 — batch 27: circulation-independent healing proof of concept

This batch analyzes Old Letter BOOK 058C82FB.

## Research result

An anonymous spell researcher says they created a proof-of-concept healing spell that does not require the body's natural circulation to distribute the healing effect.

They explicitly say the modified process is:
- inherently less efficient;
- less refined because the application has not been studied much.

The writer refuses to continue the work and tells the requester that further development is their responsibility.

## Significance

This source implies that at least some conventional healing-spell behavior in this research tradition assumes or benefits from natural bodily circulation.

A circulation-independent variant is possible, but inefficient.

The intended use is not stated.

Possible applications to undead, damaged circulatory systems, flesh constructs, or other abnormal bodies are plausible, but none should be promoted without a source connecting this letter to a specific project.

## Relationship to flesh-magic doctrine

Thaer independently describes ordinary Restoration healing as a reversal of damage and flesh magic as repair through more natural bodily processes.

That conceptual overlap makes the letter relevant to the mod's broader body-magic research.

However, no direct source currently identifies Thaer as either the writer or requester.
