# Semantic pass 13 — batch 36: High Tide customer arrangement

Source: BOOK 05661008.

The High Tide advert markets the ship as a better sleeping/lodging option for Raven Rock visitors/workers.

It says:
- the ship berths far enough offshore to provide an undisturbed night's sleep;
- customers should use the dinghy at the far end of the dock;
- Geldis receives a share for every customer he sends to the ship.

This adds a referral/revenue-sharing relationship between the High Tide and Geldis's local business.

It also reinforces the High Tide's role as a floating inn rather than simply a private vessel.

The advert's complaint about "slaving away on some strange rock" is sales rhetoric and should not be interpreted as literal slavery.
