# Semantic pass 13 — batch 26: Kornalus and elemental frostbite spiders

This batch analyzes Intercepted Correspondence BOOK 05259717 and Kornalus's letter BOOK 05656DCC.

## Intercepted report

The Reach correspondence describes Kornalus as an Altmer previously expelled by an unnamed "caller" after causing a frostbite-spider infestation.

The writer says Kornalus later:
- created a legion of tame spiders;
- gave them elemental cloak spells;
- captured a fort;
- chose a strategically important position between Karthwasten and a major Forsworn redoubt.

The writer is admiring and informal, so the exact scale word "legion" may be rhetorical. The successful militarized-spider operation is the core report.

## Kornalus's own research letter

Kornalus writes to someone named Servos.

He says losing access to the Whiteridge Barrow imbuing chamber has reduced the potency of magical properties he can place in spiders.

Despite that limitation, he reports progress and says he has demonstrated that fully grown frostbite spiders can be used instead of the small albino spiders.

His goal is explicitly combat effectiveness.

He also asks after Servos and Servos's sister, indicating a preexisting research/social connection.

## Synthesis

The two sources converge on Kornalus as a spider-imbuing researcher who scales magical spider technology from small experimental subjects toward combat-capable adult frostbite spiders.

The intercepted report gives the operational outcome; Kornalus's letter gives the technical limitation and research direction.

## Evidence boundaries

- Whiteridge Barrow's imbuing chamber is said by Kornalus to improve magical potency; its exact mechanism is not explained.
- "Legion" is preserved as the correspondent's wording rather than converted into a numeric force size.
- Servos's identity and the unnamed "caller" remain unresolved.
