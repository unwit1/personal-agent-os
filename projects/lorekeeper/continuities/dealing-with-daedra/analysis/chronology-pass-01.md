# Relative chronology — pass 01

This chronology records only sequences supported inside the Dealing with Daedra continuity. Dates from base Elder Scrolls history are not independently re-litigated here.

## Historical/background arcs

### Gray-Wave clan
- Hyg Gray-Wave is listed as founder, born 3E 179.
- Family wealth/property expands across several holds.
- By the late family period, members display sudden magical ability and unsanctioned conjuration.
- Births are increasingly centralized at the hunting lodge.
- The final 37 children are officially recorded as stillborn.
- Vigilants pursue legal challenges and Daedric-activity allegations.
- The clan lineage ultimately ends and the lodge remains under disputed inheritance.
- Present-day School research reconstructs the case and leads to the Hermaeus Mora/Seeker deduction.

The census contains internally awkward date wording ("ended as of 4E 123" alongside a final birth stated as 4E 174). Preserve the source text rather than silently harmonizing it.

### Rorikstead pact
- An older settlement called Rorik's Steading is associated with an earlier bargain tradition.
- The modern Rorik later discovers/repeats a Clavicus Vile fertility bargain.
- The bargain produces fertile land at the cost of fertile women dying in childbirth.
- A widower's suicide note supplies a victim-side contemporary perspective.
- A Skaafin confirms Vile's involvement and predicts cyclical collapse/repetition if the bargain is broken.

### Darklings
- Darklings historically serve Nocturnal.
- Nocturnal's power is said to decline; many Darklings defect toward Hircine, including Darklight.
- Arincine refuses conversion and eventually attaches herself to the Riften/Thieves Guild environment.
- The player undergoes Shadow Stone/Shrike initiation.
- Deyanira Katrece provides Ichor, orders restoration of an old site, and orders punishment of defectors.
- Later Arincine perceives Nocturnal's power as increasing again.

### Syndicate / Bashnag
- A precursor occult group possesses a ritual that grants Daedric power.
- A Falkreath witch enters its inner circle and shares the ritual with two others.
- A coup occurs; a winged horror kills most of the old group.
- Survivors/traitors establish a criminal group south of Kynesgrove.
- Carsten and Bashnag are remembered as cofounders: Carsten handles business, Bashnag fieldwork.
- Bashnag breaks away with elite spellswords.
- Carsten becomes cautious about empowering additional employees.
- Bashnag interferes with Syndicate shipments and is marked for termination.
- A Bashnag-associated underling likely forms Skegor's youth occult group.

## Current-era research/cult arcs

### Probable Thalmor research
- A coordinated facility conducts essence-extraction and patron-attunement research.
- Patron Notes explicitly treats the essence experiments as a parallel project.
- Ancano is referred to as an attached agent at the College of Winterhold.
- The essence program proposes a second phase called Lorkhic suppression with population-scale testing in Skyrim.
- The report author requests approval; the surviving corpus does not establish whether population-scale testing actually occurred.

### Grados / Mageblight
- Grados develops or deploys artificial Mageblight as an anti-mage project.
- A public PSA independently describes the affliction and offers a cure through Alinor-linked alchemists.
- Grados uses criminal/smuggling networks to source precursor material.
- His anti-Thalmor campaign and cosmological theories develop around the same period.

### Cult of the Return
- The Herald interprets current events through a restorationist Dragon-Cult eschatology.
- The movement recruits marginalized mortals and develops a modern priest/soldier structure.
- Draugrisation, dragon-bone recovery, and preparation for Alduin's return are active present-tense goals.

### Flesh/Deep Ones
- Thaer creates the God-Mother/protoplasmic organism.
- A Family cult grows around the experiment.
- Thaer expands into modular flesh construction, polyps, and Deep-One research.
- Mine surveys and field investigations are used to find underground sites and potential specimens.

## Chronology caveat

Many narrative branches can occur in flexible player order. This file therefore separates historical dependency from gameplay ordering. Do not infer a single canonical player-route chronology when quests can be encountered independently.
