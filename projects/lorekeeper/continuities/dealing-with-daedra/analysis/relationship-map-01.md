## Network pattern

Three economies overlap:
1. material economy — contraband, ink, candles, alchemical materials, bodies, soul gems, weapons;
2. social economy — favors, Service Coupons, employment, membership, blackmail, recruitment, influence;
3. metaphysical economy — souls, patron bonds, Daedric deals, Ichor, blessings, curses, and ritual access.

This overlap is one of the defining worldbuilding patterns of the mod.
