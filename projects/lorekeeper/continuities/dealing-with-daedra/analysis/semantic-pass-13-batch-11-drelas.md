# Semantic pass 13 — batch 11: Drelas, transmutation, and the Book of Curses

This batch connects two previously separate lore clusters: Arcadia's alchemical history and the Book of Curses page hunt.

## Arcadia's account

Arcadia says Drelas was a brilliant alchemist who ran the shop before her.

She says:
- she learned from him as his assistant;
- he became obsessed with transmutation;
- he stopped ordinary potion work;
- he eventually sold the shop to fund further experiments;
- Arcadia's Cauldron emerged from that transfer;
- Drelas moved to an old hunting cabin/cottage in the mountains northwest of Whiterun;
- later letters from him read like the ravings of a madman.

Arcadia rejects transmutation as a myth and treats Drelas as a cautionary example.

## Nurelion correspondence

Nurelion writes directly to Drelas (BOOK 05871809).

He rejects Drelas's theory that the White Phial's legendary behavior implies transmutation and refuses to provide information about Curalmil's resting place because he considers Drelas's research obsessive and dangerous.

Drelas drafts an unsent reply (0587180A) insisting he has already developed a proof-of-concept transmutation process and believes Curalmil's private work may let him refine it.

A mod-added spell/tome named "Transmute?" / "Spell Tome: Transmute Mineral Ore?" exists (SPEL 05871805 / BOOK 05871807), supporting the existence of a gameplay implementation associated with the research without by itself validating every historical claim Drelas makes about Curalmil.

## Book of Curses connection

The Vampire Master later identifies a Dunmer named Drelas in a cottage northwest of Whiterun as a holder/participant in the Book of Curses contest (INFO 05534FCE).

After the page is recovered, the Master says "we beat him to Drelas' page" (0553A0D6), and another line groups Drelas with "the other players" (055E1A88).

The name, race, and exact cottage location align with Arcadia's former employer.

This is strong evidence that the alchemist Drelas and the Book-of-Curses Drelas are the same narrative person.

## Interpretation

Drelas's trajectory can now be reconstructed:
1. talented professional alchemist and Arcadia's mentor/employer;
2. increasing obsession with transmutation;
3. sale of the shop to fund research;
4. isolation in Drelas' Cottage;
5. correspondence with Nurelion over Curalmil/White Phial research;
6. participation in or possession of a page from the Book of Curses contest.

The source set does not establish whether the Book caused his transmutation obsession, followed it, or was simply another dangerous pursuit.
