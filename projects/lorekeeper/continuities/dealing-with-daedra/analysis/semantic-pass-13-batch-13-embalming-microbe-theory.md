# Semantic pass 13 — batch 13: embalming and invisible-organism theory

This batch analyzes Old Letter BOOK 058C82F9.

## Decomposition theory

The anonymous writer proposes that decomposition is driven by living creatures too small to see.

They explicitly acknowledge that the idea sounds implausible but say experiments appear to support it.

The same model is extended to disease: the writer suggests similarly tiny organisms may cause ulcers and dysentery.

## Poison/medicine relationship

The letter describes an uncommon paralytic poison that, at small doses, can treat ulcers and dysentery.

The writer's hypothesis is that:
- the substance is lethal to the unseen organisms;
- a small dose is only inconvenient to the patient;
- a much larger quantity could suppress decomposition in a corpse.

The proposed preparation uses swamp fungal pods and imp stool, followed by detailed alchemical instructions.

## Reliability

The letter is explicitly scientific speculation rather than omniscient narration.

Store separately:
- observed/claimed experiment: the writer believes tests support an invisible-living-agent model;
- hypothesis: those agents cause decomposition and certain diseases;
- proposed application: high-dose preparation as embalming preservative.

The source is important because it shows an in-world naturalistic disease/decomposition theory coexisting with magical explanations elsewhere in the continuity.
