# Cursed Tome arc — batch 01: recruitment and enslavement

Continuity: `tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra`

This batch intentionally covers only ten INFO records so the arc can be committed in small, auditable increments.

## Records analyzed

1. **0553A10C** — Frysla opens by identifying the player as someone who wants "something more".
2. **0553A10D** — Frysla narrows that desire to power.
3. **0553A10F** — she offers a small amount of magical ability.
4. **0553A111** — she explicitly names the price as **freedom**: the recruit must work for her boss until released.
5. **0553A112** — she normalizes the arrangement by comparing it to joining an army.
6. **0553A116** — acceptance leads the recruit to a rune behind the stables to meet the new boss.
7. **0553A0F9** — the Vampire Lord identifies himself as the recruit's new Master and explicitly restates the exchange as "power for freedom".
8. **0553A0FA** — he orders the recruit to the ritual circle and promises to carve symbols of power into their flesh, creating a novice bloodmage.
9. **0553A0FE** — when the recruit refuses, the Master reveals that he cast a **Curse of Obedience** while they slept.
10. **0553A0F5** — the Master explains that the enslaved recruit will participate in a "game" to reconstruct an artifact, the Book of Curses.

## Interpretation

The initial recruitment is exploitative but not wholly concealed: Frysla explicitly states that freedom is the price before the recruit agrees. The key escalation is the Master's unilateral conversion of that vague service obligation into magical slavery.

This distinction matters for later branches. Frysla can truthfully insist that she disclosed a price, while the Master can still be understood as having imposed conditions far beyond what a reasonable recruit was told.

The bloodmage initiation is likewise not merely employment training. The Master's language describes permanent bodily inscription and a supernatural status change.

## Evidence status

- Frysla's stated bargain terms: direct character testimony.
- Acceptance route through the stable rune: direct quest/dialogue fact.
- Master's identity as a Vampire Lord and self-declared owner/master: direct dialogue fact.
- Curse of Obedience imposed during sleep: direct dialogue fact.
- Bloodmage ritual: direct dialogue fact; mechanical corroboration should be checked in the next implementation-focused batch.
- Book of Curses "game": direct dialogue fact; organizer identity is not established in this batch.
