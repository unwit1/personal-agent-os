## Note 15: drained Forsworn

The writer uses inflammatory anti-Forsworn rhetoric and argues the bodies prove Forsworn brutality.

The physical observation—Forsworn corpses completely drained of blood—is separable from the writer's causal/political conclusion.

Do not automatically conclude the Forsworn killed their own.

## Note 17: distributed disappearances

The writer recognizes a common pattern across a broad area and infers a central operation with access to multiple roads.

That is an investigative hypothesis, not a confirmed perpetrator identity.

## Note 24: unstable bandit leadership

A criminal correspondent says their boss is becoming unstable and obsessed with a strange sword while an excavation fails to produce profit.

The writer also reports conflict with another bandit group near Dwemer ruins over a caravan.

This is useful local criminal-network evidence but does not identify the sword in the note itself.

## Note 28: smuggler encounter

The smuggler describes a female/avian-looking attacker from a cave.

The slang description is insufficient to normalize the attacker to hagraven, harpy, or another creature without placement/quest evidence.
