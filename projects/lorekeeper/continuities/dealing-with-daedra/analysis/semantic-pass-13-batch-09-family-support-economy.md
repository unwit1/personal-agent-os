# Semantic pass 13 — batch 09: Family support economy

This batch covers Bjar (4 exact-Subject INFO), Salonia (3), and Rithrannir (2).

## Bjar: material conversion for the forces below

Bjar says he formerly smelted ore from the mine for export.

He now smelts both mine ore and recovered Dwemer equipment and sends the resulting material below.

He specifically buys:
- Dwemer arms;
- Dwemer armor;
- raw Dwemer metal.

He asks that active automata not be destroyed unnecessarily because they kill Falmer.

This shows the underground society treating Dwemer ruins as both a material source and an ecological/military asset.

## Salonia: fertility testimony and food taboo

Salonia says she miscarried her first child and had been told she could never have another.

She attributes restored fertility to the God-Mother's blessing.

She says her second child was born healthy but with numerous "holy mutations" and now lives below as part of the God-Mother.

She hopes for a third child to offer.

Salonia sells fruit and vegetables to feed the faithful because, in her words, they are unworthy to consume meat.

Her fertility/healing explanation is devotional testimony. The existence of her belief, child-offering aspiration, produce trade, and meat taboo are direct social facts.

## Rithrannir: surface fundraising

Rithrannir says he had been crippled, unable to work, and dying of disease before becoming stronger through the Family's transformation.

He now processes animal remains and Forsworn gear and sells the material in Markarth to raise money for the Family.

This provides a concrete bridge between the hidden flesh cult and ordinary surface markets.

## Economic pattern

Together these minor members show that the Family is supported by:
- smelting and reuse of Dwemer material;
- surface sale of scavenged/processed goods;
- produce provisioning;
- controlled meat ideology;
- transformation narratives that generate loyalty and recruitment.

The Family is therefore a materially organized community rather than only Thaer's laboratory.
