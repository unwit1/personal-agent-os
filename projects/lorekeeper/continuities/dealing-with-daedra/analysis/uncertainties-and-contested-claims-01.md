# Uncertainties and contested claims — pass 01

This file prevents strong-sounding character lore from silently becoming objective continuity fact.

## Keep attributed

- Grados's Thalmor/Tower plan and Talos oversoul model: character theory.
- The Herald's Dreamsleeve/Draugrisation mechanics and ancient Dragon-Cult history: cult doctrine.
- Beldam Annes's descriptions of rival covens: partisan ethnography.
- Lysara's identification of the Night Mother with Mephala: uncorroborated cult claim.
- Brethren Priest's Udyrlistig/Hermaeus-Mora/Lurker deep history: inherited cult history plus admitted speculation.
- Hooded Figure's claim that Imperial accounts of the Oblivion Crisis may be propaganda: Dagonist rhetoric.
- Sadren's Snow-Elf/Wispmother historical model: research hypothesis based on recovered memories and artifacts.
- Patron Notes cosmology: research report; experimental conclusions vary in certainty.
- Talmeth Dres's historical/racial commentary: strongly biased personal testimony.

## Cross-source inferences, not direct identifications

- Gatekeeper patrons ~= Ideal Masters.
- Toruld's "masters"/Soul Cairn powers ~= Ideal Masters.
- The Worm/Revenant-Moon faction appears Mannimarco-aligned, but the Unsent Letter does not name its author or faction in plain text.

## Stronger continuity facts

- Mageblight exists in this continuity and is magicka-parasitic.
- The Syndicate and Eastmarch Gentleman's Club are linked.
- Darkling initiation uses a Shrike, Nocturnal-aligned Ichor, and one of three ritual paths.
- Gatekeepers trade souls/materials for Soul Cairn power.
- The Rorikstead pact is independently supported by Rorik's confession, a victim-side suicide note, and Skaafin dialogue.
