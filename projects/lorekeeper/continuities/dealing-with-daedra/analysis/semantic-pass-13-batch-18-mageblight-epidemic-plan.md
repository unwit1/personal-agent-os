# Semantic pass 13 — batch 18: Mageblight epidemic-export plan

This batch reconciles BOOK 052F7B91 (*Unacceptable Progress*) with the existing Mageblight sources.

## Internal name: Magebane

The note complains that too few Legion soldiers are "contracting Magebane".

The plugin's implementation also uses Magebane as an internal/mechanical name for several disease/anti-magic spell records, while public-facing sources call the spreading affliction MageBlight.

Given the shared infection/anti-magicka context, Lorekeeper should treat Magebane here as the operational/internal label for the Mageblight system unless contradictory evidence emerges.

## Strategic objective

The note's goal is not merely to infect troops currently stationed in Skyrim.

It says:
- active Legion troops will eventually finish their rotation and return to other provinces;
- enough must become carriers to trigger an epidemic after redeployment;
- distribution must increase in quantity and number of hubs;
- the opportunity will be lost if "the elves" notice before critical spread;
- failure will result in the local operator being replaced.

This is direct evidence of an intentional epidemic-export strategy.

The author/sponsor is not identified in the note. The editor ID includes "syndiecate", but editor naming alone should not be used to promote a definitive in-world author.

## Public Mageblight evidence

The public PSA (0520D4F1) describes:
- an actively spreading disease;
- progression into a curse-like state;
- magicka-regeneration restriction;
- heightened magic sensitivity/craving;
- magicka parasitism;
- spread through close contact and tainted mana potions, ale, and wine.

Grados separately says Mageblight is artificially created from Chaotic Creatia and spread through fluids.

## Cure campaign

A later notice (0563CEE4) says the Alchemists' Union of Alinor has funded enough free cure for Skyrim natives through licensed alchemists.

The cure campaign proves a large organized response exists.

It does **not** establish that the Alinor organization created the disease. In fact, the internal epidemic note's fear that "the elves will take notice" is compatible with an outside group attempting to spread the disease before an elven response shuts it down.

## Strategic synthesis

Mageblight is therefore not only an anti-mage technology.

At least one operation attempts to weaponize ordinary troop rotation and civilian supply contamination to export an epidemic beyond Skyrim.

The sponsor and ultimate target population remain unresolved.
