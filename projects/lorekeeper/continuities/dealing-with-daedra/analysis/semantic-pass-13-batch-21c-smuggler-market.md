# Semantic pass 13 — batch 21c: smuggler market, bounties, and EETC circumvention

This batch analyzes the smuggler target list, captain bounty list, and two near-duplicate market bulletins.

## High-margin goods

BOOK 0527D20C lists goods desired from Skyrim and pays in untraceable silver ingots.

The market particularly values:
- Dwemer puzzle boxes;
- Dwemer dynamo cores;
- Dragon-Cult jewellery;
- dragon ivory;
- ebony;
- malachite;
- Dwarven metal.

Stolen goods are accepted but discounted when they retain identifying marks.

## EETC monopoly

Bulletins 0559FB0F and 054E8A09 are near-duplicate versions of the same smuggler opportunity.

Both say the network is interested in circumventing the East Empire Trading Company's monopoly on ebony and malachite.

They also emphasize Dwemer curiosities, Dragon-Cult jewellery, dragon bones, and high-value goods too hot to resell normally.

One bulletin says a Winterhold Inn associate provides current berth coordinates; the other says the smugglers are currently berthed off Winterhold.

These should be treated as variants of one operational notice, not separate lore events.

## Maritime bounties

BOOK 0527D20A places bounties on:
- Captain Haldyn / Blood Horkers — signature grimoire — 3 gold bars;
- Captain Hargar / Blackblood Marauders — signature dagger — 2 gold bars;
- Captain Volf / Dainty Sload — signature EEC necklace — 1 gold bar;
- Captain Safia / Red Wave — signature ring — 1 gold bar.

The signature objects function as proof-of-kill/provenance tokens.

## Significance

The smuggler economy uses:
- untraceable metal rather than only septims;
- provenance-sensitive pricing;
- mobile berth coordinates;
- monopoly evasion;
- maritime bounty contracting;
- archaeological/occult commodities.

This supports Plautis's dialogue that the network is decentralized and maritime rather than a single formal organization.
