# Semantic pass 13 — batch 01: Cultist Wraith and Soulgem Dust

This batch intentionally analyzes a single speaker cluster: 11 exact-Subject INFO records for the Gatekeeper Cultist Wraith (NPC 051D00DD), plus the directly associated Dust items/scripts and one supply bulletin.

## Wraith as Gatekeeper service provider

The Cultist Wraith is not merely a summoned combatant. It speaks, trades, blesses, and trains.

It asks mortals to bring empty white soul gems and says they are shattered into shards, mixed with withered soul-grass from its master's realm, and used to "beckon the soldiers through the void" (INFO 0559FB68, 0559FB6A).

This provides an internal production explanation for the Gatekeepers' Soulgem Dust economy: mundane/white soul-gem material is combined with Soul-Cairn-associated matter and used in conjuration/necromantic practice.

## Dust as currency and magical substrate

The Wraith accepts Soulgem Dust as payment.

For eight units of ordinary Soulgem Dust:
- INFO 0562DB10 / script TIF__0562DB10 removes 8 Dust and casts a Grave-touch/Soul-Cairn-affinity effect on the player.
- INFO 0562DB11 / script TIF__0562DB11 removes 8 ordinary Dust and returns 8 Gravetouched Soulgem Dust.

The Wraith describes the second operation as making the Dust "darker, purer" and better for weaving spirits into stronger trinkets.

Conjuration training also uses Dust as payment: INFO 056D0D24 and its script remove 8 Dust and increase Conjuration.

The Wraith eventually refuses further blessing because the mortal "would not survive" and says advanced teaching requires beseeching the Masters directly (0562DB0F, 056EF495).

## External market

The book Looking for Supply (BOOK 055A9D7B) says a new substance called Dust is circulating, mages strongly desire it, normal suppliers do not appear to control it, and product is observed moving near Nightgate Inn.

Separate Gatekeeper dialogue says their friendly criminal associates buy the faction's Dust.

This means Dust has crossed from internal ritual material into Skyrim's black market.

## Interpretation

High-confidence continuity facts:
- the Gatekeepers/Wraith use empty white soul gems in a Soul-Cairn-linked Dust process;
- ordinary Soulgem Dust can be ritually altered into Gravetouched Soulgem Dust;
- Dust functions as payment for blessings and Conjuration instruction;
- Dust is sold outside the cult.

Source-limited interpretation:
- the Wraith calls the relevant realm its master's realm and later directs the player to the "Masters"; the wider Gatekeeper corpus strongly suggests Soul Cairn patrons/Ideal Masters, but the Wraith does not independently identify those patrons by name.
