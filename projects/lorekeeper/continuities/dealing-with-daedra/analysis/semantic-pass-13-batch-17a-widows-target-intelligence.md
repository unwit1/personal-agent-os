# Semantic pass 13 — batch 17a: Widows target intelligence

This batch analyzes BOOK 0569E033, an operational target list for potential unhappy-spouse recruitment.

The note is not just gossip. Each entry pairs a strained marriage with a plausible murder cover story.

## Frabbi / Kleppr — Markarth

The note observes the couple's public hostility and proposes exploiting Markarth's Forsworn violence.

Suggested cover: murder Kleppr and stage the scene as a Forsworn attack.

## Olda / Horgeir — Dragon Bridge

The note cites Horgeir's drinking and the area's wilderness/cliffs.

Suggested cover: make a death/disappearance resemble an alcohol-related accident, with the body potentially lost downstream/out to sea.

## Ahlam / Nazeem — Whiterun

The note sees Ahlam as lonely/frustrated but harder to recruit because of her Kynareth-temple ties.

Open murder in Whiterun is judged difficult.

Suggested method: poisoned imported alcohol intended for Nazeem, exploiting his habit of giving prestige gifts to the Jarl after sampling them himself.

## Nivenor / Bolli — Riften

The note cites Nivenor's money motive, rumored infidelity, and resentment of Bolli's Mara-inspired charity.

Suggested cover: a robbery/mugging while Bolli is doing charity in the Ratway.

## Beitild / Leigelf — Dawnstar

The note identifies business rivalry between the spouses' mines and proposes a mining accident as plausible cover.

An edit then warns that Beitild is rumored to have attracted Dark Brotherhood attention and orders the cult not to interfere.

## Operational pattern

The Widows systematically map:
- marital resentment;
- target routine;
- local violence/crime;
- institutional affiliation;
- plausible deniability;
- interference from rival assassins.

This is a more developed intelligence practice than spontaneous spouse murder.

The note proposes operations; it does not prove every listed murder occurs in every playthrough.
