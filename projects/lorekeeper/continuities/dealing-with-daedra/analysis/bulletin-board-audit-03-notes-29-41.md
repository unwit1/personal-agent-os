## Notes 31–32: disappearance geography

The northern coast and Cold Rock Pass both receive public disappearance warnings.

The coast notice suspects slavers operating from an island or berthed vessel.

Cold Rock Pass evidence is limited to missing travellers and abandoned belongings with no bodies.

These should remain separate unresolved incident clusters unless another source links them.

## Note 34: strange lights

The notice observes strange lights, then speculates that Imperial "elf-magic" is preparing an attack on Windhelm.

The observation and political interpretation must be stored separately.

## Note 37: blood-draining spiders

The warning describes frostbite spiders that leave victims fully drained of blood and hunt farther than expected.

This is an ecological observation report; it does not identify Kornalus or another modifier.

## Note 38: corpse-preservation logistics

An unnamed researcher says the northern coast is better than a proposed fortress because glacier conditions preserve flesh and shipwrecks provide a steady corpse supply.

The writer explicitly prefers undead flesh to bones.

This is direct evidence of necromantic material procurement but the researcher identity is unresolved.

## Note 40: Calcelmo supply

A correspondent says Calcelmo's latest Dwemer oil batch was sufficiently flammable but Fort Sungard's seizure prevents further deliveries for the foreseeable future.

This is evidence of Calcelmo participating in a Dwemer-material supply relationship; the recipient's identity/purpose remains unresolved.

## Note 41: anomalous patrol deaths

The military writer distinguishes the attacks from normal Stormcloak methods because bodies show unnatural burning/freezing and requests Synod or College of Whispers battlemages.

The magical attacker is not identified.
