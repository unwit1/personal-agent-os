# Semantic pass 13 — batch 04: unauthorized Midden student

This batch covers nine exact-Subject INFO records for Student NPC 057E83A9.

## Unauthorized research

The Student is encountered in the Midden beneath the College of Winterhold.

They initially claim to belong there, then admit through evasive dialogue that they are conducting "secret experiments" and fear other College members will steal the credit.

They also say the area is off limits and that discovery of an outsider would bring investigation.

## Experimental charm spell

The Student proposes testing a variant charm spell on the player in exchange for passage.

The player can explicitly characterize the proposed experiment as sexual coercion/rape and threaten violence.

Alternative outcomes include:
- bribing the Student with 2,000 septims as "research funding";
- consenting to the test in exchange for scrolls of the ordinary Charm spell.

The branch is useful because it distinguishes an experimental charm variant from the mod's ordinary Charm spell (SPEL 05291A1B / 05291A1C).

## Institutional boundary

Nothing in this exchange establishes College authorization.

The opposite is more strongly supported:
- the Student says the Midden is off limits;
- the research is described as secret;
- the Student fears investigation;
- access can be bought privately.

This should therefore be stored as an example of unauthorized research occurring inside/under a major magical institution, not as College policy.

## Lore significance

The scene adds a mundane ethical layer to the mod's repeated use of mind-affecting magic. Charm is not only an adventuring mechanic or cult technique; researchers can also treat it as experimental technology whose consent implications are recognized by characters in-world.
