# Semantic lore analysis — pass 01

This pass promotes a first set of evidence-backed semantic layers from the complete Dealing with Daedra text import. It focuses on high-volume explicitly resolved speakers and on lore clusters where dialogue can be checked against written sources.

## Deterministic dialogue attribution

INFO records were resolved only where exactly one GetIsID CTDA condition points to a known NPC record. This resolves 1,469 of 1,809 INFO records and 2,188 of 2,674 response strings (about 82%). Unresolved, multi-candidate, alias-driven, scene-driven, or condition-ambiguous records remain unassigned.

## Major continuity clusters

1. Cult of the Return / revived Dragon Cult — the Herald teaches Draugrisation, a restored priestly hierarchy, Alduin's return, dragon-soul anchoring in bones, and recruitment of Skyrim's marginalized as future soldiers and clergy.
2. North-Jerall witches — Beldam Annes describes a Hircine-oriented, lycanthropic cell tradition and a philosophy of witchcraft based on twisting existing structures rather than simply imposing change through magicka.
3. Flesh magic / the Family — Thaer treats the surrounding religion as a social shell around an experimental flesh-magic program involving meat, blood, bone, breath, soul, Daedric vestiges, polyps, and the God-Mother organism.
4. Gatekeepers / Soul Cairn commerce — a necromantic organization trades souls and artifacts with powerful Soul Cairn patrons. Dialogue considers the Ideal Masters the likeliest identity but explicitly leaves the identification uncertain.
5. Toruld's Vigilant schism — Vigilant Toruld loses faith in Stendarr and turns toward Soul Cairn powers as weapons against Daedra, reproducing the coercive soul practices he originally opposed.
6. Mageblight / Chaotic Creatia — Grados describes an artificial magicka-parasitic affliction made from Chaotic Creatia. Its existence and symptoms are independently corroborated by the in-world PSA: MageBlight.
7. Widows of Mephala — Lysara Carius rebuilds a hereditary Mephalan tradition centered on betrayal, spouse/lover sacrifice, youth, illusion, poison, identity alteration, and social infiltration.
8. Deep Ones / Udyrlistig — underground priests describe a multigenerational transformation of mortals into Deep Ones. Their deeper troll/Lurker/Hermaeus-Mora origin story is partly inherited myth and partly explicitly acknowledged conjecture.
9. Clavicus Vile and Rorikstead — multiple sources in this continuity attribute Rorikstead's fertility to a recurring bargain with Clavicus Vile whose cost is the life of fertile women during childbirth.
10. Criminal/cult interlock — the Syndicate, smugglers, Gentleman’s Club, cults, witches, and other groups repeatedly exchange labor, contraband, magical materials, recruits, information, and access. Daedric practice is presented as an underground economy as much as a collection of isolated shrines.

## Reliability policy

- Direct continuity fact: mechanics/records or multiple independent in-world sources establish an event or institution inside this mod continuity.
- Character testimony: reliable for what a named speaker says or believes, not automatically for metaphysical or historical truth.
- Cult doctrine: preserves a group's theology/myth without flattening it into objective history.
- Research hypothesis/speculation: source itself is tentative or a character infers from incomplete evidence.
- Base-canon interaction: official TES names/events may be referenced, but mod claims remain scoped to tes.mod.dealing-with-daedra.
