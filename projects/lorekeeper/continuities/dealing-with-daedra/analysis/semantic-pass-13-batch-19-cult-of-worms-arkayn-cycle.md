# Semantic pass 13 — batch 19: Cult of Worms and the Arkayn Cycle

This batch analyzes Recipe BOOK 051FE1A7 and its directly associated ritual/item records.

## Cult attribution

The note explicitly addresses "bretheren of the Cult of Worms".

The writer claims divine inspiration from the Worm-God and instructs finders to consume/pass on the knowledge.

This is direct evidence of distributed Cult-of-Worms ritual knowledge inside Skyrim.

## Arkayn Cycle

The writer says the recipe creates an elixir that "partially corrupts the Arkayn Cycle within our mortal vessels".

The ritual symbolism uses:
- fresh egg — start of life / cycle begins;
- human skull — end of life / cycle ends;
- filled Black Soul Gem — the interim, where renewal is halted by confinement.

The ingredients must be combined at an altar to the Worm-God in a place of death.

## Mechanical corroboration

The plugin contains:
- ALCH 051FE1A3 — Elixir of the Inverted Cycle;
- SPEL 051FE19B / 051FE19C — Ritual of Arkayn Spite;
- MISC 0508A472 — Worm-God Favour;
- Focus Ring: Worm-God and Worm-God Attunement records elsewhere in the pact system.

This strongly supports the recipe as an implemented ritual technology rather than flavor text alone.

## Wider continuity

Other sources use "Arkayn Cycle" language:
- the Cult of the Return describes Draugr as existing outside the Arkayn Cycle;
- Vigilant theology presents Arkay as reclaiming mortal souls into a rebirth cycle;
- Gatekeepers and Worm-aligned necromancers deliberately interfere with ordinary soul passage.

These sources agree that "Arkayn Cycle" names a life/death/soul-order concept inside the mod, but they disagree sharply about whether it is sacred, escapable, or something to corrupt.

## Identity boundary

"Worm-God" strongly points toward Mannimarco in Elder Scrolls terminology and aligns with the separate Revenant-Moon/Mannimarco evidence.

The recipe itself does not spell out the name Mannimarco. Keep the explicit label Worm-God in primary claims and treat the Mannimarco identity through the existing inference layer.
