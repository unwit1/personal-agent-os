# Semantic pass 13 — batch 39: Sadren's live-in servant recruitment

Source: BOOK 0597F35A.

Sadren recruits a prospective live-in servant/maid from Whiterun.

The offer promises:
- higher pay than the target currently receives from Hulda;
- lodging/work at Sadren's tundra estate;
- possible participation in research;
- possible acquisition of minor magical knowledge.

The estate is described as roughly halfway along the Whiterun–Rorikstead road, reached by a dirt path.

## Relationship to later evidence

Sadren's household task list shows the live-in servant role includes ordinary domestic labor.

Sadren's dialogue also shows assistants can become participants in dangerous Wispmother possession/memory experiments.

The recruitment letter does **not** disclose those experimental risks.

Therefore the continuity supports a recruitment asymmetry:
- advertised: better pay, domestic employment, possible magical learning;
- actual possible role: domestic service plus hazardous research assistance.

Do not assume every servant is used in the Wispmother experiment, but the risk exists within Sadren's assistant system.
